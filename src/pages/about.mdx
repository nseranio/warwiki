## About

**WARWIKI** is a focused resource for functional reconstructive urology — built to reflect the field as it is practiced, not as it is partitioned.

It brings together domains often treated separately, aligning them around shared principles of reconstruction, function, and anatomy. The intent is simple: a single, coherent space for how reconstructive urology is actually understood and applied.

</section>

<section className={styles.section}>

## Why WARWIKI

The name honors [Richard Turner-Warwick](/docs/roots/surgeons/s-z/richard-turner-warwick), whose work embodied a unified approach to reconstruction. His perspective — unconstrained by artificial divisions — remains instructive.

WARWIKI is built in that spirit: less emphasis on boundaries, more on fundamentals.

</section>

<section className={styles.section}>

## Mission

To develop a high-quality, durable reference for functional reconstructive urology:

- Clinically grounded
- Principled in approach
- Relevant across the full spectrum of reconstruction

The aim is not breadth alone, but clarity and utility.

</section>

<section className={styles.section}>

## Collaboration & Peer Review

WARWIKI is intended to evolve as a collaborative, peer-reviewed platform.

Ongoing contributions and critical review are essential to maintaining accuracy, relevance, and rigor. The long-term goal is a resource shaped by the community it serves.

</section>
