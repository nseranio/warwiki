## Prompt to paste

**Time window: [TIME WINDOW]. Depth: [DEPTH].**

Give me the new and impactful publications **[TIME WINDOW]** across genitourinary reconstructive surgery, urogynecology, reconstructive pelvic surgery, and all related fields. Be exhaustive and organize the answer domain-by-domain using the categories below. For each domain, include the most impactful RCTs, guidelines, guideline updates, systematic reviews/meta-analyses, pivotal/registry trials, and consensus statements. Prioritize practice-changing evidence, and for each item note the study or guideline name, journal, year, design, and the key finding with effect sizes where available. End with a summary table of the highest-impact items sorted by clinical impact, and flag any major ongoing trials with pending readouts.

## Optional additions

- Prioritize large RCTs, Cochrane reviews, strong systematic reviews, pivotal trials and major guidelines over narrative reviews or isolated small studies.
- For each item, give a DOI, PMID or official source link; distinguish online publication date from issue date.
- Flag changes to standard of care or guideline recommendations and explain limitations, absolute benefits, harms and follow-up.
- Identify trial protocols and pending readouts separately from completed results.
- Specify the country and regulator for any claimed drug or device approval.
