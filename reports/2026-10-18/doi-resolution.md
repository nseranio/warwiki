# DOI/PMID resolution of the October 11 leftovers (October 18, 2026)

Ledger: `doi-resolution.tsv` (one row per reference: October 11 class, page, ref number, text, decision, identifier, evidence). Working files (gitignored): `reports/audit-v2/sources-local/doi-2026-10-18/`.

**Binding conflicts (150).** These were not reference/paper disagreements: on October 11 every one had matched author, year and title, and was reverted only because a new DOI or PMID changes the claim-gate binding of the claims that cite it. Each was re-fetched from Crossref or PubMed and re-matched with a stricter rule (author, year, title ≥0.95 or journal/volume/page, book identity, DOI resolves). 138 passed; 8 were rejected (five *Int J Gynaecol Obstet* abstract-reprint DOIs for articles the pages cite in their original journals, one *Obstet Gynecol Surv* abstract-reprint DOI for a *J Am Coll Surg* article, one *Urol Int* DOI for a reference to the Chinese-language *Zhonghua Yi Xue Za Zhi* paper, and Zhang 2019 *Asian J Androl*, whose registered DOI ends in a semicolon and whose Crossref first author differs from PubMed); 4 already carried their PubMed link.

**Ambiguous (295).** Codex author pass, then an independent Codex verifier that re-fetched every record: 103 exact (92 DOI, 11 PMID) and 19 labelled reference errors (all but one were subtitle or end-page differences, accepted as the same item; amb037 had a wrong end page, corrected to 173–205). Verifier: 121 accept, 1 accept with correction, 0 reject; a mechanical author/year/title check found no conflicts. 81 remain uncertain (candidate exists, identity not certain; mostly different editions or chapters) and 92 have no identifier (print-only chapters, textbooks, guidelines).

**Claim gate.** Adding an identifier re-keys every gated claim that cites the reference, so 238 + 164 re-keyed claims went through Codex rechecks (2 reviewers, 1 verifier; five rounds including final-text checks). Where the checker could not open the source (mostly paywalled book chapters), the identifier was held back so the claims keep their existing status: 57 identifiers. Three more were held back on two pages that carry another session's uncommitted edit (penile reconstruction, recurrent UTI).

**Net:** 190 identifiers added (97 from the binding list, 93 from the ambiguous list); 60 verified identifiers held back; 8 rejected; 81 uncertain; 92 none. The rechecks also produced 34 reviewed corrections and citation placements (listed in CHANGELOG, October 18).
