## Disagreements with the old Journal Club record

- **“Urodynamics did not change management.”** This is too absolute. In the linked secondary analysis, a urodynamics-driven global treatment-plan change was recorded for **41/294 (14%)** women; surgery was canceled in **4/294 (1.4%)**, and the procedure changed in **16/294 (5.4%)**. The randomized arms nevertheless had similar overall operation distributions and outcomes. Locator: Sirls 2013, Results: Treatment, Table 2.
- **“It can be skipped before a sling.”** The conclusion requires the trial's structured office assessment and selected, primary, uncomplicated stress-predominant population. The trial allowed several continence operations; it did not randomize sling techniques. Locator: Nager 2012 PDF pp2–3,5–6.
- The old approximate **“~77% in both arms”**, sample size **630**, year, DOI, and AUA/SUFU/ACOG direction agree with the sources. The exact per-protocol estimates are **203/264 (76.9%)** with urodynamics and **200/259 (77.2%)** with office evaluation only (Nager 2012 PDF p5).
