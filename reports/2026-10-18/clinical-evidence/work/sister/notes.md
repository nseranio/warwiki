## Old Journal Club record disagreements

| Old wording | Source finding | Locator |
|---|---|---|
| “Women undergoing first surgery” | Prior incontinence surgery was allowed; 13% of sling and 15% of Burch patients had undergone it. | Albo 2007 PDF p5 / journal p2147, patient characteristics. |
| “de novo urge incontinence with the sling” as part of its excess morbidity | New *treated* urge incontinence occurred in 11 women in each arm (about 3% each). The excess of treated postoperative urge incontinence was mainly persistent disease: 79/326 versus 59/329. | Albo 2007 PDF p6 / journal p2148. |
| “durable benchmark, especially for complex and revision cases” | The primary report follows patients for 24 months and enrolled a minority with previous incontinence surgery; it does not establish comparative outcomes specifically for revision or complex cases. | Albo 2007 PDF pp5,9–10 / journal pp2147,2152–2153. |
| “go-to in mesh-averse or salvage settings” | AUA/SUFU statement 12 lists autologous fascial sling and Burch among options for index patients. The SISTEr report did not randomize mesh-averse or salvage cohorts to those strategies. | AUA/SUFU local guideline printed p2, statement 12; Albo 2007 PDF pp2–5. |

The old record's two-year success percentages and P values match the source. Its success percentages are Kaplan–Meier estimates, not simple observed fractions, and its unstated denominators should not be inferred from 326 and 329 randomized assignments.
