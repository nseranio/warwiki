## Source reconciliation

- The 2021 article's **abstract** prints the adjusted difference as `+3.6%` with a CI of `−11.6 to 4.6`; its Results and Table 2 give **−3.6 percentage points** for the sling-minus-AUS *continence* difference. The 2022 full report re-estimates the same endpoint at **−3.4 points** (95% CI −11.7 to 4.8). The trial file and page use the 2021 Results/Table 2 value for the primary report and preserve the difference in the file note. Source: Abrams 2021 PDF pp 812, 814, 817; HTA PDF p 53, Table 7.
- The 2021 article records **6 sling events in 6 men** and **13 AUS events in 11 men** among 180 and 173 operated men. The full 2022 report records **8 events in 8 sling men** and **15 events in 13 AUS men** among 180 and 175 operated men, adding two sling mesh erosions and two AUS-arm operations. These are report-version differences. Source: Abrams 2021 PDF pp 817–819, Table 3/Figure 1; HTA PDF pp 49–50, Tables 5–6.
- At 24 months the strict endpoint met the stated 15-point noninferiority margin (21/140 versus 22/148 continent; adjusted difference −0.6 points, 95% CI −9.2 to 8.0; P = 0.001). The less-strict definition did not meet it: 44/140 versus 51/148 continent; adjusted difference −4.0 points, 95% CI −16.7 to 8.8; P = 0.045 against the one-sided 0.025 threshold. Source: HTA PDF p 53, Table 7.
