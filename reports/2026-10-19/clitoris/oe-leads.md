## Anatomy
- Total extent 9–11 cm; only glans and prepuce external. Glans ~6–8 mm long, 4–5 mm wide, no erectile tissue, densely innervated. Body ~25–29 mm (paired corpora, septum, tunica albuginea). Crura ~50–52 mm along ischiopubic rami under ischiocavernosus. Vestibular bulbs ~52 mm, spongy, under bulbospongiosus. Prepuce/frenulum from anterior fusion of labia minora; suspensory ligament superficial/intermediate/deep. (Pauls 2015 Clin Anat 10.1002/ca.22524; O'Connell 2005 J Urol 10.1097/01.ju.0000173639.38898.cd; Longhurst 2024 Clin Anat 10.1002/ca.24113 meta-analysis; Jackson 2019 AJOG 10.1016/j.ajog.2019.06.048; Golden 2026 Abdom Radiol 10.1007/s00261-025-05179-1 MRI)
- SGS 2025 structured review: 687 terms for 22 components; standardized terminology; "bulbs of the clitoris" vs "of the vestibule" (Shinnick 2025 AJOG 10.1016/j.ajog.2025.06.025).
- Histology mirrors penile tissue (PDE5, NOS); unfused deep fascia so less rigid erection (Baskin 1999 J Urol 10.1016/S0022-5347(01)68052-2; Greenwald 2026 Pract Radiat Oncol 10.1016/j.prro.2025.11.013). Bulbs: thinner investing membrane (Tappy 2023 AJOG 10.1016/j.ajog.2023.02.011).
- Clitourethrovaginal complex (Jannini 2014 Nat Rev Urol 10.1038/nrurol.2014.193; Wei 2023 Arch Gynecol Obstet 10.1007/s00404-023-06977-y).
- Dorsal nerve of clitoris (pudendal S2–S4) along ischiopubic ramus, under symphysis, dorsal 11–1 o'clock; nerve density greater dorsally; ventral midline safe zone (Jackson 2019; Tappy 2024 IUJ 10.1007/s00192-024-05811-6). Fiber counts: mean ~5,140 per DNC (~10,281 total) in transmasculine patients (Uloko 2023 J Sex Med 10.1093/jsxmed/qdac027); Tunçkol 2024 Sci Rep 10.1038/s41598-024-72898-8; Oakley 2013 J Sex Med 10.1111/jsm.12230.
- Cavernous nerve (inferior hypogastric plexus) at 5 and 7 o'clock along vagina/urethra; spongious nerve to bulbs (Moszkowicz 2011 J Sex Med 10.1111/j.1743-6109.2010.02182.x; Bekker 2012 J Sex Med 10.1111/j.1743-6109.2012.02711.x — also slings and clitoral nerves).
- Arterial supply internal pudendal (dorsal, deep, bulbar arteries); lymphatics to deep inguinal nodes. Bulbocavernosus reflex.
- Embryology: genital tubercle, weeks 9–13; homologues (Baskin 2018 Differentiation 10.1016/j.diff.2018.08.001).

## Physiology
- NO → sGC → cGMP → PKG → BKCa → smooth-muscle relaxation; PDE5 degrades cGMP; iberiotoxin abolishes (Gragasin 2004 FASEB J 10.1096/fj.04-1978com). Sildenafil relaxes rabbit clitoral cavernosum (Vemulapalli 2000 Life Sci 10.1016/s0024-3205(00)00596-8). RhoA/ROCK and alpha-adrenergic tone; testosterone and estradiol effects (Comeglio 2016 J Sex Med 10.1016/j.jsxm.2016.10.007; Munarriz 2003 J Urol 10.1097/01.ju.0000075352.03144.15).
- Orgasm: rhythmic pelvic floor contractions; no refractory period; CUV model (Puppo 2013 Clin Anat 10.1002/ca.22177; Pope 2026 Obstet Gynecol 10.1097/AOG.0000000000006338 female orgasmic disorder).

## Clitoral adhesions, phimosis, keratin pearls (expand existing clitoral-phimosis page)
- 2025 Delphi definitions: phimosis = inability to retract prepuce to expose glans; adhesion = tethering of prepuce to glans; grade mild/moderate/severe (Krapf 2025 BJOG 10.1111/1471-0528.70066).
- In-office lysis + keratin pearl excision (LCA-KPE): clitoral pain 6.91 → 2.50, FSFI 12.12 → 17.68, recurrence ~28%, median 2 repeat procedures among recurrences; "painful but worthwhile" (Krapf 2024 J Sex Med 10.1093/jsxmed/qdae034).
- Nonsurgical (blunt forceps) lysis: pain improved 76%, arousal 63%, orgasm 64%, 38% of externally anorgasmic achieved orgasm, 83% satisfied; self-report, no validated instrument (Myers 2022 J Sex Med 10.1016/j.jsxm.2022.06.011).
- Topical estrogen resolved keratin pearls in prepubertal girls (Bragiel 2023 JPAG 10.1016/j.jpag.2022.10.002) — adult-facing context only.
- LS adhesions: sharp dissection + oxidized regenerated cellulose (Surgicel) sutured with Vicryl, no recurrence at 1 year, 3 adolescents (Breech 2000 JPAG 10.1016/s1083-3188(99)00029-7); 2 postmenopausal women >18 months (Wyman 2018 Obstet Gynecol 10.1097/AOG.0000000000002919).
- CO2-laser lysis of phimosis: mild reagglutination 25%, reoperation 15% (Kroft 2012 JOGC 10.1016/S1701-2163(16)35243-4). Chmel 2019 J Sex Med 10.1016/j.jsxm.2018.12.012 (phimosis surgery outcomes).
- LS maintenance: 507-woman cohort, compliant vs partially compliant: symptom control 93.3% vs 58.0%, new scarring 3.4% vs 40.0%, SCC/VIN 0% vs 4.7% (Lee 2015 JAMA Dermatol 10.1001/jamadermatol.2015.0643). Daily potent TCS after adhesiolysis until healed (Lee & Fischer 2018 Am J Clin Dermatol 10.1007/s40257-018-0364-7). BASHH 2024 taper: daily 4 wk, alternate days 4 wk, twice weekly 4 wk (Edwards 2025 Int J STD AIDS 10.1177/09564624241311629). Clobetasol vs placebo RR 2.85 (Chi 2012 JAAD 10.1016/j.jaad.2012.02.044).
- Postmenopausal labial adhesion: estrogen-only maintenance recurred 3/3; estrogen + dilation none over ~24 months (Ibanez 2025 Maturitas 10.1016/j.maturitas.2025.108823).
- Petrolatum after manual separation 22% vs 36% recurrence, P=.43 (Wejde 2018 BMC Pediatr 10.1186/s12887-018-1018-x).
- Anesthesia: lidocaine–prilocaine cream on genital mucosa 5–10 g for 5–10 min (FDA label); 1% lidocaine infiltration.
- Topical testosterone not useful in genital LS (Cochrane 10.1002/14651858.cd008240.pub2).

## Clitorodynia
- Localized vulvodynia subtype; 2015 ISSVD/ISSWSH/IPPS terminology; 126-woman descriptive study (Parada 2015 J Sex Med 10.1111/jsm.12934). Exclude keratin pearls, epidermal inclusion cyst (DiCarlo-Meacham 2020 JPAG 10.1016/j.jpag.2020.01.150).

## Clitoromegaly
- Adult glans length >10 mm or clitoral index >35 mm²; Society for Endocrinology 2025 androgen-excess guideline: urgent workup for rapid onset, virilization, postmenopausal onset; testosterone, androstenedione, DHEAS, 17-OHP (Elhassan 2025 Clin Endocrinol 10.1111/cen.15265). Pseudoclitoromegaly from epidermal inclusion cysts after FGM (Rouzi 2010 Hum Reprod 10.1093/humrep/deq126). Non-virilizing causes (Jesus 2016 Urology 10.1016/j.urology.2015.10.020).

## Clitoral priapism
- Drug-induced (trazodone classic; nefazodone; pregabalin–duloxetine), pelvic malignancy, idiopathic. Stop agent; oral pseudoephedrine (Unger 2014 J Sex Med 10.1111/jsm.12465; Pescatori 1993 J Urol 10.1016/s0022-5347(17)36450-9; Medina 2002 Obstet Gynecol 10.1016/s0029-7844(02)02084-7; Nguyen 2023 AJHP 10.1093/ajhp/zxac257).
