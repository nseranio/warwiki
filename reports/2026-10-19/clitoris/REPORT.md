## Leads left out

- The exact 11–1 o'clock dorsal-nerve course, a universal ventral-midline “safe zone,” dorsal-nerve fiber counts, suspensory-ligament layer count, deep-inguinal lymphatic route, and bulbocavernosus-reflex claims were omitted because they were not needed here or the cited material did not justify a general operative rule.
- Claims about unfused fascia causing a less rigid erection, detailed RhoA/ROCK or sex-steroid effects, an obligatory orgasm pathway, rhythmic contraction counts, and absence of a refractory period were omitted. The NO cascade is explicitly tied to its experimental tissue basis.
- The lead's fixed clitoromegaly cutoffs (glans length >10 mm or index >35 mm²) were omitted because anatomy varies and the androgen-excess guideline uses clinical progression and laboratory findings for triage.
- The pediatric estrogen/keratin-pearl case, pediatric petrolatum recurrence comparison, and unverified exact lidocaine–prilocaine dose/contact-time were omitted from adult management. Anesthesia is stated at the level documented in the clitorodynia review.
- Values for an arousal device after radiation, clitoral vibratory threshold, hormone/Doppler associations, and FGM/C reconstruction outcomes were not imported into these anatomy/condition pages; their management belongs on existing dedicated pages.
- The Ibanez estrogen-plus-dilation result is explicitly identified as postmenopausal **labial fusion**, not isolated clitoral phimosis. The cellulose-barrier reports are small case reports and are stated with their populations and follow-up.
