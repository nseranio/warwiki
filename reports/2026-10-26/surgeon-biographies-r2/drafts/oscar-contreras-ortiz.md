BIOGRAPHY:

## Biography

Oscar Contreras Ortiz helped turn urogynecology into an international field of teaching and publication.[^1] A founding participant in IUGA's 1976 Mexico City meeting, he later organized three of its annual scientific meetings in Argentina.[^1] Those meetings joined scientific sessions with the social program he arranged for visiting colleagues.[^1]

With Donald Ostergard he founded the International Urogynecology Journal and served as one of its first editors.[^1] He later donated his share of the journal's ownership to IUGA, helping place its publication within the professional society.[^1] He taught urogynecology in Argentina, elsewhere in South America, and in the United States.[^1]

Harry Vervest, writing in IUGA's anniversary book, remembered his sustained interest in colleagues and their families alongside his work on the society and journal.[^1] That personal attention accompanied the meetings and teaching through which Contreras Ortiz connected clinicians across countries.[^1]

His work on publication was practical as well as institutional.[^1] The journal was initially self-published before moving to a commercial publisher, and Contreras Ortiz remained closely involved as an editor.[^1] IUGA's history places that journal alongside annual meetings as a means of sharing research across a field that was still organizing itself.[^1] His decision to transfer ownership to IUGA tied the journal's future to the society he had helped form.[^1]
