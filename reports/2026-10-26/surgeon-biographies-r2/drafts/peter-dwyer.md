## Biography

Peter Dwyer joined a developing international urogynecology community while working as Stuart Stanton's fellow in London in the early 1980s.[^3] He has said that he joined IUGA to present research, meet colleagues, and keep contact with the fellows and friends he met there.[^3] Back in Melbourne, he took charge of the Mercy Hospital for Women urodynamics clinic, a service that allowed him to develop his work in urogynecology.[^4] Later trainees, including Anna Rosamilia, describe him as a mentor in subspecialty training.[^5]

The International Urogynecology Journal became another route for that teaching.[^6] As co-editor with Paul Riss from 2011, Dwyer added video, image, digest, and mentorship sections and created a Fellows Editor role for early-career clinicians.[^6] The work linked clinical research with the professional relationships that had first drawn him to IUGA.[^3][^6]
