## Biography

Hugh Shingleton established the gynecologic oncology division at the University of Alabama at Birmingham after moving there in 1969 and directed it until 1985.[^1] He was a founding member of the Society of Gynecologic Oncology, the Gynecologic Oncology Group, and the society that became AUGS.[^1]

His work on cervical cancer extended into textbooks used by gynecologic oncology trainees.[^1] He later chaired obstetrics and gynecology at UAB and served on the editorial board of *Gynecologic Oncology* and the gynecologic oncology subspecialty board.[^1]

Former colleagues recalled that he concentrated on trainees' strengths while encouraging them to grow.[^1] The memorial names four later Society of Gynecologic Oncology presidents and other society and board leaders among his trainees.[^1]
