## Biography

Sanjay Kulkarni began introducing urethroplasty in his hometown in India in the 1980s.[^4] In 1995 he established a hospital with two operating theatres and 20 beds; by 2021 it had grown into a 100-bed urology centre.[^4] He also started the Kulkarni School of Urethral Surgery, which has hosted surgeons from other countries for training.[^4]

Kulkarni introduced buccal mucosa urethroplasty in 1997, applied it to the penile urethra in 2000, and developed a one-sided dissection technique in 2009.[^4] In the interview he discusses mentors and recommends workshops and operating-theatre observation to surgeons developing their technique.[^4]
