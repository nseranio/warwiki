## Biography

Donald Ostergard's work in urogynecology began during obstetrics and gynecology residency, when Jack Robertson brought new tools for examining the female urethra to Harbor General Hospital.[^6] Their female urology clinic used those tools to evaluate women with incontinence.[^6] On a 1975 sabbatical, Ostergard visited clinicians developing urodynamics in Oslo, London, and San Francisco and reviewed the field's sparse literature.[^6]

He brought microtransducer testing into the female urology clinic he opened in 1977 and began training a fellow the following year.[^6] Postgraduate courses brought physicians from other centers to learn incontinence evaluation.[^6] With colleagues, he helped establish IUGA and the Gynecologic Urology Society.[^6]

Ostergard later collaborated with Oscar Contreras Ortiz on the International Urogynecology Journal and became its managing editor.[^6] He also traces subsequent fellowship programs and society leaders to his trainees.[^6] In a talk on stress incontinence, he contrasted reported results of anterior repair and Burch colposuspension and asked surgeons to review their own patients' outcomes.[^6] His later account describes concerns about vaginal mesh complications informed by earlier experience with prosthetic material.[^6]
