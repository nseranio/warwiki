## Biography

Božo Kralj came to urogynecology after work on cervical insufficiency and the urinary consequences of cervical cancer treatment.[^2] From 1969 he developed urogynecology at Ljubljana University Medical Center, introduced functional electrical stimulation for incontinence, and brought urodynamic testing into hospitals across Slovenia.[^2] He broadened his training through a Fulbright scholarship in the United States, a British Council scholarship in London, and research visits.[^2]

His awareness of urinary incontinence among women led him to become one of IUGA's 11 founding members in 1976.[^2] He later chaired its 1987 annual meeting in Ljubljana and served as its president.[^2] He also led the Slovenian Association of Gynecologists and Obstetricians and the Italian Urogynecology Association, and published a 2010 editorial on pelvic floor dysfunction.[^2]

Colleagues remembered Kralj as a teacher who shared knowledge freely and gave younger clinicians room to develop their work.[^2] After leaving clinical leadership, he served as dean of two health-sciences faculties.[^2]
