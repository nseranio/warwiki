## Biography

Douglas Marchant established the Tufts Breast Health Center in 1978, bringing specialists together to care for women with breast disease.[^2] Colleagues described its shared approach to diagnosis and treatment as a departure from the separate services common at the time.[^2]

Marchant had trained in surgery and obstetrics and began teaching gynecology at Tufts in 1954.[^2] He later directed its cancer institute.[^2] Robert Kennison, one of his former residents, remembered a demanding surgical teacher who expected precision; radiologist Marc Homer recalled that Marchant welcomed changes that could improve care or make it less harmful.[^2]

After leaving Tufts in 1992, Marchant established another Breast Health Center at Women and Infants Hospital in Providence.[^2]
