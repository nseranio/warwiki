BIOGRAPHY:

## Biography

Harold Drutz argued for pelvic floor care that brought urology, gynecology, and colorectal services together.[^1] During a travelling fellowship he worked with clinicians in Africa and Europe, then joined Mount Sinai Hospital in Toronto in 1976.[^1]

He trained his first urogynecology fellow in the early 1980s and became head of the University of Toronto division in 1986.[^1] At Baycrest he led a pelvic floor clinic for older women.[^1] By 2022, he had mentored 44 fellows, some of whom subsequently developed training programs of their own.[^1]

Drutz's research included lower urinary tract pharmacology, thyroid function, and pelvic procedures.[^1] He coedited a multidisciplinary textbook with Sender Herschorn and Nicholas Diamant and was founding president of the Canadian Society of Pelvic Medicine.[^1]
