## Findings that changed the page

- Replaced claims of universal superiority, identical outcomes, fixed age/stage candidacy and mandatory partner acceptance with patient-led goals, alternatives, frailty and informed consent. Permanent loss of penetrative vaginal intercourse is distinct from loss of all sexual activity.
- Corrected partial versus total definitions: partial repair can be performed for a posthysterectomy vault; hysterectomy is an additional individualized decision.
- Removed universal cytology+HPV+endometrial biopsy and statements that the uterus becomes absolutely inaccessible. Investigate bleeding/abnormal findings and screening/risk appropriately; future evaluation is harder, not rendered unnecessary or impossible.
- Removed universal preoperative UDS and automatic sling insertion for occult SUI. Preserved NICE's specific recommendation for UDS before SUI surgery with anterior/apical prolapse/other complicated assessment. Distinguish symptomatic SUI, occult leakage and no incontinence; discuss staged versus concurrent treatment.
- Removed unsafe or unsupported generalization of dilute vasopressin/pituitrin/local anesthetic recipes without dosing, blind knife friction for ulcerated epithelium, one-finger tunnels as a general standard, mandatory aggressive perineorrhaphy, fixed 3×1 cm endpoint and anatomic safety reduced to one landmark. Retained appropriately qualified operative sequence protecting distal anterior tissue, urethra, bladder and rectum with hemostasis and patent channels.
- Removed pooled-looking success/reoperation/mortality/UTI ranges derived from disparate denominators. The PFDN original's 75/103 POP-Q≤1 and 96/103≤2 are examination outcomes at one year, not 95% of all 152. Decision satisfaction is 125/132; outcome satisfaction is 124/132 in the main. Revised page explicitly says decision satisfaction.
- Corrected sling material: the Grzybowska abstract's upper **14% revision** comes from the full table/narrative's **3/21 autologous fascial slings (FitzGerald 2003)**, not synthetic MUS. Do not repeat that endpoint under a synthetic-sling label. Original 2003 trial was not read; attribution comes from the fully read review.
- Corrected body-image claim to potential improvement, with exact prospective responders (80 better,49 same,2 worse). Added practical emptying assessment, catheter plan and follow-up access without inventing a universal postoperative schedule.

## Source discrepancies kept internal

- Raina: abstract's no-hysterectomy N=5,775 is not the denominator of the quoted 9.5% in table 2 (438/4,631 known subtypes). Main/table numbers imply a different all-no-hysterectomy rate. Avoided raw 11.4% vs9.5% claim and reported verified adjusted OR1.927 [1.445–2.568]. Its discussion's 2.3 percentage-point statement and some comparator totals differ from table arithmetic. Do not infer additional causal adjustment or postdischarge risk.
- Grzybowska: clinical search ended June2020, not 2022/2026. Subjective improvement definitions/response levels vary, and several table percentages are internally inconsistent (for example Hullfish4/40 printed4.3%). Abstract's MUS14% issue described above. Tables summarize older mixed materials and cannot authorize modern device-specific rates.
- Hoehn: abstract9.2% reoperation conflicts with9/88 in main; baseline period2014 vs2013, several subgroup counts/p-values and missing median follow-up in table3. Only51/88 at last visit; small survival risk sets. No inference of technique/suture equivalence or five-year complete follow-up. The modeled technique graphic is not an anatomic clinical validation.
- Yildiz: anatomical POP-Q≤2 success94.8% in abstract/table3 versus89.6% in main/discussion; all58single-center retrospective. EBL~785mL is calculated from hematologic values, not simply observed intraoperative loss. Text cites the TXA RCT for an unrelated4,776-patient database outcome statement. Table Hb unit labels, additional-procedure wording, and references require caution. Removed its numerical headline and claim of universal superiority.
- PFDN2008: appendix satisfaction distinguishes decision and operation-outcome items; the abstract blurs them. Table4's anatomic inequality typography is awkward, but narrative unambiguously identifies POP-Q stage≤1/≤2. Concomitant incontinence treatments included55syntheticMUS,2xenograft,13plication,1collagen and were not randomized; no causal prophylactic sling conclusion.
