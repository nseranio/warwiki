## First-pass items now settled

- **Full discussion/table access:** Statements 9–14 and 19–23, Tables 2–5 now available; the collapsed-panel limit is superseded for these details.
- **PE doses/timing:** Statement 9 discussion/Table 2 gives daily paroxetine 10–40 mg, sertraline 50–200 mg, fluoxetine/citalopram 20–40 mg, clomipramine 12.5–50 mg. Table 2 gives on-demand paroxetine 20 mg (with or without 10 mg daily), sertraline 50–100 mg and clomipramine 25–50 mg; discussion specifies 3–6 h before intercourse. Daily treatment starts delaying ejaculation in 5–10 days, with full effect in 2–3 weeks. Dapoxetine 30/60 mg 1–2 h before intercourse is in the discussion, not Table 2. These reported regimens do not expand Statement 9's positive first-line list.
- **Second-line PE:** Statement 10 allows considering on-demand tramadol after first-line failure (Conditional/C), Table 2 25–100 mg; Statement 11 allows considering α1-antagonists (Expert Opinion). Reported Table 2 daily PE doses: terazosin 5 mg, alfuzosin 6–10 mg, silodosin 4 mg, tamsulosin 0.4 mg, doxazosin 4 mg. These are not BPH starting-dose instructions.
- **SNRI details:** duloxetine 40 mg/day modest benefit and no recommendation for PE; venlafaxine 75 mg/day no significant benefit over placebo (Statement 14 discussion).
- **DE framework:** substitute/adjust/stage withdrawal of contributing drugs with their prescriber (Statement 22, Clinical Principle); oral pharmacotherapy risk-benefit evidence insufficient (Statement 23, Expert Opinion). Tables describe possible efficacy, not recommended standard therapy or proven benefit.
- **Accessible DE dose facts, retained internally where no matching site claim exists:** Table 4 lists cyproheptadine 4–12 mg 3–4 h before sex, bethanechol 20 mg 1–2 h before, amantadine 100–400 mg for two days before or 75–100 mg twice/three times daily, bupropion 75 mg twice/three times daily, buspirone 5–15 mg twice daily. Table 5 additionally lists oxytocin 24 IU intranasal/sublingual during activity, yohimbine 5.4 mg three times daily and cabergoline 0.25–2 mg twice weekly. No broad DE section or new page created; underlying drug efficacy/safety is not settled by these dose lists.
- **Testing:** positive post-ejaculatory sperm finding in the dry-climax context supported by Statement 19 discussion; a negative result is not a universal diagnosis of emission failure.
