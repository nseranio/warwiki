Answer only what the named document itself says. For each numbered item, quote the recommendation or figure verbatim with its strength rating and level of evidence. If the document does not say it, or you cannot access the document itself, reply "not stated" or "cannot verify". Give the full citation (title, year, section number, DOI or URL) for every quote.

In the EAU Guidelines on Upper Urinary Tract Urothelial Carcinoma (2026):
