# Assessment tools: source and correction record

September 12, 2026. The complete original 399-line MDX and complete revised MDX, including references, were actually read. This is a scoped correction of the page, not a claim that every underlying paper was accessible in full or that the clinical corpus is cleared.

## Corrections

- Corrected all MSIGS grades and the original standing-cough protocol. Removed automatic procedure selection and universal pad-weight thresholds; preserved symptoms, test conditions and observational limitations.
- Separated FSDS (12 items), FSDS-R (13) and FSDS-DAO; corrected FSFI-6 to include pain. Restricted FSFI-9 to the studied 898 peri/postmenopausal women with hot flashes and its continuous-outcome purpose. A screening threshold does not diagnose dysfunction or replace distress assessment.
- Corrected DSDS sensitivity, PROMIS's missing erectile-function domain and the PISQ-IR active/inactive pathways. Removed incorrectly interchangeable drug-trial endpoints and unsupported psychometric generalizations.
- Replaced duplicated incorrect POP-Q staging with the canonical examination section. Corrected PFDI/PFIQ item counts, domains and source. Distinguished IIQ-7 from UIQ-7.
- Corrected optional AUA OAB questionnaire/diary use, IPSS versus BOO, ICIQ-UI SF scoring, LUTSqol population, male stricture PROM scope and the distinction between LSE and USS. Removed universal use of BPH-surgery satisfaction validation in unrelated reconstruction.
- Practical selection now avoids duplicating UDI-6 inside PFDI-20 and preserves exact versions/recall periods.
