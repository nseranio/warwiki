## Independent source checks

- [Madhu 2018 ICS POP-Q module](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/nau.23740): measurement definitions, examination conditions, and relevant staging passages. Ba/Bp correctly refer to the upper vaginal walls; GH/PB are recorded on strain and TVL with the prolapse reduced at rest. The article’s simplified stage-0 statement was not substituted for the original Bump C-or-D criterion.
- [Guralnick 2018 ICS-Uniform Cough Stress Test](https://www.ics.org/Wasabi/Documents/DocumentsDownload.aspx?DocumentID=5291): full relevant position, volume, number-of-coughs, interpretation and accessory-testing sections from the cached primary manuscript. The page correctly uses supine/lithotomy, 200–400 mL appropriate to the patient, up to four forceful coughs, synchronous observed urethral leakage, and separately documented accessory testing.
- [Laycock and Jerwood 2001 PERFECT](https://pazonamd.com/Laycock2001PERFECTscheme.pdf): original Oxford grading and P/E/R/F/ECT methods. Repetitions use the patient’s measured hold duration with four-second rests; “Every Contraction Timed” is the original acronym. The page avoids inventing a universal normal threshold or treating the exercise proposal as a validated universal prescription.
- [Barbier 2023 IUC clinical-evaluation chapter](https://www.iuga.org/images/2025/03/Articles_IUC_Chapter_2.pdf): applicable prolapse-examination conclusions and occult-SUI definition/predictive-value sections. The reported 40% positive and 91% negative predictive values are medians across heterogeneous studies, not patient-specific predictions. Empty-bladder examination, repeat upright assessment when needed, and caution about cervical traction are supported.
- [Frawley 2021 ICS muscle-assessment report](https://doi.org/10.1002/nau.24658): relevant sacral-reflex definitions and interpretation. Sacral reflex testing is distinguished from voluntary contraction and sensory examination.
- [NICE NG123](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng123/resources/urinary-incontinence-and-pelvic-organ-prolapse-in-women-management-pdf-66141657205189): current indexed primary recommendations 1.3.15–1.3.17 and relevant prolapse assessment recommendations. The page correctly avoids routine Q-tip testing and automatic urodynamics after a positive stress test.
