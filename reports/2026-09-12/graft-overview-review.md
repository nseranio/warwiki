## Substantive corrections

- Removed the recommendation to use ORISE gel: the FDA recall record specifies all batches and December 2022 product removal for foreign-body reactions and mass-like changes. The old technical paper's use of the agent is historical, not a current recommendation.
- Corrected two unrelated PubMed links. The Zhao ureteroplasty paper is PMID **26123519**, DOI **10.1016/j.urology.2015.06.006**; the old link 26199153 is an unrelated Peyronie's editorial. The Converse inosculation paper is **1975**, PMID **1104028**, DOI **10.1016/0007-1226(75)90031-4**, with Smahel and Harper among the authors. The old title/year/journal/authors were conflated, and PMID5786095 concerns drugs and visual perception. The separate 1969 imbibition paper is PMID **4889411**.
- Restricted Converse's 48-hour observation to the rat experiment; removed rigid graft-survival/immobilization rules and the claim that Zhao's four-patient series proves obligatory omental support.
- Aligned oral-lip versus genital labial tissue, free graft versus vascularized flap/bowel, Duckett versus TIP, FTSG/STSG contraction, and penile skin availability. Removed false universal donor-size, donor-safety and preferred-graft hierarchies.
- Corrected Palmer's median follow-up to 18 months (10 months was recurrence timing), Howard's six urethral plus one vaginal reconstruction, and the single-patient nature of the 21 × 3 cm graft report. Retained separate rectal-graft series with explicit nonpooling and follow-up limits.
- Corrected sigmoid versus McIndoe terminology and ileocecal continence versus antireflux mechanism; aligned current urethral, MRKH and ED restrictions with the corresponding detailed pages/guidance.

## Remaining limits

The rectal series may overlap, but the publications do not establish a precise deduplicated count. Granieri includes 12 minimally invasive harvests and one open harvest during a radiation-associated rectourethral-fistula repair, with varied graft configurations and two combined buccal grafts. Its lack of reported bowel harms does not establish absence of donor morbidity. The public overview therefore describes a heterogeneous reconstruction cohort and does not pool the reports or claim comparative safety. Full manuscripts, videos and any external supplements remain pending where not explicitly recorded above.
