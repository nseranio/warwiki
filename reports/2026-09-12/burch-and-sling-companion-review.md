## Clinical and diagram corrections

Burch now distinguishes primary/index selection from fixed-urethra and salvage cases; omits mandatory urodynamics for every uncomplicated primary patient; and separates defined trial outcomes from broad cure claims. CARE's corrected result is scoped to abdominal sacrocolpopexy and its composite endpoint. Concomitant surgery is individualized. Long-term observational results retain their follow-up, denominator and endpoint limitations. No universal safety ranking, fixed recovery period, mesh/staple-to-suture extrapolation or technique-video superiority claim remains in the rewrite.

The Burch diagram was withdrawn along with the PVS diagram. The bladder/urethral connection, vaginal anchor geometry and direct-lifting headline were misleading; two independent visual checks support withdrawal. Prior assets and generators remain recoverable from Git. See [Burch withdrawal](burch-figure-withdrawal.json) and the independent diagram report in the source-reading directory.

The completed reviews exposed additional internal source errors: duplicate Ward urgency rows, revision procedures substituted for broader voiding dysfunction, rectocele used under a general prolapse label, SF-36 subscales double-counting participants, arm-size reversals, inconsistent pooling claims and a one-versus-two-suture table reversal. The site uses consistent original-trial or well-scoped guideline claims; no silently recalculated replacement meta-analysis was invented.
