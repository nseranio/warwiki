### Wound adjuncts

Corrected matrix composition, staged-product assumptions, Epicel's burn threshold and RECELL's autograft requirement. Removed a JAMA clinical review's misidentification as an11-trial amnion meta-analysis and universal skin-substitute healing claims. Replaced the HBOT promotional summary with a cross-link to the corrected hub. Seven no-longer-used legacy references were removed; the complete original bibliography remains recoverable in git and the access inventory.

## Source inconsistencies not imported

- RICH-ART2025 introduction discusses cost/outcome findings from a separate observational CMS study. Those are not randomized long-term trial results.
- HOT2 really used2.4ATA. Severe bleeding/incontinence were underrepresented, not uniformly excluded by a formal grade3criterion. Its table and abstract round bowel-score changes differently.
- SWHSI-2's hazard ratio is not literally an8% reduction in elapsed healing time; serious-event counts and patient counts are not interchangeable.
- Toppen has conflicting percentages/weighted versus unweighted presentation in selected tables. Use the consistent main adjusted association with its limitations, not every isolated rate/subgroup entry.
- Custis Table2 labels scar width in centimeters while narrative/abstract use millimeters. The page uses the qualitative no-difference result rather than perpetuating that unit conflict.
- Cochrane NPWT has extraction/denominator problems in exploratory subanalyses; the independent report explains why the page keeps the overall moderate-certainty SSI/dehiscence findings without adopting every pooled subgroup.
- The HPRA document contains questionable normobaric/physiological wording outside the site's selected claims (including insulin-absorption and oxygen-handling explanations). It was not copied into a general oxygen protocol.
