## Changes that affect interpretation

- **Tissue engineering:** added the five-child human tubular-scaffold experience and its two later interventions. Restored trial-only EAU advice for cultured oral mucosa and adverse-bed/length restrictions for cell-free grafts. Corrected Fiala overall versus site-specific patency, Palminteri's under/over4cm comparison and the later below3cm selection suggestion. Living grafts, acellular matrices and cell-seeded tubes are treated separately.
- **MukoCell:** corrected national German §4b(3) authorization versus an asserted EU CE mark. The comparative cohort does not establish noninferiority. Graft choice, anatomy and techniques differed; oral morbidity measurement was limited and cases overlap an earlier report. The prospective multicenter series had major follow-up attrition and a post-hoc recurrence definition.
- **Bladder mucosa:** corrected VVF treatment-era versus follow-up duration, predominantly benign postoperative cohorts, differing graft operations and closure/continence. Restored the considerable pediatric reoperation burden, animal-model limits and four animal deaths in scaffold augmentation. Removed a radiation-specific success guarantee and an unsupported routine cancer-surveillance rule inferred from one case.
- **Postauricular skin:** removed guaranteed hairlessness, absence of donor morbidity and equivalence to oral mucosa. The skin-graft and perichondral donor studies used different operations. Stricture length does not set donor-graft length, and no universal10cm limit or formal third-line rank was supported.
- **Intestinal segments:** clarified vascularized bowel versus free graft, residual motility after detubularization, ileocecal outlet continence versus ureteric antireflux and appendix-only harvest. Added guideline-based B12 and bacteriuria follow-up. Corrected Khoury DOI to `10.1016/S0022-5347(01)64404-5` and Rowland to `10.1016/S0022-5347(17)44428-4`.
- **Saphenous vein:** corrected Austoni's combined prosthesis intervention, mean13-month follow-up and 76/80 respondent satisfaction; Yurkanin's aided/unaided erections; and the small graft comparison's lack of equivalence testing. Added contemporary surgical-selection principles. Corrected transplant experience denominators, harvest versus extension length, SVC versus IVC and center-specific bovine use. Cardiac harvest risks no longer appear as expected GU percentages; the pig ureter experiment used jugular vein.

## Source inconsistencies retained in the audit

The Karapanos abstract gives repeated-dilation success as31.3% versus81.3%, p=.003. Main Tables1/3 and Discussion give **5/16 versus10/12 (31.3% versus83.3%), p=.006**. Both the foundations page and the operative minced-buccal companion now use the table-based values. The study's p=.155 proportion comparison differs from its Kaplan–Meier log-rank p=.203; these tests are not interchangeable.

Ram-Liebig's complete-case percentages (46/65 and30/39) differ from the time-to-event estimates67.3%/58.2%. The page uses the latter with attrition explained. Some internal abstract/table counts and the at-risk display are inconsistent; no unsupported reconciled patient count or causal experience/catheter-duration rule was invented.

de Kemp's abstract/flowchart study totals and clinical summary counts do not agree. Its matrix subgroup numbers also differ from the original El-Kassaby abstract. The revised page does not copy the review's pooled counts or use the outdated review to describe current availability. EAU tissue-transfer text itself contains an impossible20–110% range and an outdated assertion about absence of native-graft comparisons; neither was reproduced.
