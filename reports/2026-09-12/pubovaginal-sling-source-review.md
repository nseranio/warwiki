## Corrections applied

- Separate traditional bladder-neck PVS from modified midurethral fascial operations. Asfour's 6 cm describes the skin incision; the graft is approximately 1 × 8 cm. Graft dimensions, suture carriage and tensioning are technique-specific. The small 2025 adjustment RCT does not establish a universally safe gap or prove equivalence.
- Align AUA selection language: fixed/immobile urethra is not synonymous with an isolated ISD pressure threshold; concurrent urethral repair differs from a remote healed surgical history. Intentionally obstructing salvage PVS requires a separate emptying plan. Bladder-neck closure details are cross-linked to the dedicated hubs.
- Separate SUI-specific success, overall continence, cumulative survival estimates, observed dryness, satisfaction and reoperation. Preserve SISTEr's hypermobility population and Burch comparator; E-SISTEr's preferential enrollment and changed outcome; Khan's long-term respondent selection; and Nair's 107 respondents from 165 originally randomized patients.
- Use the completed Cochrane traditional-sling review with its incorporated February 2017 search cutoff and mixed historical materials. Do not treat nonsignificant pooled estimates as equivalence or convert historical material-specific catheter/urgency findings into contemporary autologous instructions.
- Hong's registry has 41,533 total operations, but only 160 rectus-fascia and 81 fascia-lata cases. Its 30-day associations and missing prior-surgery/retention information do not establish a superior donor site. Offiah's observational survey includes only 34/90 invited fascia patients and does not justify a universal procedure preference.
- Correct the Milose salvage citation, previously misassigned to Buckley's donor-site cohort. Preserve the differences between stress-leakage cure and complete dryness in salvage populations. Remove unsupported blanket complication-free, donor-equivalence and predictive-threshold claims.
- Withdraw the misleading PVS schematic: its hammock is drawn at mid/distal urethral level despite a bladder-neck label, and an arm overlaps the pubis silhouette. Delete its public asset, generator and registry entry; the prior Git version and provenance remain recoverable in the [withdrawal record](pubovaginal-sling-figure-withdrawal.json). No replacement anatomy or clinical sign-off is invented.
- Correct the shared enlarged-figure dialog to an opaque theme-aware surface. This addresses visible article text bleeding through the toolbar and description in both desktop and mobile views.

The peer's E-SISTEr finding was resolved: cumulative wording now applies only to continence; satisfaction is separately reported as 148/179 versus 126/172 respondents. Public remarks about rejected wording were replaced with direct operative guidance.

## Source discrepancies retained internally

The complete Cochrane read exposed material, outcome, denominator and unit problems that are not reproduced as clinical estimates: Shin arm reversal, Okulu pad-count values under pad-weight labels, conflicting hospital-stay units, narrative/table sample-size disagreements, and non-estimable placeholders. Historical dura-mater and cadaveric-fascia trials dominate some catheter/urodynamic estimates; long-term scar pain is mixed with short-term composite wound outcomes. Original trials require separate reading before reuse of disputed values.

Grigoryan's actual plots identify operative time/stay as **standardized mean differences**, despite the abstract's RR label. Apparent reversed favor labels, a sample-size inconsistency and possible related-cohort dependence remain unresolved without the full main. Hong and Offiah have additional table/prose inconsistencies; the reports preserve them without silently manufacturing corrected analyses.
