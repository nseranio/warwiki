# Wound-healing adjuncts: independent peer review

Reviewed September 12, 2026. Complete revised `docs/01-foundations/surgical-principles/wound-healing-adjuncts.mdx` read, including all tables, admonition and 30 references. Read-only review for introduced clinical inference, safety and source-identity problems; no source-page edits and no clinical clearance.

**Verdict: no blocking introduced clinical error found within the independently checked scope.** The draft distinguishes primary-incision NPWT from open-wound healing and graft bolsters, limits donor-site and diabetic-foot findings to their populations, and correctly separates drug/device labels from broader reconstructive use. Its hydrogel wording does not reproduce the numerical/direction errors found in the source review.

## Independent checks and exact access

- **NPWT primary closure:** complete Cochrane 2022 XML main/back matter, all 178 tables, six appendices and 387 reference entries plus all 16 graphics viewed from the readable manuscript PDF. See [complete source report](npwt-cochrane-source-review.md). SSI RR 0.73 (0.63–0.85), dehiscence RR 0.97 (0.82–1.16) and possible increased blistering are correctly framed. This evidence does not establish graft take or Fournier/open-wound healing.
- **Hydrogel and Brölmann:** see [independent source report](hydrogel-cochrane-source-review.md). Hydrogel complete HTML text, seven tables, four appendices and 58 references read; six review graphics remain unreadable. Both included original trials were read completely with their tables, references and all five figures. Brölmann is complete-abstract access only. The brief site conclusion stays within those boundaries.
- **SWHSI-2 2025:** independently read the complete main HTML article, all three tables, all 28 references and viewed both figures. Cache `/tmp/tools-swhsi-read.txt` (60,360 characters), source `/tmp/wound8-swhsi.html`, graphics `/tmp/wound8-swhsi-gr1.jpg` and `-gr2.jpg`. [Primary article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12062190/), DOI 10.1016/S0140-6736(25)00143-6. The 686 randomized participants, 349/337 allocation, predominantly lower-limb/vascular setting, median healing 187/195 days and HR 1.08 (0.88–1.32) are accurate. Systemic infection was excluded; local wound infection was not universally excluded. Exudate-management effectiveness was not assessed. Main-text economics supports the cautious lack-of-established-cost-effectiveness wording. **The separate 70-page supplementary appendix/protocol/economic analyses were not read in this peer review.**
- **SWHSI-2 source nuance:** its prose describes HR 1.08 as an 8% reduction in healing time, which is not a general interpretation of a healing hazard ratio. WARWIKI correctly reports the HR and medians without copying that claim. Main text/table also differ over whether five control serious adverse events occurred in four or five participants; the site does not reproduce this discrepancy. These issues are not new errors in the draft.
- **IWGDF 2023:** independently read introductory text and the complete introductory list of recommendations 1–29 (including 2a/28a), not the full guideline, underlying review, evidence tables or all rationales. The nonroutine cellular/acellular substitutes and platelet/growth-factor statements, conditional placental product/defined leukocyte-platelet-fibrin patch options, and distinction between postsurgical and nonsurgical foot NPWT match those recommendations. Source `/tmp/wound8-iwgdf.pdf/.txt`; [official guideline](https://iwgdfguidelines.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/IWGDF-2023-07-Wound-Healing-Guideline.pdf).
- **Regranex May 2026:** directly read the current DailyMed highlights and full prescribing-information sections 1–5 (with later sections returned incidentally, not counted as a complete-label read). Label supports sufficiently perfused diabetic neuropathic ulcers extending to subcutaneous tissue, usual wound care, no intended primary-closure use, application-site neoplasm contraindication, individual risk assessment with other known malignancy and no intravaginal use. [Current label](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=377b3021-13d7-96d5-e063-6394a90a8ca3). Medication Guide, full IFU and all clinical-trial tables were not independently reread here.
- **Epicel and RECELL:** directly read both complete FDA product-indication pages, not their linked IFUs/SSEDs. Epicel includes deep dermal/full-thickness burns ≥30% TBSA. RECELL has age/indication restrictions and full-thickness skin defects require meshed autografting. [Epicel FDA](https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/approved-blood-products/epicel-cultured-epidermal-autografts); [RECELL FDA](https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/approved-blood-products/recell-autologous-cell-harvesting-device).

Local `/tmp/wound8-apligraf-p1.png`, `-p2.png`; [insert](https://www.apligraf.com/pdf/apligraf-pi-300-111-12.pdf).
