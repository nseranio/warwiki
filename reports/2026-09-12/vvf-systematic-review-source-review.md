September 12, 2026. Thompson JC et al. *Obstet Gynecol.* 2024;143:229–241; DOI 10.1097/AOG.0000000000005468; [author manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11975400/).

Root read the entire main text, one table, 62 references and all four actual graphics: selection flowchart, route comparison, flap comparison and trimming comparison. The supplement URL returned a download-challenge page, not the 233.7 KB PDF; no appendix is claimed read.

The search ends September 9, 2022. Forty-six included reports cover 11 perioperative domains. Vaginal flap comparisons did not demonstrate an overall closure benefit, with substantial design/population limitations. This is not proof that interposition is unnecessary in every complex or irradiated repair.

Source inconsistencies must not be copied: the catheter total in Table 1 is 345 despite the narrative's 189 and 524; flap totals distinguish Martius-only studies (717) from all six vaginal-flap studies (924). Figure 3 labels Eilber as India and pools its 120 mixed flaps, not 34 Martius cases. Some design labels conflict between table and narrative. Singh's route selection was not randomized. These findings reinforce checking the original trials and denominators before using specific estimates.
