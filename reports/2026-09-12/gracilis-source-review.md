# Gracilis source review — September 12, 2026

The entire original and revised [gracilis article](../../docs/01-foundations/surgical-principles/flaps/gracilis.mdx) was read. The dedicated staged-urethroplasty companion was reread and already had the correct interval. The [access register](gracilis-reference-access.json) preserves all 62 original and 40 revised bibliography entries, including removed sources. Six source mains and 22 actual figures were read/inspected this pass; this does not mean every cited paper, supplement or operative video was read.

## Changes that affect interpretation or technique

- Separated pedicled coverage from distant free functional transfer. Protected the dominant proximal blood supply, made nerve handling procedure-specific, corrected the tibial attachment and qualified arterial origins and anatomical averages. Removed generic detach-both-ends instructions and universal postoperative drain rules.
- Separated initial fistula healing from final healing after additional procedures, and closure from continence or reversal of diversion. Corrected the populations and denominators in Wexner, Vanni, Sbizzera, Jenkins and Whetzel; retained their observational limitations.
- Corrected the original prelaminated reconstruction to eight weeks between stages and the five-case follow-up series to its reported mean interval. Identified overlapping Palmer/Rozanski cohorts rather than adding them; a 20-year treatment experience is not 20-year patient follow-up.
- Removed universal flap-survival and donor-risk guarantees, automatic irrigation-salvage advice, unverified guideline endorsements and misleading comparisons across unrelated operations. Historical urinary/fecal graciloplasty is separated from static interposition.
- Incorporated the 2026 persistent-perineal-wound review and post-prostatectomy fistula review at their actual evidence level. Small new comparative cohorts were screened, not promoted as practice-changing evidence.

## Remaining original-source work

The register separately identifies abstract-only originals, including anatomical, harvest and outcome series. Neither metadata matching nor reading their abstracts counts as full-manuscript verification. The 2026 de Angelis review's full main and figures remain behind publisher access; only its complete abstract and bibliography entries 1–26 were read. Sterling 2022, the new French comparison, the other recent RUF review and most historical sources still need their original full texts.

The inherited Zinman DOI resolved to a different Barbagli review; the correct Zinman identity and abstract were located and the mismatched entry removed. The inherited NCCN version/recommendation was not verified and was removed. The Cochrane VVF source is a protocol previously read in full, not a new completed comparison. The ASCRS guideline attempt did not yield a full guideline. The external atlas and playlist remain unreviewed as complete resources.

Garoufalia's unread attachments are Figure S1 (TIFF) and Tables S1–S3 (DOCX); Vila's unread attachment contains search strategies and risk-of-bias assessment. No claim of complete clinical clearance is made. Research downloads remain outside the repository and deployment.
