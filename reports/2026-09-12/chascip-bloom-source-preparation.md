## Direct technique reports

- **CHASCIP, Ciudad 2025**, DOI 10.1002/micr.70075, PMID 40448967: complete indexed primary abstract read, publisher metadata/reference material screened. Wiley full-text/PDF access failed. The report confirms eight men, stage III penoscrotal disease, bilateral pedicled lymphatic SCIP transfer, two complications, no observed recurrence and mean 34-month follow-up. Full operative Methods, graft donor/thickness, node inclusion, figures and any supplementary content remain unverified. The draft therefore removes the hypogastric FTSG recipe, fixed excision planes, obligatory node harvest, doubled drainage capacity, cure claims and unmatched comparative rankings. It does not infer that an unverified technical detail is necessarily false.
- **BLOOM, Sim 2025**, DOI 10.1007/s11695-025-07847-z, PMID 40198493: complete primary abstract, publisher preview, references, disclosures and supplementary-video description read. The public Springer page explicitly identifies **left gastroepiploic** lymph nodes in the video description. Springer-provided first-page preview on ResearchGate was read; it ends during Methods and is not the full paper. Full Methods/results, two actual clinical photographs and the approximately 188 MB supplementary video remain unread. Correct right-pedicle/station-4d attribution; remove specimen assumptions, zero-morbidity guarantees, universal selection rules and first-ever combined-surgery claims. Single limb-case evidence cannot establish genital effectiveness or superiority over staged care.

All 34 original available MED citation records/abstracts across the two original pages were actually read in `/tmp/genital11-primary.json`. That is an abstract/identity screen, not full review of 34 papers. Most small donor series are omitted from the proposed public pages because they do not establish the claimed comparisons.

## VLNT review screened and rejected as donor-ranking evidence

**Shah 2026**, DOI 10.1016/j.bjps.2026.03.005, PMID 41950607, PMC13384956: full main narrative, all five tables including footnotes, 59 references, all five actual main figures and six supplementary funnel plots read. XML `/tmp/genital11-PMC13384956.xml`; actual images and all six supplements `/tmp/genital11-PMC13384956-media/`, obtained from the public Europe PMC supplementary-files endpoint. The extraction tables were reread separately in row format.

The review excludes combined techniques and does not test BLOOM. Its donor subgroup comparisons use heterogeneous uncontrolled reports. Additional internal defects discourage reuse of exact pooled rankings:

- Title/abstract and Results give inconsistent total patient counts; adverse-event percentages also differ between abstract and Results.
- Figure 5 headings label the low cellulitis values as preoperative and the high values as postoperative, the reverse of Tables 4–5 and the narrative.
- Figure 3B gives Chen 2020 as 2.60 and Cheng 2023 as 0.20, while Table 5 lists 12.26 and 20.25. These are not small rounding differences.
- Figure 4 gives Schaverien 2021 as n=14 and 12.70, whereas Tables 4–5 list n=19 and 45.7%; the table's Moon row contains 12.7 mL. This is a source inconsistency, not a verified correction to the original study.
- The six actual funnel plots do not resolve those extraction/label problems. Study-level records would be needed to reconstruct estimates; this audit does not invent corrected pooled results.

The revised drafts retain a concise account of the named procedures and general safety principles, with no new small-series outcome table or claim that a particular donor is best.
