# Penile/preputial graft and flap correction — September 12, 2026

Both complete original foundations MDX pages, their reference lists and the complete rewritten pages were read. All 41 unique original DOI identities and available primary indexed abstracts were checked. This is a substantive correction, not complete clinical certification. The [source access ledger](penile-tissue-reference-access.json) distinguishes full manuscripts, selected text, abstracts and inaccessible material. Research copies remain outside the repository/deployment.

## Main corrections

- Separated **free skin grafts** from **pedicled skin flaps** throughout. PeeBuSt and Alrefaey compare grafts; Dubey compares a skin flap with BMG. The latter's operating times, penile complications and dribbling cannot be assigned to a free graft. Mathur's flap predictors and GURS flap-preference survey likewise do not establish free-graft risk.
- Replaced unsupported equivalence claims with actual designs, analyzed denominators, follow-up and uncertainty. A nonsignificant superiority test does not demonstrate equivalence.
- Aligned EAU2026 tissue selection: favor a graft when equally suitable; a flap can supply a poorly vascularized bed; oral mucosa is the usual graft choice; avoid genital skin in LS; avoid a free graft made into a complete tube in one stage. Circumcision limits inner prepuce but is not an absolute exclusion of every distal-shaft transfer.
- Corrected anatomy without erasing historical terminology. Grossman's study used 12 cadavers; Kureel's six subjects had exstrophy/epispadias. The original McAninch paper uses Buck's fascia terminology; Carney/McAninch distinguish its supporting role from the dartos vascular pedicle. Kim's detailed operative description was checked. No deliberate division of the deep dorsal arterial supply is implied.
- Corrected graft preparation: preserve dermis and remove excess tissue; do not prescribe aggressive thinning to a translucent membrane. Hair-free donor selection and recipient perfusion matter.
- Corrected McAninch cohort attribution: 1998 had 66 patients, 7/54 onlay versus7/12 tube recurrences, mean41 months; eventual95% success included reintervention. The original1993 cohort had ten patients and shorter follow-up. These cannot be merged.
- Corrected DOIG to69/80, Barbagli1998 to a mixed31-skin/six-buccal cohort, Karapanos to12 selected patients with fistula/dribbling events, and Cambareri to62 patients with overlapping complications. Removed unsupported operating-time, maximum-length, zero-complication and universal superiority claims.
- Distinguished staged pediatric evidence from adult stricture outcomes. Manasherova used successive historical cohorts. Wang2023 excluded first-stage complications from its overall rate and had shorter follow-up for staged repair. Small or retrospective studies do not establish a validated4.55-cm treatment threshold or universal dartos coverage rule.
- Retained limited contemporary technique information with appropriate scope. Zhao2026 is a54-person retrospective short-follow-up report, not practice-changing comparative evidence. Burn coverage, vaginal lining and sensate clitoral reconstruction have different requirements; adult urethroplasty percentages cannot be exported to those applications.

## Full manuscripts and pivotal supplement

### PeeBuSt — Tyagi2022

[Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00345-021-03843-x), PMC8520101. Read the entire main manuscript, both tables/footnotes, all30 reference entries, funding/disclosures, both figure images and **the entire DOCX supplement** (statistical plan, operative methods and TablesS1–S4). The supplement was retrieved from Europe PMC after direct publisher/PMC download paths failed.

The trial randomized100; its flow diagram confirms47 penile/inner-preputial graft and48 buccal recipients analyzed. At18 months success was42/47 versus44/48. The supplement assumed83% versus59% success to power detection of a **24-percentage-point difference**. It was not an equivalence/noninferiority design. Follow-up anatomy was assessed conditionally after flow testing; any reintervention counted as failure. The30-month curve retained only11 BMG and10 skin recipients at risk.

Internal issues were not copied: an intention-to-treat sentence reverses arm labels relative to the main outcome table; TableS4's88 successes/seven failures differs from the86/nine primary count. Its predictor estimates were not adopted. The supplement's positive OR confidence interval was read directly rather than treating typographic dashes in the main text as negative ratio limits.

### Alrefaey2025

[Primary article](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-14191-w), PMC12343868. Read the whole main manuscript, both tables/footnotes,22 references, disclosures and both figure images. No separate supplementary file was listed in the PMC package.

The flow diagram resolves the denominators:98 randomized,49 per arm; five skin and one buccal recipient lost to follow-up, leaving44/48. Success was41/44 versus47/48 at12 months; median follow-up20 months. The methods specify a superiority design; registration occurred after enrollment began. Stricture site and etiology differed materially at baseline despite broad textual claims of comparability. These limitations and the small sample prevent a graft-equivalence conclusion. No inconsistent functional-score P values were imported.
