# Skin anatomy and skin graft review — September 12, 2026

Complete original and revised text, tables and references were read for the skin donor-anatomy, split-thickness skin graft (STSG), and full-thickness skin graft (FTSG) pages. This is a substantive correction pass with scoped primary-source checks. It is **not** verification that every cited manuscript and supplement has been read or that all clinical claims have received final sign-off.

## Changes

- **Skin anatomy:** separated epidermis/dermis from hypodermis; restricted stratum lucidum to thick glabrous skin; corrected vascular-layer descriptions and the distinction between relaxed skin tension lines and Langer's lines. Restricted cadaveric thickness/perfusion measurements, pig flap experiments and finite-element predictions to their actual settings. Removed universal donor-ranking, flap-thinning and clinical stretch-threshold implications.
- **Both graft pages:** corrected greater immediate recoil in FTSG versus greater later contraction in STSG; removed a purported harvest-depth guarantee against hair. Distinguished epidermis-containing FTSG from de-epithelialized dermal patches. Replaced universal graft-choice conclusions with study-specific findings and limitations.
- **GU reconstruction:** applied the 2026 EAU tissue-transfer restrictions on hair-bearing urethral grafts, genital skin in LS-related stricture and single-stage tubularized free grafts. Prioritized oral mucosa when suitable. Replaced the unverified NCCN claim with the current EAU penile-cancer reconstruction guidance actually consulted.
- **Outcome denominators:** corrected the mixed-indication Alwaal 52/54 result; Plamadeala's 91 graft recipients within 204 reconstructions and 59/68 satisfaction respondents; Jeng's three graft-loss events versus four surgical failures; Parnham's 16/172 evaluable recurrences; Wu's pediatric FTSG-versus-fixation comparator; and selected subgroup results in urethral reconstruction. Clarified that absence of a significant difference in small retrospective studies does not establish equivalence.
- **Gynecologic reconstruction:** restored supported dilation as first-line for most treatment-ready patients with MRKH. Corrected the direction and uncertainty of revision-vaginoplasty morbidity, the partial use of additional grafts within larger cohorts, and the requirement for postoperative dilation.
- **Harvest and dressings:** removed an unsupported dermatome-calibration rule, incorrect manual-harvest preference, universal drug/lubricant recipes, and purported cardiovascular safety from six patients. Added the 2025 NPWT RCT meta-analysis with heterogeneity and protocol limitations. Distinguished recipient graft-area take from patient success and hydrogel from hydrocolloid. Corrected the donor-dressing account using the complete 2023 Cochrane review.
- **Reference hygiene:** removed duplicate Greenhalgh bibliography entries and a duplicated inline citation; renumbered the affected numeric anchors consistently. Added explicit EAU, ACOG, Borges and NPWT review citations. Corrected Gül's 2024 online/2026 issue dates and the NPWT review's final pagination.
