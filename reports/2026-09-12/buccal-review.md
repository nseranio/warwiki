## Substantive corrections

- Removed “scarless healing,” infection resistance from saliva/flora and reliable graft-take claims presented as clinical guarantees. Mechanistic oral biology does not certify survival of a transferred graft.
- Removed fixed5-mm safety margins, mandatory bilateral harvest above7 cm and excessively thin graft instructions. Preserved duct identification, donor assessment, dermis/lamina-propria integrity, recipient contact, hemostasis and individualized anesthesia. The existing conceptual image remains labeled as an illustration, without operative-plane clearance.
- Separated Soave's randomized noninferiority finding from Wood's small prospective pain-score comparison. Replaced “most experienced surgeons leave open” with an individualized decision supported by AUA/EAU and systematic reviews.
- Corrected the GURS survey to134/350 respondents and95% circumcised/84% uncircumcised flap comparison. Survey preference and perceived success are not measured comparative outcomes.
- Corrected Doležel's38-person posterior series:65%3-year recurrence-free survival,81% only allowing auxiliary DVIU. New incontinence was2/18 initially continent patients. The broad65–81% range had obscured reintervention.
- Limited Sterling2024 to selected nonobliterative postprostatectomy/postradiation cases. No observed new SUI does not prove an advantage over transection. Its functional data cannot establish erectile safety in generally potent men.
- Scoped distal inlay and Bracka outcomes to the actual cohorts; removed pediatric uptake guarantees and promotion of isolated novel techniques as broadly established applications. Original references remain in the access ledger even when removed from the article.
- Rebuilt donor morbidity around study-specific populations, response rates and methods. Wood had49/110 questionnaire respondents and57 harvests; Barbagli's bilateral predictor came from a particular553-person closed-cheek cohort; Castagnetti involved mixed oral donors/ages; Desai was40 patients. Persistent symptoms are not uniformly rare or never bothersome.
- Removed the universal LS50%-versus80% staging rule derived from eight versus five patients. Aligned with current EAU selection for a suitable single-stage oral graft versus staging when tissues require it.
- Removed the implication that greater blood loss improves graft success or that cardiometabolic risks are irrelevant. Meyer's selected517-person retrospective association is not a perfusion assay or treatment target.
- Added restrained2025 pooled recurrence evidence: associated factors do not establish causation or a7-cm exclusion rule. Removed unconditional “no negative sexual impact” and “BMG immune to LS” claims.
- Retained the operative video at the end immediately before References. Corrected the two new companion links using actual custom slugs.
