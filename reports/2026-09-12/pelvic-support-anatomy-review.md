## Corrections

- Corrected the pelvic ring to two sacroiliac joints and one symphysis; distinguished the obturator canal from the foramen, neural sacral foramina from venous channels, and left common iliac vein direction.
- Removed fixed bony distances as guarantees of safe fixation, trocar passage, nerve blocks, port placement or individual pelvic dimensions. Sacrospinous fixation intentionally engages the ligament.
- Corrected anterior longitudinal ligament thickness and sample size. The 176-model CT rectangle is a proposal, not an outcomes-validated safe zone. Nerve distances are medians with ranges, not universal boundaries.
- Distinguished the surgical plane from the whole presacral region and competing fascial nomenclatures. The rectal peritoneal reflection is not a fixed S2–S3 roof. Rectosacral release is procedure-dependent.
- Corrected blind clamping/tacking advice, explicitly protecting neural foramina; indigo carmine is not a hemostatic agent. Physiological deterioration requires escalation rather than prolonged local attempts.
- Distinguished anterior straddle injury from posterior PFUI, the limited predictive value of fracture displacement, and male versus female urethral evaluation.
- Removed universal tailgut-cyst transformation and mandatory-excision claims. Added a limited 2026 chordoma-consensus update; sacrectomy and urinary diversion are not inherent to every posterior pelvic exenteration.
