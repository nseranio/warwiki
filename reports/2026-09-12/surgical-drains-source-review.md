# Surgical drains — source review, September 12, 2026

## Scope and changes

Fully read the original and final Jackson-Pratt, Blake and Penrose MDX pages. The [access register](surgical-drains-reference-access.json) preserves all 78 inherited reference entries, including sources removed from the public pages, and maps all 19 current entries. A full MDX read, source metadata match and complete paper read are different records. No clinical certification is asserted.

- Made JP the shared decision page; Blake and Penrose retain device-specific selection, handling and care. Removed duplicated evidence tables and historical padding without discarding the inherited bibliography from the audit record.
- Corrected JP geometry: the family includes flat/round perforated and channel drains. Flat does not mean fluted, millimeter width is not French size, and reservoirs are not all 100 mL. Removed unsupported universal suction-pressure and 25%-full emptying rules.
- Removed claims that Blake is clog-proof, prevents tissue ingrowth or produces superior urologic outcomes. Manufacturer cardiothoracic comparisons against rigid chest tubes do not establish superiority over every JP configuration. Retained whole-assembly MRI/latex precautions from the reservoir instructions.
- Replaced blanket routine-drain and drain-omission rules with procedure-specific guidance. Trial populations with watertight prostatectomy anastomoses do not establish a policy for complex repairs, irradiation, diversion or established leaks.
- Corrected drain creatinine interpretation: cystectomy cutoffs are screening findings with incomplete sensitivity, low positive predictive value and selective imaging; a near-serum result does not exclude a localized leak. Removed the universal POD1 ratio of 1.2 and the implication that a normal result proves healing.
- Removed universal 30–50 mL/day removal and routine pre-removal imaging rules. WHO supports clinically appropriate removal and advises against extending prophylaxis solely for a drain, with limited-certainty evidence. MAMBO is procedure-specific nonrandomized evidence, not proof of an optimal cutoff for every operation.
- Corrected Penrose sampling and care: open drainage does not make laboratory testing categorically impossible; dressing contamination and established-drain colonization limit interpretation. Removed animal-to-human infection-rate extrapolations and the implication that simple hardware needs no patient education.

## Complete original mains

**Regmi 2021, DOI 10.4103/iju.IJU_396_20:** complete main, all three tables, 19 references/disclosures and both actual ROC figures. In 340 cystectomy patients, 16 leaks were identified. The ratio of 1.12 had 68.8% sensitivity and 80.9% specificity for all detected leaks; 1.18 describes a different analysis excluding neobladder–urethral leaks. Positive predictive values were low and imaging was selective in conduit patients. Testing was generally POD4–5, not a universal POD1 protocol. A localized collection could escape drain detection. Corrected Hannah's initials to PTF.

**Ma 2023, DOI 10.1093/bjsopen/zrad143:** complete main, 32 references/disclosures and all four actual figures. Six studies included 1,480 patients; only two were randomized, totaling 299 patients. A nonsignificant pooled comparison is not automatically a formal demonstration of noninferiority. Complete supplementary DOCX text, all 19 rendered pages, all ten actual supplementary figures and both tables were inspected. The separate three-page PRISMA checklist text was fully read. Minor main/supplement pooled-effect discrepancies were not exported. The Makita source reports events in its abstract, whereas the meta-analysis counts patients with events; the original Japanese paper remains unread, so this is an unresolved denominator question rather than a declared error.

**Lakha 2024, DOI 10.3389/ti.2024.13030:** complete main, 33 references/disclosures, all three actual figures and graphical abstract. Four retrospective transplant cohorts, 2,002 recipients and no RCTs. Wide confidence intervals, heterogeneity and missing outcome data limit inference; no equivalence or general benefit claim was adopted. Appendix search text was read; its separate external Ovid details were not accessed.

**Mamuyac 2019, DOI 10.1002/lary.27710:** complete main, 12 references and all four actual figures. Bench measurements on three 100-mL bulbs support a model-specific compression/filling effect. The results do not establish universal pressure targets, patient outcomes or a rule to empty every reservoir at 25% capacity. No separate supplement identified in the accessible article.

**Thomson 2014, DOI 10.1002/14651858.CD010933.pub2:** complete Cochrane main, plain-language summary, all three appendices, excluded-study table, references and disclosures. No eligible randomized trials of drainage after groin dissection were found. This is not evidence that closed drainage is superior. No actual figures or separate supplements listed in the accessible article. Reading the excluded-study table does not count as reading those original papers.

**Ghenassia 2026, DOI 10.1007/s00345-026-06272-w, U-LEAK:** complete nine-page article from the UroCCR repository, including all 27 references/disclosures, all three actual figures and the actual comparison tables. The comparative cohort contained 119 patients; treatment was not randomized and confounding by indication remains substantial. Several table counts/percentages and flow-diagram totals are internally inconsistent; no questionable numerical management estimates were copied. Failure to demonstrate faster resolution is not proof that intervention is unnecessary. No separate supplement identified in the PDF; publisher supplement inventory was not fully inspected.

**Pouwer 2017, DOI 10.1016/j.ygyno.2017.06.031, MAMBO:** complete eight-page published article and university cover, all three tables, 33 references/disclosures and all four actual figures. Sequential nonrandomized Dutch cohorts included 141 women and 251 groins, using high-vacuum Redon drains after vulvar-cancer inguinofemoral lymphadenectomy. Protocol deviations were substantial, particularly in the volume-controlled cohort. Reduced lymphocele occurrence cannot establish a universal GU output threshold or a JP-versus-Penrose effect. No separate supplement referenced in the PDF.

**Blachman-Braun 2025, DOI 10.1016/j.urolonc.2025.03.013:** complete author manuscript, three tables, 31 references/disclosures and actual main figure. The series included repeated operations in some patients and a broad leak definition; these procedure-level observations cannot be treated as independent randomized comparisons. Conservative management included drainage/catheter care, and prolonged device durations in this referral cohort are not a recommended schedule. All five linked supplements remain unread: PMC instance URLs returned HTML browser challenges, alternative article URLs returned 404, and Europe PMC returned a no-files XML response rather than an archive. These were not mistaken for successful image/DOCX retrievals.

**Kundu 2010, DOI 10.1111/j.1464-410X.2010.09230.x:** complete main, all three tables, six references and disclosures. No figures or separate supplements listed. This older mostly-open PN cohort used a leak-duration definition and retained drains as conservative treatment. Its abstract/results percentages and delayed-leak proportions contain inconsistencies; no numerical removal rule or universal observation policy was adopted. It remains in the inherited-source register rather than being added as new evidence.

## Guidelines, manufacturer documents and scope limits

- **EAU 2026:** complete online prostatectomy subsection 6.2.2.e, Pelvic drain; complete RCC subsection 7.2.3.e, Placement of a drain, and the related evidence-summary row. Watertight-anastomosis selection, the PLND evidence gap and absence of robotic-PN randomized evidence were checked. These are complete relevant subsections, not complete guideline reads or reads of every cited original.
- **WHO 2018:** complete section 4.26 recommendations, rationale and references. Complete 30-page Web Appendix 27 text, all actual study-selection, forest and funnel plots, and actual risk-of-bias/GRADE tables inspected. The prophylaxis recommendation is conditional with low-quality evidence; removal-timing evidence is very low quality and largely breast/orthopedic. Apparent discrepancies in individual-trial rows were not exported; the underlying trial originals were not all read.
- **IDSA/ASM 2024, DOI 10.1093/cid/ciae104:** complete Interventional Radiology and Drain Devices subsection, PDF pp64–65, read. This supports caution about colonization after more than three days and specimen collection; the entire 100-plus-page guideline was not read in this batch.
- **Ethicon BLAKE/J-VAC 2021 brochure and IFU:** all 11 pages of extracted text read, including the actual IFU wording. Actual diagrams were not inspected: web screenshot calls yielded only text references, and local download attempts returned 403. Text covers device dimensions, connections, tissue ingrowth, handling/removal and the spring-reservoir MRI/latex warning. These manufacturer statements are not clinical superiority evidence.
- **Cardinal 2021 catalog:** complete 16-page text read and actual product diagrams/tables on pages 3–15 inspected. The catalog includes channel and perforated JP tubes, multiple materials, 100/400-mL bulbs and latex radiopaque Penrose products. Official Canadian product text and the 2012 product-line announcement were also read. A distributor-hosted manufacturer catalog is not a current model-specific IFU; no full current JP IFU was obtained.

## Abstract-only and remaining source work

Complete indexed abstracts were read for Khansa 2018, Swartz 2012, Kowalewski 2020, Williams 2017, Chenam 2018, Kriegmair 2016 and Makita 2020. Williams/Chenam/Kriegmair were re-read with an extractor preserving literal comparison symbols. Chenam stopped at 189 of 312 planned patients and excluded an intraoperative leak; Kriegmair studied open PN. Khansa/Swartz are not comparative patient-outcome trials proving a favored drain design. Publisher attempts returned access errors or HTML challenges; these mains remain unread.

Mahmud 2025, Oh 2023, WSES 2023 and Musser 2014 full files were downloaded but are still unread at this checkpoint. Other inherited citations have metadata-only records or no DOI, not credited abstract/full-paper reads. Removing unsupported text does not silently close those access/review gaps.

The bounded recent-literature search did not identify a verified new large practice-changing drain trial for these pages. Small studies, conference posters and narrative reviews were not added for novelty. This is not a claim that the entire literature has been exhausted.
