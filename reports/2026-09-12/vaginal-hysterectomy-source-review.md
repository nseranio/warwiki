# Vaginal hysterectomy and vNOTES — source follow-through

Review date: September 12, 2026. Continuing whole-site review; no complete clinical clearance. Source files remain outside the deployment. Full current vaginal-hysterectomy and vNOTES MDX originals were read; original copies are preserved temporarily before revision.

## Reading completed before page revision

- **Pickett 2023 Cochrane:** complete 558,938-character article text, all seven summary tables, nine inline appendices, study/analysis tables and 173 reference groups. Actual full-size graphics remain unresolved. See the [dedicated follow-through](hysterectomy-cochrane-follow-through.md). It compares routes, not sealing devices; historical recovery averages are not prescriptions. VH versus abdominal operating time and transfusion are uncertain. Sparse urinary injuries and sensitivity to trial quality constrain rankings.
- **Balgobin 2024 SGS review:** complete abstract plus the entire **51-page, 20-appendix supplement**, including all extracted text and all three actual forest plots (PDF pages 17–19); pages 16–21 and final pages 50–51 inspected/read as recorded in the access register. Main manuscript and separate main graphics remain unread. Appendix 10 verifies time −15.9 min (95% CI −23.3 to −8.5), blood loss −36.9 mL (−56.9 to −17.0), stay −0.2 days (−0.4 to −0.1), pain −1.4 (−1.7 to −1.1) and opioid use −10.0 MME (−25.9 to 6.0). The last CI includes no difference. Plots show substantial heterogeneity (stay I² 87.3%, pain 79.5%, opioids 97.1%) despite an evidence table calling estimates consistent. Pain pools repeated follow-up times from some cohorts. No major-injury superiority inferred; underlying trials are not all individually read.
- **LAVA 2025, PMID 40473285 / PMC12142165:** entire 47,613-character main article, two tables, 26 references and both actual figures read. Planned 3,250, randomized 75, only 62 underwent surgery (32 LH, 30 AH); underrecruitment prevented its planned noninferiority analysis. Two versus four major complications and identical 7.5-week median recovery cannot establish equivalence. No VH arm; concurrent pelvic-support/bladder procedures excluded. Seven linked DOCX supplement requests returned HTML rather than documents; supplements, detailed protocol and reviewer comments remain unread. A table calls an injury vesicovaginal where narrative calls it ureterovaginal; do not export that inconsistent label.
- **Yildiz 2025, PMID 41370495 / PMC12680420:** entire 33,710-character main, two tables and 17 references read. One actual CONSORT image remains unread. Single-center 144-patient trial; no operating-time advantage; concomitant BSO differed markedly; excluded urogynecologic procedures and advanced prolapse. Internal prose/table discrepancies include ileus counts, pain mean-versus-median description, a prior-surgery percentage and unclear uterine-volume units. Not selected as a practice-changing addition; registry and underlying cited papers remain unread.
- **Cassani 2026, PMID 41882424:** complete publisher HTML main, 21 references and all five actual figures read. The local 310-character article download was a challenge page and does not represent the source; the real main was read in the web tool. Sixty-three RCTs, 7,470 participants, search through January 15, 2025. LH versus AH ureter injury OR 7.74 (2.46–24.37), combined urinary injury OR 2.66 (1.47–4.83); very sparse events and broad intervals for newer routes. Only 48 vNOTES participants in this dataset. Early-recognized injuries, not systematic ascertainment of delayed injuries. Overlaps the older Cochrane evidence base and is not an independent cohort. Publication-year meta-regression cannot prove risk unchanged over time.

## Cassani supplement audit

All seven real supplemental assets were ultimately retrieved after rejecting HTML placeholders. S1 (study table) and S7 (rates) DOCX extracted text read completely; all eight rendered S1 pages and the single S7 page now inspected. Roy 2011/2012 and Sarlos 2012 contain route labels in the patient-count column: verified in actual layout, not an extraction artifact. S2/S3/S4: all three slides and all nine actual panels now inspected, including the WMF panels rendered through LibreOffice. S5: all four actual PDF pages inspected; only page 1 contains the traffic-light table, pages 2–4 are blank. S6 is a 41-page individual risk-of-bias PDF: first 20,000 raw characters read plus every one of 62 forms reviewed through validated structured extraction (22 answers and six judgments per form); the entire raw 182,429-character compact text has not been directly read. Do not inflate structured-table coverage into a full visual/raw-text read.

Main text risk-of-bias categories sum to 71 despite 63 trials. S6 contains 62 forms, missing V26, with 27 low / 27 some-concerns / 8 high overall judgments; one form has a domain marked some concerns but overall low. Figure 2 has 31 zero-bladder-event studies versus 27 in prose. S2 panel A confirms the direct VH-versus-AH bladder OR 2.19 (0.71–6.73), not the prose upper bound 2.63. Its slide heading incorrectly says LH-versus-AH, whereas the trial/arm data and direct-evidence table identify VH-versus-AH. S3/S4 show different pooled and predictive intervals; they must not be substituted for the main network estimates. S7's values match pooled estimates, not simply raw event fractions; they are not universal patient risk predictions. These inconsistencies and sparse events preclude safety rankings.

## Original-reference and newer full-paper gaps

All 19 original VH indexed abstracts were read. Actual full-text attempts for the 17 non-PMC originals returned HTTP 403, Springer challenge HTML or Elsevier handoff HTML. This documents unsuccessful access, not proof that no lawful copy exists. Original citations will remain in the access register even when unsupported claims are removed from the public page.

**VANH 2026, PMID 42137913:** complete raw indexed abstract read, full paper request returned 403. Two Dutch centers, 113 analyzed (71 VANH, 42 VH): same-day discharge 87.3% versus 71.4%, OR 2.76 (1.04–7.25). No demonstrated difference in reported complications, readmissions, recovery or quality of life. Primary short-term discharge result is not proof of rare-event safety or robotic benefit. Full methods, flow diagram and supplements remain unread.

**Acil 2026, PMID 42392355:** complete abstract reread; 90 ASA I–II patients and short-term pain/recovery outcomes. Main article/supplements remain unread; Elsevier handoff HTML is not full text. **Sun/Li letter, PMID 42562192:** citation only, no abstract; full text unread. Its existence alone is not evidence of any particular criticism.

## Revision targets

Correct the source of apical-support guidance; remove automatic dysplasia/hyperplasia indications, absolute eligibility language, outdated universal complication rates, routine gabapentin and fixed recovery prescriptions. Attribute Jeppson to SGS rather than ACOG. Separate technique description from comparative evidence and observational associations from causal route rankings. Keep videos before References. Reconcile vNOTES with conventional VH without transferring conventional-route outcomes to robotic platforms.


## Additional current guidance actually read

**Sartori et al., Febrasgo 2026 position statement, PMID 42500195 / PMC13399375:** complete 58,262-character HTML text in three contiguous reads, all recommendation rows, every operative section, final considerations, disclosures and 52 references. No article figures or separate supplement identified. Used narrowly for apical suspension with prolapse hysterectomy, individualized uterine preservation and cystoscopy during vaginal uterosacral suspension. Its broad operative statistics were not imported. Problems to preserve for future source reconciliation include a mismatched Serati 2015 DOI, conflation of some hysteropexy/colpopexy references, a polyglactin long-lasting label and older blanket activity restrictions. Reading this statement does not validate all of those details or read its 52 underlying papers.

**ACOG/AUGS 214:** the current AUGS guidance directory was read and confirms 2019 publication/reaffirmation 2024. Direct official ACOG retrieval returned 402; the BoardPass facsimile returned 404 through both web and direct download; the LWW link failed. This original bulletin remains unread in this pass. The revised page instead cites the directly read Febrasgo statement for apical support, keeping ACOG 701 for benign-route selection only.
