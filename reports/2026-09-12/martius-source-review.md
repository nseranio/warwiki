## Corrections

- Distinguished the modern fibrofatty modification from historical muscle harvest. Replaced contradictory anterior/posterior target-to-pedicle rules with actual pedicle/reach assessment; removed routine right-side harvest and universal no-drain assertions.
- Removed the universal less-than-5% complication guarantee, routine recurrence/dyspareunia benefit, and cross-study flap rankings. Anatomical closure, continence, symptoms and retreatment are separate outcomes.
- Replaced the 2026 Cochrane **protocol** as an outcome source with the completed Singh randomized trial and Thompson systematic review. No results were invented for the protocol.
- Corrected Eilber: 34 Martius recipients among 207 total; Pushkar: cumulative vaginal-cohort success is not success among the 86 Martius cases; Malde diverticulectomy: persistent de novo SUI denominator is the 41 initially continent women, not all 70.
- Removed the erroneous Ko “7 of 8 Martius cures”: eight were cured after reoperation, seven using Martius, and that statement does not supply the denominator of all Martius recipients. Removed case-level reuse/mesh-coverage observations as routine guarantees.
- Corrected the ACOG/AUGS date to 2017 and separated vaginal sling exposure from erosion into bladder/urethra. Salvage bladder-neck closure requires a separate drainage plan.
- Removed claims of Martius/gracilis equivalence and a mandatory treatment sequence from nonrandomized Pastier data. Procedure counts exceeded patient counts, and later success included additional operations. Smoking was a study-specific predictor, not the only universal predictor.
- Did not reproduce Trompetto's inconsistent “91.3% (22/24)” value; 22/24 is 91.7%. Did not assign mixed mesh-fistula outcomes in Blaivas to the Martius subgroup.

## Access limits and currentness

The remaining original papers were checked through their full indexed abstracts; that is not full manuscript/figure/supplement verification. The retained Malde, Eilber, Pushkar, Rothenberger, Kapriniotis, Swindon, Pastier and Blaivas papers remain limited in this respect. Publisher attempts for Kapriniotis and Swindon did not expose complete manuscripts; Swindon showed the abstract and a supporting-file listing only. Rothenberger's issue is 2026, with online publication in 2025. Removing a claim does not turn its original citation into a completed full-text read.

Searches checked current Martius/fistula reviews and trials. A small 2026 fibrin-glue trial (PMID41688883) was identified, but has not received a complete source read and is not presented as a practice-changing result. The user's priority remains major comparative evidence and guidance. The established reconstructive indications still rely heavily on observational studies.
