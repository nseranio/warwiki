# Granieri 2019 primary-paper read — September 12, 2026

Independent reviewer source: Granieri et al., *J Urol* 2019;201:1164–1170, DOI [10.1097/JU.0000000000000087](https://doi.org/10.1097/JU.0000000000000087), PMID30864909. The [deposited published PDF](https://fileserver-az.core.ac.uk/download/484169812.pdf) contains a repository cover and seven article pages; the cover's journal label is incorrect.

Complete main text, bibliography, sole table and all six figures/captions were read. All seven article pages were rendered and visually inspected. PDF SHA256: `0912bda744be4eba812b88c3d8e6b9a6531153df16bbead86f20b3bd0fdf42b3`. Research PDF/text/images remain outside the repository.

The heterogeneous cohort includes 12 minimally invasive harvests and one open harvest for radiated RUF, with varied configurations and two combined buccal grafts. Two of 13 patients recurred at median follow-up 13.5 months. No bowel harms were reported; surveillance and questionnaires limit that conclusion. Reconstructive harms included glans dehiscence, fistula and compartment syndrome. The discussion helps clarify a garbled recurrence-timing passage; that wording was not copied.

No supplement is mentioned in the article, but the publisher's external inventory was inaccessible. No videos or individual patient data were reviewed.

## Overlap assessment

The paper does not enumerate reused patients. Independently read Palmer2016 and Howard2019 abstracts, plus the original [SAGES2017 Howard abstract](https://www.sages.org/meetings/annual-meeting/abstracts-archive/robotic-transanal-minimally-invasive-rectal-mucosa-harvest/), share institutions, recruitment intervals and techniques. This supports possible overlap, not an exact duplicate count. Their full manuscripts were not read in this bounded check.

Editorial decision: keep individual reports separate. Do not sum them into an independent worldwide patient total, infer comparative donor safety or claim complete clinical verification.
