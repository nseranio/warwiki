## Interpretation and internal discrepancies

The review compares benign hysterectomy routes, excluding device-only comparisons and cancer. Many trials exclude concurrent pelvic-floor surgery. Faster recovery after vaginal versus abdominal surgery is better supported than rare-injury comparisons. Some injury/transfusion findings lose statistical significance when unclear-randomization trials are excluded. Historical care pathways are not current protocols.

Do not export unresolved internal discrepancies: Table 6 fever absolute risks conflict with its relative effect; robotic recovery has a mean-sign inconsistency; analysis 2.14.3 lists a suspicious 43-day hospital-stay difference; some intervention/count descriptions disagree. Absence of significance does not demonstrate equivalence, and zero-event cells do not establish zero risk. The published Masterson route-only summary remains appropriate.
