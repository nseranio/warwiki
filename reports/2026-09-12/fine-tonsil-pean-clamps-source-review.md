## Actual full-source reading

**Pinder/Wilson/Hilton 2011 Cochrane**, DOI 10.1002/14651858.CD002211.pub2, PMC7097733: read all **53,843 extracted HTML characters** in three contiguous slices, including both search appendices, all characteristics/analysis tables and references. Inspected **all four actual forest plots**. Two trials randomized 254 patients; bleeding analyses contain 250 after exclusions, and 12-day analgesia analysis contains 47. Cold-dissection comparators also used diathermy for hemostasis. This is not a trial of Schnidt versus another clamp. Search ends October 2010; no new 2026 treatment recommendation is inferred. The PDF request returned HTML, but the complete HTML and plots were obtained. Separate original trial reports remain unread.

The source itself has scope discrepancies: the Kujawski characteristics row labels the older group as children, whereas the main states that it includes adults; the incomplete-outcome judgement is high risk in the table versus unclear in the prose. The displayed intraoperative blood-loss CI loses minus signs in one prose sentence; the analysis table and actual plot show −21.56 mL (−27.26 to −15.85). These findings are recorded, not exported as pelvic-surgery effects.

**Crumplin 2023, Vascular forceps and clamps**, DOI 10.1093/bjs/znac380: complete actual publisher main, all seven captions, disclosures and **all seven actual illustrations** read. Used for the distinction between crushing hemostats and temporary vascular-occlusion instruments, plus a narrow Péan name attribution. The article is a narrative instrument explanation. Its unsupported-looking “40 N/m²” compression statement is not reused as a pressure threshold.

**Teleflex catalog:** complete relevant text and actual illustrations/tables on PDF pages **61, 71, 72, 75, 76, 257 and 566** (printed 60, 70, 71, 74, 75, 256 and 565). This is seven selected catalog pages, not the whole catalog or IFUs. Page 71's entry for 181830 has a straight-pattern preamble but a curved row; that inconsistent entry was not selected for the site. Verified descriptions/tables were used for the other product examples.

STERIS CA8610 title/specification body, Anthony Schnidt description/features/geometry/handle options and the accessible Aesculap BH837R listing were read. The Aesculap dynamic tabs and separate IFUs were not read. Manufacturer descriptions establish dimensions and design; advertising claims about injury prevention, ergonomics or slippage are not clinical comparative evidence.
