## Findings

- The page preserves the trauma-versus-iatrogenic scope distinction, avoids universal ischemia/return-to-theatre clocks, and emphasizes temporary control, exposure, perfusion and timely definitive treatment.
- The REBOA numbers match the verified randomized-strategy analysis; the credible interval and posterior probability are not misrepresented as a conventional statistically significant 90-day mortality result. Actual balloon uptake and crossover are retained.
- PROCOAG, FiiRST-2, CRYOSTAT-2 and the two whole-blood trials are described as different strategies. The page does not convert null superiority findings into equivalence, promote subgroup artifacts, transfer raw product-unit definitions across trials or confuse empirical PCC with anticoagulant reversal.
- Suprahepatic plus infrahepatic caval control and hepatic inflow occlusion are correctly distinguished from incomplete hepatic exclusion. The page does not endorse routine atriocaval shunting, benign iliac sacrifice or automatic abandonment of a newly recognized salvageable renal injury.
- References and visible citation associations are coherent in this draft. No MDX syntax problem was apparent during full reading; automated authoring/link/build checks remain root's responsibility.

One nonblocking precision improvement is available: the PATCH row could state that the six-month primary outcome was observed in 1,131 of 1,310 randomized patients, and the PROCOAG safety percentages could carry 56/161 versus 37/157. These details are already explicit in the maintainer source reports; the present brief table does not falsely claim complete follow-up. No extra small-study additions are needed for this page.
