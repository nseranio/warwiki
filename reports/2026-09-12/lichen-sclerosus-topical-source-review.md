## Interpretation that changed the pages

- **PRP:** Goldstein randomized 30 women 2:1; one withdrew before treatment, leaving 19 PRP and 10 saline recipients. Histological inflammation improved in 5/19 versus 5/10; between-group analysis p=0.542. Patient symptom change was −7.74 versus −9.44, p=0.654. Two injections were six weeks apart. Bruising was the only reported adverse event; small size and short follow-up cannot establish uncommon-harm safety. Device clearance for preparation is not approval of PRP to treat LS. Uncontrolled satisfaction and exploratory adipose/PRP subgroups no longer determine treatment positioning.
- **Laser:** Mitchell randomized 40, but the analysis called ITT excluded three without post-treatment biopsy (37 analyzed); 35 completed the protocol. The sham delivered minimal energy to preserve masking. Histology and between-group symptom results were negative. The main/table report slightly inconsistent rounding and intervals; no dubious effect-size interval was imported. Hecken's 245-person, open-label comparison measured three-month symptoms and favored laser. Different regimens, comparators and endpoints prevent treating these as interchangeable estimates. Patient-chosen crossover is not a randomized treatment-sequence comparison.
- **Guideline:** adult genital PRP/laser and selected intralesional vulvar corticosteroids have an open recommendation, not established comparative benefit. Potent/ultrapotent topical corticosteroids remain first line. Pediatric recommendations differ. The entire 2025 adaptation retains the inherited 2021 systematic-search cutoff; publication in 2026 is not a 2026 search.
- **Injection safety:** removed a universal 40-mg vulvar ceiling, including the contradictory dosing-table entry. Resistant hyperkeratotic plaques require exclusion of neoplasia before injection. Intraurethral clobetasol series often required ongoing treatment; avoiding intervention is not permanent cure. Pediatric preputioplasty comparisons are observational and do not establish superiority over circumcision.
- **Topical treatment:** separated condition-specific potency, corrected the ACOG summary identity, removed nonsignificance-as-equivalence language and unsupported numerical risk categories. TCI maintenance is selective; controlled LS does not imply an automatic switch from effective corticosteroid treatment. Unqualified custom evidence-level rankings were replaced by study designs and limits in three drug tables.
- **Cancer prevention:** Adams measures exposure associations, not assigned maintenance adherence. The reported clobetasol RR 0.62 (95% CI 0.42–0.94) is a two-year observational comparison, not a guaranteed long-term preventive effect. TCI nonsignificance does not prove equivalence or no risk.
- **PEARLS:** 320 is the base sample calculation, inflated to 400 for missing outcomes/nonadherence. The July author response says recruitment target was reached by April 2026. Prior VIN/vulvar SCC is excluded. Both arms can treat flares; the trial does not compare steroid treatment with abandoning care. Its primary self-reported flare endpoint is unmasked, with blinded later scarring assessment; cancer is a secondary outcome and the trial is not established as powered for cancer prevention.
- **Psoriasis:** Amiri includes mixed facial, inverse and genital populations; the 71% clearance number is from one intertriginous pimecrolimus trial, not a pooled genital outcome. Follow-up is mostly eight weeks or less. No included study measured systemic TCI concentrations. The review's positive summary does not establish indefinite genital safety.

## Source inconsistencies retained internally

Tasker reports 616 participants in its abstract and 610 in its results; no combined patient count was imported. Some early trial data in that review predate final publication, so Mitchell's final original results take precedence. Amiri's reference/table labels and some narrative trial counts differ; numerical claims require the original reports. PEARLS prose says trial management is blinded while Table 3 classifies management/data staff as unblinded. The primary-analysis population also refers to receiving at least one application, which is narrower than strict all-randomized ITT. Its main text contains an unrelated internal SharePoint link alongside the protocol citation; that link was not treated as an instruction or accessed. These do not change its protocol-only status, but should be checked against final reports.
