## Further complete main-source reads and exact boundaries

- **ISGE 2016 original guideline**, DOI10.1016/j.ejogrb.2016.03.040: all ten pages, two tables, six recommendation panels, 122 references and the actual sole figure read. Author-hosted copy: https://www.guptalegal.co.uk/pdfs/ISGE.pdf; `/tmp/access10-isge2016.pdf/.txt`. - **SUBITE 2026**, DOI10.1007/s10029-026-03700-z, PMC13186860: complete 76,063-character main text, four tables, 52 references; all twelve supplied actual graphics inspected (including image tables); all three complete supplementary DOCX files read (search/registry detail, peer-review response, disclosures). `/tmp/access10-subite2026.xml/.txt`, `/tmp/access10-subite-media/`. Seven RCTs and five nonrandomized comparative studies, 5,886 overall participants; hernia analysis includes 3,973. Hernia OR0.47 (0.38–0.58). Abstract dehiscence estimate0.60 (0.38–0.93) differs from main/figure0.58 (0.42–0.82); randomized subgroup0.63 (0.29–1.38) is nonsignificant. Supplement2 explicitly says an omitted nonrandomized dehiscence study was added during revision, while the abstract appears unchanged. Table4/SSI study-count, STITCH arm-label, registry-number and search-date discrepancies remain; no unqualified dehiscence benefit, independent component effect or universal high-certainty claim copied. Industry relationships recorded. Funnel plots with few studies cannot exclude publication bias.
- **Morarasu 2025**, DOI10.1111/codi.70073, PMC11933735: complete 37,893-character main text, two tables, 31 references and all fifteen actual graphics read. `/tmp/access10-morarasu2025.xml/.txt`, `/tmp/access10-morarasu-media/`. Three RCTs, one alternate-week controlled trial and three cohorts; 3,807 participants overall. Hernia benefit persists in randomized subgroup; SSI and dehiscence differences do not. Do not relabel nonsignificance as noninferiority. Several table/time-point and text/figure inconsistencies: suture-length forest plot CI crosses zero (P=.28), unlike a main-text significance claim; STITCH SSI numbers require endpoint reconciliation before reuse. Page uses the supported qualitative conclusion rather than copying those values.
- **Park 2012 CO2 embolism review**, DOI10.3349/ymj.2012.53.3.459, PMC3343430: complete 33,513-character main text, 59 references, all four actual image tables and the actual four-panel TEE figure read. `/tmp/access10-co2-review2012.xml/.txt`, `/tmp/access10-co2-media/`. Historical Durant/head-down, aggressive fluid and catheter recommendations are not treated as current universal instructions. Table/main PaCO2 and incidence discrepancies not copied. Newer AHA recommendations control positioning in the rewritten page.
- **AHA 2025 Part10, section13: Gas embolism**, DOI10.1161/CIR.0000000000001380: entire relevant section on the official AHA website read: all five recommendation rows, synopsis and all five supporting-text paragraphs. This is a **complete relevant section read, not a whole 94-page guideline read**; only the first16 of25 section references were visible in the indexed publisher copy. Official source: https://cpr.heart.org/en/resuscitation-science/cpr-and-ecc-guidelines/adult-and-pediatric-special-circumstances-of-resuscitation. Life-threatening gas embolism: 100% oxygen; level supine or recovery positioning; CPR supine; hyperbaric oxygen reasonable, and aspiration may be reasonable only for known accessible right-ventricular gas. The low-level evidence includes different gases and mechanisms; no mandatory head-down maneuver or blanket hyperbaric treatment for incidental microbubbles.
