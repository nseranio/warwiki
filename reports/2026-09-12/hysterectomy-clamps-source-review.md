## New high-yield evidence and limits

**Balgobin et al., 2024 Society of Gynecologic Surgeons systematic review** is the relevant addition. Its complete indexed/publisher abstract supports the narrow published summary: 19 comparative pedicle-sealing studies, approximately 37 mL less estimated blood loss and 16 minutes shorter surgery, with modest pain/stay differences. Those estimates do not establish lower major-complication risk or superiority for abdominal, oncologic or reconstructive operations. The main-text request redirects to a subscription-gated abstract. Its malformed abstract operating-time CI (upper limit “-85”) has not been silently repaired or published.

The **2024 vaginal-technique network meta-analysis** (PMID 39038513) was screened at complete-abstract level. It combines 10 RCTs and 7 observational studies (1,577 women); it was not used to generate a vNOTES safety ranking or treated as an independent patient cohort confirming other overlapping reviews.

All inherited indexed abstracts were read, including Paily's technical case series, the 2014 and 2017 systematic reviews, Fitz-Gerald's 40-patient trial, Hefni's 116-patient vaginal trial, De Leo's 262-patient technique trial, and the retrospective oncology comparisons. Full original journal papers beyond the three completed sources remain open. No source is labeled inaccessible solely because its abstract was retrieved.
