- **Mohamed 2026 renal meta-analysis**, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.urolonc.2026.05.017): complete indexed abstract read. DOI led to a linking page; journal full text returned 403. Narrow qualitative integration of 14 retrospective cohorts; no causal/equivalence claim or unverified confidence intervals. Full methods, individual cohorts and supplementary analyses remain unread.
- **Castellan 2012**, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.juro.2011.12.105): complete indexed abstract read; original PDF returned 403. The denominator is 29 patients with several gastric-segment reconstructions, not 29 identical isolated gastrocystoplasties. Three cancers are a cohort observation, not a population lifetime-risk estimate.

## 2026 quality-of-life review: considered, not adopted quantitatively

[Mediana 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13101609/), DOI 10.1016/j.euros.2026.03.005: complete main, three tables, 27 references, disclosures and actual PRISMA figure read. All six DOCX supplements were retrieved through Europe PMC's public supplementary-file endpoint after individual PMC downloads returned HTML challenges. All text, eight actual forest plots and the PRISMA logo were inspected. All 14 rendered supplement pages were visually checked (including a blank final page in supplement 1).

The global-health and urinary-function associations lose statistical significance in the reported sensitivity analyses. Overall pooled sexual function is not significant. The main table's Gellhaus urinary values differ from the forest plot, which uses ONB mean 50.1/SD 30.6. Other discrepancies include the urinary p value and internally inconsistent BMI meta-regression entries. These findings warrant resolving extraction/reporting issues against original datasets before adopting numerical claims. They do not establish that every analysis is wrong. No new diversion ranking or treatment recommendation was imported.

## Page corrections

- Separated reservoir detubularization from tubular drainage/channel construction, and geometric storage capacity from the Laplace relationship.
- Replaced universal bowel lengths, renal-function thresholds, complication rankings and continence guarantees with configuration-specific considerations.
- Corrected jejunal potassium/hydrogen **absorption** from urine; the previous secretion explanation was wrong.
- Distinguished Mainz I/II, colon-conduit circumstances and appendiceal onlay/interposition; preserved links to operative and pharmacology hubs.
- Replaced pooled-looking percentages with the actual study denominators and follow-up context. Removed an unsupported diversion-use frequency table and small-study superiority framing.
- Removed blanket five-year cystoscopy/ten-year cytology instructions. Applied adult NLUTD guidance within its population, retained symptom-triggered evaluation and lifelong functional follow-up, and kept prior-cancer/rectosigmoid surveillance separate.
- Added the final large USC-STAR RCT and narrowly interpreted 2026 renal synthesis. These are explicitly abstract-level integrations, not claimed full-paper reads.

Additional source cautions: the Indiana paper uses an isolated alkalosis label despite describing bicarbonate depletion/acidosis; it was not copied. The channels paper has inconsistent percentages in some subgroup rows; its unadjusted time-to-first-revision comparison is not significant. Chang's small series does not establish no cancer/metabolic risk; a dysplasia finding in its body should not disappear behind the abstract's reassuring conclusion. Ali-El-Dein 2021 uses an eGFR below 40 as a relative contraindication in its abstract; the inherited blanket 30 cutoff was removed instead of generalized to other reconstructions.
