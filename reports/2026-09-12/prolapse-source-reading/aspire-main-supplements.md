## Source problems and analysis details not to propagate

- The current JAMA visual abstract retains a curve legend with SCPblue and TVMorange, whereas corrected mainFigure2 labels TVMaqua and SCPorange for the same ordering of the two lower curves. The visual abstract thus appears to retain the pre-correction arm/color reversal. Use the corrected main figure and numerical tables, not the visual-abstract curves. The visual abstract also abbreviates the endpoint as a year3 rate rather than emphasizing time to first composite failure.
- Primary safety groups125/120/115 use a **hybrid definition**, not pure as-treated: intraoperative complication/contraindication conversions retain randomized index surgery, while preoperative/very-early noncomplication switches are reassigned. SAPv2 physical322 explicitly defines this and resolves misleading shorthand elsewhere.
- Published UTI counts43/125,17/120,42/115 in main/open-ended MedDRA listing differ from comprehensive prompted-complication UTI counts61/125,37/120,61/115(eTable8). They use different ascertainment; do not combine or silently substitute.
- Main new/worsening dyspareunia9–11% uses all randomized-treated participants, not only sexually active women. The separate PISQ-IR de-novo calculation has stable-activity/respondent denominators.
- Supplement2eTable7 unadjusted and pre-FDA-order-only SCP superiority sensitivities have99%CIs crossing1 and P=.015/.016. Direction is consistent, but not every sensitivity reaches the prespecified significance threshold.
- Supplement2p25 prints SCP cumulative failure `3% (36/111)` in the FDA-order sensitivity row; arithmetic suggests32%, not3%. p26 exclusion footnote18participants lists groups summing19. Preserve as source discrepancies rather than invent a corrected participant flow.
- Main and supplement suture-exposure descriptions/denominators differ; the endpoint is **after12weeks through follow-up**, not incidence at a single12-week visit.
- Satisfaction-scale direction footnote conflicts with narrative interpretation; protocolv6 physical136 explicitly says greaterSDS-PFD means greater satisfaction, and the actual supplement2p37 footnote agrees; do not quote its raw1.2score as a calibrated high-satisfaction measure without verifying scoring. SAP merely says mean of six items.
- SAPv2 cover physical306 saysFeb20,2023, while footers remainv1/Aug17,2022; final amendment summary calls earlier versionSep7,2022. Multiple residual terms “hysteropexy”, “two arms”, “uterovaginal” appear in this post-hysterectomy three-arm protocol. Definitions of mesh erosion also retain language overlooking SCP as another mesh source. Do not copy these passages into clinical guidance.
- SAPv2 physical390/395 explicitly labels analysis excluding years4–5 **post hoc**. Fourteen late events excluded(2NTR/5SCP/7TVM); proportional-hazards testP=.155 in MITT. Full-follow-up PHtestP=.04 means the main HR is an average effect across time. Do not call every sensitivity prespecified.
- SAP functional-activity scoring text divides a sum into an item average, then applies a sum-based65/52formula; SF12 attachment also appears to add an extra multiply-by10 to already-scaled published coefficients. These are internal algorithm-text concerns, not demonstrated errors in analyzed data. Do not implement calculators from this protocol without original scoring validation.

## Additional protocol findings from the completed133–263 range

- E-ASPIRe (physical185–190, repeated in amendment211–217) is a planned observational extension, not a new randomized intervention or already reported ten-year result. It excludes women already reoperated for prolapse and those not receiving one of the three study procedures; follow-up stops upon further prolapse reoperation. Limited virtual participation reduces burden but has POP-Q/mesh examination only when women attend routine clinical care. Retention calculations exclude prior ineligible women and deaths. Therefore do not describe future retention as a percentage of all376 randomized women without checking the eventual publication.
- Protocol plans data collection through December31,2026 and study completion including analysis June30,2027; medianmaximum follow-up9years, only22%potential10years. These are historical planned dates, not independently verified current recruitment or results status. No long-term ASPIRe results are claimed from this protocol.
- PPAR distinguishes resolved/unresolved events from whether they cause lasting patient harm; severity, expectedness, attribution, outcome, seriousness and continuation are separate concepts. FASt studies the Robinson frailty markers and TimedUpandGo versus outcomes; neither appendix contains final ASPIRe clinical results. The complete image-only PRO-CTCAE list is a version1.0 generic item library, not a recommended comprehensive clinic questionnaire.
- Historical protocol evidence-table labels and narrative contain internal inconsistencies (e.g., success headings using failure-direction anatomic thresholds; recurrence/reoperation ranges not spanning all rows; CARE sample/lost-to-follow-up ambiguity; SUPeR planning sample184). They were all read, but no underlying original trials or estimates are certified by those protocol tables.
- SAPv1 cover at physical218 is datedSeptember7,2022 while its footer still saysAugust17,2022; v2 retains old footers as previously noted. Aim2/Aim3 headings swap labels in one summary table while both planned comparisons remain recognizable. Noninferiority alpha is.03/one-sided97%upper limit; it is not.025/97.5%, and the final main figure and supplement agree on.03.
- SAP primary classification expressly allows nonfailure at an attended visit despite missing components; later failure remains cumulative. At-visit/transient failure is a separate secondary definition. Prespecification of these rules does not remove potential bias from missing exams; avoid claiming complete anatomic follow-up.
