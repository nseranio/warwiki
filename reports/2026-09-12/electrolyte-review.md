# Electrolyte page review — September 12, 2026

The complete original 376-line MDX and its 20-reference list were read, followed by a complete read of the rewritten page and rereading of final peer-review amendments. This records source-page reading and scoped verification, not complete clinical clearance. The current content hash is in `../2026-09-11/full-site-review/foundations-rest.json`.

## Substantive corrections

- Replaced the incorrect hyperkalemia grading (which called only >8 mmol/L severe) and deterministic ECG-by-potassium progression. Added current UKKA thresholds, continuous monitoring, calcium equivalence, insulin-associated glucose monitoring through six hours and potassium reassessment; removed routine bicarbonate and misleading emergency binder efficacy comparisons.
- Removed the causal claim that preoperative potassium repletion lowers MACE. Wahr and Arora are observational; Arora used <4 mmol/L as its low category, not the page's <3.5. Added the large TIGHT K randomized trial with its actual population, denominators, noninferiority margin and limits on extrapolation.
- Distinguished total/albumin-adjusted from ionized calcium; corrected indiscriminate calcitriol/thiazide use, forced hydration/loop-diuretic treatment and the restriction of denosumab to refractory hypercalcemia.
- Corrected magnesium–potassium sequencing, PPI prevalence generalization, inorganic-salt generalization and urinary-conduit output misclassified as gastrointestinal loss. Separated monitored replacement from arrest/torsades treatment and removed claims that ordinary conduit patients routinely need chronic IV magnesium.
- Replaced heterogeneous phosphate-dose rules with context-specific replacement and monitoring, accurately framing the 2025 umbrella review's limitations. Refeeding now points to the canonical nutrition pathway instead of mixing ASPEN and NICE doses.
- Corrected diversion segment physiology, fixed-length/time B12 assumptions, universal bicarbonate/potassium targets and the suggestion that appliance changes alter conduit drainage. Removed the unsupported radiation-nephrocalcinosis section.
