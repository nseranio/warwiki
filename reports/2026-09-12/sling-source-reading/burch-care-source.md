## CARE correction and applicable outcome

The complete original-author correction letter confirms a urinary-stress-test coding error discovered during independent public-data replication. Corrected three-month composite SUI rates are **33.6% Burch versus57.4% control, P<.001**, replacing23.8/44.1 (and hence replacing WARWIKI's rounded24/44). The detailed notice's indexed opening also identifies49 versus89 women; denominators146/155 follow arithmetic and indexed missing-outcome counts, but full corrected Table3 is not read here. The correction letter says the trial's overall conclusion remains supported.

Population in original current abstract: women reporting no preoperative stress-incontinence symptoms undergoing **abdominal sacrocolpopexy** for prolapse;322randomized157Burch165control. Primary endpoint at3mo is a composite of symptoms, stress testing or treatment—not pure patient-reported bothersome SUI and not long-term standalone Burch cure. Original indexed narrative excludes fixedurethra and cautions against generalizing to vaginal prolapse operations or other continence procedures. Those are selected passages, not a complete methods read.

Bothersome SUI in current abstract remains6.1% Burch versus24.5% control; urge endpoint32.7/38.4%,P=.48. These are different outcomes from corrected33.6/57.4 composite. Enrollment stopped at first interimanalysis for benefit; do not erase that design limitation or turn observed short-term benefit into a guarantee.
