### What can be supported, with limits

- This is long-term follow-up of the randomized **standard versus short autologous fascial sling** trial, not a new107-person randomization and not a comparison with mesh.
- The original flow diagram starts with **165 randomized:81standard and84short**. Deaths14/15 and other losses2/1 leave133questionnaires sent(65/68);26nonresponses(13each) leave **107responses:52standard and55short**. Thus80% is the response proportion among questionnaires sent;107/165=**64.8% of the original randomized population**. This distinction matters for survivor/nonresponse bias and durability claims.
- Abstract reports no statistically significant standard–short difference at17years for IIQ/UDI, leakage or urgency. That is not a demonstrated equivalence/noninferiority result. Satisfaction67.2% is not the continence-cure proportion. Improved symptoms relative to baseline coexist with later deterioration.
- Actual Figure2 shows large early score improvement and later recurrence/deterioration for some measures. Its equally spaced categorical time points include3,6,12months and17years; the drawn linear trend lines should not be reinterpreted as a continuous annual trajectory or rate of deterioration. Do not extract exact outcome counts from these illustrative lines.
- Appendix1 separately asks perceived surgical success, strict never-wet-since-surgery status, improvement/recurrence duration, complications, subsequent surgery, medical treatment, catheter/pad use and satisfaction. These are different constructs; satisfaction and perceived success cannot substitute for standardized objective cure.
- Original-trial funding is Research and Development,NHSWales; no conflicts are declared. ReceivedJuly7,2023; acceptedNovember15,2023; publishedFebruary1,2024; issueMarch2024.
- Reasonable use: a narrowly worded long-term durability/supporting follow-up citation, preserving selection and definition limits. The unread main prevents a detailed appraisal of missing-data modeling, exact reoperation rates, eligibility and adverse-event ascertainment.

### High-value cautions from actual figures

1. Abstract describes **20RCTs in the review,10 in meta-analysis**, with searching for publications beforeSeptember2023. That does not establish20independent comparisons or a search through2024/2026. Full methods are needed to assess trial independence, mixed routes/materials and selection.
2. **Continuous-outcome effect measure is mislabeled in the abstract.** Actual Figure3 explicitly says **standardized mean difference**, not risk ratio: operative timeSMD2.87(2.56–3.19), I²97%; hospital staySMD1.92(1.44–2.41), I²96%. These are neither relative risks nor literal2.87minutes/1.92days. Do not repeat the abstract's RR label or translate standardized differences into absolute recovery times.
3. Figure3's positive AFS-minus-SMUS time/stay values are placed on a side labeled favoringAFS, despite numerical means showing longerAFSprocedures/stays and the abstract describing shorterSMUStimes. The “favours” labels should not guide clinical interpretation. Similar adverse-outcome axis concerns occur in Figure2; read group counts and outcome direction explicitly.
4. **Internal denominator discrepancy:** Tcherniakovsky2009 is20AFS versus21SMUS in Figure2A/B, but Figure3A assigns **41AFS versus21SMUS** for duration. The41matches the entire trial size in the separately read Cochrane characteristics. Do not silently fix the pooled analysis; obtain the full original/main before reusing its precision.
5. Figure2C lists both **Guerrero2010 and Khan2014**. The public reference list identifies the Guerrero trial and its later Khan follow-up. Potential shared-cohort dependence needs full-methods/original-report clarification; do not treat the two names as proven independent cohorts.
6. Figure2A displays235/263 versus239/259, RR0.97(0.92–1.03). Its near-null point estimate does not prove equivalence. Figure2D's pooled0.12(0.03–0.50) derives from **0/146 versus15/146** across four small contributing samples, not an all-complication or all-patient guarantee. Figure2E's urgency estimate is2.84(1.13–7.10),18/203 versus5/195. These are actual visible figure values; precise panel-outcome definitions and ascertainment still require the missing full caption/methods.
7. The review mixes retropubic and transobturator constructions in its references, with substantial overlap with older trials reviewed by Cochrane. It should not be presented as a completely independent large-trial confirmation of one contemporary operative technique.
8. Authors declare no conflicts. ReceivedSeptember5,2023; acceptedFebruary16,2024; publishedMarch23,2024; issueApril2024. PROSPEROCRD42023412868 is cited, but the registration itself was not read in this task.
