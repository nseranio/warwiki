## Applicability and certainty

The review includes **55 trials / 5,417 women**, with 5,198 contributing an outcome at the latest assessment available for their study. Those figures are not a single complete follow-up cohort. The review links 152 reports; many trials are small, older, poorly reported, or available only as abstracts. Five used quasi-random allocation. Only three trials supplied results at or beyond five years. Allocation concealment and blinding are often unclear, with inevitable nonblinding of many surgical comparisons. Do not add a formal GRADE certainty label: none was identified in the read range.

“Open retropubic colposuspension” combines **Burch, Marshall–Marchetti–Krantz (MMK), and some paravaginal procedures**. “Traditional sling” combines autologous rectus fascia with older lyodura, Teflon and Gore-Tex/PTFE constructs, among others. “Midurethral sling” can combine TVT and transobturator procedures, even where narrative shorthand says TVT. These are important limits on transplanting pooled figures into a Burch-only or modern autologous-sling page.

Subgroup labels “urodynamic diagnosis” and “symptom diagnosis” describe study entry criteria, not a randomized comparison of diagnostic strategies. They do not establish that every current SUI patient requires urodynamics. In several multi-procedure comparisons, the particular operation was chosen rather than randomly assigned within a randomized broad-treatment arm. Long-term quality of life, sexual function, symptomatic prolapse, retreatment and harms remain incompletely reported. A nonsignificant comparison does not itself prove equivalence or noninferiority.

The laparoscopic comparison reports shorter hospitalization/catheterization and faster activity recovery, but results depend on historical care pathways, unblinded discharge decisions, route/technique variants and heterogeneity. They are not instructions for a current fixed hospital stay or catheter duration. The same applies to historical Burch/MMK catheter-duration differences. Economic comparisons likewise depend on old health-system and procedural costs, and the brief economic commentary is not a contemporary cost-effectiveness ranking.

## Source errors, discrepancies, and editorial traps

1. **Five-year cure contradiction:** abstract/principal summary says roughly **70% dry**, while Implications for practice says roughly **80% dry**. The 85–90% one-year and 70/80% five-year statements are broad historical summaries, not one consistently defined pooled endpoint. Prefer contemporary direct trials and explicitly defined outcomes over reproducing either unqualified headline.
2. **All-slings medium-term significance:** abstract says no significant overall difference while reporting RR **1.18 (1.01–1.39)**. The comparison table confirms this CI. Do not reproduce the blanket null claim.
3. **Traditional-sling medium-term CI:** one narrative occurrence pairs RR **1.35** with CI **1.03–1.37**, which belongs to a different long-term result. The abstract and table give **1.11–1.64** for the medium-term estimate.
4. **Short-term objective sling comparison:** abstract gives **1.24 (0.93–1.67)**, while main text/table give **1.21 (0.84–1.75)**; the table has **7 studies/603 women**, not the narrative's eight trials. Flag the internal mismatch rather than silently blending the estimates.
5. **Burch/MMK subgroup presented as headline:** the abstract's **0.38 (0.18–0.76)** is the urodynamic-diagnosis subgroup. The overall table is **0.72 (0.52–0.99)**. This is resolved by the complete comparison 9 table, rather than an unexplained conflicting number.
6. **E-SISTEr starting denominator:** narrative says **482/665**; its own included-study record correctly gives **655** original randomized participants. The previously read original E-SISTEr main also establishes 655, 482 extension participants and 357 five-year completers. These denominators must remain distinct.
7. **SISTEr perforation typo:** review narrative says **3% vs 0.06%**. The previously read original SISTEr table has **10/329 Burch vs 2/326 sling**, i.e. about **3.0% vs 0.6%**, corresponding to RR 4.95 (1.09–22.44). The review's percentage has an extra zero.
8. **Relative reduction arithmetic:** RR **0.41** is described as nearly 40% less; it represents roughly **59% relative reduction**, not 40%. Absolute risk depends on the relevant comparator population.
9. **Sand material mix-up:** the review identifies Sand 2000's sling as **Gore-Tex/PTFE**, but later calls that study's urethrolysis/erosion findings TVT findings. Do not assign those harms to modern polypropylene TVT or autologous fascia without checking the original material.
10. **Prolapse overstatement versus anterior repair:** the extracted pooled estimate is **RR 2.51 (0.62–10.10), 5 studies/447 women**, random effects with heterogeneity. Unqualified statements of a proven higher rate overstate that comparison. This is separate from the sling comparison's clearer anatomic prolapse signal.
11. **Paravaginal heading/text mismatch:** the section for MMK versus paravaginal repair says no Burch-versus-paravaginal trial was found, despite comparison 10 containing a 36-woman Burch trial. Treat the sentence as a likely copied comparator error, not evidence that the Burch comparison is absent.
12. **Included-study Albo outcome shorthand:** its stated overall-success definition omits the negative pad-test component of the original two-year SISTEr composite. The original trial, not this abbreviated table, should define the two-year endpoint. E-SISTEr's longer-term definition also differs and should not inherit the original pad-test requirement.
13. **Ankardal counts need original reconciliation:** its included-study entry has N=187, refers to 211 recruited/198 with one-year follow-up, while listed intervention counts 79/72/49 sum to 200. Do not use that entry to reconstruct a participant flow without the original reports. Multiple reports from this cohort are not independent studies.
14. **Empty analysis cells:** `0.0 [0.0, 0.0]` sometimes accompanies zero studies or “Totals not selected.” These are display placeholders, not evidence of a zero treatment effect.
15. **Economic study count wording:** the commentary's count wording is internally uneven (four versus five); four TVT comparisons plus one laparoscopic comparison account for five evaluations. This does not affect the clinical trial estimates, but argues against copying a headline count without scope.

## Actionable use

For the Burch page, retain Burch as an established SUI operation, use original SISTEr/E-SISTEr for autologous-sling efficacy/harms with exact endpoint and follow-up definitions, and describe older Cochrane results as comparator-specific historical synthesis. Distinguish Burch from MMK, all slings from autologous slings/MUS, and anatomic prolapse from symptomatic prolapse. Avoid fixed recovery promises, universal cure rates, unproven equivalence, and claims that 2017 publication means a clinical search through 2017.
