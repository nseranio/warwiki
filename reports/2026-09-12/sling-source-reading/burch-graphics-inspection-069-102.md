## Concrete source inconsistencies

- **7.10, ordinal69:** actual plot displays pooled hospital-stay MD **1.53days [1.07,1.98]**,366open/348lap, I²89%, random effects. Yet the relevant narrative says no attempt was made to derive a typical estimate because of heterogeneity. The plot and narrative are inconsistent about whether pooling was done. Avoid quoting the pooled duration as an expected modern recovery difference.
- **7.11, ordinal70:** actual plot displays pooled return-to-normal-activity MD **15.10days [0.33,29.88]**,76open/74lap, I²93%, random effects.
