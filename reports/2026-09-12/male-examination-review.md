## Corrections

- Removed caliber-to-operation rules, mandatory blind sounds/Hegar passage, forceful calibration and scheduled postoperative dilation masquerading as surveillance. Replaced them with indicated testing and risk-adjusted follow-up.
- Removed claims that palpation determines obliteration, microscopic LS or a safe dissection plane; meatal location no longer prescribes a hypospadias operation. Replaced stigmatizing terminology.
- Corrected male LS distribution, biopsy indications, genital-skin restrictions and topical treatment/circumcision context. Removed unverified lifetime cancer percentages, routine DVIU mapping, mandatory full excision and universal serial RUG.
- Corrected Peyronie's stability versus calcification, erection assessment alternatives, unvalidated angle/IIEF cutoffs and unsupported length thresholds.
- Removed automatic cancer diagnosis from failed transillumination and routine CT for every right varicocele. Torsion is not excluded by a preserved reflex or arterial flow.
- Corrected the RUF opening to the anterior rectal wall, removed routine blind tract instrumentation, male Martius language and fixed-plane-to-York-Mason/diversion rules.
- Removed unvalidated male perineal-body thresholds, catheter-balloon traction and deterministic sacral-reflex predictions of continence, erectile recovery or detrusor behavior. Added consent, examination limitations and urgent findings.
- Replaced the blanket examination template with a concise checklist of findings actually assessed; this does not start the deferred Epic project.
