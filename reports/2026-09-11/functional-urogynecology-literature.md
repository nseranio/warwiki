# Functional urology and urogynecology evidence update — September 11, 2026

## Scope and method

This is a targeted evidence update, not a completed independent clinical review of every statement. The principal new-literature window was **June 28–September 11, 2026**, reflecting the last recorded media/content update. Important earlier omissions and inaccurate guideline interpretations encountered during the review were also corrected and are identified below. No clinician `lastReviewed` date was reset. Modified pages have a narrowly described `evidenceUpdated` / `evidenceNote`.

Topics screened: female SUI, POP, OAB/UUI, sacral/tibial neuromodulation, NLUTD, recurrent uncomplicated UTI, GSM, and directly associated pharmacology. Male reconstruction, fistulas, BPH, and general site engineering were assigned elsewhere. Searches used official AUA/EAU/FDA sources, PubMed records, publisher pages, and Europe PMC primary-article metadata/abstracts. The shared `literature-inbox/latest.json` supplied additional candidates; its dates and publication-type flags were treated as discovery metadata, not proof of novelty or trial design.

For recent paywalled articles, numerical findings were checked against the indexed article abstract. These are published journal reports, not conference-only abstracts, but access to an abstract does **not** mean the full methods, supplement, or risk of bias were independently audited. No treatment recommendation was changed solely from a meeting abstract, preprint, promotional release, or automated screening label.

## Guideline and regulatory corrections

- **NLUTD and SCI:** restored moderate-risk classification; CIC dependence alone is not high risk. Corrected high-risk annual AUA imaging, removed recommendations for routine screening cystoscopy in chronic-catheter users, and removed a rigid waiting period after SCI. The EAU cadence is explicitly distinguished from AUA. [AUA diagnosis/evaluation](https://doi.org/10.1097/JU.0000000000002235), [AUA treatment/follow-up](https://doi.org/10.1097/JU.0000000000002239), [EAU 2026](https://uroweb.org/guidelines/neuro-urology/chapter/the-guideline).
- **Storage pressure:** removed the claim that DLPP below 40 or compliance above 10–12 establishes universal safety; retained historical context and combined assessment. [Primary adult pressure study](https://doi.org/10.1002/nau.24911).
- **Beta-3 agonists:** corrected adult/pediatric indication conflation and the claim of reliable adult NDO pressure reduction. Symptom benefit must not be used as evidence of renal protection. [EAU drug section](https://uroweb.org/guidelines/neuro-urology/chapter/the-guideline), [manufacturer label](https://www.astellas.us/docs/Myrbetriq_WPI.pdf), [DailyMed](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=aaaf3bea-1b21-4edf-9300-b7159ea9ede8).
- **SNM:** corrected “not first-line”/complete-SCI-only wording to reflect the actual AUA restriction for SCI/spina bifida; acknowledged EAU selected neurogenic use. [AUA treatment](https://doi.org/10.1097/JU.0000000000002239).
- **OAB and botulinum procedure:** removed rigid third-line framing and “equivalent” therapy wording. Shared decision-making can precede medication trials; labeling/coverage are separate. [AUA 2024](https://doi.org/10.1097/JU.0000000000003985).
- **rUTI:** aligned the diagnostic section and algorithm with symptom-linked laboratory confirmation and testing for each acute episode; corrected D-mannose specialist-subgroup overinterpretation in the pharmacology companion. [AUA/CUA/SUFU 2025](https://doi.org/10.1097/JU.0000000000004723).
- **Estrogen:** replaced “all boxed warnings gone in November 2025 / liberal use” claims with the FDA's product-specific chronology. [FDA February 12, 2026 announcement](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-labeling-changes-menopausal-hormone-therapy-products).
- **POP mesh:** corrected Class III reclassification from 2011 to 2016, and removed language implying routine current US indications for transvaginal POP mesh. [FDA timeline](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/urogynecologic-surgical-mesh-implants/fdas-activities-urogynecologic-surgical-mesh), [FDA overview](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/implants-and-prosthetics/urogynecologic-surgical-mesh-implants).

## No-change and excluded findings

- **EAU female LUTS 2026 is a reprint of 2025**, with only formatting updates. It must not be advertised as a new clinical recommendation update. [Official changes](https://uroweb.org/guidelines/non-neurogenic-female-luts/summary-of-changes/2026).
- Official AUA inventory still lists female SUI 2023, OAB 2024, NLUTD 2021, GSM 2025, and rUTI 2025; no invented 2026 replacement was inserted. [AUA inventory updated July 14, 2026](https://helpdesk.auanet.org/hc/en-us/articles/40948838469143-Guidelines).
- The 2026 ACR recurrent-UTI imaging update was already cited; its August issue date does not make it newly published after June. It was online April 13. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41973892/).
- Bilateral SNM neurogenic bladder/bowel cohort: 20 participants, nonrandomized within-patient testing, 12 permanent implants. Not sufficient to reverse AUA SCI/spina bifida guidance. [Full article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13355243/).
- **Retraction surveillance:** the 60-month needleless mini-sling RCT was retracted August 4, 2026; its 18-month report also has an August retraction record. Neither title/DOI/author pattern was found in the relevant existing sling evidence. No retracted result was added. [60-month notice](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42550251/), [18-month notice](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42593547/).
- **BEST sacrocolpopexy suture RCT** and **return-to-baseline activity after POP repair** have 2026 issue dates but earlier 2025 dissemination metadata. Kept as an operative-efficiency follow-up queue, not labeled discoveries from July–September. BEST is recognized by the society as a 2025 article in a 2026 issue. [AUGS record](https://www.augs.org/membership/augs-awards/linda-brubaker-prize-paper-award/), [BEST](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41252163/), [activity analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41263593/). The latter is a secondary recovery analysis, not a randomized comparison of postoperative activity restrictions.
- **Polyacrylamide injection anesthesia RCT:** identified for the procedural-efficiency queue; exact earliest publication date/full protocol not verified and no injection anesthetic recipe changed. [Record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40986885/).
- Recent digital-therapy, machine-learning, and small rehabilitation studies were screened but did not justify replacing established care. The collector's “priority” flag is not a determination of clinical importance.

## Addendum — methenamine companion audit, September 11, 2026

This was a targeted consistency/safety correction before recurrent-UTI template reuse, not a new clinical guideline announcement or a complete independent review of these long articles.

### Findings and changes

- **2025, not 2022:** corrected the false attribution of methenamine endorsement to AUA 2022. The 2025 amendment adds statement 15, a conditional Grade C option for women with rUTI. Removed a forced sequence implying every non-antibiotic strategy must fail before antibiotic prophylaxis.
- **Acidic mechanism versus supplementation:** removed routine vitamin C and replacement-cranberry acidification instructions across all four companion pages. The AUA 2025 discussion advises against concomitant vitamin C. ALTAR explicitly did not investigate the added value of acidification. The US label's conditional pH/clinical-response language does not establish routine vitamin-C benefit; the label provides no universal numeric urine-pH target.
- **US label safety:** prescribing details remain centralized in the non-antibiotic prevention hub. Listed renal insufficiency, severe hepatic insufficiency, severe dehydration, and sulfonamide interaction as stated in the US label; did not invent an eGFR/CrCl threshold for methenamine. Removed transplant advice implying methenamine could be added to TMP-SMX. Prescription methenamine is no longer called a supplement.
- **Evidence limits:** retained ALTAR's absolute recurrence rates and non-inferiority margin, qualified resistance claims by treatment period, and removed “resistance-proof”/“highest-yield” wording. Removed an unsupported 1–2-week onset claim. The old catheterized-patient acidifier study used methenamine mandelate; it is not an ALTAR-like trial of uncomplicated rUTI. Removed the incorrect statement that incomplete emptying shortens formaldehyde contact time; ALTAR excluded neurogenic dysfunction and SINBA did not support routine methenamine in SCI.
- **Adjacent workflow contradictions:** removed mandatory negative test-of-cure cultures in asymptomatic uncomplicated nonpregnant women and claims that 2–5 years of prophylaxis are established as safe/resistance-free. Qualified asymptomatic-bacteriuria advice after renal transplant by the IDSA first-month evidence gap.
- **Citation repair:** an acidifier-page “systematic review” claim about urine pH previously pointed to a coronary-angiography contrast-nephropathy review. Replaced it with the actual primary urinary-acidifier crossover study; renumbered citations after removing unused references.
