## Source-access limitations

Most rare-fistula reports are small observational series or case reports. Abstract verification is identified separately from full manuscript/section reads. Current EAU, ASCRS, AGA and WSES passages are applied to their actual populations. Unverified operative minutiae are not converted into instructions. Detailed unresolved notes remain in the JSON.

The colovesical review corrects the anti-TNF14-patient denominator, Chen74 surgical cohort, Foley-only bladder management after bowel resection, mixed robotic fistula cohort and OTS4/8treated outcomes. The 2026 Zimniak proposed diagnostic pathway is identified as hypothesis-generating; its inaccurate historical Melchior table values were not adopted.
