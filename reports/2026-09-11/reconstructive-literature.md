# Reconstructive urology and functional male-outlet evidence update

Research and editing date: September 11, 2026. This is a targeted editorial update, not a comprehensive clinical review or systematic review of the entire specialty.

## Scope and method

The principal interval was **June 28–September 11, 2026**, anchored to the last substantive content/media update rather than the September handoff save. Earlier omissions were also checked when a current guideline or recently indexed paper materially affected existing content. Male urethral and ureteral reconstruction, bladder/diversion anastomoses, AUS, fistula and sexual reconstruction were searched; BPH procedures were subsequently added because of the 2026 AUA guideline and newly published HoLEP review. Female pelvic-floor topics were assigned separately.

Searches combined official EAU/AUA sources, PubMed-indexed original studies and corrections, publisher pages, and the shared Europe PMC screening inbox (`literature-inbox/latest.json`). The shared inbox contained 1,212 metadata candidates across the site's broader scope; this agent did **not** clinically verify every record. Targeted title/topic, publication-date, trial/review, and correction screening helped prioritize checks. Primary PubMed XML records were used when the web interface returned an access challenge. Complete publisher abstracts were distinguished from full articles, and guideline PDFs from secondary summaries. Search-engine snippets, press releases, device publicity, meeting abstracts, and metadata alone were not used to establish new numerical treatment recommendations.

Publication dates require manual reconciliation. A 2026 issue can contain a 2025 online article. The tamsulosin trial's publisher gives July 6, although the metadata inbox reports July 1; AUA's August issue papers appeared online May 7. This is a reason for human screening, not a reason to discard the collector.

## Important remaining verification limits

**AUA 2026 BPH recommendations:** the [current official guideline page](https://www.auanet.org/guidelines-and-quality/guidelines/bph-guideline) was verified, but the full recommendations and download are login-gated. Public official publication abstracts establish the edition, scope, and evidence-search end date (December 15, 2025); they do not reproduce every eligibility range or recommendation grade. Third-party rehosts and AUA news prose were not used to reconstruct exact recommendations. A clinician with legitimate guideline access should complete a procedure-by-procedure reconciliation for HoLEP/AEEP, Aquablation, PUL, WVTT, iTIND, Optilume BPH, PAE, TURP, and simple prostatectomy. Current EAU recommendations added here were checked in the official full PDF. Older AUA statements remain explicitly historical, not newly certified as current.

**PHOENIX penile-prosthesis registry: superseded rates withdrawn; corrected endpoints still need reconciliation.** The [July 23 correction](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41443-026-01235-6) states that incorrectly registered data from one center were removed: **38 patients, cohort 1,072 to 1,034**, with numerical changes. The accessible publisher notice was checked, but corrected endpoint details are subscription content. Existing `penile-implants/index.mdx` and `penile-implants/complications.mdx` cite the original DOI 10.1038/s41443-025-01080-z. Both pages now display the correction notice and withhold the superseded early-event percentages. No corrected rates were invented. The original reference year/issue information was also clarified. A clinician with access to the corrected tables should restore verified endpoint-specific numbers when available.

**Dutasteride/tadalafil trial correction:** [August 13 notice](https://doi.org/10.1111/bju.70417), PMID 42592907, corrects [Lee et al.](https://doi.org/10.1111/bju.70163), first online February 10, 2026. The correction record is verified, but its substantive content is not. Exact-title/DOI searches did not identify a current site citation to that trial. Do not add or summarize its results until the correction is read.

**Reference integrity:** the conduit paragraph removed in this batch also did not agree with the currently displayed original paper's outcome figures. Separately, the publisher's expression of concern concerns reference reliability. Both problems illustrate that a resolvable DOI, numerous references, and passing citation lint do not certify a claim. High-priority follow-up is claim-to-source checking, including title/author/DOI identity and corrections, before converting narrative evidence into practice documentation.

## Follow-up priorities for reliability and practice use

1. Reconcile PHOENIX corrected numbers and obtain legitimate full AUA 2026 access before presenting those exact numerical details as current.
2. Normalize every comparative outcome table: design, population, denominator, outcome definition, follow-up, loss to follow-up, uncertainty, and funding. Trial surgical retreatment, medication restart, claims reoperation and spontaneous device reports are different measurements. The PUL table is now labeled, but the broader BPH matrix still needs substantive harmonization.
3. Audit dosing, device instructions and operative parameters separately from literature summaries. Aquablation prototype-era hemostasis wording was found during this targeted pass; that warrants checking other inherited equipment descriptions.
4. Preserve one source of truth per claim. The AUS device page now directs detailed AUSCO outcomes to the procedure hub. Future guideline changes should trigger review of disease, drug, device and technique companion pages together.
5. Recheck forceful statements still outside this batch, such as categorical female-AUS radiation contraindication language and sweeping superiority claims in technique-variant tables. Their presence is not validated by this update.
6. Build smartphrases from clinician-reviewed decisions and editable patient-specific placeholders, not copied pooled outcome tables or automatically generated operative findings. This update prepares evidence, not a complete practice-ready order set.
