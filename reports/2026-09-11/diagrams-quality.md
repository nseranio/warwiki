# Diagram quality audit and implementation

September 11, 2026. This supersedes the earlier five-figure sample report.

**All 55 SVGs now have an individual editorial/source disposition, accessible text equivalent, view/population and units, declared scale, version, source-access record and explicit pending clinical review. All 55 were rendered and inspected as contact sheets; all visible text bounding boxes were checked in headless Chromium. This is not named clinician approval of 55 operative drawings.** Exact operative-plane validation remains open where the source record says so.

## Clinical and geometric corrections

- **POP-Q and male pressure-flow:** retained the earlier corrected sign convention, stage II ±1 cm range, TVL-dependent III/IV limit, Aa/Ap anatomical definitions, male-only BOOI >40 / 20–40 / <20 thresholds and hypothetical-data labels. [ICS standards](https://www.ics.org/Publications/ICS%20Standards%202020-2021.pdf)
- **BMG placement:** redrew the three panels as explicitly simplified graft–bed interfaces, with direct apposition instead of a apparent suspended graft; removed the unsupported 66:34 preference statement. The Asopa full-text access was inconsistent and is recorded as such. [Barbagli technique](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15745501/), [Asopa technique](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4708270/)
- **Z-plasty:** the previous vertices actually made 120° arm-to-central-limb angles despite 60° labels. Corrected the equal-limb geometry and calculated ideal gains with `100 × (sqrt(5 − 4 cos θ) − 1)`: approximately 24%, 47%, 73%, 99%, 124%. Removed an unsupported clinical 50–70% claim. The geometric limit is distinct from living-tissue gain. [Authored procedural source](https://www.aafp.org/afp/2003/0601/p2329.pdf)
- **Slings and stricture:** removed a universal single-incision least-pain ranking and a spongiofibrosis-depth-only selection rule; moved crowded urethral labels. [FDA route overview](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/urogynecologic-surgical-mesh-implants/stress-urinary-incontinence-sui), [EAU stricture principles](https://uroweb.org/guidelines/urethral-strictures/chapter/disease-management-in-males)
- **Ureteroenteric comparison:** removed superseded 3.7% versus 0% stricture figures from the image, completing the earlier article correction. Bricker/Wallace are now presented as constructions, without an outcome rank.
- **Urodynamics:** synthetic flow curves no longer label low plateau as diagnostic of obstruction or 15 mL/s as a universal normal cutoff; sensation volumes are explicitly invented examples. [ICS good urodynamic practice](https://www.ics.org/folder/standardisation/current-ics-standardisations/d/international-continence-society-good-urodynamic-practices-and-terms-2016-urodynamics-uroflowmetry-cystometry-and-pressure-flow-study/download)
- **Other qualifications:** removed universal bladder-rupture rules, psoas/Boari antireflux requirements, incision-risk ranking from mixed cohorts, VVF mandatory excision/interposition, implant reservoir/pressure prescriptions, graft-take guarantees, fixed catheterization schedules and Peyronie length-preservation promises. Separated double from spiral Monti variants, removed a fixed TUU vascular crossing rule, corrected SSLF fingerbreadth-to-centimeter equivalence and replaced a fixed USLS ureter distance with anatomical variation. Priapism drawings no longer imply larger windows improve outcomes; T-shunt geometry now depicts a T, and proximal shunts are labeled historical/limited evidence.
- **Perineal map:** relabeled a misleading literal coronal slice as an expanded male compartment map. Deep-pouch tissue is not portrayed as a uniform striated muscle sheet. The relevant new anatomical study involved ten elderly cadavers. [Kampelmann study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13069139/)

## Reusable implementation

- `src/data/figures.json` is the figure/source registry; `src/data/figures.ts` provides a typed lookup for original and Docusaurus content-hashed local SVG paths. Unknown and remote images retain the original Docusaurus renderer. The registry gzips to approximately **16.3 kB**; there are no figure-time remote requests.
- `src/components/ClinicalFigure/` adds an image button, an accessible text/source panel and a native enlarged dialog. Full-size mode allows horizontal inspection on narrow screens. Escape, close and backdrop handling restore focus; the viewport is keyboard focusable. Existing article alt text and adjacent captions remain supported.
- `src/theme/MDXComponents/index.tsx` routes registered Markdown images through the component. Its outer markup uses phrasing elements so Markdown's surrounding paragraph stays valid during server rendering and hydration. The modal is portaled into the body.
- All 55 generators call `scripts/diagrams/lib/metadata.js`. Every exported SVG has a short root, title, full description, JSON provenance record and visible version/source/review footer. Full source URLs, access limitations, authorship uncertainty and licensing uncertainty travel with the downloaded vector. No named illustrator or reuse license was invented.
- `scripts/diagrams/lib/quantitative.js` shares BOOI thresholds/boundaries with the nomogram and computes the Z-plasty table. Regression tests include the **actual rendered Z arm angle**, BOOI boundaries, registry coverage and provenance.
- Figure captions/alt were aligned in **39 noncanonical pages**. Existing internal links and inline reference suffixes were retained. The seven practice-topic canonical pages and the OASIS pair were reserved for the clinical/root agents.

## Remaining review limits

The registry explicitly distinguishes checked full-text guidelines, checked abstracts, a source merely mapped, and inconsistent/full-text access. A guideline can support an operation's principle without validating every drawn suture or tissue plane. The pediatric guideline is mapped at landing-page level; detailed construction still requires chapter/technical review. Priapism's AUA page returned a tool access error and remains mapped, not falsely marked fully read. Normal-adult penile anatomy has only a partially applicable hypospadias imaging source in this pass. Full operative verification remains important for these entries, the BMG ureteral wrap, complex bowel channels, and nearby neurovascular hazards in pelvic suspension.

All clinical-review records remain `pending`, with reviewer and date `null`. The site does not claim that an identified clinician approved this collection. Exact sources and limitations are exposed with each figure so the next clinician review has a concrete starting point.
