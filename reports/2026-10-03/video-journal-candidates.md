## Excluded

- **Primary oncology, stones and male infertility** (about 145 UVJ titles: partial and radical nephrectomy, prostatectomy, RPLND, PCNL and ureteroscopy, varicocele).
- **Pediatric-patient videos** whose operation has no adult-identical WARWIKI page (LUAA reimplant, infant pyeloplasty, ureterocele incision, posterior urethral valves, distal hypospadias, primary epispadias and exstrophy repair). RALIMA is in tier B because the technique is the adult one.
- **Cancer cystectomy diversion videos** (Codex proposed several for the ICUD page, which already has 3 videos).
- **Already embedded:** UVJ single-port toolbox, SP bladder diverticulectomy, PATIO VVF repair.

## Method

Crossref metadata for 20 journals (UVJ in full; others from 2015, filtered to Creative Commons licences and video or technique wording); OpenAlex abstracts for 327 of 405 UVJ articles; a scope filter; independent matching against the 1,102-page inventory by Claude and by Codex; HEAD checks of every proposed MP4; page scans of 1,208 IBJU and FVVO articles for hosted video; YouTube and Vimeo oEmbed checks. Working files (off-git): the session scratchpad `vj/` directory.
