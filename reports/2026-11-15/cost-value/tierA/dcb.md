## 2. Section text

## Cost and Value

Model-based analyses estimated that paclitaxel-coated balloon dilation cost less than usual endoscopic management over 5 years in recurrent urethral stricture, and the Ontario assessment rated the effectiveness evidence behind its model as low quality. The Ontario Health (Quality) health technology assessment (published 2026) built a probabilistic 5-year Markov model from the Ontario public-payer perspective (2025 Canadian dollars, 1.5% annual discount rate) comparing paclitaxel-coated balloon dilation with usual care, a mix of urethral dilation and DVIU, in men with recurrent bulbar stricture. The balloon cost $1,476.44 less per person (95% credible interval −$3,217.15 to $112.40) and was cost-saving in about 97% of simulations; results were sensitive to the balloon's effectiveness, the time horizon and device cost. The 5-year budget impact of public funding for 2,747 men was net savings of about $0.74 million. The assessment found no head-to-head comparison of the balloon with DVIU or with urethroplasty.<sup>[[33]](#ref33)</sup>

A cohort Markov model for the English NHS (5-year horizon, NHS and Personal Social Services perspective, 2019/2020 prices; funded by Laborie Medical Technologies, with two authors affiliated with the company) estimated that Optilume cost £2,502 less per patient than endoscopic management for recurrent anterior stricture (£6,620 versus £9,122), and £243 less in a scenario analysis against urethroplasty. Optilume was cost-saving in 93.4% of 1,000 probabilistic iterations, and varying inputs one at a time changed the result only for the monthly probability of symptom recurrence after endoscopic management.<sup>[[34]](#ref34)</sup> The Ontario assessment's account of the NICE external assessment of the same manufacturer model reported savings of £2,510 versus endoscopic management with procedures split evenly between outpatient and hospital settings, and £1,877 with all procedures as hospital day cases.<sup>[[33]](#ref33)</sup>

For DVIU and dilation versus urethroplasty, see [Cost and Value on the DVIU page](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/dviu#cost-and-value).
