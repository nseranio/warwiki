## Section text: obstetric.mdx

## Cost and Value

Surgical repair versus no surgery was estimated at $54 per DALY averted in a lifetime Markov model from Uganda's National Health System perspective (US dollars; published 2018); microcosted repair was $378 per procedure, and modeled lifetime disability for a hypothetical 20-year-old woman fell from 8.53 to 1.51 DALYs.<sup>[[54]](#ref54)</sup>

A Fistula Foundation retrospective program analysis across 143 hospitals in 27 countries estimated $58 per DALY averted for 13,235 vesicovaginal and rectovaginal fistula repairs performed in 2021–2022, from $7.6 million in direct treatment expenses and 131,433 modeled DALYs averted (published 2024); the effect estimate used country-specific average life spans and counted only repairs that achieved closure with continence.<sup>[[55]](#ref55)</sup>

Complex obstetric fistula repair cost more than simple repair in a Mozambican provider-perspective retrospective costing analysis of hospital data collected in 2021 (Mozambican meticais; published 2024): the mean cost per patient was MZN 21,145.68 (USD 344) for complex repair and MZN 14,937.21 (USD 243) for simple repair.<sup>[[56]](#ref56)</sup>

## Section text: female-fistula.mdx

### Cost and Value

Vaginal vesicovaginal fistula repair cost less than open abdominal repair in a retrospective, single-surgeon UK NHS cohort of 47 women (healthcare-provider perspective, 2015 pounds; operative and admission costs with 30-day readmissions assessed): mean costs were £3,381.50 and £4,608.69, respectively, with first-attempt closure in 91% and 86%.<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup> Transvaginal repair also had lower hospitalization costs than transvesical repair in a Chinese retrospective cohort of 57 women (published 2023): 17 underwent transvaginal and 40 transvesical repair, with first-attempt closure in 82.3% and 75%, respectively, over a mean 18.5 months of follow-up.<sup>[[9]](#ref9)</sup>

## Notes

- The Ugandan model's abstract does not state a price year; “published 2018” is used. The full text specifies a 2016 exchange rate, but that is not explicitly the price year.
- The Fistula Foundation authors are all affiliated with the foundation that funded the repairs. Its analysis uses direct treatment expenses and country-specific life spans; the abstract does not name a formal payer perspective or price year, so neither was inferred.
- The Mozambique study reports 2021 data collection but does not explicitly designate 2021 as its price year. Its USD conversions use a stated exchange rate; the section retains the original meticais and the study's reported USD values.
- The UK study's full text explicitly uses a UK NHS healthcare-provider perspective and costs as of December 2015. The Chinese study reports a hospitalization-cost comparison but no cost amounts in its English PubMed abstract. Its cohort was mostly postsurgical fistulas, with one difficult-labor case.
- PMID 25911172 (Barone 7- versus 14-day catheter RCT) rejected for cost prose: no cost outcome reported; its clinical results already appear on the obstetric and trial pages.
- PMID 22542213 (10- versus 14-day catheter RCT) rejected: its abstract predicts lower cost but reports no measured costs.
- PMID 36458065 (Indian transvaginal versus minimally invasive comparison) rejected: it asserts lower cost without reported costing methods or cost figures in the full text; comparator data came from a separate review.
- PMID 29859118 (Nigeria/Uganda interviews) rejected for this section: qualitative access and transport costs, not an evaluation of repair or prevention strategies.
- No fistula-specific economic comparison of prevention strategies was identified in the targeted searches; the obstetric page's prevention section remains unchanged.
- Formal PubMed efetch checks for `Retracted Publication`, `RetractionIn`, `ExpressionOfConcernIn`, and `ErratumIn` were attempted for all five cited PMIDs, but `eutils.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov` did not resolve in this sandbox. PubMed records and targeted retraction/erratum searches surfaced no notice; **formal status and errata remain unverified**.
- Crossref resolution was attempted for all five DOIs, but `api.crossref.org` did not resolve in this sandbox. Each DOI matches PubMed and its publisher's article metadata; **Crossref checks remain pending**. Run `npm run refs:new` after applying the draft and before publication.
