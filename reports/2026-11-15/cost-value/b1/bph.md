## Cost and Value

HoLEP was the most cost-effective base-case procedural option in a US Medicare payer lifetime microsimulation (published 2026): for 30–80 cc prostates, HoLEP cost $8,431 and produced 12.25 QALYs versus $6,557 and 11.98 QALYs with water vapor therapy, or $6,931 per QALY gained with HoLEP versus water vapor therapy; for 80–150 cc prostates, HoLEP cost $8,361 versus $19,012 for simple prostatectomy, with 12.25 versus 12.26 QALYs.<sup>[[24]](#ref24)</sup> In a US commercial-insurance claims cohort of 25,407 men treated from 2015 to 2021 (published 2024), water vapor therapy had the lowest adjusted index-procedure total health care cost at $2,637; among procedures with extended follow-up data, HoLEP had the lowest combined index and five-year cost at $31,926 and prostatic urethral lift the highest at $36,596.<sup>[[25]](#ref25)</sup>

HoLEP had similar median global hospitalization cost to TURP for prostates of 70 cc or less and lower cost than open simple prostatectomy for larger glands in a prospective Italian single-center analysis of 151 men (published 2020): €2,151.69 versus €2,185.61 for HoLEP and TURP, and €2,174.15 versus €4,064.97 for HoLEP and open prostatectomy.<sup>[[26]](#ref26)</sup> GreenLight PVP cost less than TURP in a prospective, nonrandomized three-center Ontario study of 140 PVP and 24 TURP patients (six-month cost-utility analysis, 2011 Canadian dollars, published 2013): CA$3,891 versus CA$4,863 per patient, with 0.448 versus 0.441 QALYs.<sup>[[27]](#ref27)</sup>

PAE was the most cost-effective option among PAE, prostatic urethral lift, Aquablation, water vapor therapy and medical management in a US Medicare ambulatory-surgery-center payer model (five-year Markov analysis, published 2023): $64,842 per QALY gained with PAE versus medical management, and PAE was favored over each minimally invasive procedure in probabilistic pairwise comparisons.<sup>[[28]](#ref28)</sup> PAE had lower cost and more QALYs than TURP in a five-year post hoc cost-utility analysis of a single-center randomized trial from the Spanish National Health System perspective (published 2023): €4,117.13 and 4.572 QALYs with PAE versus €4,297.58 and 4.487 QALYs with TURP, with reintervention rates of 12% versus 0%.<sup>[[29]](#ref29)</sup> In a UK NHS retrospective cost-utility analysis of the observational UK-ROPE cohort (133 PAE and 31 TURP patients, 12-month horizon, published 2021), PAE cost less but yielded fewer QALYs than TURP, with £64,798.10 saved per QALY lost with PAE versus TURP.<sup>[[30]](#ref30)</sup>
