### Section text

## Cost and Value

Extended low-molecular-weight heparin (LMWH) became cost-effective as postoperative VTE risk rose in a US health-system, 1-year decision-tree model of major oncologic abdominal surgery (2013 US dollars): compared with 7 inpatient days alone, 21 additional days after discharge had lower cost and more QALYs when VTE risk exceeded 2.39%, and was preferred at $50,000 per QALY when risk exceeded 0.88% with generic LMWH or 1.22% with brand-name LMWH.<sup>[[34]](#ref34)</sup> A US Markov model of four weeks of enoxaparin after primary debulking for stage IIIC ovarian cancer (published 2012) reported modeled cost savings at 5 years.<sup>[[35]](#ref35)</sup>

For minimally invasive staging of clinically early-stage endometrial cancer, no extended pharmacologic prophylaxis was less costly and more effective than 28-day apixaban or enoxaparin in a US decision model (published 2023) informed by a retrospective cohort of 844 patients, whose observed VTE rate was 0.36%.<sup>[[36]](#ref36)</sup>

Among patients undergoing gynecologic cancer surgery, apixaban had lower aggregate cost and better modeled outcomes than enoxaparin in a US short-term decision model of 1,000 patients (published 2020).<sup>[[37]](#ref37)</sup> In the Society of Urologic Oncology survey already cited on this page, respondents reported cost and insurance barriers for apixaban.<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup>

### Section text

### Cost and Value

Alvimopan lowered modeled postoperative ileus costs after radical cystectomy in a US cost-consequence analysis alongside the randomized phase 4 trial: using 2012 Medicare reimbursement rates and drug acquisition costs, Cubist Pharmaceuticals-affiliated authors found $2,340 less in ileus-related costs per patient with alvimopan versus placebo (p = 0.04), while the $2,640 reduction in total combined costs did not meet their significance threshold (p = 0.068).<sup>[[41]](#ref41)</sup> A US healthcare-payer decision tree (published 2013) estimated ileus-related costs of $1,495 with routine alvimopan versus $1,597 without it per cystectomy patient, assuming a 15.6% baseline ileus rate and a 50% relative reduction with treatment; the model found savings if ileus prolonged admission by at least 3.5 days, occurred in at least 14% of patients, or treatment reduced relative risk by at least 44%.<sup>[[42]](#ref42)</sup>

Compared with alvimopan, naloxegol had lower reported costs in a US single-center retrospective ERAS cohort (published 2022): among 117 cystectomy patients, the reported difference was $344.20 per day, equivalent to $2,065.20 over a 6-day hospital stay, while median length of stay was 6 days with each drug and ileus occurred in 14% and 17%, respectively.<sup>[[43]](#ref43)</sup>
