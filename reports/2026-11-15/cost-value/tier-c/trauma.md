## 2. Section text

### Bladder Trauma

```mdx
### Cost and Value

Selective cystography reduced projected testing expenditure in a randomized prospective blunt-trauma study (published 1993): restricting cystography to patients with gross hematuria, versus testing for any hematuria or pelvic fracture, would have saved $26,322 in the study cohort; bladder injuries occurred only in the gross-hematuria group.<sup>[[17]](#ref17)</sup>
```

### Acute Urinary Retention

```mdx
### Cost and Value

Alfuzosin before and after a successful TWOC lowered modeled costs in a UK NHS 6-month decision analysis using clinical and resource-use data from a randomized trial (published 2005): alfuzosin 10 mg daily during hospitalization for BPH-related AUR and for 6 months after a successful TWOC saved £349 versus placebo and £892 versus immediate prostatectomy.<sup>[[68]](#ref68)</sup>
```

### Fournier's Gangrene

```mdx
## Cost and Value

Preoperative CT was associated with similar reported costs to proceeding to debridement without CT in a single-center retrospective comparison of 76 patients (published 2023): $130,000 ± $102,000 versus $142,000 ± $152,000 (p = 0.37), with no significant difference in time to surgery.<sup>[[28]](#ref28)</sup>

After debridement, negative-pressure wound therapy and conventional dressings had similar total treatment costs in a 10-patient comparison (published 2009); the negative-pressure group had fewer dressing changes.<sup>[[29]](#ref29)</sup>

For patients needing fecal diversion after debridement who had preserved sphincters and no rectal injury, a 48-patient retrospective comparison (published 2015) associated a bowel management catheter with lower hospital expense and fewer additional operations than colostomy.<sup>[[30]](#ref30)</sup> A systematic review and meta-analysis of 27 studies (1,482 patients; published 2021) found higher total hospital cost and more procedures among patients who underwent a diverting stoma than among those who did not.<sup>[[31]](#ref31)</sup>
```

### Priapism

```mdx
## Cost and Value

Admission accounted for most US hospital charges associated with priapism in a Nationwide Emergency Department Sample analysis of 2006–2009 encounters (published 2013): mean charges were $1,778 for an ED encounter ending in discharge and $41,909 for an admitted encounter; estimated annual charges were $123.9 million, 86.8% from admissions.<sup>[[34]](#ref34)</sup>

Treatment before acute malleable penile prosthesis placement generated an estimated mean $83,818 in Medicare-based costs in a 14-man US public-hospital priapism series (published 2015); all patients were discharged within 24 hours after implantation.<sup>[[44]](#ref44)</sup> A six-patient early soft penile prosthesis series (published 2021) reported a device cost of €1,769 and a surgery-related National Health System reimbursement of €3,856.75.<sup>[[45]](#ref45)</sup>
```

## 6. Notes

- Retraction check: NCBI efetch XML for all nine cited PMIDs (16104911, 8507053, 18789410, 37857761, 33864128, 26730041, 25536880, 34839644, 23841493) showed no `Retracted Publication`, `RetractionIn`, `ExpressionOfConcernIn`, or `ErratumIn`. No errata found.
- DOI check: Crossref returned HTTP 200 for all seven new DOI-bearing references and existing priapism ref34. Fuhrman 1993 has no DOI in its PubMed record.
- Industry ties: none of the cited PubMed records showed an author affiliation with a maker or a funding statement naming one. The Ozturk abstract names the VAC maker as background, which is not a disclosed tie. The Annemans first-author email domain mentions IMS Health, but the PubMed record does not establish manufacturer affiliation or funding.
- The alfuzosin study is a decision model using clinical and resource-use data from a randomized trial, not a direct within-trial analysis. Its abstract reports no cost price year, so the section says "published 2005."
- The Fuhrman study evaluated who underwent cystography, not CT versus fluoroscopic cystography. It is an older randomized prospective study with an embedded cost analysis; the section reports its historical cohort result and does not turn gross hematuria alone into a current imaging rule.
- The Fournier cost comparisons report no cost price year or formal perspective. The Ganapathy abstract does not identify the type of cost behind its dollar figures. The Ozturk and Eray abstracts provide cost direction without amounts. The Sarofim review's higher stoma cost is observational and may reflect patient selection.
- The two priapism prosthesis studies are small uncontrolled series. Tausch reports preimplant resource costs, not a direct early-versus-delayed implant ICER. Palmisano reports device cost and reimbursement, not a treatment comparator. The section states only those measurements.
- **No section: renal-trauma.mdx.** PMID 33009338 and PMID 34392984 examine repeat imaging and interventions, without economic outcomes. PMID 32022507 includes hospital costs in full-text tables but compares two centers and does not provide a decision-relevant cost evaluation of RAE versus surgery or NOM. Other NOM/nephrectomy papers use length of stay as a cost proxy.
- **No section: testicular-torsion.mdx.** Adult imaging and transfer searches found no qualifying economic evaluation; pediatric-only torsion studies were outside this rollout's adult focus.
- **No section: penile-fracture.mdx.** A claims study reports injury-associated spending, without an economic comparison of imaging, surgery timing, or nonoperative care.
- **No section: damage-control-gu-trauma.mdx.** Available damage-control comparisons report clinical outcomes without GU-specific costs; the PFUI economic section already covers its own decision.
- **No section: trauma-assessment.mdx.** REACT-2's whole-body CT evaluation concerns general severe trauma rather than a GU-specific imaging or diversion decision.
- **Rejected: 40808897 (Fournier).** National Inpatient Sample cost comparison is Fournier versus nonperineal necrotizing infection, not a care choice on this page.
- **Rejected: 2025 Ramadhan et al. Fournier NPWT cohort.** Its abstract reports numerical wound-material costs, but full-text table ranges and standard deviations are internally inconsistent.
- **Rejected: 2022 delayed-primary-closure AUA abstract.** Conference abstract only, without a full journal article.
- **Rejected: 21683318 (Fournier).** Spanish-language original article; the English record is a translated abstract of a non-English study.
- **Rejected: 10992026 (bladder).** Narrative CT cystography article says it avoids the additional cost of conventional cystography but provides no measured economic comparison.
- **Rejected: Belgian alfuzosin cost analysis.** Conference presentation, not a full journal article.
