## Section text

## Cost and Value

Obliterative repair was associated with lower median total cost than laparoscopic repair in a US Medicare retrospective claims cohort of 11,779 women aged 65 or older (2011 to 2017 procedures, 2018 US dollars): $6,294 for colpocleisis or vaginectomy, $6,766 for vaginal reconstructive repair and $8,347 for laparoscopic repair.<sup>[[29]](#ref29)</sup> In a Japanese retrospective cohort of 890 prolapse operations (2013 to 2017, published 2021), operative labor and consumables cost $180 for vaginal hysterectomy with colpocleisis, $1,500 for laparoscopic hysterectomy with uterosacral ligament suspension and $1,589 for laparoscopic sacrocolpopexy, in US dollar amounts reported by the authors.<sup>[[30]](#ref30)</sup>

For low-risk women undergoing LeFort colpocleisis, no routine endometrial evaluation had superior cost-utility to transvaginal ultrasound or endometrial biopsy in a US decision model from the health plan perspective using Medicare charges (2012 US dollars, published 2014) at willingness-to-pay thresholds of $50,000 and $100,000.<sup>[[31]](#ref31)</sup>

## Notes

- PubMed efetch checked PMIDs 35662357, 33496049 and 24763159: no `Retracted Publication`, `RetractionIn`, `ExpressionOfConcernIn` or `ErratumIn`; PMID 35662357 has a `CommentIn` record, not an erratum. Crossref returned HTTP 200 for all three DOIs.
- The Medicare cost categories are laparoscopic (including sacrocolpopexy), vaginal reconstructive and obliterative (colpocleisis or vaginectomy); they are not a colpocleisis-only comparison. The paper reports a price year but does not name a cost-aggregation horizon or formal economic perspective; those were omitted. The Japanese study reports operation labor and consumables, not total hospital cost; its abstract gives no price year, so the text uses publication year.
- No qualifying published cost comparison of LeFort versus total colpocleisis, or colpocleisis with versus without concomitant hysterectomy, was identified. The Kandadai model concerns preoperative endometrial evaluation before LeFort colpocleisis, a separate decision discussed on the page.
- Pessary-versus-surgery: Ben et al. 2024 (PEOPLE trial economic evaluation; pessary-page ref 23) does not identify colpocleisis as a separate surgery arm; Hullfish et al. 2011 (pessary-page ref 24) models vaginal reconstructive surgery, not colpocleisis. Their results remain on the pessary page and are linked, not repeated.
- Raina et al. 2023 (home-page ref 4) compares complications with and without hysterectomy but reports no cost outcome; excluded from the cost section.
- Baban et al. 2024, PMID 38113134, compares chargemaster listings rather than published treatment costs or cost-effectiveness; excluded.
- Arunkalaivanan and Ghosh, ICS conference abstract comparing colpocleisis and pessary costs, was excluded as a conference-only abstract.
- Bretschneider et al. 2022 reports in its open-access full text that one author consults for Boston Scientific and another for Renalis, and reports NICHD support. The PubMed record shows no maker affiliation or manufacturer funding statement, so no industry attribution appears in the section under the brief's rule.
