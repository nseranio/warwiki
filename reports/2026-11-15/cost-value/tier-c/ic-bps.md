## 2. Section text for the home page

## Cost and Value

Intravesical hyaluronic acid/chondroitin sulfate (HA/CS) had an incremental cost-effectiveness ratio of €3,735–€8,003 per QALY gained versus DMSO in a 6-month, randomized, open-label multicenter trial of 110 women with IC/BPS (direct healthcare costs; published 2017); two authors were affiliated with IBSA, maker of the HA/CS product.<sup>[[65]](#ref65)</sup> In the Austrian open-access health system, a pharmacoeconomic comparison of intravesical hyaluronan with oral pentosan polysulfate (PPS), amitriptyline and nonspecific symptomatic therapies (published 2013; 1-, 5- and 10-year horizons) estimated hyaluronan to have the lowest total treatment costs at each horizon under standard assumptions and sensitivity analyses.<sup>[[66]](#ref66)</sup> The Canadian Urological Association guideline weighed the high cost of oral PPS alongside its limited benefit and potential visual harm when making its conditional recommendation against use.<sup>[[28]](#ref28)</sup>

US commercial and Medicare Supplemental claims associated IC/BPS with nearly twice the all-cause billed healthcare costs of matched controls: a matched-cohort analysis (published 2026; follow-up after diagnosis) reported $20,288 versus $10,222 per patient-year, respectively (rate ratio 1.98).<sup>[[67]](#ref67)</sup> An Allergan-funded US MarketScan claims analysis (published 2017; first 12 months after diagnosis) associated IC/BPS with $7,223 higher adjusted total healthcare costs than matched controls and Hunner lesions with $6,895 higher costs than non-Hunner IC/BPS.<sup>[[68]](#ref68)</sup> In a US claims analysis of 749 beneficiaries younger than 65 (published 2006; first year after diagnosis), mean direct medical costs were 130% higher than in matched controls; among the 152 patients with disability data, indirect work-loss costs were 84% higher.<sup>[[69]](#ref69)</sup>

## 6. Notes

- PubMed efetch XML for PMIDs 27654012, 23921624, 42578322, 28345436 and 16445303 showed no `Retracted Publication`, `RetractionIn`, `ExpressionOfConcernIn` or `ErratumIn` entries. No errata were reported for these cited papers.
- Crossref returned HTTP 200 for all five new DOIs. The first request for 10.1002/nau.23091 returned HTTP 429 and resolved to 200 on retry.
- PMID 27654012: the PubMed abstract names Ialuril/IBSA, and the XML gives two authors' affiliations as IBSA R&D. PMID 28345436: the PubMed COI statement names Allergan funding and an Allergan-employed author. PMID 42578322 has Purdue Pharma and Knoa Pharma author affiliations, but the record does not establish that either makes an option compared in this section.
- Price years are absent from the abstracts, so monetary figures use publication years. The HA/CS trial abstract reports direct healthcare costs but no named payer, formal perspective or country; none is inferred. The Austrian model abstract reports no currency totals or formal perspective. The claims studies report billed or direct costs rather than a formal economic perspective.
- Barua et al., PMID 26590137: considered but excluded. Its cross-trial pharmacoeconomic comparison uses heterogeneous response definitions and is less decision-ready than the direct HA/CS–DMSO trial.
- Stanford et al., PMID 18585523: considered but excluded. Oral-agent cost associations come from treatment cohorts without a causal treatment comparison in the abstract.
- Lin et al., PMID 30073771: considered but excluded. Taiwanese reimbursement versus rheumatoid arthritis does not compare IC/BPS treatment choices.
- Ludwig et al., PMID 31899034: considered but excluded. It evaluates PPS-associated maculopathy, not the cost or cost-effectiveness of screening.
- PubMed searches found no qualifying economic evaluation specific to IC/BPS onabotulinumtoxinA, Hunner lesion fulguration, sacral neuromodulation, heparin/lidocaine, hydroxyzine or cyclosporine. The two pharmacology pages receive only pointers, with no separate Cost and Value section.
- The reviewer's `npm run refs:new` remains the post-application gate; it was not run on the read-only repository.
