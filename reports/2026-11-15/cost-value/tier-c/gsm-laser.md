## Section text: Vaginal Laser Therapy

## Cost and Value

A 1-year US decision tree for GSM-associated moderate dyspareunia, from the patient-cost perspective (model results in 2017 US dollars; published 2020), favored fractional CO₂ laser at a $50,000-per-QALY threshold: the ICER was $16,372 per QALY gained with CO₂ laser versus oral ospemifene, using a $2,733 Stanford out-of-pocket price for three laser sessions.<sup>[[67]](#ref67)[[32]](#ref32)</sup> A 1-year UK NHS and personal social services Markov model (published 2024) favored estriol at a £20,000-per-QALY threshold: the ICER was £183,260 per QALY gained with CO₂ laser versus estriol, with the laser effectiveness estimate drawn from two trials involving 88 women and the three-session laser cost estimated from private-sector prices.<sup>[[68]](#ref68)</sup>

## Section text: GSM

## Cost and Value

In NICE's 1-year UK NHS and personal social services Markov model (published 2024), estriol had lower cost and more QALYs than nonhormonal moisturizer/lubricant and a 60.0% probability of being preferred at £20,000 per QALY; when local vaginal estrogen was excluded, prasterone ranked first at £2,548 per QALY gained with prasterone versus moisturizer/lubricant.<sup>[[25]](#ref25)</sup>

A US retrospective claims analysis by Duchesnay-affiliated authors (published 2019) reported lower mean all-cause health care costs among ospemifene users than users of local vaginal estrogen formulations.<sup>[[26]](#ref26)</sup> CADTH's 10-year Canadian publicly funded payer reanalysis of a Markov model (published 2022) estimated ospemifene to cost $175 more and yield 0.001 fewer QALYs than a weighted mix of local vaginal estrogen formulations.<sup>[[27]](#ref27)</sup>

Among women who were not candidates for local vaginal estrogen, a lifetime NHS Scotland Markov model by authors including a Shionogi employee (published 2021) estimated £14,138 per QALY gained with ospemifene plus nonhormonal moisturizers/lubricants versus moisturizers/lubricants alone; its probability of cost-effectiveness was 89% at £20,000 per QALY.<sup>[[28]](#ref28)</sup>

The [Vaginal Laser Therapy](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/vaginal-laser-therapy#cost-and-value) page compares the laser models.

## Notes

- Retraction/erratum check: NCBI efetch XML for PMIDs 32562659, 39637093, 31432687, 38713788, 32390072 and existing ref 32 (35484706) showed no `Retracted Publication`, `RetractionIn`, `ExpressionOfConcernIn` or `ErratumIn` flags or linked corrections.
- Crossref: DOI endpoints for 10.1016/j.ajog.2020.06.032, 10.2217/cer-2019-0091, 10.1007/s40258-020-00589-2 and existing ref 32 (10.1111/aogs.14353) each returned HTTP 200. The NICE and CADTH evidence reviews have no DOI in their PubMed book records.
- Wallace's accepted full text lists a Stanford grant to Sokol from Cynosure, but PubMed's efetch record contains no maker affiliation or COI/funding statement. Under the brief's PubMed-record rule, the page prose does not label that tie. Dymond has a Shionogi B.V. author affiliation, and Faught has Duchesnay Inc. author affiliations in their PubMed records.
- The NICE report calls its model “NICE 2023” internally; the public evidence review is dated November 2024. Its base case is 1 year and its sources use mixed cost years, so the section uses “published 2024.” Its laser effect estimate was based on CO₂ trials; it is not an Er:YAG model.
- The CADTH analysis reworked a sponsor-submitted model. Its comparison is ospemifene against a weighted mix of local estrogen products. The source labels the amount with `$` but does not spell out the currency or a price year, so the section preserves `$` and uses “published 2022.”
- VeLVET and Li 2021 *JAMA*: efficacy trials without trial-based economic evaluations; not cited for cost.
- Ippolito 2025 Cochrane SUI review (PMID 40709601): its economic search found no evaluation comparing vaginal laser with other SUI treatments; no SUI cost text is proposed.
- Mortensen 2022 (existing ref 32): narrative review quoting the $2,733 model input; retained only as the existing citation alongside Wallace's primary model.
- Recurrent-UTI prevention cost models were excluded because that decision already has a Cost and Value home on the recurrent-UTI page.
- `npm run refs:new` remains for the reviewer after applying this draft to the pages; no repository page was edited here.
