# Full texts to download (November 12, 2026)

For Codex. Download each item below as a PDF. **Legitimate access only:** Jefferson library or institutional login, publisher or society sites, PubMed Central or Europe PMC, or the owner's accounts. No third-party uploads (Scribd, ResearchGate copies, course folders, shadow libraries). Never open auanet.org. Skip anything that needs a purchase and record it as blocked.

When done, write `DOWNLOAD-STATUS.md` in each folder: one line per item, `got` or `blocked` with the reason (paywall without Jefferson access, not found, purchase only). Every arrival is checked against each page that cites it, and the figures trimmed for lack of a full text are restored where the text supports them.
