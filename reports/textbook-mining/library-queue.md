## Order and why

Book 1 is fixed as the pilot regardless of rank (already an established
decision). Books 2 onward are ordered by expected yield: how much a
dedicated, current, specialty-focused source is likely to add beyond what
WARWIKI's guideline-driven audit already captured. Broad/older/general
references and pure-anatomy or general-operative atlases rank lower because
WARWIKI's most-built sections (urethroplasty, AUS/IPP) already have deep,
guideline- and IFU-sourced coverage from the audit -- the marginal new
detail from a general textbook there is smaller than from a current,
narrow monograph on a comparatively less-built section (neuro-urology,
fistula, complications-specific).
