## Not verifiable

- **TFUUG2-080-001**: the chapter's "vaginal exposure in 5% of patients at ten-year follow-up" attributed to Ford et al.'s Cochrane review could not be confirmed against that review's actual abstract (PMID 26130017), which reports an overall erosion rate of ~2.1–2.4% without a ten-year-specific stratification. Rejected.
- **CFIPRS-014-002** (partial): the chapter's "mean pain scores decreased from 7 (±1.7) to 3.5 (±3.3) at a mean 6.4-month follow-up... trend favoring thigh dissection over vaginal excision" figure, attributed by the chapter to "Rigaud and colleagues," does not match Rigaud 2010's actual abstract (PMID 20639025: n=32, not 8; VAS 7.3±1.5→3.4±3; mean follow-up 10 months, not 6.4; no TOT-vs-TVT subgroup comparison for pain in the abstract). This specific figure was dropped; the rest of the finding (technique description, Reynolds 2012 outcome) was adopted with verified sourcing.
- **TFUUG1-037-004** (partial): the chapter's printed "vesicovaginal fistula among complications in 24–48%" and "80–99% short-term success" figures, attributed to Karim et al. 2020, do not match that review's actual abstract (PMID 31468095: complication rates 24%/28% with ~21% of each being SUI, no VVF rate stated; success 67%/92% laser, 80%/98% endoscopic). These specific figures were dropped/replaced with the verified abstract numbers.
