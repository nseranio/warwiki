### Deferred questions (B)

1. **q-flap.mdx — Morey et al. 2000 Q-flap follow-up denominator/duration.** AMUGRS ch. 17 states 13/15 patients followed for a mean 42.6 months (range 12–102), with the other two followed for 6 months and one focal failure at 5 years. TRUS08 ch. 57 instead states 10/13 "excellent" results among patients followed for a mean 42 months, with two of the original 15 lost to follow-up. The PubMed abstract for the primary paper (Morey AF, Tran LK, Zinman LM. *BJU Int.* 2000;86(9):1039-42, PMID 11119098) reports the flap-length and complication data already on the page but no follow-up duration or denominator, so neither textbook account could be verified against it. Someone with full-text access to the original 2000 paper should resolve which denominator is correct before any follow-up statistic is added to the page (findings AMUGRS-017-002, TRUS08-057-004).
