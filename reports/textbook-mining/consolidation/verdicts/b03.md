## Deferred questions

None. All conflicts in this batch were resolved by applying the standing rule that WARWIKI's current, guideline- or recent-evidence-sourced position stands over an older or unsourced textbook-chapter position (APAGS2020-112-001, APAGS2020-119-001, APAGS2020-022-001).

## Not verifiable

None dropped for unverifiability in the adopted set — every new citation was confirmed with `scripts/audit/pubmed.py` (search/pmid) before use, and one self-guessed DOI (Kehrer 2018) and one self-guessed DOI (Fifer 1993) were caught and corrected against the tool's output rather than left wrong. APAGS2020-022-001's unsourced 85%/50% hemodynamic figures were treated as not verifiable and dropped (see rejects above, not carried into any adopted text).
