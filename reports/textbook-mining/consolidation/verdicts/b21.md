## Not verifiable

- **PIS-011-004** (Karpman distal-biologic-cap case series): the chapter's specific "4 patients, 5.5-month average follow-up (range 3–8)" figures are not stated in the PubMed abstract for Karpman et al. 2020 (PMID 31918982), which only says "small sample size and short duration of follow-up." Adopted the technique description and the qualitative outcome ("none had recurrent impending erosion") without the unconfirmed exact counts.
- **PIS-012-002** (Reinstatler review, chronic pain): the "68% of 22 patients" figure is not stated in the review's own PubMed abstract (PMID 28735683), which has no results section (it is a narrative review of the literature). Adopted only the qualitative teaching point (pain persists more often than it resolves after revision/explant for noninfectious pain, with relief mainly when malposition was the cause).
- **PIS-001-001** (Sun transplant cohort): the "infection 4% vs 0%, P = 1.00" figure is not stated in the PubMed abstract (PMID 29656065), which reports comorbidities and the matched reoperation rate (11.5% vs 11.5%, P = 1.00, confirmed) but not infection specifically. Retained given the close match on every other reported figure (n=26/26, reoperation rate, P-values, 2-piece-vs-3-piece finding), but this specific rate could not be independently confirmed from the abstract alone.
- **PIS-001-002** (Çayan 883-patient comparison): the exact percentages (patient satisfaction 99.2%/90.3%; partner dissatisfaction 11.2%/3.8%/3.3%) are not stated in the PubMed abstract (PMID 31155387), which reports the overall comparison as P < .001 without the specific rates. Retained as the chapter states them (plausible given the abstract's own direction and magnitude of effect — 3-piece and 2-piece implants "significantly higher" couples' satisfaction than malleable), but not independently confirmed from the abstract.
- **AMUGRS-047-001** (rejected, not adopted): dropped entirely rather than partially verified, since its only cited source is a 2009 journal-supplement abstract (Sellers/Dineen/Wilson, *J Sex Med* suppl. 2009;6:447) not retrievable through the PubMed tool.

## Corrections to the mining findings

- **WPPPS-003-006** (scrotoplasty dehiscence): the chapter's cited source, a 2015 AUA meeting abstract by "Sulaver et al.," was superseded by the full peer-reviewed paper (Gupta NK, Sulaver R, et al. *J Sex Med.* 2019;16(4):602-608, PMID 30852109). The published paper gives a different, more precise cohort (n=103, not 104) and identifies **two** independent dehiscence predictors — diabetes (OR 6.1) and V-Y plasty scrotoplasty technique (OR 4.9) — contradicting the chapter's claim that diabetes was the only assessed predictor. WARWIKI now cites the published paper with the corrected numbers and both predictors.
- **PIS-008-001** (cadaver measurements): the chapter's Henry et al. figures (decompressed bladder 5–8 cm/mean 6.4 cm/15–25°; filled bladder 1.5–4 cm/mean 2.4 cm; iliac vein 2.5–4.5 cm/mean 3.4 cm/20–57.5°) differ slightly from the verified PubMed abstract (5–8 cm/mean 6.45 cm/15–30° [22.8° mean]; filled bladder 2–4 cm/mean 2.61 cm; iliac vein 2.5–4 cm/mean 3.23 cm/20–60° [36.4° mean]). Used the verified abstract numbers on the page. The chapter's added Trendelenburg-position claim (20° Trendelenburg increasing distended-bladder distance to 2.6 cm vs 2.0 cm) is not in the abstract and was not added to the page.
- **PIS-008-001** (Ziegelmann cadaver locations): the chapter's summary ("45% between internal/external oblique, 10% retroperitoneal, 5% above peritoneum, 5% intraperitoneal") omits the largest subgroup (35% deep to the rectus muscle) and relabels "45% deep to external oblique fascia and lateral to rectus" as "between the internal and external oblique layers." Used the verified abstract's full, more precise breakdown (35% deep to rectus, 45% deep to external oblique/lateral to rectus, 10% retroperitoneal, 5% preperitoneal, 5% intraperitoneal; 20 reservoirs, 10 cadavers) instead.
