One judgment call is flagged above rather than deferred: TFUUG1-001-001 (IUGA founding) technically falls outside the brief's rule 5 carve-out ("established operative technique or anatomy") for textbook-only citation, since it is an organizational-history fact. I adopted it because (a) I independently verified every specific claim against the primary chapter text rather than trusting the finding's paraphrase, and (b) the page's own existing convention already cites organizational websites, not peer-reviewed papers, for the parallel AUGS and ICS founding narratives. If the calling session disagrees, the fix is a two-line removal (the new "International Urogynecological Association" subsection and its one timeline row), since nothing else depends on it.
