- APAGS2020-059-001's specific device dimensions (two 5–6 × 2–3 cm buccal strips, five 2.5 × 6 cm gelatin sponges, 12 × 3 cm fenestrated silicone stent, 7–8-day inspection, 3-month near-continuous stent use) — the Teng et al. 2019 abstract (PMID 31150693) does not mention gelatin sponges, silicone stent dimensions, or these strip sizes, so the chapter's footnote attribution of this specific device protocol to that paper could not be confirmed. Not adopted.
- FGPFR-062-001's Feroze et al. 1975 comparison (PMID 1148138, verified real) was not cited — the newer, more clinically relevant Creatsas 2010 series was sufficient.
- TFUUG1-037-001's ESSIC hydrodistension protocol detail (fill to 80 cmH2O for 3 minutes, then partial refill to 20-50% for inspection): the cited paper (Nordling J et al., Eur Urol 2004;45(5):662-9, PMID 15082211) is real and correctly identified, but PubMed has no abstract indexed for it, so the specific numeric protocol could not be confirmed against the source text. Dropped rather than added on the chapter's paraphrase alone.
