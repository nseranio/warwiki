## Deferred questions

1. **Martius flap pedicle-to-artery mapping (urethrovaginal-fistula-repair.mdx, HINMAN5-079-001).** Two textbooks (HINMAN5 ch. 79 and TFUUG2 ch. 110), written by different author teams, both state that an anterior pedicle is supplied by the internal pudendal artery and a posterior pedicle by the external pudendal artery — the reverse of the current WARWIKI page and of what a different chapter in APAGS2020 (ch. 83, also by Karram) implies. Neither HINMAN5 nor TFUUG2 cites a source for its mapping. This needs a primary anatomic/technique reference (ideally the full text of Wilson 2017, already cited on the sister martius-flap-vvf.mdx page, or a vascular-anatomy atlas) to resolve with confidence before any page is changed. Left unresolved; no page edited for this specific claim.

### Calling-session resolution

- **HINMAN5-079-001: reject (was defer).** The page's mapping (anterior pedicle on the external pudendal vessels, posterior pedicle on the internal pudendal vessels) matches standard labial vascular anatomy (external pudendal branches of the femoral artery supply the anterior labium; posterior labial branches of the internal pudendal artery supply the posterior labium), the Elkins, DeLancey and McGuire 1990 source cited on `martius-flap-vvf.mdx`, and `martius.mdx`. The two uncited chapter statements are reversed; no change.
