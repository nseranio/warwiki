## Not verifiable

- **CWW13-047-001** — the finding's "urinary incontinence prevalence was 40%" (attributed to Verkuijl et al. 2023) is not stated in the paper's PubMed abstract, which gives only fecal incontinence (12%) and constipation (14%) prevalence and reports that urinary incontinence "frequently" co-occurs without a percentage in the abstract text available. Adopted the confirmed 12% fecal-incontinence figure only; dropped the 40% urinary-incontinence figure.
- **AMUGRS-037-001** — the finding attributes "35% success after urethrotomy, mean recurrence at 26 months, open repair in about half by 6.6 years" to Hafez AT et al., *BJU Int.* 2005;95:403-6 (PMID 15679803). The verified abstract of that paper is about one-stage perineal anastomotic *urethroplasty* (not urethrotomy) in 35 boys, reporting 89% initial success and 100% final success after redo — it does not support the finding's numbers. Dropped the specific figures; kept only the general, textbook-attributed teaching points (endoscopic treatment limited to short minimally fibrotic strictures, no repeat endoscopic treatment, delayed anastomotic repair favored for posterior pediatric trauma strictures, transpubic/partial-pubectomy exposure sometimes needed).
