# Questions for the user

Findings the incorporation agents could not settle from current guidelines or
primary evidence. Nothing was changed on the site for these.

1. **Q-flap (Morey 2000) follow-up numbers** (`q-flap.mdx`; AMUGRS-017-002,
   TRUS08-057-004). Two books report the same series differently: AMUGRS says
   13 of 15 followed for a mean 42.6 months with one focal failure at 5 years;
   TRUS08 says 10 of 13 "excellent" at a mean 42 months with 2 of 15 lost to
   follow-up. The PubMed abstract reports neither. Resolving it needs the full
   text of Morey AF et al., BJU Int 2000 (doi 10.1046/j.1464-410x.2000.00974.x).
   The page's existing outcome wording is unchanged.
