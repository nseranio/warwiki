# Questions for the user

Findings the incorporation agents could not settle from current guidelines or
primary evidence. Nothing was changed on the site for these.

1. **Q-flap (Morey 2000) follow-up numbers** (`q-flap.mdx`; AMUGRS-017-002,
   TRUS08-057-004). Two books report the same series differently: AMUGRS says
   13 of 15 followed for a mean 42.6 months with one focal failure at 5 years;
   TRUS08 says 10 of 13 "excellent" at a mean 42 months with 2 of 15 lost to
   follow-up. The PubMed abstract reports neither. Resolving it needs the full
   text of Morey AF et al., BJU Int 2000 (doi 10.1046/j.1464-410x.2000.00974.x).
   The page's existing outcome wording is unchanged.

2. **Proximal dilator size caution** (`surgical-approaches.mdx` or revision pages; see `verdicts/b22.md`). Whether to add a caution against proximal dilators smaller than 9 mm (perforation risk). The only source is a short *J Sex Med* pictorial piece with no indexed abstract to check; the page's usual sequence starts at 8 mm. Not added.

3. **Manual modeling hand position** (`manual-modeling.mdx`; see `verdicts/b22.md`). The current page and a *Penile Implant Surgery* chapter disagree on where the non-dominant hand applies counterpressure (glans versus distal shaft) during modeling over an inflated prosthesis. Settling it needs the full text of Wilson and Delk 1994 and Lucas 2020. The page is unchanged.
