Corrections: Y-V and V-Y are opposite incision-to-closure directions but not interchangeable bladder-wall, cutaneous, meatal and UPJ procedures. The multiple-cutaneous-Y-V length equation cannot predict luminal gain, and no single perfusion, flap-necrosis or full-thickness rule spans these sites. Shamout's 18 post-TURP BNC cases had 100% freedom from re-instrumentation and 61% storage symptoms at mean 14.8 months; ≥2 failed treatments was cohort entry, not a universal definition. Zang is a technical report, not comparative evidence of a leak/time advantage. Abramowitz's 19-person endoscopic mucosal realignment is distinct from bladder-wall Y-V and had median six-month follow-up. Szydełko's 25/25 study found 86% versus 95% success without proof of equivalence; crossing vessels were actually more common in its Y-V arm, contradicting the page's blanket contraindication. Sennert's 55/57 meatal symptom relief is a selected retrospective series with six patients followed under one year. Knoll's 3.5–6.5 cm gain came from **prosthesis plus V-Y plus debulking**, not the isolated flap. Myers reported 31 patients/51 gluteal flaps, 100% survival **with 42% minor complications and two abscess reoperations**. The 1.6% Fournier loss rate is pooled across mixed flap designs. Rouzier's 45/50 dyspareunia improvement is perineoplasty, not V-Y-specific. Post-radiotherapy dilation and flap-versus-graft language was narrowed to what the cited review/case can support.
