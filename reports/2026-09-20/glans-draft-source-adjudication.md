## Source access actually obtained

Current guideline supports resection for staging, suitable organ-sparing options with strict follow-up, frozen section in *cases of doubt* rather than routine use, no fixed safe 5 mm histologic margin, and risk-specific recurrence counseling. Sections on regional/systemic disease were not reviewed for these pages.

## Draft decisions and applied corrections

1. **Retain practical surgery.** Kept resurfacing and glansectomy step sequences, plane anatomy, STSG handling, dressing options, tables, references, and videos. Changed the glansectomy plane label from “standard vs salvage” to corporal-tip assessment, as the current EAU guidance specifies.
2. **Correct guideline/selection overstatement.** Resurfacing is especially useful for extensive, residual, or recurrent PeIN and may suit selected superficial invasion; glansectomy is an option when local excision or resurfacing is unsuitable. The guideline's strong recommendation is for suitable *organ-sparing surgery*, not a blanket first-line endorsement of either individual operation.
3. **Correct the margin and frozen-section rules.** The draft's fixed ≥5 mm threshold and routine/dominant frozen-section language were rejected. EAU reports no universally safe margin width, a retrospective signal at <1 mm tumour-to-margin distance, and weak support for frozen sections when completeness is in doubt. Histologically negative invasive margins remain essential.
4. **Retain the useful Shabbir findings the draft would have deleted.** The 25-person CIS series reported 12/25 positive margins, 7/25 further surgery, and 5/25 unsuspected invasion. These are cohort-specific and do not prove that positive margins are harmless.
5. **Correct mixed-cohort attribution.** Elst's 550-person, 29%-recurrence, 99%-five-year-CSS cohort includes several glans-sparing procedures and is lower risk. It is not a resurfacing outcome estimate and does not show that recurrence is harmless for higher-risk glansectomy. Roussel's 897-person glansectomy cohort showed an adjusted survival association with recurrence.
6. **Correct functional inference.** Falcone enrolled 99, but only 44 were functionally evaluable (12 TGR, 10 WLE, 22 glansectomy). The published within-group IIEF p-values cannot prove causal between-procedure superiority. Parnham did not collect functional or sexual outcomes. The numerical series results remain visible with denominators.
7. **Keep TODGA as a named technique.** Malone's report supports its use and early mobilization in that series; neither “largely replaced quilting” nor equivalent comparative take was verified.
