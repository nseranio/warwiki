# Needles and sutures — targeted shared-source review

September 20, 2026. Both complete current pages, their tables, links and reference lists were read. This is a **partial** clinical/technical source review, not clearance. No imported clinical data, figures or videos occur on either page. The linked barbed-suture deep-dive was reused for the upper-tract caution; its source package was not reread.

## Source access and corrections

- Ethicon's 2015 [needle chart](https://www.ethicon.com/na/sites/na/files/2018-06/needle_true_to_scale_diagram.pdf) was read for the taper/blunt code listings: **CTX is taper; CTXB, CTB and SHB are blunt**. The diagram is a manufacturer catalogue, not a comparative outcome trial. The page's code table and fascial example were corrected. Other needle-model/attached-suture specifications still need product-code checks.
- The [Cochrane blunt-needle review](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD009170_use-blunt-needles-compared-sharp-needles-preventing-needle-stick-injuries-surgical-staff) public summary and methods/results were read: 10 RCTs, 2,961 operations, glove-perforation RR 0.46 and self-reported needlestick RR 0.31. Its studies did **not** test subcuticular skin closure or patient wound outcomes. Full PMC main/tables were attempted but a reCAPTCHA blocked access. Author attribution was corrected to Saarto et al.; fascial advice is conditional on suitable tissue.
- [Potter 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39325023/) original abstract was read: artificial-skin tear-through force BB 4.07 N, PS-3 3.75 N, PC-3 3.26 N. The inherited page reversed the direction and extrapolated to BMG dehiscence; both were corrected. The unrelated 66,397-puncture complication statistics were removed because this skin study contains no biopsy series. [Williams 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26399610/) original abstract establishes 40/107 and 9/107 **scrub-nurse histories**, not per-transfer rates. [Seki 1988](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3277473/) abstract supports a training-model grip difference, not a patient benefit. [Maranhao 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33332606/) abstract supports the retained 26-G atraumatic spinal-needle ranking; this is a spinal, not epidural, comparison.
- Ethicon [PDS II D7438](https://www.ethicon.com/na/epc/code/d7438?lang=en-default), [Vicryl Plus VCP683G](https://www.ethicon.com/emea/epc/code/vcp683g?lang=en-default) and [standard Vicryl J507G](https://www.ethicon.com/emea/epc/code/j507g?lang=en-default) product-specification fields were read. The latter two list polyglactin-910 56–70-day absorption; triclosan does **not** make Vicryl Plus slow-absorbable. Some portal description fields conflict with structured product fields, so final code/region IFUs still need review.
- [Silver 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26979258/) and [Romeo 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37835063/) original abstracts show that knot sequence/material/test context matters: the five-throw plateau in one oral bench experiment cannot override a six-throw monofilament half-hitch finding in another. Their full methods, figures and clinically chosen products remain unread.
- [Jalalzadeh 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40053348/) original abstract reports RR **0.75** (31 RCTs, 17,968 participants), not inherited 0.79; [2022 high-quality-trial review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35644158/) abstract reports no statistically significant difference (OR 0.90), and [SIS 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40285475/) guideline abstract recommends triclosan-coated suture for **abdominal incision closure**, not every urologic anastomosis or infected device field. Full study tables/guideline text remain unread.
- [Anand 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35549514/) abstract gives pyeloplasty redo RR 6.00 (95% CI 0.78–46.14): non-significance does not prove comparable redo risk. The original [Liatsikos Quill series](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23292256/) (5/6 recurrences) and [Radford pediatric V-Loc series](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29116864/) (4/10 adverse drainage, three redos) were checked at abstract level and added to the summary's references. Favorable selected series and useful robotic technique remain on the page. The dedicated barbed page has the deeper source discussion.
- [Dumville 2014 Cochrane](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25431843/) abstract supports no clear SSI difference for selected surgical skin incisions but higher adhesive dehiscence (RR 3.35); the inherited citation was an unrelated regional-anesthesia infection guideline and was replaced. The [Al Saffar 2024 technical paper](https://tau.amegroups.org/article/view/122416/html) main text and Figure 3 caption were read: corporotomy/tunical closure belongs to a **transcorporal salvage AUS variant**, not routine AUS implantation. Its video and original figure image were not inspected; device IFU remains separate.
