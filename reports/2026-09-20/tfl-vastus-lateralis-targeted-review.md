**Corrected claims:** Höckel's inguinal TFL preference and Zelken's flap-selection table are **authors' schemes**, not universal standards. Gandolfi's six SCIA/three IGA injections were not 6/7 and 3/7 *success rates*, and accessory flow after LCFA injury is a feasibility hypothesis rather than guaranteed salvage. Hoch's induced weakness is not postoperative TFL donor morbidity. Nirmal's 75%-versus-17% contrast is an **ilioinguinal subgroup** in a small nonrandomized study; 3/20 TFL groins had minor skin necrosis, hence 17/20 (85%) without it. Wu's bladder-wall use and 3/4 normal voiding are four-case experience. Zelken's urinary leaks (3) and infections (6) span mixed ALT/VL designs; its 3/9 VL-MC congestion is a VL subgroup. Wong's one short-thigh/pedicle-tension failure is a planning caution, not a categorical contraindication. Nelson's 42%-versus-15% major-complication contrast compares 19 **mixed thigh** flaps with 114 VRAM, not isolated ALT-VL. Di Summa's 27% dehiscence is 6/22 followed reconstructions and may require secondary treatment. ALT-only perineoscrotal/phalloplasty findings and the mixed Fournier review were separated from VL-specific effects. Toia's LEFS 67.7-to-67.4 is 23 selected segmental/chimeric harvests; Puladi's >30% and ≥50% muscle-reduction statements arise from **predictive gait simulation**, not validated clinical thresholds. The inherited 89% sensory and gait figures are not asserted as VL rates without population verification.
