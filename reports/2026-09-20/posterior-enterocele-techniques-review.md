**Pages:** Halban culdoplasty and Moschcowitz procedure. Both full current MDX pages, all references, links and absence of imported data/figures/videos were checked. This is a **partial, source-scoped review**, not a surgical protocol or clinical clearance. It builds on the [Halban](../2026-09-19/halban-recovery-review.md) and [Moschcowitz](../2026-09-19/moschcowitz-recovery-review.md) recovery reviews without repeating their source reads.

## Corrections and preservation

- Halban now distinguishes **classic sagittal cul-de-sac closure** from Wu's **modified USL-to-cuff colpopexy**. No trial result or apical benefit is assigned to isolated Halban. The McCall comparison no longer falsely makes rectal serosa a routine fixation target. Cosson supplies a directly described, but combined, sacrocolpopexy adjunct.
- Moschcowitz now identifies the **vaginal** route and actual three-arm trial result, corrects the Baessler follow-up denominator, and drops unsupported current-use dominance, mandatory sacrocolpopexy sequence, one-to-one ventral-rectopexy replacement, comparative ureteral-safety ranking and procedure-specific constipation rate.
- Both pages retain the operative orientation, exposure, tissue-plane and serial-closure teaching, comparison tables, indications, complications and links. Halban retains all 12 inherited references and gained three directly relevant references. Moschcowitz retains eight of nine inherited references and gained two. Its one removed citation, Wald et al. 2021, addresses benign anorectal disorders rather than a Moschcowitz operative claim; its publisher full text returned a paywall, and no claim on this page can honestly cite it. It remains recoverable in Git history. No section, table or media item was removed. The exact **3–5 suture count** was removed because the cited accessible technique source says *several*, without that range. These are specific attribution corrections, not blanket content removal.
- Safety language is anatomy-based: identify the ureters and assess patency according to the entire repair. No numerical Halban-versus-Moschcowitz ureteral-injury inference is made.
