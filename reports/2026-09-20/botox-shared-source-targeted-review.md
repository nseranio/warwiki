## Source access and corrections

- The [AbbVie therapeutic prescribing PDF](https://www.rxabbvie.com/pdf/botox_pi.pdf) was opened as a 52-page document. Its visible header says **Revised: 11/2023** even though the indexed filename/metadata mentions October 2024. Read relevant adult/pediatric bladder dosing and prophylaxis (pp. 3–6), contraindications (p. 13), PVR/CIC and trial-risk Tables 10–12 (pp. 15–16), adverse-event Tables 14/16/17 (pp. 18–19), pregnancy/lactation (pp. 24–25). The [DailyMed listing updated February 2026](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=a6f42753-d31e-4b23-8da0-4b990fee4b38) is a *Sportpharm/Enovachem relabeled* product, not proof of a February 2026 AbbVie label revision; its sections 2.3–2.4, 5.13, 8.1–8.2 and Tables 10–12 were spot-checked against the manufacturer PDF. Adult bladder prophylaxis uses the multi-day non-aminoglycoside template. A single preprocedure IV option under anesthesia belongs to **pediatric NDO**, not the adult template. Pregnancy/lactation are not section-4 contraindications; inadequate human pregnancy data, animal fetal effects and absent milk data warrant individualized counseling. The OAB CIC 6.5%/0.4% and noncatheterizing adult NDO 30.6%/6.7% are separate trial populations, not interchangeable risks.
- The [original ROSETTA JAMA article](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2565290) was read in its accessible HTML: abstract, eligibility/treatment and results including Figure 1 text, six-month outcome and limitations. Its main text reports **386 randomized**, abstract treatment arms **192 Botox + 189 SNM = 381**, and **364** primary-analysis participants. The 200-U trial yielded an exploratory complete-UUI-resolution endpoint of 20% versus 4%, Botox versus SNM; this is not the labeled OAB 100-U comparison. The procedure and adjunct pages now use consistent denominators rather than calling 381 the total randomized.
- [Roman et al. 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41150180/) abstract reports a pooled complete-resolution direction favoring SNM, opposite ROSETTA's six-month exploratory endpoint. The MDPI publisher full text returned 429 and PMC returned a browser check; indexed publisher excerpts contain its claimed result but not a complete accessible Figure 4/study contribution package. The public page retains the study with the specific contradiction and cautions against promoting this pooled endpoint to a universal choice rule. Figure 4 and study-level inputs remain **unresolved**.
- [Liu et al. 2015](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0134803) full HTML methods/results/discussion and Tables 2–3 / Figure 2 captions were read. In a retrospective human-biopsy comparison (16 injected, 11 lipotoxin), suburothelial cleaved SNAP-25 was detected after injection but **not after lipotoxin**; urothelial P2X3 decreased in five lipotoxin responders. The earlier assertion that lipotoxin cleaved SNAP-25 in the urothelium was wrong; the corrected sentence does not infer afferent-only safety from small samples. Actual figure pixels and underlying patient-level data were not reviewed.
- Earlier OAB male/retention and pivotal-source notes in [the recorded review](oab-male-botox-retention-review.md) and [pharmacology page ledger](../2026-09-11/full-site-review/pharmacology.md) were reused where scoped; those records do not validate the other off-label series or all label-adjacent claims.
