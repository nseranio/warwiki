## Source scope and changes

- Plochocki 2016 and Zifan 2018 original PubMed abstracts, **not full articles**, were checked for EAS subdivisions and perineal muscle continuity. The 89.2% independent inferior-rectal origin is now attributed to one cadaveric sample, and the unsupported `>90%` diffusion-tract continuity claim was removed. DeLancey 2024 original abstract and figure descriptions, **not full paper**, support presenting the perineal-complex triad as a proposed model rather than a universal cause of prolapse or SUI.
- Elsevier Complete Anatomy relevant female deep-dorsal-vein entry was read; the inherited statement that the clitoral vein empties into the **prostatic** plexus was corrected to the **vesical** plexus. Male drainage remains separately described. The wider venous and Martius pedicle descriptions still need a full technical-source review.
- Chéhensse 2017 original human spinal-generator PubMed abstract and 2013 systematic-review abstract were checked, **not full papers**. Removed the false claim that a spinal injury *above* L3 usually abolishes ejaculation; L3–L5 involvement predicted penile-vibratory-stimulation failure in the 2017 cohort.
- The RCOG Green-top 29 PDF was directly accessible; its classification and EAS/IAS repair recommendation pages were read, **not all 19 pages**. Corrected 3c/4th-degree OASIS anatomy and the different partial- versus full-thickness EAS repair options. The ASCRS 2023 fecal-incontinence guideline's relevant evaluation and treatment sections were previously reviewed in the pelvic-neuroanatomy batch and reused here; symptoms are multifactorial, not determined by one sphincter defect.
- The three inherited raster figures and local perineal-layers SVG were visually inspected. They were not altered. The male OpenStax image has pre-existing right-edge label cropping at full image view; the SVG labels itself conceptual and clinical-review-pending. Figure provenance and every label were **not** independently clinically cleared.

The page also narrows a nerve-free clock-face injection claim, deterministic cauda-equina/ALS outcomes, automatic pudendal decompression and mandatory flap interposition. Those are safety-boundary corrections, not complete evidence reviews of the respective procedures. Four new references were added; the other 21 were not source-by-source cleared.
