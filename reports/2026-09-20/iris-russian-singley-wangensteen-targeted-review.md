# Iris, Russian, Singley and Wangensteen forceps — targeted review

September 20, 2026. All four complete current MDX pages, use/technique tables and inherited references were read. No imported clinical data, page diagram or video is present. This is **partial claim review, not clinical clearance**.

## Evidence actually read

- **Iris:** [Teleflex model 181610](https://www.teleflexsurgicalcatalog.com/pilling/product/181610-iris-tissue-forceps) direct relevant manufacturer page: straight 1×2-toothed, 4 in/10 cm. Other model surfaces and IFUs were not read. [Kirkup 1996](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2502851/?page=6) online abstract read; its page-image PDF and named-model history were not read.
- **Russian:** [Sklar model 19-2280](https://www.sklarcorp.com/p/sklar-russian-tissue-forceps-8/19-2280) direct manufacturer page: 8-inch cupped, radially serrated thumb forceps marketed for thicker tissue/wound closure. [Cheng/Hannaford 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25408249/) complete PubMed abstract is a liver-tissue finite-element laparoscopic-jaw model, not a Russian-vs-DeBakey clinical study. Full paper/figures unread.
- **Singley:** [FDA GUDID Ambler 33-401RG](https://accessgudid.nlm.nih.gov/devices/00190660073657) full relevant labeler-submitted record: 7-inch serrated fenestrated jaw; [Teleflex P13044](https://www.teleflexsurgicalcatalog.com/pilling/product/p13044-tuttle-singley-tissue-forceps) direct product body: 13 inches, jaw geometry not stated. [Brown 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24366187/) complete abstract concerns fenestrated **laparoscopic parallel-occlusion** jaws, not Singley thumb forceps or clinical serosal injury; full paper/tables/figures unread.
- **Wangensteen:** [B. Braun BD173R](https://surgical-instruments.bbraun.com/en-01/p/BD173R/wangensteen-durogrip-dressing-forceps-tweezers-tc-straight-230-mm-9-jaw-width-6-mm-serrated-cross) direct manufacturer specification: 230 mm, 6-mm **cross-serrated** jaw. This contradicts the old universal 3×3-tooth description. Brown 2014 does not test Wangensteen. Kirkup abstract and indexed historical references do not establish the inherited 3×3 performance comparison or a specific obstruction-mortality reduction attributable to these forceps. Named-model IFU and historic full text remain open.

## Focused correction and preservation pass

Iris: corrected universal default, fixed length and unsupported cosmetic/trauma ranking; kept fine-closure applications and technique. Russian: kept cupped/radial-serration description and uses while removing a fictitious comparative tissue-injury ranking and guaranteed no-crush ureter/renal handling. Singley: kept fenestrated design, open reconstruction applications and peritoneal-entry teaching, but separated model lengths and bench fenestration retention from unproven “no crush” clinical claims. Wangensteen: corrected the central 3×3 identity error throughout design, comparison and technique; retained broad handling applications, cautions and historical context without repeating an unsupported mortality number.

No heading, comparison table, inherited reference or media was removed. Manufacturer descriptions support the specified models, not a mandatory instrument policy; abstract-only biomechanical work cannot settle patient outcomes. **Open next source action:** inspect exact local model IFUs and the relevant full biomechanical/historical articles if stronger handling or comparative statements are needed.
