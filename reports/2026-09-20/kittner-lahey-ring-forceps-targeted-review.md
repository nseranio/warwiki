Braun's 3×3 “Lahey Tenaculum Forceps” contradicts the page's one-hook naming rule. Corrected the clinic's founding city to Boston and separated RLN history from unsupported instrument-invention causality. Retained uses, limitations, comparisons and six inherited references, with specimen integrity and delicate tissue cautions. Complete historic/technical papers and local model IFU remain open.

Andrews' Allis-tenaculum trial cannot prove ring-forceps cervical bleeding, pain, or “atraumatic” superiority. Preserved practical prep, packing, sponge-stick, handling and comparison sections. Exact model IFUs, full ACOG/formulation labels and procedure-specific cervical-grasp evidence remain open.
