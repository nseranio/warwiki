# Adjacent fixation and suture-device review — 2026-09-21

Four complete current pages were read. This cluster corrects a dangerous site-of-attachment conflation: Sebastian's 64/41/15 N experiment fixed PVDF tape to a **porcine cervix**, not a sacral promontory. Jansen's separate **porcine sacral-ligament** PVDF experiment measured 65 N for two sutures, 25 N for three ProTack tacks in a row, and 38 N for three in a triangle (n=10 each). Neither predicts clinical success. The prior Moroni “level III equivalence and pain” verdict was not that review's result. Yoshizawa compared **sutures+tackers versus sutures**, while Vieillefosse's tacker-only cohort found an observational pain-intensity association without a significant time or pain-incidence difference. Vaginal anchoring and glue RCTs do not test the sacral anchor. Technical anatomy and references remain available; unsafe universal tack-count, zone, pain-mechanism and device-preference assertions were narrowed.

The 5-mm RD180 IFU (P/N 022227F, all six pages, including use sequence and warnings) specifies compatible RD QUICK LOAD sutures, a normally retracted needle, a squeeze–release pass followed by a separate remote rearm cycle after clearing tissue, single-use handling and urinary foreign-body warning. Its name does not support a 180° articulation claim. The manufacturer product page and September 17, 2026 revision letter say the 5-mm RD180 SKU 021100 is being revised to RD360 Plus; this is **not** labeled a recall. TI-KNOT insert P/N 030639N (all six pages, loading, firing, warnings) specifies a separate single-fastener quick load, single-**patient** use and up to 12 firings, not a one-fire handpiece. Dash/Warner's full technical article was read for the mucosa-to-mucosa RD180 bites and the authors' four-month cystoscopy/possible exposed-clip calcification. Kaldany's full publisher HTML and Figures 11–12 **captions** describe a slightly bent 5-0 PDS needle with straight shaft/short tip, grasped parallel with a second driver for retrieval; not a deep full-curve needle. The existing operative video was retained; its timestamp/content was not independently watched.
