Specific corrections: the Tokyo definition includes subcutaneous-pedicled and supercharged as well as perforator-pedicled designs; its meeting was in 2009 and publication in 2011. The 1.6% Fournier flap-loss estimate is a mixed-flap pool, not a propeller rate. The posterior-thigh PU-revision example is one patient, not proof of general reliability. Shin's 16 patients/27 flaps describe the new PPSF cohort; comparator size and survival cannot be silently combined. Witte's 1,988 patients span all reconstructed indications, whereas the 91% rectus figure applies to 910 APR patients and is not comparative superiority. Sisti's complications span body regions. Chaput's apparent supercharging benefit comes from nonrandom lower-limb groups; Pérez's heparin report is specialist salvage of mixed congested flaps with substantial transfusion, not routine prophylaxis. Huang's size findings concern distal lower limbs, and its flap-to-defect ratio association was nonsignificant.
