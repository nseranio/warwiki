## Focused source/diff findings

- **Primary closure:** Muallem's original open-paper indexed Table 2 reports 51 primary closures and 126 reconstructions, median resected tumor diameters 23 and 45 mm, and grade-III healing disorders **7/51 versus 16/126** (p=1); these group medians are not size thresholds. The Dutch Delahaije open-paper indexed results report **184/394** wound complications and **167/394** breakdowns, with reconstruction preferentially used for larger, recurrent and anatomically difficult tumors; its reported adjusted OR 1.2 (95% CI 1.1–1.2) is observational, not proof of procedural harm. Mullen's original indexed abstract establishes a retrospective premalignant-excision cohort, not a randomized antibiotic rule. Dias-Jr's original abstract/PDF-indexed text reports ~30 versus ~50-day mean healing in small selected groups. The Toulouse paper was relabeled an author algorithm, not an international mandate. Source details: [Muallem](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8997096/), [Delahaije](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12153637/), [Mullen](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30870300/), [Dias-Jr](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31482981/). Original full PDFs, figures and supplements were **not** systematically read in this pass; retrieved source text was original abstract/table excerpts indexed by search.
- **Grafts:** [Lavoué original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24090498/) supports 13 selected operations, 31% occult invasion and normal-range sexual function **among FSFI-assessed patients**, not universal recovery. [Dainty original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15896830/) is a seven-case fibrin/NPWT report; “up to 22%” is background conventional-graft context, not its own failure rate. [Lee 2014 original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22958590/) is 26 mixed-sex perineal patients, retrospectively assigned by surgeon. [Cao 2022 original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36161796/) is an 86-person mixed-site RCT: overall take 97.2% versus 90.2%, irregular/high-mobility subgroup 97.6% versus 81.7%. [Tremblay 2026 original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41615004/) describes **two male patients** after necrotizing infection; it cannot prove vulvar rehabilitation efficacy. The 2025 16-RCT meta-analysis and general graft mechanics were reused at the exact abstract/source scope in [the prior STSG/FTSG review](stsg-ftsg-claim-check.md), not reread or promoted. NPWT is an option for a difficult bed, not universal standard care.
- **Atlas:** Reused documented Kwong, Toulouse, FGM/C, LS and flap source reviews without duplicate full-text research. [Kwong original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40162503/) gives **69 women/136 mixed flaps**; day-7 126/136 and days-8–30 113/136 are pooled flap outcomes, while sexual activity 5/54→11/45, no incontinence 26/54→37/46 and diagnostic acceptance 40/45 use respondent subsets. No primary-closure comparator exists. [WHO FGM/C fact sheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/female-genital-mutilation) states >230 million. The [prior FGM/C review](fgm-fat-foldes-manero-targeted-review.md) documents that Almadori's 38/40 studies concern clitoral reconstruction, mostly Foldès-style—not 95% of patients; Meremikwu pain OR 79.67 is uncontrolled before/after; Mañero n32/FSFI 16→29 has its own [original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29528921/) and was previously mis-cited to Negosanti. The [WHO 2025 recommendations](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK614880/) support individualized Type-III deinfibulation timing and selected, counseled clitoral reconstruction. The [prior LS review](defibulation-perineoplasty-principles-targeted-review.md) supports individualized maintenance and cohort-specific relapse denominators. The [prior VRAM review](vram-omental-peritoneal-targeted-review.md) and [Eseme original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36077834/) support a proportional between-series donor-site comparison, not a randomized flap ranking. A wrong IPAP→SCIP link was fixed. Published technique options and routes remain, now identified as location-dependent examples rather than mandatory first-line choices.
