## Focused source/diff findings

- **Balloon dilators:** the prior page conflated plain urethral and ureteral balloons, the Optilume urethral DCB and the separate Optilume BPH system. FDA labeling now anchors the urethral DCB as semi-compliant, 0.038-inch-wire compatible, available in 18–36 Fr and 30/50 mm, with 3.5 µg/mm² paclitaxel. Universal material, pressure, hold-time, safety and outcome claims were removed. The AUA DCB recommendation is correctly limited to selected recurrent bulbar strictures shorter than 3 cm.
- **Filiforms/followers:** current BD and Teleflex configurations replace universal material/length assumptions. Subjective loss of resistance and urethral length are no longer accepted as bladder confirmation; objective drainage, endoscopic, contrast/fluoroscopic or ultrasound confirmation is required before dilation. The multiple-filiform maneuver is retained only as historical teaching, not a contemporary safety recommendation. Heyns and Cochrane outcomes are confined to the older strategy rather than a modern product claim.
- **Guidewires:** Clayman, Torricelli, Amasyali, Sarkissian, Pedro and Graversen findings are explicitly labeled bench/ex-vivo evidence. Torricelli found Glidewire variants needed relatively high puncture force but also produced the highest friction, contradicting the old universal “lowest-friction/safest” framing. The selection table is now a compatibility and task framework, “preoperative RUG” was corrected to retrograde pyelography, prophylactic ureteral stents are selective rather than injury-prevention guarantees, and safety-wire evidence is presented as a debated selective practice.
- **S-shaped dilators:** current Cook IFU/product facts anchor the 0.038-inch wire, 8–20 Fr set, individual 22–26 Fr sizes, hydrophilic preparation and 37-cm/sideport configuration. The page no longer says guidewire use eliminates false passage, ranks devices by unsupported safety/cost, or mandates blanket antibiotics. Objective intravesical confirmation and minimal dilation needed for the intended catheter are emphasized.
