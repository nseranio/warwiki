# Posterior thigh, PMTP and SCIP — targeted partial review (September 20)

Read all three current MDX articles, references and embedded tables (no local imported clinical data, media or figures). These are **distinct vascular designs**: posterior thigh may use descending IGA/PFA territories, PMTP rotates on a PFA perforator, and SCIP uses SCIA branches. The September recovery reports for posterior thigh/PMTP and the preceding island-groin/MCFAP/medial-thigh source report were reused only at their documented abstract scope.

## Checked source packages and changes

- [Friedman 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20220559/) original abstract: 27 posterior-thigh flaps/19 complex patients, 26 transfers, 53% early wound-healing complications and seven secondary procedures. Narrowed unsupported attribution of all early morbidity to irradiated patients rather than the operation; the design still teaches abdominal avoidance. Full technique/outcome tables remain unread.
- [Kwon 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33675678/) original abstract: eight 180° PFA-perforator PMTP perineal repairs, all survived without major complication, all donor sites primarily closed. [Wishart 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32839109/) original abstract and prior recovery record: 21 vPMT flaps/12 patients, with mixed genital/perianal sites and transfer modes. Corrected pooled “all PMTP propeller” framing and universal every-thigh anatomy; retained operative teaching and all comparisons, scoping nonmatched outcome cells.
- [De Gelder 2025](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC12105959/fullTextXML) openly available primary article: read complete XML main body including methods, outcome subsections, Tables 1–2 text and figure captions. Actual figures and the three linked design supplements were **not** visually reviewed. In 55 patients, the 14% complete urethral-loss figure is **8/55 overall**, not 8/37 unilateral urethras. The unilateral ALT-shaft/SCIP-urethra group had six complete losses (6/37), four partial losses and 14/37 fistula/stricture interventions; bilateral SCIP had two complete urethral losses among eight patients. The ten shaft-only SCIP flaps had no complete loss but one partial tip loss. Standing voiding was 32/37 after further interventions in unilateral urethra and 5/8 bilateral, versus an 82% combined summary; the paper's “32 (89%)” subgroup percentage is arithmetically inconsistent with 32/37 (86.5%), so the page retains the count/denominator and flags the discrepancy. The authors report their center moved away from routinely pairing ALT shaft with SCIP urethra after December 2019; the old page had recommended it as the “best” adjunct. This is an important selection correction, not evidence to withdraw SCIP as an option.
- Reused [D'Arpa 2019 original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30688908/) for 93 mixed ALT phalloplasties and the distinct urethral subgroup complication percentages; its full tables are pending. Reused the immediately preceding [island-groin/SCIP source record](island-groin-mcfap-medial-thigh-targeted-review.md) for Goh/Gentileschi/Jeong **abstract-only** coverage. Replaced the false Gentileschi “~101 flaps, four donor complications” attribution: the relevant cohort is 32 vulvar patients/34 SCIP flaps, with three patients experiencing wound complications in the abstract, not a donor-site event count. The accessible Toulouse 2025 record does not establish a universal first-line SCIP or PMTP ranking; full algorithm text remains inaccessible.
