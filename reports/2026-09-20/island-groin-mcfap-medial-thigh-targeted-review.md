# Island groin, MCFAP and medial thigh — targeted review (September 20)

Complete current MDX reads: 339, 294 and 240 lines; 44, 30 and 16 retained/new references respectively. No locally imported clinical figure, data module or video. Reused only the documented abstract scope of earlier [island-groin](island-groin-flap-recovery-review.md), [MCFAP](mcfap-flap-recovery-review.md) and [medial-thigh](medial-thigh-flap-recovery-review.md) recovery reports; their blanket removals are superseded. The related [Singapore](igap-singapore-labia-targeted-review.md) and [ALT](anterolateral-thigh-targeted-review.md) work remains partial, not inherited clearance.

- **Historical source correction:** [Franchi et al. 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11479417/) full open main text (through references; no separate figure or supplement) rereads Taylor/Daniel's operation: their 1973 free ilio-femoral flap used **SIEA** when SCIA was too small. The former page assigned it to an SCIA island-groin flap and called it the first free transfer of any tissue. The original [1973 record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4200573/) has no abstract and its full text was not accessed. A distinct #44 reference was added without removing the 43 inherited entries.
- **Island-groin outcomes:** [Goh 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25357163/) original abstract: 210 **SCIP** flaps across body sites, 176 lower limbs, ten losses, two donor complications and five debulkings; its “thinnest” language is the authors' conclusion, not universal comparative proof. [Sahai 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33906811/) abstract: 29 scrotal patients with satisfactory outcomes but no stated 100% flap-survival endpoint. [Gentileschi 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34866009/) abstract: 34 SCIP flaps in 32 selected vulvar patients, **three patients** with wound complications, not proof of universal feasibility. [Jeong 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41885534/) abstract: venous anatomy in 22/30 cadaver specimens and two losses among 64 clinical cases **without venous congestion**. [Alammar 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41720016/) systematic-review abstract: 619 patients/625 **mixed** Fournier flaps, 1.6% pooled loss; its named common flaps were medial thigh, pudendal thigh, ALT and gracilis, not SCIA groin. Full operative/series tables and review subgroup tables were not accessed.
- **MCFAP/anatomy:** [Hallock 2003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14595180/) four selected groin cases and separate conjoint muscle branch; [Peek 2009](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19182616/) original 43-dissected-limb/14-case abstract distinguishes musculocutaneous and septocutaneous perforators; [Eom 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21285777/) 40 **freestyle** upper-thigh flaps, 90% MCFA origin, one-third musculocutaneous, two-thirds septocutaneous, 39 survivors. [Tomaszewski 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26966661/) meta-analysis abstract (4,351 limbs) reports the **MCFA's own** parent origin, distinct from the **gracilis pedicle's** parent. PMC indexed sections were encountered but direct full opening challenged, so full tables/figures/supplement were **not** read. [Coskunfirat 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21346527/) original seven-case abstract does not establish the title's superiority as a comparative result. [Izumi 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23896164/) 15 breast flaps included **three true-perforator and twelve muscle-sparing** designs; aggregate pedicle/weight and absence of donor seroma must not become universal true-MCFAP specifications. Full operative figures/tables remain open.
- **Shared medial-thigh claims:** [Wang 1987](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3602166/) original abstract has 20 cadaver limbs and 15 flaps/13 patients for the proximal communicating plexus; it does not prove tolerance of arbitrary vascular compromise. [Huang 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25596518/) original abstract: **27 mixed perforator flaps in 16 women, only eight MCFAP**, all survived, three **patients** had wound debridement/closure. [Arquette 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34510081/) original PAP abstract: eight of 15 patients had **any complication**, with three donor and three recipient dehiscences, rather than 53.3% minor wound dehiscence. [Soper 1989](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2812660/) 21 short-gracilis flaps in 11 patients, ten excellent caliber/depth and one bilateral necrosis after irradiation/chemotherapy; this is a **muscle-bearing** flap, distinct from MCFAP. [Weitgasser 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8396873/) indexed abstract gives TMG outcomes but direct full text challenged; no complete TMG source package read.

The focused diff/source pass corrected cross-family attribution, specific denominators, unsupported superiority/default rankings and fixed-width/irradiation universals.
