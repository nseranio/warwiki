# aOAP, bipedicled scrotal flap, and Cecil-Culp — targeted review

Complete current MDX reads and focused source/diff review, 2026-09-20. This is **partial claim verification**, not clinical clearance. Reused the narrow Cecil-Culp corrections in `restoration-correction-review.md`; did not repeat its historical source search. Practical anatomy, operative teaching, cross-links, and references were retained.

| Page | Verified correction | Open source boundary |
|---|---|---|
| aOAP | O'Dey 2010 abstract supports the 20 cadaver specimens, 80/20 perforator anatomy, 1.3 ± 0.3 cm landmark, and ~7 × 15 cm territory; that cadaveric presence is not a clinical guarantee. O'Dey FGM/C abstract supports 119 enrolled, 36% aOAP, 94.1% one-year attendance, and **p < 0.001** combined-program symptom/sensation results; no flap-specific causal estimate. O'Dey LS abstract supports 61 treated and 53 bilateral aOAP flaps. De Luca 2023 review says LS diagnosis is usually clinical and biopsy is indicated for uncertainty, failed treatment, or suspected neoplasm, not universally mandatory. Unsupported “same flap” GCIPF equivalence and cross-series superiority rankings were narrowed. | O'Dey full texts, operation figures/tables/supplements, GCIPF original pedicle/design description, and long-term LS recurrence by flap remain unread. No aOAP-specific complication or durability rate inferred from the combined FGM/C cohort. |
| Bipedicled anterior scrotal | Fakin abstract reports 43 siliconoma patients, 9% partial necrosis, 12% donor hematoma, 19% partial disruption, and 4.37/5 mean satisfaction; “100% flap survival” obscured partial necrosis. Lumbiganon indexed abstract supports a small retrospective single-/two-stage comparison without significant infection/dehiscence/reoperation differences. The 2025 open systematic review (full relevant methods, study table, pooled complication table, discussion/limitations read) found 32 case reports/series, 368 patients, no low-risk-of-bias study and no valid method comparison proving superiority. Hair distribution and stage choice are individualized; no blanket claim that a contaminated bed requires staging or that flaps outperform grafts in sexual function. | Fakin, Lumbiganon, Mendel, Pribaz/McLaughlin, Yao, and EPAP full operative texts/figures/supplements remain unread. Exact Fakin dissection dimensions/plane, two-stage interval, variant outcomes, and patient-specific wound/vascular thresholds require primary technical verification. |
| Cecil-Culp | Marshall 1979 abstract locates 8% among **234 operated patients in its center**, not all people with hypospadias. Ehle 2001 abstract reports no recurrence in **15 selected boys** at mean 21 months (range 1–62); no general “100% success” estimate. Weiss 2017 is one atypical suprapubic trauma adaptation. Clarified that fistula coverage, urethral substitution, and skin replacement do not share universal tubularization, catheter, or stage-timing steps. Preserved the AUA lichen-sclerosus genital-skin restriction from the existing restoration review. | Original Cecil/Culp and later procedure full texts, figures, exact drainage/timing regimens, comparative posterior-stricture cohorts and modern BMG outcome tables are not fully re-read; the page remains partial and its historical steps are not a universal operative protocol. |
