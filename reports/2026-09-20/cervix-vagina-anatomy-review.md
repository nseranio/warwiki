# Cervix and vaginal-anatomy audit — September 20, 2026

Two complete current MDX pages, all inherited bibliography entries and the vaginal page's Blausen image were read. This is a **partial claim audit**, not clinical clearance. The earlier recovery reports were used only as provenance; their blanket-removal conclusions are superseded by the content-preservation policy.

## Source use and corrections

- **Cervix:** The original [ASCCP 2023 endocervical-curettage standard](https://doi.org/10.1097/LGT.0000000000000710) supports risk- and colposcopy-specific sampling, not mandatory postmenopausal sampling at every screening visit. The original [2017 Querleu–Morrow classification](https://doi.org/10.1245/s10434-017-6031-z), including its C1/C2 sections and Table 1, identifies **C1 as nerve-preserving and C2 as non-preserving**; the inherited page reversed them. The [ESGO/ESHRE/ESGE pocket guideline](https://www.esgo.org/media/2025/05/Pocket-Guidelines_Fertility-sparing-in-gynae-cancers.pdf) was checked for fertility-sparing selection; its underlying guideline is a 2024 publication despite the pocket PDF's 2025 URL. The [EAU fistula section](https://uroweb.org/guidelines/non-neurogenic-female-luts/chapter/disease-management) supports individualized repair and a weak interposition recommendation for radiated fistulas, not universal flap failure/success. The [Timmons cervical-remodeling review](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tem.2010.01.011) supports increased distensibility in ripening; an isolated contrary wording in its text warrants reconciliation rather than treating it as an outcome study. The inherited fixed ureter-distance, injury-frequency and visualization guarantees were scoped as approximate anatomy, not definitive safety claims.
- **Vagina:** The [Barnhart MRI original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16478763/) reports a 62.7-mm cohort mean; the inherited range came from a different cast study and cannot be attached to that mean. The [Rahn 2023 IMPROVE original main article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10427941/) was retrieved as complete HTML and its design, results, main tables, limitations and figure captions inspected; the 12-month composite failure estimate was **19% estrogen vs 9% placebo** (adjusted HR 1.97, 95% CI 0.92–4.22), while surgeon-rated tissue appearance improved. Its full supplement and actual figure images were not adjudicated. The [2024 36-month follow-up abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38710269/) also reports no significant surgical-success benefit; its main text is not credited read. The [2025 Zhou pessary trial's complete main/tables](../2026-09-12/prolapse-source-reading/principles.md) and [NICE NG123 sections 1.6–1.9](../2026-09-12/prolapse-source-reading/principles.md) were reused from the documented reading; Zhou's figure/protocol/appendix remain open. The EAU fistula section was checked for approach and flap scope. The actual Blausen image is a **frontal schematic**, not the inherited “sagittal section,” so its alt text and caption were corrected.
- Both pages retain their substantive anatomy, physiology, operative-context sections and all inherited references. No video or imported clinical dataset is present. The vaginal image is retained. New sources are numbered after existing bibliographies; no inherited citation was removed.
