# Paravaginal repair: scoped source correction (20 September 2026)

The complete current [paravaginal repair page](../../docs/04-surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/anterior/vaginal-paravaginal-repair.mdx), all its sections, tables, 29 references, related links and linked original SVG were read before and after editing. The figure source registry and generated SVG metadata/footer were checked; the schematic itself was visually inspected. **This is a partial claim review, not clinical or figure sign-off.** No page heading, operative sequence, named variation, reference or media was deleted. The three misleading aggregate comparison rows were replaced with source-scoped interpretations; the original study-specific results remain on the page.

- [Richardson's original PubMed record/abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7465150/) confirms the 233-procedure historical SUI/cystourethrocele series and reported >95% functional result at 2–8 years. The page, figure registry and generated SVG formerly linked PMID **7464134**, an unrelated testosterone-hydroxylase paper. All current source links now point to **7465150**. Richardson full text remains inaccessible through the linked Ovid route; the schematic remains explicitly conceptual, not a universal cystocele mechanism or clinical validation.
- The accessible [Arenholt 2017 review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5429977/) was read in its anatomy, operative-comparison, examination and imaging discussions: two operative-validation populations found paravaginal defects in **up to 47%**, with inconsistent examination sensitivity/specificity. It does not support a universal 60–80% prevalence, dismiss all ultrasound, or make retropubic inspection a routine diagnostic test. [Chen 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5218807/) abstract/discussion supports the 83% **within-model variation** from 30 cases and 30 controls; this is not prevalence or causality. The [published correction record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28092304/) was identified, but its text was not accessible and the actual MRI figures were not reviewed. The [Arenholt 2019 abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30506182/) reports **46** six-month completers, **20** isolated and **26** combined operations, and **39% recurrence for the cohort**, not for a pubococcygeus-defect subgroup.
- [Rude 2021 original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32711009/) supports the named four-lateral/four-midline PDS lattice description, 109-patient single-surgeon cohort, 12/109 POP-Q Ba recurrences, 19% conservatively managed transient retention and 4% de novo SUI. Its actual Figure 1/full text was not obtained (publisher landing accessible; Europe PMC full-text endpoint 404). The former table compared its outcomes against incompatible colporrhaphy, lateral-only and cross-stitch endpoints and inferred a causal retention difference; now only technique and source-specific outcomes are compared. [Huang 2022 abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34992032/) gives **93/98 at 12 months, 61/71 at 24 and 16/21 at 48**; later proportions are not rates in all 98 enrolled patients (publisher landing accessible; Europe PMC full-text endpoint 404).
- [Menefee 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22067717/) reports 99 randomized, 78 with ≥2-year follow-up, 58/46/18% arm-specific anatomic failures, nonsignificantly different 13/12/4% composite failures and 14% polypropylene-mesh erosion. [Minassian 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23508540/) compared **polyglactin-augmented vaginal colporrhaphy**, not unaugmented native tissue, against abdominal paravaginal repair in 70 women; concurrent hysterectomy differed. The [Cai 2025 publisher main text](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00404-024-07864-w) was read in Methods, Results and Discussion for the selected 102-person symptomatic-SUI/stage-2–3-POP group, 12-month PGI-I primary endpoint, objective SUI and anterior anatomy endpoints, and 48/51 versus 47/51 follow-up. Its separately linked full tables/figure were not read. The Methods and Results report inconsistent recruitment periods, so no stronger practice recommendation is inferred. Reused the prior documented [FDA mesh-route review](pop-mesh-complications-review.md); Menefee's transvaginal polypropylene POP-mesh arm is historical US evidence, not a current marketed option.
