## Source access and attribution

- **AUA/GURS/SUFU incontinence after prostate treatment, 2019 amended 2024:** the official unabridged PDF and an alternate 2019 PDF returned HTTP 403 on direct access. Official indexed guideline text and the AUA journal indexed guideline summary supported the narrow surgery-timing, cystourethroscopy and selective-urodynamics corrections; the complete 2024 PDF, tables and references were **not** read. Reacquire via legitimate AUA access before closing those guideline-dependent claims.
- **EAU Management of Non-neurogenic Male LUTS, live disease-management chapter:** read the male UI/PFMT, electrical-stimulation, bulking, male sling, AUS and ProACT sections and recommendations, including relevant table text and a figure caption. The entire chapter, figure image and underlying device studies were not read; the page edition was not pinned. It supports phenotype-dependent selection, a selective rather than mandatory electrical-stimulation role, weaker sling outcomes with severity/radiation, and avoiding ProACT after radiation. It does not make comparative pooled device percentages a head-to-head trial.
- **MASTER original publisher article via Bristol repository, Abrams et al, Eur Urol 2021;79:812–823:** read relevant title/methods/results, Tables 1–2, safety text and discussion in the 13-page PDF; not figure pixels or supplements. The 380-person trial's strict 12-month *any-leakage* primary endpoint was 134/154 (87.0%) sling versus 133/158 (84.2%) AUS, satisfying its prespecified noninferiority test. Secondary symptom/satisfaction outcomes favored AUS. Six sling versus 11 AUS recipients had serious adverse events (13 AUS events). These are not 87% and 84% cure rates or proof that sling is preferable for all severities.
- **Queissert et al, J Clin Med 2022;11:81:** original 11-page repository PDF; read methods, results/main table and discussion/limitations, not figure pixels or appendix. This retrospective nine-center series of 88 post-AdVance patients reported 67/88 (76.1%) social continence at mean 42.5 months. It cannot establish ATOMS superiority over AUS.
- **AUSCO:** primary PubMed abstract checked for 115 implanted at 17 sites, 91/97 (94%) ≥50% pad-weight reduction, 61/101 (60%) zero-pad use and 9/115 (7.8%) revisions at 12 months. Reused the more extensive abstract and selected full-methods/results/discussion reading documented in `reports/2026-09-11/full-site-review/surgical-incontinence.md`. Complete tables, figures and supplements were not read here; single-arm results are not comparative effectiveness.
- **Lenfant French first-AUS cohort** and **Sacco propensity-matched AUS versus fixed-sling cohort:** primary abstracts only. Lenfant's 8,475-person reintervention-free estimates are 71/57/40% at two/five/ten years (removal or replacement, not mechanical failure alone). Sacco's 35-per-arm matched observational 12-month 0–1-pad/day outcome was 94.3% AUS versus 68.6% sling. Neither full methods, tables nor supplements were accessed.
