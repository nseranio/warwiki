# Anchorsure, Capio and i-Stitch — targeted review (2026-09-21)

Three complete current MDX pages, including references and cross-device comparisons, were read. No imported clinical data, local figure or video is attached. This is a **partial claim review**, not clinical clearance. Changes are **local only**; no commit, push or deployment is authorized now.

## Source access and adjudication

- **Manufacturer/IFU:** Neomedic's current Anchorsure product description and the complete three-page English DIMA/Neomedic [IFU](https://adlerinstruments.com/wp-content/uploads/Anchorsure-Vaginal-Vault-Prolapse-by-Neomedic-IFU-Adler-Instrument-Company.pdf) were read. The IFU is **March 2016**, explicitly says to request an update after 24 months, and specifies PEEK anchor, USP-0 polypropylene, needle/kit contents, sterile single use, contraindications, operative placement, processing and neurovascular warnings. The newer product page does not replace a current model IFU. Boston Scientific's [current Capio catalog](https://www.bostonscientific.com/en-US/products/pelvic-floor-reconstruction/capio-slim.html), listed model and dart-suture rows, and [FDA 510(k) summary](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf17/K172060.pdf) support throw/catch, single-use and model-specific compatibility, not clinical superiority. A.M.I.'s [i-Stitch product instructions](https://ami.at/product/i-stitch/) and [2024 catalog](https://ami.at/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/220519_Urogyn_EN.pdf) support reusable instrument, disposable hollow-needle-slider loading unit, blunt ball-shaped suture tip, caudal/cranial variants, finger guidance and native-tissue/mesh uses. Full current reprocessing and jurisdiction-specific IFUs remain needed.
- **Field notice:** the complete five-page [Boston Scientific June 27, 2024 notice](https://mda.gov.my/index.php/documents/recall/3291-capio-slim-suture-capturing-device-pdf/file) and its affected-product/lot attachment were read. Certain Capio SLIM lots could fail to catch the dart; repeated attempts could cause hemorrhage. This is **not** a universal product withdrawal. The pages direct model/lot checking without copying a stale lot list.
- **Clinical outcomes:** [Plair 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34856573/) indexed abstract: 24 anchor vs 23 capture, no detected one-week pain difference, not an equivalence trial or cure study. [Evangelopoulos 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38852315/) abstract: 20/20 pilot, day-one pain 3.40 versus 1.60 favoring capture; no broad superiority. [Lo 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41287197/) abstract: 676 mixed tacker cases, **only 19 Anchorsure alone**; reports 27 dislodged and 649 secure yet also 2.2% incidence, a numerical inconsistency requiring full text. Two bladder and four operator injuries were in the dislodged group. Neither cure nor dislodgement percentages can be assigned to Anchorsure alone. [Ahmadian 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-52719-w) abstract/methods and result/table excerpts: 115 enrolled, 104 followed at three months, FSFI 19.15 to 21.84; uncontrolled procedure-associated change, not a device effect. [Mowat 2018](https://obgyn.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/ajo.12720) publisher abstract: 51-person prospective Capio series, 84% one-week and 16% six-week buttock pain (7% analgesia), not universal incidence. [Maggiore 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22941353/) indexed abstract: 44 Capio/42 traditional, shorter operative time and lower blood loss in this nonrandomized cohort, 86.4%/92.9% Capio objective/subjective success at three years. [Luffarelli 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40273645/) abstract: 163 **total** across sequential techniques at two centers, 86 versus 98 minutes, no significant blood-loss difference.
- **Anatomy and synthesis:** [Manning 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25476808/) abstract describes five preserved pelves/hemipelves: i-Stitch penetrated coccygeal arterial branches; Capio/Caspari/Endostitch remained within ligament in those specimens. It does not quantify patient events. [Amiri 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39499312/) abstract pools **3.8% nerve injury across 125 studies/10,216 cases**, mentions Capio highest and Anchorsure among higher reoperation groups but supplies no device-specific denominators. The former **Capio 3.8%** and Anchorsure comparative tables were wrong. [Chene anatomical 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejogrb.2024.03.039) indexed abstract and [clinical pilot online 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41344161/) abstract/publisher excerpts describe three cadavers and 15 patients respectively; visualization feasibility and zero observed complications do not show injury prevention. The pilot is in a 2026 issue. The Cochrane [transvaginal mesh/native-tissue review](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD012079_should-transvaginal-mesh-biological-grafts-or-native-tissue-be-utilised-repair-vaginal-prolapse) concerns procedure/material selection, not Capio comparative performance.
