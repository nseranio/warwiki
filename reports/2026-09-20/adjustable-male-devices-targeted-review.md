# Adjustable male devices: focused source and preservation review

September 21, 2026. Four full current MDX pages were read, including their outcome/complication tables, 28+7+15+8 inherited references and two VideoCards. No local clinical-data or figure imports were present. All four remain **partial claim reviews**, not clinical clearances; all edits are local and unpublished.

## Source locations and decisions

- **ATOMS:** the historical A.M.I. 2016 eight-page product brochure describes an outside-in, four-point arm fixation and scrotal adjustment port; text and figure captions were read, not figure pixels, and it is not a current model IFU. Queissert's previously documented original 11-page study supports a post-AdVance salvage cohort (J Clin Med 2022;11:81, online 2021), not comparative superiority. Paynter et al. *IJU Case Rep* 2025;8:44–46, full HTML case/discussion read but cystoscopic figure pixels not inspected, documents late urethral erosion in a previously irradiated, strictured patient. The existing long-term cohort also names **port erosion** among explants. Removed “essentially unreported erosion,” categorical best-device/durability and “only generation to implant” advice; retained all cohorts, techniques and 28 inherited references, adding brochure and case report. Exact model IFU and inherited original cohort tables/figures remain open.
- **REMEEX:** Neomedic's current product-description text distinguishes tensioning 24–48 hours after implantation from later reconnection of the external manipulator during a minor procedure. The prior “incision-free at any time” statement was wrong; current model IFU remains unavailable. Chiu et al. *J Clin Med* 2022;11:6764, accessible seven-page original PDF, methods, Tables 1–4 and discussion read; figures/supplements not identified. Two of 18 patients had bowel injuries during retropubic passage after transperitoneal prostatectomy; authors discuss unrepaired peritoneal opening as a possible anatomy-specific contributor. This is the **REMEEX** source, not the Argus allegation carried unresolved in the preceding batch. The 11.1% single-center event is not a general incidence. Retained all seven inherited references, tables and route teaching; original Sousa-Escandón/Kim cohort packages remain open.
- **ProACT:** FDA-posted P130018 physician IFU (29 pp), indications/contraindications, adverse-event Table 3, anesthesia, implantation text and adjustment Table 6 read; other tables/figures and their pixels were not all independently reviewed. IFU pp. 17–21 specifies general/spinal and fluoroscopy/cystoscopy, up to 1.5 mL initial inflation, no adjustment before six weeks; pp. 25–26 suggest about four-week intervals, up to 1 mL each per visit, 8 mL ceiling per balloon, 23-gauge needle and 0.5 mL reductions for retention. Table 3 records 19/123 with bladder/urethral perforation and 19/123 with retention through 24 months. Replaced unsourced general/local technique and generalized low complication rates; kept the TRUS cohort as a distinct published variant and all 15 references/video. Full Gregori and other inherited outcome originals remain open.
- **Virtue:** Coloplast product/sell-sheet text and brief safety statement read (the latter is **not** full IFU), including possible mesh erosion and four-arm mechanism; visual diagrams were not independently inspected. Original Roumeguère 2022 publisher abstract reports 117 analyzed, at 36 months 19% strict cure, 35% objective improvement and 34% subjective improvement; the publisher full text/tables could not be opened. Coloplast's earlier trial poster instead advertises 39% cure/72% improvement at 36 months: population, timepoint and denominator discrepancy **unresolved**, not mixed into the published-abstract row. McCall 2016 original abstract reports 31 analyzed and a 68% composite failure with 22% explant/20% AUS at 55-month median; these separate cohorts and endpoints must not be compared as longitudinal decline. Corrected the 51%-versus-35% objective row, grade IIIb generalization and “no erosion across studies.” Retained all eight inherited references, technique/outcome tables and video, adding two manufacturer sources. Complete Roumeguère, Comiter and other cohort tables/figures remain open.
