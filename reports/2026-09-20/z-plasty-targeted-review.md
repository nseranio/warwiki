Corrections: the 60° Z-plasty's ~73% gain and 90° reorientation are ideal geometry, not guaranteed clinical effect or a rule for other flap angles. Serial flaps do not prove universally lower tip-necrosis risk. The pediatric series' **97% was follow-up completion**, with stable correction among those observed, not overall success in all 100. Stewart's subsequent circumcision **1/11 versus 5/22 (p=0.33)** was not a statistically significant difference, although cosmetic ratings favored Z (p=0.005); its 82% avoidance is across both techniques. Dockray's 8.9/10 and 9/96 subsequent circumcisions cover **mixed V-Y/Z techniques**, not Z alone. Dagi's 33%/67% estimates describe plastic-surgeon involvement in Mohs cases, not Z-plasty use. Peyronie's tunical incision/grafting is a distinct operation; the AUA guideline does not endorse skin Z-plasty for that indication. ACOG says Z-plasty **may** help transverse-septum repair, not that it is mandatory. Vassallo's 75%/85% outcomes are across four operations, with only three Z-plasty recipients. The Plymouth authors' recommendation was retained as their proposal, not elevated to a universal rule. Practical technique, small series and original figure were retained.
