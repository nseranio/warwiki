1. **Corrected:** Dargan's aperture test had been taught as intraoperative calibration of *actual graft thickness* across all three pages. It tested aperture behavior, not cut skin. Replaced that instruction with model-specific inspection/service and graft assessment.
2. **Corrected:** Egro's injury-event percentages had been called Zimmer market share and air-device utilization; guard/pressure associations had been presented as comparative risk. Now explicitly scoped to survey-reported lacerations and attributed causes, with missing exposure denominators.
3. **Corrected:** Padgett's "fully autoclavable" and model-independent power/clip specifications. Handpiece and attached cable differ from the power supply and wall cord; model and voltage distinctions retained. Comparative ergonomic superiority, default-use claims and phalloplasty extrapolation removed without deleting the published breast-flap technique.
4. **Added device-specific safety:** Zimmer Air/AN serial-specific 2025–26 action, linked to the phase-2 original notice. Avoided a blanket recall or a claim that all models are affected.
5. **Narrowed:** universal genital graft thickness, donor-site choice, cosmetic outcome and "single most common" GU indication; retained practical ranges and examples.
