# Reverse, staged and ventral-slit scrotal flaps — 2026-09-20

Complete current MDX and references were read on three restored pages. All remain **partial claim reviews**, not clinical clearances. Useful technique, anatomy, tables and references were retained; no media was removed. No new OpenEvidence query or publication.

## Direct source access

- Gao/Qu 2019 [open original PDF](https://artefacts-discovery.researcher.life/full_text/DA-2/f4/f4e5e69bb6aa398e810d5d30175b44f7/full_text/dae5a2d6512850c1e3a5df9d994a915a.pdf): full case/method/results/discussion and Figure 2–3 legends read (pp. 1–3); image panels not visually inspected. One 31-year-old post-circumcision case, 5-cm-wide defect, 6 × 6 cm anterior-scrotal flap, reversed dorsal-to-ventral wrap, ventral Z-suture and anti-retraction stays; reported no complication through ten years. **Figure 3 legend says pedicled on bilateral anterior scrotal arteries.** The text does not describe proximal arterial ligation, prove retrograde flow or test a damaged proximal pedicle. The preexisting page's claimed arterial-division step was unsupported and potentially harmful; corrected prominently.
- McLaughlin/Abbassi/Pribaz 2024 [original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37285217/): eight staged patients, all satisfactory outcomes, two minor complications. Publisher DOI and a legitimate search for full text did not yield the operative paper. Exact 3–6-week interval, tissue plane, graft-loss count and figure-based details unresolved.
- Lumbiganon 2023 [publisher abstract](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/iju.15209), reused from the prior scrotal cluster: 42 treated, **35 in flap comparison** (23 single, 12 two-stage); fever 56.52% vs 8.33%, complication-free 43.48% vs 83.33%, longer two-stage hospital stay. Wound infection, dehiscence and reoperation differences were nonsignificant; retrospective selection limits causal ranking. Full tables/adjustments unread.
- Zucchi 2010 [original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20092845/): ten selected trapped-penis patients, 6–12-week interval, three/four nonresorbable trans-scrotal sutures, one superficial hematoma, median follow-up 20 months and VAS 97; all reported erections and intercourse after stage 2. Full technique/figures unread.
- Westerman et al. 2015 [author-hosted full original PDF](https://drallenmorey.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Ventral-Slit-Scrotal-Flap.pdf): methods, operative sequence, results, limitations and all figure legends read (pp. 1–4); figure images and online Supplementary Tables 1–2 not visually inspected. Fifteen highly selected trapped-penis patients without significant suprapubic adiposity/lymphedema; distal **2-cm** relaxing incisions, deep dartos mobilized with skin, **tunica-albuginea-to-dorsolateral-deep-dermis penopubic fixation** and permanent penoscrotal angle sutures. Mean follow-up **12 months (3–29)**; qualitative chart-review satisfaction 11/15, recurrence 3/15, no perioperative complications. The initial abstract-only concern about anchoring was revised after full text established the correct fixation anatomy.
- Fakin, Murányi, Mendel and Parasca outcomes were **reused by attribution** from the immediately preceding scrotal-flap claim records, not re-researched.
