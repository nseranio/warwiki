## VRAM corrections

- Kim: 77 operated, **60 evaluable**; 98.3% was apparent DIEA flow on available postoperative imaging, not universal flap perfusion. Its 75% rectal-cancer figure was a cohort characteristic, not a pooled indication rate.
- Paprottka: 12 selected patients received five VRAM and eight gracilis flaps; the ten living survey respondents were three VRAM/seven gracilis. Neither aggregate closure nor the continence contrast proves one flap's superiority.
- Escandón: 56 retrospective USF repairs, with early sepsis 3% omental/27% VRAM/18% primary. Adjusted recurrence favored omentum versus primary, **not versus VRAM**. Removed the universal omental-first rule.
- Proctor's smoking/radiotherapy associations were **univariate**; Nichols studied factors associated with VRAM **selection** rather than complication causation. The 2025 IGAM cohort, Nelson's mixed-thigh comparison, Stein's matched series and Eseme's proportional meta-analysis retain their observed numbers without blanket flap rankings.
- Trapero's 270°/180° inset is its authors' technical proposal. Robotic muscle-only harvest is distinct from skin-bearing VRAM; Davila's 16-versus-20 mixed comparison did not prove fewer major complications or an intact abdominal wall.
- Routine donor mesh was removed as a universal instruction. Mortensen's 58-person collagen-mesh RCT did not demonstrate a one-year CT-hernia reduction (8/24 vs 12/24, P=0.38); Barnes's 100-person retrospective analysis found no added benefit with achievable primary closure and noted more infections requiring intervention in its mesh group. Other defects may still need reinforcement. PDS was correctly identified as absorbable.

All original VRAM anatomy, technical sections, applications, comparisons, video and 25 references were preserved; two donor-closure studies were added. No public audit notice or blanket deletion. A focused final diff/source check was performed.
