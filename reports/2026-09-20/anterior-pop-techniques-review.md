# Anterior POP techniques: source-fit correction (20 September 2026)

The complete current [anterior colporrhaphy](../../docs/04-surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/anterior/anterior-colporrhaphy.mdx) and [Michigan four-wall](../../docs/04-surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/anterior/michigan-four-wall.mdx) MDX pages, all inline tables, references and media listings were read before and after editing. Neither imports clinical data or figures. The two colporrhaphy video listings and Michigan supplementary operative video were **not played**; no video technique verification is claimed. Both reviews remain **partial**, not clinical clearance.

- Reused the documented full relevant [NICE NG123/FDA/anterior Cochrane source read](../2026-09-12/prolapse-source-reading/principles.md) and complete [ASPIRe main/supplement review](../2026-09-12/prolapse-source-reading/aspire-main-supplements.md). NICE allows surgery when nonsurgical care is ineffective **or declined**, does not impose one surgical POP-Q threshold, and advises against prophylactic incontinence surgery in continent women. The FDA currently has no marketed transvaginal POP mesh device in the US; this differs from SUI slings and abdominal POP mesh. The 2026 anterior Cochrane public summary/abstract, **not its embargoed full main**, supports the comparative RR claims; historical mesh comparisons are not a US availability recommendation. The full ACOG/AUGS bulletin remains inaccessible from the prior source work.
- The [Chmielewski abstract/indexed trial account](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21545996/) concerns **114 randomized to three procedures**, including polyglactin mesh; the **88% (66/75 evaluable)** one-year composite, also reported in a [published outcomes review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3678959/), is not an isolated colporrhaphy rate. Full original paper/tables were not retrieved. The [Mothes abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25430736/) reports 438 women **all receiving anterior and posterior repairs**, with many additional procedures; its event percentages now carry that denominator, not an isolated anterior-surgery label. The [Altman original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21561348/) supports the stated randomized one-year endpoint, 3.2% mesh-exposure reintervention, bladder-perforation and new-SUI contrasts. The unrelated retropubic-colposuspension citation no longer purports to quantify anterior-repair urgency.
- The [Eilber primary full article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3857933/) was read at design, cohort flow, Table 2 and limitations: **2,756 vaginal cases included anterior, posterior and combined repairs**, and 20.2% versus 11.6% was an anterior-subgroup **observational association**, vulnerable to coding and severity confounding. Table 2 prints 258 anterior-plus-apical cases while results text prints 256; the page does not propagate either inconsistent subgroup count. The [OPUS original abstract](https://www.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/NEJMoa1111967) provides 337 randomized women without preoperative SUI symptoms: 23.6% versus 49.4% urinary incontinence/treatment at **3 months**, and 27.3% versus 43.0% incontinence at **12 months**. The old page conflated endpoint/time and asserted a universal 40% de novo SUI risk. [AUA/SUFU unabridged guideline statement 19 and its prolapse discussion](https://www.auanet.org/documents/Guidelines/PDF/2023%20Guidelines/SUI%20Unabridged%20FINAL%20080223.pdf) were checked as indexed original excerpts for selected shared-decision framing; the full PDF was not read. This is distinct from NICE's no-prophylaxis recommendation.
- The [Kearney/DeLancey abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12576257/) and complete [Larson 2013 accessible main article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3860104/) were checked for the actual **apical-incision/four-apical-wall** method, selective redundant-tissue excision and follow-up flow. Larson identified 453 operations, 392 contact-eligible and 242 satisfaction respondents; **90%** applies to those respondents. Anatomy for 162/242 was from mixed visits at mean 4.3 years; only 70 had a mean-eight-year study examination (57/70 at/above hymen). Four-wall does not require full anterior/posterior/paravaginal reconstruction for every patient. The previous table compared mismatched cohorts and wrongly called 86% an eight-year result. ASPIRe's native-tissue arm was 82% conventional SSLF/18% USLS, **not Michigan**, and its modelled 36-month composite results cannot be assigned to Michigan. The 2026 Danish SSLF cohort also does not isolate it. Larson's actual figures and supplementary video were not inspected; only main text, tables and captions were read. Its discussion has an internal 242/390 phrase, whereas methods/results/Table 1 give 242/392; the page uses the latter.

The inherited [2015 ACOG UI bulletin identity](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26488524/) and [2017 open retropubic-colposuspension Cochrane review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6483458/) remain as general symptom-classification and distinct-procedure context, respectively; neither is used to quantify isolated anterior-repair risk, and neither full source was reread here.

Remaining source packages: full Chmielewski, Mothes, OPUS and AUA/SUFU body/supplements where needed for further counseling detail; Graefe/Šumak/Balzarro/Lo and other inherited long-term anterior outcomes; the Morgan/Paraiso/Goldberg Michigan-related full texts and the actual Larson figures/video. These are recorded as unresolved, with substantive technique and existing media retained. No heading, table, inherited reference or video was deleted; only unsupported universal/causal interpretations, one mismatched comparison table's numerical rows and one off-topic complication assertion were replaced with scoped descriptions.
