## Claim outcomes and real source access

- The [2012 Cochrane review](https://eprints.staffs.ac.uk/271/1/CD000947.pdf) (author-university copy; 73-page full PDF available) was read at its abstract, intervention descriptions, included-trial methods, short-term pain/analgesia results and discussion. The 16 trials enrolled 8,184 women, comparing **continuous versus interrupted overall repair**, commonly changing vaginal, muscle and skin layers together. It explicitly says good-quality evidence is lacking for the traditional claim that a vaginal lock prevents shortening. The page now keeps that teaching as a historical rationale, not a demonstrated outcome. The source's entire 73 pages, all forest-plot images and supplements were not independently inspected. A [2025 17-RCT meta-analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40961850/) abstract likewise supports short-term benefit for continuous over interrupted repair, not isolated locking; its full text/figures remain unavailable in this pass.
- The official [ACOG PB 198 page](https://www.acog.org/clinical/clinical-guidance/practice-bulletin/articles/2018/09/prevention-and-management-of-obstetric-lacerations-at-vaginal-delivery) lists **2025 reaffirmation** but gates full text. A legitimate [obstetrics-society copy of the original bulletin](https://tjodistanbul.org/uploads/prevention-and-management-of-obstetric-lacerations-at-vaginal-deliverynumber-198.pdf) supplied relevant full pages 6–7: continuous repair is preferred for second-degree tears, while **running-locking or interrupted** sutures are options for a bleeding vaginal laceration and **interrupted or continuous locking** for cervical laceration after an apical stitch. The bulletin calls these latter choices experience/expert opinion with no comparative closure data. The page had incorrectly described running-locking as the sole recommendation. The [2010 suture-material Cochrane abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20556745/) was checked: it compares materials, not locking.
- The original [Muffly 2012 abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22208830/) documents 285 **bench knots** exposed to petroleum gauze or saline (mean failure 116.7 versus 123.8 N; p=.002), not bacitracin ointment, mineral oil, dry knots or patient outcomes. The [Johanns knot-mechanics main paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10246910/) shows friction interacts with geometry, pretension and plastic deformation. The [Coleridge 2017 abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28146293/) concerns large-gauge **equine** knots in balanced electrolyte solution, carboxymethylcellulose, equine serum or fat, not a human saline-glove trial. The [Alnaqi 2023 abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36316513/) tests seven-day strand immersion, not intraoperative knot security. These distinctions replaced the unsupported claim that saline selectively lubricates only the glove and proved a clinical product ranking.
- The complete relevant recommendation page of the [CDC 2017 guideline PDF](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/79361/cdc_79361_DS1.pdf) was read: no antimicrobial **incision application** for SSI prevention (IB); **consider** triclosan-coated sutures (II, weak/moderate), not a Class-I endorsement or direct test of lubrication. The [SHEA/IDSA 2022 update](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10867741/) relevant irrigation passage says **bacitracin irrigation** is contraindicated because injectable bacitracin was withdrawn after anaphylaxis reports. The [Smack 1996 RCT abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8805732/) compares **postoperative dermatologic wound** ointments: evaluable infection 9/440 versus 4/444 (p=.37), contact dermatitis 0 versus 4 (p=.12). Neither determines intraoperative coating risk. Roy's institutional before/after irrigation result was retained but no longer described as confirming no benefit. The rest of the page's 11 references, practical links, tables and caution about unverified ad-hoc coating remain.

No heading, reference, figure, video or manufacturer instruction was removed. The comparison table was retained and relabeled around actual tested exposure rather than a fabricated safety ranking. No public audit notice was added and no new OpenEvidence query was made.
