# Conservative UI therapy: bounded claim review (21 September 2026)

Three complete current MDX pages, their tables, links and all 77 inherited references were read: behavioral therapy, pelvic-floor PT and pelvic-floor electrical stimulation. They import no clinical data or local figures/video. This is a **partial claim review**, not clinical clearance. No section, table, inherited reference or media was removed.

| Source and actual access | Consequential finding and edit |
|---|---|
| Cameron et al, AUA/SUFU idiopathic OAB 2024, [journal full HTML](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/nau.25532), Table 1 and statements 10-14 and 23-25 read; unabridged evidence tables not read. Prior OAB review in `sol-high-oab-pilot.md` and `oab-treatment-database-review.md` reused for the shared framework. | Bladder training and behavioral care offered to OAB; TTNS is non-invasive but PTNS is minimally invasive. NICE does not offer TTNS for OAB, a difference from AUA's selectable non-invasive options. Added explicit citations. |
| OPAL, [BMJ 2020 original HTML](https://www.bmj.com/content/371/bmj.m3719), abstract, eligibility, results and discussion including Table 1/Figure 1 captions; complete table cells, figure pixels and supplement not inspected. | 600 women with **stress or mixed UI**, excluding isolated urgency UI and inability to contract, showed no meaningful 24-month benefit from routine EMG biofeedback added to supervised PFMT (ICIQ-UI SF MD −0.09, 95% CI −0.92 to 0.75). PTNS ranking is within heterogeneous female UI trials, not proof of universal OAB superiority. Modality teaching preserved with corrected provenance. |
| Stewart et al, [Cochrane 2017 original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29271482/); full review and outcome tables unavailable in this pass. | “Moderate-quality cure/improvement vs no active or sham” overspecified certainty; comparisons differ. | Subak et al, [PRIDE original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19179316/) and [publisher record](https://www.nejm.org/doi/abs/10.1056/NEJMoa0806375); full tables/supplement not read. | Verified 338 overweight/obese women and 47% vs 28% six-month mean episode reduction; significant stress but not urge subgroup difference. Added primary attribution and population scope. |
| Goode et al, [2011 original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21224456/); full PMC paper not yet checked in this batch. | Persistent post-prostatectomy trial supports no added benefit from biofeedback plus ES over behavioral treatment (55% vs 51% reduction), counterbalancing early postoperative indirect network rankings. |

The electrical-device contraindication list had treated heterogeneous research exclusions as universal prohibitions, including a blanket six-month surgery interval and grade-II prolapse cutoff. The list now calls for route/model-specific IFU review and targeted precautions. This **does not certify any IFU**. Generic current ceilings and exact needle placement were also demoted from instructions to illustrative study settings. The open source task is to obtain representative intravaginal, surface, magnetic and PTNS device IFUs and test each contraindication and parameter separately; do not apply this generalized paragraph to device-specific pages.
