## Focused source/diff gate

Both pages retain every substantive heading, blade/handle selection table, grip, ergonomic, history and related-link section. No page media or figures existed. Practical GU examples were retained as **options**, not mandatory model prescriptions. On the handles page, the inherited Kakarala rat-glossectomy citation was misused for Bard-Parker history; it remains appropriately cited on the blades page and was replaced here by Elson's scalpel history. The unrelated Anderson reproductive-hazard citation was replaced by Jagger's actual sharps-surveillance study. Other inherited references were retained. The only narrowed unsourced detail was the 3-nm obsidian edge measurement; Disa's supported scar findings remain.
