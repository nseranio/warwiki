# Iliococcygeus suspension: anatomy and outcome-source correction (20 September 2026)

Read the complete current [iliococcygeus page](../../docs/04-surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/apical/iliococcygeus-suspension.mdx), its 14 inherited reference entries and links, technique, outcome and risk tables before editing; read the revised page and 16-reference list afterward. Accessible original abstracts and the relevant guideline/terminology sections were checked as detailed below; this is **not** a claim that every full paper was read. No imported data, page figure or embedded video exists. All sections and practical operative teaching remain. **This is partial source review, not clinical or operative sign-off.** Existing apical/SSLF source reports were used as an index, not counted as a new read.

- The primary [AUGS–IUGA 2020 terminology report](https://www.augs.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Joint_Report_on_Terminology_for_Surgical.3.pdf), relevant iliococcygeus definition, steps and variants, locates fixation in **medial iliococcygeus parietal fascia, typically caudal to the ischial spine (~1 cm inferior)**. The old page repeatedly said **1–2 cm anterior and medial**, fixed two sutures per side, and required full-thickness vault bites; those were not universal standardized steps. The report permits unilateral/bilateral and absorbable/nonabsorbable variants, describes several interrupted sutures and says fascia-versus-muscle capture cannot always be distinguished. Its [published erratum](https://www.augs.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/erratum.pdf) concerns USLS Figures 3–4, not this text; **Figure 7 itself was not visually inspected**. The anatomic target is intended to reduce neurovascular proximity, not proven to reduce clinical injury.
- The **current** [AWMF/DGGG S3 guideline 015-006 v6.0](https://register.awmf.org/assets/guidelines/015-006l_S2e_Descensus_genitalis-Diagnostik-Therapie_2026-06.pdf) was released June 2026. Its cover identifies S3 despite a legacy `S2e` filename. Read §6.6, recommendation **6.E54** and the middle-compartment summary: iliococcygeus fixation may be considered when no adequate alternative exists (evidence grade 2b, recommendation grade 0), because randomized and systematic long-term comparative evidence is absent. This supersedes the 2016 recommendation; the old ACOG-specific Level-A inference was not substantiated by the accessible bulletin abstract and is no longer assigned to this procedure. The 2026 guideline cites the 53/83/96% small series and 39-person 10-year cohort without making them comparative proof.
- [Meeks 1994](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7802052/) original abstract: 110 retrospective complex-defect operations, minimum three-year follow-up, **4 recurrent defects** all involving the anterior wall, **41/110 postoperative complications**, three hemorrhages >750 mL, two transfusions, one each bowel/bladder injury, and mean catheterization **6.1 days**, which is **not a urinary-retention rate**. The former broad low-morbidity and universal anterior-failure claims were narrowed.
- [Maher 2001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11430954/) original abstract: **50 ICG and 78 SSLF operations**, but only **36 matched pairs** analyzed; mean follow-up 21/19 months and **50% power to detect a 20% success difference**. The 91/94% subjective, 53/67% objective, 78/91 satisfaction and 54/39-day recovery figures are from this underpowered nonrandomized comparison; nonsignificant success results do **not** establish equivalence or a lower pain/injury risk. [Peters 1995](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7778650/) compared a related **coccygeus-fascial**, not identical iliococcygeus, target: 81 versus 30 vaginal operations; ten abdominal operations excluded; 96/80% were **projected** two-year cures in a retrospective study. [Milani 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24030216/) had 36 ICG versus 41 abdominal SCP, 78/140-minute operating times, **150/100-mL median blood loss** and nonsignificant 22/15% relapse; this does not prove equivalent durability.
- [Serati 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25653034/) abstract provides **39/44 subjective** and **37/44 objective** success at median 68.8 months; [Serati 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33237354/) has **32/39 subjective** and **29/39 objective** success at median 120 months. They are publications from the same group, not established independent trials; cohort overlap and attrition need full methods. [Suh and Jeon 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29747142/) reports 144 retrospective patients, median four years and estimated 75.3/48.0% mild/advanced hysteropexy success; it supports selected counseling, not a universal prohibition. [Milani 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28321474/) is a one-case video tutorial abstract, not low-morbidity population evidence. The [2026 levator-MRI study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41547955/) did not test ICG fixation outcomes or establish a levator-defect contraindication.
