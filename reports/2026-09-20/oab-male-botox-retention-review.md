## Source coverage and finding

- [Yokoyama et al 2021, publisher-hosted open-access full text](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11255-021-02962-z): read methods, Table 1 including the retention definition/footnote, results, safety, discussion and limitations. The trial randomized 250 patients; 248 were in the efficacy analysis (186 women, 62 men). Of 32 treated men, 4 had a urinary-retention adverse event, all in the 11-man higher-PSA subgroup; 3/92 treated women had one. One male event was a residual-urine *sensation* coded as retention despite PVR &lt;200 mL. The male PSA split was post hoc, small, and a proxy rather than measured obstruction; suspected BOO and baseline PVR ≥100 mL were excluded. These findings support targeted emptying/outlet counseling, **not** a general sex-specific CIC estimate. Figure images and the parent RCT's separate full report were not independently adjudicated; this claim relies on the article text and Table 1.
- [Mateu Arrom et al 2020 primary-study abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31479408/): abstract only; the publisher result also displayed the abstract. Among 146 men, 91 (62.3%) reported treatment response, 19 (13%) had urinary retention (Clavien–Dindo grade 2), and higher bladder outlet obstruction index was associated with lower response and more complications. The full article, subgroup definitions, and any supplement were not accessed, so no female comparator or universal rate is inferred.
- The [2024 AUA/SUFU OAB guideline](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/nau.25532) and [BOTOX US label](https://www.rxabbvie.com/pdf/botox_pi.pdf) were **reused**, not re-credited as new full reads. The database report documents guideline statements 23–29 and label §5.13/Table 10: pooled idiopathic-OAB CIC initiation was 36/552 (6.5%) on 100 U vs 2/542 (0.4%) placebo. That pooled CIC endpoint cannot be directly compared with this single-center male cohort's retention events or used to declare a male/female relative risk.

The OAB condition page's blanket “men have higher intermittent-catheterization rates than women” sentence was replaced with source-scoped emptying/outlet counseling and explicit uncertainty, with both primary studies added to its references. The Botox procedure page now labels 62.3%/13% as observations from **one 146-man retrospective cohort**, not a general male risk. No heading, table, video, practical option, or prior reference was removed. The source/claim preservation reread found no new dosing assertion or drug-indication change.
