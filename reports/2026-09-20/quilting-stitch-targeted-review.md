## Corrections grounded in originals

- [Aldaqadossi 2014 author-hosted four-page original](https://asopahospital.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2.-Aldaqadossi_et_al-2014-International_Journal_of_Urology.pdf): read methods, operative descriptions, results and Table 1 text. Alternating assignment of 25 dorsal-onlay and 22 dorsal-inlay men; 88% versus 86.4% observed success at mean 22.6 versus 24.2 months. Corrected “equivalent” to similar observed rates without an equivalence design.
- [Cordon 2014 original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24631104/) compares pseudospongioplasty with conventional **ventral** spongioplasty (37/46 versus 47/56 success), not ventral versus dorsal grafts. [Mousa 2025 original open article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11772550/) supports the 5% versus 25% 3-month erectile-dysfunction observation only in selected primary nontraumatic proximal-bulbar patients; methods show the center switched approaches over time. Main methods/results/operative figure captions read; actual figure art and linked supplementary tables were not obtained.
- [Sterling 2023 publisher abstract](https://bjui-journals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/bju.16007/) reports 44 patients, 95% patency at **mean** 36-month follow-up, not a fixed-time endpoint. Detailed publisher full text/video remained unavailable. [Shahrour 2019 original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31039367/) reports only four VUAS patients, all still incontinent at the 3-month 100% early-patency assessment. [Azuma 2021 original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33960024/) clarifies the 0/23 versus 5/24 first-stage stomal-revision finding and 19/20 success in both groups reaching second stage.
- [Barbagli 2006 original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16806665/) describes **six** fibrin-glue cases, no restrictures at 6/12-month checks, mean follow-up 16 months (range 12–24), not an all-patient 12–24-month negative outcome. The DOI/publisher route did not supply full text; a separate author technical article found in search is not the same outcome paper. In contrast, the [Barbagli 2016 author-hosted seven-page original](https://asopahospital.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/6.-One-stage-Penile-Urethroplasty-Using-Oral-Mucosal-Graft-and-Glue.pdf) explicitly uses **Glubran 2 cyanoacrylate, not fibrin glue**. Read main methods, results, Table 1 text and figure captions; figure artwork/video were not inspected. Retained its 12/14 at median 16 months as an uncontrolled case series and included reported tunica injuries/UTI. No paper here proves faster surgery, angiogenesis or superior graft take versus sutures.
- [Cao 2022 RCT abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36161796/) supports 97.2% versus 90.2% early skin-graft take in 86 patients, strongest in irregular/high-mobility areas, measured postoperative days 5–7; it does not rank all genital fixation methods. [Malone 2011 abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20840546/) reports one re-grafting in the standardized 29-operation TODGA phase, **not measured 97% graft uptake**; the publisher PDF returned 403. The eggcrate bolster remains a non-NPWT method. [Weld 2006 animal/bench abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16750256/) concerns urinary-tract approximation, not human graft quilting; the original strength figure was therefore removed from graft-specific benefit language.
