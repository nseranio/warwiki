# Reconstructive principles and flap-library hubs — September 20, 2026

Complete current and revised MDX was read for three linked hubs: [flaps in GU reconstruction](../../docs/01-foundations/surgical-principles/flaps-gu-reconstruction.mdx), [reconstructive ladder](../../docs/01-foundations/surgical-principles/reconstructive-ladder.mdx) and [plastic surgery principles](../../docs/01-foundations/surgical-principles/plastic-surgery-principles.mdx). The existing individual flap reviews and September 12 penile-tissue report were reused as cross-checks. These are **partial claim reviews, not clinical clearances**; no new OpenEvidence pull was made.

The flap hub had exported animal and non-GU design measurements as universal human rules. Original indexed abstracts for [Taylor's angiosome work](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21460653/), [Stell's pig experiments](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/324339/), [human laser-speckle observations](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34313635/), [a pig length/thickness comparison](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4764830/), [Larrabee's 27-piglet tension experiment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/6712084/) and [Holzbach's 38-rat delay experiment](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18815084/) support narrower language. The page now distinguishes random, axial and perforator-based designs; does not turn one adjacent angiosome, a 250-g force, a fixed width ratio or a five-day delay into a genital-flap rule. The 2026 [Alammar flap-only review abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41720016/) supports 619 selected patients/625 flaps and 1.6% reported total loss, **not** an immediate-coverage mandate during active Fournier infection or superiority over grafting. Flap selection, Y-V, fistula interposition and series-specific outcomes were qualified without deleting the navigation table, anatomy or operative teaching. The new Memarzadeh reference and all 30 inherited references remain.

In the ladder, [Kopechek's open review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12130067/) treats 6.5 days to Fournier closure and >60% shorter convalescence as findings from *different selected series*. The [Glass systematic-review abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28602266/) does not support a blanket NPWT bacterial-reduction claim. [Patino's urologic skin-graft review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31239824/) distinguishes immediate primary from later secondary contraction; full-thickness graft contraction is not zero. Device/matrix types and staging were kept distinct, and the overgeneralized >95% GU free-flap rate was removed because its citation is a [125-TRAM breast cohort](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9385952/). The incorrect Gottlieb/Krieger and Serletti PubMed links were replaced with the verified original DOI identities; useful ladder options and links were retained.

The history page's *Plastic Surgery* (Elsevier 2017) and *Grabb and Smith's Plastic Surgery* (Wolters Kluwer 2020 eighth edition) were conflated. [Publisher](https://www.uk.elsevierhealth.com/plastic-surgery-9780323356961.html) and [NLM](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/nlmcatalog/101744719) catalogue records confirm they are different books. [Gillies/Millard](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/pbgzsspp) is 1957, not 1950; the [BnF 1564 record](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb310581629) is Paré's *Dix livres*, not his later *Œuvres*. The historic lists and modern principles were retained but clearly presented as site paraphrases until full originals are compared. A universal suture-caliber prescription was replaced with technique-specific selection.

Outstanding source work is exact: full original Taylor/Stell/Larrabee/Holzbach/Memarzadeh methods and figures; all retained geometric, donor/perforator and GU-flap source packages; Alammar's study-level table and selection limitations; device-specific current IFUs; original Mathes/Nahai, Gottlieb/Krieger and Paré/Gillies/Millard passages; full *Plastic Surgery* and *Principalization* chapters; and the comparative GU outcomes behind ladder illustrations. Indexed abstracts, publisher records and a review's relevant sections are not claimed as complete manuscripts. The structured [claim batch](../2026-09-11/full-site-review/claim-batches/2026-09-20-surgical-principles-hubs.json) records each page's final hash and unresolved claims.
