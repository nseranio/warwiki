# Adson, Bonney, DeBakey and Gerald thumb forceps — targeted review

September 20, 2026. Complete current text of all four pages and their reference lists read; no imported clinical data, diagrams or video declarations on these pages. This is a **partial claim review, not clinical clearance**.

## Source access and outcome

- **Manufacturer catalog pages read directly:** CooperSurgical Brown-Adson 7×7; Teleflex Bonney 640524 (1×2, 3.3 mm, 6¾ in) and 181327 (2×3, 6¾ in); Teleflex Gerald 351901 (DeBakey-type jaw) and 351905 (1×2); Teleflex DeBakey KM49168 (1.5 mm listing) and KM49182 (2.0 mm listing). The 181327 page internally calls 6¾ in “15 cm,” so no universal Bonney length was inferred. These are model specifications, **not** full reprocessing/clinical IFUs or proof of comparative safety.
- **Primary indexed abstracts read:** [Marucci 2000](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10945938/) tested aluminum jaw patterns gripping sheep stomach, not any named Adson/Bonney/DeBakey/Gerald forceps. Larger teeth improved grip but increased measured trauma versus wave jaws. [Cheng/Hannaford 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25408249/) was a liver finite-element grasper model, not a Bonney clinical comparison. [Rodrigues 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22028014/) applied force to sutures in porcine tissues; its 11.43 N fascia/1.25 N fallopian-tube values are not Bonney grip limits. Full papers, tables, figures and supplements were not read.
- **Closure evidence read:** [2026 STITCH long-term follow-up](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamasurgery/article-abstract/2847017), complete accessible abstract/key points (34% versus 49% cumulative hernia at 13 years; 25 versus 43 mm mean width at final follow-up), and [2022 EHS/AHS guideline](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10364727/), relevant KQ3 recommendation and supporting paragraphs including the weak small-bites recommendation and ≥4:1 suture:wound ratio. Neither tested a Bonney instrument. STITCH full text/tables/supplements were not read.
- **Other cited scope:** [Custis 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25875131/) indexed abstract and indexed article-method excerpt show a dermatologic adhesive-strip versus no-strip split-wound trial, not an Adson comparison; direct PMC article was challenged and not fully read. [Herrel/Hsiao 2013](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3739128/) indexed microsurgical technique excerpts and the cited ASRM abstract support magnified anastomotic care but do not mandate a particular Gerald jaw. Their full packages were not re-reviewed.

## Corrections and preservation

- **Adson:** removed “default for nearly every closure,” inherent smooth-tip safety on nerves/vessels/mucosa, and the incorrect Custis-to-Adson cosmetic inference. Kept variants, tissue-matching technique, uses and all inherited references.
- **Bonney:** made teeth/length model-specific, identified Rodrigues as a suture-loading experiment, separated STITCH closure technique from forceps choice, removed an unsupported less-total-trauma comparison and corrected the nonexistent thumb-forceps “hoop” passing instruction. Kept closure workflow, indications, safety limits, history and bibliography. The Teleflex 181327 length conversion conflict is noted above rather than reproduced as a clinical dimension.
- **DeBakey:** “atraumatic” is no guarantee against intimal/serosal/mucosal injury; Marucci did not test a DeBakey device. Narrowed universal ranking, ureter full-thickness grasp, neurovascular preservation and MIS-standard claims. Kept serration explanation, selected uses, practical technique and references.
- **Gerald:** distinguished catalog jaw patterns; Marucci did not prove toothed Gerald is less traumatic than smooth Gerald or establish a layer-by-layer model mandate. Narrowed thin-lumen handling and tissue-specific recommendations while retaining the comparison/technique tables and all inherited references.

**Open:** Read exact local model IFUs and relevant original bench full texts before asserting sterilization, jaw force, quantified safety or superiority. Resolve each remaining tissue-specific instrument preference against technical/operative sources if it is to be elevated beyond an educational option.
