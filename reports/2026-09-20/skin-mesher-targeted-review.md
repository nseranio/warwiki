# Skin mesher — targeted review (2026-09-20)

Complete current and final `mesher.mdx` read, including tables, technique, links and 14 inherited references; no imported page figure/video/dataset. Prior graft/dermatome reviews were used only as context, not clearance. The consequential correction is **model-specific carrier orientation**: [Zimmer's product-family brochure](https://www.zimmerbiomet.lat/content/dam/zimmer-biomet/medical-professionals/surgical-and-cement/zimmer-dermatome-an/skin-grafting-dermatomes-and-meshers-brochure.pdf) describes the **smooth side** for Zimmer Skin Graft Mesher (cutter selects ratio) and **grooved side** for MeshGraft II (carrier selects ratio). The [manufacturer-authored MeshGraft II manual, distributor-hosted](https://a-zortho.com/images/PDF/ZimmerMeshgraftInstructions.pdf), relevant pp. 1–3 and 7, permits either epidermal- or dermal-side-up graft placement for different transfer workflows, warns that the wrong carrier face may shred graft, and prohibits heating/autoclaving the single-use carrier. Its present official revision was not verified. [Herbert 1990 abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2367588/) describes petroleum jelly on a **Tanner** grooved carrier; it is not universal preparation for these two devices.

The [FDA 3:1 carrier recall record](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfres/res.cfm?id=205595) identifies model 00219501300 and lots 65292843/65390419 with 1.5:1 ridge pattern under a 3:1 label. The original record was accessible in the preceding source pass; current direct access returned 503, but its indexed FDA entry reproduced model, lots, reason and action. Page warning is lot-specific, not a blanket mesher recall.

Study scope: [Kan 2020 abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33037725/) reports 51 patients/57 operations, actual expansion 1.16/1.61/2.32 and coverage 1.02/1.29/2.18 for nominal 1.5/3/6, with lower coverage on concave beds. [Henderson 2012 abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22960468/) is **ex-vivo porcine skin**; its 1.36/1.80 and same-axis 2.3× results are no longer blended with Kan's clinical table or presented as a human re-meshing protocol. [Pripotnev 2017 abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28602593/) reports a 210-patient large-burn review and a Canadian surgeon survey; “100%” applies only to responding surgeons experienced with ≥3:1 grafts, not all Canadian burn surgeons. [Lucas 2017 original publisher excerpt](https://doi.org/10.21037/tau.2017.07.06) supports one team's 1.5:1 scrotal practice, not a GU-wide 2:1 default. Pope 1990 is veterinary and cannot establish human sheet-equivalent cosmesis.

The page's “ongoing” Rijpma trial statement was outdated. [Noureldin 2022 original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35248428/) is a randomized **40-child burn** trial; its table remains with that scope. The [published Rijpma 2025 trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40407682/) enrolled **70 intra-patient, two-center** cases and reported greater day-8 take with mesh than Meek (87 ± 19% vs 79 ± 25%, p=.003), with more donor skin and less achieved expansion; the 3-month scar findings favor mesh on most items. The [full 2025 correction](https://doi.org/10.3390/ebj6030039) fixes a cited scar-color reference/discussion wording without changing the authors' conclusions. Original main-paper abstract and indexed relevant methods/results/table excerpts were checked, but publisher HTML returned 429, author-hosted PDF 403, and its complete figures/supplements were **not** reviewed. No 12-month primary result was identified in the targeted search; do not describe it as established.
