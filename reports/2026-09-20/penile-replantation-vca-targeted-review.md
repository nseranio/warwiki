## Focused source/diff findings

- **Acute replantation:** the directly read penile-amputation section of the [ACS 2025 GU-injury guideline](https://www.facs.org/media/ya5hcu0s/genitourinary_guidelines.pdf) supports timely corporal/urethral reconstruction, urgent microsurgical replantation, urgent specialist transfer, two-bag preservation, a macroscopic fallback when transfer and microsurgery are unavailable, and mental-health evaluation. The page now leads with that guidance instead of promising restoration or calling every detailed case step a universal rule.
- **Wang and Jin reports:** the [Wang abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35300997/) is one 25-year-old patient with 10-hour ischemia, 7-hour surgery, Qmax 25 mL/s, near-normal sensation and EHS 4 at six months. The [Jin abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35723457/) reports the authors' glans appearance, micturition, complication and sensation observations. Those outcomes are now explicitly source-specific. Their full video/article packages were not accessible, so fine operative details remain useful teaching but are not clinically cleared.
- **Small-series boundaries:** the Sanger n=4 observations, historical microsurgical/macroscopic patterns, pediatric series, leech/HBO reports and allotransplant experience are no longer presented as expected rates, fixed target counts or comparative effects. A single older infant's fistula is not generalized to an age effect, and adjuncts remain reported case strategies rather than proven salvage regimens.
- **Penile VCA:** the fully accessible 2023 Lopez review was checked in its relevant text, tables and figure captions (image panels not independently inspected). Its five-transplant/two-explant count is historical. It reports vascular congestion and/or hematoma requiring intervention in all five historical cases and tissue necrosis in all but one; the page now cites that source rather than a cadaveric anatomy paper and explicitly rejects a stable-rate interpretation.
- **All-VCA rejection evidence:** the open [Knoedler 2026 systematic review](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/immunology/articles/10.3389/fimmu.2026.1733221/full) included 136 recipients but only one penile VCA. Its abstract, methods, relevant results/table context and limitations were read. The 60% first-year acute-rejection figure and treatment percentages are now labeled all-VCA aggregates dominated by upper-extremity, face and abdominal-wall recipients; overlapping grades/treatments are not penile-specific incidence estimates.
- **Technique and ethics:** case-specific urethral/corporal repairs, immunosuppression targets and prophylaxis were retained but labeled as such. Cadaveric gender-affirming and anterior-pubic-osteotomy studies remain feasibility evidence. Recipient fertility is separated from donor gonads, deceased-donor ethics from recipient flap morbidity, and tacrolimus monotherapy from immune tolerance.
