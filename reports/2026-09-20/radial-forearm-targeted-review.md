The earlier [recovery review](radial-forearm-recovery-review.md) documents a narrower, superseded removal approach; this pass retained the substantive anatomy, technique variants, phalloplasty/urethral applications, prosthesis context and donor counseling, with 44 original references and two verified new ones. It remains a **partial** clinical audit.

Corrections: Falcone's fewer-than-two-veins signal belongs to its 25-patient series, not a universal anastomotic minimum; [Yuan 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9187185/) reports four single-vein cases without vascular events among 38 free flaps. [Tousidonis 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8347905/) found the 39% Doppler-plan-change rate in 87 **oral-cancer** patients, not all phalloplasty candidates. One 237-flap zero-ischemia series does not license omission of perfusion assessment. The [Netshiongolwe 2025 abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41133896/) explicitly grades comparison evidence low/selection-biased and gives a 290-versus-516-minute RFFF/pALT ordering that conflicts with its written “ALT shorter” statement; the page now flags, rather than resolves, that discrepancy. [Wang 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41492140/) is a 57-patient retrospective cohort, not a universal flap-risk ranking; its stricture comparison is nonsignificant. The comparative table was changed from unpaired ranges and absolute quality rankings to anatomy/decision dimensions, while the study-specific numbers above it remain.

Donor closure: Falcone's 34 selected FTSG-versus-Integra/STSG patients showed short-term group differences. The open [Marquez 2024 main and Tables 1–4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11368217/) were read for this claim: 15 volunteers, including only **two** STSG-only patients, no significant patient-reported function/scar or volume advantage for either dermal matrix. Its figures and online volumetric-analysis video were **not** inspected. These small, differently timed comparisons cannot prescribe routine Integra. The page also now distinguishes an optional prosthesis, two-case shaft-only report, selected staged/pedicled variants, individual sensation, and no validated >10-cm threshold.
