# Pudendal flap cluster — targeted source review, September 20

Three complete restored foundation pages were read (EPAP, IPAP, lotus petal); their tables, links and references were inspected. No page imports a local figure, video or clinical data. The separately linked EPAP operative atlas is a companion requiring its own claim review. Earlier IPAP/lotus recovery reports only checked small abstract subsets and their blanket removals were superseded; no source was treated as full text because of those reports.

## Source scope and decisions

- [Tsukuura 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41054917/) original abstract: one 9 × 14-cm hemi-scrotal EPAP with anterior scrotal nerve, primary donor closure, seven-month sensation and no reported pain/stretching. Publisher full text was inaccessible. Changed blanket guaranteed protective sensation and no-pain claims to one-case observations; retained operative steps as technical teaching pending the figures/full method.
- [Phoon 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25037499/) original abstract: six cadavers, eight hemiscrotal and two bipedicled STEPA flaps; mean 1.1-mm thickness, 11-cm (10–12) pedicle and 2.81/4.44-mm external pudendal vessels at the femoral origin. These are **cadaveric free-flap**, not isolated clinical EPAP perforator, measurements. Separated the two throughout. A later [2026 clinical STEPA case](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42495906/) also invalidates “not yet clinical”; it reported 2–3-mm harvested thickness, not 1.1 mm. No comparative source supports the old table's absolute sensation, pain, donor-risk and thickness ranks, so those cells became design-dependent descriptions. No reference or technique section removed.
- [Hashimoto 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24675194/) original abstract: 45 cases/71 flaps, 67 complete, four partial necroses, no total failures; handheld Doppler in its free-style design. Preserved its five listed reconstruction-region counts verbatim as potentially overlapping, not an invented partition. Removed the extrapolation that all major series have zero risk.
- [Legemate 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29428586/) original abstract itself states six of 34 flaps as “9%,” arithmetically 17.6%; nine of 34 second procedures is 26.5%. Marked its published denominator conflict rather than choosing an unverified correction. Full cohort table is needed.
- [Confalonieri 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28570452/) original abstract says 284 women yet lists 234 V-Y and 128 LPF, with event fractions 27/128 and 14/106. Those cannot be reconciled here. The reported 21% versus 13% was nonsignificant (p=0.588); the authors favor tunneled LPF for function/cosmesis in primary malignancy, but full subgroup definition and denominators remain open. Both pages now avoid settling the arms from contradictory abstract numbers.
- [Giroux 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33454227/) original letter abstract: 15 Doppler-mapped subjects, 24 perforators, 27.3-mm mean landmark distance, *routine ultrasound and dissection not always required*. Its printed diameter threshold is “>5 mm,” unlike this page's inherited “>0.5 mm”; no threshold is asserted pending the actual method. A landmark-based design cannot support a general instruction to skip Doppler for a free-style isolated-perforator flap.
- [Hellinga 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29309324/) original abstract: 93 patients/137 flaps; 30.1% no complications, 51.7% grade I–II, 18.3% grade IIIb, 14% donor morbidity. These are **one cohort's patient-level rates**, not pooled or flap-loss rates. Replaced fabricated pooling and cross-study zero-risk comparison; original full tables needed for flap loss and the age model.
- [Coltro 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25456285/) abstract supports preserved tactile/pain/thermal/vibration testing in 25 APER patients at 12 months; it is not proof of every IPAP indication or sexual function. [Han 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37489969/) abstract supports 12.9% versus 37.5% wound complications in its 47-patient selected cohort; neither source was promoted to a universal result.

## Remaining exact access action

Obtain the full publisher articles or institutional copies of Tsukuura/ Phoon for EPAP anatomy and operative figures; Legemate and Confalonieri for conflicting denominators and subgroup tables; Giroux for the printed perforator-diameter discrepancy; Hellinga for flap-loss and model details. Then check Coltro, other IPAP/lotus technical variants and outcome originals, Witte's comparative synthesis, and the linked EPAP atlas separately. The current pages are **partial**, not clinically cleared. This is a local unpublished review, with no commit, push or deployment.
