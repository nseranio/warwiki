# Kelly plication: scoped source correction (20 September 2026)

Read the complete current [Kelly page](../../docs/04-surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/anterior/kelly-plication.mdx), its 14 references, operative steps, tables and links before and after editing. No figure, imported clinical dataset or video is attached. Preserved all practical sections and references. This is **partial claim review, not clinical clearance**. The September 12 [Burch/sling companion report](../2026-09-12/burch-and-sling-companion-review.md) already covers SISTEr endpoints, SUCRA and NICE NG123; those reviews were reused, not repeated.

- [Beck 1991 original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1945199/) is PMID **1945199**, not the unrelated **1945202** formerly linked. The 75% versus 94% genuine-SUI cure comparison concerns **194 patients** inside a 519-anterior-colporrhaphy/25-year series. No randomization or common follow-up is reported in the abstract. Its 6% new detrusor instability applies across surgeries; 11% de novo incontinence applies to **one prolapse subgroup**. Full text and arm-specific methods/denominators were not obtained.
- [Park and Miller 1988 original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/3353049/) is PMID **3353049**, not unrelated **3281007**. The 680-patient observational comparison reports **69% Kelly versus 66% MMK more than three years** after surgery, not a Kelly 1-year rate. Full time-to-event/complication tables were not obtained.
- [Bergman and Elia 1995 original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7631729/) confirms 127 randomized, 107 assessed at one year, **93 at five years**, and 37% Kelly/43% Pereyra/82% Burch five-year objective success. The 30/46/91% cotton-swab findings are restricted to that trial's evaluated patients. The Elsevier/Ovid full paper and arm-level attrition tables remain to access.
- The [Cochrane anterior-repair review](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD001755_anterior-vaginal-repair-urinary-incontinence-women) abstract and indexed original [methods/results sections](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6483165/) identify the comparator as **open retropubic suspension, mostly Burch but including an MMK arm**; the page now supplies the actual 1–5-year, >5-year and reoperation denominators and avoids calling all comparisons Burch. The PMC main was browser-challenged during this pass; only indexed relevant excerpts, the Cochrane official summary and abstract were available. The review's morbidity reporting is too sparse for a comparative safety hierarchy.
- [İncebıyık and Adak 2026 original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41653288/) identifies a **prospective nonrandomized** 48-PLP/92-TOT six-month cohort, with more severe baseline symptoms in the TOT group. PLP is **not Kelly plication**. The nonsignificant 77.1% versus 88.0% pad-count-defined result does not establish equivalence or long-term safety. The publisher full text, operative technique and adverse-event table were not obtained.

Still open: original full methods/tables for Beck, Park, Bergman and PLP; the complete Cochrane risk-of-bias/outcome tables; inherited mechanism, named variants, exact suture placement and complication comparisons against their primary technical/anatomy sources; other reference identities and current guideline scope beyond reused NG123. These access gaps do not justify removing the useful operative description. Epic remains paused.
