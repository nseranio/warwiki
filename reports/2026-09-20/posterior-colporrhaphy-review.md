Source-to-claim corrections:

- [Mowat/Cochrane 2018](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD012975_surgical-management-pelvic-organ-prolapse-women): complete official public abstract and plain-language summary read. The four transanal-versus-transvaginal trials enrolled 191 total, but recurrence and awareness RRs use **two trials, 87 women**, and obstructed-defecation RR uses **three trials, 113**. The RRs are **transanal relative to transvaginal**, not the reverse. Dyspareunia difference is inconclusive. The review finds no routine graft/mesh advantage, and its three-biologic-trial recurrence RR 0.55 (CI 0.30–1.01) is not a proven benefit. Full review tables/plots/supplements were not obtained; PMC direct access challenged and Europe PMC XML returned 500.
- [Paraiso 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17132479/) original abstract: 106 randomized into colporrhaphy 37, site-specific 37, graft-augmented site-specific 32; one-year assessed denominators **28/27/26** with failures **4/6/12**. The overall graft-arm difference does not prove a colporrhaphy-versus-native-site-specific advantage. [Abramov 2005](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15684158/) original abstract: 183 versus 124 in a retrospective, nonrandomized comparison; 14% versus 33% recurrence beyond the midvaginal plane and group-specific postoperative bowel/sexual symptom prevalences. Full papers and adjusted comparisons remain unread. [Karram/Maher 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24142058/) abstract supports an older, qualified Grade B preference for midline plication without levatorplasty, not a definitive modern superiority claim.
- [Nüssler 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9270303/) accessible original main Methods, Results, compartment subgroups and limitations read. The **32,086** figure covers all POP operations; **5,846** were isolated posterior native repairs. Any-compartment reoperation after that posterior subgroup was **11.5% at five years / 16.4% at ten**; same-posterior-compartment reoperation was **3.5% / 4.4%**. The 70% posterior patient-reported cure came from a separate five-year survey excluding reoperated women. Linked figures/supplements were not independently inspected. [Gillor 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30904935/) original abstract: 93 mixed native/mesh repairs, 39/54, mean 5.3 years; 20% clinical recurrence, 32% prolapse symptoms and 33% obstructed defecation are pooled, not native-only.
- [Weber 2000](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10871485/) original abstract: **19% of 81** sexually active before/after surgery had postoperative dyspareunia; **26% of 53** after posterior colporrhaphy and **38% of 21** after concomitant Burch. These are postoperative prevalences, not 19–26% de novo posterior-repair risks. [Bergman 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26935306/) abstract supports a one-year *null difference found* in 577 posterior operations, not proven equivalence of rapid/slow suture absorption.
- [Gluck 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9866699/) original Methods, Tables 2–5 and limitations read: retrospective, selected **49 STARR versus 24 colporrhaphy**; baseline rectocele size and bowel symptom burden differed. The **1.9 versus 3.1 cm** numbers are mean **reductions**, not postoperative sizes or randomized route effects; tables contain apparent typographic inconsistencies, so no new precise symptom comparison was promoted. [Abdelnaby 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33051805/) original abstract supports the CCCS/PISQ values in a **nonrandomized 72-versus-159** LVMR/colporrhaphy cohort, not route superiority. A newer [2025 40-patient RCT](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40423835/) was identified for later full-method and applicability review, not folded into a recommendation from its abstract.
- [Nüssler 2019 recurrent-rectocele registry](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30627830/) original abstract supports historical 626-patient, one-year OR 2.06 but not a present-day US mesh option. The current [FDA regulatory history](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/urogynecologic-surgical-mesh-implants/fdas-activities-urogynecologic-surgical-mesh) confirms the **2018 posterior** and **2019 all remaining transvaginal POP mesh** stop-sale actions, distinct from abdominal sacrocolpopexy mesh. Reused the dated [prior FDA source reading](../2026-09-12/prolapse-source-reading/aspire-main-supplements.md) and checked the current FDA page for drift.
