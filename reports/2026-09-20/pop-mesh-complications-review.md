# POP/SUI mesh complications: targeted safety correction (20 September 2026)

The complete current [mesh complications page](../../docs/04-surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/mesh-complications.mdx) was read, including its classification, management/imaging/outcome tables, procedures, 37 inherited references and related links. It has no imported clinical data, figure or video. This is a **partial page audit**, not clearance of the many inherited numerical outcome and removal claims. The [19 September mesh/graft recovery review](../2026-09-19/mesh-graft-recovery-review.md) supplied context; current sources were checked for the specific edits below.

- The current [FDA mesh overview](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/implants-and-prosthetics/urogynecologic-surgical-mesh-implants) was read across its order, existing-implant advice and three distinct mesh uses. No FDA-approved transvaginal POP mesh product is marketed in the US; the 2019 stop-sale order does not cover SUI slings or transabdominal POP mesh. The inherited selected-patient recommendation was changed to historical/jurisdiction-specific context, not a present US device option. The [joint ACOG/AUGS bulletin abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31663890/) confirms its 2019 interim FDA-order update; full bulletin not reread. Country-by-country ban claims were removed because the cited Cochrane review cannot establish current local law.
- The complete [ICS/IUGA classification calculator](https://www.ics.org/complication) was read. The page had misassigned C1–C7, T1–T4 and omitted S0/S5; these codes now match the official calculator. Divisions/pain modifiers are linked to the full tool rather than improvised.
- The original [ACOG/AUGS Committee Opinion 694 PDF](https://home.smh.com/sections/services-procedures/medlib/docs_articles/OBGYN/ABOG_2017_August/ABOG_Management_694_080817.pdf) was read at recommendations, diagnostic and management sections, Table 1, short/long-term voiding dysfunction, vaginal exposure, POP mesh pain, and asymptomatic removal advice. The [AUGS publication register](https://www.augs.org/clinical-patient-resources/clinical-publications/) lists it as reaffirmed in 2021. The page now distinguishes improving emptying from true retention/high residual, avoids a fixed six-week wait, and keeps tailored drainage, estrogen, specialist referral and removal options. The Martius option is expressly limited to the [Al-Badr 2013 single-case abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23295200/), not attributed to a guideline or treated as standard care; its full text was not read.
- [Frigerio 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37332147/) was checked at abstract plus indexed Wiley results/table excerpts; the full publisher page returned 403. Its 100% sensitivity/NPV came from **three** lower-tract exposures among 100 women (one urethral, two bladder), not a validated universal rule-out test. [Viragh 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30106346/) abstract documents 15 surgical events among 124 selected women and a 93%/72% sensitivity/specificity trade-off when intramural mesh is counted; intraluminal-only sensitivity was 53%. The page now names endpoint/denominator limits and avoids implying ultrasound can replace cystoscopy. The [ACR 2022 abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35550798/) and indexed variant text support question-specific multimodality imaging, but its complete narrative was not retrieved; MRI-versus-ultrasound details remain a source gap.
- The inherited claim of universal **>95% sacrocolpopexy success at five years** was not supported by its cited exposure paper and conflicts with varied outcome definitions in the [E-CARE follow-up](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23677313/) (abstract/indexed tables only in this pass). It was replaced by definition-dependent outcome language without erasing the apical option or exposure warning.

All clinical sections, tables, operative/removal descriptions, links and 37 inherited references were preserved; the joint-bulletin author was corrected and FDA and Martius case-report sources were added. Exact remaining work: review the full MacCraith/Yeung/CARE and other rate sources against the cross-procedure table; obtain full Frigerio and current ACR narrative for imaging details; assess the 2020–24 removal studies, surgical techniques and outcomes by indication; verify the jurisdiction-specific regulatory status only if the page later names a country. No unreviewed rates in those sections are newly certified by this batch.
