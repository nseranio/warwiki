# Female SUI needle-route instruments — targeted review, 21 September 2026

Three complete current pages read: midurethral sling trocars, Raz-Pereyra carrier, Stamey needle. Technical teaching, comparison tables, links and all original references were retained. Changes are local, not published. All three remain **partial**, not clinically cleared.

## Source access and decisions

- Reused the documented TOMUS, 2017 MUS Cochrane and female-sling-guidance readings in `full-length-sling-route-evidence-review.md` and `female-sling-route-guidance-review.md`. TOMUS objective composite met 12-month equivalence (80.8% vs 77.7%); subjective composite (62.2% vs 55.8%) did not. Its 15/298 vs 0/299 bladder perforations invalidate the page's “5 more per 1,000” line. The Cochrane pooled route comparison and current EAU recommendation support cystoscopy with all synthetic MUS, not only retropubic passes. NICE's UK restriction of transobturator use differs from US route options.
- Read English pp. 4–7 (text, warnings and operative steps; not all translated pages or image pixels) of Ethicon's [TVT EXACT IFU, LAB0012869v7 (February 2021)](https://www.jnjmedtech.com/system/files/pdf/GYNECARE%20TVT%E2%84%A2%20EXACT.pdf): one single-use steel-shaft/plastic-handle trocar is loaded sequentially into two implant-connected white sheaths; the separate rigid catheter guide is reusable. The specified pass is vaginal-to-suprapubic, with cystoscopy after both sheaths and before mesh positioning. Other kit IFUs, present stock revision and device-specific comparative safety remain open. No universal angle/shaft-risk causal claim is warranted.
- Read the complete six-page text, methods, Tables 1–7 and figure captions (not figure pixels or supplements) of [Zyczkowski 2014](https://ppm.edu.pl/docstore/download/SUM170f27b0a2a54cd6a4d9c2b41bca5ec6/0000078020.wcag.pdf): sequential nonrandom TVT, branded TOT and self-prepared TOT cohorts; **both** TOT cohorts used a Stamey needle. It is not a Stamey-versus-helical-trocar trial. Ludwig 2016 original open search-indexed sections/abstract and Figure 4 caption support 29/35 versus 13/46 within a 3–5 mm gap in small nonrandom PVDF-TOT cohorts; direct PMC access recaptcha and Wiley full text inaccessible. This is a positioning result, not proven clinical superiority.
- Read the current [EAU female-LUTS surgery recommendations](https://uroweb.org/guidelines/non-neurogenic-female-luts/chapter/disease-management) and relevant [NICE NG123 recommendations](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng123/chapter/recommendations). Reused prior AUA/SUFU 2023 indexed statements; its full guideline PDF remains inaccessible (403). NICE 1.5.14 says do not offer needle suspension; this does not mean a Stamey **instrument** cannot carry PVS sutures.
- The [Bailly/Carlson 2017 PVS technical review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28616116/) abstract and PMC-indexed operative passages describe finger-guided suprapubic passage of Stamey eyelets **or a clamp**, then cystoscopy. Direct PMC full text returned recaptcha; no source established the site's Raz carrier as mandatory, a 25–30 cm/forked design, a universal 1×8 cm graft or one fixed suburethral level. The [Brubaker 2012 SISTEr five-year abstract](https://repository.niddk.nih.gov/publications/1673) gives strict continence 30.8% fascial sling vs 24.1% Burch and different satisfaction; no 2017 update or superiority against all alternatives was found. Full article/tables remain unread.
- The [CSS Surgical current Stamey listing](https://www.cssurgical.com/stamey-needles.html) identifies straight, 15° and 30° variants, not length/reprocessing. The [2021 case report abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34020989/) and indexed operative description show an **eyelet**, nylon suture and Dacron bolster, with one very late erosion case. Direct PMC and BMJ full text were blocked (recaptcha/403); no incidence can be inferred. Actual model IFU remains open.
- Primary abstracts were read for [Kondo 1998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9720540/), [Nigam 2000](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11111183/), [Gofrit 1998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9748682/), [Conrad 1997](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9112503/), [Clemens 1998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9679880/), and [Ramon 1991](https://scholars.duke.edu/publication/676748). Corrected mixed-procedure denominator (Nigam early 93% and repeat surgery), Gofrit's overall versus pure-SUI rates, and the 36-person recurrent series' comparative overreach. These abstracts do not establish complete long-term harm profiles.
- The [2017 needle-suspension Cochrane abstract/indexed text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6483551/) gives 91/313 versus 47/297 failures at one year or more against open retropubic suspension, based on small low-certainty trials; sling comparison inconclusive. Full Cochrane publisher was 403, direct PMC recaptcha, Europe PMC error. Keep its full text/tables as an exact unresolved source action.
- The [Zahn 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17329523/), [Madhuvrata 2012](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/97348/) and [Kim 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30865553/) abstracts support narrowly scoped cadaver anatomy, mixed-design inside-out vaginal injury and selected high-risk pooled comparisons; full tables/figures were not accessed. The original angle/vascular/bowel rate and blanket long-term cure claims were not supported at their existing precision, so they were narrowed instead of replacing the teaching.
