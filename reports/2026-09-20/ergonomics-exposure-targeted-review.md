## Consequential corrections

- The 2018 [surgeon WMSD meta-analysis](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamasurgery/fullarticle/2666200) reports pooled diagnosis prevalence, not a 40% prospective injury risk. Its 12% work-change figure has a WMSD denominator; 19% institutional reporting came from one smaller included survey, not all injured surgeons. The [urologist survey](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24417180/) had 285 respondents, of whom 245 (86%) reported a recent complaint; its modality odds ratios are associations, not causal effects.
- The EMG 7.4% versus 5.3% figure belongs to [Dalsgaard's paired cross-sectional hysterectomy study](https://research.regionh.dk/da/publications/robotic-surgery-is-less-physically-demanding-than-laparoscopic-su/) of 12 experienced surgeons: it is median neck activity as percent of maximal EMG, **not** time above 50% MVC, and low-back activity was higher in robotic cases. [Hotton's multicenter randomized ROBOGYN study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36175710/) measured perceived Borg/NASA-TLX workload rather than EMG. [Norasi's survey](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10709269/) had only 14 predominantly robotic respondents; 1/14 reported NMSD, so it cannot establish prevention by robotic modality.
- [Park's 2017 cohort](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28059962/) included 66 surgeons **and OR staff**, not 66 surgeons; no statistically detected duration or approach difference does not prove equivalence. The [ASGE 2023 guideline](https://www.asge.org/docs/default-source/guidelines/asge-guideline-on-the-role-of-ergonomics-methodology.pdf?sfvrsn=602fe5c_1) makes a conditional microbreak recommendation on very-low-certainty evidence; 100%/91.7%/83.3% came from a small 12-day self-report pilot cited within it. The [2025 review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41087097/) pooled active-break standardized effects but described passive breaks narratively.
- [Giagio's randomized trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30672801/) compared program versus control **at six months**, not before-versus-after. Its full manuscript Table 2 gives lower-back symptoms of 31/58 (53.4%) versus 49/68 (72.0%), p=.04; the abstract gives a discordant percentage pair, so the page uses tabulated counts and flags that disagreement. Analgesic use was 15.5% versus 30.9% between groups. Physical inactivity OR 2.44 is a separate observational association. The [resistance-training commentary](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12400452/) extrapolates its proposed 20-minute twice-weekly dose and mortality discussion from other populations; it is not a surgeon outcome trial. [Gleave 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jsurg.2025.103512) is a narrative review citing a prior self-report program, not a new trial proving performance.
- Practical setup, posture, instrument, team and exercise guidance and all 25 references remain. Fixed robotic break and compression-stocking thresholds were qualified; retractors reduce rather than eliminate manual retraction. No heading, table, reference, image, video or manufacturer instruction was removed. No public audit notice was added.
