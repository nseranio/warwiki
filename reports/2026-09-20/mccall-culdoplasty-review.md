# McCall culdoplasty — source-scope review (20 September 2026)

Read the complete current and revised MDX, its indications, operative variants, outcome/comparison/complication tables, 16 final references, links and two VideoCards. No imported data or embedded figure. The videos were preserved but not watched. This is a **partial** clinical/operative audit, not clearance.

The consequential error was the page's attribution of **SUPeR five-year 38%/53% failure** to Menefee 2024. The cited trial is **ASPIRe**, in women with vault prolapse *after total hysterectomy*; its native-tissue arm was mostly sacrospinous fixation with some USLS, **not McCall**. The documented [complete ASPIRe source review](../2026-09-12/prolapse-source-reading/aspire-main-supplements.md) was reused: modeled **36-month composite failure 43% native repair versus 28% sacrocolpopexy**, with anatomy, symptoms and retreatment in the endpoint. The erroneous trial name, time point and direct McCall implication were corrected throughout this page.

Other source-to-claim corrections:

- [Niblock 2017](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5389989/) original Europe PMC full-text XML: operative Methods, relevant Results, Tables 1–5 and ureter-obstruction account read. Its **70 vaginal McCall** operations used one or two internal sutures plus an external suture and routine cystoscopy; one suture was released after obstruction was detected. The comparison group had **73 laparoscopic uterosacral plications** with a different route and different surgeon. This supports practical technique and safety checking, not identical abdominal/laparoscopic outcomes or a randomized route advantage. No figure or supplement was present in the XML. The paper's remaining tables/discussion were not read for this claim.
- [Cruikshank/Kovac 1999](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10203653/) complete original abstract, also [reviewed earlier](../2026-09-19/moschcowitz-recovery-review.md): 100 randomized at vaginal hysterectomy to three prophylactic closures; three-year posterior enterocele **2/32 McCall-type, 10/33 vaginal Moschcowitz-type, 13/33 peritoneal closure**. It does not estimate treatment of established post-hysterectomy vault prolapse. Full paper unavailable in this pass.
- [Alas 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28779416/) original abstract: retrospective **38 advanced versus 273 lesser** uterovaginal-prolapse cases, advanced defined by exteriorized POP-Q point relative to vaginal length. Overall success **76.3% versus 68.5%, p=.3553** means no difference detected, not equivalence; anterior failures **18.4% versus 6.2%**. [Bushra 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33984523/) original abstract: retrospective, single-surgeon **490** modified McCall cases, mean 2.8 years; objective support **97.1%**, subjective success **94.1%**, vault reoperation **1.0%**, ureteral kinking **2.9%**. These are distinct populations and endpoints, not universal rates.
- [Schiavi 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29518641/) original abstract: retrospective **200 McCall/214 Shull**, median 8.9 years, **two vault prolapses in each group**; McCall shortened vaginal length more and Shull group had greater sexual-function-score improvement. [Spelzini 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27048368/) original abstract: retrospective Shull selection by age/sexual activity; approximately **8 mm** longer vaginal length, no significant anatomic difference detected. [Novara 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31351324/) original abstract: retrospective **155 McCall/69 Shull**, surgeon-familiarity selection, median **15/13 months**, objective cuff recurrence **4/155 versus 1/69** without a statistically detected difference. None establishes equivalence or a general complication ranking.
- [Parisi 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32594190/) original abstract: retrospective **34/34**, stage ≥II recurrence **11/34 traditional versus 2/34 DLS**, but further treatment **2/34 versus 1/34**, mean follow-up ~23 months. [Ettore 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39110177/) original abstract: observational **85/86** comparison; length **8.3 versus 6.4 cm** at 12 months, with no established recurrence difference. These observations support named variants, not a proven universal winner.
- [Arkan 2026 issue / online 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41337896/) original abstract: prospective **46/46** vNOTES high-USLS/McCall cohort, cuff prolapse **1/46 versus 6/46** at 12 months, length **9.0 versus 8.2 cm**, one ureteral and one bladder injury in McCall arm. The issue year was corrected; its small short-follow-up, mixed route/technique design cannot supply a general 0–1% injury rate. Publisher full text returned 403. [Silva 2025](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0301211525009601) public indexed abstract/method excerpts report **four included studies, 497 women** and qualitative synthesis with heterogeneity; the indexed study-selection prose inconsistently says six before exclusions. It does not justify a McCall recurrence ranking; publisher direct access returned 403.

The 2026 concomitant-continence and 2024 transvaginal-mesh Cochrane reviews remain contextual, not sources for McCall suture steps; their previously documented public reviews were reused from [POP source reading](../2026-09-12/prolapse-source-reading/principles.md) and [posterior review](posterior-colporrhaphy-review.md). The source-specific comparisons replaced a cross-study percentage league table. All 14 inherited references, both videos, operative variants and related links were retained; two original technical/comparative references were added. No substantive section was replaced with an audit notice.
