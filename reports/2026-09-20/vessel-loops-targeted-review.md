# Vessel loops — 21 September 2026

The full original/revised 119-line MDX, two comparative tables, all 11 inherited references and related links were read; no imported data, local figures or media. **Partial claim review, not clinical clearance.** All sections, uses, tables, references and links remain; three sources were added. Corrections concern overstated atraumatic/ureteral claims, product geometry and IFU, trial designs and denominators, wound-closure time, and ex-vivo-to-clinical inference.

- [Pons-Riverola 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-17592-z): full accessible article text, methods, results, Tables 1–3 values and figure captions read; figure pixels and underlying individual dataset not read. Twelve ex-vivo tests (8 Potts, 4 Rummel), human cadaveric femoral/porcine aorta, all occluded; 305.75 vs 564.50 g mean initiation force (p=0.027). Its abstract gives Potts SD 106.07 g, results 73.50 g; Table 3 assigns 106.07 to the micro-size subgroup, not the technique. The page avoids the contested SD. Actual histologic injury, ischemia time and clinical superiority were **not** measured. Manufacturer TELIC supported the experiment/materials; this is not an IFU.
- [Manship 1985](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4014883/) and [Moore 1985](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4014882/): primary abstracts read, not full SEM images/tables. The atherosclerotic study observed injury from tested DeBakey/Cooley/Fogarty/bulldog clamps but none by SEM from double Silastic loops after 15-minute application in sampled amputation segments; the normal canine experiment is similarly scoped. This cannot assert no injury with all loops/tensions, vessels or prolonged occlusion. [Ho 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18721046/) primary abstract supports the feeding-tube/Hem-o-Lok technique and <15-second placement in its 25-patient series; full text and comparison absent.
- [TELIC DORMO-LOOP catalog](https://www.surgicalholdings.co.uk/media/files/TELIC%20ENGLISH%5B1%5D.pdf) PDF page 14, including product table (400 mm, 0.7–5.3 mm widths), material, purpose and color mapping, read as manufacturer catalog, not full current IFU. [DeRoyal radiopaque vessel-loop IFU](https://www.deroyal.com/docs/default-source/product-literature/0-1530-vessel-loop-ifu.pdf?sfvrsn=6d482848_4), revised 8/2022, both pages read: single use, no cutting/reprocessing/resterilizing, count/remove and radiographic limitations; nonsterile **new** supply may be processed by the specified ethylene-oxide method. These are distinct examples, not universal product dimensions or permissions. Actual in-service product IFU remains required.
- [Kakagia 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22377275/) and [Johnson 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30064725/) primary abstracts read; full texts/tables unavailable. Kakagia randomizes 50 patients with 82 wounds, reporting shorter closure, five VAC patients needing grafts and contemporaneous €14 vs €135/day costs. Johnson stopped early; 5/5 vs 1/9 result is the small subset still open after first reoperation, not all randomized patients. [Onoe 2023](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12891-023-06849-1) accessible full text, methods/results, Table 1/2 referenced and figure captions read, not table-image cells or figure pixels: 13 shoelace vs 12 historical comparisons, 0 vs 6 grafts, no significant closure-time difference; group medians are internally inconsistent between abstract/results and discussion, so exact comparison withheld. [Arumugam 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33161435/) and [Steen 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33065680/) primary abstracts read only; electric-burn wound series is nonrandomized, pediatric 33-case no-ED-return outcome is not zero recurrence across full follow-up. [Lutnick 2025](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12695471/) full relevant methods/results, Table 2 and 25-study review Table 5 plus limitations read, not figure pixels/supplement: retrospective small shoelace subgroup and heterogeneous qualitative review suggest less grafting than packing, no significant closure-time difference in that cohort.
