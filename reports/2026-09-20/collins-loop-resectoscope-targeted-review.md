The September 12 endoscopy review in [`tools.md`](../2026-09-11/full-site-review/tools.md) was reused for unchanged background; it was not counted as fresh full-text access. ## Preservation and remaining gaps

All operative-use sections, selection/complication tables, specialized loops, hysteroscopic context, links and inherited references were retained; no page figure, video or imported dataset existed. The inherited NCCN bibliography entry remains a secondary background reference, **not** a source used to adjudicate the new repeat-TURBT criteria; its exact current version remains to be checked. Changes narrowed claims without deleting useful teaching. A focused diff/source check also corrected an initially malformed EAU URL before validation.

Still unfinished: exact current IFUs for the chosen monopolar and bipolar resectoscope/electrode/generator combinations (including voltage, mode, irrigant, insulation and reuse); full Cochrane and ERBT RCT/meta-analysis tables/figures/supplements for stronger numerical comparisons; full source review of inherited specialty-loop, hysteroscopy, complication-rate and oncologic references; and procedure-specific anesthetic/irrigation protocols. These gaps remain internal and prevent clinical clearance.
