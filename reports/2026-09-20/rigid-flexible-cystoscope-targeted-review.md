September 20, 2026. Both complete current pages, tables, references, links and video-card declarations were read. The [September 12 tools report](../2026-09-11/full-site-review/tools.md) was checked for prior source work; it did **not** document a dedicated review of these two cystoscope pages. Both are **partial claim reviews, not clinical clearances**. All edits remain local and undeployed.

[EAU 2026 NMIBC Diagnosis](https://uroweb.org/guidelines/non-muscle-invasive-bladder-cancer/chapter/diagnosis): relevant §§5.8, 5.10–5.12 full online text and recommendations read. | AUGS recommends cystoscopy at prolapse reconstruction except isolated posterior repair and confirming ureteral efflux; it does **not** mandate a universal two-lens sequence. EAU supports complete inspection and nuanced blue-light/NBI detection claims, not categorical recurrence or universal optical superiority. Practical lens/inspection teaching and both video cards were retained. |
| [AUA/SUFU 2025 microhematuria](https://www.auanet.org/documents/Guidelines/PDF/2025%20Guidelines/MH%20Unabridged%2002.2025FINAL.pdf) and [AUA/CUA/SUFU 2025 rUTI](https://www.auanet.org/documents/Guidelines/PDF/2025%20Guidelines/rUTI%20Update/rUTI%202025%20Unabridged%20Final.pdf): official **guideline-statement excerpts** retrieved; direct full-PDF web opening failed, so risk tables/discussions were not read. EAU still recommends against routine prophylaxis for flexible/rigid urethrocystoscopy after weighing low absolute benefit and resistance. Holmes' noninferiority applies to two trial devices and −10% margin. Female pain RCTs differ; no universal equivalence claim. |

Obtain exact rigid sheath/bridge/telescope IFUs and chosen reusable-scope reprocessing instructions; verify implant-specific AUS details and the full Otis-Chapados table before applying ex-vivo estimates to a patient. Read full AUA guideline risk tables/discussions, full Cochrane prophylaxis/blue-light reviews and the inherited single-use cost, infection, environmental, optical and surveillance studies before broader numerical conclusions. The flexible page's single-use cost/waste values and enhanced-imaging surveillance estimates remain **study-specific but incompletely adjudicated**. Video titles/clinical demonstrations were not independently watched; the declarations were preserved. No clinician sign-off is claimed.
