## Source access and outcome

- **NIHR Fader et al 2008, 224-page HTA original PDF:** read abstract/methods/results/conclusions on pp 4–5 and 11, pertinent community design/preference/cost sections pp 55, 86–93, and nursing-home discussion pp 124 and 136, including reported table text. Other tables/appendices and figure pixels were not inspected. The three crossover studies support historical UK design choices, not universal 2026 rankings. The associated 2008 Cochrane review's full HTML comparison descriptions were also read selectively, not every pooled analysis.
- **AUA/SUFU 2024 OAB guideline:** official indexed PDF statements 9–14 and journal summary excerpts on management strategies; direct full PDF could not be retrieved. Guideline says no RCT compares broad UUI management strategies head-to-head, while the NIHR trial compares particular absorbent designs. Both propositions can coexist; keep source access partial.
- **ICS working-group pad-test teaching module (Krhut 2014), Araujo 2022 diagnostic systematic review, and Domínguez Argomedo 2026 PPI cohort:** primary abstracts read. The 2022 review reports sensitivity 60–93% and specificity 60–84% for long-duration protocols, not the 1-hour test. Its 1-hour tests were more accurate but less reproducible. The 2026 cohort's overall rho 0.94 reflects 8,787 paired measurements in 2,040 post-prostatectomy men, not a universal count-to-weight rule. Full papers, tables, protocol figures and supplements not read.
- **Graham et al 2025 Cochrane IAD prevention update:** primary abstract read, including 15 trials/1,020 participants, heterogeneous small studies and low/very-low certainty. Product-specific cleanser or barrier superiority cannot be confidently promised. The earlier skin-care references on the page remain to be reviewed in full.
- **CDC CAUTI guideline evidence review:** read relevant external-versus-indwelling sections, evidence table 2A recommendations and nursing-home/SCI caveats. External sheaths are for selected cooperative men without retention/obstruction; evidence for a composite benefit is low quality and cannot prove reductions in each individual infection outcome.
- **Fletke et al 2024 AAFP corrected full HTML:** read external-device indications/contraindications and Table 1; its 300-mL PVR orientation is not a universal safe cutoff. **Saint 2006 RCT** and **Macaulay 2015 device trial** primary abstracts were read; 75-person RCT composite benefit and 56-person preference/activity differences retain their population limits. Full results tables/supplements not accessed.
- **Coloplast Conveen Optima:** manufacturer US product page read for model-specific integrated adhesive, silicone and *up to* 24-hour wear. Manufacturer 2016 UK fitting-guide searchable full text covers six fit steps, single use/≤24 hours and bag emptying; PDF figure pixels and current complete model IFU were not accessed. Do not extrapolate to other sheaths or claim no skin irritation.
