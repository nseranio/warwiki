September 20, 2026. The complete current article, all 34 references, tables and links were read. It has no imported clinical dataset, page figure or video. This is a **partial** clinical/technical review, not clearance. The prior September 20 restoration and needles/sutures review were reused; their source access was not silently upgraded.

## Claim outcomes and exact source access

- Reused the [restoration review](selective-content-restoration.md) of Medtronic V-Loc catalog/technical fields, Corza Quill configuration/material/sizing fields and J&J product-family text. Those were targeted manufacturer sections, **not complete current device IFUs**. The article correctly keeps Quill's one-size-up recommendation product-specific and bench failure rates outside patient outcomes.
- Reused the [needles/sutures review](needles-sutures-targeted-review.md) abstract checks: [Liatsikos 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23292256/) 5/6 Quill recurrences, [Radford 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29116864/) 4/10 pediatric adverse drainage/three redos, and [Anand 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35549514/) five-study pooled redo RR 6.00 (95% CI 0.78–46.14). The current page appropriately treats these as conflicting, small or imprecise signals, not a brand-wide rule. No full papers/tables were reopened.
- Newly read [Gupta 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27418451/) original abstract: 82% PGA-PCL versus 24% V-Loc 180 failure refers to a specific 4-N saline-tank Lapra-Ty/Hem-o-Lok **closure-system test**, not clinical renorrhaphy failures. Full paper/figures remain unread.
- Newly read [Li 2015](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0131167) full original HTML methods/results/discussion and Table 2 text: nine studies/786 patients, VUA time savings, leak 7.08% versus 3.37% (OR 2.17, CI 0.86–5.50), no statistically significant difference in specified continence intervals. The page correctly avoids calling non-significance equivalence or making VUA results a graft-quilting rule. Individual forest-plot images and supplements were not inspected.
- Newly read [Hu 2025](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12885-025-13825-6) open main methods/results/discussion and [Tables 1](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12885-025-13825-6/tables/1) and [2](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12885-025-13825-6/tables/2): 25 versus 25 selected T1 RAPN patients, WIT 11.76 versus 14.56 minutes. Its double-needle barbed and Vicryl arms used different closure systems. Table 3/4 pages, image panels and operative-video supplements were not inspected; long-term renal benefit cannot be inferred.
- Newly read [Clapp 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31183792/) original abstract: 18 published suture-related obstructions plus 14 MAUDE reports, with the authors recommending exploration for these cases. Its case-level table/full text were not available. The article's management row was scoped explicitly to the reported cases.
- Newly found [Sassun 2026](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00464-026-12580-4) publisher **abstract only** (full article paywalled): among 20,260 intra-abdominal barbed-suture operations, 264 obstruction diagnoses, 102 obstruction reoperations and 279 total abdominal reoperations, none was attributed to suture entrapment in operative-note review. Added this denominator evidence without erasing the case reports or claiming zero risk. No non-barbed comparison, full methods, source tables or figure panels were accessed.
