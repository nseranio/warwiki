# Manual dermatome cluster — targeted review (2026-09-20)

Complete current MDX, tables, links and reference reads for **Padgett-Hood drum** (7 refs), **Goulian/Weck** (4 inherited + 1 new catalog ref), and **Humby** (8 inherited + 1 historical-object ref). Earlier September 20 recovery reviews covered superseded shorter versions, not clinical clearance of restored content. No page figure, video or imported dataset exists. Practical teaching and references were retained; corrections were claim-specific.

## Reused and newly checked source access

- [Kuo & Ohara 2003 original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12562349/): six gluteal/perianal hidradenitis patients, Padgett-Hood drum used on excised tissue, 0.012–0.020-inch settings, 1.5× meshing, no recurrence over 8–36 months, one 3 × 4 cm graft-necrosis/infection event. Full original PDF appeared in search but was not read; no GU/scrotal cohort.
- [Tang 2010 original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20647934/): 0.024–0.026-inch very thick grafts for burned mobile limbs using **a Padgett dermatome**, without naming a drum model; no randomized contracture comparison. Full paper not read.
- [Jaquet 2012 original main HTML](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaotolaryngology/fullarticle/1106947): relevant surgical methods, abstract, Tables 1–3 and discussion accessed. Thigh STSG harvested at 0.35–0.45 mm with **a Padgett dermatome**, not identified as drum; case-control comparison was STSG versus ulnar-based donor closure in head/neck RFFF, not phalloplasty/device models. Figure panels not inspected.
- [Hunt 1979 original indexed full-text/abstract](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1397028/): 60 deep dorsal-hand/finger burn patients; eschar excision used **Humby or Goulian-Weck**, then mesh graft. 100% take in all but four hands is a combined-series outcome, not knife-specific. Complete article figures/tables were not accessed after PMC challenge.
- [Wormald 2020 Cochrane original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32882071/): one RCT, 61 pediatric partial-thickness burn patients, **very-low-certainty** estimates with little/no demonstrated difference in healing, infection, operating time or six-month scar outcomes. Full 2020 review not read in this batch.
- [Teleflex/Pilling Goulian catalog](https://www.teleflexsurgicalcatalog.com/surgical/pilling/specialty/general?brand_ids%5B%5D=1&brand_ids%5B%5D=2&page=39&term=): manufacturer listings for **interchangeable fixed-gauge** guards, 2 3/8-inch listed guard length and multiple nominal depths. Full current IFU and regional availability not established.
- [Science Museum Humby object record](https://collection.sciencemuseumgroup.org.uk/objects/co176847/humby-skin-grafting-razor-in-wooden-case-razors): **Thomas Graham Humby**, 1934 frame design, 1936 simpler knife with adjustable roller. Original 1934/1936 BMJ articles not read.
- [Tehrani 2006 original abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16799313/): 238 respondents/30 British units, 61% powered preference, 40% perceived litigation concern; belief survey, not device-superiority proof.
- [Cohen 2020 original publisher abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0305417920303491): 15-surgeon single-center pomelo simulation (8 senior, 7 resident); 0.15 versus 0.45 area consistency measure, not patient outcome. [Cao 2023 original abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0305417922001942): retrospective 43-versus-43 burn cohort, not a randomized or GU comparison.
- [Dargan 2025 aperture abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40313029/) carried from the immediately preceding [powered-device review](dermatome-device-targeted-review.md); it did not measure harvested tissue and does not validate a Humby feeler/scalpel test. No duplicate source read.

## Claim outcomes

1. **Padgett-Hood:** kept the six-patient reused-skin case example but removed claims that all diseased excision specimens can be reused in GU sites or that no powered device can work on detached tissue. Tang and Jaquet name **Padgett**, not **Padgett-Hood**, so their thickness and RFFF observations are no longer misattributed to the drum. Removed unsupported drum-specific free-flap/phalloplasty and commercial-availability assertions. Adhesive-drum preparation is no longer mixed with powered-dermatome skin lubrication.
2. **Goulian/Weck:** replaced a continuously adjustable guard claim with manufacturer-listed **interchangeable fixed-gauge guards**. Hunt's graft-take outcome is explicitly a mixed Humby/Goulian excision series. Removed device-use ranking, glans/pediatric outcome extrapolation, and an unsupported assertion that hydrosurgery is universally more tissue-sparing/better.
3. **Humby:** corrected inventor from George to **Thomas Graham** and distinguished 1934 frame from 1936 knife. Removed unverified universal blade interchange/reprocessing and the powered-device scalpel-test calibration instruction. Hunt's combined outcome, retrospective Cao comparison, very-low-certainty Cochrane result and nonclinical Cohen simulator are now labeled with their actual denominators/designs.

## Unresolved — all three remain partial

Current manufacturer IFUs, complete original drum-care/dermal-graft papers, Kuo/Hunt/Tang full packages and figure panels, Weck/Goulian guard/blade compatibility, contemporary device availability, and genital/pediatric/oncologic procedure-specific evidence remain open. No scrotal, penile, phalloplasty or vaginoplasty protocol is clinically cleared by these burn/hand/head-and-neck reports. Full source and diff review is necessary before stronger selection or superiority claims. Local edits only; publication is deferred.
