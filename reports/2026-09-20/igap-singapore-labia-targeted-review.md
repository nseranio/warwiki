## Source scope and corrections

- [Gould 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41190571/) original abstract describes 194 **mixed gluteal** reconstructions, not an IGAP-only cohort. [Benedict 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37418850/) original abstract compares 69 IGAP/BIGAP with 47 VRAM in a retrospective cohort; nonsignificant complication differences do not establish equivalence. [Kreisel BIOPEX-2](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39908025/) original abstract reports a **negative primary** 30-day healing endpoint, with lower abscess/drainage as secondary outcomes. Later [post-hoc win-ratio analysis](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41406011/) is not a replacement for the primary endpoint. Abstracts read; complete Gould/Benedict/BIOPEX tables and supplemental methods not accessed. Wiley/PMC and publisher access did not yield the full necessary package.
- [Thiele 2020](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/oncology/articles/10.3389/fonc.2020.00668/full) **full HTML main text** read, including methods, Tables 1–4 and discussion: its GFF was **subfascial**, 12 GFF versus 17 gracilis patients, shorter elevation and more sitting pain in GFF; no significant overall complication difference. Figure 3 **caption**, not image, read; no supplement. [Winterton 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23098585/) original abstract supports 124/127 surviving GFFs, not a universal zero-loss rate. Earlier [Legemate 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29428586/) abstract labels six of 34 wound events “9%” despite arithmetic of 17.6%; its full denominator table is needed.
- [Monstrey 2001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11304597/) original abstract reports complete survival of 31 selected Singapore flaps and no dilation in that series. [Gleeson 1994](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8088603/) original abstract reports four partial and one complete bilateral necrosis among eight oncologic patients, not a universal radiation contraindication. [Clifton 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27682131/) original abstract describes **ongoing dilation** after postradiation stenosis repair. [Han 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37489969/) compares IPAP against PAP/TUG, not axial Singapore flap; the [Alammar Fournier review](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.burns.2026.107888) pooled flap-loss estimate cannot be assigned to one technique. Full operative method/functional tables for these series remain unread.
- [Gupta 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35654273/) original abstract has seven mixed fistulas, one radiation VVF dry only after later colpocleisis, a specifically described capacious vagina/regular menses in one UVF case and a dry vesicolabial case at six months; it does **not** state an overall 6/7 flap-only closure rate. Elsevier full case table/operative figures unavailable. [Rothenberger](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41385083/) original abstract maps ten cadaveric **Martius fat pads**; right versus left vascular density differs by 8%, medial versus lateral vascularity does not significantly differ. Online 2025, print 2026. [Kwong 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40162503/) original abstract covers 136 **mixed vulvar flaps** in 69 women: 126/136 none/mild day 7, 113/136 days 8–30, five necrotic flaps in two women; questionnaire improvements in subsets are not technique-specific causal effects.
