# Perineorrhaphy claim-scope review — 20 September 2026

Read the complete current [perineorrhaphy page](../../docs/04-surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/posterior-enterocele/perineorrhaphy.mdx), all 24 references, links, tables and operative steps before and after editing. No imported data, embedded figure or video exists. This is a **partial** claim-level review, not clinical clearance. All practical technique, anatomy, outcomes, obstetric context, related links and inherited references remain; no section or table was deleted. The prior [NICE/POP source reading](../2026-09-12/prolapse-source-reading/principles.md) is reused for general route context, not counted as a perineorrhaphy trial.

Primary-source checks and corrections:

- [Kanter 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4670594/) main Introduction, Methods, Results, technique discussion and Tables 1–2: surgeon survey, not efficacy evidence. It documents variable indications, muscle selection, septal attachment (110/183), levator plication (29/183) and no standardized technique. The linked survey supplement was not read. Kept the teaching steps as examples, distinguished optional septal attachment and separate levator plication, and removed an unverified ACOG-specific operative instruction.
- [Swieten 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12898559/) accessible full-text XML: abstract, Methods, Table 1, Results and limitations checked. The 5.2 vs 4.5 cm GH, obesity OR 2.3 and parity OR 30 describe **surgeon selection** among 285 operations, not the risk reduction or benefit of perineoplasty. No postoperative outcomes were measured in that report.
- [Bonglack 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8929254/) accessible main text, Methods, Tables 1–4, Results and discussion checked; figure images/supplements not inspected. Among 56 followed at three months, 29 received surgeon-selected perineorrhaphy. Corrected p from `<0.001` to `<0.01`, specified resting versus Valsalva GH, noted the 0.5-cm postoperative change also occurred without perineorrhaphy, and retained the -1.5 vs -0.5 cm contrast without causal attribution. [Mothes 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36493583/) was accessible as original abstract only: 121 combined repairs, 44 to 31 mm, 87% cure/90% partial-or-full goal achievement. Its full methods/outcome tables were not read; the page now attributes these values to that cohort, not isolated efficacy.
- [Rotstein 2023](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10540923/) accessible full-text XML: eligibility, operative/follow-up methods, Tables 1–2, Results and limitations checked. The 131 operated/119 symptom-follow-up population had postpartum deficient perineum and excluded concomitant POP/incontinence surgery. The -9.1/33 symptom change is uncontrolled and cannot establish prolapse prevention; the page now says so. Figure images and supporting files were not inspected.
- [Sutkin 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6995410/) original indexed main Methods/Results/discussion and publisher abstract checked. OPTIMAL's secondary comparison was 190 with versus 184 without **posterior repair**; posterior technique was not standardized and may or may not have included perineorrhaphy. The 66.7% vs 62.0%, aOR 1.07 result is not an isolated perineorrhaphy effect. Original PMC full retrieval was challenged; source-image/supplement packages remain unread. [Siff 2020](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7526641/) original indexed Methods/Results/discussion checked: postoperative resting GH is a risk marker; the exploratory 3.5-cm cutoff had weak discrimination and is not a proven operative target. Full tables/appendices were not retrieved.
- [Blomquist 2018 JAMA](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2718794) original Methods, Table 3 and Results checked: the GH 3-cm HR 3.0 and ≥3.5-cm HR 9.0 are correctly attributed to **Blomquist**, stratified by delivery mode, for incident POP—not to a “Handa 2019” paper and not to treatment efficacy. [DeLancey 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39800182/) original abstract supports the 68% **resting MRI urogenital-hiatus area** contrast in anterior-predominant POP, a different measure than POP-Q GH length; full MRI paper/figures remain to review. [Kochová 2019](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29994970/) original abstract supports the histology percentages in sampled postmenopausal tissue; full histology sampling details not read.
- [Cervantes 2026](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40981998/) original abstract only: retrospective 715 native-tissue vaginal POP operations, 281 with perineoplasty, retention defined as failed retrograde voiding trial requiring discharge catheter. Corrected p to `<0.01`, stated the precise endpoint and avoided causal inference. The publisher full paper/adjusted model is not accessible in this pass. [Shakhaliev 2024](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11446006/) protocol and [ClinicalTrials.gov record](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05422209) checked: registry status is **unknown** after July 2024, with no posted results; “ongoing RCT” is not a current verified status. The protocol's planned n=310 is not an outcome. [AAFP 2021](https://www.aafp.org/afp/2021/0615/p745) and [Kettle/Cochrane 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23152204/) distinguish immediate obstetric tear repair from elective POP surgery and support only the obstetric short-term continuous-suture and skin-closure pain statements.

Open claims: obtain the full ACOG PB214 before restoring perineorrhaphy-specific ACOG wording; obtain the Mothes and Cervantes full methods/tables and assess adjusted outcomes; inspect Siff tables/appendices, DeLancey MRI figures, Kanter survey supplement and remaining inherited anatomy/sexual-function references before any complete disposition. No inaccessible full text was presented as fully read. Epic remains paused.
