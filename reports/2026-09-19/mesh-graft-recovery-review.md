# Mesh and graft augmentation evidence recovery — September 19, 2026

## Scope

The prior overview mixed historic transvaginal-mesh studies, non-current product claims, procedural rules, complication management, and abdominal mesh comparisons. It was reduced to the current U.S. regulatory boundary and a deliberately narrow comparative frame. This is not clinical clearance or a management protocol.

## Sources read or reused

- **FDA urogynecologic mesh pages**, checked September 19, 2026: the FDA states that no FDA-approved surgical-mesh product is marketed in the U.S. for transvaginal POP repair. It ordered the remaining manufacturers to stop selling/distributing their products on April 16, 2019. The FDA separately describes transvaginal POP mesh, transabdominal POP mesh, and SUI mesh slings, and says that asymptomatic people with an existing implant do not need removal solely because it is present.
- **Yeung et al., 2024 Cochrane review** (PMCID: PMC10936147; doi:10.1002/14651858.CD012079.pub2): accessible author-manuscript/abstract record located, but its full text was not retrievable through the current text service. Existing durable source records were reused only for its high-level distinction between transvaginal mesh, grafts, and native repair. No exact Cochrane effect estimate is imported.
- **Menefee et al., 2024** (PMCID: PMC11112501; doi:10.1001/jamasurg.2024.1206): author-manuscript record located but full text was not retrievable through the current text service. It was not used for page claims.
