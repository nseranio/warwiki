# Foldès clitoral reconstruction evidence recovery — September 19, 2026

## Scope

The prior page converted descriptive anatomical/technical literature, an incomplete-follow-up cohort, and combined-procedure reports into a standardized protocol and broad benefit claims. It was rebuilt as a bounded evidence overview.

## Sources read

- **Foldès et al., 2012** (PMID: 22695031): directly read abstract. The 2,938-person prospective cohort had 866 one-year attendees (29%); anatomic and self-reported outcomes among follow-up participants plus immediate complications were reported. It has no comparator.
- **Meremikwu et al., 2026** (PMID: 41582690): directly read abstract. Thirteen studies, mostly Foldès-style, reported improvements across measured outcomes but evidence certainty was very low for all outcomes.
- **Botter et al., 2021** (PMID: 33931348): directly read abstract. Anatomical/technical review; it notes results still require evaluation and surgery should not be the only therapy.
- **Auricchio et al., 2021** (PMID: 33845447): directly read systematic-review abstract; substantial bias, loss to follow-up, and limited validated assessment.
- **Almadori et al., 2024** (PMID: 38923662): directly read scoping-review abstract. Foldès-style surgery predominated in published studies; multidisciplinary and counselling reporting were limited.
- **O'Dey et al., 2024** (PMID: 37647530): previously recovered abstract-only combined-program evidence; outcomes cannot be attributed to Foldès or a single component.

## Changes made

- Removed fixed surgical steps, measurements, anaesthesia, aftercare, selection rules, and comparative outcomes.
- Removed procedure-specific global improvement/complication claims and single-component attribution from combined reports.
- Retained the large cohort with its 29% one-year follow-up and the very-low-certainty systematic-review boundary.

## Unresolved

- Primary full texts, figures, tables, supplements, and detailed operative materials were not fully reviewed.
- No recovered randomized or direct comparative study establishes technique selection or individual benefit.

No broad clinical verification is asserted.
