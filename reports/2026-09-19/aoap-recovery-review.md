# aOAP flap evidence recovery — September 19, 2026

## Scope

The prior page made detailed operative, selection, comparative, and long-term outcome claims beyond the directly recoverable evidence. It was rebuilt as a bounded evidence overview, not a flap protocol or clinical clearance.

## Sources read

- **O'Dey, Bozkurt, and Pallua, 2010** (PMID: 20869760): directly read abstract. This 10-cadaver/20-specimen study describes the anterior-obturator perforator anatomy, musculocutaneous/septocutaneous distribution, reported location, and reported skin territory. Its full article was not accessible in this pass.
- **O'Dey et al., 2024, FGM/C reconstruction** (PMID: 37647530): directly read abstract. The 119-person retrospective single-center cohort used several reconstructive procedures; aOAP was used in 36%. Reported one-year outcomes, one major flap loss, and secondary ambulatory interventions belong to the combined program and cannot be assigned to aOAP.
- **O'Dey et al., 2024, lichen sclerosus** (PMID: 38955111): directly read abstract. The 61-person retrospective series includes skinning vulvectomy/reconstruction; bilateral aOAP was used in 53 people. It reports one-year symptom findings and several minor reversible complications, without a comparator or long-term disease-control data.
- **Wendelspiess et al., 2024** (PMCID: PMC11202299; PMID: 38927919): complete accessible systematic review/meta-analysis read. Its 49-study, 1,840-patient vulvoperineal cancer reconstruction analysis is indirect for aOAP and for benign/FGM/C contexts; short-term complications were comparable and quality-of-life evidence sparse.

## Changes made

- Removed prescriptive flap planning, dissection, tunneling, inset, and aftercare rules.
- Removed claims of tissue superiority, predictable donor-site outcomes, safety guarantees, comparative flap rankings, and a universal lichen-sclerosus treatment ladder.
- Retained source-specific anatomy and cohort descriptions, while labeling combined procedures, retrospective design, and one-year follow-up.

## Unresolved

- The three aOAP primary clinical/anatomy papers were abstract-access only in this pass; their full texts, figures, tables, supplements, and detailed operative methods were not reviewed.
- No independent multicenter replication, randomized comparison, or directly identified head-to-head aOAP comparison was recovered.
- The cancer-reconstruction review cannot establish outcomes for aOAP, lichen sclerosus, or FGM/C reconstruction.

No broad clinical verification is asserted.
