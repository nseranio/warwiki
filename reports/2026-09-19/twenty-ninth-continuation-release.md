# Twenty-ninth continuation release — September 19, 2026

## Pectopexy evidence recovery

The pectopexy page has been rebuilt around a defensible boundary: it identifies the bilateral iliopectineal anchor and explains that it avoids sacral-promontory dissection, without converting that anatomic difference into an overall safety or outcome guarantee. Fixed technique, product, mesh, suture, platform, selection, learning-curve, and surveillance claims were removed.

The durable [recovery review](pectopexy-recovery-review.md) records the complete accessible Peng2023 main and Sauerwald2016 cadaver study, the reused complete Cochrane source scope, and the 2025 scoping review's abstract-only access. The scoping review found only four randomized studies among 30 heterogeneous reports, with inconsistent patient-centered outcome reporting. Original randomized, long-term, meshless, robotic/LESS, and product-specific reports remain unresolved rather than being represented as verified.
