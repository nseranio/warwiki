# Glans resurfacing evidence recovery — September 19, 2026

## Scope

The inherited page combined a detailed surgical protocol, superiority claims, oral-mucosa comparison, and data from cancer, trauma, urethral reconstruction, and phalloplasty. It was rebuilt as an evidence-bounded oncologic overview.

## Sources reviewed

- **EAU Penile Cancer Guideline, 2026 disease-management chapter:** directly read current web guidance. It describes resurfacing for extensive/residual/recurrent PeIN and selected superficially invasive lesions, the staging value of resection, the heterogeneous invasive-cancer series, and the need to discuss local recurrence and follow-up.
- **Pang et al., 2022** (PMID: 34903488): abstract reviewed. This systematic review of glansectomy/reconstruction reports heterogeneous, mostly small series with wide outcome ranges.

## Changes made

- Removed prescriptive steps, measurements, dressing and catheter rules, and assertions that resurfacing is superior to glansectomy or penectomy.
- Removed oral-mucosa comparisons and outcome tables derived from unrecovered source packages or unrelated indications.
- Retained the guideline-level role of resurfacing, common split-thickness graft use, pathology/staging value, and follow-up boundary.
