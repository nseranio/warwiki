# Moschcowitz procedure evidence recovery — September 19, 2026

## Scope

The prior page treated historical descriptions and small combined-procedure reports as a contemporary operative protocol. It was rebuilt around the directly available randomized abstract and prospective-series abstract. This is not clinical clearance or a procedural protocol.

## Sources read

- **Cruikshank and Kovac, 1999** (PMID: 10203653; doi:10.1016/s0002-9378(99)70656-3): complete PubMed abstract read. At vaginal hysterectomy, 100 women were randomized to a vaginal Moschcowitz-type closure, McCall-type culdoplasty, or peritoneal closure. At three years, the reported posterior-superior prolapse/enterocele counts were 2/32, 10/33, and 13/33 respectively. This supports McCall over the studied vaginal Moschcowitz-type procedure in that setting only.
- **Baessler and Schuessler, 2001** (PMID: 11339915; doi:10.1016/s0029-7844(00)01205-9): complete PubMed abstract read. The 33-person prospective series combined sacrocolpopexy, pouch-of-Douglas obliteration, and posterior mesh extension. It cannot isolate the effect of any component and had mean 26-month follow-up.
- **Winters et al., 2000** (PMID: 11114564; doi:10.1016/s0090-4295(00)00662-2): complete PubMed abstract read. Its “always” adjunct recommendation is an author recommendation from a review/technical description, not a comparative evidence mandate; it was not retained.
