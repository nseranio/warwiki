# Genitourinary VCA evidence recovery — September 19, 2026

## Scope

The inherited page presented a detailed operative, immunosuppressive, comparative-outcomes, and global-case protocol. The available direct evidence is a very small and heterogeneous case literature. It was rebuilt as a bounded overview.

## Sources reviewed

- **Lopez et al., 2023** (PMID: 36627487; PMCID: PMC9838304): review text was available. It reports five penile transplants worldwide since 2006 and two explantations in the experience then available; this is historical review scope, not a current registry count. It also frames candidate selection, adherence, psychosocial assessment, and unanswered long-term functional and immunological questions.
- **van der Merwe et al., 2017** (PMID: 28823494): abstract reviewed. One Cape Town case with 24-month follow-up; early vascular and wound events plus immunosuppression-related adverse events and recipient-reported outcomes are case-specific.
- **Cetrulo et al., 2018** (PMID: 28509699): abstract reviewed. One US case; postoperative complications, steroid-resistant rejection treated with antithymocyte globulin, and seven-month partial sensation/tumescence are case-specific.
- **Redett et al., 2019** (PMID: 31693813): bibliographic record recovered; the full correspondence and detailed outcome package were not recovered.
- **Caplan et al., 2017** (PMID: 28514260): bibliographic/abstract-level ethical framing used only for the need for oversight, consent, and management of failure; detailed recommendations were not carried forward.
