# EPAP hemi-scrotal flap evidence recovery — September 19, 2026

## Scope

The prior page generalized one EPAP case into a detailed protocol, comparison with many other flaps, sensory/functional benefit, and claims of superiority. It was rebuilt as a bounded feasibility overview, not a reconstructive protocol or clinical clearance.

## Sources read

- **Tsukuura et al., 2025** (PMID: 41054917): directly read abstract. One 40-year-old man with a post-replantation circumferential skin deficit received a 9 × 14 cm Doppler-planned, sensate EPAP hemi-scrotal flap. The authors reported uneventful short-term recovery and favourable seven-month findings. Full text, figures, video, and supplementary material were not recovered.
- **Carrera et al., 2009** (PMID: 19016690): directly read abstract. A 15-cadaver microvascular study describes inferior external pudendal/perineal systems and three inter-anastomosed scrotal territories. It does not specify the EPAP technique or establish patient-level reliability.

## Changes made

- Removed fixed Doppler, flap-design, dissection, nerve-preservation, rotation, inset, donor-closure, and aftercare instructions.
- Removed claims that EPAP is first, superior, reliably sensate, avoids dyspareunia/tethering, or outperforms named alternatives.
- Retained the index-case details as source-specific findings and the separate anatomical background.

## Unresolved

- The EPAP source is a single abstract-access case report; full methods, figures, sensory testing, and long-term outcome are unread.
- No direct comparison, replication cohort, or patient-level performance study was recovered.
- Inherited anatomy, nerve, and alternative-flap citations were not recovered as complete sources and are not used for detailed claims.

No broad clinical verification is asserted.
