# Glans reconstruction evidence recovery — September 19, 2026

## Scope

The former page blended oncologic reconstruction, trauma, and phalloplasty into a generic technique summary without citations. It was rebuilt around the currently available EAU penile-cancer guidance and a systematic review, while keeping non-oncologic reconstruction at a high-level boundary.

## Sources reviewed

- **EAU Penile Cancer Guideline, 2026 disease-management chapter:** directly read current web guidance. It presents organ-sparing options by disease extent, describes the role of resurfacing and glansectomy, and emphasizes recurrence-risk discussion and follow-up.
- **Pang et al., 2022** (PMID: 34903488): abstract reviewed. Twenty heterogeneous glansectomy studies; ranges in recurrence, graft loss, stenosis, and reported function/cosmesis are not individual predictions.

## Changes made

- Removed uncited technique lists and the unsupported implication that the same reconstructive options apply across cancer, trauma, and phalloplasty.
- Retained only the guideline-level oncologic role, the common use of split-thickness grafting after glansectomy, and indication-specific outcome domains.

## Unresolved

- No direct source package was recovered for the trauma or phalloplasty subtypes.
- Full texts, supplementary material, technique comparisons, and current local institutional protocols were not reviewed.
- No claim of a preferred graft, flap, or operative sequence is made.

No broad clinical verification is asserted.
