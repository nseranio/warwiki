# Berger 2024 model follow-through — September 19, 2026

The complete accessible PMC author manuscript for Berger et al. 2024 (PMCID: PMC10994006; doi:10.1097/AOG.0000000000005511) was reread to recover the model-label issue documented in the September 13 source register.

## Confirmed directly in the main manuscript

- Methods state that reoperation rates were compared with a **logistic regression model**.
- Table 3 is captioned “Adjusted odds ratio estimates … in multivariable logistic regression model.”
- The results prose nevertheless calls the analysis “Cox multivariate regression” and “Cox regression model” while reporting adjusted odds ratios. The existing source record correctly treats the logistic model/Table 3 adjusted ORs as the supportable description and does not call them hazard ratios.
