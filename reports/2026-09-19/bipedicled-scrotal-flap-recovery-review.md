# Bipedicled anterior scrotal flap evidence recovery — September 19, 2026

## Scope

The prior page presented a detailed operative protocol, universal stage-selection rules, comparative superiority, and durable functional predictions beyond the accessible evidence. It was rebuilt as a bounded overview, not a reconstructive protocol or clinical clearance.

## Sources read

- **Fakin et al., 2017** (PMID: 27871930): directly read abstract. This retrospective 43-person silicone-injection cohort reports its operative time, short mean follow-up, complications, satisfaction score, and self-reported postoperative function. Full text and figures were not accessible in this pass.
- **Mendel et al., 2023** (PMID: 37004847): directly read abstract. This retrospective 22-person mixed-indication series reports early/late complications, revision and questionnaire findings; only 12 people completed the questionnaire. Full text and supplements were not accessible in this pass.
- **Lumbiganon et al., 2023** (PMID: 37257041): directly read abstract. This retrospective 42-person foreign-body-granuloma cohort compares selected single- and two-stage groups. Fever and the no-complication endpoint differed statistically; infection, dehiscence, and reoperation did not. Full text and treatment-assignment details were not accessible in this pass.
- **McLaughlin et al., 2024** (PMID: 37285217): directly read abstract. This eight-person Level-V staged case series reports satisfactory outcomes and two minor complications. Full text and figures were not accessible in this pass.
- **Carrera et al., 2009**: directly identified only through the inherited citation; its full source and abstract were not recovered in this pass. The revised page uses it only for a high-level reported-anatomy description.
