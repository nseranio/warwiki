# Laparoscopic lateral suspension evidence recovery — September 19, 2026

## Scope

The prior LLS page converted variable procedure descriptions, pooled heterogeneous rates, and immature comparisons into prescriptive tunnel/mesh instructions and broad claims of superiority. It was rebuilt as a source-bounded overview. This review is not clinical clearance or an operative protocol.

## Sources read or reused

- **Plotti et al., 2025** (PMCID: PMC12072532; doi:10.3390/jcm14093056): complete accessible main text, tables, figures, and references read. This unregistered systematic review includes 12 prospective/randomized reports. It describes heterogeneous techniques, outcome definitions, and limited longer-term evidence. Its reported rates are study-level synthesis, not individualized predictions.
- **Malanowska-Jarema et al., 2024** (PMCID: PMC10931691; doi:10.3390/jcm13051348): complete accessible main text, tables, methods, and figures read. This single-center randomized comparison enrolled 93 women and had 89 twelve-month completers. It used specific inclusion/exclusion criteria, supracervical hysterectomy, study-specific meshes, and protocolized techniques; it cannot establish long-term equivalence or a general technique rule.
- **Ñíguez-Sevilla et al., 2025** (PMCID: PMC11943180; doi:10.3390/jcm14062069): complete accessible main text, tables, methods, and figures read. The report is explicitly preliminary: 106 operated participants with 1–12 months of follow-up, against a planned 182 for its stated noninferiority design. The authors say completion is required for relevant conclusions.
- **Maher et al., 2023 Cochrane review** (doi:10.1002/14651858.CD012376.pub2): reused from complete durable source reading in `reports/2026-09-13/apical-source-reading/`. Relevant comparison groups mix lateral/round-ligament procedures with pectopexy and are sparse/imprecise; no exact pooled estimate was reused.

## Changes made

- Removed fixed port, dissection, tunnel-boundary, mesh dimension/type, suture, tack, tensioning, and peritonization instructions.
- Removed universal patient-selection statements, the claimed short learning curve, exact success/complication/erosion/reoperation rates, and broad claims of lower risk or comparative superiority.
- Removed a source-specific mesh-erosion association as a universal mesh/patient-management mandate.
- Retained the non-sacral lateral-route identity, source-specific short-term randomized context, explicit limitations, and symptom-directed follow-up.

## Unresolved or intentionally not imported

- The older mesh-erosion cohort, individual technique-video papers, robotic variants, and individual posterior-compartment/product reports were not recovered as complete primary sources in this pass.
- The 2025 systematic review's supplement was listed but not separately reviewed. It is not represented as read.
- The embedded video is an educational demonstration, not evidence of a validated universal technique.

No broad clinical verification is asserted.
