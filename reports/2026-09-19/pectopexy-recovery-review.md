# Pectopexy evidence recovery — September 19, 2026

## Scope

The previous page mixed incompatible pectopexy configurations, small comparative cohorts, a cadaver experiment, and related lateral-suspension procedures into prescriptive operative steps and confident comparative outcome claims. It was rebuilt as a bounded overview. This is a current-page audit, not clinical clearance or a procedural protocol.

## Sources read or reused

- **Jongjakapun et al., 2025 scoping review** (doi:10.1007/s00192-025-06232-9): publisher abstract/metadata only; full text was paywalled. The accessible abstract reports 30 studies (four RCTs), 2,715 participants, 85 distinct outcomes, inconsistent symptom/PROM reporting, and predominantly tertiary-center studies. It supports the page's heterogeneity and evidence-limit statements only, not detailed technical or comparative-effect claims.
- **Peng et al., 2023** (PMCID: PMC10613309; doi:10.1038/s41598-023-45871-0): complete accessible main text, tables, methods, and figures read. It is a retrospective single-center three-group comparison (23 uterine-preserving pectopexy, 78 pectopexy with hysterectomy, 69 hysterectomy with laparoscopic high USLS), with differing uterine status and concomitant procedures and one-year follow-up. It can show feasibility and the risk of confounding; it cannot establish a preferred anchor, mesh, or patient-selection rule.
- **Sauerwald et al., 2016** (PMCID: PMC4741420; doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0144143): complete accessible main text, table, and figures read. Eight fresh cadavers/16 ligaments and 33 material-specific trials compared fixation constructs. The experiment does not determine clinical durability, mesh safety, or a universal fixation rule.
- **Maher et al., 2023 Cochrane review** (doi:10.1002/14651858.CD012376.pub2): reused from the durable complete-review reading in `reports/2026-09-13/apical-source-reading/`. Its pectopexy category includes lateral/round-ligament procedures; the evidence is small and imprecise. The earlier source record also identifies internal numerical/prose inconsistencies, so no exact pooled number was reused.

## Changes made

- Removed fixed ports, angles, dissection instructions, product/mesh configurations, suture/tack rules, device recommendations, learning-curve threshold, and surveillance-window mandate.
- Removed numerical success, complication, recurrence, and comparative-time claims; they were not valid as universal conclusions across the heterogeneous sources.
- Removed assertions of lower overall risk, less bowel dysfunction/back pain, or superiority over sacrocolpopexy, USLS, or SSLF.
- Retained only the operation identity, the fact that it avoids sacral-promontory dissection, explicit uncertainty, source-bounded feasibility context, and symptom-directed follow-up.

## Unresolved or intentionally not imported

- The 2025 scoping-review full text and supplements were not available in this session.
- Original randomized pectopexy trials, long-term series, meshless variants, robotic/LESS reports, and product-specific mesh/fixation reports were not newly recovered as complete sources. Their prior page claims were removed rather than carried forward.
- The videos are educational resources, not evidence of validated technique or outcome superiority.

No broad clinical verification is asserted.
