# Halban culdoplasty evidence recovery — September 19, 2026

## Scope

The prior page assigned Halban exact outcomes and the lowest ureteral-risk ranking despite no directly identified Halban randomized comparison. It was rebuilt as a bounded historical overview. This is not clinical clearance or a procedural protocol.

## Sources reused

- **Cruikshank and Kovac, 1999** (PMID: 10203653): the directly read PubMed abstract compared vaginal Moschcowitz-type closure, McCall-type culdoplasty, and peritoneal closure at vaginal hysterectomy. It did not include Halban. It therefore supports neither a Halban efficacy estimate nor a direct Halban-versus-McCall/Moschcowitz conclusion.
- **Baessler and Schuessler, 2001** (PMID: 11339915): the directly read PubMed abstract reports a 33-person prospective combined sacrocolpopexy, pouch-of-Douglas-obliteration, and posterior-mesh-extension series. It cannot isolate a closure approach or establish an independent benefit.

## Changes made

- Removed fixed technique, suture count/material, anatomic-safety distance, required urinary-tract test, and postoperative management rules.
- Removed claims that Halban has the lowest ureteral risk, is equivalent to Moschcowitz, has a known recurrence rate, or is demonstrably inferior to McCall.
- Retained only the descriptive sagittal-closure concept, the lack of a direct Halban randomized arm, and the limited combined-procedure context.

## Unresolved

- Original full papers and the inherited Raz, Holley, Ranney, Given, Milani, Miklos, and Cadeddu reports were not recovered as complete sources in this pass.

No broad clinical verification is asserted.
