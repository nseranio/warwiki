# Hysteropexy recovery review — September 19, 2026

## Scope

This record recovers the lost batch-26 evidence needed to revise the Manchester-Fothergill, sacrospinous hysteropexy, and sacrohysteropexy pages. All three current pre-edit MDX files, their tables, and their reference lists were read in full. Final edited pages were reread. This is a scoped correction record, not clinical sign-off or a claim that every inherited citation was fully read.

## Reused and recovered evidence

- **SAM / Enklaar 2023** (doi:10.1001/jama.2023.13140; correction doi:10.1001/jama.2023.17655): the September 19 checkpoint records a prior complete main-text and 30-page SAP read. The original temporary report is absent, so this batch does **not** falsely relabel it as a new independent full-source read. It rechecks the documented corrections: eligibility was point D no lower than −1 cm, not point C; cervical stenosis was 2/212; repeat surgery in previously operated compartments is not all repeat surgery; and PGI-I categories should not be collapsed.
- **SAM economic evaluation / Schulten 2025** (doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0336030; PMC12594370): complete accessible HTML main text, tables, reference list, author responses/peer correspondence contained in the PMC record, and the actual main CEAC figure were read. It is a two-year, 26-Dutch-hospital, societal/healthcare-perspective analysis alongside the 434-participant SAM randomized trial. It reports lower societal cost with Manchester and no statistically significant QALY difference; the current page correctly treats this as a setting- and time-bound estimate rather than transferable proof of superiority. The listed CHEERS checklist and four supplementary PDFs could not be downloaded because PMC returned a download challenge, so their actual files remain unread.
- **SAVE-U five-year follow-up** (doi:10.1136/bmj.l5149; PMC6734519): complete accessible HTML main text, tables, references, flowchart, and Kaplan–Meier figure read locally. The source defines the apical endpoint and composite success, allows concomitant repair, and supplies the trial-specific right-sided operative protocol. Its five-year results have non-blinded-intervention and attrition limitations.
- **SUPeR five-year report** (doi:10.1016/j.ajog.2021.03.012; PMC8328912): complete accessible author-manuscript main text, tables, references, and all three main figures read locally. It studied transvaginal **polypropylene Uphold LITE mesh** sacrospinous hysteropexy, not native-tissue hysteropexy. The accessible article explicitly reports the interval-censored model and that proportional hazards were not met; the article’s linked supplemental package was not recovered and remains unresolved.
- **LAVA** (doi:10.1111/1471-0528.16242): the earlier recovery lead reported a main/figure read, but the durable report was lost. This batch retains only the narrow published comparison and 12-month noninferiority framing already recorded in the Cochrane/apical package; it does not add detailed numerical or operative claims pending source recovery.
- **Cochrane apical review 2023** (doi:10.1002/14651858.CD012376.pub2): use is attributed to the complete, durable September 13 text-reading package. Its actual graphics remain inaccessible as legible figures; no new graphic-read credit is claimed.

## Changes

- Corrected SAM eligibility, stenosis denominator, repeat-operation framing, PGI-I wording, pathology wording, and the transportability of the Dutch economic estimate on the Manchester page.
