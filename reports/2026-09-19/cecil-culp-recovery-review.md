# Cecil-Culp evidence recovery — September 19, 2026

## Scope

The prior page conflated multiple historical staged techniques and made detailed contemporary stricture, procedural, timing, outcome, and complication claims beyond the recoverable evidence. It was rebuilt as a bounded historical overview, not a clinical protocol or clearance.

## Sources read

- **Marshall et al., 1979** (PMID: 430630): directly read abstract. It describes a Cecil modification with concurrent scrotoplasty in a difficult proximal-hypospadias subset of a 13-year experience; it does not provide a comparator or a numerical current outcome estimate.
- **Ehle et al., 2001** (PMID: 11182352): directly read abstract. A retrospective 15-boy recurrent-hypospadias-fistula series reported no recurrent fistula at mean 21-month follow-up. This is an uncontrolled selected cohort.
- **Weiss et al., 2017** (PMID: 28454986): directly read abstract. A single 12-year-old shotgun-injury case used a modified suprapubic marsupialization/tissue-transfer concept; it is not a general trauma protocol.
- **AUA Urethral Stricture Disease Guideline Amendment, 2023**: directly read guideline statement. In lichen-sclerosus-proven urethral stricture, surgeons should not use genital skin for reconstruction; follow-up for symptomatic recurrence is advised.
