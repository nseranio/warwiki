## Your preferred structure

- General new-patient and return-visit shells for common encounter information.
- Separate problem-specific HPI and assessment/plan modules, so a visit can include more than one problem without duplicating the whole note.
- A general operative-report shell plus the actual procedure's technique, findings and postoperative-plan modules.
- Maximum useful autopopulation of verified chart facts, with dates and graceful handling of missing data.
- Explicit selections for symptoms, examination findings, counseling, consent, decisions and events that actually occurred. Avoid defaulting to normal findings or asserting a step was performed.

## When ready, follow this sequence

1. **Collect examples.** Bring de-identified phrases and operative examples you like, plus local Epic documentation about available SmartLinks/SmartLists. Use a private workspace for personal source material.
2. **Verify the clinical content.** Check actionable thresholds, doses, contraindications, counseling estimates and follow-up against the current evidence. Record your clinical review separately from automated source checks.
3. **Build the small initial library.** Agree on a naming scheme, write the general shells, then create independent HPI/A&P modules for the seven problems. Keep evidence details in the source library and relevant encounter documentation in the note.
4. **Map autopopulation locally.** Confirm each Epic field's actual behavior, date, units and missing-data output. Do not invent portable SmartLink IDs; these vary by institution. Documenting a plan does not itself place an order.
5. **Add procedures.** Select the operations and office procedures you actually perform most often. Combine a generic report with reviewed procedure details and your own de-identified operative style.
6. **Test before use.** Use the institution's test/training environment with routine, atypical, missing-data and multi-problem cases. Check for contradictions, stale text, duplicate information and unsupported assertions.
7. **Refine after use.** Track edits after insertion, omissions and note-completion time. Version approved templates and flag them for review when their supporting evidence changes.
