# Clinical Evidence

Search every trial by name, treatment, endpoint or NCT number, or choose an area and a clinical question to list its trials in date order. The selected trial's bottom line and headline results open beside the list (on phones, in place of it), with a link to its full appraisal of design, results, harms, limitations and guideline context.
