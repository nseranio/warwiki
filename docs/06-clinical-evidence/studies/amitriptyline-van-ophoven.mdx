# van Ophoven amitriptyline trial: Oral Amitriptyline for Interstitial Cystitis

<TrialBottomLine id="amitriptyline-van-ophoven" />

The van Ophoven trial randomized patients meeting interstitial cystitis symptom criteria to self-titrated amitriptyline or placebo under double blinding; after four months, the primary O'Leary-Sant symptom/problem score improved more with amitriptyline.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

<TrialQuickHit id="amitriptyline-van-ophoven" />

## Design

The enrolled group included 44 women and 6 men meeting National Institute of Diabetes, Digestive and Kidney Diseases symptom criteria. The primary endpoint was change in the O'Leary-Sant IC symptom and problem index. Secondary measures were pain and urgency visual analog scales, plus frequency and functional bladder capacity from a 48-hour voiding log. The reported efficacy analysis used the 24 patients available in each arm after one withdrawal per arm.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Results

Mean primary score fell from 26.9 to 18.5 with amitriptyline and from 27.6 to 24.1 with placebo, changes of −8.4 and −3.5 points. The derived between-arm difference in change was −4.9 points (p = 0.005). Pain and urgency intensity also favored amitriptyline (p &lt; 0.001). Urinary frequency and functional bladder capacity improved more in the amitriptyline arm, but between-arm comparisons did not meet statistical significance (p = 0.063 and p = 0.083, respectively).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Surgery and treatment delivery

Patients self-titrated in 25 mg increments at one-week intervals, up to 100 mg, and continued assigned treatment for four months.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Adverse events

Anticholinergic effects occurred in 22/24 (92%) evaluable amitriptyline patients and 5/24 (21%) placebo patients. Dry mouth was reported in 79% of the amitriptyline arm. One participant in each arm withdrew because of side effects.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Limitations

The efficacy comparison included 48 of 50 randomized patients and covered four months. The small sample and self-titrated dosing leave the relationship between achieved dose, symptom response and tolerability unresolved.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## In conversation

<InConversation id="amitriptyline-van-ophoven" />

The later ICRN trial enrolled treatment-naïve patients and gave both arms education and behavioral modification. Its intention-to-treat global response endpoint did not differ significantly between amitriptyline and placebo; van Ophoven instead measured change in a symptom/problem index.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Guidelines and approvals

AUA's 2022 IC/BPS guideline cites this trial and includes amitriptyline among the oral medications that may be administered, listed alphabetically with no hierarchy <Rec>(Option (Evidence Strength: Grades B, B, C, and B))</Rec>. Amitriptyline carries the first Grade B in that sequence.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
