# Padma-Nathan 2008: Nightly Sildenafil After Nerve-Sparing Radical Prostatectomy

<TrialBottomLine id="nightly-sildenafil-rp" />

**Padma-Nathan 2008** was a double-blind randomized trial of nightly sildenafil versus placebo in men with normal preoperative erectile function after bilateral nerve-sparing radical retropubic prostatectomy. Unassisted erectile function after a drug-free period was more frequent among sildenafil recipients who completed assessment.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

<TrialQuickHit id="nightly-sildenafil-rp" />

## Design

Treatment began four weeks after surgery. Men received sildenafil 50 or 100 mg nightly or placebo for 36 weeks, followed by eight weeks without study drug. The endpoint required a combined score of at least eight on International Index of Erectile Function questions 3 and 4, which assess penetration and maintenance after penetration, plus a positive answer that erections were adequate for satisfactory sexual activity.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Results

After washout, 14/51 sildenafil completers (27%) and 1/25 placebo completers (4%) met the spontaneous-function endpoint (Fisher exact P = 0.0156). Enrollment stopped early after a blinded interim review suggested a lower overall response than anticipated.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

In a nocturnal penile tumescence and rigidity subanalysis of 54 men, week-48 unassisted responders numbered 4/17 (24%) with 50 mg, 6/18 (33%) with 100 mg, and 1/19 (5%) with placebo. Penile plethysmography showed gradual improvement in rigidity in the sildenafil groups; tip rigidity at or above 55% best distinguished responders from nonresponders.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Surgery and treatment delivery

The operation was bilateral nerve-sparing radical retropubic prostatectomy. The treatment strategy began during early postoperative recovery and assessed erections after stopping medication, rather than erectile function while taking sildenafil.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Limitations

Early closure left 76 men who completed endpoint assessment, and the reported comparison uses completers. The nocturnal-rigidity analysis included a subset of those men.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Guidelines and approvals

The AUA Erectile Dysfunction guideline (2018) advises informing men seeking erectile preservation after radical prostatectomy or radiotherapy that early PDE5 inhibitor use may not improve spontaneous, unassisted erectile function <Rec>(Moderate Recommendation; Evidence Level: Grade C)</Rec>.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
