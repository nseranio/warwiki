# Vasudeva 2015: Dorsal Versus Ventral Onlay Buccal Mucosal Graft Urethroplasty

<TrialBottomLine id="vasudeva-dorsal-ventral" />

**Vasudeva 2015** was a single-center randomized comparison of dorsal versus ventral onlay buccal mucosal graft urethroplasty in patients with long-segment incomplete bulbar stricture; surgical success did not differ significantly at 12 months.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

<TrialQuickHit id="vasudeva-dorsal-ventral" />

## Design

The trial enrolled patients with an incomplete bulbar stricture longer than 2 cm at V. M. Medical College and Safdarjang Hospital, New Delhi. Eighty eligible patients were randomized equally between the two graft positions. The investigators compared symptom score, maximum urinary flow, intraoperative parameters, and complications. Baseline symptom scores, flow rates, and intraoperative measures did not differ significantly between the groups.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Results

At 12 months, reported surgical success was 92.5% after dorsal onlay and 90% after ventral onlay. Mean International Prostate Symptom Score percentage improvements were reported as 324.95% and 353.59%, respectively, and mean maximum flow rate percentage improvements as 208.43% and 201.93%. Between-group symptom-score and flow-rate comparisons were not statistically significant at 3 or 12 months.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Surgery and treatment delivery

Each arm received a buccal mucosal graft onlay for the bulbar stricture. The randomized technical distinction was graft placement on the dorsal or ventral urethral surface.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Adverse events

The investigators reported comparable complication rates between the two groups.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Limitations

Enrollment was confined to one center, with 40 randomized patients per arm. That sample limits precision for differences in uncommon complications, and 12-month follow-up gives limited information about later stricture recurrence.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Guidelines and approvals

The EAU 2026 Urethral Strictures guideline cites this trial in its review of graft location and recommends choosing a dorsal, dorsolateral, or ventral approach according to surgical practice, expertise, and intraoperative findings <Rec>(Strong)</Rec>.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
