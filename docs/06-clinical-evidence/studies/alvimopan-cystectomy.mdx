# Alvimopan after radical cystectomy: A multicenter randomized trial of gastrointestinal recovery

<TrialBottomLine id="alvimopan-cystectomy" />

In a multicenter, double-blind randomized trial of patients undergoing radical cystectomy with planned postoperative intravenous opioid patient-controlled analgesia, oral alvimopan accelerated gastrointestinal recovery compared with placebo.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

<TrialQuickHit id="alvimopan-cystectomy" />

## Design

The trial randomized 280 patients, 143 to alvimopan and 137 to placebo; 277 entered the modified intention-to-treat analysis. GI-2 recovery required both first tolerance of solid food and first bowel movement. The investigators also assessed discharge timing, hospital stay, ileus-related morbidity, opioid use, and adverse events. Cardiovascular events underwent independent adjudication.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Results

GI-2 recovery occurred at 5.5 versus 6.8 days (HR 1.8; p &lt; 0.0001), meeting the primary efficacy objective. Mean postoperative stay was 7.4 versus 10.1 days (mean difference −2.7 days; p = 0.0051), and ileus-related morbidity was 8.4% versus 29.1% (risk difference −20.7 percentage points; p &lt; 0.001). The reported opioid consumption was comparable between groups.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

An economic analysis of the same randomized cohort, using 2012 Medicare reimbursement rates and drug acquisition costs, estimated $2,340 lower mean ileus-related cost per alvimopan patient (p = 0.04). The $2,640 lower mean total combined cost did not meet the analysis's conventional significance threshold (p = 0.068).<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Surgery and treatment delivery

The assigned oral alvimopan dose was 12 mg, with a maximum of 15 inpatient doses. Most participating high-volume centers used open laparotomy; the trial required postoperative intravenous opioid patient-controlled analgesia rather than epidural analgesia.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Adverse events

Postoperative ileus was reported as an adverse event in 7% versus 26%, and as a serious adverse event in 5% versus 20%. Independently adjudicated cardiovascular adverse events occurred in 8.4% versus 15.3% (p = 0.09). Other adverse-event and serious-adverse-event incidence was comparable in the published summary.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Limitations

The predominantly open, high-volume-center population and exclusion of epidural analgesia limit application to other cystectomy pathways. The cost analysis used modeled reimbursement and acquisition prices rather than a hospital's observed expenditures.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Guidelines and approvals

The AUA/ASCO/SUO non-metastatic muscle-invasive bladder cancer guideline, amended in 2024, recommends μ-opioid antagonist therapy to accelerate gastrointestinal recovery after radical cystectomy unless contraindicated <Rec>(Strong Recommendation; Evidence Level: Grade B)</Rec>.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
