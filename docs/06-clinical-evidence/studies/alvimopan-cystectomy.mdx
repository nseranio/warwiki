# Alvimopan after radical cystectomy: A multicenter randomized trial of gastrointestinal recovery

<TrialBottomLine id="alvimopan-cystectomy" />

In a multicenter, double-blind randomized trial of patients undergoing radical cystectomy with planned postoperative intravenous opioid patient-controlled analgesia, oral alvimopan accelerated gastrointestinal recovery compared with placebo.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

<TrialQuickHit id="alvimopan-cystectomy" />

## Design

The trial randomized 280 patients at 21 high-volume academic sites, 143 to alvimopan and 137 to placebo; 143 and 134, respectively, entered the modified intention-to-treat analysis after three placebo-assigned patients did not undergo protocol-specified radical cystectomy. GI-2 recovery was the later of first solid-food tolerance and first bowel movement. Discharge order written and postoperative hospital stay were key secondary endpoints in a prespecified sequential testing order, followed by ileus-related morbidity. Cardiovascular events underwent independent adjudication.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Results

GI-2 recovery was faster with alvimopan in the modified intention-to-treat analysis: Kaplan–Meier mean 5.5 versus 6.8 days (HR 1.773, 95% CI 1.4–2.3; p &lt; 0.0001), meeting the primary endpoint. The means were censored at discharge or day 10; 12% versus 25% had not achieved GI-2 by then. Mean time to discharge order was 6.9 versus 7.8 days (HR 1.668, 95% CI 1.3–2.2; p = 0.0002), and mean postoperative stay was 7.44 versus 10.07 days (difference −2.63 days; p = 0.0051); both key secondary endpoints met the prespecified threshold. Ileus-related morbidity was 8.4% of 143 versus 29.1% of 134 (difference −20.7 percentage points; p &lt; 0.001), including nasogastric tube insertion in 7.7% versus 24.6%. The reported opioid consumption was comparable between groups.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

The preplanned economic analysis found lower modeled ileus-related costs during hospitalization and follow-up: $15,684 for 143 alvimopan patients versus $18,023 for 134 placebo patients (difference −$2,340, 95% CI −$4,613 to −$342; bootstrap p = 0.040). Mean total combined costs over the same period were $18,087 versus $20,726 (difference −$2,640, 95% CI −$5,506 to $7; p = 0.068); during the index hospitalization alone, combined costs were $15,855 versus $18,187 (difference −$2,333, 95% CI −$4,624 to −$301; p = 0.042). The analysis used 2012 Medicare reimbursement rates and drug acquisition costs. During protocol hospitalization, postoperative nasogastric tube procedures occurred in 10/143 (7.0%) versus 33/134 (24.6%; p &lt; 0.001); total parenteral nutrition use was reported in 10% versus 25% (p = 0.001).<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Surgery and treatment delivery

The assigned oral alvimopan dose was 12 mg 30 minutes to 5 hours before surgery, then twice daily until discharge or 7 days, with a maximum of 15 inpatient doses. Among randomized patients, open surgery was recorded for 122/143 (85.3%) versus 114/137 (83.2%); mean operative time was 5.8 hours in both arms. The pathway encouraged ambulation on postoperative day 1, offered liquids on day 3 and solids on day 4, and required postoperative intravenous opioid patient-controlled analgesia without epidural analgesia.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

The economic analysis counted surgery as hospital day 1 and found a mean 8.44 versus 11.07 total hospital days (p = 0.005), one day more in each arm than the primary report's postoperative stay. Patients received a mean 11.38 alvimopan doses versus 10.98 placebo doses.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Adverse events

Postoperative ileus was reported as a treatment-emergent adverse event in 10/143 (7.0%) versus 36/137 (26.3%; p &lt; 0.001), and as a serious adverse event in 4.9% versus 20.4%. Serious adverse events overall occurred in 25.7% versus 48.9%; urinary tract infection was a serious adverse event in 3.5% versus 1.5%. Independently adjudicated cardiovascular events occurred in 12/143 (8.4%) versus 21/137 (15.3%; p = 0.09), with estimated relative risk 0.55. There were five versus four deaths, none considered study-drug related. All-cause readmission within 30 days occurred in 23.8% versus 26.9% (p = 0.58).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Limitations

The predominantly open, high-volume-center population and exclusion of epidural analgesia limit application to other cystectomy pathways. The cost analysis used modeled reimbursement and acquisition prices rather than a hospital's observed expenditures.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Guidelines and approvals

The AUA/ASCO/SUO non-metastatic muscle-invasive bladder cancer guideline, amended in 2024, recommends μ-opioid antagonist therapy to accelerate gastrointestinal recovery after radical cystectomy unless contraindicated <Rec>(Strong Recommendation; Evidence Level: Grade B)</Rec>.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
