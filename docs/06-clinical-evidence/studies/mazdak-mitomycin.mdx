# Mazdak mitomycin C trial: Submucosal Injection after Internal Urethrotomy

<TrialBottomLine id="mazdak-mitomycin" />

This prospective randomized trial compared submucosal mitomycin C injection with urethrotomy without mitomycin C in 40 men with anterior urethral strictures and found fewer recurrences in the mitomycin group at six months.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

<TrialQuickHit id="mazdak-mitomycin" />

## Design

Twenty men were assigned to each group. The report describes recurrence as the outcome and compares groups with a chi-square test. The abstract gives neither a prespecified significance threshold nor a definition of recurrence. Blinding is not reported.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Results

At the six-month assessment, recurrence occurred in 2/20 men (10%) after mitomycin C and 10/20 (50%) without mitomycin C (p = 0.006). The report describes this difference as statistically significant.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Surgery and treatment delivery

Urethrotomy was performed under general anesthesia with direct vision. The intervention group received 0.1 mg mitomycin C submucosally at the urethrotomy site; the other group received no mitomycin C.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Adverse events

The indexed abstract gives no adverse-event or injection-site complication rates.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Limitations

The comparison was small, and the six-month assessment gives limited information about later stricture recurrence. The abstract does not specify how recurrence was diagnosed or describe blinding.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Guidelines and approvals

The AUA urethral stricture guideline (amended 2023) allows DVIU as an initial treatment for a short bulbar stricture &lt;2 cm <Rec>(Conditional Recommendation; Evidence Level: Grade C)</Rec>. Its discussion cites Mazdak for the recurrence finding and notes limited long-term follow-up for mitomycin C injection.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

The EAU 2026 urethral stricture guideline advises that intralesional injections be used only within a clinical trial <Rec>(Weak)</Rec>.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
