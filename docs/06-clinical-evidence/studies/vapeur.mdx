# VAPEUR-RCT: Rezūm Water Vapor Therapy Versus Combination Pharmacotherapy for Benign Prostatic Obstruction

<TrialBottomLine id="vapeur" />

**VAPEUR-RCT** was a multicenter, pragmatic randomized trial of Rezūm water vapor thermal therapy versus an alpha-blocker plus a 5-alpha-reductase inhibitor in sexually active men with symptomatic benign prostatic obstruction; at one year, urinary symptom improvement favored Rezūm, while superiority for sexual-function preservation was not demonstrated after multiple imputation.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

<TrialQuickHit id="vapeur" />

## Design

The trial randomized 151 men: 75 to Rezūm and 76 to combination pharmacotherapy. Its two primary endpoints were change from baseline to one year in International Prostate Symptom Score (IPSS) and Male Sexual Health Questionnaire (MSHQ) score. The [registered protocol](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04838769) specified open-label assignment.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Results

Mean IPSS improvement was 10.8 ± 6.8 points with Rezūm and 6.2 ± 7.7 points with medication (difference in change scores −4.6 points, 97.5% CI −7.6 to −1.6; p &lt; 0.001). MSHQ changed by +1.1 ± 16.9 versus −5.2 ± 16.4 points; superiority for preservation of sexual function was not demonstrated with multiple imputation.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

Quality-of-life scores changed by −2.7 ± 1.8 versus −1.8 ± 2.0 points (p = 0.01). Surgical retreatment occurred in 1.3% versus 9.2%, while pharmacological retreatment occurred in 12% versus 1.3%. The combined retreatment comparison had a hazard ratio of 0.52 (CI 0.25–1.1; p = 0.08). IPSS worsened by at least four points in 2.7% versus 13%; catheterization beyond 90 days occurred in 0% versus 1.3%.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Surgery and treatment delivery

The procedural arm received Rezūm transurethral water vapor thermal therapy. The medication arm received an alpha-blocker with a 5-alpha-reductase inhibitor.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Adverse events

Treatment-related adverse events occurred in 40% after Rezūm and 28% with medication; serious adverse events occurred in 12% and 1.3%, respectively. The report described most Rezūm events as procedure-related.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Limitations

Open-label assignment could influence patient-reported symptom and sexual-function scores. The one-year results leave later comparative retreatment and adverse-event outcomes unresolved.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
