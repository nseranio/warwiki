# Naspro 2006: Holmium Laser Enucleation Versus Open Prostatectomy for Prostates &gt;70 g

<TrialBottomLine id="holep-vs-open-naspro" />

Naspro and colleagues randomized 80 men with obstructive symptoms and prostates &gt;70 g to holmium laser enucleation of the prostate (HoLEP) or open prostatectomy; at 24 months, urodynamic and uroflowmetry findings were comparable, while HoLEP shortened catheterization and hospital stay and open surgery took less operating-room time.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

<TrialQuickHit id="holep-vs-open-naspro" />

## Design

The Milan group enrolled consecutive patients from March 2003 through December 2004, allocating 41 to HoLEP and 39 to standard open prostatectomy. Baseline assessment included the International Prostate Symptom Score, International Index of Erectile Function, and complete urodynamic testing. The same assessments were scheduled at 1, 3, 12, and 24 months. The report's abstract describes perioperative measures, functional testing, and complications without naming a prespecified primary endpoint.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Results

Urodynamic and uroflowmetry measures improved from baseline in both arms and remained improved at 24 months; the abstract describes the between-arm findings as comparable without giving arm-specific estimates. Mean time to catheter removal was 1.5 ± 1.07 days after HoLEP and 4.1 ± 0.5 days after open surgery (P &lt; 0.001). Mean hospital stay was 2.7 ± 1.1 versus 5.4 ± 1.05 days (P &lt; 0.001).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Surgery and treatment delivery

Mean operating-room time was 72.09 ± 21.22 minutes for HoLEP and 58.31 ± 11.95 minutes for open surgery (P &lt; 0.0001). The abstract calls the comparator standard open prostatectomy and gives no further operative technique or morcellation details.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Adverse events

Blood loss and transfusions were lower with HoLEP (P &lt; 0.001). The authors described late complications as comparable between arms; the abstract gives no event counts or complication categories.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Limitations

The trial enrolled 80 men. The abstract does not name a primary endpoint or report arm-specific 24-month functional estimates, complication counts, or the number assessed at each follow-up visit.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Guidelines and approvals

The EAU 2026 male LUTS guideline includes this trial among its open-prostatectomy comparisons and recommends offering HoLEP to men with moderate-to-severe LUTS and benign prostatic obstruction as an alternative to TURP or open prostatectomy <Rec>(Strong)</Rec>.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
