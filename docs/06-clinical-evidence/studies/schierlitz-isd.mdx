# Schierlitz ISD: Retropubic Versus Transobturator Tape for Intrinsic Sphincter Deficiency

<TrialBottomLine id="schierlitz-isd" />

**Schierlitz ISD** randomized 164 women with urodynamic stress urinary incontinence and intrinsic sphincter deficiency to retropubic tension-free vaginal tape (TVT) or transobturator tape; urodynamic stress incontinence at six months was present in 14/67 (21%) versus 32/71 (45%) assessed women, respectively (P=.004).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

<TrialQuickHit id="schierlitz-isd" />

## Design

The two urogynecology centers allowed concomitant pelvic organ prolapse repair. The primary endpoint was the presence of urodynamic stress incontinence at six months. Operative complications, symptomatic incontinence requiring another operation, and quality-of-life questionnaires were secondary outcomes. Urodynamic follow-up was available for 138 of the 164 randomized women.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Results

The six-month primary endpoint favored retropubic TVT in the assessed population.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

At three years, the follow-up report evaluated symptomatic stress incontinence requiring repeat surgery as its primary outcome. Repeat surgery had occurred in 1/72 (1.4%) TVT recipients and 15/75 (20%) transobturator recipients; the reported risk ratio for transobturator tape versus TVT was 15 (95% CI 2–113; P&lt;.001). Urogenital Distress Inventory and Incontinence Impact Questionnaire short-form scores and patient-rated success improved in both groups, without a reported between-group difference.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Surgery and treatment delivery

The operations used a retropubic TVT route or a transobturator tape route. Pelvic organ prolapse repair could be performed at the same operation.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Adverse events

Nine transobturator recipients and no TVT recipients had another sling operation by six months.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Limitations

The six-month primary analysis included urodynamic assessments for 138 of 164 randomized women. The three-year comparison used repeat surgery, a different endpoint from the initial urodynamic assessment, with outcomes available for 72 TVT and 75 transobturator recipients.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Guidelines and approvals

The AUA/SUFU surgical SUI guideline, amended in 2023, cites this trial's three-year report in its route comparison. For index patients selecting a midurethral sling, it permits a retropubic or transobturator route <Rec>(Conditional Recommendation; Evidence Level: Grade A [retropubic/transobturator midurethral sling]/Grade B [single-incision sling])</Rec>.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
