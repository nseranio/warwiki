# Schierlitz ISD: Retropubic Versus Transobturator Tape for Intrinsic Sphincter Deficiency

<TrialBottomLine id="schierlitz-isd" />

**Schierlitz ISD** randomized 164 women with urodynamic stress urinary incontinence and intrinsic sphincter deficiency to retropubic tension-free vaginal tape (TVT) or Monarc transobturator tape. At six months, urodynamic stress incontinence was present in 14/67 (21%) assessed TVT recipients versus 32/71 (45%) assessed transobturator recipients (P=.004).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

<TrialQuickHit id="schierlitz-isd" />

## Design

The two urogynecology centers allowed concomitant pelvic organ prolapse repair. Intrinsic sphincter deficiency required a maximum urethral closure pressure of ≤20 cm H2O and/or a leak-point pressure rise of ≤60 cm H2O. Patients and surgeons were unblinded. The primary endpoint was the presence of urodynamic stress incontinence at six months. Operative complications, symptomatic incontinence requiring another operation, and quality-of-life questionnaires were secondary outcomes. Urodynamic follow-up was available for 138 of the 164 randomized women.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Results

At six months, symptomatic urodynamic stress incontinence led to repeat sling surgery in 0/67 assessed TVT recipients and 9/71 (12.7%) assessed transobturator recipients (P=.003). A separate intention-to-treat estimate of *requests* for repeat surgery counted withdrawals and losses to follow-up as failures, and asymptomatic non-testers as successes: 5/82 (6.1%) versus 13/82 (15.9%), respectively (risk ratio 2.6, 95% CI 0.9–9.3; P=.08).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

Repeat surgery for symptomatic stress incontinence, the three-year follow-up report's primary outcome, occurred in 1/82 (1.2%) randomized TVT recipients and 15/82 (18.3%) randomized transobturator recipients (1/72 and 15/75 followed, respectively); the transobturator-versus-TVT hazard ratio was 15 (95% CI 2–113; P&lt;.001), with a rate difference of 17.1 percentage points (95% CI 8.4–25.8). The only TVT repeat operation occurred at 43.7 months; median time to repeat surgery in the transobturator arm was 15.6 months (IQR 11.8–41.4). UDI-6 scores improved in both groups (36-month medians 3 versus 4; between-group P=.78), and both groups rated success at a median of 90 (P=.32). IIQ-7 follow-up medians were 0 in both groups, while its reported between-group change difference was 2.1 (interval 0.3–4.3; P=.03).<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Surgery and treatment delivery

The retropubic arm used Gynecare TVT and the transobturator arm used the Monarc subfascial hammock sling. Both tapes were tensioned over fine scissors, with routine cystoscopy. Concomitant prolapse repair was performed in 29/82 (35%) TVT recipients and 26/82 (32%) transobturator recipients; catheters after sling-only surgery were removed at operation end, while concomitant repair often entailed a catheter and vaginal pack for 24–48 hours.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Adverse events

Bladder perforation occurred in 6/82 (7.3%) TVT recipients versus 0/82 transobturator recipients; vaginal wall perforation occurred in 0/82 versus 4/82 (4.9%). Voiding difficulty required an indwelling catheter for 1–5 days in 7/82 versus 3/82 and self-catheterization for 5–28 days in 2/82 versus 1/82. Tape loosening occurred in 1/82 in each arm and division in 2/82 versus 1/82. One transobturator recipient had a groin abscess requiring drainage and partial sling removal, with persistent obturator pain at six months.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Limitations

The six-month primary analysis included urodynamic assessments for 138 of 164 randomized women; 17 asymptomatic women declined repeat testing. The three-year report used repeat surgery, a different endpoint from the initial urodynamic assessment, with follow-up available for 72 TVT and 75 transobturator recipients.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Guidelines and approvals

The AUA/SUFU surgical SUI guideline, amended in 2023, cites this trial's three-year report in its route comparison. For index patients selecting a midurethral sling, it permits a retropubic or transobturator route <Rec>(Conditional Recommendation; Evidence Level: Grade A [retropubic/transobturator midurethral sling]/Grade B [single-incision sling])</Rec>.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
