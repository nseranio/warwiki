# Dubey 2007: Dorsal Onlay Buccal Mucosa Versus Penile Skin Flap Urethroplasty

<TrialBottomLine id="dubey-bmg-vs-flap" />

**Dubey 2007** randomized 55 patients with complex anterior urethral strictures to dorsal onlay buccal mucosa graft or penile skin flap urethroplasty and reported no significant success difference, with longer operations and more penile morbidity after flap repair.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

<TrialQuickHit id="dubey-bmg-vs-flap" />

## Design

Stricture sites included pendulous, bulbar, and bulbopendulous urethra; their distributions and mean lengths were described as comparable between groups. Buccal mucosa was assigned to 27 patients and a penile skin flap to 28. The report assessed operative time, hospital stay, complications, recurrence, and patient satisfaction.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Results

Study-reported success was 89.9% with buccal mucosa and 85.6% with a flap (P &gt; 0.05). The reported percentages cannot be reconstructed as whole-patient counts from the randomized arm sizes. Patient recommendation was 89% versus 65%, respectively (P = 0.001); respondent counts were not given.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Surgery and treatment delivery

Mean operative time was 162 minutes with buccal mucosa and 224 minutes with a penile flap (P = 0.001).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Adverse events

Troublesome postvoid dribbling occurred in 4 of 27 buccal mucosa patients (14.8%) and 9 flap patients; the abstract prints 34.1% for the flap arm, which does not reconcile with 9 of 28 randomized patients. In the flap arm, 6 patients had superficial penile skin necrosis, 1 had extensive skin loss requiring grafting, and 2 had penile torsion. Overlap between the skin-loss cases was not specified. Minor morbidity was reported in 25.6% of buccal mucosa patients and settled within four weeks.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Limitations

The success definition, outcome denominators, allocation method, and prespecified significance threshold were not available. Median follow-up was described as comparable between groups but its duration was not supplied.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Guidelines and approvals

The EAU 2026 urethral stricture guideline cites this trial in its graft-versus-flap comparison and recommends a graft when both are equally indicated <Rec>(Strong)</Rec>.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
