# Tadalafil once daily for ED: Placebo-Controlled Tadalafil 5-mg and 10-mg Dosing

<TrialBottomLine id="tadalafil-daily-ed" />

Porst and colleagues randomized 268 men with erectile dysfunction to tadalafil 5 mg daily, tadalafil 10 mg daily or placebo for 12 weeks; both tadalafil doses improved IIEF erectile-function scores, successful penetration and intercourse completion versus placebo (all comparisons P &lt; 0.001).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

<TrialQuickHit id="tadalafil-daily-ed" />

## Design

The multicenter, double-blind trial assigned men to placebo, tadalafil 5 mg and tadalafil 10 mg in a 1:2:2 ratio. Its primary efficacy measures were change in the IIEF erectile-function domain and change in Sexual Encounter Profile diary responses for penetration (SEP2) and successful completion of intercourse (SEP3). Patient-reported improvement and an endpoint IIEF erectile-function score of 26–30 were secondary measures.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Results

Mean IIEF erectile-function score changed by +9.7 points with 5 mg, +9.4 with 10 mg and +0.9 with placebo. Successful-penetration responses increased by 36.5, 39.4 and 11.2 percentage points, respectively; successful-intercourse responses increased by 45.5, 50.1 and 13.2 percentage points. Each tadalafil dose differed significantly from placebo on these primary measures (P &lt; 0.001).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

At endpoint, 84.5%, 84.6% and 28.3% reported improved erections, respectively. IIEF erectile-function scores of 26–30 occurred in 51.5%, 50.5% and 8.3%.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

In the later open-label 5-mg extension, mean IIEF erectile-function score was 10.4 points above the original baseline at one year; 208/234 (88.9%) completed that extension.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> The same report also included a separate two-year extension of another randomized study.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> A retrospective analysis of men from the one-year extension found that 63/136 (46.3%) who had improved or maintained normal function at the end of treatment still met that definition four weeks after stopping tadalafil.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

## Adverse events

In the randomized trial, nine of 268 men (3.4%) discontinued because of adverse events.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> Events reported in at least 5% of patients were dyspepsia, headache, back pain, upper abdominal pain and myalgia.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> The extension report recorded no drug-related serious adverse events across its one- and two-year cohorts.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Limitations

The randomized comparison lasted 12 weeks. The reported abstract gives no arm-specific adverse-event rates or confidence intervals for the primary measures. The open-label extension included participants from another trial, and its post-treatment analysis selected men who had responded during treatment.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

## Guidelines and approvals

The AUA erectile dysfunction guideline (2018) advises discussing an FDA-approved oral PDE5 inhibitor with men with erectile dysfunction, including benefits and risks, unless contraindicated <Rec>(Strong Recommendation; Evidence Level: Grade B)</Rec>.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>
