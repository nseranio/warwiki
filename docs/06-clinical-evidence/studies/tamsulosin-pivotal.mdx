# Tamsulosin US pivotal studies: Modified-release alpha blockade for symptomatic BPH

<TrialBottomLine id="tamsulosin-pivotal" />

The primary multicenter, double-blind phase 3 trial randomized 756 men with symptomatic BPH to tamsulosin 0.4 mg daily, tamsulosin 0.4 mg daily for 1 week then 0.8 mg daily, or placebo; both doses improved AUA symptom score and peak urinary flow over 13 weeks, with more adverse events at the higher dose.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

<TrialQuickHit id="tamsulosin-pivotal" />

## Design

Change in total AUA symptom score and peak urinary flow (Qmax) were the primary efficacy measures in the Lepor trial and its extension.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup> US 93-01 also designated at least 25% symptom-score reduction and at least 30% Qmax improvement as primary measures.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> The investigators also assessed symptom subscales, patient bother, and orthostatic vital signs. The independent US 93-01 study randomized 735 men after a 4-week single-blind placebo evaluation period and evaluated 0.4 mg daily and a regimen of 0.4 mg daily for 1 week followed by 0.8 mg daily against placebo during 13 weeks of double-blind treatment.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[3]](#ref3)[[4]](#ref4)</sup>

## Results

In the primary trial, both tamsulosin doses produced statistically significant improvements in the two primary measures versus placebo. Qmax improvement after the first 0.4 mg dose was evident within 4 to 8 hours.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

Both doses improved symptom response in the independent US 93-01 study: at 13 weeks, at least 25% AUA score reduction occurred in 133/244 (55%) taking 0.4 mg, 134/238 (56%) assigned 0.8 mg, and 95/235 (40%) taking placebo (P = 0.01 for each active group versus placebo). Mean scores fell by about 25% in each active group, with mean changes of −5.09 and −5.76 points. Mean Qmax increased by 1.52, 1.79, and 0.93 mL/s, respectively; the 0.8 mg comparison met significance (P = 0.007), whereas the 0.4 mg comparison did not (P = 0.064). At least 30% Qmax improvement occurred in 82/244 (34%), 78/237 (33%), and 56/235 (24%), respectively (P &lt; 0.05 for each active group versus placebo). Symptom and flow improvements appeared by the first treatment week.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

The initial symptom-score gains in both active groups persisted during a 40-week double-blind extension: 418/618 (68%) primary-trial completers continued their assigned drug and dose, and 400 entered the efficacy analysis.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)[[4]](#ref4)</sup> At the 53-week endpoint, at least 25% symptom-score reduction occurred in 111/137 (81%) taking 0.4 mg, 103/132 (78%) taking 0.8 mg, and 72/123 (59%) taking placebo.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> Qmax changes from original baseline were +1.69, +2.10, and +0.43 mL/s, respectively; improvement from baseline was significant in both active groups (P &lt; 0.001), but not placebo.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Adverse events

In the primary trial, adverse events with 0.4 mg were comparable to placebo and were somewhat more frequent with 0.8 mg.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> Positive orthostatic tests were comparable across groups.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> In US 93-01, positive orthostatic tests at visit 4 occurred in 9 (4%) assigned 0.4 mg, 12 (5%) assigned 0.8 mg but still receiving 0.4 mg, and 2 (1%) assigned placebo (P = 0.012 for the second active group versus placebo).<sup>[[3]](#ref3)[[4]](#ref4)</sup> Abnormal ejaculation occurred in 27/248 (11%), 45/244 (18%), and 1/239 (&lt;1%), respectively; serious adverse events occurred in 5/248 (2%), 8/244 (3%), and 6/239 (3%).<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> Among men entering the extension, adverse events led to discontinuation in 5% of the 0.4 mg group, 16% of the 0.8 mg group, and 6% of placebo; over the combined 53 weeks of double-blind treatment, abnormal ejaculation was reported in 10%, 26%, and 0%, respectively.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> No new clinically significant orthostatic hypotension occurred in the double-blind extension.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Limitations

The primary randomized treatment period lasted 13 weeks. Only 418 of 618 patients entered its extension, which selected for men willing and able to continue assigned treatment.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
