# Intralesional interferon α-2b: Placebo-controlled treatment of Peyronie's disease

<TrialBottomLine id="interferon-peyronies" />

**Intralesional interferon α-2b** was tested against saline placebo in a multicenter, single-blind, parallel study of 117 men with Peyronie's disease. Among 103 completers assessed at least four weeks after the six-injection course, curvature, plaque size and density, and pain improved more with interferon; the between-group erectile-function difference was not significant.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

<TrialQuickHit id="interferon-peyronies" />

## Design

The study enrolled 55 men in the interferon arm and 62 in the saline arm at eight centers; mean age was 55.1 years.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> Entry required Peyronie's disease for at least 12 months, a single plaque, curvature of at least 30° on erection, and no calcified plaque. Curvature, plaque size and density, pain, International Index of Erectile Function (IIEF) scores, and penile hemodynamics were assessed before and after treatment. The paper did not designate a primary endpoint or describe the allocation method.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Results

Curvature fell from 49.9° to 36.4° with interferon (50 completers) and from 50.9° to 46.4° with saline (53 completers), a 9.0° greater mean reduction derived from the rounded arm means (p &lt;0.01). Mean plaque area fell from 4.8 to 2.2 cm² versus 4.5 to 3.6 cm² (between-group p &lt;0.001), and patient-rated plaque density fell from 2.29 to 1.52 versus 2.07 to 1.84 on a 0–3 scale (p &lt;0.05). Pain on erection resolved in 21/31 men with baseline pain given interferon (67.7%) versus 9/32 given saline (28.1%).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

Erectile-function scores increased from 18.32 to 20.80 with interferon and from 17.98 to 19.05 with saline; the between-group difference was not significant (p &gt;0.05).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

In the 39-patient, one-center Doppler subset, peak systolic velocity rose from 32.85 to 37.92 cm/s in 19 interferon recipients and fell from 31.36 to 30.48 cm/s in 20 saline recipients. The number classified as having no vascular pathology rose from 6/19 to 11/19 with interferon and remained 5/20 with saline.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> The Doppler report defined nonvascular status as peak systolic velocity &gt;30 cm/s, end-diastolic velocity ≤5 cm/s, and resistive index &gt;0.8.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> In that report, resistive index rose from 0.82 to 0.89 with interferon (within-arm p &lt;0.05), and borderline arterial insufficiency fell from 7/19 to 2/19 (within-arm p &lt;0.05); end-diastolic velocity and erectile-function scores did not change significantly in either arm.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

## Surgery and treatment delivery

Interferon α-2b (5 million units in 10 mL saline) or 10 mL saline alone was injected into the plaque every other week for six injections over 12 weeks.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> The investigators used a 10 mL syringe and 25-gauge needle with a multiple-puncture technique, then assessed outcomes at least four weeks after the final injection.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> The Doppler report describes a 1% lidocaine circumferential block before injection, a 27-gauge needle for its multiple-puncture technique, and direct pressure for at least two minutes afterward.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> Study completion was 50/55 in the interferon arm and 53/62 in the saline arm.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Adverse events

Flu-like symptoms (fever, chills, and arthralgia), sinusitis, and minor penile swelling with ecchymosis were reported after interferon injections. The symptoms responded to over-the-counter nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs taken before injection and lasted no longer than 36 hours; no patient withdrew because of an injection complication.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> The Doppler report recorded minor penile swelling with ecchymosis in 2/20 saline recipients.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> The AUA guideline also calls for counseling about sinusitis and minor penile swelling before treatment.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Limitations

The study was single-blind, and its allocation method and primary endpoint were not reported.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> The Doppler findings came from a 39-patient subset; the Doppler report describes its analysis as randomized and unblinded.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> Fourteen of the 117 enrolled men did not complete the study; outcomes were assessed at least four weeks after the injection course.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Guidelines and approvals

The AUA's 2015 Peyronie's disease guideline states that clinicians may administer intralesional interferon α-2b <Rec>(Moderate Recommendation; Evidence Strength Grade C)</Rec> and should counsel patients about sinusitis, flu-like symptoms, and minor penile swelling <Rec>(Clinical Principle)</Rec>.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
