# Intralesional interferon α-2b: Placebo-controlled treatment of Peyronie's disease

<TrialBottomLine id="interferon-peyronies" />

**Intralesional interferon α-2b** was tested against saline placebo in a multicenter, single-blind randomized trial of 117 men with Peyronie's disease; after the 12-week injection course, curvature, plaque size and density, and pain improved more with interferon, while the change in mean erectile-function score did not differ significantly.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

<TrialQuickHit id="interferon-peyronies" />

## Design

The interferon arm enrolled 55 men and the saline arm 62. Mean age was 55.1 years. The AUA guideline describes trial eligibility as Peyronie's symptoms lasting more than 12 months, curvature of at least 30°, and no calcified plaque. Curvature, plaque size and density, pain, International Index of Erectile Function (IIEF) scores, and penile hemodynamics were assessed before and after treatment.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Results

Curvature and plaque size and density improved more, and pain resolved more often, with interferon. Mean IIEF scores rose in both arms without a significant between-group difference.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

An earlier Doppler report examined 39 patients from the same randomized cohort, 19 assigned interferon and 20 saline. Penile blood-flow improvement was greater with interferon, and the proportion classified as having no vascular pathology increased in that arm. The erectile-function domain did not improve significantly in either arm of this subset.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

## Surgery and treatment delivery

Interferon α-2b was injected into the lesion at 5 million units every other week for 12 weeks; controls received 10 mL saline on the same schedule.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> The Doppler subset describes six injections. Study completion was 50 of 55 in the interferon arm and 53 of 62 in the saline arm.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

## Adverse events

Flu-like symptoms were frequent with interferon; reported adverse effects were generally mild to moderate and short-lived. The AUA guideline also calls for counseling about sinusitis and minor penile swelling before treatment.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Limitations

The Doppler findings came from a 39-patient subset. Fourteen of the 117 enrolled men did not complete the study, and outcomes were assessed at the end of the injection course.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

## Guidelines and approvals

The AUA's 2015 Peyronie's disease guideline states that clinicians may administer intralesional interferon α-2b <Rec>(Moderate Recommendation; Evidence Strength Grade C)</Rec> and should counsel patients about sinusitis, flu-like symptoms, and minor penile swelling <Rec>(Clinical Principle)</Rec>.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
