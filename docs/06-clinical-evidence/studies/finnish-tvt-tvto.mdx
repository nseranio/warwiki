# Finnish TVT versus TVT-O trial: Retropubic versus Transobturator Midurethral Tape for Stress Incontinence

<TrialBottomLine id="finnish-tvt-tvto" />

The **Finnish TVT versus TVT-O trial** randomized women undergoing primary surgery for stress urinary incontinence to retropubic tension-free vaginal tape (TVT) or transobturator tension-free vaginal tape (TVT-O) at seven hospitals; objective and subjective cure did not differ significantly at two months, while early complications and postoperative opioid use were higher with TVT-O.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

<TrialQuickHit id="finnish-tvt-tvto" />

## Design

Seven Finnish hospitals enrolled 273 women. Preoperative and two-month assessments included a cough stress test, the Urinary Incontinence Severity Score, the Detrusor Instability Score, the short-form Incontinence Impact Questionnaire and Urogenital Distress Inventory, and a visual analog scale. The investigators also recorded operation and theater time, length of stay, and intraoperative and immediate postoperative complications.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Results

The two-month report found improvement within both groups on every condition-specific quality-of-life questionnaire used, without a between-group difference. The five-year report defined objective success as a negative stress test, a negative 24-hour pad test, and no retreatment for stress incontinence. Objective cure was 84.7% after TVT and 86.2% after TVT-O; treatment satisfaction was 94.2% and 91.7%, respectively, with no statistically significant between-group differences.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> At five years, 95% of included women could be assessed according to protocol.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Surgery and treatment delivery

The comparison used the retropubic TVT and transobturator TVT-O procedures. Of 273 randomized women, 267 underwent the allocated operation: 136 received TVT and 131 received TVT-O. Hospital stay was significantly longer after TVT-O.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Adverse events

The primary report found a statistically higher overall complication rate and greater postoperative opioid analgesia use after TVT-O. The investigators judged the complication difference not clinically significant. At five years, complications were described as low and similar between groups, with no late-onset tape-material adverse effects observed.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Limitations

The initial assessment was at two months. The five-year objective endpoint combined stress testing, 24-hour pad testing, and freedom from retreatment, whereas the primary report assessed early cure and perioperative outcomes.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Guidelines and approvals

**AUA/SUFU (2023).** For index patients who select midurethral sling surgery, clinicians may offer a retropubic, transobturator, or single-incision sling <Rec>(Conditional Recommendation; Evidence Level: Grade A [retropubic/transobturator midurethral sling]/Grade B [single-incision sling])</Rec>. The unabridged guideline cites the Finnish five-year report in its evidence review.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
