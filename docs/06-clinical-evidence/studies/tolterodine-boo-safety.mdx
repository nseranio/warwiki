# Tolterodine BOO Safety: Tolterodine in Men With Overactive Bladder and Bladder Outlet Obstruction

<TrialBottomLine id="tolterodine-boo-safety" />

In a randomized trial of men with overactive bladder, detrusor overactivity and urodynamic bladder outlet obstruction, 12 weeks of tolterodine versus placebo produced comparable maximum flow and voiding-pressure measurements, but a greater rise in postvoid residual volume.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

<TrialQuickHit id="tolterodine-boo-safety" />

## Design

The primary report analyzed 221 men older than 40 years: 149 received tolterodine 2 mg twice daily and 72 received placebo. Detrusor overactivity and outlet obstruction were confirmed by urodynamics. Maximum urinary flow rate (Qmax) and detrusor pressure at maximum flow (pdetQmax) were the primary endpoints. The study also measured bladder outlet obstruction index (BOOI), filling cystometry, postvoid residual volume and adverse events.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Results

The median between-group Qmax difference was −0.7 mL/s (95% CI −1.6 to 0.4); the reported pdetQmax difference was −7 cm H2O. BOOI changed by −9 with tolterodine versus 0 with placebo (P &lt; 0.02). Volume at first detrusor contraction favored tolterodine by 59 mL (95% CI 19–100), and maximum cystometric capacity by 67 mL (95% CI 35–103). Postvoid residual rose by 25 mL with tolterodine versus 0 mL with placebo (P &lt; 0.004).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Adverse events

Urinary retention was reported in 0/149 tolterodine recipients and 1/72 placebo recipients. Overall adverse-event incidence did not differ significantly between groups.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Limitations

Treatment lasted 12 weeks, and one urinary retention event was reported. The trial's primary outcomes were urodynamic measurements; the single event gives little precision for the risk of uncommon retention.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Guidelines and approvals

The EAU 2026 guideline on non-neurogenic male LUTS cites this trial in its antimuscarinic safety discussion. It recommends muscarinic receptor antagonists for men with moderate-to-severe predominantly storage LUTS <Rec>(Strong)</Rec> and recommends against antimuscarinic OAB medication when postvoid residual exceeds 150 mL <Rec>(Weak)</Rec>.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
