# REINVENT: Nightly versus On-Demand Vardenafil After Nerve-Sparing Radical Prostatectomy

<TrialBottomLine id="reinvent" />

**REINVENT** was a multicenter, double-blind, double-dummy randomized trial of nightly vardenafil, on-demand vardenafil, and placebo in 628 men scheduled for bilateral nerve-sparing radical prostatectomy; neither vardenafil schedule significantly increased the proportion with an IIEF-EF score of at least 22 after a two-month washout.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

<TrialQuickHit id="reinvent" />

## Design

Eligible men were 18–64 years old and had a preoperative International Index of Erectile Function erectile-function domain (IIEF-EF) score of at least 26. Randomization occurred across 87 centers in Europe, Canada, South Africa, and the United States. The primary endpoint assessed unassisted function after withdrawal of study treatment. Sexual Encounter Profile question 3 (SEP3) measured successful intercourse as a secondary outcome.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Results

After washout, the proportion reaching IIEF-EF at least 22 did not differ significantly among groups; neither active schedule met the primary endpoint. SEP3 success after washout likewise showed no significant between-group difference. During randomized treatment, on-demand vardenafil produced higher IIEF-EF scores and SEP3 response rates than placebo.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Surgery and treatment delivery

Participants were scheduled for bilateral nerve-sparing radical prostatectomy. Study treatment ran for nine months under double blinding, followed by two months of single-blind washout and an optional two-month open-label period. The primary assessment followed the washout, separating erections assisted during dosing from erectile function after drug withdrawal.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Limitations

The primary assessment ended two months after treatment withdrawal; the later treatment period was open-label.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## In conversation

<InConversation id="reinvent" />

REACTT later tested daily and on-demand tadalafil after bilateral nerve-sparing prostatectomy, also assessing unassisted erectile function after a drug-free period. Its washout was shorter than REINVENT's.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Guidelines and approvals

The AUA erectile-dysfunction guideline (2018) advises informing men who want to preserve erectile function after prostate-cancer treatment that early PDE5-inhibitor use may not improve spontaneous, unassisted erections <Rec>(Moderate Recommendation; Evidence Level: Grade C)</Rec>.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> The EAU sexual and reproductive health guideline (2026) states that data are inadequate to support a specific penile-rehabilitation regimen <Rec>(Weak)</Rec> and recommends starting pro-erectile treatment at the earliest opportunity after radical prostatectomy <Rec>(Weak)</Rec>.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>
