# Solifenacin pivotal trial (Cardozo 2004): Once-Daily Solifenacin for Overactive Bladder

<TrialBottomLine id="solifenacin-pivotal" />

In a multinational, double-blind, randomized trial of patients with overactive bladder, solifenacin 5 mg and 10 mg once daily reduced mean micturitions per 24 hours more than placebo over 12 weeks.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

<TrialQuickHit id="solifenacin-pivotal" />

## Design

Cardozo and colleagues randomized 911 patients across three arms. The primary efficacy variable was change from baseline in mean micturitions per 24 hours at the end of treatment. Secondary variables included urgency, nocturia, and incontinence episodes per 24 hours and mean volume voided per micturition.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Results

Mean micturitions per 24 hours fell by 2.37 with solifenacin 5 mg, 2.81 with solifenacin 10 mg, and 1.59 with placebo (P = 0.0018 and P = 0.0001, respectively, versus placebo). Mean urgency episodes per 24 hours fell by 2.84 (51%) and 2.90 (52%) with the two solifenacin doses (P = 0.003 and P = 0.002 versus placebo). The 10 mg dose reduced mean nocturia episodes by 0.71 (38.5%) versus 0.52 (16.4%) with placebo (P = 0.036). Incontinence and urge-incontinence episodes also decreased significantly with both doses versus placebo.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Surgery and treatment delivery

Participants took oral solifenacin 5 mg or 10 mg, or placebo, once daily for 12 weeks.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Adverse events

Dry mouth, mostly mild, was reported by 7.7% of the 5 mg group, 23% of the 10 mg group, and 2.3% of the placebo group.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Limitations

The comparison lasted 12 weeks. Each fixed dose was compared with placebo; no direct statistical comparison between solifenacin doses was reported.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## In conversation

<InConversation id="solifenacin-pivotal" />

Chapple and colleagues published a separate four-arm trial that also tested solifenacin against placebo and included immediate-release tolterodine. Its participants and outcomes are distinct from Cardozo's trial.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
