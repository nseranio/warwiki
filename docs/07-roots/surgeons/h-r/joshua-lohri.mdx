## Biography

Joshua Mark Lohri is a urologist and faculty member in CAMC's urology department.[^1][^2] Lohri graduated from the West Virginia School of Osteopathic Medicine and completed urologic surgery residency training at CAMC, where Lohri served as chief resident.[^1] Lohri completed a fellowship in reconstructive urology, female urology, and urodynamics at Duke University Medical Center.[^1]
