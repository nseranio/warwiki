## Biography

Judith C. Hagedorn is an associate professor of urology at the University of Washington and directs its urology residency program.[^1][^2][^3] She earned her medical degree from Stanford School of Medicine and remained there for her urology residency.[^1] She completed a genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship at the University of Washington under Hunter Wessells.[^1]

## Contributions

Her confirmed citations address genitourinary trauma, including pelvic fracture urethral injury and repeat imaging after blunt renal trauma. They also cover transperineal rectourethral fistula repair and Fournier's gangrene, including skin-sparing debridement.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
