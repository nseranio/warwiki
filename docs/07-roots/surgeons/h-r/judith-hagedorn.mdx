## Contributions

Hagedorn's cited work covers Fournier's gangrene and renal trauma.[^5][^6][^7]

- **Contemporary review of Fournier's gangrene** (2017). Lead author, with Hunter Wessells, of a review of diagnosis, prognostic tools, resuscitation, antibiotics, early wide debridement with planned repeat debridement, and later genital and perineal reconstruction.[^5] Cited on WARWIKI: [Corticosteroids](/docs/foundations/pharmacology/perioperative-eras/corticosteroids).
- **Skin-sparing debridement in Fournier's gangrene** (2024). Senior author of a *Journal of Urology* article on the skin-sparing approach to debridement.[^6] Cited on WARWIKI: [Necrotizing Fasciitis / Fournier's Gangrene](/docs/special-populations/05a-trauma-emergencies/fourniers-gangrene).
- **Secondary overtriage in renal trauma** (2019). Led a National Trauma Data Bank study of 8,156 transferred patients with isolated renal injury in which 37% met criteria for secondary overtriage, most often with low-grade injuries.[^7]
