## Key Publications

Hagedorn's cited work covers Fournier's gangrene and renal trauma.<sup>[P1](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P2](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P3](#publication-7)</sup>

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Contemporary review of Fournier's gangrene** (2017). Lead author, with Hunter Wessells, of a review of diagnosis, prognostic tools, resuscitation, antibiotics, early wide debridement with planned repeat debridement, and later genital and perineal reconstruction.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Skin-sparing debridement in Fournier's gangrene** (2024). Senior author of a *Journal of Urology* article on the skin-sparing approach to debridement.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[2]} citations={citations}>

**Secondary overtriage in renal trauma** (2019). Led a National Trauma Data Bank study of 8,156 transferred patients with isolated renal injury in which 37% met criteria for secondary overtriage, most often with low-grade injuries.

</KeyPublication>

</ol>
