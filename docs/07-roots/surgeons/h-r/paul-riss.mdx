## Known for

- **IUGA urogynecology training guidelines** (2002). Coauthored IUGA training standards for urogynecology and reconstructive pelvic surgery.[^4]
- **Graphical representation of POP-Q**. Developed a graphical aid for teaching pelvic organ prolapse quantification measurements.[^5] Cited on WARWIKI: [Apical Prolapse](/docs/clinical-conditions/03c-pelvic-support/apical-prolapse).
