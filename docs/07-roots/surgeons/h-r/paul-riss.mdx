## Biography

Paul Riss and Engelbert Hanzel developed IUGA's Exchange program for countries where urogynecology was still developing, with a rule that at least half of each course's speakers come from the host country.[^1] Riss later served as a joint editor in chief of the *International Urogynecology Journal* with Peter Dwyer.[^1] During their editorship, the journal introduced video articles and other formats.[^1]

Manidip Pal recalled using Riss's teaching of pelvic floor anatomy in a textbook chapter and receiving his comments on the manuscript.[^1] Pal also described Riss's visual explanation of POP-Q measurement.[^1] Kari Bø recalled his preference for conservative treatment as a first step and his invitations to discuss evidence for pelvic floor muscle training.[^1]

One colleague remembered an operative workshop at SCB Medical College where Riss declined travel support and donated materials to the department.[^1] Other tributes remembered his kindness toward younger editors and trainees.[^1]
