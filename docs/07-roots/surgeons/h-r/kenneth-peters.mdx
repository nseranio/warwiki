## Contributions

Peters's cited work examines tibial nerve stimulation for overactive bladder, including the SUmiT and STEP studies, a device review, and implantable-device studies. Other cited papers address sacral and pudendal neuromodulation for voiding dysfunction and pelvic pain, as well as treatment of interstitial cystitis/bladder pain syndrome.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Sacral versus pudendal stimulation crossover trial** (2007). Compared sacral and pudendal nerve stimulation in a blinded randomized crossover study of refractory interstitial cystitis.[^8]
- **SUmiT trial of tibial nerve stimulation** (2010). Led a multicenter randomized sham-controlled trial of percutaneous tibial nerve stimulation for overactive bladder.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Percutaneous Tibial Nerve Stimulation](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/percutaneous-tibial-nerve-stimulation).
- **STEP study of sustained tibial nerve stimulation** (2013). Reported 24-month treatment and symptom outcomes among initial responders to percutaneous tibial nerve stimulation.[^10]
