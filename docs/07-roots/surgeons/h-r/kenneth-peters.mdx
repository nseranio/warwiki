## Key Publications

Peters's cited work examines tibial nerve stimulation for overactive bladder, including the SUmiT and STEP studies, a device review, and implantable-device studies. Other cited papers address sacral and pudendal neuromodulation for voiding dysfunction and pelvic pain, as well as treatment of interstitial cystitis/bladder pain syndrome.<sup>[P4](#publication-3)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P2](#publication-9)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-7)</sup>
