## Biography

Dylan Thomas Hoare is a general and reconstructive urologist in Barrie, Ontario.[^1][^2] Hoare received his medical degree from Queen's University in 2015.[^1] Hoare trained in urology at the University of Alberta, where he was listed among incoming residents in 2015.[^3][^4] Hoare completed the 2020–2021 reconstructive urology fellowship at the University of Alberta, where Keith F. Rourke directed the program.[^3][^5][^6]

## Contributions

Hoare's cited work examines bulbar and fossa navicularis urethroplasty techniques and surveys perioperative urethroplasty practices.[^7][^8][^9]
