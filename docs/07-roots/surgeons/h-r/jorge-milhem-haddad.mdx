## Known for

- **FEBRASGO urogynecology and vaginal-surgery manual** (2015). Coordinated the Brazilian federation’s manual covering pelvic organ prolapse, urinary incontinence, pelvic floor disorders, and vaginal surgery.[^3]
- **Five-year randomized comparison of prolapse repairs** (2020). Coauthored a multicenter randomized follow-up comparing five-year outcomes of native-tissue and polypropylene-mesh pelvic organ prolapse repair.[^4]

## Contributions

His cited consensus addresses treatment and prevention of recurrent uncomplicated urinary tract infection in Latin America.[^2]
