## Biography

Maria Francesca Monn is an assistant professor of surgery in the Division of Urology at Southern Illinois University School of Medicine.[^1][^2] Monn earned her medical degree and completed her urology residency at Indiana University School of Medicine.[^3] She completed a 2019–2020 reconstructive urology fellowship at Eastern Virginia Medical School's Devine-Jordan Center, with Kurt A. McCammon as fellowship director.[^3][^4][^5]

## Contributions

Monn's cited work covers urethral and ureteral reconstruction, including a penile skin island flap for fossa navicularis stricture, minimally invasive treatments for posterior urethral stenosis, and ileal ureter substitution. It also examines full-thickness skin grafting during adult acquired buried penis repair.[^6][^7][^8][^9][^10]
