## Biography

Rachel A. Mann is an assistant professor in the University of Minnesota Department of Urology and its associate residency program director.[^1][^2] She graduated from the University of Michigan Medical School and completed urology residency at the University of Minnesota.[^3][^1] She completed a genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship at Lahey Hospital and Medical Center in 2024, in the program directed by Alex J. Vanni.[^1][^2]
