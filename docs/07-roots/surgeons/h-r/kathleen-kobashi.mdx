## Training and career

<div className="sp-facts">

- **Current position** Chair, Department of Urology, Houston Methodist Hospital; professor of clinical urology, Weill Cornell Medicine.[^1]
- **Former position** Virginia Mason, joined in 1999.[^2]
- **Medical school** Drexel University (formerly Hahnemann).[^2]
- **Residency** Urology, University of California, Irvine.[^2]
- **Fellowship** Urogynecology and reconstructive pelvic surgery, Cedars-Sinai, Los Angeles, before joining Virginia Mason in 1999.[^2]
- **Leadership** Past president, Society for Urodynamics, Female Pelvic Medicine and Urogenital Reconstruction.[^2]

</div>

## Contributions

Her cited work includes the 2023 AUA/SUFU guideline update on surgery for female stress urinary incontinence and chapters on urethrovaginal fistula management and repair.[^3][^4][^5]

- **AUA/SUFU female stress urinary incontinence surgical guideline** (2017). Chaired the AUA/SUFU panel that issued evidence-based guidance on surgical treatment of female stress urinary incontinence.[^6]
