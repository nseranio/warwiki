## Biography

Kathleen C. Kobashi chairs the Department of Urology at Houston Methodist Hospital and is a professor of clinical urology at Weill Cornell Medicine.[^1] She received her MD from Drexel University, formerly Hahnemann, and completed her urology residency at the University of California, Irvine.[^2] She completed a urogynecology and reconstructive pelvic surgery fellowship at Cedars-Sinai in Los Angeles before joining Virginia Mason in 1999.[^2] She is a past president of the Society for Urodynamics, Female Pelvic Medicine and Urogenital Reconstruction.[^2]

## Contributions

Her cited work includes the 2023 AUA/SUFU guideline update on surgery for female stress urinary incontinence and chapters on urethrovaginal fistula management and repair.[^3][^4][^5]
