## Biography

Kathleen C. Kobashi chairs the Department of Urology at Houston Methodist Hospital and is a professor of clinical urology at Weill Cornell Medicine.[^1] Kobashi earned her medical degree at Drexel University and completed a urology residency at the University of California, Irvine.[^2] Kobashi completed a female pelvic medicine and reconstructive surgery fellowship at Cedars-Sinai in Los Angeles.[^3] Kobashi is a past president of the Society for Urodynamics, Female Pelvic Medicine and Urogenital Reconstruction.[^2]

## Contributions

Kobashi's cited surgical chapters address urethrovaginal fistula repair, alongside a guideline update on surgical treatment of female stress urinary incontinence.[^4][^5][^6]
