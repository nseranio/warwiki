## Biography

John A. Occhino is chair of the Division of Urogynecology and professor of obstetrics and gynecology at Mayo Clinic in Rochester, Minnesota.[^1] He earned his medical degree at the University of Cincinnati and completed an obstetrics and gynecology residency at Good Samaritan Hospital.[^2] He completed a female pelvic medicine and reconstructive surgery fellowship at Mayo Clinic; John B. Gebhart was among his mentors.[^1][^3] He directed Mayo Clinic's urogynecology and reconstructive pelvic surgery fellowship from 2015 to 2025.[^2]

## Contributions

His cited papers describe surgical approaches to urogenital fistula repair, including robotic and endoscopic techniques. They also cover colpocleisis techniques and ureteral stent placement.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
