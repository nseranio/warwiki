## Biography

Paul E. Perito is a urologist at Perito Urology in Coral Gables, Florida.[^1][^2][^3] He attended the University of Maryland Medical School and completed general surgery and urology residency training at Jackson Memorial Hospital and the University of Miami.[^3] He completed a fellowship in prostatic disease at Flinders Hospital and Flinders University in South Australia.[^2][^3] He has served as president of the Society of Prosthetic Surgeons.[^2]

## Contributions

Perito's cited papers address inflatable penile prosthesis implantation through an infrapubic incision, abdominal wall reservoir placement, and a pump pseudo-malfunction pattern.[^4][^5][^6][^7]
