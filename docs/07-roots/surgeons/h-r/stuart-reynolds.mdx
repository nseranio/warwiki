## Contributions

His cited work comes from the Vanderbilt functional urology group: mesh complication surgery, onabotulinumtoxinA outcomes, sacral neuromodulation, and textbook chapters on slings and outlet obstruction after incontinence surgery.[^5][^6][^7][^8]

- **Obturator foramen dissection for transobturator mesh excision** (2012). Led a series of 8 women with intractable groin or obturator pain after transobturator mesh in which residual mesh was found and removed through a lateral groin incision in every case, and 5 were cured of pain or infection at a mean 6 months.[^5] Cited on WARWIKI: [Mesh Complications & Removal](/docs/surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/mesh-complications).
- **Urinary retention after onabotulinumtoxinA for overactive bladder** (2015). Senior author of a clinical-practice review of 160 patients in which 35% developed retention after a first injection; a preoperative postvoid residual of 100 mL or more was followed by retention in 94%.[^6] Cited on WARWIKI: [Botulinum Toxin (OAB / Storage)](/docs/foundations/pharmacology/storage-oab/botulinum-toxin).
