## Contributions

McClung's early work covers staged urethroplasty, renal trauma management and TURNS multicenter outcomes of artificial urinary sphincter placement; his current practice is gender-affirming genital surgery.[^1][^3][^4][^5]

- **Staged urethroplasty** (2014). Lead author, with Hunter Wessells, of the staged urethroplasty chapter in *Advanced Male Urethral and Genital Reconstructive Surgery*.[^3] Cited on WARWIKI: [Bracka Two-Stage Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/bracka-two-stage).
- **Immediate surgical management of renal trauma** (2013). Led a National Trauma Data Bank analysis of 1,183 patients who had open renal surgery within 24 hours of admission: nephrectomy rates were 54% after penetrating and 83% after blunt injury, and 86% had concomitant surgery for other abdominal injuries.[^4]
- **Artificial urinary sphincter erosion** (2014). Coauthored the TURNS prospective study of 386 sphincters at 8 institutions: 31 (8.0%) were explanted, with higher rates after prior radiation, prior UroLume stent placement and 3.5 cm cuffs.[^5] Cited on WARWIKI: [Artificial Urinary Sphincter (Device)](/docs/foundations/tools/biomaterials/prosthetics/artificial-urinary-sphincter).
