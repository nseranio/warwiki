## Key Publications

McClung's early work covers staged urethroplasty, renal trauma management and TURNS multicenter outcomes of artificial urinary sphincter placement; his current practice is gender-affirming genital surgery.[^1]<sup>[P1](#publication-3)</sup><sup>[P2](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P3](#publication-5)</sup>

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Staged urethroplasty** (2014). Lead author, with Hunter Wessells, of the staged urethroplasty chapter in *Advanced Male Urethral and Genital Reconstructive Surgery*.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Immediate surgical management of renal trauma** (2013). Led a National Trauma Data Bank analysis of 1,183 patients who had open renal surgery within 24 hours of admission: nephrectomy rates were 54% after penetrating and 83% after blunt injury, and 86% had concomitant surgery for other abdominal injuries.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[2]} citations={citations}>

**Artificial urinary sphincter erosion** (2014). Coauthored the TURNS prospective study of 386 sphincters at 8 institutions: 31 (8.0%) were explanted, with higher rates after prior radiation, prior UroLume stent placement and 3.5 cm cuffs.

</KeyPublication>

</ol>
