## Biography

Daniel Rapoport is a clinical instructor in the University of British Columbia Department of Urologic Sciences and practices primarily at Richmond Hospital.[^1] He earned his medical degree from the University of British Columbia in 2003 and completed its urology residency in 2008.[^2] He completed a Duke University fellowship in reconstructive urology, neurourology, and urodynamics in 2008–2009.[^1]

[^1]: [Dr. Daniel Rapoport Metrovan Urology](https://www.metrovanurology.com/dr-daniel-rapoport). Accessed October 2026.
