## Contributions

- **Spanish validation of the ICIQ-UI Short Form** (2004). Led validation of the Spanish-language International Consultation on Incontinence Questionnaire Short Form for assessing urinary incontinence.[^4]
- **Tratado de uroginecología** (2004). Coedited a Spanish-language treatise on urogynecology and urinary incontinence.[^5]
- **Spanish validation of the PISQ-IR** (2017). Senior-authored a Spanish transcultural validation of the PISQ-IR sexual-function questionnaire for women with prolapse or urinary incontinence.[^6]
