## Biography

Elaine Redmond is a consultant urologist at Cork University Hospital, treating adult and pediatric patients.[^1] She attended medical school at University College Cork and completed the Irish urology residency program in 2019.[^2] In 2020, she completed a GURS-accredited reconstructive urology fellowship at the University of Alberta under Keith Rourke.[^1][^2] She then completed a pediatric urology fellowship at BC Children's Hospital in Vancouver.[^1]
