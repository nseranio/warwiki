## Biography

Paul J. Rusilko is an associate professor at the University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine and director of UPMC's Reconstructive Urology and Men's Health Division.[^1] He earned his medical degree from Lake Erie College of Osteopathic Medicine and completed a urology residency at Northeast Ohio Medical University.[^1] He completed a reconstructive and trauma urology fellowship at Detroit Medical Center under Richard Santucci.[^1][^2] He serves on the board of directors of the Society of Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgeons.[^1]

## Contributions

His cited work covers adult acquired buried penis repair, including escutcheonectomy and penile skin grafting, postoperative urinary and sexual function, surgical complications, and associated penile pathology.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
