## Biography

Maria Antonia Ocampo Gomez practices urology at Fundación Valle del Lili.[^1] Ocampo Gómez studied medicine at Universidad del Rosario.[^2] Ocampo Gómez completed urology training at Universidad del Rosario.[^1] Ocampo Gómez completed a 2023–2024 reconstructive urology fellowship at the Kulkarni Reconstructive Urology Center in Pune, with Sanjay B. Kulkarni as program director.[^3][^4][^5]

## Contributions

Ocampo Gómez's cited work covers urethral reconstruction, including pelvic fracture urethral injury, an approach to penile strictures, classification of bulbar urethral necrosis, and gracilis flap repair of rectourethral fistula.[^6][^7][^8][^9]
