## Biography

Tobias S. Köhler is a professor of urology at Mayo Clinic in Rochester, Minnesota.[^1][^2] He earned his medical degree from the University of Minnesota Medical School and completed urology residency at the University of Minnesota.[^3] He completed an andrology fellowship at Northwestern University.[^3] He is president of the Sexual Medicine Society of North America.[^1]

## Contributions

Köhler's confirmed citations cover penile prosthesis recommendations and implant complications, including glans ischemia, alternative reservoir placement, and perioperative antithrombotic management. Other cited studies address collagenase, penile traction, and treatment options for Peyronie's disease.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
