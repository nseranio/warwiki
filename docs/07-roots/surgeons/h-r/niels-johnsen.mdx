## Biography

Niels Vass Johnsen is an associate professor of urology at Vanderbilt University Medical Center and director of its Genitourinary Reconstruction and Trauma Fellowship.[^1][^2] He received his medical degree from Tulane University School of Medicine and completed urology residency at Vanderbilt University Medical Center.[^2] Johnsen completed a fellowship in male genitourinary reconstruction and trauma at the University of Washington in the program directed by Hunter Wessells.[^2][^3][^4]

## Contributions

His cited work addresses genitourinary trauma, including pelvic fracture urethral injury, bladder rupture and blunt lower urinary tract trauma, renal imaging after injury, and the American College of Surgeons management guideline.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]
