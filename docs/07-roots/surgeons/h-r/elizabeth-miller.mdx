## Biography

Overlake listed Elizabeth A. Miller under urology in 2022 and urogynecology in 2025.[^1][^2] Elizabeth A. Miller received an MD from Washington University in 1994.[^3] Miller completed a reconstructive urology fellowship at Duke University in 2000–2001.[^4]
