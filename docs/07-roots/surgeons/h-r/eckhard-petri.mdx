## Biography

Eckhard Petri trained in urology and gynecology in Mainz.[^2] He began organizing scientific urogynecology meetings there, continued them in Idar-Oberstein, and carried them to Schwerin after moving there as a department chief.[^2] The Schwerin meeting drew leading urogynecologists and later alternated annually with a meeting in Halle.[^2]

Petri helped found IUGA and later served as its president.[^4] He chaired its 1983 meeting in Mainz, co-chaired the 1985 Budapest meeting, and served on the *International Urogynecology Journal* editorial board.[^4] His visiting surgery and teaching extended beyond Germany; IUGA's memorial records fellows from Asia, Europe, and the Americas.[^2][^4]

Colleagues in AGUB remembered him as open to new developments while asking for evidence.[^2] He wrote *Gynäkologische Urologie* across four revised editions and also practiced oncologic and breast surgery.[^2][^4]
