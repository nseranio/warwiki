## Contributions

- **Founding the International Urogynecological Association** (1976). Participated in IUGA’s founding group and later served as its secretary-treasurer and president.[^5]
- **IUGA/ICS pelvic floor terminology** (2010). Coauthored the joint IUGA/ICS terminology report for female pelvic floor dysfunction.[^6] Cited on WARWIKI: [Mesh Complications & Removal — POP and SUI Mesh](/docs/surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/mesh-complications).
