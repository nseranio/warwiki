## Key Publications

Nikolavsky's cited work includes transurethral ventral buccal mucosa graft inlay for distal urethral strictures, liquid buccal mucosa graft treatment, and gracilis-buccal flap reconstruction for devastated urethras. His cited papers also address urinary complications and neourethral strictures after gender-affirming genital surgery, as well as posterior urethral stenosis after prostate cancer treatment.<sup>[P3](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-8)</sup>
