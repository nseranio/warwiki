## Contributions

Nikolavsky's cited work includes transurethral ventral buccal mucosa graft inlay for distal urethral strictures, liquid buccal mucosa graft treatment, and gracilis-buccal flap reconstruction for devastated urethras. His cited papers also address urinary complications and neourethral strictures after gender-affirming genital surgery, as well as posterior urethral stenosis after prostate cancer treatment.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

- **Transurethral ventral buccal graft inlay for distal urethral stricture** (2016). Described transurethral placement of a ventral buccal mucosal graft for fossa navicularis and distal urethral strictures.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Transmeatal OMG Ventral Inlay (Nikolavsky)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/transmeatal-omg-ventral-inlay).
- **Staged repair of long neophallus urethral strictures** (2022). Senior-authored a report demonstrating staged repair of long, complex strictures of the neophallic urethra.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Phallic Urethra: Prelaminated Buccal Mucosa Graft](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery/phallic-urethra-prelaminated-bmg).
