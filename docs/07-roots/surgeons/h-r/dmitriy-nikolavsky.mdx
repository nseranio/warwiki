## Biography

Dmitriy Nikolavsky is professor of urology and director of reconstructive urology at SUNY Upstate University Hospital in Syracuse, New York.[^1] He earned his medical degree at Wayne State University School of Medicine.[^2] He completed a urology residency at William Beaumont Hospital in Royal Oak, Michigan.[^2] He completed a reconstructive urology fellowship at the University of Colorado from 2011 to 2012.[^2][^3]

## Contributions

Nikolavsky's cited work includes transurethral ventral buccal mucosa graft inlay for distal urethral strictures, liquid buccal mucosa graft treatment, and gracilis-buccal flap reconstruction for devastated urethras. His cited papers also address urinary complications and neourethral strictures after gender-affirming genital surgery, as well as posterior urethral stenosis after prostate cancer treatment.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
