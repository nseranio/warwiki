Her work focuses on pelvic floor research rather than clinical practice.[^1]

## Contributions

Her confirmed citations examine pelvic organ prolapse natural history and surgical outcomes, including long-term sacrocolpopexy results and incontinence prevention during prolapse repair. They also cover treatment-response fluctuations after onabotulinumtoxinA and sacral neuromodulation and complications of retropubic incontinence surgery.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
