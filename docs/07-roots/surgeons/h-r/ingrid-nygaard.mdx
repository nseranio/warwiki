## Key Publications

Her confirmed citations examine pelvic organ prolapse natural history and surgical outcomes, including long-term sacrocolpopexy results and incontinence prevention during prolapse repair. They also cover treatment-response fluctuations after onabotulinumtoxinA and sacral neuromodulation and complications of retropubic incontinence surgery.<sup>[P3](#publication-3)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-7)</sup>
