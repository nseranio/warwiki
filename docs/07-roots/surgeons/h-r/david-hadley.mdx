## Biography

David A. Hadley is a urologist with Kettering Health Medical Group at its Troy, Ohio clinic.[^1] He graduated from Loma Linda University School of Medicine and completed his urology residency at the University of Utah.[^1] He completed a University of Colorado fellowship in female pelvic medicine and reconstructive surgery in 2011 under Brian J. Flynn.[^1][^2]

## Contributions

Hadley's cited papers address lower urinary tract reconstruction, including vesicourethral anastomotic stricture repair, continent urinary channels for adults with neurogenic bladder, and York-Mason repair of recto-urinary fistulae.[^3][^4][^5]
