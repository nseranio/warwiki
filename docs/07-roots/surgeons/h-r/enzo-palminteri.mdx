## Biography

Enzo Maria Filippo Palminteri is listed as a section head in the urethral and genital surgery unit at Humanitas Cellini in Turin.[^1][^2] Palminteri also works as a consultant in urethral and genital reconstructive surgery at Centro Chirurgico Toscano in Arezzo and Hesperia Hospital in Modena.[^2] Palminteri earned a medical degree at the University of Palermo in 1991 and completed urology specialization at the University of Florence in 1997.[^2] Palminteri began collaborating with Guido Barbagli in 1993; the two established the Center for Urethral and Genital Surgery in 1998.[^2]

## Contributions

The cited work examines graft urethroplasty for bulbar and penile strictures, including staged buccal grafting, dorsal and ventral double grafts, and small intestinal submucosa grafts.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Two-stage buccal mucosal graft urethroplasty** (2002). Reported a two-stage buccal mucosal graft approach for complex urethral reconstruction.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Bracka Two-Stage Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/bracka-two-stage).
- **Dorsal and ventral double buccal-graft bulbar urethroplasty** (2008). Described combined dorsal and ventral buccal mucosal grafting to augment a narrow bulbar urethra.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Palminteri Double-Face (Two-Sided) BMG Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/palminteri-double-face).
