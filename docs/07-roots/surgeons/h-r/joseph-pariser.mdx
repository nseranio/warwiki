## Biography

Joseph J. Pariser is an associate professor of urology at the University of Minnesota Medical School and co-director of M Health Comprehensive Gender Care.[^1][^2] He earned his medical degree and completed urology residency at the University of Chicago.[^3] He completed fellowships in genitourinary reconstructive surgery at the University of Minnesota and in trauma and genitourinary reconstructive urology at Detroit Medical Center.[^2]

## Contributions

Pariser's cited work includes an adult acquired buried penis repair classification, penile prosthesis anchoring after radial forearm free flap neophallus reconstruction, and management of complications after feminizing genital surgery.[^4][^5][^6]
