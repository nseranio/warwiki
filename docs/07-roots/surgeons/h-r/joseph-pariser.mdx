## Contributions

Pariser's cited work covers adult acquired buried penis, revision of bladder augmentation, and complications of feminizing gender-affirming genital surgery.[^7][^8][^9]

- **Classification of adult acquired buried penis repair** (2018). Led a series of 64 repairs graded in five categories of surgical complexity; 91% were successfully unburied, high-grade complications occurred only in the complex group, and 47% of patients had urethral stricture regardless of complexity.[^7] Cited on WARWIKI: [Buried Penis](/docs/clinical-conditions/03g-genital-scrotal/buried-penis), [Buried Penis Repair](/docs/surgical-techniques/04e-genital-reconstruction/buried-penis-repair).
- **Augmentation cystoplasty revision without additional bowel** (2019). Led a report of seven patients with an hourglass deformity or a non-detubularized augment revised by detubularization or completion cystotomy; six reported symptom improvement and one later required an Indiana pouch.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Principles of Bladder Augmentation](/docs/surgical-techniques/04b-bladder-reconstruction/augmentation-principles).
- **Management of complications after vaginoplasty** (2021). Senior author of a review describing operative approaches to complications after penile inversion vaginoplasty.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Feminizing Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery/feminizing-urethroplasty), [Meatal Stenosis Revision](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery/meatal-stenosis-revision).
