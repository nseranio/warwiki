## Key Publications

Pariser's cited work covers adult acquired buried penis, revision of bladder augmentation, and complications of feminizing gender-affirming genital surgery.<sup>[P1](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P2](#publication-8)</sup><sup>[P3](#publication-9)</sup>

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Classification of adult acquired buried penis repair** (2018). Led a series of 64 repairs graded in five categories of surgical complexity; 91% were successfully unburied, high-grade complications occurred only in the complex group, and 47% of patients had urethral stricture regardless of complexity.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Augmentation cystoplasty revision without additional bowel** (2019). Led a report of seven patients with an hourglass deformity or a non-detubularized augment revised by detubularization or completion cystotomy; six reported symptom improvement and one later required an Indiana pouch.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[2]} citations={citations}>

**Management of complications after vaginoplasty** (2021). Senior author of a review describing operative approaches to complications after penile inversion vaginoplasty.

</KeyPublication>
