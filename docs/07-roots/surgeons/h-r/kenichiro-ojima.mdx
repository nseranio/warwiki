## Biography

Kenichiro Ojima is a urologist at Nishisaitama Chuo National Hospital.[^1][^2] Ojima graduated from the National Defense Medical College in 2011.[^1] Ojima undertook specialist training and graduate medical education at the National Defense Medical College and earned a degree through research on urethral regenerative medicine.[^1]

## Contributions

Ojima's cited papers examine urethral reconstruction for anterior stricture, including perineal urethrostomy, staged buccal mucosal urethroplasty, and excision and primary anastomosis. They also assess patient-reported outcomes after urethroplasty and deep lateral incision for vesicourethral anastomotic stenosis.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
