## Biography

Jacob Ramon is listed by Tel Aviv University as a clinical professor emeritus in its Faculty of Medicine.[^1] Ramon completed Duke University's reconstructive urology fellowship with George D. Webster in 1988–1989.[^2][^3]

## Contributions

Ramon's cited papers describe pelvic fracture urethral repair, needle suspension for recurrent stress incontinence, and fascial patch repair of urethrovaginal fistula.[^4][^5][^6]
