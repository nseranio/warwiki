## Contributions

His cited papers examine female lower urinary tract evaluation with urodynamics and endorectal coil MRI, as well as outcomes after Skene gland cyst excision and vesicovaginal fistula repair.[^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Upper-tract risk assessment in multiple-sclerosis neurogenic bladder** (2005). Studied upper-tract abnormalities and clinical and urodynamic risk factors in patients with multiple-sclerosis-related bladder dysfunction.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Multiple Sclerosis](/docs/clinical-conditions/03d-nlutd/nlutd-multiple-sclerosis).
- **Preoperative urodynamics and voiding dysfunction after incontinence surgery** (2008). Led an analysis finding that normal preoperative urodynamic tests did not predict voiding dysfunction after Burch colposuspension or sling surgery.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Urodynamics](/docs/evaluation/ancillary-tests/urodynamics).
