## Known for

- **Immediate penile prosthesis implantation for refractory ischemic priapism** (2009). Led a 50-patient report of immediate prosthesis implantation after refractory ischemic priapism.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Priapism Shunts & Decompression](/docs/surgical-techniques/04j-sexual-dysfunction/priapism-shunts-decompression).
- **Peyronie’s disease management guidelines** (2010). First-authored an evidence-based consensus guideline covering medical and surgical management of Peyronie’s disease.[^9]
- **Penile prostheses after phalloplasty** (2018). Senior-authored a 247-patient series reporting outcomes and revisions after inflatable prosthesis implantation in neophalluses.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Erectile Prosthesis (Penile Implant) After Gender-Affirming Phalloplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery/penile-implant-after-phalloplasty).

## Contributions

His cited work addresses phallic reconstruction, including radial forearm free flap phalloplasty and erectile device placement after phalloplasty. Other cited papers address surgical management of ischemic priapism, including decompression and penile prosthesis placement.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
