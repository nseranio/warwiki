## Contributions

His cited work addresses phallic reconstruction, including radial forearm free flap phalloplasty and erectile device placement after phalloplasty. Other cited papers address surgical management of ischemic priapism, including decompression and penile prosthesis placement.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
