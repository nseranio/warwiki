## Biography

Axel Ingelman-Sundberg was professor and head of obstetrics and gynecology at Sabbatsberg Hospital, Karolinska Institute, Stockholm, from 1949 to 1979.[^1] Ingelman-Sundberg studied medicine at Uppsala University.[^1] Ingelman-Sundberg co-founded the International Urogynecological Association in 1976.[^1] Ingelman-Sundberg served as the association's president from 1976 to 1980.[^2]
