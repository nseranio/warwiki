## Biography

Axel Ingelman-Sundberg was professor and head of obstetrics and gynecology at Sabbatsberg Hospital, Karolinska Institute, Stockholm, from 1949 to 1979.[^1] Ingelman-Sundberg studied medicine at Uppsala University.[^1] Ingelman-Sundberg co-founded the International Urogynecological Association in 1976.[^1] Ingelman-Sundberg served as the association's president from 1976 to 1980.[^2]

## Contributions

- **Ingelman-Sundberg pubococcygeus repair for stress urinary incontinence** (1947). Described an extravaginal pelvic floor repair using pubococcygeus muscle to support the bladder neck in stress urinary incontinence.[^4] Cited on WARWIKI: [Needle Suspensions and In Situ Slings (Historical)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/historical-bladder-neck-suspensions).
- **Co-founding the International Urogynecological Association** (1976). Co-organized the association’s founding at the 1976 FIGO meeting and served as its first president.[^5]
