## Biography

Kevin J. Krughoff is a reconstructive urologic surgeon at Oregon Urology Institute in Springfield, Oregon.[^1] He earned his MD at the University of Colorado School of Medicine and completed urology residency at Dartmouth-Hitchcock Medical Center.[^1] He completed a fellowship in reconstructive urology and genitourinary cancer survivorship at Duke University from 2021 to 2022.[^1]

[^1]: [Kevin J. Krughoff, MD. Oregon Urology Institute](https://oregonurology.com/divi_overlay/kevin-j-krughoff-md/). Accessed October 2026.
