## Biography

Nathan Y. Hoy is an assistant professor in the University of Alberta's Department of Surgery.[^1] Hoy completed medical school and urology residency at the University of Alberta.[^2] Hoy completed a male genitourinary reconstruction and prosthetics fellowship at Cleveland Clinic, where Kenneth Angermeier is listed as program director.[^2][^3] Hoy directs the University of Alberta's reconstruction fellowship.[^4]

## Contributions

Hoy's cited papers examine urethroplasty technique and outcomes, including augmented anastomotic repair, long-term recurrence factors, and revision surgery.[^5][^6][^7][^8]
