## Contributions

His cited work studies surgical approaches and reoperation after pelvic organ prolapse repair, including the ASPIRe trial and anterior vaginal wall repair. Other cited trials compare treatments for urinary incontinence, including sacral neuromodulation, onabotulinumtoxinA, and midurethral slings.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]

- **Anterior prolapse repair randomized trial** (2011). Led a randomized comparison of anterior colporrhaphy, porcine-dermis reinforcement and polypropylene-mesh repair.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Paravaginal Repair](/docs/surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/paravaginal-repair).
- **ASPIRe apical suspension trial** (2024). Led a multicenter randomized trial comparing sacrocolpopexy, transvaginal mesh and native-tissue repair for vaginal vault prolapse.[^11] Cited on WARWIKI: [Mesh & Graft-Augmented Prolapse Repair](/docs/surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/mesh-graft-augmented-repairs).
