## Key Publications

His cited work studies surgical approaches and reoperation after pelvic organ prolapse repair, including the ASPIRe trial and anterior vaginal wall repair. Other cited trials compare treatments for urinary incontinence, including sacral neuromodulation, onabotulinumtoxinA, and midurethral slings.<sup>[P2](#publication-11)</sup><sup>[P3](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-8)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-9)</sup>
