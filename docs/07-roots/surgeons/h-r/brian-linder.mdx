## Biography

Brian J. Linder is a professor of urology and obstetrics and gynecology and vice chair of research in the Department of Urology at Mayo Clinic.[^1] He completed medical school at George Washington University.[^2] He completed a urology residency and a urogynecology fellowship at Mayo Clinic.[^2]

## Contributions

His cited work covers surgical approaches to genitourinary fistulas and outcomes after artificial urinary sphincter placement and revision.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
