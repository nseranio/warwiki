## Biography

Charles R. Powell is a professor of urology and obstetrics and gynecology at Indiana University School of Medicine.[^1] He earned his MD from Indiana University School of Medicine and completed his residency at the University of Illinois College of Medicine in Chicago.[^2] He completed a fellowship at the University of Iowa Roy J. and Lucille Carver College of Medicine.[^2] He has served on the American Urological Association Practice Guidelines Committee.[^2]
