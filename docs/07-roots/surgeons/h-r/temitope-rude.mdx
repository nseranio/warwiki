## Biography

Temitope O. Rude is a clinical assistant professor of urology at Stanford University School of Medicine and a surgeon at Stanford Health Care's Pelvic Health Center and Urology Clinic.[^1] She received her MD from Stanford University School of Medicine in 2013 and completed her urology residency at NYU Langone Medical Center in 2018.[^1][^2] She completed a Female Pelvic Medicine and Reconstructive Surgery fellowship at the University of Southern California in 2020.[^1]

[^1]: [Tope Rude, MD's Profile. Stanford Profiles](https://profiles.stanford.edu/temitope-rude). Accessed October 2026.

[^2]: [Meet Dr. Temitope O. Rude: A Trailblazer in Urology, Innovation, and Inclusive Healthcare. Department of Urol](https://med.stanford.edu/urology/news/drrude.html). Accessed October 2026.
