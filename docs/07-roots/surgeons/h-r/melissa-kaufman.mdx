## Contributions

Kaufman's cited work addresses urinary incontinence treatments, including artificial urinary sphincter outcomes, autologous cell therapy, and sling surgery. Her cited guideline work also addresses genitourinary syndrome of menopause.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

- **AUA/SUFU/AUGS genitourinary syndrome of menopause guideline** (2025). Chaired the joint guideline on identifying, counseling, and treating patients with genitourinary syndrome of menopause.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Vaginal Laser Therapy](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/vaginal-laser-therapy).
- **Artificial Urinary Sphincter Clinical Outcomes trial** (2026). Led the prospective multicenter AUSCO study reporting continence, safety, and quality-of-life outcomes after AMS 800 implantation.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Artificial Urinary Sphincter (Procedure)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/artificial-urinary-sphincter).
