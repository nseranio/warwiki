## Biography

Melissa R. Kaufman is the Patricia and Rodes Hart Professor of Urologic Surgery and chief of the Division of Reconstructive Urology and Pelvic Health at Vanderbilt University Medical Center.[^1][^2] She earned her medical degree from the University of Arkansas College of Medicine and completed urology residency at Vanderbilt University Medical Center.[^3] She completed a female pelvic medicine and reconstructive surgery fellowship at Vanderbilt under Roger Dmochowski.[^2][^3] She is a past president of the Society of Women in Urology.[^3]

## Contributions

Kaufman's cited work addresses urinary incontinence treatments, including artificial urinary sphincter outcomes, autologous cell therapy, and sling surgery. Her cited guideline work also addresses genitourinary syndrome of menopause.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
