## Biography

Jerilyn M. Latini is a urologic surgeon at VA Boston health care and an associate professor at Boston University Chobanian & Avedisian School of Medicine.[^1][^2] She earned her MD at Dartmouth Medical School and completed urology residency at Lahey Clinic Medical Center.[^2] She completed a genitourinary surgery and neurourology fellowship at the University of Iowa in 2003.[^2]

## Contributions

Her cited papers examine reconstructive operations for urinary dysfunction, including fascia lata sling cystourethropexy, ileovesicostomy, and simple cystectomy. She also coauthored an SIU/ICUD consultation on urethral stricture terminology, anatomy, and etiology.[^3][^4][^5][^6]
