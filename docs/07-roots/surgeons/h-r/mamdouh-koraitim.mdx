## Contributions

Koraitim's cited papers repeatedly address pelvic fracture urethral injury, including preoperative assessment, posterior urethroplasty approaches, and erectile dysfunction after injury.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]

- **145-case posterior urethral stricture series** (1995). Reported treatment selection and outcomes for 145 post-traumatic posterior urethral strictures and disruptions over 17 years.[^7] Cited on WARWIKI: [Male Urethroplasty — Incisions & Approaches](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/male-urethroplasty-incisions-approaches).
- **Comparison of initial pelvic fracture urethral injury management** (1996). Compared initial management approaches in 100 male pelvic fracture urethral injuries and reviewed outcomes reported for 771 additional patients.[^8]
- **Preoperative decision-making for posterior urethroplasty** (2004). Analyzed imaging and endoscopy findings from 167 post-traumatic posterior urethral defects to guide operative selection.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Core-Through Urethrotomy](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/core-through-urethrotomy).
