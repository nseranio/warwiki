## Biography

Catherine Harris is an affiliated clinical associate professor of urology at Stanford University and directs genitourinary trauma and reconstruction at Santa Clara Valley Medical Center.[^1][^2] Harris completed urology residency at the University of California, San Francisco, in 2014.[^3] Harris completed UCSF's male genitourinary reconstruction and trauma fellowship in 2014-2015 while Benjamin N. Breyer directed the program.[^4][^2] Harris is listed as director of genitourinary trauma and reconstruction at Santa Clara Valley Medical Center.[^2]

## Contributions

Harris's cited reconstructive work covers split-thickness skin grafting for male genital reconstruction, rectourethral fistula repair after prostate cancer treatment, and urethral stricture scoring in anterior urethroplasty.[^5][^6][^7]
