## Key Publications

Nager's cited work examines urodynamic testing and incontinence severity before stress incontinence surgery. It also compares sacrospinous hysteropexy with vaginal hysterectomy for uterovaginal prolapse, including five- and ten-year outcomes and factors associated with surgical failure.<sup>[P1](#publication-9)</sup><sup>[P3](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-8)</sup>
