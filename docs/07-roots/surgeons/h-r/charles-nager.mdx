## Known for

- **VALUE trial of preoperative urodynamic testing** (2012). Led a randomized trial finding office evaluation alone noninferior to added urodynamics before surgery for uncomplicated, demonstrable stress incontinence.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Stress Urinary Incontinence (Female)](/docs/clinical-conditions/03a-storage-incontinence/sui-female).
- **SUPeR randomized trial of mesh hysteropexy versus hysterectomy** (2019). Led a multicenter trial comparing vaginal mesh hysteropexy with hysterectomy and uterosacral suspension for uterovaginal prolapse.[^10]

## Contributions

Nager's cited work examines urodynamic testing and incontinence severity before stress incontinence surgery. It also compares sacrospinous hysteropexy with vaginal hysterectomy for uterovaginal prolapse, including five- and ten-year outcomes and factors associated with surgical failure.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
