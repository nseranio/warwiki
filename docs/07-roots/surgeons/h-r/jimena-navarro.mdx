## Biography

Jimena Navarro is a urologist and researcher at UROKUL in Pune, India.[^1] Navarro completed a 2023–2024 reconstructive urology fellowship at the Kulkarni Urosurgery Institute, with Sanjay Kulkarni as program director.[^1][^2]

## Contributions

Navarro's cited work covers urethral reconstruction, including bulbar urethral necrosis, pubectomy for pelvic fracture urethral injury, and gracilis flap repair of rectourethral fistula. Navarro's cited chapters also address surgical ergonomics and penile prosthesis insertion after urethroplasty.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
