## Contributions

Hudak served on the American Urological Association Urotrauma Guideline panel (2011–2015) and coauthored the 2014 guideline; the AUA lists him on the panel for the 2017 and 2020 amendments.[^3][^4] He also coauthored the US military Joint Trauma System clinical practice guideline on urologic trauma management (2017, revised 2024).[^2]

- **Penile plication without degloving for Peyronie's disease** (2013). Led a review of 154 men treated through a 2 cm longitudinal penile incision regardless of curvature severity; of 132 who answered the questionnaire, 96% reported improved curvature and 93% reported erections adequate for intercourse.[^5] Cited on WARWIKI: [Tunica Plication](/docs/surgical-techniques/04j-sexual-dysfunction/peyronies-disease/tunica-plication), [Peyronie's Disease (Treatment Algorithm)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04j-sexual-dysfunction/peyronies).
- **Reconstruction of failed urethroplasty** (2014). Lead author, with Allen Morey, of the textbook chapter on redo urethral reconstruction.[^6] Cited on WARWIKI: [Male Urethral Stricture](/docs/clinical-conditions/03b-voiding-outlet/urethral-stricture), [Pelvic Fracture Urethral Injury (PFUI)](/docs/special-populations/05a-trauma-emergencies/pfui).
