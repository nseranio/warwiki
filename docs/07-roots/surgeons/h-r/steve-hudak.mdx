## Biography

Steven J. Hudak is a professor of urology at UT Southwestern Medical Center.[^1] He earned his medical degree at the University of Iowa Carver College of Medicine and completed urology residency at the San Antonio Uniformed Services Health Education Consortium.[^1][^2] He completed a reconstructive urology fellowship at UT Southwestern Medical Center in 2012.[^2] He directs the UT Southwestern urology residency and genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship.[^3]

## Contributions

Hudak's cited work on urethral stricture addresses reconstruction after failed urethroplasty, the 7-flap perineal urethrostomy, and quality of life during intermittent self-dilation.[^4][^5][^6]
