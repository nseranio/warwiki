## Training and career

<div className="sp-facts">

- **Current position** Professor of urology, UT Southwestern Medical Center; Distinguished Chair in Urology for Urologic Reconstruction, in Honor of Allen F. Morey, M.D. (2023)[^1][^2]
- **Program director** UT Southwestern urology residency (since 2021) and Urologic Trauma, Reconstruction, and Prosthetics Fellowship (since 2023)[^2]
- **Medical school** University of Iowa Carver College of Medicine (2004)[^2]
- **Residency** Urology, San Antonio Uniformed Services Health Education Consortium (2010)[^2]
- **Fellowship** Urologic trauma, reconstruction, and prosthetics, UT Southwestern Medical Center (2010–2012)[^2]
- **Military service** US Army, 15 years of active duty, retiring as lieutenant colonel; attending urologist at Brooke Army Medical Center (2012–2019) and deputy chief of its urology section (2017–2019)[^1][^2]
- **Leadership** Board of Directors, Society of Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgeons (2018–2022); chair, AUA Young Urologists Committee (2019–2020)[^2]
- **Fellows trained** Fellowship mentor to 11 UT Southwestern reconstructive urology fellows since 2019[^2]

## Key Publications

Hudak served on the American Urological Association Urotrauma Guideline panel (2011–2015) and coauthored the 2014 guideline; the AUA lists him on the panel for the 2017 and 2020 amendments.[^3]<sup>[P3](#publication-4)</sup> He also coauthored the US military Joint Trauma System clinical practice guideline on urologic trauma management (2017, revised 2024).[^2]
