## Known for

- **Longitudinal natural history of prolapse** (2004). Handa measured incidence, progression and remission of pelvic organ prolapse during repeated examinations of menopausal women.[^7]
- **Mothers' Outcomes After Delivery cohort** (2011). Handa co-led the MOAD cohort and reported associations between delivery mode and pelvic floor disorders five to ten years after childbirth.[^8]
- **Long-term delivery-mode and pelvic-floor outcomes** (2018). A MOAD analysis associated cesarean delivery with lower incidence of several pelvic floor disorders and operative vaginal delivery with higher prolapse incidence.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Perineorrhaphy](/docs/surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/perineorrhaphy).

## Contributions

Her cited work examines pelvic floor disorders after childbirth, posterior prolapse and defecatory symptoms, sexual function, and prolapse repair. Two cited studies address vaginal complications after cystectomy.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
