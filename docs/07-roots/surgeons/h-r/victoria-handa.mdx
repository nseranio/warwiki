## Key Publications

Her cited work examines pelvic floor disorders after childbirth, posterior prolapse and defecatory symptoms, sexual function, and prolapse repair. Two cited studies address vaginal complications after cystectomy.<sup>[P3](#publication-9)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-3)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-6)</sup>

</KeyPublication>
