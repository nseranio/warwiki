## Biography

Victoria L. Handa is professor of gynecology and obstetrics at Johns Hopkins and directs the department at Johns Hopkins Bayview Medical Center.[^1] She earned her medical degree at the University of Pennsylvania School of Medicine and completed an obstetrics and gynecology residency at the University of California, San Francisco.[^1] She completed a urogynecology fellowship at the University of California, Irvine, and directs the Women's Center for Pelvic Health and Reconstructive Surgery.[^1]

## Contributions

Her cited work examines pelvic floor disorders after childbirth, posterior prolapse and defecatory symptoms, sexual function, and prolapse repair. Two cited studies address vaginal complications after cystectomy.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
