## Biography

Rajveer S. Purohit is Director of Reconstructive Urology and Professor of Urology at The Mount Sinai Hospital.[^1] He earned his medical degree at Columbia University and completed his urology residency at the University of California, San Francisco.[^1] He completed a fellowship in pelvic reconstruction and voiding dysfunction with Jerry Blaivas at the Urocenter of New York.[^2] He co-directs Mount Sinai's reconstructive urology fellowship, which is certified by the Society of Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgeons.[^1]

## Contributions

Purohit's confirmed citations cover female urethral reconstruction, including MRI assessment of urethral diverticula, and complications of gender-affirming vaginoplasty, including vaginal stenosis and rectal injury.[^3][^4][^5][^6]
