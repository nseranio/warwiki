## Biography

Roger K. Khouri Jr. is a reconstructive urologist at Northwell Health and an assistant professor of urology at the Zucker School of Medicine at Hofstra.[^1] He earned his medical degree at the University of Michigan and completed his urology residency at the University of Texas Southwestern Medical Center.[^2][^3] He completed a fellowship in genitourinary reconstructive surgery and men's health at Cleveland Clinic under Kenneth Angermeier.[^2][^4]

## Contributions

Khouri's confirmed papers address male stress urinary incontinence through severity assessment, comparison of artificial urinary sphincters with slings, and pressure-regulating balloon herniation. A separate confirmed paper covers posterior urethral reconstruction during rectourethral fistula repair.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]
