## Biography

Andrew C. Peterson is a professor of urology and vice chair for education at Duke University.[^1] Peterson received his medical degree from Dartmouth Medical School, completed urology residency at Madigan Army Medical Center in 2002, and completed a reconstructive urology fellowship at Duke in 2003.[^2] Peterson directs Duke's urology residency program and its Reconstructive Urology and Genitourinary Cancer Survivorship Fellowship.[^2]

## Contributions

Peterson's cited work covers urethral stricture and lichen sclerosus management, artificial urinary sphincter revision, and reconstruction after prostate cancer treatment and radiation.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
