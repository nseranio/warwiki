## Contributions

Peterson's cited work covers urethral stricture reconstruction and lichen sclerosus treatment, artificial urinary sphincter revision, and complications of prostate cancer treatment, including pubic symphysis osteomyelitis and the irradiated bladder.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Pubic-symphysis osteomyelitis management series** (2015). Senior-authored a case series describing pubic-symphysis osteomyelitis after prostate-cancer treatment and proposing a management algorithm.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Urethropubic Fistula](/docs/clinical-conditions/03f-fistulas/in-males/urethropubic).
- **Pubic-bone resection for urosymphyseal fistula** (2017). Senior-authored a surgical series assessing pain after pubic-bone resection for urinary fistula with associated osteomyelitis.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Urethropubic Fistula](/docs/clinical-conditions/03f-fistulas/in-males/urethropubic).
