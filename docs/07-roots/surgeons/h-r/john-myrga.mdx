## Biography

John M. Myrga is a faculty member at the University of Pittsburgh after completing a 2025–26 fellowship in male genitourinary reconstruction and trauma at UCSF.[^1] John Myrga, MD, attended the University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine and completed a residency at the University of Pittsburgh Medical Center.[^2] Myrga's UCSF fellowship was directed by Benjamin N. Breyer.[^3][^1]

## Contributions

Myrga's cited papers focus on adult acquired buried penis repair, including surgical outcomes, W-plasty, suprapubic fascial reconstruction, and lichen sclerosus in repair patients.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
