## Contributions

Mulhall's cited work covers Peyronie's disease, including natural history, deformity assessment, injections, grafting outcomes, and implant-associated curvature correction. Other citations examine erectile dysfunction assessment and treatment through penile Doppler ultrasound, cavernosometry, intracavernosal injection, prosthesis surgery, and erection-hardness measurement. Mulhall coauthored the AUA guideline on evaluation and management of testosterone deficiency.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]
