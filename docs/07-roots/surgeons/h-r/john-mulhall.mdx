## Key Publications

Mulhall's cited work covers Peyronie's disease, including natural history, deformity assessment, injections, grafting outcomes, and implant-associated curvature correction. Other citations examine erectile dysfunction assessment and treatment through penile Doppler ultrasound, cavernosometry, intracavernosal injection, prosthesis surgery, and erection-hardness measurement. Mulhall coauthored the AUA guideline on evaluation and management of testosterone deficiency.<sup>[P4](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-8)</sup><sup>[P8](#publication-9)</sup>
