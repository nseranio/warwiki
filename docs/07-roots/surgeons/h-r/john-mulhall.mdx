## Biography

John P. Mulhall is a urologic surgeon and director of the Male Sexual and Reproductive Medicine Program at Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center.[^1][^2] Mulhall earned his MD from University College Dublin Medical School and completed a urology residency at the University of Connecticut Health Center.[^1][^2] Mulhall completed a sexual and reproductive medicine and surgery fellowship at Boston University Medical Center in 1996.[^3] Mulhall served as president of the Sexual Medicine Society of North America in 2011.[^4]

## Contributions

Mulhall's cited work covers Peyronie's disease, including natural history, deformity assessment, injections, grafting outcomes, and implant-associated curvature correction. Other citations examine erectile dysfunction assessment and treatment through penile Doppler ultrasound, cavernosometry, intracavernosal injection, prosthesis surgery, and erection-hardness measurement. Mulhall coauthored the AUA guideline on evaluation and management of testosterone deficiency.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]
