## Biography

Travis J. Pagliara practices urologic surgery at Hennepin Healthcare in Minneapolis.[^1] Pagliara attended the University of Tennessee Health Science Center and completed a urology residency at the University of Minnesota.[^1] Pagliara completed a fellowship in reconstruction, prosthetics, and trauma urology at UT Southwestern Medical Center.[^1]

## Contributions

Pagliara's cited work on urologic prosthetics covers difficult inflatable penile prosthesis inflation and alternative balloon or reservoir placement, including a high submuscular technique.[^2][^3][^4]
