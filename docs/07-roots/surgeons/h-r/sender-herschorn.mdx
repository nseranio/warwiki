## Key Publications

Herschorn's cited work covers urinary incontinence treatment, including onabotulinumtoxinA trials, urethral bulking agents for female stress incontinence, and male slings after prostatectomy. Other cited papers address augmentation cystoplasty, Hemi-Kock continent stomas, and vaginal fistula repair.<sup>[P4](#publication-2)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-3)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P8](#publication-6)</sup>
