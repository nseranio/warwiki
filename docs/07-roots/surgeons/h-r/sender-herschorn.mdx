## Contributions

Herschorn's cited work covers urinary incontinence treatment, including onabotulinumtoxinA trials, urethral bulking agents for female stress incontinence, and male slings after prostatectomy. Other cited papers address augmentation cystoplasty, Hemi-Kock continent stomas, and vaginal fistula repair.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]

- **Fascial sling and bladder-neck tapering for male neurogenic incontinence** (1992). Reported outcomes of fascial slings with bladder-neck tapering in men with neurogenic incontinence undergoing bladder augmentation.[^7]
- **Botulinum toxin trial for neurogenic detrusor overactivity** (2011). Led a randomized Canadian trial of intradetrusor onabotulinumtoxinA for antimuscarinic-refractory neurogenic incontinence.[^8]
- **Botulinum toxin versus solifenacin trial for refractory overactive bladder** (2017). Led a randomized comparison of onabotulinumtoxinA, solifenacin and placebo for refractory overactive bladder with incontinence.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Botulinum Toxin (OnabotulinumtoxinA &amp; Variants)](/docs/foundations/pharmacology/storage-oab/botulinum-toxin).
