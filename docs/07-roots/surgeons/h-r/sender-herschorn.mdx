## Training and career

<div className="sp-facts">

- **Current position** Professor, University of Toronto Division of Urology; attending urologist, Sunnybrook Health Sciences Centre[^1]
- **Medical school** McGill University Medical School (graduated 1972)[^1]
- **Residency** Urology training, University of Toronto (completed 1978)[^1]
- **Fellowship** Clinical fellowships in urodynamics, incontinence, and reconstruction, Institute of Urology, London, and Baylor College of Medicine, Houston (1979)[^1]

## Contributions

Herschorn's cited work covers urinary incontinence treatment, including onabotulinumtoxinA trials, urethral bulking agents for female stress incontinence, and male slings after prostatectomy. Other cited papers address augmentation cystoplasty, Hemi-Kock continent stomas, and vaginal fistula repair.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
