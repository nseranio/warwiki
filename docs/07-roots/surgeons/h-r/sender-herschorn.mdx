## Biography

Sender Herschorn is a professor in the University of Toronto Division of Urology and an attending urologist at Sunnybrook Health Sciences Centre.[^1] He graduated from McGill University Medical School in 1972 and completed urology training at the University of Toronto in 1978.[^1] He completed clinical fellowships in urodynamics, incontinence, and reconstruction in 1979 at the Institute of Urology in London and Baylor College of Medicine in Houston.[^1] He is a former president of the Canadian Urological Association and a former general secretary of the International Continence Society.[^1]

## Contributions

Herschorn's cited work covers urinary incontinence treatment, including onabotulinumtoxinA trials, urethral bulking agents for female stress incontinence, and male slings after prostatectomy. Other cited papers address augmentation cystoplasty, Hemi-Kock continent stomas, and vaginal fistula repair.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
