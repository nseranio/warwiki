## Contributions

Rourke's cited work centers on urethroplasty for anterior and radiation related strictures, including anastomotic and graft techniques, revision surgery, and long term outcomes.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

- **Cost analysis of primary urethroplasty** (2005). Modeled costs of primary urethroplasty versus direct-vision internal urethrotomy for short bulbar urethral stricture.[^9]
- **Canadian male urethral stricture guideline** (2020). Led authorship of Canadian guidance on diagnosing male urethral stricture and choosing endoscopic treatment or urethroplasty.[^10]
- **Prospective patient-reported urethroplasty outcomes** (2020). Reported prospective patient assessments of voiding and other outcomes after urethroplasty.[^11]
