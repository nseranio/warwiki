## Biography

Scott A. MacDiarmid practices in the Alliance Division of Urology Specialists of the Carolinas in Greensboro, North Carolina.[^1] MacDiarmid is chief medical officer of Bright Uro.[^2] MacDiarmid received a medical degree and completed a urology residency at Dalhousie University in Halifax, Nova Scotia.[^3] MacDiarmid completed reconstructive urology and urodynamics fellowships at Duke University and the University of Otago, as well as reconstructive urology and neuro-urology fellowships at the University of Sheffield.[^3]

## Contributions

MacDiarmid's cited work covers treatments for overactive bladder and urgency urinary incontinence, including tibial nerve stimulation and onabotulinumtoxinA. An earlier cited paper addresses mesh graft urethroplasty for complex anterior urethral stricture.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
