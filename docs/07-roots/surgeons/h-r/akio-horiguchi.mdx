## Contributions

Horiguchi's cited work covers urethroplasty for male and female urethral strictures using oral mucosal grafts, vaginal flaps, staged repair, and anastomosis. Other cited papers address pelvic fracture urethral injury and the use of magnetic resonance imaging to plan repair.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Delayed urethroplasty series for pelvic-fracture urethral injury** (2022). Reported surgical and patient-reported outcomes for 211 men undergoing delayed anastomotic urethroplasty after pelvic-fracture urethral injury.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Pelvic Fracture Urethral Injury (PFUI)](/docs/special-populations/05a-trauma-emergencies/pfui).
- **Japanese urethral-stricture guideline clinical questions** (2024). First-authored the clinical-question paper accompanying Japanese Urological Association guidance on urethral stricture diagnosis and treatment.[^9]
