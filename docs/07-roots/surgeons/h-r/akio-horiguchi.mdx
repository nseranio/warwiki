## Biography

Akio Horiguchi is professor and director of the Division of Reconstruction at the Center for Trauma, Burn, and Tactical Medicine, National Defense Medical College Hospital in Japan.[^1] Horiguchi graduated from Keio University School of Medicine and subsequently trained in surgery and urology there.[^2] Horiguchi was a urology research fellow at Cornell University.[^2] Horiguchi directs a Society of Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgeons Global Fellowship program.[^2]

## Contributions

Horiguchi's cited work covers urethroplasty for male and female urethral strictures using oral mucosal grafts, vaginal flaps, staged repair, and anastomosis. Other cited papers address pelvic fracture urethral injury and the use of magnetic resonance imaging to plan repair.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
