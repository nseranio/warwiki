## Contributions

Lue's cited work examines penile erection anatomy and vascular physiology, erectile dysfunction treatment, and vascular surgery. His cited Peyronie's disease work describes venous patch grafting, tunical plication, and extra-tunical grafting.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **H-incision with venous graft** (1998). Lue described tunical plaque incision, often H-shaped, with a venous patch graft for complex Peyronie-related deformity.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Plaque Incision / Excision with Grafting](/docs/surgical-techniques/04j-sexual-dysfunction/peyronies-disease/plaque-incision-grafting).
- **16-dot penile plication** (2002). Lue and Gholami reported a multiple-plication technique for correcting congenital and Peyronie-related penile curvature in 132 patients.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Tunica Plication for Peyronie's Disease](/docs/surgical-techniques/04j-sexual-dysfunction/peyronies-disease/tunica-plication).
- **T-shaped shunt for prolonged ischemic priapism** (2009). Lue and colleagues reported a distal T-shaped shunt with intracavernous tunneling for prolonged ischemic priapism.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Priapism Shunts & Decompression](/docs/surgical-techniques/04j-sexual-dysfunction/priapism-shunts-decompression).
- **Extra-tunical grafting for Peyronie deformity** (2018). Lue and colleagues described graft placement outside the tunica for penile hourglass and indentation deformities.[^11] Cited on WARWIKI: [Tunica Plication for Peyronie's Disease](/docs/surgical-techniques/04j-sexual-dysfunction/peyronies-disease/tunica-plication).
