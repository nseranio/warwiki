## Key Publications

Lue's cited work examines penile erection anatomy and vascular physiology, erectile dysfunction treatment, and vascular surgery. His cited Peyronie's disease work describes venous patch grafting, tunical plication, and extra-tunical grafting.<sup>[P5](#publication-3)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P8](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P9](#publication-7)</sup>

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**H-incision with venous graft** (1998). Lue described tunical plaque incision, often H-shaped, with a venous patch graft for complex Peyronie-related deformity.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**16-dot penile plication** (2002). Lue and Gholami reported a multiple-plication technique for correcting congenital and Peyronie-related penile curvature in 132 patients.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[2]} citations={citations}>

**T-shaped shunt for prolonged ischemic priapism** (2009). Lue and colleagues reported a distal T-shaped shunt with intracavernous tunneling for prolonged ischemic priapism.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[3]} citations={citations}>

**Extra-tunical grafting for Peyronie deformity** (2018). Lue and colleagues described graft placement outside the tunica for penile hourglass and indentation deformities.

</KeyPublication>
