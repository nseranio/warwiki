## Biography

Cheryl B. Iglesia directs female pelvic medicine and reconstructive surgery at MedStar Washington Hospital Center and is a professor of obstetrics and gynecology and urology at Georgetown University School of Medicine.[^1] Iglesia earned her medical degree at the University of Maryland School of Medicine and completed residency at the University of Florida College of Medicine in Jacksonville.[^1] Iglesia completed a urogynecology and reconstructive pelvic surgery fellowship at Rush-Presbyterian St. Luke's Medical Center under Linda Brubaker, followed by pelvic floor clinical neurophysiology training at Methodist Hospital and Indiana University under Thomas Benson.[^2][^3] Iglesia served as president of the Society of Gynecologic Surgeons in 2022–2023.[^4]
