## Biography

Cheryl B. Iglesia directs the Female Pelvic Medicine and Reconstructive Surgery section at MedStar Washington Hospital Center and is a professor of obstetrics, gynecology, and urology at Georgetown University School of Medicine.[^1][^2] She earned her medical degree at the University of Maryland School of Medicine and completed residency at the University of Florida College of Medicine in Jacksonville.[^3][^1] Iglesia completed a fellowship in female pelvic medicine and reconstructive surgery at Rush-Presbyterian St. Luke's Medical Center, followed by training in pelvic floor clinical neurophysiology at Methodist Hospital and Indiana University.[^4] She has acknowledged Linda Brubaker as a surgical mentor.[^5]
