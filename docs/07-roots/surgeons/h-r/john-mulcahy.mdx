## Contributions

Mulcahy's cited work covers penile prosthesis infection prevention and salvage, implantation approaches, and repair of noninfectious device complications, including distal cylinder extrusion.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Double-cuff artificial urinary sphincter** (1993). Reported adding a second urethral cuff for persistent severe stress incontinence after artificial urinary sphincter placement.[^8]
- **Immediate salvage of infected penile prostheses** (1996). Described device removal, washout, and immediate replacement during the same operation for selected penile prosthesis infections.[^9]
- **Distal corporoplasty for penile prosthesis cylinder extrusion** (1999). Described creating a new corporal cavity to reposition a laterally extruding penile prosthesis cylinder.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Non-Infectious Complications of Penile Prosthesis Surgery](/docs/surgical-techniques/04j-sexual-dysfunction/penile-implants/complications).
