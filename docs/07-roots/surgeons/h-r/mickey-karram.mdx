## Contributions

Karram's cited work covers pelvic organ prolapse and stress urinary incontinence surgery, including native tissue repairs, slings, urethral bulking, and mesh complications. His cited chapters and case reports also address urethral and vaginal reconstruction and fistula repair.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]
