## Biography

Rudolf Hohenfellner held the urology chair at Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz and directed its urology clinic until 1996.[^1][^2] Hohenfellner was appointed to the newly created chair of urology at Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz.[^1] Hohenfellner served as president of the Société Internationale d'Urologie from 1994 to 1997.[^1]

## Contributions

Hohenfellner's cited papers describe continent urinary diversion with Mainz pouch I and rectosigmoid Mainz pouch II, including outcomes after pelvic irradiation and follow-up of metabolic effects.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
