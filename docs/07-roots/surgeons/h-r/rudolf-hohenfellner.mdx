## Contributions

Hohenfellner's cited papers describe continent urinary diversion with Mainz pouch I and rectosigmoid Mainz pouch II, including outcomes after pelvic irradiation and follow-up of metabolic effects.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Psoas-hitch ureteroneocystostomy series** (1984). Senior-authored a 181-patient report detailing indications, operative principles and results of psoas-hitch ureteral reimplantation.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Ureteral Reimplantation](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/ureteral-reimplantation).
- **Mainz pouch I** (1985). Co-authored the original description of an ileocecal pouch for bladder augmentation and continent urinary diversion.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Mainz Pouch I](/docs/surgical-techniques/04c-urinary-diversion/mainz-pouch-i).
- **Mainz pouch II** (1993). Co-reported a detubularized rectosigmoid reservoir for continent urinary diversion.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Ureterosigmoidostomy and Rectosigmoid Diversion](/docs/surgical-techniques/04c-urinary-diversion/ureterosigmoidostomy).
