## Contributions

Martins's confirmed publications cover urethral stricture evaluation and urethroplasty, including post-radiation stenosis and functional outcomes after bulbar repair. They also address rectourethral fistulas and bladder outlet damage after pelvic cancer treatment.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
