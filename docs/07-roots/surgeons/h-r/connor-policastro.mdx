## Biography

Connor G. Policastro, MD, is section head of reconstructive urology at Ochsner Health.[^1] He attended medical school and completed his urology residency at SUNY Upstate Medical University.[^1] He completed a genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship at Atrium Health Wake Forest Baptist under Ryan Terlecki.[^1][^2]

## Contributions

His cited studies examine dorsal onlay buccal mucosa graft urethroplasty for posterior urethral stenosis after endoscopic prostate procedures or radiotherapy.[^3][^4]
