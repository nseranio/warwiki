## Biography

Ty T. Higuchi, MD, PhD, is an associate professor in the Division of Urology at the University of Colorado School of Medicine.[^1] He earned his medical degree at the University of Nebraska College of Medicine.[^2] He completed urology residency at Mayo Clinic in Rochester, where he was chief resident.[^2] He completed a urology fellowship at Cleveland Clinic in 2012.[^2]

## Contributions

His cited work examines bladder cancer risk and surveillance after augmentation cystoplasty for congenital bladder anomalies.[^3][^4]
