## Contributions

His cited work includes national forecasts of pelvic floor disorder prevalence and surgical volume written with Jennifer Wu, and perioperative outcome studies and trials from the Ohio State division.[^3][^4]

- **Forecasting pelvic floor disorders in US women** (2009). Coauthored a projection from census data and NHANES prevalence estimates in which the number of US women with at least one pelvic floor disorder rises from 28.1 million in 2010 to 43.8 million in 2050.[^3] Cited on WARWIKI: [POP Overview](/docs/clinical-conditions/03c-pelvic-support/pelvic-organ-prolapse), [Clinical Operator Mindset](/docs/resources/hidden-curriculum/overview).
- **Preoperative laboratory testing before midurethral sling** (2019). Senior author of a NSQIP analysis of 12,368 women in which 74.2% had preoperative serum tests and the odds of 30-day complications did not differ with or without testing (adjusted OR 0.98).[^5]
- **Continence pessary versus disposable continence device** (2022). Coauthored a randomized trial, stopped early at 50 participants, in which 80% of women using a continence pessary and 75% using a disposable intravaginal device reported treatment success at 4 weeks.[^6] Cited on WARWIKI: [Poise Impressa](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/poise-impressa).
