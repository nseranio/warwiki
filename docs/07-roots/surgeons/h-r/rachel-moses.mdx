## Biography

Rachel A. Moses practices urology at Dartmouth Hitchcock Medical Center and is an associate professor of surgery and of The Dartmouth Institute at Geisel School of Medicine.[^1] Moses earned an MD at the University of Kansas School of Medicine and completed urology residency at Dartmouth Hitchcock Medical Center.[^1] Moses completed a reconstructive urology fellowship at the University of Utah in 2018, in the program directed by Jeremy B. Myers.[^1][^2]

[^1]: [Rachel A. Moses, MD, MPH. Dartmouth Health](https://providers.dartmouth-health.org/662/rachel-a-moses). Accessed October 2026.

[^2]: [Reconstructive Urology Fellowship. U of U School of Medicine](https://medicine.utah.edu/surgery/urology/education/fellowships/reconstructive). Accessed October 2026.
