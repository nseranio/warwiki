## Contributions

Kuo's cited papers examine botulinum toxin delivery for overactive bladder, interstitial cystitis or bladder pain syndrome, and urethral sphincter dysfunction. Other cited work addresses urodynamic assessment and treatment of dysfunctional voiding.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Augmentation cystoplasty series for ketamine cystitis** (2014). Coauthored an early 14-patient series reporting reduced pain and increased bladder capacity after augmentation enterocystoplasty for refractory ketamine cystitis.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Ketamine Cystitis](/docs/clinical-conditions/03h-pelvic-pain/ketamine-cystitis).
- **Botulinum toxin trial for interstitial cystitis/bladder pain syndrome** (2016). Led a randomized trial in which bladder botulinum toxin injections plus hydrodistention reduced pain more than saline injections plus hydrodistention.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Botulinum Toxin (OnabotulinumtoxinA &amp; Variants)](/docs/foundations/pharmacology/storage-oab/botulinum-toxin).
