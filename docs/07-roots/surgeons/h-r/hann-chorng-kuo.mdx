## Biography

Hann-Chorng Kuo directs the Department of Urology at Hualien Tzu Chi Hospital, serves as vice chief executive officer of the Buddhist Tzu Chi Medical Foundation, and is a professor of urology at Tzu Chi University College of Medicine.[^1] Kuo graduated from National Taiwan University College of Medicine and trained in urology at National Taiwan University Hospital.[^1] Kuo is editor in chief of the Tzu Chi Medical Journal and previously served as president of the Taiwan Continence Society and the Taiwan Urological Association.[^1][^2]

## Contributions

Kuo's cited papers examine botulinum toxin delivery for overactive bladder, interstitial cystitis or bladder pain syndrome, and urethral sphincter dysfunction. Other cited work addresses urodynamic assessment and treatment of dysfunctional voiding.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
