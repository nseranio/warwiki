## Biography

Victor W. Nitti, MD, is a professor of urology and obstetrics and gynecology at UCLA, where he is chief of urogynecology and reconstructive pelvic surgery and fellowship director.[^1] He earned his medical degree at Rutgers New Jersey Medical School and completed his urology residency at SUNY Downstate.[^2] He completed a fellowship in female urology, neurourology, and reconstructive urology at UCLA.[^3] He has identified Shlomo Raz as a mentor.[^4]

## Contributions

His cited work includes randomized and long-term studies of onabotulinumtoxinA for overactive bladder and a review covering neurogenic detrusor overactivity.[^5][^6][^7]
