## Key Publications

His cited work includes randomized and long-term studies of onabotulinumtoxinA for overactive bladder and a review covering neurogenic detrusor overactivity.<sup>[P2](#publication-10)</sup><sup>[P3](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-7)</sup>

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Video-urodynamic criteria for female bladder outlet obstruction** (1999). Nitti proposed diagnosing female outlet obstruction through radiographic narrowing during a sustained detrusor contraction, without a fixed pressure-flow cutoff.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**EMBARK phase 3 overactive-bladder trial** (2013). Nitti led a placebo-controlled phase 3 trial of intradetrusor onabotulinumtoxinA for overactive bladder with urinary incontinence.
