## Contributions

His cited work includes randomized and long-term studies of onabotulinumtoxinA for overactive bladder and a review covering neurogenic detrusor overactivity.[^5][^6][^7]

- **Practical Urodynamics** (1998). Nitti edited a clinical textbook on performing and interpreting urodynamic studies.[^8]
- **Video-urodynamic criteria for female bladder outlet obstruction** (1999). Nitti proposed diagnosing female outlet obstruction through radiographic narrowing during a sustained detrusor contraction, without a fixed pressure-flow cutoff.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Primary Bladder Neck Obstruction](/docs/clinical-conditions/03b-voiding-outlet/primary-bladder-neck-obstruction).
- **EMBARK phase 3 overactive-bladder trial** (2013). Nitti led a placebo-controlled phase 3 trial of intradetrusor onabotulinumtoxinA for overactive bladder with urinary incontinence.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Intradetrusor OnabotulinumtoxinA](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/intradetrusor-botox).
