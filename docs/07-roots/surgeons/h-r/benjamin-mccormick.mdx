## Biography

Benjamin J. McCormick is a clinical assistant professor of surgery and a urologic surgeon at the University of Utah.[^1] He attended Vanderbilt University School of Medicine and completed his urology residency at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.[^1] He completed a reconstructive urology and gender affirming surgery fellowship at the University of Utah, where Jeremy Myers directs the fellowship program.[^1][^2]

## Contributions

McCormick's cited work examines urinary diversion after pelvic radiation injury and management of genitourinary trauma, including pelvic fracture urethral injury and high-grade renal trauma.[^3][^4][^5][^6]
