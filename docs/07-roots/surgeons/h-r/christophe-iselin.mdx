## Biography

Christophe E. Iselin is an honorary professor at the University of Geneva and practices urology in Geneva with Hirslanden Clinique des Grangettes.[^1] He earned his medical degree at the University of Geneva in 1984 and trained in surgery and urology at Geneva University Hospitals.[^2] He pursued advanced training in surgical uro-oncology, reconstructive urology, and urodynamics at Duke University from 1996 to 1998.[^1] He served as president of the Swiss Urological Society from 2013 to 2014.[^3]
