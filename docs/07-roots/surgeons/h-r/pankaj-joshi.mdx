## Contributions

Joshi's cited work addresses urethral reconstruction, including mucosal-sparing augmented non-transected anastomosis and spiral preputial graft repair of panurethral stricture. Other confirmed papers cover bulbar urethral necrosis and pelvic fracture urethral injury.[^8][^9][^10][^11][^12]
