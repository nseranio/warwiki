## Key Publications

Joshi's cited work addresses urethral reconstruction, including mucosal-sparing augmented non-transected anastomosis and spiral preputial graft repair of panurethral stricture. Other confirmed papers cover bulbar urethral necrosis and pelvic fracture urethral injury.<sup>[P3](#publication-15)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-9)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-10)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-11)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-12)</sup>
