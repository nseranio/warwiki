## Contributions

Joshi's cited work addresses urethral reconstruction, including mucosal-sparing augmented non-transected anastomosis and spiral preputial graft repair of panurethral stricture. Other confirmed papers cover bulbar urethral necrosis and pelvic fracture urethral injury.[^8][^9][^10][^11][^12]

- **Double-face buccal graft female urethroplasty** (2020). Described dorsal and ventral inlay buccal grafts for female urethral stricture reconstruction.[^13]
- **Three-dimensional pelvic fracture urethral injury models** (2020). Reported CT-derived three-dimensional models for planning urethroplasty after pelvic fracture urethral injury.[^14]
- **MsANTA urethroplasty** (2022). Described mucosa-sparing augmentation without urethral transection for near-obliterative bulbar strictures.[^15] Cited on WARWIKI: [Principles of Urethral Reconstruction](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/urethral-reconstruction-principles).
