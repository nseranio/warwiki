## Biography

Stephen Dwight Mark is a urologist at Christchurch Hospital and a clinical lead for urology at Te Whatu Ora Wairarapa's Masterton Hospital.[^1][^2] Mark earned an MB ChB from the University of Otago in 1983 and completed urological training in 1992.[^2][^1] Mark undertook a reconstructive and urodynamic urology fellowship with George Webster at Duke University Medical Centre and a pediatric fellowship with David Frank at Bristol Children's Hospital.[^1] Mark is a clinical senior lecturer at the University of Otago, Christchurch, and a former president of the Urological Society of Australia and New Zealand.[^1]
