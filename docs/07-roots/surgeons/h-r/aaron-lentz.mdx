## Biography

Aaron Claude Lentz is a professor of urology at Duke University School of Medicine.[^1] Lentz earned an MD at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill and completed surgery and urologic surgery residencies there.[^1] Lentz completed a genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship at Duke University School of Medicine in 2010–2011.[^1] Lentz directs Duke's SMSNA Select Fellowship in Advanced Prosthetic Urology and Male Sexual Health.[^2]

## Contributions

Lentz's cited work addresses inflatable penile prosthesis design and reservoir placement and urethral injuries associated with penile prostheses.[^3][^4][^5]
