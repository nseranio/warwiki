## Biography

Oscar Contreras Ortiz participated in IUGA's founding meeting in Mexico City in 1976 and later organized its 1989, 1998, and 2003 annual scientific meetings in Argentina.[^1] He taught urogynecology in Argentina, elsewhere in South America, and in the United States.[^1]

With Donald Ostergard he founded the *International Urogynecology Journal* and served as one of its first editors.[^1] The journal was initially self-published before moving to a commercial publisher.[^1] Contreras Ortiz later donated his share of its ownership to IUGA.[^1]

Harry Vervest, writing in IUGA's anniversary book, remembered Contreras Ortiz's sustained interest in colleagues and their families alongside his work on the society and journal.[^1]
