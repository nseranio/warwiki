## Biography

Matthias D. Hofer is a board-certified urologist at Urology San Antonio's Men's Health Clinic.[^1] He attended medical school at the University of Ulm in Germany and completed a urology residency at Northwestern University in Chicago.[^1] He completed a fellowship in urologic reconstruction, trauma, and prosthetics at UT Southwestern in 2016, in a program directed by Allen F. Morey.[^1][^2]

## Contributions

His cited papers address urethral reconstruction for radiation-induced strictures, complex reoperative hypospadias, and distal strictures. Other cited work examines artificial urinary sphincter complications and ureteral reconstruction using downward nephropexy, buccal mucosa grafts, or appendiceal flaps.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
