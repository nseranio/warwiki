## Known for

- **Urinary incontinence in women** (2006). Coauthored a clinical review of evaluation and treatment of female urinary incontinence.[^7] Cited on WARWIKI: [Absorbent Products and the Pad-Weight Test](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/absorbent-products).
- **Familial pelvic floor disorder genetics** (2009). Co-led familial pelvic floor research reporting linkage to a locus on chromosome 9q21.[^8]

## Training and career
