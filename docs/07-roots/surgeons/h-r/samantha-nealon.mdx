## Biography

Samantha W. Nealon, MD, practices urology with Mid-Atlantic Kaiser Permanente Medical Group at its Largo Medical Center in Maryland.[^1][^2][^3] Nealon earned a medical degree at the University of South Carolina School of Medicine in Greenville and completed urology residency at the University of South Florida in Tampa.[^4][^2] Nealon completed a genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship at UT Southwestern Medical Center in 2022 in a program then directed by Allen F. Morey.[^3][^5]

## Contributions

Nealon's cited work covers endoscopic management of bladder neck contracture and urethral stricture, as well as penile prosthesis revision and implant surgery in corporal fibrosis.[^6][^7][^8][^9]
