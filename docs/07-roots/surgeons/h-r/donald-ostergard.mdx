## Biography

Donald R. Ostergard is emeritus faculty in obstetrics and gynecology at the University of California, Irvine.[^1] Ostergard earned his medical degree at the University of California, San Francisco.[^2] Ostergard was a resident at Harbor General Hospital in Torrance, California, during the mid-1960s.[^3] Ostergard served as secretary-treasurer when the Gynecologic Urology Society was established in 1979.[^3]
