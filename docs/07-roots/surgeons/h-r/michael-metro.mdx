## Biography

Michael J. Metro practices reconstructive urology with Jefferson Health in Philadelphia.[^1] Metro earned a medical degree at the University of Pittsburgh and completed urology residency at the Hospital of the University of Pennsylvania.[^1] Metro completed UC San Francisco's male genitourinary reconstruction and trauma fellowship in 2001–2002.[^1][^2] Metro is listed among Jefferson's teaching faculty for male reconstruction, urologic trauma, and male prosthetics.[^3]

## Contributions

Metro coauthored the SIU/ICUD consultation section on anterior urethral lichen sclerosus.[^4]
