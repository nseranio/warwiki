## Key Publications

Metro's cited work covers Peyronie's disease plication outcomes, robotic buccal mucosa graft ureteroplasty and the SIU/ICUD consultation on lichen sclerosus urethral stricture.<sup>[P1](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P2](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P3](#publication-8)</sup>

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Functional penile length after plication** (2023). Senior author of a series of 28 men with Peyronie's disease in which functional penile length, measured on the erect penis from pubis to glans tip, rose from 11.1 cm before plication to 12.5 cm after correction.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Robotic ureteroplasty with buccal mucosa graft** (2017). Coauthored the Temple series of 12 proximal or mid ureteral strictures not suited to excision and anastomosis; 10 (83%) were clinically and radiologically successful at a median follow-up of 13 months.

</KeyPublication>
