## Biography

Alexander T. Rozanski is a urologist at Houston Methodist Urology Associates.[^1][^2] He attended the University of Texas at Houston Medical School and completed urology residency at UT Southwestern Medical Center.[^2] He completed a genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship at Lahey Hospital and Medical Center in 2020, in the program directed by Alex J. Vanni.[^2][^3]

## Contributions

His cited studies address urethral and bladder outlet reconstruction, including internal urethrotomy with intralesional mitomycin C, buccal mucosa graft urethroplasty with a gracilis flap, perineal urethrostomy, and immediate urethral repair after artificial urinary sphincter cuff erosion.[^4][^5][^6][^7]
