## Known for

- **ATLAS stress incontinence treatment trial** (2010). Led a randomized comparison of continence pessary, behavioral therapy, and their combination for stress urinary incontinence.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Continence Pessary](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/continence-pessary).
- **ESTEEM mixed urinary incontinence trial** (2019). Coauthored a trial finding a small symptom difference with behavioral and pelvic floor therapy added to sling surgery.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Mixed Urinary Incontinence](/docs/clinical-conditions/03a-storage-incontinence/mixed-incontinence).
- **SUPeR uterovaginal prolapse trial** (2019). Coauthored a trial comparing vaginal mesh hysteropexy with vaginal hysterectomy and uterosacral suspension for uterovaginal prolapse.[^11]

## Contributions

Her cited trials compare treatments for female urinary incontinence, including midurethral slings, Burch colposuspension, fascial slings, onabotulinumtoxinA, and sacral neuromodulation. Her prolapse studies compare apical suspension and hysteropexy procedures and assess perioperative vaginal estrogen.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
