## Key Publications

Her cited trials compare treatments for female urinary incontinence, including midurethral slings, Burch colposuspension, fascial slings, onabotulinumtoxinA, and sacral neuromodulation. Her prolapse studies compare apical suspension and hysteropexy procedures and assess perioperative vaginal estrogen.<sup>[P4](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P8](#publication-8)</sup>
