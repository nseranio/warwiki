## Biography

Christopher J. Loftus is a urologist with Kaiser Permanente in Oakland, California.[^1][^2] Loftus attended Case Western Reserve University for medical school and completed urology residency at the University of Washington.[^3] Loftus trained in the University of Minnesota's 2022–2023 genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship, led by Sean Elliott with Joseph Pariser on its faculty.[^2][^4]

## Contributions

Loftus's cited studies address ureteral access sheath injury, including subsequent ureteral strictures and a comparison of sheaths and ureteral wall trauma.[^5][^6]
