## Contributions

- **Pelvic Floor Disorders Research Foundation** (2001). Started a foundation that provided research seed funding to fellows and early-career faculty studying pelvic floor disorders.[^2]
- **AUGS Quality Improvement and Outcomes Research Network** (2015). As AUGS president, announced a network for developing pelvic-medicine quality measures, guidance and appropriate-use criteria.[^3]
