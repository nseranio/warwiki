## Biography

Kirtishri Mishra is a MetroHealth urologist and assistant professor at Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine.[^1] He earned his MD at Case Western Reserve School of Medicine and completed a urology residency at University Hospitals in Cleveland.[^1] He completed a gender-affirming and reconstructive surgery fellowship at New York University Medical Center, whose fellowship lists Lee C. Zhao as director.[^1][^2]

## Contributions

Mishra's cited work covers gender-affirming genital reconstruction, including malleable penile prosthesis insertion after phalloplasty and a vaginoplasty book chapter.[^3][^4]
