## Known for

- **Intralesional verapamil study for Peyronie's disease** (1994). Reported an early clinical series evaluating intralesional verapamil injections for Peyronie's plaques.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Peyronie's Disease Agents](/docs/foundations/pharmacology/sexual-medicine-andrology/peyronies-disease-agents).
- **Peyronie's disease surgical treatment algorithm** (1997). Developed and prospectively evaluated an algorithm selecting plication, plaque incision with grafting, or prosthesis according to deformity and erectile function.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Tunica Plication for Peyronie's Disease](/docs/surgical-techniques/04j-sexual-dysfunction/peyronies-disease/tunica-plication).
- **Human pericardial grafting for Peyronie's disease** (2003). Reported penile straightening after plaque incision or partial excision using processed human cadaveric pericardium as the graft.[^11] Cited on WARWIKI: [Tutoplast-Processed Allografts](/docs/foundations/tools/biomaterials/biological-grafts/tutoplast-allografts).

## Contributions

Levine's cited work covers surgery for Peyronie's disease using plication, grafting, and penile prostheses, as well as intralesional verapamil and traction therapy. Other cited papers examine chronic scrotal content pain and microsurgical spermatic cord denervation.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
