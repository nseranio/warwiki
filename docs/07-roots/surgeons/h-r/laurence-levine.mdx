## Key Publications

Levine's cited work covers surgery for Peyronie's disease using plication, grafting, and penile prostheses, as well as intralesional verapamil and traction therapy. Other cited papers examine chronic scrotal content pain and microsurgical spermatic cord denervation.<sup>[P4](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P2](#publication-10)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-8)</sup>
