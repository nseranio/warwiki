## Biography

Laurence A. Levine is a professor of urology at Rush Medical College.[^1][^2] Levine received his medical degree from the University of Colorado School of Medicine.[^2] Levine completed a surgery residency at Tufts Medical Center and a urology residency at Harvard Medical School.[^3] Levine served as president of the Chicago Urological Society from 2004 to 2005.[^2]

## Contributions

Levine's cited work covers surgery for Peyronie's disease using plication, grafting, and penile prostheses, as well as intralesional verapamil and traction therapy. Other cited papers examine chronic scrotal content pain and microsurgical spermatic cord denervation.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
