## Biography

Jack R. Robertson (1917–2015) was a professor emeritus of obstetrics and gynecology and urogynecology at the University of Nevada Medical School.[^1][^2] Robertson helped establish the International Urogynecological Association in 1976 and served as its president from 1981 to 1984.[^3] Robertson also served as president of the Gynecologic Urology Society in 1979–1980.[^4]
