## Biography

Eric S. Rovner is a professor of urology at the Medical University of South Carolina and directs its section of voiding dysfunction, female urology, and urodynamics.[^1][^2] He attended Albert Einstein College of Medicine and completed his urology residency at the Hospital of the University of Pennsylvania.[^3][^4] He completed a UCLA urology fellowship under Shlomo Raz.[^4] He directs MUSC's urogynecology and pelvic reconstructive surgery fellowship and is a past president of the Society of Urodynamics, Female Pelvic Medicine and Urogenital Reconstruction.[^5][^1]

## Contributions

His cited work covers female urethral diverticula and diverticulectomy, including dorsal or circumferential reconstruction and a systematic review. Other cited papers address intradetrusor onabotulinumtoxinA for neurogenic detrusor overactivity and its administration.[^6][^7][^8][^9][^10]
