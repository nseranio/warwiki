## Biography

Eric S. Rovner is a professor of urology at the Medical University of South Carolina and directs its Urogynecology and Pelvic Reconstructive Surgery Fellowship.[^1][^2] He received his MD from Albert Einstein College of Medicine in 1991.[^3] He completed his urology residency at the University of Pennsylvania and then trained as a UCLA Urology fellow under Shlomo Raz.[^4] He is a past president of the Society of Urodynamics, Female Pelvic Medicine & Urogenital Reconstruction.[^1]

## Contributions

Rovner's cited work addresses female urethral diverticula through a review, chapters on diverticula and diverticulectomy, and a study of circumferential diverticulum reconstruction. Other cited papers address onabotulinumtoxinA administration and long-term treatment outcomes in neurogenic detrusor overactivity.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]
