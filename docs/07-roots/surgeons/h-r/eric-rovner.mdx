## Contributions

His cited work covers female urethral diverticula and diverticulectomy, including dorsal or circumferential reconstruction and a systematic review. Other cited papers address intradetrusor onabotulinumtoxinA for neurogenic detrusor overactivity and its administration.[^6][^7][^8][^9][^10]

- **Reconstruction of dorsal or circumferential female urethral diverticula** (2003). Reported diagnosis and surgical reconstruction of complex dorsal and circumferential female urethral diverticula.[^11] Cited on WARWIKI: [Urethral Diverticulectomy](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/urethral-diverticulum-repair).
- **Posterior urethral-flap bladder-neck closure** (2011). Described transvaginal bladder-neck closure using a posterior urethral flap for women with catheter-related urethral destruction.[^12] Cited on WARWIKI: [Bladder Neck Closure](/docs/surgical-techniques/04ab-bladder-neck-reconstruction/outlet-continence/bladder-neck-closure).
- **Minimum reporting data set for female stress-incontinence surgery** (2024). Led recommendations for a common minimum data set in publications on female stress-incontinence surgery.[^13]
