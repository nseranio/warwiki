## Known for

- **Sonourethrography for urethral stricture evaluation** (1988). Co-developed ultrasound imaging of urethral strictures to assess their extent before reconstruction.[^3] Cited on WARWIKI: [Ultrasound in Reconstructive Urology](/docs/evaluation/imaging/ultrasound).
- **Circular fasciocutaneous penile flap for urethral reconstruction** (1993). Described a circular penile skin flap for single-stage reconstruction of extensive urethral strictures.[^4] Cited on WARWIKI: [Orandi Flap (Longitudinal Ventral Penile Skin Flap)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/orandi-flap).

## Principal Contributions

His UCSF team studied sonourethrography and magnetic resonance imaging for urethral injury and stricture evaluation.[^1] He created a genitourinary trauma database at San Francisco General Hospital in 1977.[^2] His textbook was titled Traumatic and Reconstructive Urology.[^1]

## The UCSF Fellowship

McAninch trained fellows in male genitourinary trauma and reconstruction at UCSF and San Francisco General beginning in 1989.[^2]

## Recognition

UCSF reported his retirement from the San Francisco General chief position in July 2013 after 36 years.[^2]
