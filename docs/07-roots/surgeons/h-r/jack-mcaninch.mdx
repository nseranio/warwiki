## Key Publications

His UCSF team studied sonourethrography and magnetic resonance imaging for urethral injury and stricture evaluation.[^1] He created a genitourinary trauma database at San Francisco General Hospital in 1977.[^2] His textbook was titled Traumatic and Reconstructive Urology.[^1]

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Sonourethrography for urethral stricture evaluation** (1988). Co-developed ultrasound imaging of urethral strictures to assess their extent before reconstruction.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Circular fasciocutaneous penile flap for urethral reconstruction** (1993). Described a circular penile skin flap for single-stage reconstruction of extensive urethral strictures.

</KeyPublication>

</ol>

## The UCSF Fellowship

McAninch trained fellows in male genitourinary trauma and reconstruction at UCSF and San Francisco General beginning in 1989.[^2]

## Recognition

UCSF reported his retirement from the San Francisco General chief position in July 2013 after 36 years.[^2]
