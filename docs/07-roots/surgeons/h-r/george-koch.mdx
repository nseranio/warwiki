## Biography

George Koch is an assistant professor of urology at The Ohio State University College of Medicine.[^1] Koch earned an MD at Georgetown University School of Medicine and completed urology residency at Vanderbilt University Medical Center.[^1] Koch completed a 2023–2024 genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship at the University of Washington and Harborview Medical Center under Hunter Wessells.[^2] Koch is assistant director of Ohio State's urology residency program and serves on the editorial board of The Journal of Urology.[^3][^1]

## Contributions

Koch's cited work addresses Fournier's gangrene through a management review and a paper on skin-sparing debridement.[^4][^5][^6]
