## Training and Career

Jordan was born in El Paso, Texas, on September 29, 1944.[^1] His EVMS fellowship combined plastic surgery, pediatric surgery, female pelvic medicine, and colorectal surgery.[^1] After Navy service, he joined the EVMS urology department in 1986 and retired from clinical practice in 2011.[^1]

## Principal Contributions

His 1999 review described forearm and ulnar forearm flaps for penile reconstruction and phallic construction, and dartos and penile skin flaps for urethral reconstruction.[^3]

- **Jordan flap for fossa-navicularis stricture** (1987). Described reconstruction of the fossa navicularis using a ventral transverse island skin flap.[^4] Cited on WARWIKI: [Sliding-T Dorsal Inlay Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/sliding-t-dorsal-inlay).
- **Vessel-sparing bulbar urethral anastomosis** (2007). Described vessel-sparing excision and primary anastomosis for proximal bulbar urethral stricture reconstruction.[^5] Cited on WARWIKI: [MANTA Urethroplasty (Mucomucosal Anastomotic Non-Transecting Augmentation)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/manta).

## The EVMS Program

The EVMS reconstruction program was later named the Devine-Jordan Center for Adult and Pediatric Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgery.[^1]

## Recognition

The AUA awarded Jordan its Lifetime Achievement Award in 2025.[^1]
