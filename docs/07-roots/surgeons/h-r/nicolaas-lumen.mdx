## Known for

- **Etiology of urethral stricture disease** (2009). Led a study of 268 patients that characterized contemporary causes of urethral stricture disease.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Male Urethral Stricture](/docs/clinical-conditions/03b-voiding-outlet/urethral-stricture).
- **Nontransecting anastomotic urethroplasty outcomes** (2016). Reported surgical and functional outcomes after nontransecting anastomotic repair of urethral strictures.[^11] Cited on WARWIKI: [Non-Transecting Bulbar Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/non-transecting-bulbar).
- **Chair, EAU urethral stricture guidelines panel** (2021). Chaired the EAU panel that issued guidance on male urethral stricture management.[^12]

## Contributions

Lumen's cited work covers urethral reconstruction for male and female stricture disease, including oral grafts, perineal urethrostomy, nontransecting repair, and reconstruction after phalloplasty. The cited work also includes EAU urethral stricture guidelines.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]
