## Key Publications

Lumen's cited work covers urethral reconstruction for male and female stricture disease, including oral grafts, perineal urethrostomy, nontransecting repair, and reconstruction after phalloplasty. The cited work also includes EAU urethral stricture guidelines.<sup>[P4](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-8)</sup><sup>[P8](#publication-9)</sup>
