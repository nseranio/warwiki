## Biography

Brian M. Inouye is an associate professor of urology at Albany Medical College and practices reconstructive urology with Albany Med Health System.[^1][^2] He earned his MD at the Renaissance School of Medicine at Stony Brook University and completed urology residency at Duke University Medical Center.[^2][^3] Brian Inouye completed a fellowship in reconstructive urology and genitourinary cancer survivorship at Duke University Medical Center in 2021.[^2][^4] He chairs the Young GURS Committee of the Society of Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgeons and serves as assistant program director for the Urology Residency at Albany Medical College.[^2]
