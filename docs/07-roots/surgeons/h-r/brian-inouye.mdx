## Key Publications

Inouye's cited work covers extirpative surgery for the end-stage irradiated bladder in prostate cancer survivors, including pubovesical fistula with pubic bone osteomyelitis, and buccal graft reharvest for recurrent urethral stricture.<sup>[P1](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P2](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P3](#publication-6)</sup>

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Extirpative surgery for the end-stage irradiated bladder** (2023). Senior author of a review of cystectomy with urinary diversion for urosymphyseal fistula, radiation cystitis and rectourethral fistula after prostate radiation, concluding that it is a safe option in selected patients.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Pubic symphysis resection and cystectomy for pubovesical fistula** (2022). Led a study of 18 prostate cancer survivors in which paired SF-12 physical composite scores rose from 30.6 to 45.5 after surgery and median annual opioid prescribing fell from 103.1 to 34.3 morphine milligram equivalents.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[2]} citations={citations}>

**Buccal graft reharvest** (2022). Led a review of 5 ipsilateral reharvests from previously closed buccal sites in 4 men with recurrent stricture; median postoperative oral health scores were 0 of 56, the same as after first-time harvest.

</KeyPublication>

</ol>
