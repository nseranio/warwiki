## Biography

Samuel J. Ivan, MD, is an assistant professor of urology at Fox Chase Cancer Center and the Fox Chase–Temple Urologic Institute.[^1] He earned his MD at the University of Toledo College of Medicine and completed his urology residency at Carolinas Medical Center.[^2] He completed a reconstructive and prosthetic urology fellowship at Fox Chase with fellowship director Jay Simhan.[^1][^3] He served as chair of the American Urological Association Residents and Fellows Committee.[^1]

## Contributions

His cited work on inflatable penile prostheses includes two multi-institutional studies of 0.05% chlorhexidine use and infection, plus a chapter on surgical management of device complications.[^4][^5][^6]
