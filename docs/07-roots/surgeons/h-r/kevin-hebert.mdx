## Biography

Kevin J. Hebert is an assistant professor of urology and program director at LSU Health Shreveport.[^1] He earned his MD at Louisiana State University Health Shreveport School of Medicine and completed a urology residency at Mayo Clinic in Rochester, Minnesota.[^2] He completed a genitourinary trauma and reconstructive surgery fellowship at the University of Utah under Jeremy Myers, MD.[^2][^3]

## Contributions

His cited work covers reconstruction of urosymphyseal and rectourethral fistulas, including pubectomy with urinary reconstruction and robotic approaches.[^4][^5][^6]
