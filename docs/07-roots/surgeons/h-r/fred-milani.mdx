## Known for

- **Long-term randomized comparison of vaginal mesh and native-tissue prolapse repair** (2018). Led seven-year follow-up of a randomized trial comparing mesh and native-tissue surgery for recurrent prolapse.[^4]
- **International definitions of prolapse-surgery outcomes** (2023). Chaired the International Urogynecological Consultation committee defining success and failure after pelvic-organ-prolapse surgery.[^5]

## Training and career

<div className="sp-facts">

- **Former position** Urogynecologist, Reinier de Graaf Hospital, Delft, Netherlands (retired from clinical practice July 2020)[^1][^2]
- **Other training** Medical and obstetrics and gynecology training, Erasmus University Rotterdam[^1]
- **Leadership** President, International Urogynecological Association (2023–2024)[^3]
- **Leadership** Chair, Dutch Pelvic Floor Society (previously)[^2]
