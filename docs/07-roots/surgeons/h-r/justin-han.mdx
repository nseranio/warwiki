## Biography

Justin S. Han serves as urology residency program director at Northwell Health and has directed male reconstructive urology at its Smith Institute.[^1][^2]

## Contributions

Han's cited work covers urethral stricture assessment, abdominal wall skin-graft urethroplasty, and a urethral fistula complication after a priapism shunt.[^3][^4][^5]
