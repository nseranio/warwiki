## Biography

Joshua D. Roth is an assistant professor of urology at Indiana University School of Medicine whose specialty is pediatric urology.[^1] He earned an MD from the University of Maryland and completed his urology residency at Indiana University.[^1][^2] He completed a pediatric urology fellowship at Riley Hospital for Children, a genitourinary reconstruction fellowship at the University of Minnesota, and a genital reconstructive surgery fellowship at Ghent University and the Belgrade Center.[^2][^3] During Roth's Minnesota GURS fellowship, Sean Elliott led the program and Joseph Pariser was among its faculty.[^3][^4]

## Contributions

Roth's cited work covers urinary reconstruction using intestinal segments, including metabolic consequences of diversion, ileal ureter reconstruction, and continent cutaneous ileocecocystoplasty. Other cited studies address augmentation cystoplasty for neuropathic bladder, sexual outcomes in exstrophy-epispadias complex, and kidney disease risk in myelomeningocele.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]
