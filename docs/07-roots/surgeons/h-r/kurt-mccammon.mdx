## Contributions

McCammon's confirmed work covers urethral stricture reconstruction, including lichen sclerosus, anastomotic and vessel-sparing repair, and graft-augmented perineal urethrostomy. Other confirmed publications address male incontinence surgery with slings and artificial urinary sphincters.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
