## Biography

Kurt A. McCammon is chair and professor of urology and holds the Devine Chair in Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgery at Old Dominion University.[^1] He received his medical degree from the Medical College of Ohio in 1992 and completed his urology residency at Eastern Virginia Medical School.[^2] He completed an adult and pediatric genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship at Eastern Virginia Medical School.[^2]

## Contributions

His cited work covers urethral stricture reconstruction, including augmented perineal urethrostomy and vessel-sparing bulbar repair, and male urinary incontinence surgery with artificial urinary sphincters and sling salvage.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
