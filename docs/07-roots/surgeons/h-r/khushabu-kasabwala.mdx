## Biography

Khushabu D. Kasabwala practices urology at Lahey Hospital & Medical Center and is an assistant professor of surgery at UMass Chan Medical School.[^1] Kasabwala graduated from Rutgers New Jersey Medical School and completed urology residency at New York Presbyterian-Weill Cornell.[^2][^1] Kasabwala completed a genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship at the University of Minnesota in 2021.[^2][^1]

[^1]: [Khushabu D. Kasabwala, MD. Lahey Health](https://physicians.lahey.org/details/4481). Accessed October 2026.
