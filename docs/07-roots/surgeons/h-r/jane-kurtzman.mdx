## Biography

Jane T. Kurtzman is an assistant professor of surgery in the University of Utah's Division of Urology.[^1] She earned her MD at SUNY Downstate College of Medicine and completed urology residency at NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital, Columbia University Irving Medical Center.[^1] She completed a genitourinary injury and reconstruction fellowship at the University of Utah under Jeremy Myers.[^1][^2] She co-founded and directs GU-IMPACTS, a program for longitudinal subspecialty urology training in Rwanda and Cambodia.[^1]

## Contributions

Her cited work addresses urethral stricture assessment and buccal mucosal graft repair for lichen sclerosus-related stricture, as well as patient-reported quality of life after urinary diversion for pelvic radiation injury.[^3][^4][^5]
