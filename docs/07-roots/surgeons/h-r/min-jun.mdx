## Biography

Min Suk Jun is a genital gender-affirming surgeon at Jun Surgical in Atherton, California.[^1] He completed urology residency at Detroit Medical Center with Richard Santucci.[^2] He completed a reconstructive and traumatic urology fellowship at Temple University with Michael Metro.[^2] He then completed a fellowship in gender-affirming genital and robotic surgery at New York University with Lee Zhao and Rachel Bluebond-Langner.[^2]

## Contributions

His cited work covers gender-affirming genital surgery, including peritoneal flap vaginoplasty, robotic vaginectomy during penile reconstruction, and neovaginal fistulas. It also covers robotic ureteral reconstruction, including appendiceal ureteroplasty.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
