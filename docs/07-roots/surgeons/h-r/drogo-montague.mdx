## Contributions

Montague's cited work addresses artificial urinary sphincter implantation, penile prosthesis outcomes in transplant recipients, and corporoplasty for penile curvature. An earlier paper examines jejunal conduit urinary diversion.[^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **1996 AUA erectile dysfunction guideline** (1996). Chaired the AUA panel that evaluated treatments for organic erectile dysfunction and issued practice recommendations.[^8]
- **Prosthesis cylinder selection in Peyronie’s disease** (1996). Compared AMS 700 CX and Ultrex cylinders for correcting deformity during penile prosthesis implantation in men with Peyronie’s disease.[^9]
- **Long-term artificial urinary sphincter outcomes** (2001). Led a series assessing long-term continence and patient satisfaction after artificial sphincter implantation for post-prostatectomy incontinence.[^10]
