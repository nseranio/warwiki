## Contributions

- **Nationwide TVT complication analysis in Finland** (2002). Co-authored an analysis of complications across 1,455 TVT operations performed at Finnish hospitals.[^3]
- **Seventeen-year prospective follow-up of tension-free vaginal tape** (2013). Led extended follow-up of a three-center TVT cohort, reporting continence and tape findings 17 years after surgery.[^4]
