## Biography

Carl-Gustaf Nilsson began his research career in Tapani Luukkainen's steroid laboratory, studying hormone-releasing intrauterine systems.[^3] He completed a thesis in that field in 1977, then worked with Ulf Ulmsten on tension-free vaginal tape (TVT) for stress urinary incontinence.[^3]

Nilsson used TVT from an early stage and followed patients for years after surgery, publishing observations on its long-term results.[^3] He supervised six theses and remained involved in assessing newer urogynecologic operations after formally retiring in 2012.[^3]

Away from medicine, he trained the Finnish sailing team at the 1976 and 1980 Olympic Games.[^3]
