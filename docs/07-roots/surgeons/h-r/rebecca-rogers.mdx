## Known for

- **Pelvic Organ Prolapse/Urinary Incontinence Sexual Questionnaire** (2001). Led development and validation of a questionnaire measuring sexual function in women with prolapse or urinary incontinence.[^9]
- **PISQ-12 short form** (2003). Led development of the 12-item short form of the prolapse and urinary incontinence sexual questionnaire.[^10]
- **PISQ-IR revised measure** (2013). Led the international revision of the sexual-function measure for women with pelvic floor disorders.[^11] Cited on WARWIKI: [Assessment Tools &amp; Questionnaires](/docs/evaluation/history-physical/assessment-tools).
- **IUGA/ICS sexual-health terminology report** (2018). First-authored a joint terminology report for assessing sexual health in women with pelvic floor dysfunction.[^12] Cited on WARWIKI: [Mesh Complications & Removal — POP and SUI Mesh](/docs/surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/mesh-complications).
