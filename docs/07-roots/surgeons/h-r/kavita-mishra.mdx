## Biography

Kavita Mishra is a clinical associate professor of obstetrics and gynecology at Stanford and a urogynecologist at the Stanford Pelvic Health Center.[^1] She earned her medical degree at the University of California, San Francisco School of Medicine and completed obstetrics and gynecology residency at Brown University and Women & Infants Hospital.[^1] She completed a urogynecology fellowship at Brown University and Women & Infants Hospital in 2016 and a transgender surgery and medicine fellowship at Cleveland Clinic in 2021.[^1]

## Contributions

Her confirmed citations address pelvic floor physical therapy, surgery for stress urinary incontinence, and racial and ethnic differences in apical prolapse surgery. They also include a trial of perioperative pelvic floor physical therapy in patients undergoing vaginoplasty.[^2][^3][^4][^5]
