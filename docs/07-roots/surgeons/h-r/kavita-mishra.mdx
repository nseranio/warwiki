## Contributions

Her confirmed citations address pelvic floor physical therapy, surgery for stress urinary incontinence, and racial and ethnic differences in apical prolapse surgery. They also include a trial of perioperative pelvic floor physical therapy in patients undergoing vaginoplasty.[^2][^3][^4][^5]

- **Multicenter vaginoplasty adverse-event study** (2023). Analyzed 30-day complications after gender-affirming vaginoplasty in a national surgical-quality database.[^6] Cited on WARWIKI: [Gender-Affirming Surgery — Overview](/docs/special-populations/05c-gender-affirming/overview).
- **FLOWER pelvic-floor therapy trial** (2023). Coauthored a randomized trial of perioperative pelvic floor physical therapy for women undergoing gender-affirming vaginoplasty.[^7] Cited on WARWIKI: [Neovaginal Stenosis Management — Stepwise](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery/neovaginal-stenosis-management).
