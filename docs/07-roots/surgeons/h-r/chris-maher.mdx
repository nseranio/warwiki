## Contributions

His cited work centers on surgical treatment of pelvic organ prolapse, including Cochrane reviews of apical and anterior compartment procedures and studies of vault and posterior vaginal wall repair. It also includes a trial comparing sling surgery with urethral bulking for stress urinary incontinence.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Randomized comparison of abdominal and vaginal vault prolapse repair** (2004). Led a randomized comparison of abdominal sacral colpopexy and vaginal sacrospinous colpopexy in 95 women.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Sacrocolpopexy](/docs/surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/sacrocolpopexy).
- **Cochrane review of transvaginal mesh and grafts for prolapse** (2016). Led a review of 37 randomized trials comparing mesh or graft repair with native tissue prolapse repair.[^9]
- **Cochrane review of apical vaginal prolapse surgery** (2023). Led an updated review of 59 randomized trials comparing operations for apical vaginal prolapse.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Mesh & Graft-Augmented Prolapse Repair](/docs/surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/mesh-graft-augmented-repairs).
