## Key Publications

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Distal urethral polypropylene sling outcomes** (2003). Prospectively evaluated stress incontinence outcomes after distal urethral polypropylene sling surgery using patient-reported symptoms, bother, and quality of life.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Transvaginal paravaginal repair for high-grade cystocele** (2005). Described a transvaginal paravaginal repair addressing central and lateral cystocele defects with a concomitant suburethral sling.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[2]} citations={citations}>

**Adipose-derived cells for lower urinary tract reconstruction** (2005). Coauthored an animal pilot study showing survival and smooth-muscle incorporation of human adipose-derived cells injected into bladder and urethra.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[3]} citations={citations}>

**MAPP research on urologic chronic pelvic pain** (2014). Served as an investigator in the NIH-funded MAPP network studying bladder pain and other urologic chronic pelvic pain syndromes.

</KeyPublication>
