## Contributions

- **Distal urethral polypropylene sling outcomes** (2003). Prospectively evaluated stress incontinence outcomes after distal urethral polypropylene sling surgery using patient-reported symptoms, bother, and quality of life.[^3]
- **Transvaginal paravaginal repair for high-grade cystocele** (2005). Described a transvaginal paravaginal repair addressing central and lateral cystocele defects with a concomitant suburethral sling.[^4]
- **Adipose-derived cells for lower urinary tract reconstruction** (2005). Coauthored an animal pilot study showing survival and smooth-muscle incorporation of human adipose-derived cells injected into bladder and urethra.[^5]
- **MAPP research on urologic chronic pelvic pain** (2014). Served as an investigator in the NIH-funded MAPP network studying bladder pain and other urologic chronic pelvic pain syndromes.[^6]
