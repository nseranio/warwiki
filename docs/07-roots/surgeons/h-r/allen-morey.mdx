## Biography

Allen F. Morey is a urologist with Urology Clinics of North Texas.[^1][^2] He earned his medical degree at the Uniformed Services University of the Health Sciences and completed a urology residency at Tripler Army Medical Center.[^2] He completed a urologic trauma and reconstruction fellowship at the University of California, San Francisco, with Jack W. McAninch from 1995 to 1997.[^2] He served as president of the Society of Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgeons in 2014.[^2]

## Contributions

His cited work covers urethral stricture management and reconstruction, including flap and graft urethroplasty, perineal urethrostomy, and the 2023 AUA guideline amendment. Other cited papers address genitourinary trauma and prosthetic treatment of male incontinence.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
