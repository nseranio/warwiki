## Contributions

His cited work covers urethral stricture management and reconstruction, including flap and graft urethroplasty, perineal urethrostomy, and the 2023 AUA guideline amendment. Other cited papers address genitourinary trauma and prosthetic treatment of male incontinence.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Buccal mucosal graft use in adult bulbar urethroplasty** (1996). With Jack McAninch, described indications and operative use of buccal mucosal grafts for adult bulbar urethral reconstruction.[^8]
- **AUA Urotrauma Guideline** (2014). Chaired the AUA guideline panel addressing evaluation and management of renal, ureteral, bladder, urethral and genital injuries.[^9]
