## Contributions

Lazzeri's confirmed publications cover urethral reconstruction, including buccal mucosal graft urethroplasty, surgery for lichen sclerosus and failed hypospadias repair, and outcomes after revision or perineal urethrostomy.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Intravesical vanilloids for neurogenic incontinence** (2004). Reported ten years of clinical experience with intravesical capsaicin and resiniferatoxin for neurogenic incontinence.[^8]
- **Intravesical nociceptin trial for neurogenic detrusor overactivity** (2006). Led a multicenter exploratory randomized trial of intravesical nociceptin/orphanin FQ for neurogenic detrusor overactivity incontinence.[^9]
