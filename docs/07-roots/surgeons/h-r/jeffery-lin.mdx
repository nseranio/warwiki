## Biography

Jeffery Shyh-Jye Lin is a urologist at Kaiser Permanente's West Los Angeles Medical Center and a clinical instructor at Kaiser Permanente Bernard J. Tyson School of Medicine.[^1][^2] Lin received a medical degree from Baylor College of Medicine.[^1] Lin completed a urology residency at Los Angeles County+University of Southern California Medical Center and a trauma and reconstruction fellowship at the University of Washington under Hunter Wessells.[^1][^3][^4][^5] Lin also completed NYU Langone's genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship.[^6]

## Contributions

Lin's confirmed citations cover reconstructive urology, including robotic buccal mucosa graft ureteroplasty, nontransecting flap pyeloplasty, and perfusion assessment during open urethral reconstruction. The cited atlas chapters address vaginoplasty and transperineal rectourethral fistula repair.[^7][^8][^9][^10][^11]
