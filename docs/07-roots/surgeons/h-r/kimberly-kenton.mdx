## Key Publications

Her work cited on WARWIKI spans Pelvic Floor Disorders Network and Urinary Incontinence Treatment Network studies, including the NHANES estimate of pelvic floor disorder prevalence in US women, and a Loyola study that found uncultivated bacteria in the adult female bladder.<sup>[P4](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-6)</sup> The American Urogynecologic Society gave her its Raymond A. Lee Award.[^1]

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Rectocele variability on defecography** (1999). Led a fluoroscopic study of 98 women having reconstructive pelvic surgery in which 78% had rectoceles, contrast retention did not match symptoms, and posterior wall prolapse grade did not differ between women with and without rectoceles.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Activity restrictions after prolapse surgery** (2021). Senior author of the 1-year follow-up of the ReCOUP trial, in which satisfaction (86.8% liberal vs 95.6% restricted) and anatomic failure (8.8% vs 7.0%) did not differ between liberal and restricted postoperative activity.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[2]} citations={citations}>

**Satisfaction with rechargeable sacral neuromodulation** (2021). Senior author of an ARTISAN-SNM secondary analysis in which 68.5% of 124 participants were very satisfied at 1 year and satisfaction tracked diary-measured symptom reduction.

</KeyPublication>
