## Contributions

Her work cited on WARWIKI spans Pelvic Floor Disorders Network and Urinary Incontinence Treatment Network studies, including the NHANES estimate of pelvic floor disorder prevalence in US women, and a Loyola study that found uncultivated bacteria in the adult female bladder.[^4][^5][^6] The American Urogynecologic Society gave her its Raymond A. Lee Award.[^1]

- **Rectocele variability on defecography** (1999). Led a fluoroscopic study of 98 women having reconstructive pelvic surgery in which 78% had rectoceles, contrast retention did not match symptoms, and posterior wall prolapse grade did not differ between women with and without rectoceles.[^7] Cited on WARWIKI: [Posterior Compartment Prolapse](/docs/clinical-conditions/03c-pelvic-support/posterior-compartment).
- **Activity restrictions after prolapse surgery** (2021). Senior author of the 1-year follow-up of the ReCOUP trial, in which satisfaction (86.8% liberal vs 95.6% restricted) and anatomic failure (8.8% vs 7.0%) did not differ between liberal and restricted postoperative activity.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Sacrocolpopexy](/docs/surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/sacrocolpopexy).
- **Satisfaction with rechargeable sacral neuromodulation** (2021). Senior author of an ARTISAN-SNM secondary analysis in which 68.5% of 124 participants were very satisfied at 1 year and satisfaction tracked diary-measured symptom reduction.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Axonics SNM System](/docs/foundations/tools/biomaterials/neuromodulation-devices/axonics-snm).
