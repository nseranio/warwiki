## Contributions

Lai's cited work covers interstitial cystitis and bladder pain syndrome, including Hunner lesion phenotypes, and guidelines for evaluation and treatment of male chronic pelvic pain. Other confirmed citations examine skin preparation before genitourinary prosthetic surgery and outcomes after complex artificial urinary sphincter revision and reimplantation.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
