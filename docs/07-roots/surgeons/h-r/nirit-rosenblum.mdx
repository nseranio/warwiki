## Biography

Nirit Rosenblum is a clinical professor of urology and of obstetrics and gynecology at NYU Grossman School of Medicine.[^1] She earned her medical degree at New York University and completed urology residency at NYU Medical Center.[^1] She completed a female urology and pelvic reconstructive surgery fellowship at UCLA Medical Center under Shlomo Raz.[^2][^1] She is listed as associate director of NYU's urogynecology and reconstructive pelvic surgery fellowship.[^3]

## Contributions

Her confirmed citations cover transvaginal and transvesical vesicovaginal fistula repair and minimally invasive female pelvic reconstructive surgery.[^4][^5][^6][^7]
