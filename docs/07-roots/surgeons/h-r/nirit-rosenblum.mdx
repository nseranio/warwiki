## Contributions

Her confirmed citations cover transvaginal and transvesical vesicovaginal fistula repair and minimally invasive female pelvic reconstructive surgery.[^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Vaginal Surgery for the Urologist** (2012). Associate-edited a surgical atlas covering vaginal approaches to prolapse, incontinence, urethral reconstruction, and fistula repair.[^8]
- **Robotic uterine-sparing sacrohysteropexy series** (2013). Coauthored a 15-patient series describing robotic uterine-sparing sacrohysteropexy for pelvic organ prolapse.[^9]
