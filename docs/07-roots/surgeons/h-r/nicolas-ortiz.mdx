## Biography

Nicolas M. Ortiz is an assistant professor in UVA Health's Urologic Reconstruction division.[^1] He earned his medical degree at the University of Texas Southwestern Medical School and completed urology residency at the University of Miami.[^1][^2] He completed a fellowship in genitourinary reconstruction, trauma, and prosthetics at UT Southwestern Medical Center in 2020, in the program directed by Allen F. Morey.[^2][^1][^3]

## Contributions

Ortiz's confirmed papers examine artificial urinary sphincter selection and complications, including comparison with a male sling, cuff erosion and permanent urethral ligation after erosion, and pressure regulating balloon herniation.[^4][^5][^6][^7]
