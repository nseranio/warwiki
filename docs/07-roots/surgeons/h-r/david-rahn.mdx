## Biography

David D. Rahn is a professor of obstetrics and gynecology at UT Southwestern Medical Center.[^1] He earned his medical degree at UT Southwestern, where he completed an obstetrics and gynecology residency and a female pelvic medicine and reconstructive surgery fellowship.[^1] He is a member of the Society of Gynecologic Surgeons and the American Urogynecologic Society.[^1]

## Contributions

His cited studies examine vaginal estrogen around prolapse repair, including preoperative tissue and symptom measures, a randomized trial, and three-year outcomes.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
