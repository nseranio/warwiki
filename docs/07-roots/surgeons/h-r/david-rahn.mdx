## Known for

- **Practice guidelines on vaginal estrogen for pelvic floor disorders** (2015). Led a systematic review and practice guideline assessing vaginal estrogen for pelvic floor disorders after menopause.[^7]
- **IMPROVE randomized trial of perioperative vaginal estrogen** (2023). Led a randomized trial finding that perioperative vaginal estrogen did not improve success after native tissue apical prolapse repair.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [The Adnexa](/docs/foundations/anatomy-physiology/genitalia/adnexa).

He is a member of the Society of Gynecologic Surgeons and the American Urogynecologic Society.[^1]

## Contributions

His cited studies examine vaginal estrogen around prolapse repair, including preoperative tissue and symptom measures, a randomized trial, and three-year outcomes.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
