## Contributions

Kulkarni's cited work covers one-sided dorsal onlay graft urethroplasty, panurethral stricture repair, and a single-stage spiral preputial graft. Other confirmed papers address pelvic fracture urethral injury and complex posterior urethral reconstruction.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

- **One-sided dorsal onlay urethroplasty** (2009). Described one-sided dissection for dorsal oral-mucosal graft urethroplasty while preserving contralateral vascular and muscular support.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Kulkarni One-Stage Panurethral Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/kulkarni-one-stage).
- **Single-stage panurethral stricture repair** (2012). Reported a 117-patient series using one-sided dissection, penile invagination and dorsal oral-mucosal grafts for panurethral strictures.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Kulkarni One-Stage Panurethral Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/kulkarni-one-stage).
