## Biography

Sanjay B. Kulkarni is a director at Urokul Hospital in Pune, India, and directs its reconstructive urology fellowship.[^1][^2] He attended BJ Medical College in Pune and completed a surgery residency at KEM Hospital in Pune.[^3] He trained in urology in England with Michael Heal at Leighton Hospital and later Richard Turner-Warwick.[^3] He served as president of the Society of Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgeons in 2014 and of the Société Internationale d’Urologie from 2019 to 2021.[^3]

## Contributions

Kulkarni's cited work covers one-sided dorsal onlay graft urethroplasty, panurethral stricture repair, and a single-stage spiral preputial graft. Other confirmed papers address pelvic fracture urethral injury and complex posterior urethral reconstruction.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
