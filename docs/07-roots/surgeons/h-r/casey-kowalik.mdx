## Contributions

- **Female urethral reconstruction graft-versus-flap series** (2014). Led a comparative series of vaginal flap and dorsal buccal graft urethroplasty for female urethral stricture.[^4] Cited on WARWIKI: [Anterior Vaginal Wall Mucosal Inlay (Önol)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/onol-vaginal-mucosal-inlay).
- **Study of women's toileting behaviors and bladder symptoms** (2019). Led a community study relating convenience, delayed, and strained voiding behaviors to self-reported bladder problems.[^5]
