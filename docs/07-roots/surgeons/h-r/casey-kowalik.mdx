## Biography

Casey G. Kowalik is an associate professor at the University of Kansas and program director for its urology residency.[^1][^2] She earned her medical degree at the University of Virginia and completed her urology residency at Lahey Hospital and Medical Center.[^3] She completed a fellowship in female pelvic medicine and reconstructive surgery at Vanderbilt University Medical Center.[^3]
