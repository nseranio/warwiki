## Biography

Asif Muneer is a consultant urological surgeon and andrologist at University College London Hospital, clinical lead for urology at UCLH Westmoreland Street Hospital, and an honorary professor of urology and surgical andrology at University College London.[^1][^2][^3] He holds an MB ChB.[^4] He completed higher surgical training in Oxford, fellowships at the University of Bern and the University of Paris, and a senior fellowship in surgical andrology at University College London Hospital.[^5][^1] He is a past honorary secretary of the British Association of Urological Surgeons and a past president of the British Society for Sexual Medicine.[^6][^3]

## Contributions

His cited work covers penile cancer surgery and reconstruction, including glansectomy, glans resurfacing, frozen section assessment of margins, and prosthesis implantation following conservative cancer surgery. Other cited work addresses penile prosthesis surgery and the management of priapism.[^7][^8][^9][^10][^11]
