## Contributions

His cited work covers penile cancer surgery and reconstruction, including glansectomy, glans resurfacing, frozen section assessment of margins, and prosthesis implantation following conservative cancer surgery. Other cited work addresses penile prosthesis surgery and the management of priapism.[^7][^8][^9][^10][^11]

- **BAUS priapism consensus** (2018). Led the named authors of the BAUS consensus document on assessment and management of priapism.[^12]
