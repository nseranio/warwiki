## Biography

Anthony Mundy is professor of urology, a consultant urological surgeon, and corporate medical director at University College London Hospitals.[^1][^2] He qualified from St Mary's Hospital Medical School in London and trained in surgery and urology at Guy's Hospital.[^1][^3] He is a past president of the British Association of Urological Surgeons.[^3]

## Contributions

His cited work covers urethral reconstruction, including anterior urethroplasty, nontransecting bulbar repair, graft techniques, and posterior urethral strictures. Other cited papers address rectourethral and urethropubic fistulas after prostate cancer treatment.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
