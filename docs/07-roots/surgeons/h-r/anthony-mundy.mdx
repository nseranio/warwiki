## Contributions

His cited work covers urethral reconstruction, including anterior urethroplasty, nontransecting bulbar repair, graft techniques, and posterior urethral strictures. Other cited papers address rectourethral and urethropubic fistulas after prostate cancer treatment.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

- **Patient-reported outcome measure for urethral stricture surgery** (2011). Senior-authored development of a patient-reported outcome measure for symptoms and quality of life after urethral stricture surgery.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Male Urethral Stricture](/docs/clinical-conditions/03b-voiding-outlet/urethral-stricture).
