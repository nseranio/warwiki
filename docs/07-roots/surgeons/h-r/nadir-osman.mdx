## Biography

Nadir Osman is a consultant urologist at Sheffield Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust and practices privately at Spire Claremont Hospital.[^1] Osman graduated with honors from the University of Liverpool in 2006 and completed specialist urology training in Merseyside and Yorkshire between 2010 and 2018.[^1] Osman completed a reconstructive urology fellowship at Royal Hallamshire Hospital with Christopher Chapple, followed by focused training in complex vaginal reconstruction in Moscow with Dmitry Pushkar.[^2][^1] Osman serves on the European Association of Urology urethral stricture guideline panel.[^1]

## Contributions

Osman's cited work includes studies of female urethral diverticulum excision, urethral stricture repair, urogenital fistulae, and a fascial sling for stress urinary incontinence. Related papers examine urethroplasty surveillance and tissue-engineered buccal mucosa grafts.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
