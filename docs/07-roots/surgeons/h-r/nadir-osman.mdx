## Contributions

Osman's cited work includes studies of female urethral diverticulum excision, urethral stricture repair, urogenital fistulae, and a fascial sling for stress urinary incontinence. Related papers examine urethroplasty surveillance and tissue-engineered buccal mucosa grafts.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
