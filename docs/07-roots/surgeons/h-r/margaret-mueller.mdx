## Contributions

Mueller's cited work on postoperative activity after urogynecologic surgery includes a randomized study of restricted convalescence and an American Urogynecologic Society clinical consensus statement.[^3][^4]

- **450-patient minimally invasive sacrocolpopexy series** (2016). Reported outcomes and complications in approximately 450 women after minimally invasive abdominal sacrocolpopexy.[^5]
- **Postoperative activity restriction randomized trial** (2017). Led a randomized trial comparing liberal and restricted activity recommendations after prolapse surgery.[^6]
