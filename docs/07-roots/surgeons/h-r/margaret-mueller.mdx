## Biography

Margaret G. Mueller is an associate professor of obstetrics and gynecology at the University of Chicago and practices urogynecology and reconstructive pelvic surgery.[^1] She earned her medical degree at Loyola University and completed an obstetrics and gynecology residency at the University of Chicago.[^2] Mueller completed a fellowship in urogynecology and reconstructive surgery at Northwestern University and later directed its urogynecology fellowship program.[^2]

## Contributions

Mueller's cited work on postoperative activity after urogynecologic surgery includes a randomized study of restricted convalescence and an American Urogynecologic Society clinical consensus statement.[^3][^4]
