## Contributions

Erick Alejandro Ramírez Pérez's published work comes from international multicenter series on dorsal buccal mucosal graft repair of post-radiation urethral stenosis and buccal graft resurfacing of the penis.[^2][^5][^6]

- **Dorsal onlay buccal graft urethroplasty after prostatectomy and radiation** (2024). Coauthored an eight-institution series of 45 men with vesicourethral anastomotic or bulbomembranous stenosis after both treatments: 7 recurred, none developed de novo incontinence and 4 of 6 men with a preoperative suprapubic catheter were continent after repair.[^5] Cited on WARWIKI: [Vesicourethral Anastomotic Stenosis](/docs/clinical-conditions/03b-voiding-outlet/vesicourethral-anastomotic-stenosis), [Dorsal Onlay Oral Mucosal Graft Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/dorsal-onlay-omg).
- **The buccal belt** (2022). Coauthored a six-institution series of 31 men treated with subcoronal circumferential buccal mucosal graft resurfacing for recurrent penile adhesions; none had recurrence at a mean follow-up of 27 months.[^6] Cited on WARWIKI: [Glans Resurfacing](/docs/surgical-techniques/04e-genital-reconstruction/glans-resurfacing).
