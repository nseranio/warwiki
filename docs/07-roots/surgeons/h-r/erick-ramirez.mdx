## Key Publications

Erick Alejandro Ramírez Pérez's published work comes from international multicenter series on dorsal buccal mucosal graft repair of post-radiation urethral stenosis and buccal graft resurfacing of the penis.<sup>[P3](#publication-2)</sup><sup>[P1](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P2](#publication-6)</sup>

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Dorsal onlay buccal graft urethroplasty after prostatectomy and radiation** (2024). Coauthored an eight-institution series of 45 men with vesicourethral anastomotic or bulbomembranous stenosis after both treatments: 7 recurred, none developed de novo incontinence and 4 of 6 men with a preoperative suprapubic catheter were continent after repair.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**The buccal belt** (2022). Coauthored a six-institution series of 31 men treated with subcoronal circumferential buccal mucosal graft resurfacing for recurrent penile adhesions; none had recurrence at a mean follow-up of 27 months.

</KeyPublication>
