## Biography

Arman A. Kahokehr is a urologist at South Terrace Urology in Adelaide, South Australia.[^1] Kahokehr earned an MBChB from the University of Auckland and completed urology fellowship training at Flinders Medical Centre and the Repatriation General Hospital.[^1] He completed Duke University's reconstructive urology and genitourinary cancer survivorship fellowship in 2017–2018 with program director Andrew C. Peterson.[^2][^1][^3]

## Contributions

Kahokehr's cited work on complications after prostate cancer treatment covers urinary pubic symphysis fistula and associated pubic bone osteomyelitis, transobturator sling mechanics after prostatectomy, and long-term genitourinary toxicity after radiotherapy. His cited reviews also address buccal mucosal ureteroplasty and classification of male anterior urethral strictures.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
