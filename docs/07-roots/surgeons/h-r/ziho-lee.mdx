## Key Publications

Lee's cited work centers on robotic ureteral reconstruction for complex strictures and injuries, including buccal mucosa graft ureteroplasty, nontransecting reimplantation, radiation-associated strictures, ureteral rest, and appendiceal substitution.<sup>[P4](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P2](#publication-11)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-8)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-9)</sup>

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Intraureteral indocyanine-green guidance** (2015). Lee described intraureteral indocyanine-green injection with near-infrared imaging to identify ureters and stricture margins during robotic reconstruction.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Robotic buccal-mucosa graft ureteroplasty series** (2021). Lee led a multi-institutional report of intermediate-term outcomes after robotic buccal-mucosa graft repair of proximal and mid-ureteral strictures.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[2]} citations={citations}>

**Ureteral-rest outcomes study** (2021). Lee's multi-institutional study associated removing hardware across a ureteral stricture before robotic reconstruction with higher surgical success.

</KeyPublication>
