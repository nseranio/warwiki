## Contributions

Lee's cited work centers on robotic ureteral reconstruction for complex strictures and injuries, including buccal mucosa graft ureteroplasty, nontransecting reimplantation, radiation-associated strictures, ureteral rest, and appendiceal substitution.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]

- **Intraureteral indocyanine-green guidance** (2015). Lee described intraureteral indocyanine-green injection with near-infrared imaging to identify ureters and stricture margins during robotic reconstruction.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Indocyanine Green (ICG)](/docs/foundations/pharmacology/intraoperative-adjuncts/visualization-agents/icg).
- **Robotic buccal-mucosa graft ureteroplasty series** (2021). Lee led a multi-institutional report of intermediate-term outcomes after robotic buccal-mucosa graft repair of proximal and mid-ureteral strictures.[^11] Cited on WARWIKI: [Augmented Anastomotic Ureteroureterostomy](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/augmented-anastomotic-uu).
- **Ureteral-rest outcomes study** (2021). Lee's multi-institutional study associated removing hardware across a ureteral stricture before robotic reconstruction with higher surgical success.[^12] Cited on WARWIKI: [Ureteral Stricture](/docs/clinical-conditions/03e-upper-tract/ureteral-stricture).
