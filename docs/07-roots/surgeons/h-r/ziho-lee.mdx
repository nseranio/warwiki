## Biography

Ziho Lee is chief of Trauma and Reconstructive Urology and an assistant professor of urology at Northwestern University Feinberg School of Medicine.[^1] He earned his medical degree from Thomas Jefferson University Sidney Kimmel Medical College and completed his residency at Temple University Hospital.[^2] He completed fellowships in advanced robotic urologic oncology and reconstruction at Temple University and in urologic trauma and reconstruction at the University of Washington.[^3] He directs Northwestern's Genitourinary Reconstruction Surgery Fellowship.[^4]

## Contributions

Lee's cited work centers on robotic ureteral reconstruction for complex strictures and injuries, including buccal mucosa graft ureteroplasty, nontransecting reimplantation, radiation-associated strictures, ureteral rest, and appendiceal substitution.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]
