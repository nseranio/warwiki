## Contributions

His cited work covers sacral and tibial neuromodulation for lower urinary tract dysfunction and urgency urinary incontinence, including implantable tibial stimulation and follow-up from the OASIS study.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]

- **Clinical study of implantable tibial nerve neuromodulation** (2018). Led a clinical study evaluating a leadless implantable tibial nerve stimulation system for overactive bladder symptoms.[^7] Cited on WARWIKI: [Implantable Tibial Nerve Stimulation (iTNM)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/implantable-tibial-nerve-stimulation).
