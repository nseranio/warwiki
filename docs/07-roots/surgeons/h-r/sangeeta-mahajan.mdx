## Contributions

Her published work covers patient goals and satisfaction after pelvic reconstructive surgery, bladder dysfunction in multiple sclerosis, and multicenter perioperative trials in pelvic reconstructive surgery run with her Cleveland fellows.[^4][^5][^6]

- **Patient-centered goals after pelvic reconstructive surgery** (2006). Led a 1-year follow-up of 70 women in which lower goal achievement was associated with falling satisfaction, and urgency incontinence (56% after surgery, 44% de novo) was the most common reason for dissatisfaction.[^4]
- **Tamsulosin to prevent urinary retention after prolapse surgery** (2021). Senior author of a multicenter double-blind trial of 119 women in which a 10-day perioperative course of tamsulosin 0.4 mg lowered postoperative urinary retention from 25.8% with placebo to 8.8%.[^6]
