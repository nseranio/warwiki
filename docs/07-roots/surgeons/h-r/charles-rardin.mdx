## Training and career

<div className="sp-facts">

- **Current position** Professor, Warren Alpert Medical School of Brown University; surgeon, Women & Infants Hospital[^1][^2]
- **Medical school** University of Rochester School of Medicine and Dentistry[^3][^1]
- **Residency** Obstetrics and gynecology, Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center[^3][^1]
- **Fellowship** Urogynecology and pelvic reconstructive surgery, Mount Auburn Hospital and Harvard Medical School, with Neeraj Kohli[^4][^5]
- **Leadership** Director, Brown's Division of Urogynecology and Reconstructive Pelvic Surgery[^1][^2]
- **Leadership** President, American Urogynecologic Society (2017–2018)[^6]

## Contributions

- **Uterine-preserving hysteropexy parallel cohort study** (2017). Co-authored an eight-center comparison of vaginal mesh hysteropexy and laparoscopic sacral hysteropexy that received an AUGS surgical paper award.[^7]
- **SUPeR trial design and surgical supervision** (2019). Contributed to the design and supervision of the multicenter trial comparing mesh hysteropexy with hysterectomy and uterosacral suspension.[^8]
