## Biography

Henry E. Ruiz is a urologist at DHR Health Urology Institute in Edinburg, Texas, and chairs the Department of Urology.[^1] He earned his medical degree from Mount Sinai School of Medicine and completed a urology residency at the University of Miami Jackson Memorial Medical Center.[^1] He completed a fellowship in reconstructive urology and urodynamics at Duke University Medical Center.[^1][^2]
