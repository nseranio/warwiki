## Biography

Karl J. Kreder is a professor of urology at the University of Iowa.[^1][^2] Kreder earned his medical degree at Georgetown University School of Medicine and completed urology residency at Tripler Army Medical Center.[^3] He completed Duke University's reconstructive urology fellowship with George D. Webster in 1989–1990.[^4][^5] Kreder chaired the University of Iowa Department of Urology from 2010 through 2026.[^6]

## Contributions

Kreder's cited publications address management of lower urinary tract dysfunction and incontinence, including sacral neuromodulation, bladder diverticula, duloxetine for overactive bladder, fascia lata sling surgery, and hemi-Kock ileocystoplasty.[^7][^8][^9][^10][^11]
