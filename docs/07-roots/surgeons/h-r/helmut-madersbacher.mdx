## Contributions

- **Madersbacher classification of neurogenic bladder dysfunction** (1990). Described neurogenic lower urinary tract dysfunction by combinations of detrusor and sphincter activity to guide treatment.[^3] Cited on WARWIKI: [Neurogenic Lower Urinary Tract Dysfunction](/docs/clinical-conditions/03d-nlutd/neurogenic-bladder).
- **Intravesical oxybutynin for neurogenic detrusor overactivity** (1991). Reported intravesical oxybutynin treatment in 13 patients with spinal cord injury and detrusor hyperreflexia.[^4]
