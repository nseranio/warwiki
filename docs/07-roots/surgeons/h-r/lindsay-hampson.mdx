## Biography

Lindsay A. Hampson is a reconstructive urologist at UCSF and serves as vice chair of education and residency program director.[^1][^2] She earned her MD at the University of Michigan Medical School and completed general surgery and urology training at UCSF.[^2] She completed a fellowship in genitourinary reconstruction and trauma at the University of Washington under Hunter Wessells and Bryan Voelzke.[^2]

## Contributions

Her confirmed citations cover male urethral stricture management, buried penis repair, and outcomes and quality of life after rectourethral fistula repair. A separate review covers the transition from pediatric to adult urologic care.[^3][^4][^5][^6]
