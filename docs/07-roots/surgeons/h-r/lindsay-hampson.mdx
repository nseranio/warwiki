## Contributions

Hampson's cited work covers buried penis repair, rectourethral fistula repair, urethral stricture management and transitional urology for adults with congenital conditions.[^5][^6][^7][^8] She received the 2023 GURS Rising Star Award and the 2024 American Urological Association Teaching Award.[^2]

- **Buried penis repair with limited panniculectomy and skin grafting** (2017). Led a University of Washington series of 42 men with a 33% 90-day complication rate, associated with body mass index; at a mean 39 months, 85% of those reached would undergo the surgery again.[^5] Cited on WARWIKI: [Buried Penis](/docs/clinical-conditions/03g-genital-scrotal/buried-penis), [Panniculectomy](/docs/surgical-techniques/04e-genital-reconstruction/panniculectomy).
- **Quality of life after transperineal rectourethral fistula repair** (2018). Led a series of 21 men with 95% success after the first repair; at a mean 45.6 months, 80% reported that surgery improved their lives, while perineal pain (53%) and mild urinary leakage were common.[^6] Cited on WARWIKI: [Transperineal Approach to RUF](/docs/surgical-techniques/04h-fistula-repair/male/transperineal-ruf-repair).
- **Male urethral strictures and their management** (2014). Lead author, with Jack McAninch and Benjamin Breyer, of a review reporting long-term success of 20–30% for dilation and internal urethrotomy and 85–90% for urethroplasty.[^7] Cited on WARWIKI: [DVIU and Urethral Dilation](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/dviu).
