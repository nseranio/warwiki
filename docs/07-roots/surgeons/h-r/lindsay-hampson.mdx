## Key Publications

Hampson's cited work covers buried penis repair, rectourethral fistula repair, urethral stricture management and transitional urology for adults with congenital conditions.<sup>[P1](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P2](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P3](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-8)</sup> She received the 2023 GURS Rising Star Award and the 2024 American Urological Association Teaching Award.[^2]

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Buried penis repair with limited panniculectomy and skin grafting** (2017). Led a University of Washington series of 42 men with a 33% 90-day complication rate, associated with body mass index; at a mean 39 months, 85% of those reached would undergo the surgery again.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Quality of life after transperineal rectourethral fistula repair** (2018). Led a series of 21 men with 95% success after the first repair; at a mean 45.6 months, 80% reported that surgery improved their lives, while perineal pain (53%) and mild urinary leakage were common.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[2]} citations={citations}>

**Male urethral strictures and their management** (2014). Lead author, with Jack McAninch and Benjamin Breyer, of a review reporting long-term success of 20–30% for dilation and internal urethrotomy and 85–90% for urethroplasty.

</KeyPublication>
