## Biography

Frank Hinman Jr. served as chief of urology at San Francisco General Hospital.[^1] He earned an MD at Johns Hopkins Medical School and completed a surgical residency in Cincinnati.[^1] He completed urology residency at the University of California after World War II and joined his father's practice.[^1] He helped found the Society of Pediatric Urology and later served as president of the Society of University Urologists.[^1]

## Contributions

His cited papers examine intestinal segment properties for bladder substitution and mechanisms of continence in conduits for continent urinary diversion.[^2][^3]

- **Hinman syndrome** (1973). Described severe bladder and upper-tract effects of dysfunctional voiding in boys without neurologic or anatomic obstruction.[^5]
