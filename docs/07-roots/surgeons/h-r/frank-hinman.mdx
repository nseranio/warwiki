## Biography

Frank Hinman Jr. grew up in a family already closely tied to urology, then made his own career across surgery, research and medical illustration.[^1][^4] After wartime Navy service, he returned to California for urology training, joined his father's practice and became deeply involved in university research and education.[^1][^4] His work on bladder defense mechanisms received prolonged research support and helped shape clinical approaches to urinary infection.[^4]

Hinman also described a form of bladder dysfunction that came to bear his name.[^4] He wrote atlases of urologic and pediatric urologic operations and a volume on surgical anatomy.[^4] He helped found the Society of Pediatric Urology in 1951 and later led the Society of University Urologists.[^4] His obituary records more than 250 scientific articles alongside the books.[^4]

He began painting during quieter periods of wartime service and continued through the end of his life.[^4] His obituary records 150 canvases, one painting published in *Life*, and two used on covers of the *Journal of the American Medical Association*.[^4] He and his wife Marion also loved sailing, and the San Francisco Yacht Club named him Yachtsman of the Year in 1998.[^4]
