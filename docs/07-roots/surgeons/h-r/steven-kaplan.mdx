## Contributions

His cited work examines treatments for benign prostatic hyperplasia and lower urinary tract symptoms, including transurethral electrovaporization, drug-coated balloon therapy, and Aquablation.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

- **MTOPS prostate-volume analysis** (2006). Led an MTOPS analysis relating baseline prostate volume to outcomes with doxazosin and finasteride combination therapy.[^9]
- **Tolterodine–tamsulosin trial for male LUTS and overactive bladder** (2006). Led a randomized trial testing combined antimuscarinic and alpha-blocker treatment in men with lower urinary tract and overactive-bladder symptoms.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia](/docs/clinical-conditions/03b-voiding-outlet/bladder-outlet-obstruction).
- **PINNACLE Optilume BPH trial** (2023). Led a sham-controlled trial of a drug-coated balloon catheter for lower urinary tract symptoms attributed to benign prostatic hyperplasia.[^11] Cited on WARWIKI: [Drug-Coated Balloon Therapy](/docs/surgical-techniques/drug-coated-balloon).
