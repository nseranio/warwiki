## Biography

Steven A. Kaplan directs the Men's Wellness Program at Mount Sinai Health System and is a professor of urology at Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai.[^1] He graduated from Mount Sinai School of Medicine in 1982 and completed a surgery internship and residency at Mount Sinai Hospital.[^1] He completed a urology residency and fellowship at Columbia University, where he worked under Jerry G. Blaivas as an AFUD fellow.[^2][^3]

## Contributions

His cited work examines treatments for benign prostatic hyperplasia and lower urinary tract symptoms, including transurethral electrovaporization, drug-coated balloon therapy, and Aquablation.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
