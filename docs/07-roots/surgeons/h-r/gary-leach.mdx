## Contributions

His cited work covers classification and management of female urethral diverticula, along with bladder neck obstruction and transvaginal bladder neck closure.[^3][^4][^5][^6]

- **Bone-fixed transvaginal bladder-neck suspension** (1988). Described bone fixation for transvaginal needle suspension in women with stress urinary incontinence.[^7]
- **AUA surgical guideline for female stress urinary incontinence** (1997). Chaired the American Urological Association panel assessing surgical treatments for female stress urinary incontinence.[^8]
