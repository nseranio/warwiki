## Biography

Edward J. McGuire (1940–2021) was professor emeritus of urology at the University of Michigan, where he had headed the Section of Urology.[^1] McGuire graduated from Wayne State University Medical School in 1965, completed urologic training at Yale under Bernard Lytton, and was a fellow at the Royal London Hospital under Sir John Blandy.[^1] McGuire was a founding member and former president of the Urodynamics Society.[^1]

## Contributions

McGuire's cited work addresses urodynamic evaluation and ileovesicostomy in neurogenic bladder. It also examines pubovaginal slings and transurethral collagen injections for urinary incontinence.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
