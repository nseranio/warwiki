## Contributions

McGuire's cited work addresses urodynamic evaluation and ileovesicostomy in neurogenic bladder. It also examines pubovaginal slings and transurethral collagen injections for urinary incontinence.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]

- **Autologous fascial pubovaginal sling** (1978). McGuire and Lytton’s report helped popularize the autologous fascial pubovaginal sling for stress incontinence with poor sphincter function.[^7]
- **Leak pressure and upper-tract risk in neurogenic bladder** (1981). His myelodysplasia cohort linked higher bladder leak pressures with vesicoureteral reflux and upper-tract deterioration.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Neurogenic Lower Urinary Tract Dysfunction](/docs/clinical-conditions/03d-nlutd/neurogenic-bladder).
- **Abdominal leak-point pressure assessment of urethral function** (1993). His study used leakage pressure during abdominal stress to assess urethral resistance in women with stress incontinence.[^9]
