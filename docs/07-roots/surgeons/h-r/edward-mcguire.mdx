## Key Publications

McGuire's cited work addresses urodynamic evaluation and ileovesicostomy in neurogenic bladder. It also examines pubovaginal slings and transurethral collagen injections for urinary incontinence.<sup>[P2](#publication-8)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-3)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-6)</sup>

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Autologous fascial pubovaginal sling** (1978). McGuire and Lytton’s report helped popularize the autologous fascial pubovaginal sling for stress incontinence with poor sphincter function.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Leak pressure and upper-tract risk in neurogenic bladder** (1981). His myelodysplasia cohort linked higher bladder leak pressures with vesicoureteral reflux and upper-tract deterioration.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[2]} citations={citations}>

**Abdominal leak-point pressure assessment of urethral function** (1993). His study used leakage pressure during abdominal stress to assess urethral resistance in women with stress incontinence.

</KeyPublication>
