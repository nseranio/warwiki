## Known for

- **Early robotic pelvic floor reconstruction** (2002). Performed an early US robotic sacrocolpopexy and senior-authored a subsequent 77-patient technique and outcomes series.[^2]
- **Direction of the first accredited US FPMRS fellowship**. Directed the Indianapolis fellowship, identified by AUGS as the first accredited urogynecology and reconstructive pelvic surgery program, for ten years.[^3]
