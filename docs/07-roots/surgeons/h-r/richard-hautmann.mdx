## Biography

Richard E. Hautmann is professor of urology and chair emeritus at the University of Ulm in Germany.[^1][^2] Hautmann studied medicine at the University of Würzburg and completed a urology residency at the University of Aachen under Wolfgang Lutzeyer.[^1] In 1974 and 1975, Hautmann worked as a part-time research fellow with Hartmut Osswald.[^1] Hautmann directed Ulm University Hospital's urology department from 1984 to 2009 and served as president of the German Urological Association from 1993 to 1994.[^2][^1]

## Contributions

Hautmann's cited work centers on ileal orthotopic neobladder construction, functional results, and long-term complications. Related papers and chapters examine urinary diversion, ureteroenteric stricture risk, and secondary diversion.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
