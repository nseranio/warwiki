## Training and career

<div className="sp-facts">

- **Current position** Professor of urology and chair emeritus, University of Ulm, Germany[^1][^2]
- **Medical school** University of Würzburg[^1]
- **Residency** Urology, University of Aachen, under Wolfgang Lutzeyer[^1]
- **Fellowship** Part-time research, with Hartmut Osswald (1974–1975)[^1]
- **Leadership** Director, Ulm University Hospital urology department (1984–2009); president, German Urological Association (1993–1994)[^2][^1]

## Contributions

Hautmann's cited work centers on ileal orthotopic neobladder construction, functional results, and long-term complications. Related papers and chapters examine urinary diversion, ureteroenteric stricture risk, and secondary diversion.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
