## Contributions

Her cited work on pelvic reconstructive surgery includes sacrocolpopexy with mesh or autologous fascia lata and assessment of fascia lata harvest morbidity. Other confirmed papers address urethral diverticulum repair, vesicouterine fistula repair, and sling outcomes in stress urinary incontinence.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
