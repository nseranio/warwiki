## Contributions

- **Posterior prolapse and masked stress incontinence** (1998). Her prospective study found that severe posterior wall defects can mask stress urinary incontinence during testing.[^4]
- **Rectocele graft augmentation randomized trial** (2012). Senior-authored a randomized trial finding no 12-month advantage from porcine subintestinal submucosal graft augmentation of rectocele repair.[^5]
