## Biography

Maxim J. McKibben is an assistant professor of urology at Atrium Health McKay Urology.[^1] He received his medical degree at Case Western Reserve University and completed his urology residency at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.[^1][^2] He completed a urological surgery fellowship at the University of Texas Southwestern Medical Center.[^2]

## Contributions

His cited papers cover reconstruction of complex urethral strictures, including perineal urethrostomy, and evaluation of male incontinence surgery using artificial urinary sphincters and transobturator slings.[^3][^4][^5][^6]
