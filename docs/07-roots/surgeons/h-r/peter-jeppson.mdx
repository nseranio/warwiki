## Known for

- **Urogynecology telemedicine guidance** (2020). Coauthored an evidence review and telemedicine guidance for urogynecologic care during the COVID-19 pandemic.[^9]
- **IUGA consultation on lifestyle measures for prolapse** (2025). Co-chaired an IUGA consultation group reviewing lifestyle changes for pelvic organ prolapse prevention and treatment.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Lifestyle Modifications for Pelvic Organ Prolapse](/docs/surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/lifestyle-modifications).

## Contributions

His cited reviews address female pelvic fistula repair, pelvic organ prolapse management, and urinary incontinence. They include a vesicovaginal fistula systematic review and an IUGA committee opinion on mixed urinary incontinence.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
