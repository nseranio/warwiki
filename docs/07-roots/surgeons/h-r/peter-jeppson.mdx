## Biography

Peter C. Jeppson is a female pelvic medicine and reconstructive surgeon at The Woman's Center in Mesa, Arizona, and an associate professor with appointments at the University of Arizona College of Medicine in Phoenix and the University of New Mexico.[^1] He earned his medical degree at Saint Louis University School of Medicine and completed an obstetrics and gynecology residency at Cleveland Clinic and Case Western Reserve University MetroHealth Medical Center.[^1] He completed a female pelvic medicine and reconstructive surgery fellowship at Brown University's Warren Alpert Medical School and Women & Infants Hospital, where B. Star Hampton was a faculty mentor.[^2][^3]

## Contributions

His cited reviews address female pelvic fistula repair, pelvic organ prolapse management, and urinary incontinence. They include a vesicovaginal fistula systematic review and an IUGA committee opinion on mixed urinary incontinence.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
