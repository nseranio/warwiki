## Biography

Hiren Patel is a clinical assistant professor of urology and a reconstructive urologist at The Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center.[^1][^2] He earned his MD and PhD from SUNY Stony Brook School of Medicine.[^2] He completed urology residency at Rutgers Robert Wood Johnson Hospital.[^2] He completed a male genitourinary reconstruction and trauma fellowship at UCSF under Benjamin N. Breyer.[^2][^3]
