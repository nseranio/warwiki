## Biography

Bradley A. Erickson is a professor of urology at the University of Iowa and director of its Division of Functional Urology.[^1][^2] He earned his medical degree at the University of Iowa Carver College of Medicine and completed urology and general surgery residencies at Northwestern University's McGaw Medical Center.[^3][^2] He completed a trauma and reconstructive urology fellowship at the University of California, San Francisco, with Jack W. McAninch.[^2][^4] He was a founding member and past president of the Trauma Urologic Reconstructive Network Surgeons.[^2]

## Contributions

Erickson's cited work examines male urethral stricture reconstruction, including graft and flap techniques, stricture staging, and functional outcomes after urethroplasty.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]
