## Contributions

Erickson's cited work examines male urethral stricture reconstruction, including graft and flap techniques, stricture staging, and functional outcomes after urethroplasty.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]

- **Uroflowmetry for detecting recurrent stricture** (2011). Studied change in maximum urinary flow rate as a noninvasive screen for stricture recurrence after urethroplasty.[^10]
- **LSE anterior urethral stricture classification** (2020). Led development and validation of a system classifying anterior urethral strictures by length, segment, and etiology.[^11] Cited on WARWIKI: [Male Urethral Stricture](/docs/clinical-conditions/03b-voiding-outlet/urethral-stricture).
