## Biography

James R. Furr is an associate professor of urology at the University of Oklahoma College of Medicine and director of male reconstruction and urologic trauma.[^1] He earned his medical degree and completed urology residency at the University of Oklahoma College of Medicine.[^2] He completed a male reconstructive urology fellowship at the University of California, Irvine, with Joel Gelman.[^2][^3]

## Contributions

His cited studies examine outcomes after staged and bulbar urethroplasty, including stricture recurrence and patient-reported urinary and sexual outcomes. Other cited work addresses complications of genital enlargement surgery and inpatient management of Fournier gangrene.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
