## Known for

- **Latissimus dorsi flap total phalloplasty** (2007). Co-reported total phalloplasty using a free musculocutaneous latissimus dorsi flap, with subsequent urethroplasty and prosthesis options.[^9]
- **Failed-hypospadias reconstruction series** (2010). Co-reported a two-center series of 1,176 patients treated for urethral or penile complications after hypospadias repair.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Hypospadias & Epispadias](/docs/special-populations/05f-lifelong-care/hypospadias-epispadias).

## Contributions

His cited papers cover penile reconstructive surgery, including Peyronie's procedures, prosthesis complications, and girth enhancement, as well as anterior urethral reconstruction and failed hypospadias repair.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
