## Biography

Billy H. Cordon-Galiano is director of Urologic Reconstruction, Trauma, and Prosthetics at Mount Sinai Medical Center in Miami Beach.[^1] He received his medical degree from the University of California, San Francisco School of Medicine and completed his urology residency at Lenox Hill Hospital in New York City.[^1] He completed a fellowship at the University of Texas Southwestern Medical Center with Allen Morey.[^1][^2]
