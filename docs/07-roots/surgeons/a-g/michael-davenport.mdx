## Biography

Michael T. Davenport is a urologist with Colorado Urology in Westminster, Colorado.[^1] He earned his medical degree from the University of Washington School of Medicine.[^1] He completed a urology residency at Stanford University School of Medicine.[^1] He then trained in reconstructive and prosthetic urology at UT Southwestern Medical Center in Dallas with Allen Morey.[^1]

## Contributions

His cited studies cover urethral reconstruction and penile prosthesis surgery, including radiation-induced strictures, synchronous urethral repair, and distal cylinder tip extrusion.[^2][^3][^4]
