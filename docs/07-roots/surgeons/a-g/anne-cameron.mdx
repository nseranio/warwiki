## Contributions

Her cited work covers adult neurogenic lower urinary tract dysfunction, including AUA/SUFU guidance on diagnosis and treatment, oral therapy, and intermittent catheterization in multiple sclerosis.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]

- **National study of bladder management after spinal cord injury** (2010). Cameron analyzed decades of national spinal cord injury data, documenting changes in catheter use and limited persistence with intermittent catheterization.[^7]
- **2024 AUA/SUFU idiopathic overactive bladder guideline** (2024). Cameron chaired and led the published AUA/SUFU guideline on evaluation and treatment of idiopathic overactive bladder.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Incontinence](/docs/surgical-techniques/incontinence).
