## Biography

Christopher M. Dixon, MD, is a clinical instructor in urology at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai.[^1] UCSF also lists Dixon as an attending physician at Good Samaritan Hospital in Suffern, New York.[^2] Dixon received a medical degree from the Medical College of Wisconsin in 1984.[^3] Dixon trained in UCSF's male genitourinary reconstruction and trauma fellowship under Jack W. McAninch.[^2][^4]

## Contributions

Dixon's cited work on urethral reconstruction covers surgical anatomy and MRI evaluation of traumatic posterior urethral injuries.[^5][^6][^7]
