## Biography

William Rutherfoord Boysen, MD, is a reconstructive urologist with Mass General Brigham and an assistant professor of surgery at Harvard Medical School.[^1] He earned his medical degree from Columbia University Vagelos College of Physicians and Surgeons and completed a urology residency at the University of Chicago Medical Center.[^1] He completed a complex genitourinary reconstruction and cancer survivorship fellowship at Duke University Medical Center.[^1] He serves as assistant program director of the Brigham and Women's Urology Residency Training Program.[^1]

## Contributions

In related cited papers, Boysen examined urinary-pubic symphysis fistula in prostate cancer survivors through histopathology of resected pubic bone and extirpative bone cultures.[^2][^3]
