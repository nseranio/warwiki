## Biography

Eric S. Glen is a retired urologist and founding member of the International Continence Society.[^1] Glen collaborated with David Rowan on a specialist urodynamic referral service at Southern General Hospital in Glasgow.[^2] Glen served as honorary secretary of the International Continence Society from 1971 to 1985.[^3]
