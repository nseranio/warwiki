## Contributions

Eun's cited work includes robotic reconstruction of ureteral strictures and injuries, fluorescence guidance during ureteral surgery, and transvesical reconstruction for vesicourethral anastomotic stenosis.[^2][^3][^4][^5]

- **Indocyanine green in ureteral reconstruction** (2015). Coauthored a report on indocyanine-green imaging during robot-assisted ureteral reconstruction.[^2] Cited on WARWIKI: [Indocyanine Green (ICG)](/docs/foundations/pharmacology/intraoperative-adjuncts/visualization-agents/icg).
- **Robotic buccal mucosa graft ureteroplasty** (2017). Coauthored a series on buccal mucosa graft repair of complex ureteral strictures.[^3] Cited on WARWIKI: [Buccal Mucosa Graft Ureteroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/bmg-onlay-ureter).
- **Robotic appendiceal interposition** (2018). Coauthored a technical report combining appendiceal interposition, calycostomy, downward nephropexy, and psoas hitch for a near-complete ureteral avulsion.[^4] Cited on WARWIKI: [Downward Nephropexy](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/downward-nephropexy).
- **Transvesical bladder neck reconstruction** (2025). Coauthored a report of robotic transvesical reconstruction for vesicourethral anastomotic stenosis.[^5] Cited on WARWIKI: [Transvesical Approaches](/docs/surgical-techniques/04ab-bladder-neck-reconstruction/bnc-vuas/transvesical-approaches).
