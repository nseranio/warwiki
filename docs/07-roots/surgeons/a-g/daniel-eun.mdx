## Biography

Daniel D. Eun is a clinical professor of urology at Thomas Jefferson University and directs its International Urology Program Development and Advanced Robotic Urologic Oncology and Reconstructive Surgery fellowship.[^1] Eun earned his medical degree at the Lewis Katz School of Medicine at Temple University.[^1] He completed a urology residency and robotic urologic oncology fellowship at Henry Ford Hospital's Vattikuti Urology Institute, where he trained under Mani Menon.[^1][^2]

## Contributions

Eun's cited papers address robotic urinary tract surgery, including transvesical bladder neck reconstruction for vesicourethral anastomotic stenosis, buccal mucosa graft and appendiceal ureter reconstruction, and simple prostatectomy after prior endoscopic treatment for benign prostatic hyperplasia.[^3][^4][^5][^6]
