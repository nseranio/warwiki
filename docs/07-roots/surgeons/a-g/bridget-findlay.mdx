## Biography

Bridget L. Findlay is a urologic surgeon specializing in genitourinary trauma and reconstruction at Mayo Clinic in Phoenix, Arizona.[^1] She earned her M.D. at Rutgers Robert Wood Johnson Medical School and completed her urology residency at Mayo Clinic in Rochester, Minnesota.[^1] She completed a genitourinary trauma and reconstruction fellowship at Mayo Clinic in Rochester, where Boyd R. Viers was program director.[^1][^2]

## Contributions

Her cited work covers surgical reconstruction of posterior, bulbar, and penile urethral strictures.[^3][^4][^5]
