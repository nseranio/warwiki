## Biography

Kevin J. Flynn practices at ThedaCare Urology in Neenah, Wisconsin, with a focus on genitourinary reconstructive surgery.[^1][^2] He attended Indiana University School of Medicine and completed urology residency at the University of Iowa in 2021.[^2][^3] He completed a genitourinary reconstruction fellowship at the University of Iowa Hospitals and Clinics in 2022.[^1][^2]

## Contributions

Flynn's cited work covers classification and surgical management of adult acquired buried penis and reconstruction of genital wound defects after Fournier's gangrene.[^4][^5][^6][^7]
