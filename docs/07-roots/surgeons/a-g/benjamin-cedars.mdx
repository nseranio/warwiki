## Biography

Benjamin E. Cedars, MD, is an attending physician at Montefiore Einstein and an assistant professor of urology at Albert Einstein College of Medicine.[^1] He earned his medical degree at Sidney Kimmel Medical College and completed residency at the University of California, San Diego.[^1] He completed a genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship at SUNY Upstate Medical University in 2025, with Dmitriy Nikolavsky as program director.[^1][^2]

## Contributions

Cedars's cited work describes buccal mucosal graft urethroplasty for posterior and distal urethral strictures and the use of a lighted urethral speculum in female urethroplasty.[^3][^4][^5]
