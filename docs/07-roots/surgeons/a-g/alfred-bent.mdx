## Biography

Alfred E. Bent was a professor of obstetrics and gynecology at Dalhousie University and head of its gynecology division as of 2010.[^1] Bent graduated from Dalhousie medical school in 1973 and returned to Halifax for residency after practicing family medicine in Wolfville.[^1] Bent completed a one-year urogynecology fellowship in Long Beach, California, in 1981–1982.[^1] Bent served as president of the American Urogynecologic Society in 1994–1995.[^2]
