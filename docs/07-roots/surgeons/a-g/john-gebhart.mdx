## Training and career

<div className="sp-facts">

- **Current position** Professor of obstetrics and gynecology, Mayo Clinic, Rochester, Minnesota; Division of Urogynecology and Reconstructive Surgery.[^1][^2]

## Known for

- **Surgical management of the constricted or obliterated vagina** (2016). Published a surgical review addressing evaluation and reconstructive options for vaginal constriction and obliteration.[^7] Cited on WARWIKI: [V-Y Advancement Flap](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps/v-y-advancement).

## Contributions

His cited work covers reconstruction for vaginal agenesis and constricted or obliterated vagina, including skin-graft neovagina creation, vaginal advancement, and Z-plasty.[^4][^5][^6]
