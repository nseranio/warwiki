## Biography

Elizabeth J. Anoia-Loftus, MD, practices urology with MidLantic Urology in Huntingdon Valley, Pennsylvania.[^1] She earned her medical degree from Georgetown University School of Medicine and completed a urology residency at Case Western Reserve University.[^1] She completed a reconstructive and female urology fellowship at Duke University with George D. Webster.[^2][^3]
