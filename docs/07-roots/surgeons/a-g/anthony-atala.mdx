## Biography

Anthony Atala is the G. Link Professor and director of the Wake Forest Institute for Regenerative Medicine at Wake Forest University School of Medicine.[^1] He received his MD from the University of Louisville School of Medicine and completed a surgery and urology residency at the University of Louisville.[^1] He completed a clinical and research urology fellowship at Children's Hospital of Boston in 1992; Alan Retik directed the fellowship program.[^1][^2] He is a founding member of the Tissue Engineering Society.[^1]

## Contributions

Atala's cited publications cover tissue-engineered bladder and urethral reconstruction, including autologous cell-seeded grafts, acellular matrices, and urethral substitution. Other cited papers cover chondrocyte injection for vesicoureteral reflux and preclinical work on engineered urethral stents and testosterone-releasing testicular prostheses.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
