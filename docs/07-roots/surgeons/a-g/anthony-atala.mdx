## Key Publications

Atala's cited publications cover tissue-engineered bladder and urethral reconstruction, including autologous cell-seeded grafts, acellular matrices, and urethral substitution. Other cited papers cover chondrocyte injection for vesicoureteral reflux and preclinical work on engineered urethral stents and testosterone-releasing testicular prostheses.<sup>[P2](#publication-9)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-7)</sup>
