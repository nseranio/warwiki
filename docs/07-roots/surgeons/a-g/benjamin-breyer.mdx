## Biography

Benjamin N. Breyer is chair and professor of urology and chief of genitourinary reconstructive surgery at UCSF Health.[^1] He earned his MD at the University of Chicago Pritzker School of Medicine and completed a urology residency at UCSF.[^2] He completed a fellowship in complex male genitourinary reconstruction and trauma surgery under Jack W. McAninch at UCSF.[^1]

## Contributions

His cited work addresses urethral stricture reconstruction and grafting for male genital repair. He also coauthored guidelines on incontinence after prostate treatment.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
