## Contributions

His cited work addresses urethral stricture reconstruction and grafting for male genital repair. He also coauthored guidelines on incontinence after prostate treatment.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Male urethral stricture management review** (2014). Breyer was senior author of a clinical review comparing endoscopic treatment and urethroplasty for male urethral strictures.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [DVIU and Urethral Dilation](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/dviu).
- **Patient-centered measurement of urethral stricture outcomes** (2017). Breyer led qualitative research identifying symptoms and effects that patients considered important for a urethral stricture outcome measure.[^9]
