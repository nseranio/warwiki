## Training and career

<div className="sp-facts">

- **Current position** Director of Women and Children's Services and the Center for Urogynecology and Pelvic Health, Holy Cross Health[^1]
- **Medical school** University of Texas Medical School at Houston[^1]
- **Residency** Obstetrics and gynecology, University of Colorado Health Sciences Center[^1]
- **Other training** Gynecologic urology clinical preceptorship, Long Beach Memorial Hospital and University of California, Irvine, with Donald Ostergard (1989)[^2]
- **Leadership** President, International Urogynecological Association (2013–2014)[^3]

## Contributions

- **IUGA guidance on vaginal graft patient selection** (2012). First-authored the second IUGA Grafts Roundtable consensus on selecting patients for vaginal graft use in prolapse surgery.[^4]
