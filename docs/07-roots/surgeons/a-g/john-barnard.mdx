## Biography

John T. Barnard II is an associate professor of urology and chief of the Division of Reconstructive Urology at West Virginia University, where he also serves as associate program director of the urology residency.[^1] He earned his medical degree at West Virginia University School of Medicine and completed his urology residency at WVU Hospitals.[^1] He completed the Eric S. Wisenbaugh Fellowship in Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgery and Prosthetics at the University of California, Irvine.[^2]
