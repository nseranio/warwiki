## Contributions

His confirmed citations address pelvic floor disorders and reconstructive surgery, including sacrocolpopexy with Burch colposuspension, cystourethroscopy, sexual function, and uterosacral ligament anatomy.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

- **PESSRI ring-versus-Gellhorn pessary trial** (2007). Led a randomized crossover trial comparing symptom relief and life impact with ring and Gellhorn pessaries.[^9]
