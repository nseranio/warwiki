## Biography

Philip J. Cheng is a urologist at RMA New Jersey whose practice includes male infertility care.[^1] He earned his medical degree at Baylor College of Medicine.[^1] He completed a general surgery internship and urology residency at Brigham and Women’s Hospital in Boston.[^1] He completed a genitourinary reconstruction and andrology fellowship at the University of Utah under James Hotaling and Jeremy Myers.[^1][^2]

## Contributions

His cited work addresses augmentation cystoplasty for neurogenic bladder and outcomes of continent urinary reservoirs, including ileocecocystoplasty and right colon pouches.[^3][^4][^5][^6]
