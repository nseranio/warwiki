## Biography

Uzoma A. Anele is an associate professor at the University of Louisville School of Medicine and a urologist with UofL Health.[^1][^2] He earned his medical degree at the University of Maryland and completed urology residency at Virginia Commonwealth University.[^3][^4] He completed an NIH-funded urologic research fellowship at Johns Hopkins and a male genitourinary reconstruction and prosthetic surgery fellowship at Cleveland Clinic, finishing the latter in 2021.[^3][^4] At the University of Louisville, he serves as division chief of adult urology, director of genitourinary reconstructive surgery, and urology residency program director.[^5][^1]
