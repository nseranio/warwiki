## Known for

- **Randomized comparison of prolapse operations** (1996). Led a randomized study comparing vaginal and abdominal reconstruction for advanced pelvic support defects.[^3]
- **Pudendal neuralgia clinical series** (2005). Described clinical findings and treatment outcomes in a 64-patient pudendal neuralgia series.[^4]
