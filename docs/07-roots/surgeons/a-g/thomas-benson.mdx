## Biography

J. Thomas Benson (1934–2008) practiced urogynecology at Methodist Hospital in Indianapolis and was a professor at Indiana University School of Medicine.[^1] Benson earned a medical degree from the University of Cincinnati in 1959 and completed residency at Methodist Hospital and Indiana University Medical Center in Indianapolis.[^1] Benson completed a fellowship in clinical neurophysiology in 1995.[^1] Benson served as president of the American Urogynecologic Society in 1991–1992.[^2]
