## Biography

Devang J. Desai practices urology in Toowoomba, operates at St Andrew's Toowoomba Hospital, and is an associate professor at the University of Queensland, Griffith University, and the University of Southern Queensland.[^1][^2] He received his medical degree from the University of Mumbai and completed specialist urology training in Australia.[^3] Desai undertook a GURS and AUA approved fellowship in reconstructive urology.[^4] In a 2025 announcement, USANZ listed Devang Desai as leader of its Paediatric and Reconstructive Specialty Advisory Group.[^5]

## Contributions

Desai's cited work covers urethroplasty, including oral mucosal graft harvesting and donor site outcomes, female double-face and bulbar flap approaches, penile retractor use, and pelvic fracture urethral injury reconstruction and imaging.[^6][^7][^8][^9][^10]
