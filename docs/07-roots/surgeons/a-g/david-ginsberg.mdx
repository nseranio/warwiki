## Biography

David Alan Ginsberg is a professor of clinical urology at the Keck School of Medicine of USC and chief of urology at Rancho Los Amigos National Rehabilitation Center.[^1] He completed medical school and a urology residency at the Keck School of Medicine of USC and its affiliated hospital, Los Angeles County Medical Center.[^1] He completed a fellowship at UCLA focused on female urology, voiding dysfunction, and urologic reconstruction.[^1]

## Contributions

His cited work includes the AUA/SUFU guideline papers on diagnosis, evaluation, treatment, and follow-up of adult neurogenic lower urinary tract dysfunction, a trial of onabotulinumtoxinA for neurogenic detrusor overactivity, and a long-term study of urologic care after spinal cord injury. Other cited work addresses urinary diversion and bladder neck closure.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
