## Key Publications

His cited work includes the AUA/SUFU guideline papers on diagnosis, evaluation, treatment, and follow-up of adult neurogenic lower urinary tract dysfunction, a trial of onabotulinumtoxinA for neurogenic detrusor overactivity, and a long-term study of urologic care after spinal cord injury. Other cited work addresses urinary diversion and bladder neck closure.<sup>[P2](#publication-8)</sup><sup>[P3](#publication-3)</sup><sup>[P1](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-6)</sup>
