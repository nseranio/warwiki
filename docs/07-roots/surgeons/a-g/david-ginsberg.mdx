## Known for

- **Phase 3 onabotulinumtoxinA trial for neurogenic detrusor overactivity** (2012). Ginsberg led a phase 3 trial of intradetrusor onabotulinumtoxinA for urinary incontinence caused by neurogenic detrusor overactivity.[^7] Cited on WARWIKI: [Intradetrusor OnabotulinumtoxinA](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/intradetrusor-botox).
- **AUA/SUFU adult neurogenic lower urinary tract dysfunction guideline** (2021). Ginsberg chaired the AUA/SUFU guideline panel addressing evaluation, risk stratification, treatment and follow-up of adults with neurogenic lower urinary tract dysfunction.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Principles of Bladder Augmentation](/docs/surgical-techniques/04b-bladder-reconstruction/augmentation-principles).

## Contributions

His cited work includes the AUA/SUFU guideline papers on diagnosis, evaluation, treatment, and follow-up of adult neurogenic lower urinary tract dysfunction, a trial of onabotulinumtoxinA for neurogenic detrusor overactivity, and a long-term study of urologic care after spinal cord injury. Other cited work addresses urinary diversion and bladder neck closure.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
