## Biography

André Guilherme Cavalcanti is an adjunct professor of urology at the Federal University of the State of Rio de Janeiro (UNIRIO).[^1][^2] Cavalcanti graduated in medicine from the Federal University of Pernambuco and completed urology residency at Rio de Janeiro State University.[^1] In 2002, Cavalcanti undertook postdoctoral fellowship training in trauma and reconstructive urology with Jack McAninch at the University of California, San Francisco.[^1][^3] Cavalcanti is president-elect of the Society of Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgeons.[^2]
