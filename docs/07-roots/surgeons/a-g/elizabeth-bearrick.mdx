## Biography

Elizabeth N. Bearrick is a reconstructive urologist at Mayo Clinic in Florida.[^1] She received her medical degree from the University of Minnesota and completed urology residency at Mayo Clinic in Rochester, Minnesota.[^1] She completed a trauma and genitourinary reconstruction fellowship at SUNY Upstate in 2024 under program director Dmitriy Nikolavsky.[^1][^2]

## Contributions

Her cited work covers urethral and ureteral reconstruction, including robotic posterior urethral repair, ureteroenteric stricture repair, and female and distal urethroplasty techniques.[^3][^4][^5][^6]
