## Known for

- **First-stage urethroplasty series** (2008). Reported outcomes and technique modifications for first-stage urethroplasty in complex anterior urethral stricture.[^9]
- **ROBUST III drug-coated balloon trial** (2022). Led the randomized ROBUST III report comparing an Optilume drug-coated balloon with endoscopic treatment for recurrent anterior urethral stricture.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Drug-Coated Balloon Therapy](/docs/surgical-techniques/drug-coated-balloon).

## Contributions

His cited work covers urethral stricture management, including the ROBUST III drug-coated-balloon study, and continent catheterizable channels and bladder augmentation.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
