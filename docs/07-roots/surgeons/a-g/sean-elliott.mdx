## Biography

Sean P. Elliott is a professor and director of reconstructive urology at the University of Minnesota.[^1][^2] He earned his MD at Baylor College of Medicine and completed a urology residency at UCSF.[^2] He completed a genitourinary trauma and reconstruction fellowship with Jack W. McAninch at UCSF.[^1] He is immediate past president of the Society of Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgeons.[^3]

## Contributions

His cited work covers urethral stricture management, including the ROBUST III drug-coated-balloon study, and continent catheterizable channels and bladder augmentation.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
