## Biography

Joceline S. Fuchs is a reconstructive urologist at MidLantic Urology in Pennsylvania.[^1][^2] She earned her medical degree from Baylor College of Medicine and completed a urology residency at McGaw Medical Center at Northwestern University.[^1] She completed a fellowship in urologic trauma, reconstruction, and prosthetics at the University of Texas Southwestern in Dallas with Allen F. Morey.[^1]

## Contributions

Fuchs's cited studies cover complex urethral reconstruction, including perineal urethrostomy, repair of traumatic and radiation-induced strictures, and rectourinary fistula repair. Her other cited work examines surgical management of Peyronie's disease and priapism and approaches to male incontinence and prosthetic surgery.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
