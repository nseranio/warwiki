## Biography

Lahey Clinic lists M. Ryan D. Farrell, MD, among its reconstructive urology fellowship graduates, with a location at Ohio Health Grant Medical Center in Columbus, Ohio.[^1] Farrell completed Rush University's urology residency as a member of its class of 2020.[^2] Farrell completed Lahey Clinic's reconstructive urologic surgery fellowship in 2020–2021; Alex J. Vanni is listed as its program director.[^3]

## Contributions

Farrell's cited work addresses recurrent urethral strictures treated with internal urethrotomy and mitomycin C, as well as dorsal inlay buccal mucosa graft reconstruction of fossa navicularis strictures. Other cited papers cover graft materials and penile prostheses in Peyronie's disease and plication for congenital penile curvature.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
