## Biography

Craig V. Comiter is a professor of urology and, by courtesy, obstetrics and gynecology at Stanford University.[^1] He is the clinic chief for Urologic Specialties and director of Stanford's Program in Female Pelvic Medicine and Reconstructive Surgery.[^1] He earned his medical degree at Harvard Medical School, completed a Harvard urology residency, and undertook a fellowship at UCLA.[^1]

## Contributions

His cited work covers surgical treatment of incontinence after prostate treatment, including selection of male continence procedures, the Virtue sling, and the AUA/SUFU guideline. Other cited papers examine urethral bulking for female stress urinary incontinence and sacral neuromodulation placement strategies.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
