## Known for

- **Virtue quadratic male sling** (2012). Co-described a four-arm male sling combining urethral relocation and compression for postprostatectomy stress incontinence.[^7] Cited on WARWIKI: [Virtue Quadratic Male Sling](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/virtue-sling).
- **Virtue sling fixation trial** (2014). Led a multinational trial comparing Virtue sling outcomes before and after addition of a fixation technique.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Virtue Quadratic Male Sling](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/virtue-sling).

## Contributions

His cited work covers surgical treatment of incontinence after prostate treatment, including selection of male continence procedures, the Virtue sling, and the AUA/SUFU guideline. Other cited papers examine urethral bulking for female stress urinary incontinence and sacral neuromodulation placement strategies.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
