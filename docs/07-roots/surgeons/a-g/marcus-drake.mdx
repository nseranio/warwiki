## Biography

Marcus J. Drake is professor of neurological urology at Imperial College London and an honorary consultant urologist at Charing Cross Hospital.[^1] He undertook medical training at the Universities of Cambridge and Oxford and earned his BM BCh at Oxford in 1991.[^1][^2] He is a trustee of the International Continence Society.[^2]

## Contributions

His cited work covers urodynamic practice and assessment of lower urinary tract dysfunction, including detrusor underactivity and urodynamics before prostate surgery.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
