## Contributions

His cited work covers urodynamic practice and assessment of lower urinary tract dysfunction, including detrusor underactivity and urodynamics before prostate surgery.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **UPSTREAM trial** (2020). Led a 26-hospital randomized trial evaluating routine invasive urodynamics before surgery for male lower urinary tract symptoms.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia](/docs/clinical-conditions/03b-voiding-outlet/bladder-outlet-obstruction).
