## Known for

- **Comparative urine-marker study in interstitial cystitis** (2002). Erickson compared multiple urine markers in patients with interstitial cystitis and controls, identifying antiproliferative factor as a diagnostic candidate.[^4]
- **Assessment of NIDDK cystoscopic criteria for interstitial cystitis** (2005). Her study found that patients meeting the NIDDK cystoscopic criteria did not form a distinct group by urine markers or biopsy findings.[^5]

## Contributions

Erickson coauthored the 2022 AUA guideline summary on diagnosis and treatment of interstitial cystitis/bladder pain syndrome.[^3]
