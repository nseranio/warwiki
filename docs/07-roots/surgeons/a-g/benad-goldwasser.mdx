## Biography

Benad Goldwasser chairs the board of Odysight.ai and is a retired urologic surgeon.[^1][^2] Goldwasser earned an MD from Tel Aviv University and completed urology residency at Chaim Sheba Medical Center in 1984.[^2][^3] Goldwasser spent one postgraduate fellowship year at Duke University Medical Center and another at Mayo Clinic between 1984 and 1986.[^3] Goldwasser previously chaired urology at Chaim Sheba Medical Center and was a professor of surgery at Tel Aviv University.[^2]

## Contributions

Goldwasser's confirmed papers examine urodynamic properties after bladder reconstruction using bowel, including ileal and colonic augmentation or replacement and the Camey neobladder.[^4][^5]
