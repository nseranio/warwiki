## Key Publications

Her research covers recovery of pelvic floor function after childbirth, pelvic floor injury from childbirth, and surgical education.[^1] She coauthored the Fundamentals of Vaginal Surgery pilot study, which developed and validated a simulation system and proficiency score for basic vaginal surgical skills.<sup>[P4](#publication-3)</sup>

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Colpopexy and colporrhaphy at hysterectomy for prolapse** (2016). Led a Michigan statewide database study of 1557 hysterectomies with prolapse as an indication; 43.1% were hysterectomy alone and 24.1% included a colpopexy, which was more likely when the surgeon specialized in urogynecology (odds ratio 8.2).

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Normal pelvic floor recovery after high-risk vaginal birth** (2020). Led a prospective cohort of 82 women after vaginal births with risk factors for pelvic floor injury and 30 after cesarean; using posterior wall support, Kegel force and levator ani tear on imaging, 46.4% met criteria for pelvic floor injury at 6 weeks and 29.6% at 6 months.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[2]} citations={citations}>

**Surgery in a postpartum pelvic floor clinic** (2020). Coauthored a review of 1138 patients seen in the Michigan Healthy Healing After Delivery clinic; 9.1% had surgery, most often anal sphincteroplasty, perineal laceration revision or rectovaginal fistula repair.

</KeyPublication>
