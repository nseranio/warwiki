## Biography

Curtis N. Crane is chief executive officer and chief medical officer of the Crane Center for Transgender Surgery in Austin, Texas.[^1][^2] Crane attended the University of Iowa College of Medicine and completed a six-year urology residency at Dartmouth Hitchcock in Lebanon, New Hampshire.[^2] Crane completed a trauma and reconstructive urology fellowship at Detroit Receiving Hospital with Richard Santucci, followed by a plastic and reconstructive surgery residency at the University of Texas Health Science Center in San Antonio.[^2] Crane also completed a fellowship in transgender surgery in Europe.[^2]

## Contributions

Crane's cited papers examine phalloplasty outcomes with anterolateral thigh versus radial forearm flaps and the use of a vascularized bulbospongiosus flap during urethral lengthening.[^3][^4]
