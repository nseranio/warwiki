## Contributions

Chung's cited work covers pelvic fracture urethral injury, radiation-induced urethral stricture, patient-reported outcomes after urethroplasty and penile plication.[^5][^6][^7][^8]

- **Stress incontinence after posterior urethroplasty for radiation-induced stricture** (2018). Led a review in which de novo stress incontinence followed excision and primary anastomosis in 33% of 36 men with radiation-induced strictures and an intact bladder neck, against 12% of 33 men with pelvic fracture urethral injury; prostatic urethral involvement increased the risk.[^5] Cited on WARWIKI: [Posterior Urethral Stenosis](/docs/clinical-conditions/03b-voiding-outlet/posterior-urethral-stenosis).
- **Early endoscopic realignment after pelvic fracture urethral injury** (2018). Led an updated Level 1 trauma center series of 32 men in which early realignment failed in 91% by intention to treat, while excision and primary anastomosis succeeded in 96% of 24 men.[^6] Cited on WARWIKI: [Primary Endoscopic Realignment (PER)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/primary-endoscopic-realignment).
- **Dorsal plication without degloving** (2014). Led a review of 118 men treated through a 2 cm shaft incision without degloving; dorsal, ventral and lateral plication gave similar correction and satisfaction, with mean length loss of 0.3–0.8 cm.[^7] Cited on WARWIKI: [Tunica Plication](/docs/surgical-techniques/04j-sexual-dysfunction/peyronies-disease/tunica-plication).
