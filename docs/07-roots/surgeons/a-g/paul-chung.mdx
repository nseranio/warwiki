## Key Publications

Chung's cited work covers pelvic fracture urethral injury, radiation-induced urethral stricture, patient-reported outcomes after urethroplasty and penile plication.<sup>[P1](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P2](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P3](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-8)</sup>

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Stress incontinence after posterior urethroplasty for radiation-induced stricture** (2018). Led a review in which de novo stress incontinence followed excision and primary anastomosis in 33% of 36 men with radiation-induced strictures and an intact bladder neck, against 12% of 33 men with pelvic fracture urethral injury; prostatic urethral involvement increased the risk.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Early endoscopic realignment after pelvic fracture urethral injury** (2018). Led an updated Level 1 trauma center series of 32 men in which early realignment failed in 91% by intention to treat, while excision and primary anastomosis succeeded in 96% of 24 men.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[2]} citations={citations}>

**Dorsal plication without degloving** (2014). Led a review of 118 men treated through a 2 cm shaft incision without degloving; dorsal, ventral and lateral plication gave similar correction and satisfaction, with mean length loss of 0.3–0.8 cm.

</KeyPublication>
