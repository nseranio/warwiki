## Biography

Paul H. Chung is an associate professor and interim chair of urology at Thomas Jefferson University, where he directs reconstructive urology and co-directs the Men's Health Program.[^1][^2] He earned his medical degree at Sidney Kimmel Medical College at Thomas Jefferson University.[^1] He completed general surgery and urology residency training at the University of Texas Southwestern Medical Center.[^1] He completed a genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship at the University of Washington Medical Center with Hunter Wessells.[^1][^3]

## Contributions

His cited studies examine urethral reconstruction, including outcomes after radiation-induced stricture and pelvic fracture urethral injury, and patient-reported measures after stricture surgery. Other cited papers address buried penis repair, male stress incontinence, and penile prosthesis surgery.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
