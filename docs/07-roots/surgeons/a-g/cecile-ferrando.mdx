## Key Publications

Her confirmed work addresses gender-affirming pelvic and genital surgery, including hysterectomy, orchiectomy, vaginoplasty techniques and complications, pelvic floor therapy, and patient-reported outcomes. Other cited studies examine pelvic organ prolapse repair, including sacrocolpopexy, sacrospinous colpopexy, and uterosacral colpopexy.<sup>[P3](#publication-3)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-7)</sup>
