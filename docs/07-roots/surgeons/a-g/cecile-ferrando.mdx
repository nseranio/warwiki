## Known for

- **Sacrocolpopexy mesh-configuration randomized trial** (2019). Co-led a randomized comparison of Y-shaped and flat mesh for laparoscopic or robotic sacrocolpopexy.[^8]
- **FLOWER pelvic floor therapy trial** (2023). Led a randomized trial of routine postoperative pelvic floor physical therapy after vaginoplasty; it found no improvement in measured outcomes.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Neovaginal Stenosis Management — Stepwise](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery/neovaginal-stenosis-management).

## Contributions

Her confirmed work addresses gender-affirming pelvic and genital surgery, including hysterectomy, orchiectomy, vaginoplasty techniques and complications, pelvic floor therapy, and patient-reported outcomes. Other cited studies examine pelvic organ prolapse repair, including sacrocolpopexy, sacrospinous colpopexy, and uterosacral colpopexy.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
