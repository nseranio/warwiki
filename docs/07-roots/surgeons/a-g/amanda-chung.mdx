## Biography

Amanda Chung is a urological surgeon with Sydney Urological Associates and a clinical senior lecturer at Macquarie University.[^1] She earned her medical degree at the University of New South Wales in 2007, completed urology specialist training in New South Wales, and became a Fellow of the Royal Australasian College of Surgeons in 2016.[^1][^2] She completed a genitourinary reconstruction fellowship at Eastern Virginia Medical School in 2017, working with Kurt McCammon.[^1][^3] She serves as Network Director of the Surgical Skills Program for the Northern Sydney Local Health District and on the board of the Society of Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgeons.[^1][^4]

## Contributions

Her cited work on urethral stricture addresses treatment of lichen sclerosus and penile inversion approaches to penile urethroplasty.[^5][^6][^7][^8]
