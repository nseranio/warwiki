## Contributions

Andrich's cited work covers urethral stricture reconstruction, including non-transecting bulbar urethroplasty and graft or staged repairs. It also covers complications of prostate cancer treatment, including urorectal and urosymphyseal fistulas and posterior urethral stricture.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
