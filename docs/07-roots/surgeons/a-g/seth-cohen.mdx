## Biography

Seth D. Cohen is a clinical associate professor of urology at NYU Grossman School of Medicine and director of its Sexual Dysfunction Program.[^1] Cohen earned a medical degree at Tulane University and completed a urology residency at Lenox Hill Hospital.[^1] Cohen completed a sexual medicine fellowship at UC San Diego Medical Center in 2014.[^1]

[^1]: [Seth D. Cohen, MD, MPH. NYU Langone Health](https://nyulangone.org/doctors/1922249135/seth-d-cohen). Accessed October 2026.
