## Biography

Linda Brubaker is listed as emeritus in UC San Diego's urogynecology division following a 2025 retirement announcement.[^1][^2] UC San Diego's faculty directory also lists Brubaker as a clinical professor of obstetrics and gynecology and reproductive sciences.[^3] She completed medical school, residency, and fellowship at Rush University.[^4] Brubaker previously served as dean of Loyola University Chicago Stritch School of Medicine and as president of the American Urogynecologic Society and the Society of Gynecologic Surgeons.[^4]

## Contributions

Her cited work covers pelvic organ prolapse repair and stress urinary incontinence, including studies of sacrocolpopexy with Burch colposuspension, Burch versus fascial sling surgery, and the OPTIMAL apical repair trial. The citations also include a trial comparing a continence pessary with behavioral therapy.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]
