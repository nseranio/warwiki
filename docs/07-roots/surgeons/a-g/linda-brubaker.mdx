## Key Publications

Her cited work covers pelvic organ prolapse repair and stress urinary incontinence, including studies of sacrocolpopexy with Burch colposuspension, Burch versus fascial sling surgery, and the OPTIMAL apical repair trial. The citations also include a trial comparing a continence pessary with behavioral therapy.<sup>[P3](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-8)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-9)</sup>
