## Contributions

Her cited work covers pelvic organ prolapse repair and stress urinary incontinence, including studies of sacrocolpopexy with Burch colposuspension, Burch versus fascial sling surgery, and the OPTIMAL apical repair trial. The citations also include a trial comparing a continence pessary with behavioral therapy.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]

- **CARE trial** (2006). Led a randomized trial finding that Burch colposuspension during abdominal sacrocolpopexy reduced postoperative stress urinary incontinence.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Burch Colposuspension](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/burch-colposuspension).
- **Bacteria in the adult female bladder** (2012). Senior-authored a study detecting bacterial DNA in bladder samples from women, challenging the assumption that the female bladder is sterile.[^11]
