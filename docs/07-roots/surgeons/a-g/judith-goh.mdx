## Contributions

Goh's cited work covers classification and repair outcomes for genital tract fistulas, postrepair urinary incontinence, and sacrospinous fixation devices and technique.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Genital tract fistula repair series** (1998). Reported outcomes from 130 genital tract fistula repairs in 116 women.[^8]
- **Fistula classification outcome study** (2008). Tested whether fistula classification predicted failed closure and residual urinary incontinence in 987 women.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Obstetric Fistula](/docs/clinical-conditions/03f-fistulas/in-females/obstetric).
