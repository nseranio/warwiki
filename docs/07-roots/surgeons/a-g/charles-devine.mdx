## Key Publications

Their work also addressed Peyronie disease, penile and genital trauma, and phallic reconstruction.[^2] Devine and his brother Patrick developed patch graft urethroplasty techniques for urethral stricture.[^2]

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Full-thickness skin patch graft urethroplasty** (1963). Helped develop and disseminate full-thickness skin patch graft repair for urethral stricture.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Devine–Horton dermal graft for Peyronie’s disease** (1974). Described dermal graft replacement of the tunical defect after excision of a Peyronie’s plaque.

</KeyPublication>

</ol>

## The EVMS Fellowship

Devine and Patrick Devine founded the adult and pediatric genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship at EVMS in 1975.[^1] Devine, Patrick Devine, and Horton also hosted operative teaching clinics in Norfolk.[^2]

## Recognition

GURS established the annual Charles J. Devine Jr. Lectureship in 2005.[^1]
