## Training and career

<div className="sp-facts">

- **Born and died** 1923, Norfolk, Virginia; February 12, 1999[^1]
- **Medical school** George Washington University School of Medicine[^1]
- **Other training** Internship, Johns Hopkins Hospital; urology fellowship, Cleveland Clinic[^1]
- **Leadership** First chair, Department of Urology, Eastern Virginia Medical School[^2]
- **Leadership** Co-founder with Patrick Devine of the EVMS adult and pediatric genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship, 1975[^1]
- **Honors** AUA Distinguished Contribution Award, 1993; Ramon Guiteras Award, 1997[^1]

## Principal Contributions

Their work also addressed Peyronie disease, penile and genital trauma, and phallic reconstruction.[^2] Devine and his brother Patrick developed patch graft urethroplasty techniques for urethral stricture.[^2]

## The EVMS Fellowship

Devine and Patrick Devine founded the adult and pediatric genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship at EVMS in 1975.[^1] Devine, Patrick Devine, and Horton also hosted operative teaching clinics in Norfolk.[^2]

## Recognition

GURS established the annual Charles J. Devine Jr. Lectureship in 2005.[^1]
