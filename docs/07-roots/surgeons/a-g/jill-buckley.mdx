## Contributions

Buckley's cited work covers urethral stricture reconstruction, female graft and flap urethroplasty, rectourethral fistula repair, and robotic urinary tract reconstruction. She also coauthored an SIU/ICUD consultation on dilation, internal urethrotomy, and stenting for male anterior urethral strictures.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **SIU/ICUD consultation on endoscopic treatment of anterior urethral stricture** (2014). First-authored an international consensus review of dilation, urethrotomy and stenting for male anterior urethral strictures.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Filiforms & Followers](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/sounds-bougies/filiforms-followers).
- **Female urethral reconstruction: graft versus flap outcomes** (2014). Co-authored a comparative series of vaginal flap and dorsal buccal graft reconstruction for female urethral stricture.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Anterior Vaginal Wall Mucosal Inlay (Önol)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/onol-vaginal-mucosal-inlay).
