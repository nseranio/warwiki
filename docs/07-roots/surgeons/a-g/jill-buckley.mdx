## Biography

Jill C. Buckley is a professor of clinical urology at UC San Diego.[^1][^2] Buckley earned her medical degree at UCLA and completed her urology residency at UC San Francisco.[^1] She completed a reconstruction and trauma surgery fellowship at UC San Francisco under Jack W. McAninch.[^2] Buckley directs UC San Diego's urology residency and serves on the board of the Society of Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgeons.[^1]

## Contributions

Buckley's cited work covers urethral stricture reconstruction, female graft and flap urethroplasty, rectourethral fistula repair, and robotic urinary tract reconstruction. She also coauthored an SIU/ICUD consultation on dilation, internal urethrotomy, and stenting for male anterior urethral strictures.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
