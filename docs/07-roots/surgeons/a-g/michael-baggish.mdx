## Biography

Michael S. Baggish was listed in 2018 as a professor of obstetrics and gynecology at the University of California, San Francisco, and with St. Helena Hospital.[^1] Baggish completed a gynecologic pathology fellowship at Johns Hopkins Hospital under J. Donald Woodruff.[^2] Baggish chaired the OB-GYN department at Good Samaritan Hospital from 1993 to 2010.[^3]

## Contributions

Baggish's WARWIKI-cited work covers gynecologic surgical anatomy and technique, including hysterectomy, laparoscopy, ureteral injury, and vaginal and vulvar procedures.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
