## Biography

Neil H. Grafstein is an assistant professor of urology and of obstetrics, gynecology and reproductive science at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai.[^1] He received his medical degree from SUNY Downstate Medical Center and completed his urology residency there.[^2] He completed a reconstructive urology fellowship at Duke University in 2006–2007, with George D. Webster as his mentor.[^2][^3][^4]

## Contributions

Grafstein's confirmed WARWIKI paper examines pelvic floor electromyography lag time as a noninvasive method to screen for primary bladder neck dysfunction in children and monitor treatment response.[^5]
