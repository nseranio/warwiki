## Biography

Ronak Gor, DO, is director of genitourinary reconstruction and trauma at Cooper University Health Care and associate professor of surgery at Cooper Medical School of Rowan University.[^1] Gor attended Lake Erie College of Osteopathic Medicine and completed a urology residency at Einstein Healthcare Network in Philadelphia.[^2] Gor completed a fellowship in genitourinary reconstruction and trauma at the University of Minnesota Medical School with Sean Elliott.[^2][^3]

## Contributions

Gor's cited work covers urinary diversion and continent catheterizable reconstruction, including patients with an existing colostomy, channel revisions, and hand-assisted laparoscopic ileocecocystoplasty.[^4][^5][^6]
