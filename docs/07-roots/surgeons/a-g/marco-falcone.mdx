## Biography

Marco Falcone is a consultant urologist at Molinette Hospital and the USU/CTO Hospital in Turin and an adjunct professor of urology at Biruni University.[^1][^2] Falcone attended medical school at the University of Turin and completed urology residency at Molinette Hospital.[^3][^1] Falcone completed a clinical fellowship in andrology, reconstructive urology, and gender surgery at St. Peter's Andrology Center and University College London Hospital in 2015-2016 under David Ralph.[^3][^1] Falcone is deputy editor of the International Journal of Impotence Research.[^4]

## Contributions

Falcone's cited work examines glansectomy and skin-graft reconstruction for penile cancer, along with surgical repair of adult acquired buried penis. Other papers cover radial forearm phalloplasty, penile prostheses including after gender-affirming surgery, and operations for Peyronie's disease.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]
