## Contributions

Falcone's cited work examines glansectomy and skin-graft reconstruction for penile cancer, along with surgical repair of adult acquired buried penis. Other papers cover radial forearm phalloplasty, penile prostheses including after gender-affirming surgery, and operations for Peyronie's disease.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]

- **Penile prosthesis outcomes after phalloplasty** (2018). Led a 247-patient series reporting outcomes of inflatable penile prosthesis placement after masculinizing phalloplasty.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Erectile Prosthesis (Penile Implant) After Gender-Affirming Phalloplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery/penile-implant-after-phalloplasty).
- **Total phallic reconstruction in genetic males** (2021). Reported staged radial forearm flap phalloplasty, with urethral reconstruction and prosthesis placement when indicated, for severe penile insufficiency.[^11]
