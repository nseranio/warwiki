## Contributions

Goueli's confirmed WARWIKI citations are the medical and procedural or surgical management parts of the 2026 AUA guideline on lower urinary tract symptoms attributed to benign prostatic hyperplasia.[^3][^4][^5][^6]

- **Adult female urethral cellular atlas** (2023). Coauthored a conference abstract describing single-cell populations in the normal adult female urethra.[^7]
- **2026 AUA LUTS/BPH guideline** (2026). Chaired the 2026 AUA guideline on evaluation and management of lower urinary tract symptoms attributed to benign prostatic hyperplasia.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [BPH & Male LUTS](/docs/surgical-techniques/bph-male-luts).
