## Biography

Ramy S. Goueli is a urologist with Denver Urology whose practice includes female pelvic medicine, reconstructive urology, neuro-urology, and complex voiding dysfunction.[^1] He earned his medical degree at Yale University School of Medicine and completed urology residency at NewYork-Presbyterian/Weill Cornell Medical Center.[^1] Goueli completed fellowship training in urogynecology and reconstructive pelvic surgery at UT Southwestern Medical Center in Dallas.[^1] He chaired the American Urological Association's 2026 guideline on lower urinary tract symptoms attributed to benign prostatic hyperplasia.[^2]

## Contributions

Goueli's confirmed WARWIKI citations are the medical and procedural or surgical management parts of the 2026 AUA guideline on lower urinary tract symptoms attributed to benign prostatic hyperplasia.[^3][^4][^5][^6]
