## Biography

Jairam Rao Eswara chairs urology at Tufts University School of Medicine and serves as division chief of urology at Tufts Medical Center.[^1][^2] He earned his medical degree through the Harvard-MIT Health Sciences and Technology program at Harvard Medical School and completed a urology residency at Massachusetts General Hospital.[^3] He trained in urologic trauma and reconstruction at Washington University in St. Louis, where Steven B. Brandes directed the Reconstructive Urology Fellowship.[^3][^4]

## Contributions

His cited work examines urinary fistulae, including urinary-cutaneous fistulae in neurogenic bladder and the association of prior pelvic radiation with outcomes after gracilis-flap and nonmuscle-flap repairs.[^5][^6][^7]
