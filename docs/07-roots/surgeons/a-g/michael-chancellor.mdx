## Biography

Michael B. Chancellor is a professor of urology at Oakland University William Beaumont School of Medicine.[^1][^2] Chancellor earned a medical degree at the Medical College of Wisconsin and completed urology residency at the University of Michigan.[^3] Chancellor completed a fellowship in neurourology and female urology at Columbia University College of Physicians and Surgeons and worked with research mentor Jerry Blaivas there.[^3][^4]

## Contributions

Chancellor's cited work examines botulinum toxin for overactive bladder and other voiding disorders, including bladder instillation of liposome-encapsulated onabotulinumtoxinA and studies of urothelial response.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]
