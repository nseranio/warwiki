## Biography

Noel A. Armenakas is an attending urologist at NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital and an adjunct clinical professor of urology at Weill Cornell Medical College.[^1] Armenakas earned a medical degree at the National University of Athens Faculty of Medicine and completed urology residency at Lenox Hill Hospital.[^1][^2] Armenakas completed a genitourinary trauma and reconstruction fellowship at the University of California, San Francisco, where Jack W. McAninch directed the trauma and reconstructive fellowship.[^2][^3]

## Contributions

Armenakas's cited work addresses reconstruction of the fossa navicularis and meatus, including resistant strictures and graft supported distal urethral repair.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
