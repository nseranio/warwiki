## Contributions

- **Prospective urethral-pressure study across pregnancy and delivery** (1982). Measured urethral pressure serially during pregnancy and postpartum, documenting reduced pressure and length parameters after vaginal delivery.[^2]
- **Anterior repair versus Burch colposuspension study** (1988). Prospectively assessed clinical and urodynamic outcomes after anterior vaginal repair and Burch colposuspension for stress incontinence.[^3]
