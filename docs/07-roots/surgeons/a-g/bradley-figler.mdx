## Biography

Bradley D. Figler is a professor of urology and plastic surgery at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.[^1][^2] He received his medical degree from Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine and completed his urology residency at Emory University Hospital.[^1] He completed a 2011–2013 fellowship in genitourinary trauma and male reconstruction at the University of Washington and Harborview Medical Center with Hunter Wessells.[^1][^2] He directs UNC's genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship.[^1]

## Contributions

His cited work describes staged and buccal graft techniques for urethral strictures. It also covers limited and outpatient panniculectomy with skin grafting for adult buried penis.[^3][^4][^5][^6]
