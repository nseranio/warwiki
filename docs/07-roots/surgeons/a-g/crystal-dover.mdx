## Biography

Crystal Dover Sadik is a Guthrie urologist practicing at Big Flats and East Corning in New York.[^1][^2] Sadik attended the University of Virginia School of Medicine and completed a urology residency at the University of Wisconsin School of Medicine and Public Health in 2012.[^2] Sadik completed fellowships at Massachusetts General Hospital and Duke University Medical Center in 2013; Duke records a six-month reconstructive urology fellowship term in 2013–2014.[^2][^3]
