## Biography

Kristy M. Borawski is a UNC Health urologist and program director of the University of North Carolina School of Medicine urology residency.[^1][^2] She earned her medical degree at Albany Medical College and completed urologic residency at Duke University.[^3] She completed a Duke fellowship in reconstructive urology, neurourology, and urodynamics in 2010.[^3][^4]
