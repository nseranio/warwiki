## Biography

Brian W. Chao is a clinical assistant professor of urology at NYU Grossman School of Medicine and a reconstructive urologist at NYU Langone Urology Associates.[^1] Chao earned an MD at New York University and completed urology residency at NYU Grossman School of Medicine.[^1] Chao completed a robotic urologic oncology fellowship at Temple University in 2024.[^1] Chao completed a genitourinary reconstruction fellowship at the University of Minnesota in 2025 in a program led by Sean Elliott with Joseph Pariser and Rachel Mann on its faculty.[^1][^2][^3][^4]

## Contributions

Chao's cited work includes a multi-institutional outcomes study and a technique report on robotic buccal mucosa graft ureteroplasty.[^5][^6]
