## Known for

- **Urothelial ATP signaling in interstitial cystitis** (2006). Compared bladder urothelial cells from patients with interstitial cystitis and controls, reporting increased extracellular ATP signaling in the patient cells.[^3]
- **Urinary biomarkers and midurethral sling outcomes** (2014). Studied inflammatory and tissue-remodeling urinary biomarkers before and after sling surgery for stress urinary incontinence.[^4]
