## Key Publications

Capogrosso's confirmed papers cover penile prosthesis outcomes, complications, and infection risks. His guideline and review papers address erectile dysfunction, Peyronie's disease, priapism, and low-intensity shock wave therapy.<sup>[P4](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-8)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-9)</sup><sup>[P8](#publication-10)</sup>
