## Known for

- **Young men presenting with erectile dysfunction** (2013). Reported that 114 of 439 men seeking initial care for erectile dysfunction were aged 40 or younger.[^11]
- **Erectile recovery after radical prostatectomy** (2019). Led a single-center analysis finding no improvement over time in adjusted erectile function recovery after radical prostatectomy.[^12]
- **INSIST-ED penile implant outcomes registry** (2019). Led a multicenter prospective registry report assessing function and satisfaction one year after penile implant surgery.[^13] Cited on WARWIKI: [Penile Implants](/docs/surgical-techniques/04j-sexual-dysfunction/penile-implants).

## Contributions

Capogrosso's confirmed papers cover penile prosthesis outcomes, complications, and infection risks. His guideline and review papers address erectile dysfunction, Peyronie's disease, priapism, and low-intensity shock wave therapy.[^6][^7][^8][^9][^10]
