## Contributions

Her clinical work covers urinary tract reconstruction and urinary diversion, urethral stricture, incontinence and voiding dysfunction in cancer survivors. Her cited studies come from the Duke male incontinence series.[^1]

- **Salvage after male sling failure** (2015). Led a review of 61 men with persistent post-prostatectomy incontinence after a failed primary sling: those who received a second transobturator sling were up to 6 times more likely to remain incontinent than those who received an artificial urinary sphincter.[^3] Cited on WARWIKI: [Male Urethral Slings](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/male-urethral-slings).
- **Leak point pressure and transobturator sling success** (2022). Led a study of 290 men treated with a male transobturator sling in which a preoperative Valsalva leak point pressure above 70 cm H<sub>2</sub>O halved the hazard of failure.[^4]
