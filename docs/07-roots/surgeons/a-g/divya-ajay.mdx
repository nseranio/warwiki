## Key Publications

Her clinical work covers urinary tract reconstruction and urinary diversion, urethral stricture, incontinence and voiding dysfunction in cancer survivors. Her cited studies come from the Duke male incontinence series.[^1]

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Salvage after male sling failure** (2015). Led a review of 61 men with persistent post-prostatectomy incontinence after a failed primary sling: those who received a second transobturator sling were up to 6 times more likely to remain incontinent than those who received an artificial urinary sphincter.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Leak point pressure and transobturator sling success** (2022). Led a study of 290 men treated with a male transobturator sling in which a preoperative Valsalva leak point pressure above 70 cm H<sub>2</sub>O halved the hazard of failure.

</KeyPublication>

</ol>
