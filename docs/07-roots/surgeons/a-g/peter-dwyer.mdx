## Biography

Peter Dwyer joined IUGA while working as Stuart Stanton's fellow in London in the early 1980s.[^3] He has said he joined to present research, meet colleagues, and keep contact with friends and fellows.[^3] Back in Melbourne, he took charge of the Mercy Hospital for Women urodynamics clinic after Norman Beischer established it.[^4] Anna Rosamilia later described Dwyer as a mentor in her subspecialty training.[^5]

Dwyer and Paul Riss became joint editors in chief of the *International Urogynecology Journal*.[^6] They added video, image, digest, and mentorship sections and introduced a Fellows Editor role for early-career clinicians.[^6]
