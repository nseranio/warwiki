## Biography

Lesley K. Carr is an associate professor emerita in urology at the University of Toronto and retired in June 2021.[^1][^2] Carr received a medical degree from the University of Toronto in 1989.[^3] Carr completed urology residency at the University of Toronto in 1994.[^4] Carr completed Duke University's reconstructive urology fellowship in 1995-96.[^2]
