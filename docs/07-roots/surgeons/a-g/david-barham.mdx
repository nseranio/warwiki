## Biography

David W. Barham practices urology at CHRISTUS Trinity Clinic Urology in San Antonio, Texas.[^1] Barham attended the Brody School of Medicine at East Carolina University and completed a urology residency at Tripler Army Medical Center.[^2] Barham completed UC Irvine's two-year reconstructive and prosthetic urology fellowship in 2023 under Joel Gelman.[^3][^4] An April 2025 Pfeiffer University profile identified Barham as deputy editor in chief of the Video Journal of Sexual Medicine.[^2]

## Contributions

Barham's cited papers focus on penile prosthesis surgery, including curvature correction during implantation for Peyronie's disease, infection prophylaxis, and implantation after ischemic priapism. Another cited paper examines five-year outcomes of the mini-jupette sling.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]
