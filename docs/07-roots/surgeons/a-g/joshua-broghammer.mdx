## Contributions

Broghammer coauthored the 2014 American Urological Association Urotrauma Guideline and its 2020 amendment.[^6][^7] His other cited work includes TURNS studies of bladder neck contracture and a review of nonoperative management of renal injury.[^8][^9]

- **AUA Urotrauma Guideline amendment** (2021). Coauthored the 2020 amendment, which updated six statements on renal, ureteral, bladder, urethral and genital trauma and added four, including suprapubic tube placement after pelvic fracture urethral injury and ultrasonography for blunt scrotal injury.[^7] Cited on WARWIKI: [Renal Trauma](/docs/special-populations/05a-trauma-emergencies/renal-trauma), [Pelvic Fracture Urethral Injury (PFUI)](/docs/special-populations/05a-trauma-emergencies/pfui).
- **Mitomycin C injection at transurethral incision of bladder neck contracture** (2015). Coauthored a six-center TURNS review of 55 men in which 58% had resolution after one incision with mitomycin C and 75% after repeat treatment, with serious adverse events in 7%.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Bladder Neck Contracture](/docs/clinical-conditions/03b-voiding-outlet/bladder-neck-stenosis), [Transurethral Incision of BNC (TUIBNC)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04ab-bladder-neck-reconstruction/transurethral-incision-bnc).
- **Conservative management of renal trauma** (2007). Lead author, with Richard Santucci, of a review of nonoperative management of renal injuries.[^9]
