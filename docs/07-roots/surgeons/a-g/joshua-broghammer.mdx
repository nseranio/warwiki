## Biography

Joshua A. Broghammer is an associate professor of urology at the University of Kansas Medical Center.[^1] He earned his medical degree from the University of Iowa and completed urology residency at Wayne State University and Detroit Medical Center.[^1] He completed a trauma and genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship at the University of Washington's Harborview Medical Center.[^1]

## Contributions

Broghammer coauthored the 2020 AUA urotrauma guideline amendment, a chapter on genital injury repair, and a multicenter study of mitomycin C injection during bladder neck contracture incision.[^2][^3][^4]
