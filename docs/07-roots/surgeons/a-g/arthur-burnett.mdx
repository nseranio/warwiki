## Key Publications

Burnett's cited work covers erectile dysfunction evaluation and treatment, including an AUA guideline and a systematic review of penile prosthesis survival. His priapism papers examine prevention of recurrent ischemic priapism with phosphodiesterase type 5 inhibitors and a corporoglanular shunt modification called the corporal "snake" maneuver.<sup>[P3](#publication-11)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-6)</sup>
