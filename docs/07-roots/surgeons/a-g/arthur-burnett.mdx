## Contributions

Burnett's cited work covers erectile dysfunction evaluation and treatment, including an AUA guideline and a systematic review of penile prosthesis survival. His priapism papers examine prevention of recurrent ischemic priapism with phosphodiesterase type 5 inhibitors and a corporoglanular shunt modification called the corporal "snake" maneuver.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

- **Nitric oxide as a mediator of penile erection** (1992). Burnett led an experimental report identifying nitric oxide signaling in penile nerves and erectile physiology.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Erectile Dysfunction (Condition)](/docs/clinical-conditions/03g-genital-scrotal/erectile-dysfunction).
- **Corporal snake maneuver for ischemic priapism** (2009). Burnett and Pierorazio described corporal tunneling through a modified distal corporoglanular shunt to evacuate blood in refractory ischemic priapism.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Priapism Shunts & Decompression](/docs/surgical-techniques/04j-sexual-dysfunction/priapism-shunts-decompression).
- **2018 AUA erectile dysfunction guideline** (2018). Burnett was lead author of the AUA guideline on diagnosis, counseling, and treatment options for erectile dysfunction.[^11] Cited on WARWIKI: [Erectile Dysfunction (Procedures)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04j-sexual-dysfunction/erectile-dysfunction).
