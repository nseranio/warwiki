## Contributions

Burnett's cited work covers erectile dysfunction evaluation and treatment, including an AUA guideline and a systematic review of penile prosthesis survival. His priapism papers examine prevention of recurrent ischemic priapism with phosphodiesterase type 5 inhibitors and a corporoglanular shunt modification called the corporal "snake" maneuver.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
