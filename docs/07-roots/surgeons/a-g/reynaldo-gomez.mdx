## Biography

Reynaldo Gómez Illanes is chief of urology at Hospital del Trabajador in Santiago, Chile.[^1][^2] Gómez earned his medical degree at the Catholic University of Chile and completed general surgery and urology residency there.[^2] Gómez completed a trauma and reconstructive urology fellowship at UCSF from 1990 to 1991 with Jack McAninch.[^3] Gómez is a past president of the Chilean Society of Urology and the Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgeons Society.[^1]

## Contributions

Gómez's cited papers address posterior urethroplasty after pelvic fracture urethral injury, including timing of reconstruction and sparing of the bulbar arteries.[^4][^5][^6]
