## Biography

Stephen A. Blakely is listed by the University of Colorado as an assistant professor at SUNY Upstate Medical University.[^1] Blakely's medical school was the University of Maryland School of Medicine, and his urology residency was at Upstate Medical University.[^2] Stephen A. Blakely appears on the University of Colorado's 2015–2016 reconstructive urology fellowship roster; the page names Brian J. Flynn as director and says Ty Higuchi joined the program in 2012.[^1]

## Contributions

Blakely's cited work examines anterior and distal urethral stricture repair and open reconstruction of vesicourethral anastomotic stricture after prostatectomy. Blakely also coauthored a chapter on urologic complications after metoidioplasty and phalloplasty.[^3][^4][^5][^6]
