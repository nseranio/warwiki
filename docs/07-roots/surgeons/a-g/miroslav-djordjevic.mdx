## Contributions

Djordjevic's cited work covers gender-affirming genital reconstruction, including vaginoplasty using penile skin and a urethral flap, metoidioplasty variants, and urethral lengthening with buccal mucosa grafts and labia minora flaps.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

- **Penile prosthesis with tunical incisions without grafting** (2013). Reported prosthesis placement with tunical relaxing incisions and neurovascular bundle coverage, avoiding grafting in selected Peyronie disease cases.[^9]
- **813-case metoidioplasty series** (2021). Senior-authored a 14-year series comparing urethral reconstruction options and reporting outcomes after 813 metoidioplasties.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Vagina-Preserving Metoidioplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery/vagina-preserving-metoidioplasty).
