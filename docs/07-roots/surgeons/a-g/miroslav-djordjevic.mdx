## Biography

Miroslav L. Djordjevic is a clinical professor of urology at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai.[^1] He earned his medical degree and completed a urology residency and pediatric urology fellowship at the University of Belgrade School of Medicine.[^2] He founded the Belgrade Center for Genital Reconstructive Surgery and served as president of the Society of Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgeons.[^1][^3]

## Contributions

Djordjevic's cited work covers gender-affirming genital reconstruction, including vaginoplasty using penile skin and a urethral flap, metoidioplasty variants, and urethral lengthening with buccal mucosa grafts and labia minora flaps.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
