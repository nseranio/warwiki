## Biography

Kenneth W. Angermeier is director of the Section of Genitourinary Reconstruction at Cleveland Clinic and professor of urology at the Cleveland Clinic Lerner College of Medicine.[^1][^2] He earned his medical degree at Indiana University School of Medicine in 1985 and completed a urology residency at Cleveland Clinic.[^1] He completed a fellowship in adult and pediatric reconstructive urology at the Devine Center for Genitourinary Reconstruction and Eastern Virginia Medical School.[^1] He is the institute's enterprise vice chair of education and served as president of the Society of Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgeons from 2009 to 2010.[^1]

## Contributions

His cited work covers urethral stricture evaluation and reconstruction, including guideline and consultation papers, augmented anastomotic urethroplasty, ventral grafts, and rectourethral fistula repair. Other cited publications address penile curvature surgery and penile prostheses.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
