## Contributions

His cited work covers urethral stricture evaluation and reconstruction, including guideline and consultation papers, augmented anastomotic urethroplasty, ventral grafts, and rectourethral fistula repair. Other cited publications address penile curvature surgery and penile prostheses.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Augmented anastomotic urethroplasty outcomes** (2007). Co-reported a 69-patient series evaluating augmented anastomotic urethroplasty for recurrent urethral stricture.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Augmented Anastomotic Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/augmented-anastomotic).
- **Adult hypospadias presentation classification** (2011). Senior-authored a three-category classification of adults presenting for hypospadias-related urethral repair.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Redo Hypospadias Repair in Adolescents and Adults](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/redo-hypospadias-adults).
