## Biography

Carlos Ignacio Calvo de la Barra is listed as an adult urologist by UC CHRISTUS.[^1] Calvo de la Barra trained as a physician and urologist at the Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile.[^1] Calvo de la Barra completed a reconstructive and functional urology fellowship at the University of Alberta, with Keith Rourke as program director.[^1][^2]

## Contributions

Calvo de la Barra's cited work examines long term urethroplasty outcomes, imaging after bulbar reconstruction, and urethral complications after prostate cancer treatment.[^3][^4][^5][^6]
