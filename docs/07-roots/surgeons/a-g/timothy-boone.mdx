## Known for

- **Nocturnal artificial urinary sphincter deactivation** (2001). Coauthored a study comparing urethral atrophy after nocturnal activation versus deactivation of an artificial urinary sphincter.[^7]
- **Artificial urinary sphincter revision outcomes** (2012). Senior-authored a comparison of primary implantation, revision and reimplantation outcomes for artificial urinary sphincters.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Artificial Urinary Sphincter (Procedure)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/artificial-urinary-sphincter).

## Contributions

His cited work covers adult neurogenic lower urinary tract dysfunction guidelines and studies of augmentation cystoplasty, ileovesicostomy, and videourodynamic diagnosis in neurogenic bladder care.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
