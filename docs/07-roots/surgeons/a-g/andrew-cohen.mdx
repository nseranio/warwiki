## Contributions

Cohen's cited work covers penile prosthesis placement in the neophallus and robotic ureteral reconstruction with appendiceal flaps; his research at UCSF applied computational fluid dynamics to urethral stricture.[^3][^4][^5]

- **Bone-anchored penile prosthesis after radial forearm phalloplasty** (2017). Led a University of Chicago series of 10 patients whose implant rear tip was anchored through a pubic corticotomy; 70% needed reoperation, and 80% had a functioning prosthesis at a mean follow-up of 49 months.[^3] Cited on WARWIKI: [Penile Implant After Phalloplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery/penile-implant-after-phalloplasty), [Radial Forearm Free Flap (RFFF)](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps/radial-forearm).
- **Robotic augmented roof ureteroplasty with appendiceal onlay flap** (2023). Senior author of a video report of a mid-to-proximal ureteral stricture repaired with a pedicled appendiceal onlay, using concurrent ureteroscopy and indocyanine green to define the anatomy.[^4] Cited on WARWIKI: [Augmented Anastomotic Ureteroureterostomy](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/augmented-anastomotic-uu), [Appendiceal Onlay / Interposition](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/appendiceal-ileal-onlay).
- **Computational fluid dynamic modeling of urethral stricture** (2019). Built a model of an idealized male urethra with 16 stricture scenarios; stricture diameter and bladder pressure had a strong effect on flow, while stricture location, length and sequence had a weaker one.[^5]
