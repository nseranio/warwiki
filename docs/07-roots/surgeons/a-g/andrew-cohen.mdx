## Key Publications

Cohen's cited work covers penile prosthesis placement in the neophallus and robotic ureteral reconstruction with appendiceal flaps; his research at UCSF applied computational fluid dynamics to urethral stricture.<sup>[P1](#publication-3)</sup><sup>[P2](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P3](#publication-5)</sup>

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Bone-anchored penile prosthesis after radial forearm phalloplasty** (2017). Led a University of Chicago series of 10 patients whose implant rear tip was anchored through a pubic corticotomy; 70% needed reoperation, and 80% had a functioning prosthesis at a mean follow-up of 49 months.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Robotic augmented roof ureteroplasty with appendiceal onlay flap** (2023). Senior author of a video report of a mid-to-proximal ureteral stricture repaired with a pedicled appendiceal onlay, using concurrent ureteroscopy and indocyanine green to define the anatomy.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[2]} citations={citations}>

**Computational fluid dynamic modeling of urethral stricture** (2019). Built a model of an idealized male urethra with 16 stricture scenarios; stricture diameter and bladder pressure had a strong effect on flow, while stricture location, length and sequence had a weaker one.

</KeyPublication>

</ol>
