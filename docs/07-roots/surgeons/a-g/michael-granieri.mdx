## Biography

Michael Granieri is a urologist and president of the Conrad Pearson Clinic.[^1][^2] He earned his medical degree at Northwestern University and completed urology residency at Duke University.[^2] Granieri completed a fellowship at NYU Langone Medical Center.[^2]

## Contributions

Granieri's cited papers examine urethral stricture reconstruction, including distal urethroplasty, rectal mucosa grafts, and patient-reported urinary and sexual outcomes after urethroplasty. Other cited papers address robotic Y-V plasty for bladder neck contracture and techniques and complications in gender-affirming genital reconstruction.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
