## Contributions

His cited work covers benign prostatic hyperplasia treatments, including the iTind device, prostatic urethral lift, photoselective vaporization, Rezūm therapy, and simple prostatectomy. Other cited studies examine outcomes of sling and prolapse mesh implants and treatments for overactive bladder.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
