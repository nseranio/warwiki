## Biography

Jessica M. DeLong is chief medical officer of Juniper Biomedical.[^1] She earned her medical degree at Eastern Virginia Medical School and completed her surgery internship and urology residency at Lahey Clinic.[^2] DeLong completed a fellowship in adult and pediatric reconstructive urology at Eastern Virginia Medical School in 2014.[^2]

## Contributions

Her cited work covers urethral stricture reconstruction, including non-transecting bulbar urethroplasty, augmented perineal urethrostomy, and a penile skin island flap. It also reports drug-coated balloon outcomes from the ROBUST I and III studies.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
