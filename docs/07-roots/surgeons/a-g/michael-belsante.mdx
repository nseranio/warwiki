## Biography

Michael J. Belsante is a urologist with Urology Associates, P.C.[^1] He earned his medical degree from the University of North Carolina School of Medicine and completed his urology residency at the University of Texas Southwestern Medical Center.[^1] He completed a 2014–2015 fellowship in reconstructive urology, female urology, and urodynamics at Duke University Medical Center and coauthored research there with Andrew C. Peterson.[^1][^2][^3] He is listed by Urology Associates as chief of the surgery division at TriStar Summit Medical Center.[^1]
