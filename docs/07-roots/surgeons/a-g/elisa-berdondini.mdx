## Biography

Elisa Berdondini is a consultant in reconstructive urethral and genital surgery at Clinica Sedes Sapientiae in Turin.[^1] Berdondini attended medical school at the University of Florence, graduating in 1998, and completed postgraduate training in urology there in 2004.[^2] From 2005 to 2015, Berdondini worked at the Centro Italiano di Chirurgia Uretrale-Genitale in Arezzo.[^1]

## Contributions

Berdondini's confirmed papers cover graft urethroplasty for male strictures, including two-sided oral mucosa grafts, small intestinal submucosa grafts, and dorsolateral penile skin grafts. The papers also describe dorsal and ventral buccal mucosa graft reconstruction for female urethral strictures. Other confirmed studies characterize male urethral stricture patterns and describe glans resurfacing and reconstruction.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
