## Contributions

His cited work examines urethral bulking agents for female stress urinary incontinence, including polyacrylamide hydrogel, calcium hydroxylapatite, and patient factors affecting injection therapy. Other cited papers address overactive bladder treatment with onabotulinumtoxinA and sacral or tibial neuromodulation.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]
