## Contributions

His cited work covers pelvic organ prolapse repair, including trials comparing apical and posterior vaginal procedures and studies of recurrence and perioperative complications. It also includes development of the PFDI-20 and PFIQ-7 questionnaires for pelvic floor disorders.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]

- **OPTIMAL prolapse surgery trial** (2014). Led a randomized comparison of uterosacral ligament suspension, sacrospinous ligament fixation, and perioperative behavioral therapy for apical prolapse.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Sacrospinous Ligament Fixation (SSLF)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/sacrospinous-ligament-fixation).
