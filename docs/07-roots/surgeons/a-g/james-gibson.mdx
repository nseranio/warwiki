## Biography

James Sedman Gibson was a professor of obstetrics and gynecology at the University of Sydney's Westmead Clinical School.[^1][^2] Gibson received an MBBS from the University of Sydney in 1959.[^2] Gibson was warden of the Westmead Clinical School from 1986 to 1991.[^1] Gibson served as IUGA president and helped establish urogynecology as an Australian subspecialty with Peter Glenning and Ian Tucker.[^3][^4]
