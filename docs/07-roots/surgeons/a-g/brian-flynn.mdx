## Biography

Brian J. Flynn is a professor of surgery in the University of Colorado School of Medicine's Division of Urology and directs its functional and reconstructive urology fellowship.[^1][^2] Flynn received an MD from Temple University School of Medicine, completed urology residency at Geisinger Health System, and completed a functional reconstructive urology fellowship at Duke University Hospital in 2002.[^2] Flynn previously served as president of the Rocky Mountain Urologic Society and the South Central Section of the American Urological Association.[^2]

## Contributions

Flynn's cited work covers reconstruction of posterior and bulbar urethral stenosis and refractory bladder neck contracture, including buccal mucosal graft and nontransecting approaches.[^3][^4][^5][^6]
