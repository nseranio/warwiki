## Contributions

Flynn's cited work covers urethral and bladder-neck reconstruction, including buccal mucosal graft approaches to posterior stenosis and refractory bladder-neck contracture, non-transecting bulbar urethroplasty, and female urethroplasty.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
