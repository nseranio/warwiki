## Biography

Katherine Jean Cotter is a Kaiser Permanente urologist whose practice focuses mainly on reconstructive urology.[^1] Cotter attended the Keck School of Medicine of USC and completed urology residency at the University of Minnesota Hospitals and Clinics.[^1] Cotter completed a fellowship in genitourinary reconstruction and prosthetics at the University of Iowa Hospitals and Clinics.[^1]

## Contributions

Cotter's cited urethroplasty studies compare transecting and nontransecting bulbar repair and describe urethral stricture etiology and surgical technique across institutions.[^2][^3]
