## Biography

David R. Couillard owns and practices urology at Roseville Urology in Roseville, California.[^1][^2] He attended the University of Vermont College of Medicine, interned in surgery at George Washington University, and completed residency at the University of California, Davis.[^3] He completed a urodynamics fellowship in female and reconstructive urology at Duke University Medical Center in 1994-95.[^3][^2]
