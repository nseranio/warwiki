## Biography

Niall T.M. Galloway retired from Emory University's Department of Urology in December 2020, after serving as an associate professor and director of the Emory Continence Center.[^1][^2] Galloway graduated from Aberdeen University medical school in 1974 and completed general surgery training in the United Kingdom.[^1] Galloway then trained in female pelvic and reconstructive surgery at Duke University School of Medicine, in the fellowship established by George D. Webster.[^1][^3][^4] Galloway established the Emory Continence Center in 1995.[^1]

## Contributions

Galloway's confirmed papers cover female pelvic reconstruction, including rectus abdominis flap repair of urethrovaginal fistula and paravaginal defect repair for stress incontinence and cystocele. They also examine gastric segments for antegrade enema access and adult lower urinary tract reconstruction.[^5][^6][^7][^8]
