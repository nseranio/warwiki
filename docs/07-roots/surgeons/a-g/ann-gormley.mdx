## Known for

- **Urinary Incontinence Treatment Network’s TOMUS trial** (2010). As network steering committee chair, Gormley oversaw the randomized comparison of retropubic and transobturator midurethral slings.[^4] Cited on WARWIKI: [Retropubic Mid-Urethral Sling](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/retropubic-midurethral-sling).
- **2012 AUA/SUFU overactive bladder guideline** (2012). Gormley was lead author of the AUA/SUFU guideline on diagnosis and treatment of nonneurogenic overactive bladder.[^5]
- **Urinary Incontinence Treatment Network’s VaLUE trial** (2012). Gormley oversaw the trial comparing office evaluation alone with added preoperative urodynamics before uncomplicated stress-incontinence surgery.[^6] Cited on WARWIKI: [Stress Urinary Incontinence (Female)](/docs/clinical-conditions/03a-storage-incontinence/sui-female).
