## Contributions

Campeau's cited work covers female bladder outlet obstruction and urethral stricture reconstruction, including a Blandy flap chapter. Other cited work examines neurogenic bladder pharmacotherapy and urodynamic antibiotic prophylaxis.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
