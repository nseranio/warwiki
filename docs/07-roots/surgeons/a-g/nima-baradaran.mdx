## Biography

Nima Baradaran is a urologist at Urology of Virginia and a clinical associate professor of urology at EVMS, Old Dominion University.[^1][^2] He earned his medical degree at Tehran University of Medical Sciences and completed urology residency at the Medical University of South Carolina.[^1] Baradaran completed a genitourinary trauma and reconstruction fellowship at the University of California, San Francisco, in 2018, in a program directed by Benjamin N. Breyer.[^3]

## Contributions

His cited work addresses urethral disorders, including female urethral diverticula, stricture recurrence after urethroplasty, and the manifestations and treatment of lichen sclerosus.[^4][^5][^6]
