## Known for

- **Damage control for urologic injuries** (1997). Described temporary drainage and delayed reconstruction strategies for genitourinary injuries in unstable trauma patients.[^2]
- **Pelvic fracture urethral injury treatment series** (2005). Coauthored a comparative series evaluating early urethral realignment and delayed urethroplasty after pelvic fracture.[^3]
