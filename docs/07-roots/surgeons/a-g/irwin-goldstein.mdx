## Contributions

His cited papers cover erectile dysfunction treatments, including sildenafil, vascular stents, and penile revascularization. Other cited work addresses collagenase treatment for Peyronie's disease and the assessment and management of women's sexual dysfunction.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]

- **Oral sildenafil erectile dysfunction trial** (1998). Led randomized studies establishing the efficacy and tolerability of oral sildenafil for erectile dysfunction.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Erectile Dysfunction (Condition)](/docs/clinical-conditions/03g-genital-scrotal/erectile-dysfunction).
- **Textbook of Female Sexual Function and Dysfunction** (2018). Co-edited a multidisciplinary textbook covering diagnosis and treatment of female sexual function and dysfunction.[^11]
- **ISSWSH consensus on persistent genital arousal disorder** (2021). Led consensus work that broadened the nomenclature to genito-pelvic dysesthesia and proposed a diagnostic and treatment process.[^12]
