## Known for

- **Managed-care interstitial cystitis epidemiology** (2005). Estimated diagnosed interstitial cystitis incidence and prevalence in a managed-care population using several case definitions.[^3]
- **MAPP Research Network leadership** (2014). Chaired the MAPP Research Network and first-authored a description of its approach to urologic chronic pelvic pain syndromes.[^4]
- **AUA interstitial cystitis/bladder pain syndrome guideline** (2022). First-authored the 2022 AUA guidance on diagnosis and treatment of interstitial cystitis/bladder pain syndrome.[^5] Cited on WARWIKI: [Supratrigonal Cystectomy with Augmentation Cystoplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04b-bladder-reconstruction/supratrigonal-cystectomy).
