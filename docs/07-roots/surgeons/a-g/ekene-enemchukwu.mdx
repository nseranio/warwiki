## Biography

Ekene A. Enemchukwu is an associate professor of urology at Stanford University School of Medicine and director of urology at its Multidisciplinary Pelvic Health Center.[^1][^2] She earned an MD and MPH from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill and completed a urology residency at Vanderbilt University.[^2] She completed a fellowship in urogynecology and reconstructive pelvic surgery, male voiding dysfunction, and neurourology at NYU Langone Medical Center.[^2] She serves on the boards of directors of the Society of Women in Urology and the Western Section of the American Urologic Association.[^2]

## Contributions

Her cited chapters address transvesical repair of vesicovaginal fistula and diagnosis of urogenital fistula.[^3][^4]
