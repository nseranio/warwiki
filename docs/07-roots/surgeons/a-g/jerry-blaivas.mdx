## Known for

- **Blaivas stress urinary incontinence classification** (1988). Proposed a classification of female stress urinary incontinence using bladder-neck descent and intrinsic sphincter integrity.[^9]
- **Autologous pubovaginal sling series for complicated stress incontinence** (1991). Reported outcomes in 67 consecutive women treated with a pubovaginal fascial sling for complicated stress incontinence.[^10]
- **Blaivas–Groutz female bladder outlet obstruction nomogram** (2000). Developed a pressure-flow nomogram to classify bladder outlet obstruction in women with lower urinary tract symptoms.[^11]

## Contributions

Blaivas's cited work covers female urethral stricture and vaginal flap reconstruction, including surgical outcomes, and the diagnosis of urethral diverticula and periurethral masses.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
