## Key Publications

Brant's cited work covers priapism shunting, artificial urinary sphincter erosion in the TURNS multicenter cohort and infection after inflatable penile prosthesis placement.<sup>[P1](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P2](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P3](#publication-6)</sup>

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**T-shaped shunt for ischemic priapism** (2009). Led a series of 13 men treated under local anesthesia with the T-shunt and intracavernous tunneling: cavernous blood flow was restored in all but 1, and all but 2 recovered erectile function.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Artificial urinary sphincter erosion** (2014). Led the TURNS prospective study of 386 sphincters placed at 8 institutions: 31 (8.0%) were explanted, with higher rates after prior radiation, prior UroLume stent placement and 3.5 cm cuffs.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[2]} citations={citations}>

**Inflatable penile prosthesis infection** (2025). Led an analysis of 18,475 US hospital implantations: the infection rate was 3.1% at a median follow-up of 3 years, and type 2 diabetes, spinal cord injury, obesity and concomitant circumcision were associated with higher rates.

</KeyPublication>

</ol>
