## Known for

- **Sacrospinous ligament fixation anatomy** (2007). Coauthored a cadaver study mapping nerves and vessels near sacrospinous ligament fixation sutures.[^7] Cited on WARWIKI: [Iliococcygeus Fascia Suspension](/docs/surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/iliococcygeus-suspension).
- **Uterosacral suspension suture anatomy** (2007). Coauthored a cadaver study measuring uterosacral suspension sutures’ proximity to ureters, rectum, nerves, and pelvic vessels.[^8]

## Contributions

Her cited work examines surgical anatomy of the abdominal wall, pelvic nerves, retropubic urethra, and vulva and vagina, including cadaveric studies with operative applications.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
