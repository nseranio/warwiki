## Contributions

His research includes medical therapy for overactive bladder in women and simulation-based teaching of pelvic anatomy.

- **Tolterodine and vaginal estradiol for overactive bladder** (2016). Led a randomized trial in 58 women comparing extended-release tolterodine with intravaginal estradiol cream; symptom bother improved within both groups at 12 weeks without a difference between them, and combined therapy brought further improvement at 24 and 52 weeks.[^4]
- **Virtual reality pelvic anatomy model** (2019). Led a randomized trial in 31 obstetrics and gynecology residents; adding a virtual reality anatomic model to a traditional curriculum did not raise knowledge scores more than the traditional curriculum alone, though most residents reported that it improved their understanding.[^5]
