## Biography

Tamsin Greenwell is a consultant urological surgeon at University College London Hospital and leads its Female, Functional and Restorative Urology Unit.[^1] She qualified in medicine at Manchester University in 1990 and trained in urology in Brighton, Guy's and Thomas's, UCLH, and the Institute of Urology.[^2] She chaired the FNUU section of the British Association of Urological Surgeons from 2016 to 2018.[^2][^3]

## Contributions

Her cited work covers female lower urinary tract reconstruction, including urogenital fistula repair with Martius flap interposition, urethral diverticulum surgery, female urethroplasty, and augmentation cystoplasty.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
