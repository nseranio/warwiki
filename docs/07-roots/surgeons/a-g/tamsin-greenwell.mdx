## Contributions

Her cited work covers female lower urinary tract reconstruction, including urogenital fistula repair with Martius flap interposition, urethral diverticulum surgery, female urethroplasty, and augmentation cystoplasty.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

- **Female urethral diverticulum review** (2015). Coauthored a clinical review of presentation, diagnosis and management of female urethral diverticula.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Urethral Diverticulectomy](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/urethral-diverticulum-repair).
- **Female urethral stricture reconstruction series** (2017). Senior-authored a series reporting outcomes of graft and flap urethroplasty for female urethral stricture.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Blandy Flap](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps/blandy).
- **Vesicovaginal fistula repair comparison** (2020). Senior-authored a series comparing success and provider costs of vaginal and open abdominal vesicovaginal fistula repair.[^11]
