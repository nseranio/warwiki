## Biography

Amjad H. Alwaal is an associate professor of urology at Rutgers New Jersey Medical School and director of reconstructive urology and men's health at University Hospital Newark.[^1][^2] He earned his medical degree at King Abdulaziz University and completed his urology residency at McGill University.[^3] He completed fellowships in urologic reconstruction and trauma and in andrology at the University of California, San Francisco, with Benjamin Breyer and Tom Lue.[^3]

## Contributions

Alwaal's cited work includes grafting techniques for male genital reconstruction, including split-thickness skin grafting and extra-tunical grafting for Peyronie's disease deformity.[^4][^5]
