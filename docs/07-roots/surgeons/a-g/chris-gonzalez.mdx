## Known for

- **Prospective ejaculatory-function study after urethroplasty** (2010). Senior-authored a prospective assessment of ejaculatory function after anterior urethral reconstruction.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Male Urethral Stricture](/docs/clinical-conditions/03b-voiding-outlet/urethral-stricture).

## Contributions

Gonzalez's cited papers cover male urethral stricture evaluation and reconstruction, including distal strictures and repeat hypospadias repair, as well as patient-reported symptoms and sexual function after urethroplasty.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
