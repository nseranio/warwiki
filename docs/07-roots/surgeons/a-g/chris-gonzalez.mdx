## Biography

Christopher M. Gonzalez is chief clinical officer for Loyola University Health System and chair of urology at Loyola University Medical Center and Stritch School of Medicine.[^1] Gonzalez earned his medical degree at the University of Iowa Carver College of Medicine and completed a general surgery internship and urology residency at the McGaw Medical Center of Northwestern University.[^2] He is president of the Society of Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgeons.[^1]
