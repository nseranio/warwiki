## Biography

Mohamed A. Ghoneim is an emeritus professor at Mansoura University.[^1] He attended the University of Cairo's Faculty of Medicine and trained in general surgery and urology at Cairo University Hospitals.[^1] He held a clinical urology fellowship at Memorial Sloan Kettering in New York and a urodynamics research fellowship at the University of Sherbrooke Medical Centre in Quebec.[^2] He directed the Urology and Nephrology Center in Mansoura from 1983 to 2002.[^2]

## Contributions

His confirmed papers describe ileal bladder substitutes and urinary diversion, including the urethral Kock pouch and serous-lined extramural ureteral reimplantation. They also cover Yang-Monti ileal substitution for long ureteral defects.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
