## Contributions

Brandes's cited work covers urethral stricture evaluation and reconstruction, including lichen sclerosus, pelvic fracture injury, and radiation-associated strictures. Other cited papers address urinary fistula repair, including repair failure after pelvic radiation.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]

- **External genital gunshot injury series** (1995). Reported a 56-patient series on evaluation and surgical repair of external genital gunshot injuries.[^10]
- **UREThRAL stricture score** (2012). Co-developed a score describing anterior urethral stricture characteristics and their relationship to surgical complexity.[^11] Cited on WARWIKI: [Male Urethral Stricture](/docs/clinical-conditions/03b-voiding-outlet/urethral-stricture).
