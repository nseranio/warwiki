## Biography

Adam S. Baumgarten is an associate professor and vice chair of clinical affairs at UAB Medicine.[^1] He earned his medical degree and completed urology residency at the University of South Florida.[^1] Baumgarten completed a urologic trauma, reconstruction, and prosthetics fellowship at the University of Texas Southwestern Medical Center.[^2]

## Contributions

His cited studies address artificial urinary sphincter selection and complications, including sling comparison, cuff erosion, pressure-regulating balloon herniation, and permanent urethral ligation after erosion. His penile prosthesis papers cover simultaneous suprapubic fat-pad excision and high submuscular reservoir placement.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
