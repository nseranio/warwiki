## Biography

Claire Burton is an assistant clinical professor in the Division of Urology and Urologic Oncology at City of Hope.[^1] She earned her medical degree at the Keck School of Medicine of the University of Southern California and completed a urology residency at UCLA.[^1] She completed a female pelvic medicine and reconstructive surgery fellowship at Stanford University in 2023 under program director Craig Comiter.[^2][^3]
