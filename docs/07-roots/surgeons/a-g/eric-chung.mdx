## Known for

- **International penile prosthesis implant consensus** (2022). Chung led an international consensus on patient selection, operative principles and postoperative care for three-piece inflatable penile prostheses.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Revision Scenarios in Penile Prosthesis Surgery](/docs/surgical-techniques/04j-sexual-dysfunction/penile-implants/revision-scenarios).
- **Asia-Pacific penile reconstruction and prosthetics recommendations** (2023). He led society recommendations covering penile prostheses, Peyronie’s disease, trauma, phalloplasty and penile augmentation.[^9]

## Contributions

His cited papers address penile prosthesis devices and implantation, including malleable devices, residual curvature correction, and changes in device design. Other papers cover Peyronie's disease management and artificial urinary sphincter technology and surgical guidance.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
