## Biography

Justin Chee is a urological surgeon in Melbourne and an adjunct lecturer in surgery at Monash University.[^1] He earned his MBBS at the University of Melbourne, began urology training in 2006, and attained FRACS (Urology) in 2011.[^2][^3] He later trained in urethral reconstruction with Sanjay Kulkarni in India and Guido Barbagli in Italy.[^3] He is a founding faculty member of the International Society of Reconstructive Urology and leads the Reconstruction and Paediatric Urology Special Advisory Group of the Urological Society of Australia and New Zealand.[^1]

## Contributions

His confirmed citations cover urethral stricture reconstruction, including vaginal-sparing buccal graft urethroplasty and a lotus petal flap chapter, as well as quality of life during intermittent self-dilation.[^4][^5][^6]
