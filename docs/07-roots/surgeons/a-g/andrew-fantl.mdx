## Contributions

- **Bladder-training trial in older women** (1991). Fantl led a randomized trial in 123 older women that found fewer incontinence episodes after bladder training.[^5] Cited on WARWIKI: [Behavioral Therapy for Urinary Incontinence](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/behavioral-therapy-incontinence).
- **Estrogen-supplementation trial for urinary incontinence** (1996). Fantl led a placebo-controlled trial finding that three months of cyclic hormone therapy did not improve incontinence outcomes.[^6]
