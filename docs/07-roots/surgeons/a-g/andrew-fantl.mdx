## Key Publications

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Bladder-training trial in older women** (1991). Fantl led a randomized trial in 123 older women that found fewer incontinence episodes after bladder training.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>
