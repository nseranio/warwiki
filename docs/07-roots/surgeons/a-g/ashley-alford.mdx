## Biography

Ashley V. Alford is a urologist with Brown Health Medical Group Urology at Newport Hospital in Rhode Island.[^1] She received her medical degree from Columbia University College of Physicians and Surgeons and completed her urology residency at the University of Minnesota.[^1] She completed a fellowship in reconstructive urology at NYU.[^1]

## Contributions

Her cited papers address robotic urinary tract reconstruction, including posterior urethroplasty outcomes, bladder neck reconstruction patency and incontinence, and single-port repair of transplant ureteral strictures.[^2][^3][^4]
