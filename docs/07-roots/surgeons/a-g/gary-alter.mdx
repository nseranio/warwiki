## Biography

Gary J. Alter practices plastic surgery privately and is an assistant clinical professor of plastic surgery at the UCLA School of Medicine.[^1] Alter earned his MD at the UCLA School of Medicine and completed urology residency at Baylor College of Medicine and plastic surgery residency at the Mayo Clinic.[^1] Alter completed a fellowship in genitourinary reconstruction and plastic surgery at Eastern Virginia Graduate School of Medicine.[^1]

## Contributions

Alter's cited papers address correction of hidden penis, reconstruction after penile enlargement surgery, and pubic and labial contouring after major weight loss. Other cited work describes labia minora and clitoral hood surgery, augmentation phalloplasty, and prosthetic implantation after phallic construction.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
