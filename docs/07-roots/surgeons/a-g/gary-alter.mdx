## Contributions

Alter's cited papers address correction of hidden penis, reconstruction after penile enlargement surgery, and pubic and labial contouring after major weight loss. Other cited work describes labia minora and clitoral hood surgery, augmentation phalloplasty, and prosthetic implantation after phallic construction.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
