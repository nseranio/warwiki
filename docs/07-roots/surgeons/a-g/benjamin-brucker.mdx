## Known for

- **Gel-infused translabial ultrasound for female urethral stricture** (2020). Brucker was senior author of the clinical description of gel-infused translabial ultrasound for assessing female urethral strictures.[^9]
- **Dorsal-onlay oral mucosa graft series for female urethral stricture** (2021). Brucker was senior author of a two-institution series reporting outcomes of dorsal-onlay oral mucosa graft urethroplasty in women.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Female Dorsal Onlay Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dorsal-onlay-urethroplasty).

## Contributions

Brucker's confirmed cited work covers overactive bladder therapies, including onabotulinumtoxinA, vibegron, and treatment options for men. It also addresses urodynamic testing and female voiding dysfunction.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
