## Contributions

Bump's cited work covers standardized terminology and staging for female pelvic organ prolapse and the PFDI-20 and PFIQ-7 short-form pelvic floor questionnaires.[^4][^5][^6]

- **POP-Q prolapse measurement system** (1996). Chaired the committee that published a standardized system for measuring and staging female pelvic organ prolapse.[^7] Cited on WARWIKI: [POP Overview](/docs/clinical-conditions/03c-pelvic-support/pelvic-organ-prolapse).
- **Duke urogynecology fellowship**. Co-founded Duke University’s urogynecology fellowship with W. Allen Addison.[^8]
