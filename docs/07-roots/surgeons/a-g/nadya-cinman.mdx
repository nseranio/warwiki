## Biography

Nadya M. Cinman is regional director of reconstructive urology for Kaiser Permanente Northern California in Oakland.[^1][^2] Cinman attended Boston University for medical school and graduated from the Hofstra/Northwell urology residency in 2011.[^3] Cinman completed UCSF's male genitourinary reconstruction and trauma fellowship in 2012 under Jack W. McAninch.[^1][^4]
