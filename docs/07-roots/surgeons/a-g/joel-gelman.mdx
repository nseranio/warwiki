## Contributions

His cited work covers urethral stricture management, including staged and buccal graft urethroplasty, posterior stenosis repair, direct-vision dilation, and a visualizing urethral sound.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Direct-vision balloon dilation of urethral stricture** (2011). Described use of a balloon dilator that permits urethral stricture dilation under direct vision.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [DVIU and Urethral Dilation](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/dviu).
- **Combined buccal graft and penile skin flap urethroplasty** (2011). Reported single-stage repair of obliterative distal urethral strictures using a dorsal buccal graft and ventral penile skin flap.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Combined Dorsal BMG + Ventral Fasciocutaneous Flap (Erickson)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/erickson-dorsal-bmg-ventral-flap).
- **Dorsal and ventral double-buccal-graft urethroplasty** (2014). Reported single-stage dorsal and ventral buccal graft reconstruction for long anterior strictures with obliterative segments.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Ventral Onlay Oral Mucosal Graft Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/ventral-onlay-omg).
