## Biography

Joel Gelman is a clinical professor of urology and chief of the Division of Reconstructive Urology at UC Irvine.[^1] He earned his medical degree from the Medical College of Ohio at Toledo and completed a urology residency at UCLA.[^2] He completed a fellowship in adult and pediatric male genitourinary reconstruction at the Devine Center in Norfolk, Virginia, under Gerald Jordan.[^1] He founded and codirects an endowed GURS fellowship in male genitourinary reconstruction at UC Irvine.[^1]

## Contributions

His cited work covers urethral stricture management, including staged and buccal graft urethroplasty, posterior stenosis repair, direct-vision dilation, and a visualizing urethral sound.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
