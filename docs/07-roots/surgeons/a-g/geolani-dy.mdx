## Biography

Geolani W. Dy is an associate professor of urology at Oregon Health & Science University.[^1][^2] She earned her MD at the Alpert Medical School of Brown University and completed urology residency at the University of Washington.[^1][^3] Dy completed a reconstructive, robotic, and gender affirming surgery fellowship at New York University in 2018–2019; NYU lists Lee C. Zhao as the fellowship director.[^1][^4] Dy serves on the board of the Society of Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgeons and directs OHSU's GURS fellowship.[^2]

## Contributions

Dy's cited work covers gender affirming genital reconstruction, including robotic peritoneal flap vaginoplasty and revision, vulvar and clitoral construction, and urologic complications after masculinizing surgery.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]
