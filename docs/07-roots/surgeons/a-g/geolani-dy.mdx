## Contributions

Dy's cited work covers gender affirming genital reconstruction, including robotic peritoneal flap vaginoplasty and revision, vulvar and clitoral construction, and urologic complications after masculinizing surgery.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]

- **Robotic peritoneal flap revision for neovaginal stenosis** (2021). Reported a 24-patient series using robotic peritoneal flaps to revise a short, stenotic or absent neovaginal canal.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Peritoneal Flap Vaginoplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery/peritoneal-pull-through-vaginoplasty).
- **TRANS Registry for genital surgery outcomes** (2024). Led development of a multisite registry incorporating patient priorities and reported outcomes after genital gender-affirming surgery.[^11]
