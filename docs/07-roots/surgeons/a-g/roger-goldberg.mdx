## Biography

Roger P. Goldberg leads the Division of Urogynecology and Reconstructive Pelvic Surgery at Endeavor Health and is a clinical associate professor at the University of Chicago Pritzker School of Medicine.[^1] He attended Northwestern University Medical School and completed an obstetrics and gynecology residency at Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center.[^2][^1] He completed a urogynecology fellowship at NorthShore University HealthSystem in 2002.[^1]

## Contributions

His cited studies examine pelvic organ prolapse surgery, including slings, sacrospinous suspension, hysteropexy, and rectocele repair. Other cited papers examine rectal mobility, obstructed defecation, and transvaginal rectopexy.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
