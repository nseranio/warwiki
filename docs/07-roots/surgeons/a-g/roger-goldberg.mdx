## Contributions

His cited studies examine pelvic organ prolapse surgery, including slings, sacrospinous suspension, hysteropexy, and rectocele repair. Other cited papers examine rectal mobility, obstructed defecation, and transvaginal rectopexy.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Suburethral slings and cystocele recurrence** (2001). Led a study associating suburethral sling placement with lower cystocele recurrence after pelvic reconstructive surgery.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Anterior Compartment Prolapse](/docs/clinical-conditions/03c-pelvic-support/anterior-compartment).
- **Evanston-Northwestern Twin Sisters Study** (2005). Used identical-twin data to examine the association between delivery mode and stress urinary incontinence.[^9]
