## Key Publications

Barbagli's cited work examines urethral stricture reconstruction with skin flaps and oral mucosal grafts, including dorsal, ventral, and staged urethroplasty. The papers also cover oral graft harvest morbidity, tissue-engineered grafts, and patient-reported outcomes after urethroplasty.<sup>[P3](#publication-3)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-7)</sup>
