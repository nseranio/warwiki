## Contributions

Barbagli's cited work examines urethral stricture reconstruction with skin flaps and oral mucosal grafts, including dorsal, ventral, and staged urethroplasty. The papers also cover oral graft harvest morbidity, tissue-engineered grafts, and patient-reported outcomes after urethroplasty.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Dorsal free-graft urethroplasty** (1996). Described placing a free graft dorsally during anterior urethral reconstruction, the basis of the Barbagli dorsal onlay technique.[^8]
- **One-stage bulbar urethroplasty outcomes series** (2008). Reported outcomes for 375 patients undergoing one-stage bulbar urethroplasty with anastomotic and graft techniques.[^9]
