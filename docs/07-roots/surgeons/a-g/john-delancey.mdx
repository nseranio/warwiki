## Biography

John O. L. DeLancey is the Norman F. Miller Legacy Professor of Gynecology and a professor of obstetrics and gynecology and urology at the University of Michigan Medical School.[^1] He received his medical degree from the University of Michigan Medical School and completed his obstetrics and gynecology residency at the University of Michigan Health System.[^2] He has served as president of the American Urogynecologic Society, the Society of Gynecologic Surgeons, and the American Gynecological and Obstetrical Society.[^2]

## Contributions

His cited work examines pelvic floor support, including apical and perineal structures, levator ani injury, and MRI models of prolapse. It also addresses urethral support and closure in stress urinary incontinence, including the hammock hypothesis.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
