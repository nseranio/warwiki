## Contributions

His cited work examines pelvic floor support, including apical and perineal structures, levator ani injury, and MRI models of prolapse. It also addresses urethral support and closure in stress urinary incontinence, including the hammock hypothesis.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Three levels of vaginal support** (1992). Described distinct apical, lateral and distal vaginal support attachments through cadaveric dissection.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Apical Prolapse](/docs/clinical-conditions/03c-pelvic-support/apical-prolapse).
- **Urethral support hammock hypothesis** (1994). Proposed that the anterior vaginal wall and endopelvic fascia provide a supportive layer against which abdominal pressure compresses the urethra.[^9]
- **MRI demonstration of levator ani defects after vaginal delivery** (2003). Documented levator ani defects on MRI in women after vaginal delivery and their absence in nulliparous controls.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [The Perineum](/docs/foundations/anatomy-physiology/pelvis-support/perineum).
