## Key Publications

His cited work examines pelvic floor support, including apical and perineal structures, levator ani injury, and MRI models of prolapse. It also addresses urethral support and closure in stress urinary incontinence, including the hammock hypothesis.<sup>[P4](#publication-3)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P8](#publication-7)</sup>
