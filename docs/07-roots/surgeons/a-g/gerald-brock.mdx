## Contributions

His cited papers examine erectile dysfunction treatments and Peyronie's disease anatomy, imaging, and surgical grafting. He also coauthored AUA/SMSNA guidelines on ischemic, recurrent, and nonischemic priapism.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Deep dorsal vein grafting for Peyronie’s disease** (1993). Reported plaque incision with deep dorsal vein grafting as part of a surgical series for Peyronie’s disease.[^8]
