## Biography

Gerald B. Brock is medical director of the Advanced Surgical Operatory at Advanced Medical Group in London, Ontario.[^1][^2] He graduated from McGill University medical school in 1986, completed his urology residency there, and then completed a fellowship at the University of California, San Francisco.[^2] He previously served as president of the International Society for Sexual Medicine.[^2]

## Contributions

His cited papers examine erectile dysfunction treatments and Peyronie's disease anatomy, imaging, and surgical grafting. He also coauthored AUA/SMSNA guidelines on ischemic, recurrent, and nonischemic priapism.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
