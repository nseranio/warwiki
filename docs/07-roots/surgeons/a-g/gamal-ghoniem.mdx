## Biography

Gamal M. Ghoniem is emeritus professor of urology at the UCI Health School of Medicine.[^1][^2] He attended Alexandria Medical School and completed urology residencies at Alexandria University Hospitals and the University of Michigan Medical Center.[^2] He completed a fellowship in neurourology and female urology at Brown University.[^2] He previously chaired the International Urogynecological Association's Research and Development Committee.[^2]

## Contributions

His cited work on Macroplastique for female stress urinary incontinence includes a randomized comparison, follow-up studies, and a systematic review and meta-analysis.[^3][^4][^5][^6]
