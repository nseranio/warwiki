## Contributions

Her research includes sister-pair studies of parity and pelvic floor disorders, vaginal reconstructive surgery under local anesthesia with sedation, and intraoperative cystoscopy dyes.[^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Urinary incontinence in nulliparous women and their parous sisters** (2005). Led a study of 143 postmenopausal sister pairs in which urinary incontinence was reported by 47.6% of nulliparous and 49.7% of parous sisters, with high concordance of continence status within pairs.[^4]
- **Local anesthesia with sedation for vaginal reconstructive surgery** (2006). Led a series in which 98 of 127 candidates chose local anesthesia with sedation for prolapse operations including colpocleisis and LeFort procedures; none required conversion to general anesthesia.[^5]
- **Anaphylaxis after intravenous fluorescein for cystoscopy** (2018). Senior author of a case report of anaphylactic shock after intravenous sodium fluorescein given to assess ureteral efflux during urethrolysis and cystoscopy.[^7] Cited on WARWIKI: [Sodium Fluorescein](/docs/foundations/pharmacology/intraoperative-adjuncts/visualization-agents/sodium-fluorescein), [Intraoperative Visualization Agents](/docs/foundations/pharmacology/intraoperative-adjuncts/visualization-agents).
