## Key Publications

Her research includes sister-pair studies of parity and pelvic floor disorders, vaginal reconstructive surgery under local anesthesia with sedation, and intraoperative cystoscopy dyes.<sup>[P1](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P2](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P3](#publication-7)</sup>

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Urinary incontinence in nulliparous women and their parous sisters** (2005). Led a study of 143 postmenopausal sister pairs in which urinary incontinence was reported by 47.6% of nulliparous and 49.7% of parous sisters, with high concordance of continence status within pairs.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Local anesthesia with sedation for vaginal reconstructive surgery** (2006). Led a series in which 98 of 127 candidates chose local anesthesia with sedation for prolapse operations including colpocleisis and LeFort procedures; none required conversion to general anesthesia.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[2]} citations={citations}>

**Anaphylaxis after intravenous fluorescein for cystoscopy** (2018). Senior author of a case report of anaphylactic shock after intravenous sodium fluorescein given to assess ureteral efflux during urethrolysis and cystoscopy.

</KeyPublication>
