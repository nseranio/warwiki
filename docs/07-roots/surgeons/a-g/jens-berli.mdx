## Known for

- **Technical framework for neourethra creation during phalloplasty** (2021). Compared single-stage and two-stage strategies for constructing the penile and bulbar urethra during phalloplasty.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Big Ben Method for Phalloplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery/big-ben-method).
- **Big Ben two-stage phalloplasty urethral-lengthening series** (2025). Reported long-term urologic outcomes for his two-stage urethral-lengthening method in 73 phalloplasty patients.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Masculinizing Gender-Affirming Surgery](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery/masculinizing-surgery).

## Contributions

Berli's cited work covers gender-affirming genital surgery, including phalloplasty techniques and outcomes, radial forearm nerve considerations, and vaginoplasty or vulvoplasty care.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
