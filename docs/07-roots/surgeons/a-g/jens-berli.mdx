## Biography

Jens U. Berli is professor of surgery and head of the Division of Plastic and Reconstructive Surgery at Oregon Health & Science University.[^1] He earned his medical degree at the University of Zurich in Switzerland.[^2] He trained in general surgery and orthopedic trauma at Triemli Hospital in Zurich and in general surgery at Johns Hopkins Hospital.[^2] He completed an integrated plastic surgery residency at Johns Hopkins Hospital and the University of Maryland Medical Center.[^2]

## Contributions

Berli's cited work covers gender-affirming genital surgery, including phalloplasty techniques and outcomes, radial forearm nerve considerations, and vaginoplasty or vulvoplasty care.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
