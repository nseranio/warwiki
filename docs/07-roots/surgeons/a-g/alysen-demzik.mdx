## Biography

Alysen Demzik is an assistant professor of urology at Rush University and practices reconstructive urology.[^1] She earned her medical degree at Northwestern University Feinberg School of Medicine and completed her urology residency at the University of North Carolina.[^1] She completed a genitourinary reconstruction fellowship at the University of Minnesota in the program led by Sean Elliott, with Joseph Pariser on the faculty.[^1][^2]

## Contributions

Her cited papers cover genital reconstructive procedures, including scrotal skin grafting after necrotizing fasciitis, iterative penile plication for Peyronie's disease, and ring flap metoidioplasty.[^3][^4][^5][^6]
