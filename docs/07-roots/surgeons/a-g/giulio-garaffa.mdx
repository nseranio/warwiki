## Biography

Giulio Garaffa is a consultant uro-andrologist at Harley Street Specialist Hospital in London.[^1] He completed his medical and specialist urological training at the University of Trieste in Italy.[^1] He undertook a three-year fellowship in andrology and complex male genital reconstruction at University College London Hospitals and became a substantive consultant in 2011.[^1]

## Contributions

His cited work covers penile and scrotal reconstruction, including radial forearm flap phalloplasty, urethral construction, and surgery for genital lymphedema. Other papers examine penile prosthesis placement after phalloplasty and ischemic priapism, and surgery for Peyronie's disease.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
