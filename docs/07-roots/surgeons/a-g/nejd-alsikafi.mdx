## Biography

Nejd F. Alsikafi practices at UroPartners in Gurnee, Illinois, and serves as a clinical associate professor at Loyola University Health System.[^1][^2] He received his medical degree from the University of Chicago Pritzker School of Medicine and completed his urology residency at the University of Chicago.[^2] He completed a fellowship in urologic trauma and reconstruction at the University of California, San Francisco, under program director Jack McAninch.[^2][^1] Alsikafi founded the Chicago Center for Urologic Reconstruction and Trauma and served as president and secretary of the Trauma and Urologic Reconstruction Network of Surgeons.[^2][^1]
