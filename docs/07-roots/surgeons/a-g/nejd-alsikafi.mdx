## Contributions

Alsikafi's cited work covers flap urethroplasty for complex anterior urethral stricture, nonoperative management of renal trauma and multicenter TURNS outcomes of robotic posterior stenosis repair.[^4][^5][^6]

- **Penile circular fasciocutaneous flap urethroplasty** (2008). As senior author on the UCSF series of 124 complex anterior strictures without lichen sclerosus, reported stricture-free survival of 95%, 89%, 84% and 79% at 1, 3, 5 and 10 years; smoking, prior hypospadias repair and stricture length of 7 to 10 cm predicted failure.[^4] Cited on WARWIKI: [McAninch Transverse Circular Penile Skin Flap](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/mcaninch-circular-flap), [Orandi Flap (Longitudinal Ventral Penile Skin Flap)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/orandi-flap), [Pedicled Penile / Preputial Skin Flap](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps/penile-preputial).
- **Urinary extravasation after renal laceration** (2006). Led a review of 61 patients from the San Francisco General Hospital trauma database: of 34 managed nonoperatively, 3 (9%) had persistent extravasation, and all 3 resolved after endoscopic ureteral stent placement.[^5]
- **Robotic repair of vesicourethral anastomotic stenosis** (2022). Coauthored the TURNS multicenter series of 32 robotic-assisted reconstructions: 75% were patent at a median follow-up of 12 months, and 11 of 13 men without preexisting stress incontinence remained continent.[^6] Cited on WARWIKI: [Vesicourethral Anastomotic Stenosis](/docs/clinical-conditions/03b-voiding-outlet/vesicourethral-anastomotic-stenosis), [Primary Reanastomosis for Refractory Posterior Stenosis](/docs/surgical-techniques/04ab-bladder-neck-reconstruction/bnc-vuas/primary-reanastomosis).
