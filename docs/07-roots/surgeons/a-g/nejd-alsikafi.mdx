## Key Publications

Alsikafi's cited work covers flap urethroplasty for complex anterior urethral stricture, nonoperative management of renal trauma and multicenter TURNS outcomes of robotic posterior stenosis repair.<sup>[P1](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P2](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P3](#publication-6)</sup>

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Penile circular fasciocutaneous flap urethroplasty** (2008). As senior author on the UCSF series of 124 complex anterior strictures without lichen sclerosus, reported stricture-free survival of 95%, 89%, 84% and 79% at 1, 3, 5 and 10 years; smoking, prior hypospadias repair and stricture length of 7 to 10 cm predicted failure.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Urinary extravasation after renal laceration** (2006). Led a review of 61 patients from the San Francisco General Hospital trauma database: of 34 managed nonoperatively, 3 (9%) had persistent extravasation, and all 3 resolved after endoscopic ureteral stent placement.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[2]} citations={citations}>

**Robotic repair of vesicourethral anastomotic stenosis** (2022). Coauthored the TURNS multicenter series of 32 robotic-assisted reconstructions: 75% were patent at a median follow-up of 12 months, and 11 of 13 men without preexisting stress incontinence remained continent.

</KeyPublication>

</ol>
