## Biography

Christopher R. Chapple is an emeritus consultant urological surgeon at Sheffield Teaching Hospitals, an honorary professor at the University of Sheffield, and a visiting professor at Sheffield Hallam University.[^1] He trained at Middlesex Hospital Medical School and received his medical degree in 1980.[^2] He began surgical training in Birmingham, then trained at Middlesex Hospital under Euan Milroy and Richard Turner-Warwick before a fellowship period visiting US reconstructive units.[^2] He served as secretary general of the European Association of Urology from 2015 to 2023.[^1][^3]

## Contributions

Chapple's cited work covers male and female urethral reconstruction, including graft augmentation and substitution urethroplasty. Other confirmed papers examine overactive and underactive bladder treatment, assessment, and terminology.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
