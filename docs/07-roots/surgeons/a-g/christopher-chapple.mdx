## Training and career

<div className="sp-facts">

- **Current position** Emeritus consultant urological surgeon, Sheffield Teaching Hospitals; honorary professor, University of Sheffield; visiting professor, Sheffield Hallam University[^1]

## Contributions

Chapple's cited work covers male and female urethral reconstruction, including graft augmentation and substitution urethroplasty. Other confirmed papers examine overactive and underactive bladder treatment, assessment, and terminology.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

- **Tissue-engineered buccal mucosa urethroplasty series** (2008). Senior-authored a clinical series reporting use of tissue-engineered buccal mucosa grafts for substitution urethroplasty.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Liquid / Minced Buccal Mucosal Graft](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/liquid-minced-buccal).
- **Twelve-month mirabegron phase 3 trial** (2013). First-authored a randomized phase 3 study comparing twelve-month mirabegron treatment with tolterodine for overactive bladder.[^10]
