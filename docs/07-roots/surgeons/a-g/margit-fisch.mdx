## Training and career

<div className="sp-facts">

- **Current position** Professor of urology and director of the Department of Urology, University Medical Center Hamburg-Eppendorf[^1][^2]
- **Medical school** Saarland University[^3]
- **Other training** Surgical year, Clinique St. Thérèse, Luxembourg (1987); specialist urology training, University Urology Clinic Mainz (1988–1992); Professor Hohenfellner as teacher and mentor[^3][^1]
- **Leadership** President, German Society of Urology (2021 and 2022)[^4]

</div>

## Known for

- **Mainz pouch II** (1993). First-authored the description of a low-pressure rectosigmoid urinary reservoir formed by detubularization and reconfiguration.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Ureterosigmoidostomy and Rectosigmoid Diversion](/docs/surgical-techniques/04c-urinary-diversion/ureterosigmoidostomy).
- **Mainz pouch III continent diversion series** (2007). Senior-authored a series evaluating all-colon continent cutaneous urinary diversion, including use after pelvic irradiation.[^11]

## Contributions

Fisch's cited work covers continent urinary diversion, including Mainz pouch configurations, ileocecal valve reconstruction, colon-pouch diversion after pelvic irradiation, and long-term bone and potassium measurements.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]
