## Biography

Margit Maria Fisch is professor of urology and director of the Department of Urology at University Medical Center Hamburg-Eppendorf.[^1][^2] She studied medicine at Saarland University.[^3] She completed a surgical year at Clinique St. Thérèse in Luxembourg in 1987 and specialist urology training at the University Urology Clinic Mainz from 1988 to 1992; she identifies Professor Hohenfellner as her teacher and mentor.[^3][^1] She served as president of the German Society of Urology in 2021 and 2022.[^4]

## Contributions

Fisch's cited work covers continent urinary diversion, including Mainz pouch configurations, ileocecal valve reconstruction, colon-pouch diversion after pelvic irradiation, and long-term bone and potassium measurements.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]
