## Key Publications

Her research covers sexual function after prolapse surgery, uterine preservation at prolapse repair, and complications of sacrocolpopexy. She was second author of a systematic review with clinical practice guidelines comparing uterine preservation with hysterectomy at prolapse surgery.<sup>[P4](#publication-4)</sup>

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Vaginal dilators after posterior colporrhaphy** (2013). Led a randomized trial in 60 sexually active women; daily dilator use from weeks 4 to 8 did not change de novo dyspareunia rates, overall sexual function or global improvement scores at 3 and 6 months.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Sexual activity and function after prolapse surgery** (2020). Led a systematic review of 67 studies in which sexual function improved or was unchanged after every type of reconstructive prolapse surgery, and de novo dyspareunia ranged from 0% to 9% outside posterior repair.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[2]} citations={citations}>

**Lumbosacral discitis after sacrocolpopexy** (2024). Reported 3 cases and reviewed 30 published cases; the complication followed abdominal, laparoscopic and robotic sacrocolpopexy, and most patients needed mesh excision together with prolonged intravenous antibiotics.

</KeyPublication>
