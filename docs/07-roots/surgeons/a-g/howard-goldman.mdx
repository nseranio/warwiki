## Biography

Howard B. Goldman directs the urogynecology and reconstructive pelvic surgery fellowship at Cleveland Clinic's Glickman Urological and Kidney Institute.[^1] He attended Albert Einstein College of Medicine and completed residency at the University of Tennessee Health Sciences Center.[^2] Goldman completed a female urology fellowship at Cleveland Clinic.[^3][^2]

## Contributions

His cited chapters and article address transabdominal and transvaginal vesicovaginal fistula repair and urethrovaginal closure. His studies also examine sacral neuromodulation screening, programming, and lumbosacral MRI in patients with an implanted device.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
