## Known for

- **ICS sacral neuromodulation best practice statement** (2018). Led an International Continence Society panel addressing patient selection, implantation, follow-up and troubleshooting for sacral neuromodulation.[^9]
- **First human study of catheter-free UroMonitor bladder pressure measurement** (2023). Led clinical testing of a wireless intravesical pressure sensor in 11 women undergoing evaluation for overactive bladder symptoms.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Glean Urodynamics System](/docs/foundations/tools/biomaterials/adjunct-specialty/glean-urodynamics).

## Contributions

His cited chapters and article address transabdominal and transvaginal vesicovaginal fistula repair and urethrovaginal closure. His studies also examine sacral neuromodulation screening, programming, and lumbosacral MRI in patients with an implanted device.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
