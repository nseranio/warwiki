## Biography

Gregory Michael Amend is a reconstructive urologist with Catholic Health Physician Partners.[^1] He earned his medical degree at the Renaissance School of Medicine at Stony Brook University and completed urology residency at UC Davis Health.[^1] He trained in the 2020–2021 male genitourinary reconstruction and trauma fellowship at UCSF, whose director was Benjamin N. Breyer.[^2]
