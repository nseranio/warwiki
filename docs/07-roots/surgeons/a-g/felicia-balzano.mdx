## Biography

Felicia L. Balzano is a urologist at Emplify Health La Crosse Urology in La Crosse, Wisconsin.[^1] Balzano completed a reconstructive urology fellowship through City of Hope in 2022, including a second year at Mayo Clinic, where Balzano collaborated with Jonathan N. Warner.[^2][^3][^4]

## Contributions

Balzano's cited papers describe transurethral mucosal realignment for bladder neck contracture and vesicourethral anastomotic stenosis, and buccal graft reconstruction for male membranous and proximal female urethral strictures. A separate paper presents an algorithm for Peyronie's disease during penile implant surgery.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]
