## Biography

Thomas William Fuller is an attending urologist at Virginia Mason Franciscan Health in Seattle.[^1][^2] Fuller studied medicine at Eastern Virginia Medical School and completed urology residency at the University of Pittsburgh Medical Center.[^3] Fuller completed a genitourinary reconstructive fellowship at the University of California San Diego.[^4][^3] Fuller is listed as program director of the Virginia Mason Franciscan Health urology residency.[^5]

## Contributions

Fuller's cited work on adult acquired buried penis covers escutcheonectomy, scrotectomy, penile skin grafting, urinary and sexual quality of life after repair, and concurrent urethral stricture disease.[^6][^7][^8]
