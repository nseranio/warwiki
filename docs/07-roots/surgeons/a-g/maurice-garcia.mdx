## Biography

Maurice Marcel Garcia is a clinical professor of urology at Cedars-Sinai Medical Center and directs its Transgender Surgery and Health Program.[^1] He earned his MD from Georgetown University School of Medicine and completed his urology residency at UCSF.[^2] He completed an andrology and neurourology fellowship at UCSF, followed by fellowships in masculinizing and feminizing genital gender affirming surgery in London and Brighton, United Kingdom.[^1]

## Contributions

His confirmed citations cover masculinizing and feminizing genital gender affirming surgery, including free flap phalloplasty, shallow-depth and right-colon vaginoplasty, and surgical decision making.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
