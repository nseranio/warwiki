## Biography

Michael L. Guralnick is a professor of urology at the Medical College of Wisconsin.[^1] He received his medical degree from McGill University and completed his urology residency at the University of Manitoba-Affiliated Teaching Hospitals.[^1] He completed a fellowship in female and reconstructive urology at Duke University Medical Center in 1999–2000.[^1] He directs the urodynamics laboratory at Froedtert Hospital in Milwaukee.[^1]

## Contributions

His cited work describes augmented anastomotic urethroplasty for urethral stricture and examines artificial urinary sphincter cuff placement and revision for incontinence.[^2][^3][^4][^5]
