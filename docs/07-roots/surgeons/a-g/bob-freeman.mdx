## Known for

- **LAS trial of laparoscopic versus open sacrocolpopexy** (2013). Freeman led a randomized equivalence trial comparing laparoscopic and open sacrocolpopexy for posthysterectomy vaginal vault prolapse.[^5]
- **PROSPECT trials of mesh, graft, and native-tissue prolapse repair** (2017). Freeman was a Plymouth principal investigator in the multicenter PROSPECT trials comparing augmentation with standard vaginal prolapse repair.[^6] Cited on WARWIKI: [Mesh & Graft-Augmented Prolapse Repair](/docs/surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/mesh-graft-augmented-repairs).
