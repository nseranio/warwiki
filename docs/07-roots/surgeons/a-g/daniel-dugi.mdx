## Contributions

His cited work covers gender-affirming genital reconstruction, including vaginoplasty and vulvoplasty planning and technique, clitoroplasty, and shaft-only phalloplasty. Other cited papers address pelvic floor physical therapy and sexual function after vaginoplasty or vulvoplasty. He also coauthored work on urethroplasty and reconstructive plastic surgery techniques.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
