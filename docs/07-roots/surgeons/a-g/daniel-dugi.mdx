## Key Publications

His cited work covers gender-affirming genital reconstruction, including vaginoplasty and vulvoplasty planning and technique, clitoroplasty, and shaft-only phalloplasty. Other cited papers address pelvic floor physical therapy and sexual function after vaginoplasty or vulvoplasty.
