## Biography

Harold P. Drutz is professor emeritus at the University of Toronto and has retired from clinical practice.[^1] Drutz graduated from the University of Toronto's medical school in 1969.[^2] He undertook a McLaughlin Travelling Fellowship in 1975, including six months with Jack Robertson at LAC-USC Medical Centre.[^1][^3] Drutz was founding president of the Canadian Society for Pelvic Medicine and also served as president of the International Urogynecological Association.[^1]
