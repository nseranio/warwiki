<div className="sp-facts">

## Known for

- **Urodynamic assessment of women reporting incontinence** (1979). Reported that symptom histories often failed to distinguish bladder instability from sphincter weakness in a 188-woman urodynamic series.[^4]
- **Placebo-controlled autologous fat bulking trial** (2001). Senior-authored a randomized trial finding periurethral autologous fat injection no more effective than saline for female stress incontinence.[^5] Cited on WARWIKI: [Autologous Fat Injection — Historical Bulking Agent](/docs/foundations/tools/biomaterials/bulking-agents/autologous-fat).

Retired from clinical practice.[^1]
