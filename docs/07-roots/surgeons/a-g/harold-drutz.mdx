## Key Publications

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Urodynamic assessment of women reporting incontinence** (1979). Reported that symptom histories often failed to distinguish bladder instability from sphincter weakness in a 188-woman urodynamic series.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

</KeyPublication>
