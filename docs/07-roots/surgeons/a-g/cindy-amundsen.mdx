## Known for

- **Sacral neuromodulation response-factor study** (2005). Led a study examining patient factors associated with response to sacral neuromodulation for refractory urgency incontinence.[^10]
- **ROSETTA randomized trial** (2016). Led the multicenter comparison of 200-unit intradetrusor onabotulinumtoxinA and sacral neuromodulation for refractory urgency urinary incontinence.[^11] Cited on WARWIKI: [Intradetrusor OnabotulinumtoxinA](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/intradetrusor-botox).
- **ROSETTA two-year outcomes** (2018). Led the two-year follow-up comparing sacral neuromodulation and onabotulinumtoxinA outcomes after treatment for refractory urgency incontinence.[^12] Cited on WARWIKI: [Sacral Neuromodulation](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/sacral-neuromodulation).

## Contributions

Amundsen's cited work covers sacral and implantable tibial neuromodulation for overactive bladder or urgency urinary incontinence, including device outcomes and lead placement. It also compares sacral neuromodulation with onabotulinumtoxinA for refractory urgency urinary incontinence through the ROSETTA trial and follow-up analyses.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]
