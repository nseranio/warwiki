## Key Publications

Amundsen's cited work covers sacral and implantable tibial neuromodulation for overactive bladder or urgency urinary incontinence, including device outcomes and lead placement. It also compares sacral neuromodulation with onabotulinumtoxinA for refractory urgency urinary incontinence through the ROSETTA trial and follow-up analyses.<sup>[P2](#publication-11)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P3](#publication-12)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-8)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-9)</sup>
