## Known for

- **Three-year vaginal mesh versus native-tissue prolapse trial** (2013). First-authored three-year outcomes comparing vaginal mesh with native-tissue prolapse repair; enrollment stopped early after mesh exposures.[^3]
- **Uterine-preserving hysteropexy comparison** (2017). Led a multicenter cohort comparing laparoscopic sacral hysteropexy with vaginal mesh hysteropexy for uterovaginal prolapse.[^4]
