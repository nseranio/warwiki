## Known for

- **Genetic associations with urinary symptoms and prolapse** (2015). Led a systematic review assessing genetic associations with female urinary symptoms and pelvic organ prolapse.[^9]
- **Genome-wide study of female urinary incontinence** (2021). Led a genome-wide association study reporting loci near EDN1 and MARCO associated with urgency and stress incontinence, respectively.[^10]

## Contributions

Cartwright's cited work covers pelvic organ prolapse and reconstructive surgery, including mesh complications, catheter removal after prolapse surgery, prolapse pathophysiology, and long-term sacrohysteropexy outcomes. His cited systematic reviews also examine nocturia incidence and its associations with falls, fractures, and mortality.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
