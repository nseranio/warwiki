## Key Publications

Cartwright's cited work covers pelvic organ prolapse and reconstructive surgery, including mesh complications, catheter removal after prolapse surgery, prolapse pathophysiology, and long-term sacrohysteropexy outcomes. His cited systematic reviews also examine nocturia incidence and its associations with falls, fractures, and mortality.<sup>[P3](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-8)</sup>
