## Biography

Rufus Cartwright is a consultant obstetrician and gynecologist at Chelsea and Westminster Hospital NHS Foundation Trust and a visiting researcher at Imperial College London.[^1] He earned his MBBS at University College London and undertook subspecialty training in urogynecology at Oxford University Hospitals NHS Trust.[^2] Cartwright worked as a research fellow with Linda Cardozo at King's College London and Vik Khullar at Imperial College London.[^3] He serves on the Scientific Committee of the International Urogynecological Association.[^1]

## Contributions

Cartwright's cited work covers pelvic organ prolapse and reconstructive surgery, including mesh complications, catheter removal after prolapse surgery, prolapse pathophysiology, and long-term sacrohysteropexy outcomes. His cited systematic reviews also examine nocturia incidence and its associations with falls, fractures, and mortality.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
