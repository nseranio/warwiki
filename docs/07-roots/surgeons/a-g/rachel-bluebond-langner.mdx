## Biography

Rachel Bluebond-Langner is the Laura and Isaac Perlmutter Professor of Reconstructive Plastic Surgery and a professor of urology at NYU Grossman School of Medicine.[^1] She earned her MD from Johns Hopkins University in 2003 and completed a plastic surgery residency at Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine in 2011.[^1] She completed a craniomaxillofacial fellowship at Hospital General Dr. Manuel Gea Gonzalez in 2012.[^1]

## Contributions

Her cited work addresses gender-affirming vaginoplasty, including vulvar construction, robotic peritoneal flap approaches, urinary function, and surgery for neovaginal stenosis and other complications. Other cited studies address patient-reported outcomes after penile reconstruction and a gracilis flap technique for vaginectomy and urethral lengthening.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
