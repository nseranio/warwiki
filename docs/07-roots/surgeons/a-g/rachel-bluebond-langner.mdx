## Key Publications

Her cited work addresses gender-affirming vaginoplasty, including vulvar construction, robotic peritoneal flap approaches, urinary function, and surgery for neovaginal stenosis and other complications. Other cited studies address patient-reported outcomes after penile reconstruction and a gracilis flap technique for vaginectomy and urethral lengthening.<sup>[P4](#publication-2)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-3)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P8](#publication-6)</sup>
