## Contributions

Her cited work addresses gender-affirming vaginoplasty, including vulvar construction, robotic peritoneal flap approaches, urinary function, and surgery for neovaginal stenosis and other complications. Other cited studies address patient-reported outcomes after penile reconstruction and a gracilis flap technique for vaginectomy and urethral lengthening.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]

- **Robotic peritoneal-flap vaginoplasty** (2019). With Lee Zhao, adapted robot-assisted peritoneal flaps to augment vaginal depth during gender-affirming vaginoplasty.[^7] Cited on WARWIKI: [Peritoneal Flap Vaginoplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery/peritoneal-pull-through-vaginoplasty).
- **Split pedicled gracilis flap in phalloplasty** (2020). Senior-authored a technique using a split gracilis flap for urethral support and pelvic dead-space closure during phalloplasty.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Gender-Affirming Vaginectomy / Colpectomy](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery/vaginectomy).
- **Five-hundred-case robotic peritoneal-flap vaginoplasty series** (2026). Senior-authored a 500-patient series reporting complications, vaginal dimensions, and sexual-function outcomes after robotic peritoneal-flap vaginoplasty.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Peritoneal Flap Vaginoplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery/peritoneal-pull-through-vaginoplasty).
