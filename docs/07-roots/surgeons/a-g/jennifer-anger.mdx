## Biography

Jennifer T. Anger is a tenured professor and chair of the Department of Urology at the University of Arizona College of Medicine – Phoenix and Banner – University Medical Group.[^1] She earned her medical degree from the University of Southern California Keck School of Medicine and completed urology and surgery residency at New York-Presbyterian Weill Cornell Medical Center.[^1] She completed a urologic reconstruction fellowship with George Webster at Duke University Medical Center, followed by a urological health services research fellowship under Mark Litwin at UCLA.[^1]

## Contributions

Her cited work covers women's lower urinary tract and pelvic floor care, including recurrent urinary tract infection guidance, pelvic floor symptom measures, and studies of colpocleisis outcomes and racial differences in stress incontinence surgery.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
