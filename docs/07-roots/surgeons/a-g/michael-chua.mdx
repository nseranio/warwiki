## Biography

Michael E. Chua is a staff urologist at the Hospital for Sick Children and an assistant professor of global surgery at the University of Toronto.[^1][^2] Chua earned his medical degree at St. Luke’s College of Medicine and completed fellowship training at Eastern Virginia Medical School.[^2][^1] He directs the pediatric urology fellowship at SickKids.[^3]

## Contributions

Chua's cited papers examine outcomes in adult male sling surgery, oral mucosal graft urethroplasty, and pediatric appendicovesicostomy. Other cited studies address posterior urethral valves and transitional care for adolescents with congenital urogenital conditions.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
