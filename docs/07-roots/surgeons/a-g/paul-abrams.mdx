## Contributions

Abrams's cited work includes International Continence Society terminology for lower urinary tract function and the development or validation of incontinence questionnaires and bladder diaries. Other cited papers examine urodynamic measures of bladder outlet obstruction and detrusor underactivity, and surgery for male stress incontinence after prostate surgery.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]

- **Abrams–Griffiths pressure-flow assessment** (1979). With Derek Griffiths, described pressure-flow assessment of bladder outlet obstruction that became the basis of the Abrams–Griffiths nomogram.[^7]
- **Lower urinary tract symptoms terminology** (1994). Proposed lower urinary tract symptoms as a term avoiding an assumed prostatic cause for urinary symptoms.[^8]
- **Overactive bladder terminology** (1999). With Alan Wein, proposed overactive bladder as a clinical term for a group of urinary symptoms.[^9]
- **ICS lower urinary tract terminology report** (2002). First-authored the International Continence Society report standardizing terms for lower urinary tract symptoms, signs, and urodynamic observations.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Urgency Incontinence & OAB](/docs/clinical-conditions/03a-storage-incontinence/urgency-incontinence-oab).
