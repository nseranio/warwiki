## Key Publications

Abrams's cited work includes International Continence Society terminology for lower urinary tract function and the development or validation of incontinence questionnaires and bladder diaries. Other cited papers examine urodynamic measures of bladder outlet obstruction and detrusor underactivity, and surgery for male stress incontinence after prostate surgery.<sup>[P5](#publication-2)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-3)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P8](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P9](#publication-6)</sup>
