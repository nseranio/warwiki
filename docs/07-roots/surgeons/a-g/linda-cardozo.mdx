## Training and career

<div className="sp-facts">

- **Born and died** 1950–2023[^1][^2]
- **Former position** Professor of urogynecology and consultant gynecologist, King's College Hospital, London[^1][^2]
- **Medical school** Liverpool University Medical School (MBChB, 1974)[^3]
- **Fellowship** Two-year research fellowship, St. George's Hospital, with Stuart Stanton (began 1976)[^2]
- **Leadership** President, International Urogynecological Association (1998–2000)[^1]

## Contributions

Her confirmed cited work on clinical measurement includes validation of the P-QOL and PGI-I instruments for prolapse and an International Continence Society report on lower urinary tract terminology.[^4][^5][^6]

- **HUT Committee review of estrogen for recurrent UTI** (2001). Led a systematic review finding evidence that vaginal estrogen could reduce recurrent urinary tract infections in postmenopausal women.[^7]
