## Biography

Linda Cardozo (1950–2023) was professor of urogynecology and consultant gynecologist at King's College Hospital in London.[^1][^2] She graduated from Liverpool University Medical School with an MBChB in 1974.[^3] She began a two-year research fellowship with Stuart Stanton at St. George's Hospital in 1976.[^2] She was president of the International Urogynecological Association from 1998 to 2000.[^1]

## Contributions

Her confirmed cited work on clinical measurement includes validation of the P-QOL and PGI-I instruments for prolapse and an International Continence Society report on lower urinary tract terminology.[^4][^5][^6]
