## Biography

Kenan Celtik is a reconstructive urologist at the San Francisco location of the Crane Center for Transgender Surgery.[^1] He received his medical degree from Hofstra Northwell School of Medicine and completed a urology residency at Houston Methodist Hospital.[^2][^1] Celtik completed a reconstructive urology, prosthetics, and gender-affirming genital surgery fellowship at Mount Sinai Hospital, with training from Miroslav Djordjevic, Robert Valenzuela, and Rajveer Purohit.[^2]

## Contributions

Celtik's cited work addresses genital reconstruction, including penile prosthesis salvage, complications after prosthesis placement following phalloplasty, and rectal injury during penile inversion vaginoplasty.[^3][^4][^5]
