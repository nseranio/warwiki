## Biography

Kunlin Yang is an associate professor and associate chief physician in urology at Peking University First Hospital.[^1][^2][^3] Yang advises master's students and serves as secretary of the Beijing Medical Association's urology branch working group on urinary tract repair and reconstruction.[^1][^2]

## Contributions

Yang's cited work covers ureteral reconstruction with ileal replacement, a Boari flap and psoas hitch, lingual mucosal onlay grafts, and robot-assisted appendiceal ureteroplasty.[^4][^5][^6][^7]
