## Biography

Soyster graduated from the University of Connecticut School of Medicine and completed a urology residency at Indiana University.[^1] Soyster completed a genitourinary reconstruction fellowship at Cleveland Clinic in June 2025, with Kenneth Angermeier as program director.[^1][^2]
