## Biography

Steve Waxman is a urologist at the Leavenworth VA hospital in Kansas and adjunct faculty at Loyola University Chicago School of Law.[^1] He earned his MD and completed his urology residency at the University of Kansas.[^1] He completed urology fellowships at Duke University, the University of Colorado, and the University of Iowa.[^1]
