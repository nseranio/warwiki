## Biography

Jay Simhan is professor and chair of urology at Temple University Hospital and the Lewis Katz School of Medicine at Temple University.[^1] He earned his medical degree at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill and completed urology residency at Temple University Hospital.[^2] He completed a fellowship in genitourinary trauma, reconstruction, and prosthetics at UT Southwestern Medical Center.[^3] He chairs the Society of Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgeons Fellowship Committee.[^4]

## Contributions

Simhan's cited work includes multi-institutional studies of dorsal onlay buccal mucosal graft urethroplasty for postradiation posterior urethral stenosis and a review of its contemporary management.[^5][^6][^7]
