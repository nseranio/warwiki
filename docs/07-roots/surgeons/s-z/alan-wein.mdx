## Known for

- **Functional classification of neurogenic voiding dysfunction** (1981). Wein proposed classifying voiding dysfunction by storage and emptying failure and by bladder or outlet function.[^9]
- **Campbell-Walsh Urology editorship** (2015). Wein served as editor-in-chief of the eleventh edition of the general urology reference textbook.[^10]

## Contributions

Wein's cited papers cover lower urinary tract pharmacology and physiology, pressure-flow analysis, and surgical approaches to incontinence and urethral disease.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
