## Contributions

His cited work on ileovesicostomy addresses operative technique, outcomes, costs, and complications in adults with neurogenic bladder.[^4][^5][^6]

- **AUA chronic urinary retention consensus definition** (2017). Stoffel chaired the AUA workgroup that defined nonneurogenic chronic urinary retention and proposed a risk-based management algorithm.[^7]
- **Neurogenic Bladder Research Group**. Stoffel helped found the multicenter group that established a spinal cord injury registry to study bladder management and quality of life.[^8]
