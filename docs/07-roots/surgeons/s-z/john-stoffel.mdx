## Biography

John Thomas Stoffel is a clinical professor of urology and chief of neurourology and pelvic reconstruction at the University of Michigan.[^1] He earned his MD from Washington University School of Medicine and trained at Massachusetts General Hospital and the Lahey Clinic for residency.[^2][^1] He completed a neurourology and reconstructive urology fellowship at Michigan with Edward McGuire.[^3] He is a founding member of the Neurogenic Bladder Research Group.[^1]

## Contributions

His cited work on ileovesicostomy addresses operative technique, outcomes, costs, and complications in adults with neurogenic bladder.[^4][^5][^6]
