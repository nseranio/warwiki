## Biography

Wesley Verla is listed by AZ Maria Middelares as a urologist with consultations in Ghent and Deinze.[^1] Verla graduated as a medical doctor from Ghent University in 2018.[^2] Verla was accepted into the Ghent University Hospital urology training program in 2017 and later earned a PhD focused on male urethral stricture disease, with Nicolaas Lumen as a mentor.[^2][^3]

## Contributions

Verla's cited work centers on urethral stricture disease, including reviews and clinical series on male and female urethroplasty, perineal urethrostomy, and vessel-sparing anastomosis. Several papers address urethral strictures after genital gender-affirming surgery.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
