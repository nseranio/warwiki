## Key Publications

Verla's cited work centers on urethral stricture disease, including reviews and clinical series on male and female urethroplasty, perineal urethrostomy, and vessel-sparing anastomosis. Several papers address urethral strictures after genital gender-affirming surgery.<sup>[P3](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-8)</sup>
