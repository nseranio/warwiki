## Biography

Mark D. Walters retired from Cleveland Clinic after serving as professor and vice chair of gynecology.[^1] Walters coedited Urogynecology and Reconstructive Pelvic Surgery.[^1]

## Contributions

Walters's cited papers address pelvic organ prolapse surgery, including anterior and posterior vaginal repairs and apical suspension, as well as stress urinary incontinence procedures and pelvic floor questionnaires.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
