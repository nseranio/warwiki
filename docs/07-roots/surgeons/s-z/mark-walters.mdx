## Known for

- **Rectocele repair and graft augmentation trial** (2006). Senior-authored a randomized comparison of three rectocele repairs that found no anatomical benefit from porcine graft augmentation.[^7] Cited on WARWIKI: [Posterior Colporrhaphy](/docs/surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/posterior-colporrhaphy).

Walters coedited Urogynecology and Reconstructive Pelvic Surgery.[^1]

## Contributions

Walters's cited papers address pelvic organ prolapse surgery, including anterior and posterior vaginal repairs and apical suspension, as well as stress urinary incontinence procedures and pelvic floor questionnaires.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
