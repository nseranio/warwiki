## Biography

Alexander J. Skokan is an associate professor of urology at the University of Washington and directs its Urology Residency Innovation and Simulation-Based Skills Training Initiative.[^1] He attended the University of Pennsylvania School of Medicine and completed urology residency at the University of Pennsylvania.[^1][^2] He completed a reconstructive urology fellowship at the University of Washington in 2020, in the program directed by Hunter Wessells.[^1][^3]
