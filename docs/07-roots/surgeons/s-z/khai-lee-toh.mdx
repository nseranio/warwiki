## Biography

Khai Lee Toh practices urology at Toh Khai Lee Urology Clinic in Mount Elizabeth Medical Centre, Singapore.[^1] He earned his medical degree at the National University of Singapore and received urological training in Singapore.[^1] He completed fellowships at Duke University in North Carolina and William Beaumont Hospital in Michigan; Duke lists Toh as a 2000–2001 reconstructive urology fellow.[^2][^3] He was president of the Singapore Urological Association from 2012 to 2014.[^2]

## Contributions

His cited work examines artificial urinary sphincter revision, including transcorporal cuff placement after erosion or urethral atrophy and outcomes after revision or secondary implantation.[^4][^5]
