## Key Publications

His cited work examines treatments for Peyronie's disease, including intralesional injections, traction therapy, tunical plication, grafting, and curvature correction during prosthesis implantation. He has also published on penile prosthesis techniques, multimodal analgesia, salvage of infected prostheses, and the Mini-Jupette graft for post-prostatectomy climacturia and mild urinary incontinence.<sup>[P3](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-8)</sup>
