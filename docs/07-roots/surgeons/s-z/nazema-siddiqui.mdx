## Biography

Nazema Y. Siddiqui is an associate professor of obstetrics and gynecology and director of research in urogynecology and reconstructive pelvic surgery at Duke University School of Medicine.[^1][^2] She earned her medical degree at the University of Michigan Medical School and completed an obstetrics and gynecology residency at Metro Health Medical Center and Cleveland Clinic in Ohio.[^3] She completed a female pelvic medicine and reconstructive surgery fellowship at Duke University Medical Center from 2007 to 2010.[^3]

## Contributions

Her cited work addresses asymptomatic bacteriuria and recurrent urinary tract infections in women, as well as perioperative and technical questions in pelvic organ prolapse surgery.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
