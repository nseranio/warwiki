## Known for

- **Sacrocolpopexy versus vaginal repair evidence synthesis** (2015). First-authored a systematic review comparing anatomical outcomes and adverse events after mesh sacrocolpopexy and native-tissue vaginal prolapse repair.[^9]
- **Urinary microbiome analysis in mixed incontinence** (2022). Led a reanalysis examining how sequencing and statistical methods affect urinary microbiome findings in women with mixed urinary incontinence.[^10]

## Contributions

Her cited work addresses asymptomatic bacteriuria and recurrent urinary tract infections in women, as well as perioperative and technical questions in pelvic organ prolapse surgery.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
