## Biography

Uwais B. Zaid, MD, practices urology with Urology of St. Louis, with a clinical focus on reconstructive and general urology.[^1][^2][^3] Zaid earned a medical degree from the David Geffen School of Medicine at UCLA and completed a urologic surgery residency at the University of California, San Francisco.[^3] Zaid completed a genitourinary reconstruction fellowship at Duke University in 2015–2016 and worked with Andrew C. Peterson there.[^3][^1][^4]
