## Contributions

His cited papers address obstetric fistula as a public health problem and approaches to its prevention in low-resource settings. They also examine the history of J. Marion Sims, the Sims speculum and position, and the ethics of nineteenth-century fistula surgery.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
