## Biography

Sandip P. Vasavada is urologic director of Cleveland Clinic's Center for Urogynecology and Reconstructive Pelvic Surgery and professor of surgery at its Lerner College of Medicine.[^1] Vasavada completed medical school at Northeastern Ohio Universities College of Medicine in 1991.[^1] After a urology residency at Cleveland Clinic, Vasavada completed a 1998–1999 UCLA Urology fellowship under Shlomo Raz.[^2] Vasavada is a past president of the Society for Urodynamics, Female Pelvic Medicine, and Urogenital Reconstruction.[^1]

## Contributions

Vasavada's cited work covers female lower urinary tract reconstruction, including urethral diverticula, urethral prolapse, vaginal stenosis, and vesicovaginal fistula repair. He also coauthored the AUA/SUFU update to the surgical guideline for female stress urinary incontinence.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
