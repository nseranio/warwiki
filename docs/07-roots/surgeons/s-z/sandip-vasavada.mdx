## Known for

- **AUA/SUFU overactive bladder guideline** (2012). Served on the panel that authored the AUA/SUFU guideline for diagnosis and treatment of nonneurogenic overactive bladder.[^8]
- **AUA/SUFU female stress urinary incontinence surgery guideline** (2017). Served on the panel that authored surgical treatment guidance for female stress urinary incontinence.[^9]

## Contributions

His confirmed cited work covers female stress urinary incontinence guidance and reconstructive procedures for urethral diverticula, urethral prolapse, vaginal stenosis, and vesicovaginal fistula.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
