## Biography

Sandip P. Vasavada is urologic director of Cleveland Clinic's Center for Urogynecology and Reconstructive Pelvic Surgery and professor of surgery at Cleveland Clinic Lerner College of Medicine.[^1] He earned his medical degree at Northeastern Ohio Universities College of Medicine and completed his urology residency at Cleveland Clinic.[^1][^2] He trained in female urology, neuro-urology, and reconstruction at UCLA under Shlomo Raz.[^1][^2] He is a past president of the Society for Urodynamics, Female Pelvic Medicine, and Urogenital Reconstruction.[^1]

## Contributions

His confirmed cited work covers female stress urinary incontinence guidance and reconstructive procedures for urethral diverticula, urethral prolapse, vaginal stenosis, and vesicovaginal fistula.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
