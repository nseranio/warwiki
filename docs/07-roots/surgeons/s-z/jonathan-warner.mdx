## Key Publications

His cited papers address urethral reconstruction, including long-segment strictures, penile inversion, and endoscopic or transurethral buccal graft techniques. Other papers describe endoscopic treatment of bladder neck contracture and vesicourethral anastomotic stenosis, including transverse mucosal realignment.<sup>[P4](#publication-3)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P3](#publication-10)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-7)</sup>
