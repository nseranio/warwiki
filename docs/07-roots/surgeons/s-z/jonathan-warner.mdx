## Contributions

His cited papers address urethral reconstruction, including long-segment strictures, penile inversion, and endoscopic or transurethral buccal graft techniques. Other papers describe endoscopic treatment of bladder neck contracture and vesicourethral anastomotic stenosis, including transverse mucosal realignment.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Penoscrotal penile inversion urethroplasty** (2016). Warner described penile inversion through a penoscrotal incision to perform oral mucosal graft urethroplasty without a penile skin incision.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Kulkarni One-Stage Panurethral Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/kulkarni-one-stage).
- **Transurethral incision with transverse mucosal realignment** (2020). Warner described endoscopic incision and mucosal realignment for bladder neck contracture and vesicourethral anastomotic stenosis.[^9]
- **Endoscopic buccal graft urethroplasty** (2023). Warner's team reported transurethral placement of a buccal graft for membranous urethral stricture.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Liquid / Minced Buccal Mucosal Graft](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/liquid-minced-buccal).
