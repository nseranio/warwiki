## Biography

Jonathan Nicholas Warner is a reconstructive urologist at Mayo Clinic in Rochester, Minnesota.[^1] He earned his medical degree at the University of Utah School of Medicine and completed his urology residency through Mayo Clinic in Arizona.[^1] Warner completed a fellowship in trauma and male reconstructive urology at Detroit Medical Center.[^2]

## Contributions

His cited papers address urethral reconstruction, including long-segment strictures, penile inversion, and endoscopic or transurethral buccal graft techniques. Other papers describe endoscopic treatment of bladder neck contracture and vesicourethral anastomotic stenosis, including transverse mucosal realignment.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
