## Contributions

- **Placebo-controlled pelvic floor electrical stimulation trial** (1995). Led a randomized, sham-controlled trial of transvaginal electrical stimulation for women with stress urinary incontinence.[^4]
- **Urodynamics and the Evaluation of Female Incontinence** (1995). Coauthored a practical guide to interpreting urodynamic studies in women with urinary incontinence.[^5]
