## Contributions

Vanni's cited work covers urethral reconstruction, including buccal mucosa graft and gracilis flap techniques, and includes the 2023 AUA urethral stricture guideline amendment. Other cited papers address rectourethral and radiation-associated urinary fistula repair, including muscle interposition.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]

- **Buccal graft with gracilis muscle-flap urethroplasty** (2015). Vanni and colleagues reported using a gracilis muscle flap as the vascular bed for buccal-graft repair of difficult long urethral strictures.[^7] Cited on WARWIKI: [Laminated Gracilis Flap Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/laminated-gracilis-flap).
- **Transanal rectal-mucosa graft harvest for urethroplasty** (2016). Vanni and colleagues reported transanal endoscopic harvest of rectal mucosa for onlay repair of long urethral strictures.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Enterourethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/enterourethroplasty).
- **Organ-sparing prostatosymphyseal fistula repair** (2016). Vanni's group described repairing radiation-associated anterior prostatosymphyseal fistulas with an interposed rectus abdominis muscle flap.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Organ-Sparing USF Repair with Interposition Flap](/docs/surgical-techniques/04h-fistula-repair/male/usf-organ-sparing-interposition).
