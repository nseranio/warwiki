## Key Publications

Vanni's cited work covers urethral reconstruction, including buccal mucosa graft and gracilis flap techniques, and includes the 2023 AUA urethral stricture guideline amendment. Other cited papers address rectourethral and radiation-associated urinary fistula repair, including muscle interposition.<sup>[P4](#publication-2)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-3)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P8](#publication-6)</sup>

**Buccal graft with gracilis muscle-flap urethroplasty** (2015). Vanni and colleagues reported using a gracilis muscle flap as the vascular bed for buccal-graft repair of difficult long urethral strictures.
