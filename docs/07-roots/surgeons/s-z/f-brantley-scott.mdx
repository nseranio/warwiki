## Biography

F. Brantley Scott was a professor at Baylor College of Medicine at the time of his death in 1991.[^1] He received his medical degree from Yale University and completed urology residency training at Baylor College of Medicine.[^1] He served as chief of the VA hospital's urology service and directed the Urodynamics Laboratory at St. Luke's Episcopal Hospital.[^1]

## Contributions

His cited papers cover artificial urinary sphincter implantation for urinary incontinence, including epispadias-exstrophy and pregnancy, and an implantable inflatable penile prosthesis for erectile dysfunction.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
