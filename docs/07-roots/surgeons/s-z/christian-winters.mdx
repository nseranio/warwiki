## Contributions

- **Human pubovaginal-sling graft histology** (2008). Winters' group compared tissue responses to synthetic, autologous and biologic sling grafts removed during revision surgery.[^2]
- **AUA/SUFU adult urodynamics guideline** (2012). Winters chaired the AUA urodynamics guideline panel and led recommendations on adult urodynamic testing.[^3]
