## Biography

Ryan P. Terlecki is professor and vice chair of urology at Wake Forest University School of Medicine.[^1] He earned his MD at Wayne State University School of Medicine and completed his urology residency at Detroit Medical Center.[^1] He completed reconstructive urology fellowships at the University of Colorado/Denver School of Medicine in Brian Flynn's program and at UT Southwestern.[^1][^2] He directs Wake Forest's GURS fellowship and served as president of the North Carolina Urological Association.[^1]

## Contributions

His cited work addresses urethral stricture management, including preoperative urethral rest, proximal bulbar reconstruction, and quality of life during intermittent self-dilation. Other cited work covers male stress incontinence surgery, including male urethral slings and artificial urinary sphincter outcomes and revision.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
