## Known for

- **UroEDIC erectile function analysis** (2011). Wessells led an analysis of whether intensive glycemic therapy affected later erectile function in men with type 1 diabetes.[^7]
- **AUA urotrauma guideline** (2014). Wessells was an author of the AUA guideline on evaluation and management of genitourinary injuries.[^8]
- **AUA male urethral stricture guideline** (2017). Wessells chaired the AUA panel that produced its male urethral stricture guideline and later chaired the 2023 amendment panel.[^9]

## Contributions

His cited work addresses urethral stricture guidance and reconstruction, including bladder neck and posterior urethral disease. It also covers pelvic fracture urethral injury and other genitourinary trauma.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
