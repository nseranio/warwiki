## Biography

Hunter B. Wessells is a professor of urology at the University of Washington and president of UW Physicians.[^1] He earned his MD at Georgetown University and completed a urology residency at the University of Pennsylvania.[^1] He completed a reconstructive urology fellowship at UCSF.[^1]

## Contributions

His cited work addresses urethral stricture guidance and reconstruction, including bladder neck and posterior urethral disease. It also covers pelvic fracture urethral injury and other genitourinary trauma.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
