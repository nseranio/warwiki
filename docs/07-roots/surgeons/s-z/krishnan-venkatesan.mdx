## Biography

Krishnan Venkatesan is director of urologic reconstruction at MedStar Washington Hospital Center.[^1] He earned his medical degree and completed a urology residency at Wayne State University School of Medicine.[^1] He completed genitourinary reconstruction fellowship training at University College London Hospital and urethral reconstruction fellowship training at the Kulkarni Institute in Pune, India.[^1] He was also a visiting fellow in urologic reconstruction at the Sava Perovic Foundation in Belgrade, Serbia.[^1]

## Contributions

Venkatesan coauthored a cited study of one-stage, one-side dissection dorsal onlay repair with oral mucosa grafts for panurethral strictures.[^2]
