## Biography

Philippe E. Zimmern is a professor of urology at UT Southwestern Medical Center.[^1] He received medical training at Necker-Enfants Malades Hospital in Paris and completed urology residencies in France and at UCLA.[^1] He spent a research fellowship year at UCLA with Shlomo Raz, M.D.[^1] He is a past president of the Society for Urodynamics and Female Urology.[^1]

## Contributions

Zimmern's cited papers address female urethral diverticula and periurethral pathology, Martius labial fat-pad grafts in vaginal reconstruction, and outcomes after vesicovaginal and urethrovaginal fistula repair.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
