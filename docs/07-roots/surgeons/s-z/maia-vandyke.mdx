## Biography

Maia E. VanDyke is an assistant professor of urology at UT Southwestern Medical Center.[^1] She earned her medical degree and completed her urology residency at UT Southwestern.[^1] She completed a genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship at UT Southwestern from 2022 to 2023 under Allen Morey and Steve Hudak.[^1]

## Contributions

Her confirmed papers address urethral reconstruction and treatment, including perineal urethrostomy, extended primary anastomosis, drug-coated balloon therapy, and permanent bulbar urethral ligation. They also examine penile prosthesis placement and revision and penoscrotal decompression for prolonged ischemic priapism.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
