## Biography

Humberto G. Villarreal is listed by City of Hope as a urologic surgeon and associate clinical professor in its Department of Surgery.[^1] He received his medical degree from the University of Texas Health Science Center at Houston.[^1] He completed a urology residency at the University of Texas Health Science Center at San Antonio.[^1] He completed a Society of Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgeons fellowship at the University of Colorado in 2018-2019, in a program directed by Brian J. Flynn.[^1][^2]

[^1]: [Dr. Humberto Villarreal, L.A.-Area Urologic Oncologist](https://www.cancercenter.com/physician-directory/humberto-villarreal). Accessed October 2026.

[^2]: [Fellowship in Functional & Reconstructive Urology](https://medschool.cuanschutz.edu/surgery/divisions-centers-affiliates/urology/education/overview). Accessed October 2026.
