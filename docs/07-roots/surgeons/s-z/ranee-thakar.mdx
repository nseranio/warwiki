## Contributions

Her cited work on obstetric perineal trauma covers isolated rectal buttonhole tears and re-suturing of dehisced perineal wounds.[^2][^3]

- **OASI Care Bundle** (2021). Led implementation of a maternity care bundle intended to reduce obstetric anal sphincter injury; a multicenter evaluation found lower adjusted injury rates.[^4]
