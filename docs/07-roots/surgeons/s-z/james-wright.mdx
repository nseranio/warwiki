## Biography

Edward James Wright is an associate professor of urology at the Johns Hopkins School of Medicine and director of urology at Johns Hopkins Bayview Medical Center.[^1][^2] He earned his MD from the Geisel School of Medicine at Dartmouth and completed a urology residency at New York-Presbyterian Weill Cornell Medical Center.[^2] He completed a fellowship in reconstructive and neurourology at Duke University School of Medicine.[^2][^3]
