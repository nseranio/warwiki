## Biography

Boyd Viers is a professor of urology at Mayo Clinic in Rochester, Minnesota.[^1][^2] Viers received his MD from the University of Nebraska Medical Center and completed urology residency at Mayo Clinic in Rochester.[^1] Viers completed a genitourinary trauma and reconstruction fellowship at UT Southwestern in Dallas with Allen Morey.[^3] Viers is Mayo's vice chair of education and directs its urology residency and trauma and genitourinary reconstruction fellowship.[^1]

## Contributions

Viers's cited papers examine artificial urinary sphincter outcomes and revision strategies. They also examine penile prosthesis pump problems and high submuscular placement of prosthetic reservoirs and balloons.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
