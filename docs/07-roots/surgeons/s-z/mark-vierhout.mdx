His professorship focused on pelvic floor dysfunction.[^1]

## Contributions

- **Recurrent prolapse repair randomized trial** (2011). Senior-authored a trial comparing trocar-guided vaginal mesh with conventional repair for recurrent prolapse; it reported anatomical outcomes and mesh exposure.[^2]
- **Dutch urogynecology working group and curriculum**. Helped establish the Netherlands Pelvic Floor Working Group and initiated a national urogynecology curriculum.[^3]
