## Biography

Steven K. Wilson is a urologist at the Institute for Urologic Excellence in La Quinta, California.[^1][^2] Wilson received his medical degree from the University of Virginia.[^1] Wilson completed a urology residency at Vanderbilt University.[^2] Wilson serves as editor in chief of the Video Journal of Prosthetic Urology.[^3]

## Contributions

Wilson's cited work covers penile prosthesis implantation approaches, device survival, and the management of infection and revision. His papers also address penile modeling over an inflatable prosthesis for Peyronie's disease.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
