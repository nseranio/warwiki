## Key Publications

Wilson's cited work covers penile prosthesis implantation approaches, device survival, and the management of infection and revision. His papers also address penile modeling over an inflatable prosthesis for Peyronie's disease.<sup>[P4](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P8](#publication-8)</sup>
