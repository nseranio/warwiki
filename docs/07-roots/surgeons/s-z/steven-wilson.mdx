## Contributions

Wilson's cited work covers penile prosthesis implantation approaches, device survival, and the management of infection and revision. His papers also address penile modeling over an inflatable prosthesis for Peyronie's disease.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

- **Penile modeling over an inflatable prosthesis** (1994). With John Delk, described manual modeling over an inflated penile prosthesis to address curvature from Peyronie’s disease.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Manual Modeling — the Wilson Technique](/docs/surgical-techniques/04j-sexual-dysfunction/peyronies-disease/manual-modeling).
- **Ectopic penile prosthesis reservoir placement** (2002). Reported ectopic reservoir placement with a lock-out valve reservoir for patients with a scarred retropubic space.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Reservoir Placement for Three-Piece Inflatable Penile Prostheses](/docs/surgical-techniques/04j-sexual-dysfunction/penile-implants/reservoir-placement).
- **Single-incision transscrotal artificial urinary sphincter placement** (2003). Described placing all artificial urinary sphincter components through one upper transverse scrotal incision.[^11]
