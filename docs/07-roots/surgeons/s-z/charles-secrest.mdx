## Biography

Charles Secrest was program director of the 2024–25 GURS fellowship at Baylor Scott and White All Saints in Fort Worth.[^1] Secrest completed a urology residency at the University of Mississippi Medical Center.[^2]

## Contributions

Secrest's cited papers on Durasphere describe a modified injection technique for stress urinary incontinence and periurethral mass formation after bulking-agent injection.[^3][^4]
