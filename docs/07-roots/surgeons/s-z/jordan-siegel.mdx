## Biography

Jordan Siegel is a urologist with Scripps Clinic Medical Group in La Jolla, California.[^1] He earned his medical degree at Tufts University School of Medicine and completed his urology residency at the University of California, Irvine.[^2] He completed a fellowship in urologic trauma, reconstruction, and prosthetics at UT Southwestern in 2015, during Allen F. Morey's tenure as the program director.[^2][^3]

## Contributions

His cited work covers reconstructive approaches to urethral stricture and adult buried penis, including combined dorsal and ventral buccal grafting, repeat excision and primary anastomosis, and an anatomical classification for buried penis reconstruction.[^4][^5][^6]
