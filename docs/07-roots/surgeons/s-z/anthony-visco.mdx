## Key Publications

Visco's cited work covers pelvic organ prolapse repair, including sacrospinous hysteropexy trials, mesh weight and discharge after sacrocolpopexy, and genital hiatus size after vaginal vault suspension.<sup>[P4](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P8](#publication-8)</sup>
