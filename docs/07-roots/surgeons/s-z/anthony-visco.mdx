## Contributions

Visco's cited work covers pelvic organ prolapse repair, including sacrospinous hysteropexy trials, mesh weight and discharge after sacrocolpopexy, and genital hiatus size after vaginal vault suspension.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

- **CARE trial reduction-urodynamics analysis** (2008). Visco led an analysis of prolapse-reduction urodynamics in stress-continent women undergoing sacrocolpopexy in the CARE trial.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [POP Overview](/docs/clinical-conditions/03c-pelvic-support/pelvic-organ-prolapse).
- **Robotic versus abdominal sacrocolpopexy outcomes** (2008). Visco coauthored an early cohort comparison of short-term anatomic outcomes after robotic and open abdominal sacrocolpopexy.[^10]
- **ABC urgency-incontinence trial** (2012). Visco led a randomized comparison of oral anticholinergic therapy and 100-unit onabotulinumtoxinA injection for urgency urinary incontinence.[^11] Cited on WARWIKI: [Intradetrusor OnabotulinumtoxinA](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/intradetrusor-botox).
