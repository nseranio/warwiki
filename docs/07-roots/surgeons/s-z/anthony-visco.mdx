## Contributions

Visco's cited work covers pelvic organ prolapse repair, including sacrospinous hysteropexy trials, mesh weight and discharge after sacrocolpopexy, and genital hiatus size after vaginal vault suspension.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
