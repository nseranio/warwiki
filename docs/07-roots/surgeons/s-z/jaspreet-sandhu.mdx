## Biography

Jaspreet S. Sandhu is an attending urologic surgeon at Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center and director of its Functional and Reconstructive Urology Fellowship.[^1][^2] Sandhu earned a medical degree from Wake Forest University.[^1] Sandhu completed a urology residency at New York Hospital/Cornell University Medical School.[^1] Sandhu then completed a fellowship in voiding dysfunction at NewYork-Presbyterian/Weill Cornell Medical Center.[^1]

## Contributions

Sandhu's cited work addresses urinary incontinence after prostate treatment, including treatment options, artificial urinary sphincter use, and AUA guidance. The confirmed citations also cover lower urinary tract symptoms attributed to benign prostatic hyperplasia, chronic pelvic pain, sacral neuromodulation use, and female urethrectomy.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
