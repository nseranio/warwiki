## Biography

Matthew J. Ziegelmann is a professor of urology and a urologist at Mayo Clinic in Rochester, Minnesota.[^1][^2] He earned his MD at the University of Minnesota Medical School.[^1] He completed a urology residency at Mayo Clinic and an andrology fellowship with Laurence A. Levine at Rush University.[^2][^1][^3] He serves on the board of directors of the Sexual Medicine Society of North America.[^2]

## Contributions

His cited work covers Peyronie's disease treatment with penile traction, collagenase injections, penile prostheses, and plaque incision with grafting.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
