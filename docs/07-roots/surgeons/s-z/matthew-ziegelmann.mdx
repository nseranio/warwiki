## Contributions

His cited work covers Peyronie's disease treatment with penile traction, collagenase injections, penile prostheses, and plaque incision with grafting.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

- **Modified glanulopexy for penile prosthesis deformity** (2018). Described a glanulopexy modification for glanular hypermobility or supersonic transporter deformity associated with penile prostheses.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Revision Scenarios in Penile Prosthesis Surgery](/docs/surgical-techniques/04j-sexual-dysfunction/penile-implants/revision-scenarios).
- **RestoreX penile traction randomized trial** (2019). First-authored a randomized trial of short-duration RestoreX traction therapy for penile curvature and length in Peyronie’s disease.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Penile Traction Therapy (PTT)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04j-sexual-dysfunction/penile-traction-therapy).
