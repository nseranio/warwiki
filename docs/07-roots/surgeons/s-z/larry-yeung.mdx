## Biography

Lawrence L. Yeung is a clinical professor of urology at the University of Florida and medical director of its Department of Urology.[^1][^2] He earned his medical degree and completed urology residency at the University of Florida.[^3] He completed a fellowship in urologic reconstruction, trauma, and prosthetic urology at Washington University School of Medicine in St. Louis.[^2]

## Contributions

His confirmed cited work includes a practice management guideline on the diagnostic evaluation and management of blunt bladder injuries.[^4]
