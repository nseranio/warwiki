## Biography

Joachim W. Thüroff lists a private urology practice in Mainz and a consultant professorship at the university urology clinic in Mannheim.[^1] He studied medicine at Philipps University of Marburg.[^1] He received urological training at Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz under Rudolf Hohenfellner.[^2] He served as president of the Société Internationale d'Urologie from 2009 to 2011.[^3]

## Contributions

Thüroff's confirmed papers cover Mainz pouch I and II for bladder augmentation and continent urinary diversion, including metabolic and bone mineral density follow-up after diversion.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
