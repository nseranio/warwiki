## Contributions

Thüroff's confirmed papers cover Mainz pouch I and II for bladder augmentation and continent urinary diversion, including metabolic and bone mineral density follow-up after diversion.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

- **Mainz pouch ileocecal reservoir** (1985). Thüroff and colleagues described an ileocecal reservoir for bladder augmentation and continent urinary diversion.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Mainz Pouch I](/docs/surgical-techniques/04c-urinary-diversion/mainz-pouch-i).
- **Mainz pouch expansion to orthotopic substitution** (1988). His 100-patient series reported Mainz pouch use for augmentation, continent diversion, and orthotopic bladder substitution.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Ileocecal Cystoplasty (Ileocecocystoplasty)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04b-bladder-reconstruction/ileocecal-cystoplasty).
