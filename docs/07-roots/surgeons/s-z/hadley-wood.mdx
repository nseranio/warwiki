## Known for

- **Spina bifida men's health guideline** (2025). Chaired a Spina Bifida Association workgroup addressing sexual health and other adult men's health issues.[^7]

## Contributions

Wood's confirmed papers address urethral reconstruction and rectourethral fistula repair, including concurrent posterior urethroplasty and gracilis interposition. Wood also coauthored the prospective Artificial Urinary Sphincter Clinical Outcomes Trial primary results.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
