## Biography

Hadley M. Wood is vice chair of research for Cleveland Clinic’s Integrated Surgery Institute, a staff member of its Center for Genitourinary Reconstruction, and professor of urology at Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine.[^1][^2] She earned her medical degree from Johns Hopkins University School of Medicine and completed a urology residency at Cleveland Clinic in 2009.[^1] She serves as editor in chief of Urology.[^1]

## Contributions

Her cited work covers urethral reconstruction, including rectal mucosa graft harvest, adult hypospadias repair, and urethroplasty during rectourethral fistula repair. Other cited papers address complex rectourethral fistula repair, male urinary incontinence procedures and outcomes, and care for adults with congenital genitourinary conditions.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
