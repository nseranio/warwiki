## Biography

Neil D. Sherman, MD, serves as chief of the Division of Urology at JFK University Medical Center in Edison, New Jersey.[^1] He received his medical degree from New York Medical College and completed a urology residency at the University of Medicine and Dentistry of New Jersey.[^2][^1] He completed a fellowship in reconstructive urology, female urology and urodynamics at Duke University Medical Center.[^1][^3]
