## Biography

Shinchi graduated from the National Defense Medical College School of Medicine in 2007 and completed graduate study there in 2018.[^1]

## Contributions

Shinchi's cited work examines reconstruction for urethral strictures and urinary outlet stenoses, including dorsal onlay, staged, and anastomotic urethroplasty, perineal urethrostomy, Y-V plasty, and deep lateral incision. The papers also assess patient-reported outcomes and MRI in pelvic fracture urethral injury.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
