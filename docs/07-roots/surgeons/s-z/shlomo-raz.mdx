## Contributions

Raz's cited work addresses transvaginal vesicovaginal fistula repair, including peritoneal and other tissue interposition flaps. It also covers pelvic organ prolapse repair and bladder neck suspension or closure.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

- **Vaginal wall sling** (1989). Described a sling made from vaginal wall tissue to support the urethra in women with intrinsic sphincter dysfunction.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Needle Suspensions and In Situ Slings (Historical)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/historical-bladder-neck-suspensions).
- **Four-corner bladder and urethral suspension** (1989). Described a transvaginal suspension intended to support both the bladder base and bladder neck in women with cystocele.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Kelly Plication](/docs/surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/kelly-plication).
