## Biography

Shlomo Raz is a distinguished professor emeritus of urology at UCLA.[^1] Raz completed a fellowship at the UCLA School of Medicine.[^2] Raz served as chief of UCLA Urology's Division of Pelvic Medicine and Reconstructive Surgery.[^3]

## Contributions

Raz's cited work addresses transvaginal vesicovaginal fistula repair, including peritoneal and other tissue interposition flaps. It also covers pelvic organ prolapse repair and bladder neck suspension or closure.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
