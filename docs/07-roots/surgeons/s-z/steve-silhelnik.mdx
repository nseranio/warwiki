## Biography

Stephen A. Sihelnik practiced at the Center for Urologic Care of Berks County, which names him among the physicians involved in its formation in 2001.[^1][^2] Steve Silhelnik appears on Duke University's roster of reconstructive urology fellows for 1983–1984.[^3]
