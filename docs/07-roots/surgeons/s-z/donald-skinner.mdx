## Biography

Donald G. Skinner was a professor and former chair of urology at the Keck School of Medicine of USC.[^1][^2] He graduated from Yale Medical School and completed general surgery training at Massachusetts General Hospital.[^3] After serving as an Air Force general surgeon, he returned to Massachusetts General Hospital for his urology residency.[^3]

## Contributions

Skinner's cited work covers continent ileal urinary diversion and neobladder reconstruction, including Kock reservoirs, T-pouch neobladders, and hemi-Kock augmentation ileocystoplasty.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
