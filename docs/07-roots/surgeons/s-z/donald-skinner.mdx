## Key Publications

Skinner's cited work covers continent ileal urinary diversion and neobladder reconstruction, including Kock reservoirs, T-pouch neobladders, and hemi-Kock augmentation ileocystoplasty.<sup>[P3](#publication-12)</sup><sup>[P1](#publication-10)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-8)</sup>
