## Contributions

Skinner's cited work covers continent ileal urinary diversion and neobladder reconstruction, including Kock reservoirs, T-pouch neobladders, and hemi-Kock augmentation ileocystoplasty.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

- **Kock pouch continent urinary diversion in the United States** (1984). Reported his team's early U.S. experience constructing continent ileal urinary reservoirs using Kock's method.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Hemi-Kock Continent Stoma](/docs/surgical-techniques/04b-bladder-reconstruction/hemi-kock).
- **Orthotopic adaptation of the Kock reservoir** (1989). Adapted Kock reservoir construction to urethral anastomosis for selected patients undergoing cystectomy.[^11] Cited on WARWIKI: [Cutaneous Vesicostomy](/docs/surgical-techniques/04b-bladder-reconstruction/cutaneous-vesicostomy).
- **T-pouch ileal neobladder** (1998). Co-reported an orthotopic ileal neobladder using a serosa-lined antireflux valve.[^12] Cited on WARWIKI: [Double T-Pouch](/docs/surgical-techniques/04c-urinary-diversion/double-t-pouch).
