## Principal Contributions

A 2020 conference abstract credited his group with gracilis muscle interposition for rectourethral fistula repair and with using gracilis muscle as a vascular bed for oral mucosa urethral grafts.[^3] The same abstract documented work on urethral squamous cell carcinoma, bladder reconstruction, and recalcitrant bladder neck contractures.[^3]

- **Q-flap panurethral reconstruction** (2000). Co-developed the Q-flap, a penile skin flap for single-stage reconstruction of extensive anterior urethral strictures.[^4] Cited on WARWIKI: [Q-Flap (Morey / Tran / Zinman)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/q-flap).
- **Intralesional mitomycin C for recurrent bladder neck contracture** (2011). Co-reported radial urethrotomy with intralesional mitomycin C for recurrent bladder neck contracture.[^5] Cited on WARWIKI: [Transurethral Incision of Bladder Neck Contracture (TUIBNC)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04ab-bladder-neck-reconstruction/transurethral-incision-bnc).
- **Buccal graft urethroplasty with gracilis muscle flap** (2015). Helped describe gracilis muscle support for buccal mucosa graft urethroplasty in high-risk, long-segment strictures.[^6] Cited on WARWIKI: [Laminated Gracilis Flap Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/laminated-gracilis-flap).

## The Lahey Center

Zinman founded Lahey’s Center for Reconstructive Urologic Surgery in 1973 and remained at Lahey for more than 50 years.[^2][^1]

## Recognition

He received the AUA Lifetime Achievement Award in 2015 and the GURS Lifetime Achievement Award in 2019.[^1]
