## Principal Contributions

A 2020 conference abstract credited his group with gracilis muscle interposition for rectourethral fistula repair and with using gracilis muscle as a vascular bed for oral mucosa urethral grafts.[^3] The same abstract documented work on urethral squamous cell carcinoma, bladder reconstruction, and recalcitrant bladder neck contractures.[^3]

## The Lahey Center

Zinman founded Lahey’s Center for Reconstructive Urologic Surgery in 1973 and remained at Lahey for more than 50 years.[^2][^1]

## Recognition

He received the AUA Lifetime Achievement Award in 2015 and the GURS Lifetime Achievement Award in 2019.[^1]
