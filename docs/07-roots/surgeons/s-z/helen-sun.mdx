## Biography

Helen H. Sun is a functional and reconstructive urology fellow at the University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus.[^1] Sun attended Case Western Reserve University for medical school and completed the Case Western Reserve University/University Hospitals Cleveland Medical Center urology residency in 2025.[^2][^3] Sun's fellowship is directed by Brian J. Flynn, and Ty Higuchi is listed on its faculty.[^2]

## Contributions

Sun's cited work covers genital and urinary reconstruction, including penile prosthesis placement after phalloplasty, female urethroplasty, and bladder-neck closure. Other cited studies examine antibiotic prophylaxis for artificial urinary sphincter surgery and delayed closure of large Fournier's gangrene defects.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
