## Biography

Kyle Scarberry is a University Hospitals urologist and assistant professor at Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine.[^1] Scarberry graduated from Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine and completed a urology residency at UH Cleveland Medical Center.[^1] Scarberry completed a reconstructive urology and genitourinary cancer fellowship at Wake Forest University Baptist Medical Center, where Ryan Terlecki directs the reconstructive urology fellowship.[^1][^2] Scarberry directs the Case Western Reserve University/University Hospitals Cleveland Medical Center urology residency program.[^3]

## Contributions

Scarberry's cited papers examine posterior urethroplasty techniques and the timing of reconstruction after pelvic fracture urethral injury.[^4][^5]
