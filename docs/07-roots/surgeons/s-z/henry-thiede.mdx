## Training and career

<div className="sp-facts">

- **Born and died** 1926–2020[^1][^2]
- **Former position** Professor emeritus, University of Rochester[^1][^2]
- **Medical school** University at Buffalo[^1]
- **Residency** Obstetrics and gynecology, Genesee Hospital and Strong Memorial Hospital[^1]
- **Other training** Surgery, Buffalo General Hospital[^1]
- **Leadership** Chair of obstetrics and gynecology, University of Mississippi School of Medicine and later University of Rochester[^1][^2]; president, American Urogynecologic Society (1988–1989)[^3]

</div>

## Known for

- **Urogynecology training objectives** (1981). Co-wrote early Gynecologic Urology Society postgraduate educational objectives submitted for resident training guidance.[^4]
- **Urogynecology fellowship credentialing advocacy** (1989). As AUGS president, asked ABOG to consider fellowship credentialing and later helped lead subspecialty discussions.[^5]
- **Urogynecology service and urodynamics at Rochester**. Established urogynecology as a subspecialty at Rochester and helped create its first urodynamics laboratory.[^6]
