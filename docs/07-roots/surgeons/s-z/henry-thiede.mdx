## Training and career

<div className="sp-facts">

- **Born and died** 1926–2020[^1][^2]
- **Former position** Professor emeritus, University of Rochester[^1][^2]
- **Medical school** University at Buffalo[^1]
- **Residency** Obstetrics and gynecology, Genesee Hospital and Strong Memorial Hospital[^1]
- **Other training** Surgery, Buffalo General Hospital[^1]
- **Leadership** Chair of obstetrics and gynecology, University of Mississippi School of Medicine and later University of Rochester[^1][^2]; president, American Urogynecologic Society (1988–1989)[^3]
