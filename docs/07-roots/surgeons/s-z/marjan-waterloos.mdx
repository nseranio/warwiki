## Known for

- **European female urethroplasty outcomes study** (2025). First-authored a multicenter study comparing reconstructive approaches and stricture-free survival in 165 female urethroplasty patients.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Female Ventral Onlay Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-ventral-onlay-urethroplasty).

## Contributions

Her cited work centers on urethral stricture diagnosis and reconstruction, including female urethroplasty, bulbar and posterior repairs, perineal urethrostomy, and urethral reconstruction in transgender patients. The cited EAU guideline addresses urological trauma.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
