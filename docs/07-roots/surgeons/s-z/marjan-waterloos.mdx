## Biography

Marjan Waterloos is medical leader for reconstructive urology and andrology at AZ Maria Middelares in Ghent, Belgium, and a reconstructive urology consultant at Ghent University Hospital.[^1][^2] She graduated in medicine from Ghent University in 2011 and completed specialist training and a fellowship in 2017.[^1][^2] She chairs the European Association of Urology guidelines panel on urological trauma and serves on its urethral strictures panel.[^1]

## Contributions

Her cited work centers on urethral stricture diagnosis and reconstruction, including female urethroplasty, bulbar and posterior repairs, perineal urethrostomy, and urethral reconstruction in transgender patients. The cited EAU guideline addresses urological trauma.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
