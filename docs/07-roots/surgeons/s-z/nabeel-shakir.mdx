## Biography

Nabeel A. Shakir, MD, is director of genitourinary reconstructive surgery at the Vattikuti Urology Institute at Henry Ford Health.[^1] He earned his medical degree from the University of Texas Southwestern Medical School and completed his urology residency at the University of Texas Southwestern Medical Center.[^1] He completed subspecialty fellowship training in genitourinary reconstructive surgery at NYU Langone Medical Center in 2021; NYU lists Lee C. Zhao as fellowship director.[^1][^2]

## Contributions

His cited work covers robotic reconstruction of posterior urethral and bladder-neck stenosis, including buccal-graft urethroplasty, repair of refractory vesicourethral anastomotic stenosis, and bladder-flap reconstruction. Other cited work addresses gender-affirming genital reconstruction, including revision vaginoplasty, vaginectomy, and repair of post-phalloplasty urethral strictures.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
