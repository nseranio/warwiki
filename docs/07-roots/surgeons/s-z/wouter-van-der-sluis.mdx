## Biography

Wouter B. van der Sluis is a plastic surgeon at Amsterdam UMC and Gender Clinic.[^1] He completed doctoral research on genital surgery for transgender women in 2016.[^1]

## Contributions

Van der Sluis's cited work covers phalloplasty, scrotal reconstruction, and penile implants after phalloplasty in transgender men. It also covers penile inversion, skin graft and intestinal vaginoplasty, vulvoplasty, and postoperative neovaginal complications.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
