## Contributions

Van der Sluis's cited work covers phalloplasty, scrotal reconstruction, and penile implants after phalloplasty in transgender men. It also covers penile inversion, skin graft and intestinal vaginoplasty, vulvoplasty, and postoperative neovaginal complications.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]

- **Revision vaginoplasty comparison** (2016). Led a 53-patient comparison of laparoscopic intestinal and perineal full-thickness skin-graft revision vaginoplasty.[^7] Cited on WARWIKI: [Intestinal Vaginoplasty (Sigmoid / Ileum / Right Colon)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery/intestinal-vaginoplasty).
- **Double-flap phalloplasty with urethral reconstruction** (2017). Reported one-stage phalloplasty combining a pedicled anterolateral-thigh shaft flap with radial-forearm free-flap urethral reconstruction.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Phallic Urethra: Tube-in-Tube and Pars Pendulans Construction](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery/phallic-urethra-tube-in-tube).
- **Multicenter ileal vaginoplasty outcomes** (2018). Led an international multicenter series describing technique and outcomes of ileal vaginoplasty, predominantly for revision reconstruction.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Intestinal Vaginoplasty (Sigmoid / Ileum / Right Colon)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery/intestinal-vaginoplasty).
