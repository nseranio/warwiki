## Contributions

Voelzke's cited work covers pelvic fracture urethral injury, radiation-associated urethral stenosis, rectourethral fistula and renal trauma, including multi-institutional TURNS cohorts.[^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Transperineal rectourethral fistula repair** (2013). Led a review of 23 rectal sphincter-preserving transperineal repairs: all 10 postoperative fistulas closed, against 8 of 13 energy-ablative fistulas, and the authors advised an interposition muscle flap after energy ablation.[^4] Cited on WARWIKI: [Transperineal Approach to RUF](/docs/surgical-techniques/04h-fistula-repair/male/transperineal-ruf-repair), [Rectourethral Fistula](/docs/clinical-conditions/03f-fistulas/in-males/rectourethral).
- **Excision and primary anastomosis for radiation-associated bulbomembranous stenosis** (2021). Led a 10-center TURNS review of 137 men with 86.9% success at a mean 32.3 months; older age and longer stenosis were independently associated with recurrence, and 21.9% later received an artificial urinary sphincter.[^5] Cited on WARWIKI: [Posterior Urethral Stenosis](/docs/clinical-conditions/03b-voiding-outlet/posterior-urethral-stenosis), [Cancer Survivorship](/docs/special-populations/05d-cancer-survivorship).
- **Early endoscopic realignment after pelvic fracture urethral injury** (2012). Senior author of a Level 1 trauma center series in which early realignment failed in 15 of 19 men, at a mean of 79 days after catheter removal.[^6] Cited on WARWIKI: [Primary Endoscopic Realignment (PER)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/primary-endoscopic-realignment).
