## Biography

Bryan Voelzke practices at Spokane Urology and lists male genitourinary reconstruction among his interests.[^1] He earned his medical degree at the University of Texas Health Science Center in San Antonio and completed urology residency at Loyola University Medical Center.[^1] Voelzke completed an advanced clinical urology fellowship in Melbourne, Australia, followed by a genitourinary reconstruction and trauma fellowship at UC San Francisco.[^1] He is a founder of the Trauma and Urologic Reconstructive Network of Surgeons.[^1]

## Contributions

Voelzke's cited work covers urethral trauma and reconstruction, including pelvic fracture urethral injury, radiotherapy-associated stenosis, and rectourethral fistula repair. Other cited studies address renal gunshot wounds, buried penis repair, and urethrocutaneous fistula repair after metoidioplasty.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
