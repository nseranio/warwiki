## Key Publications

Voelzke's cited work covers pelvic fracture urethral injury, radiation-associated urethral stenosis, rectourethral fistula and renal trauma, including multi-institutional TURNS cohorts.<sup>[P1](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P2](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P3](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-7)</sup>

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Transperineal rectourethral fistula repair** (2013). Led a review of 23 rectal sphincter-preserving transperineal repairs: all 10 postoperative fistulas closed, against 8 of 13 energy-ablative fistulas, and the authors advised an interposition muscle flap after energy ablation.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Excision and primary anastomosis for radiation-associated bulbomembranous stenosis** (2021). Led a 10-center TURNS review of 137 men with 86.9% success at a mean 32.3 months; older age and longer stenosis were independently associated with recurrence, and 21.9% later received an artificial urinary sphincter.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[2]} citations={citations}>

**Early endoscopic realignment after pelvic fracture urethral injury** (2012). Senior author of a Level 1 trauma center series in which early realignment failed in 15 of 19 men, at a mean of 79 days after catheter removal.

</KeyPublication>
