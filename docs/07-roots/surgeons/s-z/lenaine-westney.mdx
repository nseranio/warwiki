## Known for

- **Transcorporal artificial urinary sphincter outcomes** (2012). Senior-authored an outcomes series of transcorporal artificial urinary sphincter placement for men with compromised urethras.[^3]
- **Single-incision artificial urinary sphincter placement** (2018). Reported artificial urinary sphincter implantation through one perineal incision, including subdartos pump placement.[^4]
