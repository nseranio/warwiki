## Contributions

Strohbehn's cited studies address the female urethra in stress urinary incontinence: one compares magnetic resonance images with histology, and the other reports an autologous midurethral sling case series.[^3][^4]

- **MRI correlation of levator ani anatomy** (1996). Strohbehn led a cadaver MRI and dissection study defining visible levator ani anatomy for pelvic floor research.[^5]
- **Multicenter evaluation of a collapsible prolapse pessary** (2024). Strohbehn led a seven-center study comparing a collapsible pessary with participants' usual pessaries over three months.[^6] Cited on WARWIKI: [Prolapse Pessaries](/docs/surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/prolapse-pessaries).
