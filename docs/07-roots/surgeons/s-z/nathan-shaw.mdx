## Biography

Nathan Michael Shaw is a MedStar Health urologist in genitourinary reconstructive surgery who sees patients at MedStar Georgetown University Hospital and MedStar Washington Hospital Center.[^1] Shaw earned his medical degree from the University of Virginia School of Medicine and completed a urology residency at MedStar Georgetown University Hospital.[^1] He completed a male genitourinary reconstruction and trauma fellowship at the University of California, San Francisco, under fellowship director Benjamin N. Breyer.[^1][^2]

## Contributions

Shaw's cited work on posterior urethral stenosis includes a comparative review of clinical guidelines and a study of endoscopic treatment for recurrent vesicourethral anastomotic stenosis after prostate cancer treatment.[^3][^4]
