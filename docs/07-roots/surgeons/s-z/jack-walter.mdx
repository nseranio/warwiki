## Biography

Jack Ryan Walter is a urologist practicing with MultiCare in Tacoma, Washington.[^1][^2] Walter attended Loma Linda University School of Medicine and completed a residency at Madigan Army Medical Center.[^3] Walter completed a Duke fellowship in female urology and urogynecology in 2007–2008, with George D. Webster as mentor.[^4][^5]
