## Contributions

- **Introduction of midurethral sling surgery in the Netherlands**. A Dutch pelvic floor society tribute credits him with introducing midurethral sling surgery in the Netherlands.[^2]
- **Introduction of vaginal mesh surgery in the Netherlands**. The same tribute credits him with subsequently introducing vaginal mesh prolapse surgery in the Netherlands.[^3]
