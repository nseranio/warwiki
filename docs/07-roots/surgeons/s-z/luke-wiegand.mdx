## Biography

Lucas R. Wiegand, MD, practices reconstructive urology with Orlando Health Medical Group Urology.[^1] He earned his medical degree and completed his urology residency at the University of South Florida Morsani College of Medicine.[^1] He completed a reconstructive urology fellowship at Washington University in St. Louis.[^1] He is the program director of Orlando Health's urology residency.[^2]

## Contributions

Wiegand's cited ureteral reconstruction work covers replacement and onlay repair with reconfigured intestinal segments and robotic ileal interposition for radiation-induced stricture.[^3][^4]
