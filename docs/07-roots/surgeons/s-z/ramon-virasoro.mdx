## Biography

Ramón Virasoro is a visiting adjoint professor of urology at the University of Colorado Anschutz School of Medicine.[^1] Virasoro earned a medical degree from the University of Buenos Aires and completed general surgery and urology residencies at CEMIC in Buenos Aires.[^2] Virasoro completed an adult and pediatric genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship under Gerald Jordan at Eastern Virginia Medical School in 2007.[^3][^2] Virasoro co-founded a genitourinary reconstructive surgery fellowship in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic.[^2]

## Contributions

Virasoro's cited work addresses urethral stricture disease through vessel-sparing bulbar urethroplasty, fossa navicularis flap reconstruction, and treatment of lichen sclerosus. Several other cited papers report outcomes of drug-coated balloon treatment for recurrent urethral strictures in the ROBUST I and III studies.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
