## Known for

- **Vessel-sparing bulbar urethroplasty outcomes** (2015). Led a multicenter series assessing vessel-sparing excision and primary anastomosis for proximal bulbar urethral strictures.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Jordan Vessel-Sparing Excision and Primary Anastomosis](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/jordan-vessel-sparing-epa).
- **ROBUST I drug-coated balloon study** (2020). First-authored the initial ROBUST I clinical report of paclitaxel-coated balloon dilation for recurrent anterior urethral stricture.[^10]
- **ROBUST III randomized trial** (2022). Served as senior author on a randomized comparison of drug-coated balloon dilation and standard endoscopic treatment for recurrent anterior urethral stricture.[^11] Cited on WARWIKI: [Drug-Coated Balloon Therapy](/docs/surgical-techniques/drug-coated-balloon).

## Contributions

Virasoro's cited work addresses urethral stricture disease through vessel-sparing bulbar urethroplasty, fossa navicularis flap reconstruction, and treatment of lichen sclerosus. Several other cited papers report outcomes of drug-coated balloon treatment for recurrent urethral strictures in the ROBUST I and III studies.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
