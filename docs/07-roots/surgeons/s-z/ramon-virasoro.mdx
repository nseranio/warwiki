## Key Publications

Virasoro's cited work addresses urethral stricture disease through vessel-sparing bulbar urethroplasty, fossa navicularis flap reconstruction, and treatment of lichen sclerosus. Several other cited papers report outcomes of drug-coated balloon treatment for recurrent urethral strictures in the ROBUST I and III studies.<sup>[P4](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P8](#publication-8)</sup>
