## Biography

Richard A. Santucci is a senior surgeon at the Crane Center for Transgender Surgery.[^1] Santucci earned his medical degree at Baylor College of Medicine and completed urology residency at the University of Washington Medical Center.[^2] Santucci completed a fellowship in urologic trauma and reconstruction at the University of California, San Francisco, from 1999 to 2000 under Jack McAninch.[^1][^2] Before joining the Crane Center, Santucci directed the Center for Urologic Reconstruction and served as a clinical professor at Michigan State College of Osteopathic Medicine.[^3]

## Contributions

Santucci's cited work includes operative management and classification of adult-acquired buried penis, including escutcheonectomy and skin-graft repair. Other cited papers address anterior urethral stricture management and graft urethroplasty.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]
