## Contributions

Santucci's cited work includes operative management and classification of adult-acquired buried penis, including escutcheonectomy and skin-graft repair. Other cited papers address anterior urethral stricture management and graft urethroplasty.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

- **Burden of male urethral stricture disease** (2007). Led an analysis of US datasets estimating the prevalence, costs, and clinical burden of male urethral stricture disease.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Drug-Coated Balloon Therapy](/docs/surgical-techniques/drug-coated-balloon).
- **Internal urethrotomy durability study** (2010). Reported low stricture-free rates after initial and repeated direct-vision internal urethrotomy in a retrospective series.[^10]
- **AUA male urethral stricture guideline** (2017). Cochaired the AUA panel that produced recommendations for evaluating and treating male urethral stricture.[^11]
- **Pariser–Santucci buried penis repair classification** (2018). Senior-authored a five-category classification of adult acquired buried penis repairs based on surgical complexity.[^12] Cited on WARWIKI: [Ventral Slit Scrotal Flap (VSSF — Westerman / Tausch)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04e-genital-reconstruction/ventral-slit-scrotal-flap).
