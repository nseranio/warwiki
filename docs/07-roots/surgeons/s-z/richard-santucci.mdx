## Key Publications

Santucci's cited work includes operative management and classification of adult-acquired buried penis, including escutcheonectomy and skin-graft repair. Other cited papers address anterior urethral stricture management and graft urethroplasty.<sup>[P5](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-5)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-12)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P8](#publication-8)</sup>
