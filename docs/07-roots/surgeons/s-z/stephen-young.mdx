## Biography

Stephen B. Young founded the University of Massachusetts Division of Urogynecology in 1990 and directed it until 2010.[^1] Young served as president of the American Urogynecologic Society from 2004 to 2005.[^2]

## Contributions

- **Mersilene mesh suburethral sling outcomes** (2001). Led a 200-patient series reporting clinical and urodynamic outcomes after Mersilene mesh sling surgery for complicated stress incontinence.[^3]
- **Vaginal paravaginal repair series** (2001). Led a 100-patient series describing outcomes and complications of vaginal paravaginal repair for symptomatic cystocele.[^4] Cited on WARWIKI: [Paravaginal Repair](/docs/surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/paravaginal-repair).
