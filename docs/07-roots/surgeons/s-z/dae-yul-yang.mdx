## Biography

Dae Yul Yang is a professor of urology at Kangdong Sacred Heart Hospital and Hallym University.[^1] Yang graduated from Chonnam University Medical School and completed a urology residency at Hallym University's Kangnam Sacred Heart Hospital.[^1] Yang completed a urology fellowship at Kangdong Sacred Heart Hospital and an andrology research fellowship at Tulane University under Suresh C. Sikka.[^1][^2] Yang served as president of the Korean Society for Sexual Medicine and Andrology from 2016 to 2018.[^1]

## Contributions

Yang's cited studies compare hyaluronic acid and polylactic acid injections for penile augmentation and examine polylactic acid and cross-linked dextran/polymethylmethacrylate fillers.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
