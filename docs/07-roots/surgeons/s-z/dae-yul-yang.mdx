## Contributions

Yang's cited studies compare hyaluronic acid and polylactic acid injections for penile augmentation and examine polylactic acid and cross-linked dextran/polymethylmethacrylate fillers.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Glans augmentation injection technique** (2018). Reported a multicenter clinical series of cross-linked dextran gel injection for glans augmentation.[^8]
- **Randomized comparison of penile fillers** (2019). Led a multicenter randomized comparison of hyaluronic acid and polylactic acid fillers for penile girth augmentation.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Hyaluronic Acid Filler — Penile Girth Augmentation](/docs/surgical-techniques/04l-cosmetic-genital-surgery/hyaluronic-acid-filler).
