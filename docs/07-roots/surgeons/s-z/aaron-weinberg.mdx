## Biography

Aaron C. Weinberg, MD, is a urologist at Chesapeake Urology in Maryland.[^1][^2] He earned his medical degree at Boston University School of Medicine and completed a urology residency at Columbia University.[^1] He completed a fellowship in urologic trauma, reconstructive surgery, and male sexual health at NYU Langone Medical Center.[^1]

## Contributions

Weinberg's cited work includes robotic Y-V plasty for bladder neck contracture, buccal mucosa graft ureteroplasty, and robotic rectal mucosa harvest for urethral reconstruction.[^3][^4][^5]
