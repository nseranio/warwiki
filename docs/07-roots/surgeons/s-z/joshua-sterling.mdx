## Biography

Joshua Sterling, MD, MSc, is an assistant professor of urology at Yale School of Medicine specializing in trauma and genitourinary reconstruction.[^1] He earned his medical degree from New York Medical College and completed urology residency at Robert Wood Johnson University Hospital.[^1] He completed a GURS fellowship in reconstructive urology and transgender care at SUNY Upstate Medical University in 2022.[^1]

## Contributions

His cited work examines buccal mucosal grafts in urethral reconstruction, including dorsal onlay repair of postprostatectomy, postradiation anastomotic stenosis, transurethral ventral inlay repair, and prefabricated gracilis-buccal flap reconstruction of devastated urethras. His reviews cover broader urologic applications of buccal mucosa and the pathophysiology and repair of radiation-induced urethral stricture.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
