## Biography

Shyam S. Sukumar is an assistant professor of urology at Baylor College of Medicine.[^1] Sukumar graduated from Kilpauk Medical College in Chennai, India, and completed urology residency at the University of Minnesota.[^2][^3] Sukumar completed a genitourinary reconstruction fellowship at Columbia University under Steven Brandes.[^2] Sukumar directs Baylor's urology selective for medical students.[^4]
