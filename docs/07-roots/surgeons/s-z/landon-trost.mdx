## Known for

- **PTNM penile curvature classification proposal** (2024). Trost and colleagues proposed PTNM categories for Peyronie's subtypes, trauma history, non-Peyronie's curvature, and disease activity.[^10]
- **RestoreX penile traction device**. Trost invented RestoreX, a penile traction device developed with Mayo Clinic for men with Peyronie's disease.[^11] Cited on WARWIKI: [Penile Traction Therapy (PTT)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04j-sexual-dysfunction/penile-traction-therapy).

## Contributions

His cited work covers Peyronie's disease treatments, including collagenase with penile traction, intralesional interferon, and factors associated with curvature outcomes. Other cited papers address penile prostheses, erectile dysfunction devices, testosterone deficiency, and cosmetic penile enhancement procedures.[^5][^6][^7][^8][^9]
