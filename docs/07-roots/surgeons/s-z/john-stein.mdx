## Biography

John P. Stein was a professor at USC's Keck School of Medicine and practiced at USC Norris Comprehensive Cancer Center until his death in 2008.[^1] He graduated from Loyola University Stritch School of Medicine.[^2] He completed his urology residency under Donald G. Skinner's direction.[^1] He was secretary-elect of the American Urologic Association's Western Section.[^2]

## Contributions

Stein's cited work covers continent urinary diversion, including Kock ileal reservoirs, the orthotopic T-pouch neobladder, and adaptations of the T-pouch mechanism for urinary diversion and continent ileostomy. Other cited papers address orthotopic diversion after bladder contracture and diversion in women with bladder cancer.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
