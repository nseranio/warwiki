## Contributions

Stein's cited work covers continent urinary diversion, including Kock ileal reservoirs, the orthotopic T-pouch neobladder, and adaptations of the T-pouch mechanism for urinary diversion and continent ileostomy. Other cited papers address orthotopic diversion after bladder contracture and diversion in women with bladder cancer.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Orthotopic neobladder reconstruction in women** (1997). Stein led a 34-patient series reporting functional outcomes and selection considerations for orthotopic diversion in women.[^9]
- **Orthotopic T-pouch ileal neobladder** (1998). Stein and colleagues described an ileal neobladder with a serosal-lined antireflux T limb.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Double T-Pouch](/docs/surgical-techniques/04c-urinary-diversion/double-t-pouch).
- **209-patient T-pouch outcomes series** (2004). Stein led an intermediate follow-up series assessing function and complications after orthotopic T-pouch construction in 209 patients.[^11] Cited on WARWIKI: [Double T-Pouch](/docs/surgical-techniques/04c-urinary-diversion/double-t-pouch).
