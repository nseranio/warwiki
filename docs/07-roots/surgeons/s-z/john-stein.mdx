## Key Publications

Stein's cited work covers continent urinary diversion, including Kock ileal reservoirs, the orthotopic T-pouch neobladder, and adaptations of the T-pouch mechanism for urinary diversion and continent ileostomy. Other cited papers address orthotopic diversion after bladder contracture and diversion in women with bladder cancer.<sup>[P2](#publication-10)</sup><sup>[P4](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P3](#publication-11)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-7)</sup>

**Orthotopic neobladder reconstruction in women** (1997). Stein led a 34-patient series reporting functional outcomes and selection considerations for orthotopic diversion in women.

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

</KeyPublication>

**209-patient T-pouch outcomes series** (2004). Stein led an intermediate follow-up series assessing function and complications after orthotopic T-pouch construction in 209 patients.
