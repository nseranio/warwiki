## Contributions

Stein's cited work covers continent urinary diversion, including Kock ileal reservoirs, the orthotopic T-pouch neobladder, and adaptations of the T-pouch mechanism for urinary diversion and continent ileostomy. Other cited papers address orthotopic diversion after bladder contracture and diversion in women with bladder cancer.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
