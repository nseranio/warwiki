## Biography

Jennifer Tash Anger is a professor in residence of urology and vice chair of research at UC San Diego.[^1][^2] Anger earned her medical degree at USC's Keck School of Medicine and trained in urology and surgery at New York-Presbyterian Weill Cornell Medical Center.[^2] Anger completed fellowships in urologic reconstruction at Duke University Medical Center, with George D. Webster and Cindy Amundsen, and in urological health services research at UCLA.[^2][^1]
