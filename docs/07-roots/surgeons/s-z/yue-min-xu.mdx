## Known for

- **Colonic-mucosa graft urethroplasty** (2003). Reported experimental and clinical use of free colonic mucosa grafts for complex long-segment urethral reconstruction.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Enterourethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/enterourethroplasty).

## Contributions

Xu's confirmed papers cover urethral stricture reconstruction, including colonic and lingual mucosal grafts, pelvic fracture urethral injuries, and urethrovaginal or urethrorectal fistulas.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
