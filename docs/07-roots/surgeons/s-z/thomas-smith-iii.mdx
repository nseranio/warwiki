## Training and career

<div className="sp-facts">

- **Current position** Department of Urology, Division of Surgery, The University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center; urology quality officer (since 2020)[^1]
- **Medical school** University of Texas Medical Branch, Galveston (1997)[^1][^2]
- **Residency** General surgery and urology, UT Southwestern Medical Center (1997–2003)[^2]
- **Fellowship** Trauma and reconstructive urology, University of Washington and Harborview Medical Center (2008–2009), with Hunter Wessells[^2]
- **Career** Private practice, Urology Associates of Kerrville, Texas, before fellowship; instructor, University of Washington (2008–2009); assistant professor, University of Texas Health Science Center at Houston (2009–2011) and Baylor College of Medicine (2011–2018)[^1][^2]
- **Leadership** Residency program director, Baylor College of Medicine urology (2012–2015, 2016–2017); chief of urology service, Ben Taub General Hospital (2013–2018); section editor, AUA Core Curriculum (2018–2025)[^1]
- **TURNS** Founding member and past president; permanent member of the board of directors (since 2013)[^1][^2]

## Key Publications

Smith served on the American Urological Association Urotrauma Guideline panel and coauthored the 2014 guideline.<sup>[P1](#publication-3)</sup> His cited work covers damage-control management of genitourinary injuries and TURNS multicenter outcomes of artificial urinary sphincter placement.<sup>[P2](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P3](#publication-5)</sup>

<ol className="sp-pubs">

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[0]} citations={citations}>

**Urotrauma: AUA guideline** (2014). Panel member for the guideline, built on a systematic review of 372 studies, that sets statements for the evaluation and management of renal, ureteral, bladder, urethral and genital injuries.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[1]} citations={citations}>

**Damage control for urologic trauma** (2013). Lead author, with Michael Coburn, of a review of damage-control management of renal, ureteral, bladder, urethral and genital injuries in critically injured patients, which are often diagnosed intraoperatively with little or no preoperative imaging.

</KeyPublication>

<KeyPublication publication={publicationData.keyPublications[2]} citations={citations}>

**Artificial urinary sphincter erosion** (2014). Coauthored the TURNS prospective study of 386 sphincters at 8 institutions: 31 (8.0%) were explanted, with higher rates after prior radiation, prior UroLume stent placement and 3.5 cm cuffs.

</KeyPublication>

</ol>
