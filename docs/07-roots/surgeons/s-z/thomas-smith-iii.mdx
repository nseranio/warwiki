## Biography

Thomas G. Smith III is an associate professor in the Department of Urology at the University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center.[^1][^2] Smith is MD Anderson's quality officer for urology and a board member of the Trauma and Urologic Network of Reconstructive Surgeons.[^2]
