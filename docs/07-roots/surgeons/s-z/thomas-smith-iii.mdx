## Contributions

Smith served on the American Urological Association Urotrauma Guideline panel and coauthored the 2014 guideline.[^3] His cited work covers damage-control management of genitourinary injuries and TURNS multicenter outcomes of artificial urinary sphincter placement.[^4][^5]

- **Urotrauma: AUA guideline** (2014). Panel member for the guideline, built on a systematic review of 372 studies, that sets statements for the evaluation and management of renal, ureteral, bladder, urethral and genital injuries.[^3] Cited on WARWIKI: [Trauma & Emergencies](/docs/special-populations/05a-trauma-emergencies).
- **Damage control for urologic trauma** (2013). Lead author, with Michael Coburn, of a review of damage-control management of renal, ureteral, bladder, urethral and genital injuries in critically injured patients, which are often diagnosed intraoperatively with little or no preoperative imaging.[^4] Cited on WARWIKI: [Damage Control in Genitourinary Trauma](/docs/special-populations/05a-trauma-emergencies/damage-control-gu-trauma).
- **Artificial urinary sphincter erosion** (2014). Coauthored the TURNS prospective study of 386 sphincters at 8 institutions: 31 (8.0%) were explanted, with higher rates after prior radiation, prior UroLume stent placement and 3.5 cm cuffs.[^5] Cited on WARWIKI: [Artificial Urinary Sphincter (Device)](/docs/foundations/tools/biomaterials/prosthetics/artificial-urinary-sphincter).
