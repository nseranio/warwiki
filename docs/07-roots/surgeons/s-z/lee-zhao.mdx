## Contributions

Zhao's cited work examines robotic reconstruction of ureteral strictures and posterior urethral or bladder neck stenosis, including buccal mucosa grafting and Y-V plasty. Other cited studies address gender-affirming vaginoplasty and surgical revisions or complications.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]

- **Robotic buccal mucosa graft ureteroplasty** (2015). Described robotic buccal mucosa graft repair for proximal or multifocal ureteral strictures unsuitable for primary anastomosis.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Buccal Mucosa Graft Ureteroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/bmg-onlay-ureter).
- **Robotic peritoneal flap vaginoplasty** (2019). Reported robotic peritoneal flaps to augment vaginal depth during feminizing vaginoplasty.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Peritoneal Flap Vaginoplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery/peritoneal-pull-through-vaginoplasty).
- **Robotic posterior urethroplasty series** (2023). Senior-authored a 105-patient series reporting outcomes after robotic posterior urethroplasty.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Male Urethroplasty — Incisions & Approaches](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/male-urethroplasty-incisions-approaches).
