## Biography

Lee C. Zhao is a professor of urology and plastic surgery at NYU Grossman School of Medicine.[^1] Zhao directs male reconstructive surgery and co-directs the transgender reconstructive surgery program at NYU Langone.[^1] Zhao received an MD from Northwestern University and completed a urology residency at the McGaw Medical Center of Northwestern University.[^1] Zhao completed a reconstructive surgery fellowship at UT Southwestern Medical Center in 2013 with Allen Morey.[^1][^2]

## Contributions

Zhao's cited work examines robotic reconstruction of ureteral strictures and posterior urethral or bladder neck stenosis, including buccal mucosa grafting and Y-V plasty. Other cited studies address gender-affirming vaginoplasty and surgical revisions or complications.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
