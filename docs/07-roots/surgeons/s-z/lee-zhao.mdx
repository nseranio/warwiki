## Contributions

Zhao's cited work examines robotic reconstruction of ureteral strictures and posterior urethral or bladder neck stenosis, including buccal mucosa grafting and Y-V plasty. Other cited studies address gender-affirming vaginoplasty and surgical revisions or complications.[^3][^4][^5][^6][^7]
