## Biography

Michaela Sljivich is a reconstructive urologist at Mount Sinai Medical Center in Miami Beach, Florida.[^1] She earned her medical degree from the University of Texas Medical Branch in Galveston and completed urology residency at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai Hospital in New York.[^1] She completed genitourinary reconstructive surgery and sexual medicine fellowship training through Mount Sinai Health System; Rajveer Purohit directed the reconstructive urology fellowship.[^1][^2]

## Contributions

Her cited work on gender-affirming genital surgery compares vaginectomy techniques in transmasculine surgery and reports outcomes after robot-assisted sigmoid vaginoplasty.[^3][^4]
