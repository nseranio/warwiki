## Biography

Timothy J. Tausch is a urologist at Avita Health System.[^1] Tausch attended Case Western Reserve University School of Medicine and completed a residency at Madigan Army Medical Center.[^1] Tausch completed a fellowship in trauma, reconstructive, and prosthetic urology at UT Southwestern Medical Center.[^1] Tausch previously directed trauma, reconstruction, and prosthetic urology and the residency program at Walter Reed National Military Medical Center.[^1]

## Contributions

Tausch's cited work on adult buried penis describes an anatomic classification and treatment algorithm and a ventral slit scrotal flap technique.[^2][^3]
