## Contributions

Her confirmed papers study treatments for pelvic floor disorders, including midurethral and pubovaginal slings, sacrocolpopexy, native tissue apical suspension, and tibial nerve stimulation for fecal incontinence. They also examine perioperative care after pelvic floor reconstructive surgery, including catheter removal and enhanced recovery pathways.[^4][^5][^6][^7][^8]

- **CARE trial long-term sacrocolpopexy outcomes** (2013). Co-authored the extended CARE trial report on long-term prolapse, incontinence and mesh outcomes after abdominal sacrocolpopexy.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Mesh Complications & Removal — POP and SUI Mesh](/docs/surgical-techniques/04g-prolapse-repair/mesh-complications).
- **Overactive-bladder symptoms after stress-incontinence surgery** (2015). Led a longitudinal analysis of overactive-bladder symptom changes after stress urinary incontinence surgery.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Mixed Urinary Incontinence](/docs/clinical-conditions/03a-storage-incontinence/mixed-incontinence).
- **ROSETTA trial** (2016). Participated in the multicenter comparison of intradetrusor onabotulinumtoxinA and sacral neuromodulation for refractory urgency incontinence.[^11] Cited on WARWIKI: [Intradetrusor OnabotulinumtoxinA](/docs/surgical-techniques/04f-incontinence-procedures/procedures/intradetrusor-botox).
