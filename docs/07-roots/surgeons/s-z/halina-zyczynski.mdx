## Key Publications

Her confirmed papers study treatments for pelvic floor disorders, including midurethral and pubovaginal slings, sacrocolpopexy, native tissue apical suspension, and tibial nerve stimulation for fecal incontinence. They also examine perioperative care after pelvic floor reconstructive surgery, including catheter removal and enhanced recovery pathways.<sup>[P4](#publication-4)</sup><sup>[P1](#publication-9)</sup><sup>[P5](#publication-6)</sup><sup>[P6](#publication-7)</sup><sup>[P7](#publication-8)</sup>
