## Biography

Stuart L. Stanton was a consultant gynecologist and director of the urogynecology unit at St. George's Hospital in London until retiring in 2003.[^1] Stanton studied medicine at The London Hospital Medical School from 1956 to 1961.[^1] Stanton worked as a research registrar at the Institute of Urology in London in 1971–1972 under Richard Turner-Warwick.[^1] Stanton was a founding member of the International Continence Society, the International Urogynecological Association, and the British Society of Urogynaecology.[^1]

[^1]: [IUGA Mourns the Loss of Prof. Stuart Stanton](https://www.iuga.org/news/news/iuga-mourns-the-loss-of-stuart-stanton). Accessed October 2026.
