## Training and career

<div className="sp-facts">

- **Died** 21 May 2024[^2]
- **Positions** Consultant gynecologist and director, urogynecology unit, St. George's Hospital, London (until retirement in 2003); research registrar, Institute of Urology, London, under Richard Turner-Warwick (1971–1972)[^1]
- **Medical school** The London Hospital Medical School (1956–1961)[^1]
- **Leadership** Founding member, International Continence Society, International Urogynecological Association, and British Society of Urogynaecology[^1]

## Contributions

- **Burch colposuspension series** (1976). Published a colposuspension series for female stress urinary incontinence.[^3]
- **UK urogynecology subspecialty training**. Set up the first UK subspecialty training program in urogynecology, according to the cited source.[^4]
- **Founding member of continence and urogynecology societies**. Was a founding member of the International Continence Society, International Urogynecological Association, and British Society of Urogynaecology.[^5]
