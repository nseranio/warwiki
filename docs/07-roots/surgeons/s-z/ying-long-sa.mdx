## Contributions

Sa's cited studies address urethral reconstruction after pelvic fracture, including posterior anastomotic urethroplasty and urethrorectal or urethrovaginal fistula repair. Other studies examine graft reconstruction of complex urethral strictures using colonic mucosa or small intestinal submucosa.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]
