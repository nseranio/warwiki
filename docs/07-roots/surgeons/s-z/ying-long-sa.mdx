## Contributions

Sa's cited studies address urethral reconstruction after pelvic fracture, including posterior anastomotic urethroplasty and urethrorectal or urethrovaginal fistula repair. Other studies examine graft reconstruction of complex urethral strictures using colonic mucosa or small intestinal submucosa.[^2][^3][^4][^5][^6]

- **Gracilis urethral myoplasty for male incontinence** (2016). Reported a pedicled gracilis muscle flap wrapped around the bulbar urethra for acquired male urinary incontinence.[^7] Cited on WARWIKI: [The Leg & Thigh](/docs/foundations/anatomy-physiology/lower-extremity/leg-thigh).
- **Gracilis interposition for urethrorectal fistula** (2017). Reported transperineal urethroplasty with gracilis muscle interposition for pelvic-fracture urethral defects complicated by urethrorectal fistula.[^8] Cited on WARWIKI: [Combined Abdominoperineal Reconstruction](/docs/surgical-techniques/04ab-bladder-neck-reconstruction/bnc-vuas/combined-abdominoperineal).
- **Progressive transperineal posterior urethroplasty strategy** (2021). Described a proximal- or distal-transection strategy for pelvic-fracture urethral defects in a 1,637-patient surgical series.[^9] Cited on WARWIKI: [Principles of Urethral Reconstruction](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/urethral-reconstruction-principles).
- **Modified Y-V plasty for bladder outlet obliteration** (2024). Associated with modified laparoscopic Y-V plasty for selected iatrogenic bladder outlet obliteration after prostate surgery.[^10] Cited on WARWIKI: [Y-V Plasty for BNC and VUAS](/docs/surgical-techniques/04ab-bladder-neck-reconstruction/bnc-vuas/yv-plasty).
