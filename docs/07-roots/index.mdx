# Roots of Reconstruction

This section traces the intellectual and surgical heritage of the specialty, documenting the history of functional urology and pelvic reconstruction, the origins of the eponyms woven into everyday practice (Martius, Latzko, McIndoe, Leadbetter, and others), the genealogy of fellowship training programs and mentorship lineages, and the notable figures whose innovations defined the field — grounding current practice in the tradition from which it grew.
