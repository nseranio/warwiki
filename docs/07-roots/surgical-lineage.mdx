# Surgical Lineage & Fellowship Tree

The field of genitourinary reconstruction was built by a tight network of surgeons who trained one another across generations. This page maps those mentor–trainee relationships — from the foundational figures who established the discipline to the contemporary surgeons practicing today.

Use the **Lineage Tree** to trace the direct lines of surgical descent, or browse the **Directory** to search and explore individual surgeons.
