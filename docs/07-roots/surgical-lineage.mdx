# Surgical Genealogy

A directory and mentor–trainee map of **GURS** and **URPS** lineages is in active development and not yet ready to publish. If you trained in either subspecialty and can contribute fellowship rosters, lineage links, or biographical detail, please email **warwikihq@gmail.com** or open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/nseranio/warwiki/issues).
