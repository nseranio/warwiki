# Surgical Genealogy

Reconstructive urology and urogynecology were built by small groups of surgeons who trained one another across generations. This page maps those mentor–trainee lines by fellowship and lists every surgeon in a searchable directory. Profile pages cover the 105 surgeons whose work WARWIKI cites most, the URPS surgeons whose work WARWIKI cites often, and the founders of each training school; their names are linked, and each profile lists the surgeon's papers cited elsewhere on WARWIKI.

The GURS lineage from 1990 onward is adapted from Lee Zhao's [Reconstructive Urology Fellowship Family Tree](https://fellowshiptree.leezhaomd.org/), which he compiled from public sources such as institutional biographies, press releases and society pages. Earlier lineages are WARWIKI's own.

The URPS lineage covers urology-based and OB/GYN-based fellowships (female pelvic medicine, urogynecology and female urology). It was compiled by WARWIKI from fellowship alumni pages, faculty biographies and program histories, and from the trainee lists of Stuart Stanton, Linda Cardozo, Donald Ostergard, Harold Drutz and Peter Dwyer published with Schulz and Drutz's history of urogynecology.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> Where a source names a fellow's program and year but not a mentor, the mentor shown is the program director that year; fellows whose program but not mentor is known are listed in the directory by program.

Fellowship years are the year of completion. A current position appears only where the source lists it as confirmed.

Corrections and additions are welcome at **warwikihq@gmail.com** or through the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/nseranio/warwiki/issues).
