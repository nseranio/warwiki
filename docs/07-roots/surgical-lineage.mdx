# Surgical Genealogy

The reconstructive subspecialties were built by tight networks of surgeons who trained one another across generations. This page maps those mentor–trainee relationships and provides a searchable directory of individual surgeon profiles, organized by subspecialty.

Select a subspecialty below to switch between **Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgery (GURS)** and **Urogynecology & Reconstructive Pelvic Surgery (URPS)**. The *Genealogy Tree* traces direct lines of surgical descent; the *Directory* lists every profiled surgeon in that subspecialty.
