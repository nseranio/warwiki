# Surgical Genealogy

Reconstructive urology and urogynecology were built by small groups of surgeons who trained one another across generations. This page maps those mentor–trainee lines by fellowship and lists every surgeon in a searchable directory. Names with a profile page are linked; each profile also lists the surgeon's papers cited elsewhere on WARWIKI.

The GURS lineage from 1990 onward is adapted from Lee Zhao's [Reconstructive Urology Fellowship Family Tree](https://fellowshiptree.leezhaomd.org/), which he compiled from public sources such as institutional biographies, press releases and society pages. Earlier lineages and the URPS schools are WARWIKI's own. Fellowship years are the year of completion. A current position appears only where the source lists it as confirmed.

Corrections and additions are welcome at **warwikihq@gmail.com** or through the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/nseranio/warwiki/issues).
