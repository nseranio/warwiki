# Special Populations

This section provides context-specific overlays for patient groups whose care requires a modified approach, including those presenting after genitourinary trauma or emergencies, patients with oncologic or radiation-related injury, individuals undergoing gender-affirming surgery, men's health and andrology patients, and women with conditions specific to female anatomy — each chapter building on the foundational techniques while addressing the unique anatomy, biology, and goals of that population.
