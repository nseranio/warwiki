# Women's Health

The clinical layer the urogynecologist and female-pelvic-medicine specialist often manages alongside the surgical problem — menopausal genitourinary changes, recurrent infection, sexual function, and the age-appropriate cancer-screening and risk-reduction decisions that shape preoperative counseling.
