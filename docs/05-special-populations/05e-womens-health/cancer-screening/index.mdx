# Cancer Screening

**Cancer screening** is age- and risk-stratified for the three cancers most often co-managed alongside female pelvic-floor surgery and reconstructive urogynecology. The pages summarize USPSTF, ACS and ASCCP guidance for cervical cancer, USPSTF, NCCN, ACR and ACP guidance for breast cancer, and the risk-stratified approach to endometrial cancer, which has no routine screening and a separate Lynch syndrome and postmenopausal-bleeding pathway.

The pages are written for the specialist who co-manages patients with gynecologic oncology and primary care colleagues: the urogynecologist counseling a postmenopausal patient about hormone therapy and breast cancer screening, the reconstructive surgeon evaluating a tamoxifen-treated patient with new postmenopausal bleeding, or the physician confirming that cervical cancer screening is current before mesh-based surgery.
