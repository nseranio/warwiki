# Lifelong Urologic Care

**Lifelong urologic care** covers **transitional urology**, for the adolescent or young adult moving from pediatric to adult care, and **geriatric urology**, for the older adult whose anatomy, pharmacology, and treatment goals have shifted. Both concern patients whose bladder dynamics change with growth and aging, whose surveillance spans decades, and whose care needs a multidisciplinary team because a single specialist cannot deliver lifelong care alone.
