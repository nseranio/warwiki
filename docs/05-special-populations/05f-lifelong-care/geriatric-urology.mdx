# Geriatric Urology

*Content in development.*

The reconstructive and functional-urologic considerations specific to the older adult — frailty assessment, anesthetic risk, pharmacology of aging, the "treat the patient not the bladder" reframing in advanced age, and the decision framework for prosthetics, slings, and major reconstruction in patients with limited functional reserve. Anchors expected to include the AUA Geriatric Urology white paper, the Comprehensive Geriatric Assessment (CGA), Fried frailty phenotype, and the URPS curriculum's "successful aging" framework.

For perioperative considerations, see [Frailty Assessment](/docs/foundations/perioperative-care/preoperative-assessment/frailty) and the broader [Perioperative Care](/docs/foundations/perioperative-care) section.
