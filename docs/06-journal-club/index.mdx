Curated **literature and guideline resources** for functional urology, GU reconstruction, and pelvic floor medicine. The Journal Club organizes the field's primary literature and society documents into two complementary references: a topical literature database for evidence searches by clinical question, and a guideline / white-paper repository for the consensus documents that anchor day-to-day practice.

The point of the section is to keep the reconstructive surgeon's evidence layer **separate from the operative-technique pages** — when reading a Treatment Atlas page, the technical content is in front of you; when you need to verify a specific RCT, locate a guideline recommendation, or see how the consensus has shifted, the Journal Club is where the underlying evidence is curated. Society guidelines covered include **AUA, EAU, SUFU, AUGS, ICS / 6th ICI, NCCN, ACOG, ACR, ASCRS, AGA-ACG, WPATH SOC8**, and **Endocrine Society**.
