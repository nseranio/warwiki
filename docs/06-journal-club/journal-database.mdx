# Journal Database

Searchable database of curated articles in GU reconstruction (GURS), urogynecology and pelvic floor surgery (URPS), and general urology. Click **Show abstract** on any article to read the summary inline.

Use the filters to narrow by specialty, topic, or study design. Search across titles, authors, journals, and abstract text.

## How to Use This Database

- **Search bar** — finds matches in title, authors, journal name, and abstract text
- **Specialty filter** — GURS (GU reconstruction) · URPS (urogynecology/pelvic floor) · General
- **Topic filter** — Urethral Stricture · Prolapse · Incontinence · Prosthetics · Upper Tract · Medical Management
- **Design filter** — RCT · Review · Prospective · Cohort
- **Show abstract** — expands inline abstract for quick appraisal without leaving the page
- **Title link** — opens the full article via DOI in a new tab

**Adding Articles.** This database is seeded from the WARWIKI Zotero library. To suggest an article for inclusion, add it to the shared collection or contact the editorial team.
