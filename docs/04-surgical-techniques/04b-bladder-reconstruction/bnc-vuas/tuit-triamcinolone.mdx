# Transurethral Incision with Injection of Triamcinolone

Cold-knife or electrocautery incision (typically at 12 o'clock) of bladder neck contracture combined with intralesional triamcinolone injection to inhibit fibroblast proliferation; first-line endoscopic treatment for BNC/VUAS. Reported success ~65%.
