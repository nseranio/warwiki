# Combined Abdominoperineal Approach

Simultaneous abdominal and perineal exposure for complex, long-segment, or recurrent VUAS. Provides maximal access for complete excision of fibrotic segment, reanastomosis, and simultaneous cystoplasty or flap interposition when needed.
