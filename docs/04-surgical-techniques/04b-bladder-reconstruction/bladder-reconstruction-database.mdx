# Bladder Reconstruction Database

Bladder reconstruction sits at the intersection of **reservoir salvage**, **outlet salvage**, and **continence reconstruction**. Some operations enlarge a hostile low-capacity bladder, others reopen or remake a scarred bladder neck or vesicourethral anastomosis, and others create continence when the outlet itself is irreparably weak or absent. The table below brings those families together so the surgeon can think in terms of the entire reconstructed lower urinary tract rather than isolated procedures.

> In practice, these operations are often combined. An augmented bladder may still need an outlet procedure; a recurrent VUAS may end in a bladder-neck reconstruction; and neurogenic patients may require a reservoir plus a catheterizable outlet strategy.
