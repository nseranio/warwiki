# Bladder Reconstruction

Reconstructive surgery of the lower urinary reservoir: **augmentation cystoplasty** for the hostile low-capacity bladder, **continent catheterizable channels** for patients who cannot empty per urethram, and **ileovesicostomy** as an incontinent low-pressure outlet. For salvage of bladder-neck contracture and vesicourethral anastomotic stenosis (BNC / VUAS) and for continence-creating outlet procedures (Young-Dees-Leadbetter, Kropp, Pippi-Salle, BN closure), see the dedicated **[Bladder Neck Reconstruction](/docs/surgical-techniques/04ab-bladder-neck-reconstruction)** page.
