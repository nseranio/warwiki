# Gender-Affirming Surgery

Gender-affirming surgery (GAS) is the operative arm of multidisciplinary gender-affirming care, governed by the WPATH Standards of Care v8 (2022), the ACOG 2021 Committee Opinion, the Endocrine Society 2017 Clinical Practice Guideline, and the AAFP 2023 Position Paper. This atlas section covers only the genitourinary and reconstructive-urologic component of GAS. Top surgery, facial feminization and voice surgery are outside the reconstructive-urologic scope and are covered on the corresponding clinical-conditions pages in Special Populations.

The decision space divides into three cohorts. Each has its own tabbed Decision Framework and searchable Treatment Database.
