# Gender-Affirming Surgery

Gender-affirming surgery (GAS) is the operative component of multidisciplinary gender-affirming care. WPATH Standards of Care v8 (2022) includes guidance on surgery and postoperative care.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> ACOG Committee Opinion 823 addresses inclusive care, hormone therapy, and preventive care;<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> the Endocrine Society's 2017 guideline addresses endocrine treatment and surgery-related decisions and referrals;<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> and the AAFP policy, updated in 2026, addresses primary care and access to gender-affirming care.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup> This atlas section covers only the genitourinary and reconstructive-urologic component of GAS. Top surgery, facial feminization and voice surgery are outside the reconstructive-urologic scope and are covered on the corresponding clinical-conditions pages in Special Populations.
