# Incontinence Procedures

This subsection covers surgical and procedural management of urinary incontinence including female slings and suspensions, male urethral slings, artificial urinary sphincter implantation, urethral bulking agents, intradetrusor onabotulinumtoxinA injection, and neuromodulation via sacral nerve modulation and posterior tibial nerve stimulation.
