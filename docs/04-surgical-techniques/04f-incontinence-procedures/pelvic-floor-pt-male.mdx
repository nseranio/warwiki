# Pelvic Floor Physical Therapy (Male)

Pelvic floor muscle training (PFMT) directed by a specialized physiotherapist is the first-line intervention for post-prostatectomy stress urinary incontinence. Exercises target voluntary contraction and endurance of the levator ani and external urethral sphincter, improving periurethral muscle tone and sphincteric coaptation. Evidence supports earlier return to continence when PFMT is initiated preoperatively and continued postoperatively.
