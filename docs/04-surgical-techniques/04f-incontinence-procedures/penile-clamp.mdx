# Penile Clamp

A penile clamp is an external compression device applied circumferentially to the penile shaft to mechanically occlude the urethra and prevent stress urinary incontinence. Devices such as the Cunningham clamp provide a practical temporizing solution for men with mild-to-moderate SUI awaiting definitive surgical treatment or those unfit for surgery. Long-term use is discouraged due to the risk of urethral erosion, pressure necrosis, and penile skin complications.
