# Kulkarni One-Stage Panurethral Urethroplasty

Kulkarni's one-stage panurethral urethroplasty employs a single perineal incision with preservation of one-sided bulbospongiosus muscle and vas deferens to reconstruct the entire urethra from the meatus to the bulb using buccal mucosal grafts.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> The technique avoids penile skin incisions and achieves broad urethral coverage in a single operative setting. It is a landmark contribution to the management of long-segment lichen sclerosus–associated stricture disease.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
