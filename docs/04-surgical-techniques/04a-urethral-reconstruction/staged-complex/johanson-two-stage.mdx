# Johanson Two-Stage Urethroplasty

Johanson's two-stage urethroplasty is the historic reference technique for complex panurethral or recurrent stricture disease where single-stage repair is not feasible.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> In the first stage, the urethra is opened and marsupialized to the perineal skin. After a period of tissue maturation (typically 3–6 months), the second stage creates a new urethral tube by tubularizing the epithelialized strip. This approach remains an important salvage option when prior reconstructions have failed and underlies modern Bracka-style staged repair.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
