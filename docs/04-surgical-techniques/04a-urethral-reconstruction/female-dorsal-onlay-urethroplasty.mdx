# Female Dorsal Onlay Urethroplasty

Female dorsal onlay urethroplasty uses a buccal mucosal graft placed on the dorsal surface of the urethra to augment a strictured female urethra via a transvaginal approach. The graft is sutured to the periurethral fascia and held against the pubic periosteum for vascular support. Small series demonstrate good outcomes with acceptable donor-site morbidity, positioning this technique as a preferred option for female urethral stricture disease.
