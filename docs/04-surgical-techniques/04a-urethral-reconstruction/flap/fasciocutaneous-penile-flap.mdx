# Fasciocutaneous Ventral Penile Transverse Island Flap

This fasciocutaneous flap harvests a transverse island of ventral penile skin with its underlying dartos fascia and subcutaneous vascular pedicle for reconstruction of meatal or fossa navicularis stenosis. The pedicled island can be transposed to the glans to create a neomeatus or widen the distal urethra. Its robust vascularity makes it a reliable one-stage option for distal urethral stenosis associated with lichen sclerosus when local glans tissue is insufficient.
