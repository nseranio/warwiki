# Erickson Dorsal BMG + Ventral Fasciocutaneous Flap

The **Erickson / Breyer / McAninch (2012)** technique is a single-stage segmental urethral replacement designed for **long-segment penile strictures with a damaged urethral plate** that cannot be salvaged for either pure dorsal onlay grafting or pure ventral flap reconstruction.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> The hybrid combines a **dorsal buccal mucosal graft** (placed against the tunica albuginea) with a **ventral fasciocutaneous penile-skin flap** to bridge the unusable native plate.

For graft principles, see [Buccal Mucosa Graft](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/grafts/buccal-mucosa). For the ventral fasciocutaneous flap principles, see [Penile / Preputial Flap](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps/penile-preputial). For the closely related Karapanos modification, see [Karapanos Dorsal BMG + Ventral Orandi Flap](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/karapanos-dorsal-bmg-ventral-orandi).

---

## Concept

When the urethral plate is too diseased to support either dorsal-only or ventral-only repair, the operation reconstructs a **circumferentially neoformed urethra**: BMG dorsally on the tunica albuginea, and a fasciocutaneous penile-skin flap ventrally on a dartos pedicle. The two layers are quilted into a tube around an indwelling catheter.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Indications

- **Long-segment penile strictures** (panurethral or near-panurethral) with a non-salvageable plate
- **Failed prior urethroplasty** with persisting long penile defect
- Adequate non-diseased ventral penile shaft skin for flap harvest
- BMG donor sites available

Patients with **lichen sclerosus** are not candidates — genital skin should not be used.

---

## Outcomes

The original 2012 series reported:<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Mean stricture length | 7.5 cm |
| Primary success | 64% at 2.5 years |
| Secondary success (after one revision) | 79% |
| Most common failure pattern | Anastomotic recurrence at flap-graft junction |

The technique remains a **niche salvage option** for the long penile stricture with a destroyed plate, where neither pure dorsal nor pure ventral substitution alone is feasible.
