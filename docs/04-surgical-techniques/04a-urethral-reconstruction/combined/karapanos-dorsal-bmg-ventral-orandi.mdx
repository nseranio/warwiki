# Karapanos Dorsal BMG + Ventral Orandi Flap

The **Karapanos (2024)** technique refines the combined-substitution concept for **narrow penile strictures** in which the native urethral plate and corpus spongiosum are preserved rather than excised. A **dorsal buccal mucosal graft** is placed against the tunica albuginea, and a **ventral [Orandi flap](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/orandi-flap)** is rotated on its dartos pedicle to complete the neoformed lumen, yielding a **triangular cross-section neourethra** with both a graft layer and a flap layer.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

For the closely related Erickson combined repair (used when the plate must be discarded), see [Erickson Dorsal BMG + Ventral Fasciocutaneous Flap](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/erickson-dorsal-bmg-ventral-flap).

---

## Concept

Where Erickson 2012 replaced a non-salvageable plate, Karapanos 2024 **preserves** the existing plate and corpus spongiosum and augments it both dorsally and ventrally:<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

- Native plate stays in continuity (no transection, no plate excision)
- Dorsal BMG widens the dorsal half-circumference
- Ventral Orandi flap widens the ventral half-circumference
- Final lumen has a triangular cross-section

This is mechanically similar to a Palminteri two-sided BMG repair, but uses a flap ventrally — which is more durable in scarred or previously failed beds where a free graft would not take.

---

## Indications

- **Narrow penile strictures** with a viable plate that should not be sacrificed
- Salvage scenarios with previously failed graft-only repair
- Patients with adequate non-diseased ventral penile shaft skin for Orandi flap harvest

---

## Outcomes

The original Karapanos 2024 series reported:<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Median stricture length | 4.5 cm |
| Stricture-free rate | 91.7% at 38 months |
| Most common complication | Glanular dehiscence (low rate) |
