# Male Urethroplasty

Male urethral reconstruction encompasses procedures used to restore luminal continuity and adequate caliber to the urethra following stricture, trauma, lichen sclerosus, failed prior surgery, or congenital anomaly. Techniques range from simple endoscopic dilation to highly complex multi-stage flap reconstructions using oral mucosa, penile skin, gracilis muscle, or bowel. The library spans **&gt;30 distinct techniques across 8 categories**, evolving from the early staged approaches (Johanson 1953, Turner-Warwick 1960s) through the pedicled-flap era (Orandi 1968, Quartey 1983, McAninch 1993) to the BMG-dominant era (Barbagli 1996, Kulkarni 2009, Nikolavsky 2016) and an emerging minimally-invasive frontier (transmeatal, endoscopic, robotic, tissue-engineered).

---

## Decision Framework — Choosing a Male Urethroplasty

The matrix below summarizes the primary decision points. The AUA 2023 guideline anchors three of its most consequential rules — **oral mucosa is the first-choice graft**, **avoid genital skin in lichen sclerosus**, and **perineal urethrostomy is a legitimate long-term option** for high-risk patients.

| Clinical Scenario | First-Line Technique | Alternative(s) | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Short bulbar stricture (≤2 cm)** | [Non-transecting EPA / VS-EPA](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/jordan-vessel-sparing-epa) | [tEPA](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/excision-primary-anastomosis); [dorsal onlay BMG](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/dorsal-onlay-omg) | — |
| **Long bulbar stricture (2–5 cm)** | [Dorsal onlay BMG](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/dorsal-onlay-omg) (Barbagli or [Kulkarni one-sided](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/kulkarni-one-stage)) | [Ventral onlay BMG](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/ventral-onlay-omg) (proximal bulbar); [Asopa dorsal inlay](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/asopa-dorsal-inlay-omg) | [AAU](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/augmented-anastomotic) (HR 4.8 for recurrence vs dorsal onlay) |
| **Penile stricture (non-LS)** | [Asopa dorsal inlay](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/asopa-dorsal-inlay-omg) or [Kulkarni dorsolateral onlay](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/kulkarni-one-stage) | Dorsal Orandi flap; staged BMG | Ventral onlay (sacculation risk) |
| **Penile stricture (LS)** | [Staged BMG urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/kulkarni-two-stage) | One-stage dorsal inlay BMG (mild LS, viable plate) | All genital skin grafts/flaps |
| **Panurethral stricture** | [Kulkarni one-sided dorsolateral BMG](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/kulkarni-one-stage) | Preputial spiral graft (PSGU); combined BMG + flap; [perineal urethrostomy](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/blandy-perineal-urethrostomy) | — |
| **Fossa navicularis / meatal** | [Transmeatal ventral inlay BMG (Nikolavsky)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/transmeatal-omg-ventral-inlay) | [Sliding-T dorsal inlay (Rourke)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/sliding-t-dorsal-inlay); [meatotomy / meatoplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/meatotomy) | — |
| **Obliterative bulbar (no lumen)** | Palminteri two-sided BMG (dorsal + ventral) | [ntAAU](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/ntaau); staged urethroplasty | — |
| **Scarred bed / failed prior graft** | Pedicled flap ([Orandi](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/orandi-flap), [McAninch](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/mcaninch-circular-flap), [Quartey](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/quartey-flap)) ± dorsal BMG | [Gracilis + ventral BMG](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps/gracilis) (radiation / salvage) | Free graft alone (poor bed) |
| **Depleted oral mucosa** | Preputial flap; rectal mucosal graft (TEM / R-TAMIS); colonic mucosal graft (see [Enterourethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/enterourethroplasty)) | Penile skin graft | — |
| **Oral dyskeratosis (gutkha / betel nut)** | Preputial skin flap (dorsal onlay) | Penile skin graft | BMG (unsuitable oral mucosa) |
| **PFUI (posterior)** | Perineal anastomotic urethroplasty (see [PFUI](/docs/special-populations/05a-trauma-emergencies/pfui)) | Transpubic abdominoperineal (if perineal insufficient) | Repeated endoscopic procedures |
| **Older / comorbid; panurethral; patient preference** | Definitive [perineal urethrostomy](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/blandy-perineal-urethrostomy) | — | Complex reconstruction if patient declines |
