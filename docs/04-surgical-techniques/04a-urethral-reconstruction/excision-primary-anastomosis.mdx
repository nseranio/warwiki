# Excision and Primary Anastomosis (EPA)

Excision and primary anastomosis involves complete resection of the strictured urethral segment followed by spatulated end-to-end anastomosis of the healthy urethral ends. It is considered the gold standard for short bulbar urethral strictures (typically ≤2 cm) and pelvic fracture urethral injuries. Strict patient selection is essential, as tension-free anastomosis requires adequate urethral length and mobilization.
