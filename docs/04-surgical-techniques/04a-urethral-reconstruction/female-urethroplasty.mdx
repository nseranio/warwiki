# Female Urethroplasty

Female urethral reconstruction addresses stricture, diverticulum, prolapse, post-MUS-erosion scarring, and complete urethral loss / obliteration. No dorsal or ventral approach is universally preferred. Choose oral-mucosal graft or vaginal-flap reconstruction according to stricture anatomy, tissue quality and surgeon experience. AUA guidance supports urethroplasty in women because endoscopic treatment has limited durability.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

---

## Decision Framework — Choosing a Female Urethroplasty

| Clinical Scenario | Reconstructive option | Alternative(s) | Avoid / limitations |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Distal / meatal stricture (short, isolated)** | [Distal urethrectomy + advancement meatoplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/distal-urethrectomy-advancement) | [Dorsal onlay graft](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dorsal-onlay-urethroplasty) (BMG, labial, or lingual mucosa) | Repeated dilation |
| **Mid-urethral stricture, healthy vaginal tissue** | [Vaginal wall flap (ventral onlay or inlay)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-vaginal-flap-urethroplasty) ± [Martius reinforcement](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps/martius) | [Dorsal onlay graft](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dorsal-onlay-urethroplasty) | — |
| **Mid-urethral stricture, atrophic vagina** | [Dorsal onlay graft](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dorsal-onlay-urethroplasty) (BMG default; labial / lingual alternates) | — | Vaginal flap / graft (atrophic tissue) |
| **Pan-urethral or long-segment stricture** | [Dorsal onlay graft](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dorsal-onlay-urethroplasty) — extensive grafting of entire urethra | — | Repeated dilation with curative intent |
| **LS-related stricture** | [Dorsal onlay BMG](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dorsal-onlay-urethroplasty) | — | Vaginal / labial tissue (LS involvement risk) |
| **Prior MUS erosion / scarred periurethral bed** | [Ventral onlay BMG with Martius reinforcement](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-ventral-onlay-urethroplasty) | [Dorsal BMG](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dorsal-onlay-urethroplasty) | Vaginal flap (scarred bed) |
| **Complete urethral loss / obliteration** | [Vaginal-wall tubularization](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/vaginal-wall-tubularization) + pubovaginal sling | [Bladder wall flap (Tanagho or Nayyar)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/bladder-wall-flap-urethroplasty) | — |
| **Bladder neck obliteration (post-PFUI)** | [Bladder flap urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/bladder-wall-flap-urethroplasty) (anterior or posterior) | Nayyar novel anterior bladder tube | Repeated endoscopic procedures |
| **Failed prior urethroplasty** | [Combined vaginal flap + BMG](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/vaginal-flap-bmg-combined) (with Martius reinforcement when tissue is compromised) | [Vaginal flap](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-vaginal-flap-urethroplasty) (if tissue healthy); alternative graft source | Further dilation |
| **First presentation, mild symptoms** | Trial of [dilation](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dviu-dilation) after confirming an anatomical stricture | Discuss urethroplasty or intermittent self-dilation according to goals | Treating nonspecific LUTS without confirmed stricture |
