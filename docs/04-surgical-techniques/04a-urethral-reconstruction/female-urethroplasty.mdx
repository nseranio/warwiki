# Female Urethroplasty

Female urethral reconstruction addresses stricture, diverticulum, prolapse, post-MUS-erosion scarring, and complete urethral loss / obliteration. The female framework reflects emerging consensus that **dorsal onlay BMG is the preferred default** for most non-distal female strictures, with vaginal-flap and labial-mucosa options reserved for specific scenarios. Repeated dilation is discouraged — efficacy drops with each subsequent dilation and the AUA recommends urethroplasty over endoscopic management for recurrent disease.

---

## Decision Framework — Choosing a Female Urethroplasty

| Clinical Scenario | First-Line Technique | Alternative(s) | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Distal / meatal stricture (short, isolated)** | [Distal urethrectomy + advancement meatoplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/distal-urethrectomy-advancement) | [Dorsal onlay BMG](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dorsal-onlay-urethroplasty) or [labial mucosal graft](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/dorsal-onlay-labial-mucosa) | Repeated dilation |
| **Mid-urethral stricture, healthy vaginal tissue** | [Vaginal wall flap (ventral onlay or inlay)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-vaginal-flap-urethroplasty) ± [Martius reinforcement](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps/martius) | [Dorsal onlay BMG](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dorsal-onlay-urethroplasty); [dorsal labial mucosal graft](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/dorsal-onlay-labial-mucosa) | — |
| **Mid-urethral stricture, atrophic vagina** | [Dorsal onlay BMG](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dorsal-onlay-urethroplasty) | [Dorsal labial mucosal graft](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/dorsal-onlay-labial-mucosa) | Vaginal flap / graft (atrophic tissue) |
| **Pan-urethral or long-segment stricture** | [Dorsal onlay BMG](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dorsal-onlay-urethroplasty) (extensive grafting of entire urethra) | [Dorsal labial mucosal graft](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/dorsal-onlay-labial-mucosa) | Dilation (49% success; worsens fibrosis) |
| **LS-related stricture** | [Dorsal onlay BMG](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dorsal-onlay-urethroplasty) | — | Vaginal / labial tissue (LS involvement risk) |
| **Prior MUS erosion / scarred periurethral bed** | [Ventral BMG + Martius flap](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/bmg-martius-combined) | [Dorsal BMG](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dorsal-onlay-urethroplasty) | Vaginal flap (scarred bed) |
| **Complete urethral loss / obliteration** | [Vaginal-wall tubularization](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/vaginal-wall-tubularization) + pubovaginal sling | [Bladder flap (Tanagho or Nayyar)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/bladder-flap-tubularized) | — |
| **Bladder neck obliteration (post-PFUI)** | [Bladder flap urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/bladder-wall-flap-urethroplasty) (anterior or posterior) | Nayyar novel anterior bladder tube | Repeated endoscopic procedures |
| **Failed prior urethroplasty** | [BMG (dorsal or ventral) + Martius flap](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/bmg-martius-combined) | [Vaginal flap](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-vaginal-flap-urethroplasty) (if tissue healthy); alternative graft source | Further dilation |
| **First presentation, mild symptoms** | Trial of [dilation or DVIU](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dviu-dilation) (once) → if fails, proceed to urethroplasty | CIC ± intraurethral steroids (LS) | Repeated dilations (diminishing returns) |
