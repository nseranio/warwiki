# ICG-Guided Flap / Graft Perfusion Assessment

**Indocyanine green (ICG) near-infrared fluorescence angiography** is an emerging **intraoperative perfusion-assessment adjunct** for urethral reconstruction. Rather than a stand-alone technique, ICG supplements an existing flap or graft repair to identify ischemic tissue that would otherwise contribute to early necrosis, fistula, or graft failure.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

For the foundational technology and multi-domain applications, see [Indocyanine Green (ICG)](/docs/foundations/pharmacology/intraoperative-adjuncts/visualization-agents/icg). For procedure-specific use, see the constituent flap and graft articles in [Pedicled Flap Substitution](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/orandi-flap), [Graft Substitution](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/dorsal-onlay-omg), and the [Penile / Preputial Flap](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps/penile-preputial) hub.

---

## Concept

After flap or graft inset, intravenous ICG (typically 5–10 mg) is administered, and a near-infrared camera captures real-time fluorescence. Intraoperative perfusion mapping allows the surgeon to:<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

- Confirm pedicle viability before closure
- Trim ischemic flap edges before they necrose
- Verify graft-bed perfusion meets a quantitative cutoff

---

## Quantitative Cutoffs

Operating-time perfusion thresholds reported in the literature:<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

| Tissue | Cutoff |
|---|---|
| Graft beds | ≥ 60% baseline fluorescence |
| Flaps | ≥ 40% baseline fluorescence |

Tissue below these thresholds is at high risk for ischemic complication and should be revised, repositioned, or trimmed.

---

## Outcomes

The Paraboschi 2023 series first demonstrated ICG-guided onlay preputial island flap urethroplasty in pediatric hypospadias.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> Zhang 2024 extended the application to complex open adult urethral reconstruction, validating semiquantitative thresholds against postoperative outcomes.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

Direct outcome benefit (vs unguided reconstruction) is **not yet established** in randomized data; current adoption is at the level of an intraoperative quality-assurance adjunct.
