# Direct Vision Internal Urethrotomy (DVIU)

Direct vision internal urethrotomy is an endoscopic cold-knife or laser incision through the stricture scar under direct visualization. First described by Sachse in 1974, DVIU remains the most commonly performed endoscopic intervention for urethral stricture disease worldwide despite durable long-term patency rates below 30% for strictures greater than 2 cm.
