# Sliding T Dorsal Inlay

The sliding T dorsal inlay technique uses a T-shaped glans flap to reconstruct the fossa navicularis and distal urethra by advancing local tissue in a tension-free fashion. The vertical limb of the T is inlaid dorsally into the opened fossa while the horizontal limb covers the external defect, creating a wide, well-vascularized meatal opening. This elegant local tissue technique avoids the need for a free graft in selected cases of distal urethral stenosis.
