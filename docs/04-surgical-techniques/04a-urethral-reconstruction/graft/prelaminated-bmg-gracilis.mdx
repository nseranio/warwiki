# Prelaminated BMG-Gracilis Flap

The **prelaminated buccal mucosa-gracilis flap** is a two-stage salvage technique for the most devastated urethras — radiation necrosis, multiply-recurrent fistulae, lichen sclerosus with panurethral obliteration. **Stage 1** lays the BMG directly onto the gracilis muscle at its native medial-thigh location, allowing the graft to inosculate over 2–3 weeks into a composite gracilis + epithelialized mucosal sheet. **Stage 2** harvests this composite on the medial circumflex femoral pedicle and transposes it into the perineum, where the mucosal-lined surface of the muscle becomes the new urethral lumen.

The full technique, indications, anatomy, donor-site morbidity, and outcomes are covered as a section of the canonical gracilis-flap article alongside the related Vanni / Zinman one-stage ventral BMG + gracilis salvage urethroplasty.
