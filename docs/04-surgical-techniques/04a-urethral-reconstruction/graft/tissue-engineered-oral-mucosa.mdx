# Tissue-Engineered Oral Mucosa (TEOMG / MukoCell®)

**Tissue-engineered oral mucosa graft (TEOMG)** is a cultured oral keratinocyte construct grown from a small (~0.5 cm²) patient biopsy and expanded over approximately three weeks to produce a full-sized graft, eliminating the need for a large intra-oral harvest and the associated donor-site morbidity. The CE-marked European product **MukoCell®** is the most extensively studied platform.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

For the canonical native oral-mucosa hub and donor-site morbidity comparison, see [Buccal Mucosa Graft](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/grafts/buccal-mucosa). For the related liquid / minced BMG strategy, see [Liquid / Minced Buccal Mucosal Graft](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/liquid-minced-buccal).

---

## Concept

A small punch biopsy of buccal mucosa is enzymatically dissociated; oral keratinocytes are seeded onto a fibrin-based scaffold and expanded ex vivo for ~21 days. The resulting cultured construct is implanted as a free graft using standard onlay urethroplasty technique. The patient experiences only the morbidity of the small biopsy, not of a full-sized BMG harvest.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Indications

- Patients in whom large native BMG harvest is undesirable (prior bilateral harvest, dental compromise, small mouth opening, professional voice users)
- Long-segment / panurethral substitution where graft area required exceeds what one buccal cheek can supply
- Patient preference for minimal oral morbidity

---

## Outcomes

| Series | n | Follow-up | Stricture-free rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ram-Liebig 2017 multicenter (MukoCell) | 99 | 24 mo | 58.2% |
| Barbagli 2018 single-center | 22 | Median 55 mo | 68% |

Outcomes are **experience-dependent** — Ram-Liebig 2017 demonstrated a clear learning-curve effect, with success rising substantially in the second half of the cohort.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> Karapanos 2023 directly compared TEOMG vs native BMG and found native graft superior in unselected use.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

The construct remains **investigational in the United States** (no FDA approval); MukoCell is CE-marked in the EU.
