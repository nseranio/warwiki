# Asopa Dorsal Inlay OMG

The Asopa technique achieves dorsal placement of an oral mucosal graft via a ventral urethrotomy without requiring complete urethral mobilization from the corpora. After opening the urethra ventrally, the graft is tunneled through and sutured to the dorsal urethral wall, which is then held against the corpora cavernosa. This approach combines the hemostatic advantages of ventral access with the superior graft-take characteristics of dorsal placement.
