# Penile Skin / Inner Preputial Free Graft

**Penile skin and inner preputial free grafts** are non-pedicled substitutes for the urethral plate, harvested as full-thickness skin grafts from the penile shaft or inner prepuce and placed as **dorsal**, **ventral**, or **inlay** onlays. Although superseded in routine use by [Buccal Mucosa Graft](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/grafts/buccal-mucosa), penile skin remains a useful free-graft option in select scenarios — particularly the **dorsolateral inner preputial graft (DLIPG)** described by Tyagi 2021.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

For the canonical donor-site / biology hub, see [Penile / Preputial Skin Graft](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/grafts/penile-preputial-skin). For the closely related spiral-strip variant, see [Preputial Spiral Graft Urethroplasty (PSGU)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/preputial-spiral-graft).

---

## Indications

- Non-LS anterior strictures where BMG is unavailable, undesired, or already exhausted
- Patients refusing oral harvest
- Adequate non-diseased penile skin or inner preputial tissue

**Contraindicated in lichen sclerosus** — genital skin must not be used in LS strictures (AUA 2023 Strong Recommendation).<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>

---

## Outcomes

| Series | n | Follow-up | Success |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tyagi 2021 DLIPG | 53 | 48 mo | 87% |
| Dubey 2007 RCT (penile skin vs BMG) | — | — | Equivalent |
| Tyagi 2022 PeeBuSt RCT | — | — | Equivalent |
| Alrefaey 2025 RCT | — | — | Equivalent |

Three head-to-head RCTs (Dubey, PeeBuSt, Alrefaey) demonstrate **functional and anatomic equivalence** between penile-skin grafts and BMG for selected anterior strictures, although a 2024 SGUR survey found ~99% of contemporary reconstructive urologists prefer BMG.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
