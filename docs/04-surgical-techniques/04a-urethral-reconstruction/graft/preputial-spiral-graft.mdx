# Preputial Spiral Graft Urethroplasty (PSGU)

**Preputial Spiral Graft Urethroplasty (PSGU)**, described by **Bandini, Joshi & Kulkarni** in their 2023–2025 series, harvests **inner preputial skin in a continuous spiral strip** rather than a flat sheet, yielding a graft of up to **20 cm** in length. PSGU is positioned as a free-graft alternative to BMG and lingual mucosa for **panurethral stricture disease** when oral mucosa donor sites are exhausted, contraindicated, or suboptimal.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

For the canonical free-graft framework, see [Penile / Preputial Skin Graft](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/grafts/penile-preputial-skin) and [Buccal Mucosa Graft](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/grafts/buccal-mucosa). For the related pedicled-flap version, see [Quartey 'Q' Flap](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/quartey-flap) and [Pedicled Preputial Tube](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/pedicled-prepucial-tube).

---

## Concept

Inner preputial skin is harvested as a **continuous spiral** around the prepuce, then unfurled to a long, flat strip. Compared to a flat preputial graft (typically capped at ~6–8 cm), the spiral cut yields a substantially longer strip — Bandini reports up to 20 cm — sufficient to onlay or substitute a panurethral defect with a single non-oral graft.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

The graft is placed as a **dorsolateral or dorsal onlay**, following the [Kulkarni one-sided dorsolateral BMG](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/kulkarni-one-stage) technical principles for panurethral exposure.

---

## Indications

- **Panurethral stricture disease** (non-LS)
- Depleted oral mucosa (prior multi-site BMG harvest)
- Patient preference to avoid oral donor-site morbidity
- Adequate non-diseased prepuce (uncircumcised men)

PSGU is **contraindicated in lichen sclerosus**, where genital skin is unsuitable.

---

## Outcomes

The largest reported series — Bandini 2025 multicenter — included 114 patients with a median follow-up of 16 months:<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Cohort size | 114 |
| Mean stricture length | ~14 cm |
| Stricture-free rate | 90.4% at 16 mo |
| Donor-site morbidity | Mild; cosmetic preputial deformity |

Long-term durability data beyond 2 years is awaited. Direct head-to-head comparison with [Kulkarni one-sided BMG](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/kulkarni-one-stage) and [Enterourethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/enterourethroplasty) for panurethral disease has not been performed.
