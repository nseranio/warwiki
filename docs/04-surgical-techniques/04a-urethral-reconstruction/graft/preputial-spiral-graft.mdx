# Preputial Spiral Graft Urethroplasty (PSGU)

**Preputial spiral graft urethroplasty (PSGU)** is a single-stage technique described in 2023 by Kulkarni, Joshi, Basile and Bandini for panurethral strictures, defined as anterior urethral strictures longer than 10 cm. A helicoidal (spiral) harvest from the inner prepuce yields a single continuous graft of up to 20 cm for single-stage augmentation.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

For the canonical free-graft framework, see [Penile / Preputial Skin Graft](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/grafts/penile-preputial-skin) and [Buccal Mucosa Graft](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/grafts/buccal-mucosa). For the related pedicled flap, see [Quartey Flap](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/quartey-flap) and [Pedicled Preputial Tube](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/pedicled-prepucial-tube). For the alternative panurethral free-graft strategy, see [Kulkarni One-Sided Dorsolateral BMG](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/kulkarni-one-stage). For conventional flat inner-preputial and penile-skin free grafts, see the [Flat Penile Skin / Inner Preputial Free Grafts](#flat-penile-skin--inner-preputial-free-grafts) section below.

---

## Concept and Rationale

Panurethral strictures are often treated with two-stage procedures or with multiple buccal mucosa grafts (BMGs) harvested from both cheeks and sometimes the tongue. The authors propose that recurrence tends to occur where separate grafts meet.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> The spiral harvest cuts a 5 cm-wide preputial mucocutaneous strip in a helicoidal pattern to obtain one continuous graft from a limited donor area.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup> A single graft avoids inter-graft joins and oral harvest. Whether it reduces recurrence compared with multiple oral grafts has not been demonstrated.

---

## Surgical Technique

Based on the description by Kulkarni 2023:<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

1. **Preoperative assessment.** Retrograde and voiding cystourethrography and uroflowmetry (Qmax) characterize stricture length and severity. Intraoperative urethroscopy confirms stricture extent.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
2. **Graft harvest.** A 5 cm-wide strip of preputial mucocutaneous tissue is marked and harvested in a helicoidal (spiral) pattern, yielding a continuous graft up to 20 cm long.
3. **Urethral exposure.** Exposure is typically by the [Kulkarni one-sided dorsal onlay technique](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/kulkarni-one-stage): a perineal approach, penile invagination and one-sided urethral mobilization, which is intended to preserve the lateral blood supply.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
4. **Stricturotomy.** The strictured urethra is opened dorsally along its full length.
5. **Graft placement.** The spiral preputial graft is laid as a dorsal onlay over the entire length of the opened stricture and sutured to the urethral plate edges.
6. **Closure.** Standard layered closure over a urethral catheter.

---

## Indications

- **Panurethral strictures** (anterior urethral stricture &gt; 10 cm).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
- Patients with an intact prepuce (uncircumcised).<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
- Patients in whom buccal mucosa is unsuitable, for example oral dyskeratotic changes from tobacco / gutkha use, or limited oral mucosa from prior bilateral harvest.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>

---

## Prerequisites and Contraindications

- **Intact foreskin** is mandatory; circumcised patients are excluded.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)[[4]](#ref4)</sup>
- **LS-related urethral stricture.** The AUA 2023 amendment states that surgeons should not use genital skin in lichen sclerosus-proven strictures (Strong Recommendation; Grade C).<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup> The multicenter PSGU series lists absence of active LS as a limitation of the technique.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
- **Risk factors for failure of preputial skin flaps.** Mathur's series of 58 preputial skin flap repairs identified diabetes (RR 5.21) and smoking (RR 4.19) as predictors of failure, with post-infective etiology, panurethral stricture, length above 70 mm, previous urethroplasty and severe periurethral fibrosis also associated with failure. These are flap data and have not been validated for spiral free grafts.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>

## Outcomes

| Study | N | Median Stricture Length | Follow-Up | Qmax Improvement | Recurrence Rate | Complications |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kulkarni 2023 (initial series)<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> | 20 | Not specified | 12 mo | 6.5 → 15.1 mL/s | 5% (1/20) | 6/20 complications, including one grade IIIa |
| Bandini 2025 (multicenter)<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> | 114 | 16 cm | median 16 mo (IQR 7–24) | 4.5 → 24 mL/s (p &lt; 0.001) | 9.6% (11/114) | 23% (mostly Clavien-Dindo I) |

Findings from the largest multicenter series (114 patients across India, Colombia, Egypt, and Italy):<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

- Median operative time 134 minutes (IQR 123–142).
- Median Qmax improved from 4.5 to 24 mL/s (p &lt; 0.001).
- Success was defined as Qmax &gt; 10 mL/s without symptoms, which is not the same endpoint as cystoscopic patency or freedom from every intervention.
- Because the graft comes from the prepuce, no oral donor site is created, and with it no oral donor-site morbidity such as pain, numbness or restricted mouth opening.
- Preputial graft tissue lies close to the surgical field and is easily harvested, and a dorsolateral inner preputial graft series reports the procedure can be done under regional anesthesia.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>
- Preputial tissue is an option where buccal mucosa quality is compromised by tobacco or betel-nut use.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>

---

## Comparison With Buccal Mucosa Graft

The AUA 2023 guideline amendment names oral mucosa as the first-choice graft for substitution urethroplasty (Expert Opinion).<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup> Trials of conventional penile skin grafts versus BMG do not establish equivalence or validate the spiral harvest itself; they are summarized below. PSGU is supported by observational cohorts. In a 2022 survey of GURS members (134 respondents), 90% preferred multiple BMGs over a combined graft and flap for panurethral strictures, before the multicenter PSGU data were published.<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup>

---

## Limitations and Current Evidence Gaps

- **Follow-up.** The largest series has a median follow-up of 16 months (IQR 7–24), and long-term data beyond 2 years are lacking.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
- **Prepuce.** An intact prepuce is required, which excludes circumcised patients.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
- **Lichen sclerosus.** Active LS is a stated limitation.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
- **Comparators.** No randomized comparison with BMG urethroplasty (single-stage or staged) has been reported for panurethral strictures.
- **Experience.** The technique was first described in 2023 and experience is concentrated in a few high-volume centers.

## Flat Penile Skin / Inner Preputial Free Grafts

Conventional flat penile skin and inner preputial free grafts are non-pedicled substitutes for the urethral plate, harvested as full-thickness strips and placed as onlay or inlay augmentation grafts. Buccal mucosa has largely replaced them in routine use ([Buccal Mucosa Graft](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/grafts/buccal-mucosa)). Penile skin remains a free-graft option when oral harvest is unavailable, refused or exhausted by prior surgery.

### Dorsolateral Inner Preputial Graft (DLIPG)

Tyagi and colleagues described dorsolateral substitution urethroplasty with an inner preputial graft (DLIPG). In a retrospective analysis of prospectively maintained data from 80 patients with anterior strictures (mean stricture length 65 mm), success was 69/80 (87%) at a mean follow-up of 48 months (range 30–66). Success was defined as a stable Qmax above 14 mL/s or calibration with a 16 Fr catheter.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>

### RCT Evidence: Penile Skin vs BMG

Two randomized studies compared free penile skin grafts with BMG; neither tested a spiral graft in a panurethral population.

| Trial | Design | Success |
|---|---|---|
| Tyagi 2022 PeeBuSt<sup>[[10]](#ref10)</sup> | 100 randomized; 95 analyzed | PSG 42/47 versus BMG 44/48 at 18 months (p = 0.70); powered for a large difference, not equivalence |
| Alrefaey 2025<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup> | Superiority trial; 98 randomized, 92 analyzed | PSG 41/44 versus BMG 47/48 at 12 months (p = 0.346); baseline imbalances in stricture etiology and location |

The absence of a significant difference does not prove equivalence. Dubey 2007 compared a penile skin flap with BMG (55 patients) and is not a free-graft trial.<sup>[[9]](#ref9)</sup> Oral mucosa remains the AUA first-choice graft.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup>

### Contraindications

**Lichen sclerosus.** The AUA 2023 guideline amendment gives a Strong Recommendation (Grade C) that surgeons should not use genital skin in lichen sclerosus-proven urethral strictures, citing very high long-term failure rates.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup> The available spiral-graft series do not provide validated patient-specific risk estimates for diabetes or smoking.
