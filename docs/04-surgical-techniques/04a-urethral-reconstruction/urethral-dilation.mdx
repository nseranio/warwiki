# Urethral Dilation

Urethral dilation is a minimally invasive procedure in which calibrated dilators or balloon devices are passed to enlarge the luminal caliber of a urethral stricture or meatal stenosis. While technically straightforward and repeatable, dilation does not address the underlying scar tissue and carries high recurrence rates with repeated intervention. It remains a temporizing measure, particularly for meatal and fossa navicularis stenoses, or for patients who are poor surgical candidates.
