# Optilume Drug-Coated Balloon (DCB)

Optilume is an FDA-cleared drug-coated balloon catheter that combines mechanical dilation with local delivery of paclitaxel to inhibit fibroblast proliferation and reduce restenosis after urethral dilation. Clinical trial data (ROBUST III) demonstrate statistically superior patency rates at 24 months compared to dilation alone for bulbar urethral strictures ≤3 cm. It represents the first pharmacological adjunct to endoscopic urethral treatment approved for clinical use.
