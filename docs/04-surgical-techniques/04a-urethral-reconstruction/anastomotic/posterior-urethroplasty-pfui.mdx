# Posterior Urethroplasty / PFUI Repair

The full operative technique — **delayed perineal anastomotic urethroplasty** with the Webster / Ramon sequential ancillary maneuvers (bulbar mobilization → corporal splitting → inferior pubectomy → supracrural rerouting), the abdominoperineal transpubic approach for complex defects, the Scarberry early-vs-delayed timing data, the TURNS multicenter outcomes, the Plamadeala 2025 10-yr recurrence-free survival series, and MRI-based preoperative planning (PUA, gap length, cavernous body integrity) — lives on the canonical PFUI page in **Trauma & Emergencies**, alongside the acute SPT-vs-PER decision and the AAST / Colapinto-McCallum classification.
