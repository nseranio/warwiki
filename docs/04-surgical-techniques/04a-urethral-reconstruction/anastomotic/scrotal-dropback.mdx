# Scrotal Dropback Urethroplasty

**Scrotal dropback urethroplasty** is the colloquial name for the **Stage-2 maneuver** in two-stage scrotal-based urethral reconstruction — the penis is "dropped back" (separated) from the scrotum after the neourethra has matured within the scrotal tissue during Stage 1. The technique family includes the **Cecil**, **Culp**, **Pierce two-stage perineal-scrotal flap**, **Turner-Warwick scrotal drop-back** (revision perineal urethrostomy), and **Johanson staged urethroplasty** lineages — all sharing the same biological principle of marsupializing the penis into vascularized scrotal / perineal tissue and tubularizing the matured plate at a second stage.

➡ **[Cecil-Culp Procedure](/docs/surgical-techniques/04e-genital-reconstruction/cecil-culp-procedure)** — the canonical WARWIKI article. Covers historical origins (Cecil 1930s–40s, Culp Mayo modifications, Marshall scrotoplasty modification, Pierce 1979, Turner-Warwick, Kinnaird 2019), the temporary-embedding tissue-transfer principle, indications (severe proximal hypospadias, recurrent urethrocutaneous fistula with Ehle 100% success in 15 boys, penile trauma with skin loss, complex urethral stricture salvage), step-by-step Stage 1 / Stage 2 technique, scrotal vascular anatomy (Carrera 2009 microvascular study, Gil-Vernet biaxial 86% success in 37 patients), Cecil-Culp vs Johanson comparison, the Warner 2015 multi-institutional 35.7%-vs-17.5% recurrence signal favoring single-stage BMG, and the modern shift toward staged BMG urethroplasty.

For related scrotal-flap variants and the broader genital-reconstruction context, see [Genital Reconstruction Principles](/docs/surgical-techniques/04e-genital-reconstruction/genital-reconstruction-principles) and [Buccal Mucosa Graft](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/grafts/buccal-mucosa) (the modern successor tissue for staged urethroplasty).
