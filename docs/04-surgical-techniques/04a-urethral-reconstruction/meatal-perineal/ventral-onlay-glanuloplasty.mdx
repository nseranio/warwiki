# Ventral Onlay Glanuloplasty

Ventral onlay glanuloplasty applies a buccal mucosal or split-thickness skin graft to the ventral aspect of the glans penis and fossa navicularis after excising scarred meatal and perimeatal tissue.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> The graft augments the distal urethra and reconstructs the meatal opening in a single stage. This technique is well-suited for lichen sclerosus–related meatal stenosis and fossa navicularis strictures where the native tissue is unsuitable.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
