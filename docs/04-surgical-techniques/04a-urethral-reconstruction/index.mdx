# Urethral Reconstruction

This subsection covers operative approaches to the urethra including anastomotic urethroplasty, substitution urethroplasty with buccal mucosa graft or split-thickness skin graft, posterior urethroplasty and pelvic fracture urethral injury repair, and bladder neck reconstruction.
