# Urethral Reconstruction

**Urethral reconstruction** comprises the operations that restore luminal continuity and adequate caliber to the urethra after stricture, trauma, lichen sclerosus, failed prior surgery or congenital anomaly. Techniques range from simple endoscopic dilation to complex multi-stage flap reconstructions using oral mucosa, penile skin, gracilis muscle or bowel. Male and female anatomy, etiology and preferred technique differ substantially, so this section has two cohort-specific landings, each with its own Decision Framework and searchable Treatment Database.
