# Female Direct Vision Internal Urethrotomy (Female DVIU)

**Female direct vision internal urethrotomy (DVIU)** is the endoscopic incision of a female urethral stricture under direct cystoscopic visualization, typically at the 6 o'clock position. Like [female urethral dilation](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-urethral-dilation), it is a low-morbidity but low-durability option, and the **AUA 2023 guideline recommends urethroplasty over endoscopic treatment** for established female strictures.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Indications

- Short, single, primary female urethral strictures with good urethral wall quality
- Patients unwilling or unable to undergo open urethroplasty
- Diagnostic adjunct during evaluation of obstructive voiding

---

## Outcomes

Long-term results are **uniformly poor**, similar to female dilation:<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

| Metric | Approximate value |
|---|---|
| Initial symptomatic improvement | High |
| Mean durable success | ~30–47% at 24–48 mo |
| Efficacy of repeat endoscopic treatment | Falls with each attempt |

After failure, definitive [dorsal](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dorsal-onlay-urethroplasty) or [ventral](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/ventral-onlay-omg) onlay BMG urethroplasty offers durable cure.
