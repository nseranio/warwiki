# Primary End-to-End Anastomosis (Female)

**Primary end-to-end anastomosis** in women is reserved for **urethral loss or discontinuity** — distinct from stricture disease — where both ends of the urethra are healthy but separated by a short defect (e.g., after focal trauma or short obstetric injury). When the gap is short enough to permit a tension-free repair, the urethra is mobilized and the two ends are spatulated and sutured directly.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Indications

- Short focal urethral loss after trauma or obstetric injury
- Gap that closes without tension after limited urethral mobilization
- Healthy mucosa on both sides of the defect

For longer defects, see [Vaginal Wall Tubularization Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/vaginal-wall-tubularization) or [Bladder Wall Flap Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/bladder-wall-flap-urethroplasty).
