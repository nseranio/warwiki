# BMG + Martius Fat Pad Flap (Combined Female Urethroplasty)

A **ventral onlay BMG** reinforced with **[Martius fat-pad flap interposition](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps/martius)** is a hybrid technique used when the periurethral tissue is **compromised** — typically after synthetic mesh or sling erosion, prior failed urethroplasty, or radiation. The Martius flap provides a vascularized second tissue layer that protects the graft and reduces the fistula rate.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

---

## Indications

- Female urethroplasty after synthetic sling or mesh erosion
- Failed prior reconstruction with scarred / poorly vascularized periurethral bed
- Adjunctive coverage of a tenuous closure layer

---

## Outcomes

Eilber 2014 reported **97% distal-cure rate** in 70 patients with urethral diverticulum repair plus Martius interposition; analogous data for sling-erosion urethroplasty + BMG + Martius show similar performance.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
