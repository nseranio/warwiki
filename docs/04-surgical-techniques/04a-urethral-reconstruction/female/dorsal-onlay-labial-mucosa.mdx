# Dorsal Onlay Labial Mucosal Graft (Female)

The **dorsal onlay labial mucosal graft** uses **labia minora mucosa as a free graft** for female urethroplasty, anchored on the **Jena 2025** single-surgeon series (the largest reported labial-mucosa cohort to date). The technique avoids oral donor-site morbidity entirely while retaining the durability advantages of dorsal placement.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

For the canonical labial-mucosa harvest article, see [Labial Mucosa Graft](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/grafts/labial-mucosa). For the BMG counterpart, see [Female Dorsal Onlay BMG Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dorsal-onlay-urethroplasty).

---

## Concept

A strip of inner labia minora mucosa is harvested, defatted, and placed dorsally (12 o'clock) against the periurethral tissue with the urethra dissected off the symphysis — the same technical principles as dorsal-onlay BMG, with the labial mucosa substituting for the buccal donor.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Outcomes

Jena 2025 reported a single-surgeon series of **204 patients** at 2-year follow-up:<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Cohort | 204 |
| Stricture-free rate | 93.5% |
| Donor-site morbidity | Mild; no oral harvest needed |
| De novo SUI | None reported |

The technique is positioned as the **default oral-mucosa-sparing alternative** for female reconstruction in centers familiar with labial harvest.
