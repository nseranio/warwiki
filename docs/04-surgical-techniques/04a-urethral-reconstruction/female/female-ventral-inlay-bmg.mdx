# Female Ventral Inlay BMG Urethroplasty

The **female ventral inlay BMG urethroplasty** opens the urethra ventrally, **inlays a buccal mucosal graft directly into the urethrotomy without circumferential mobilization**, and closes the periurethral fascia and vaginal wall in layers. The technique is anchored on the **Mandal 2025 RCT** (the first head-to-head trial of ventral inlay vs dorsal onlay in women) and the Gaur 2024 series.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

For ventral onlay (a closely related but technically distinct technique), see [Ventral Onlay BMG Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/ventral-onlay-omg). For the dorsal counterpart, see [Female Dorsal Onlay BMG Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dorsal-onlay-urethroplasty).

---

## Concept — Inlay vs Onlay

**Inlay** = the urethrotomy is left open and a graft is placed **into** the defect, becoming part of the urethral lumen wall. **Onlay** = the urethrotomy is closed over a graft placed on the outside surface, augmenting the urethral wall thickness. The inlay approach allows shorter operating time because no urethral mobilization is required.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Outcomes — Mandal 2025 RCT

The first RCT comparing ventral inlay to [dorsal onlay BMG](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dorsal-onlay-urethroplasty) in women:<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

| Metric | Ventral inlay | Dorsal onlay |
|---|---|---|
| Stricture-free rate | 89–95% | 88–93% |
| Operating time | 30 min | 44.5 min |
| Blood loss | Less | More |
| Continence outcome | No de novo SUI | No de novo SUI |

The trial established **non-inferiority** of ventral inlay BMG with shorter operating time and less blood loss than dorsal onlay.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
