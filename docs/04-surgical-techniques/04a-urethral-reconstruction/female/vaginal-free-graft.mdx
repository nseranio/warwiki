# Vaginal Free Graft Urethroplasty (Female)

The **vaginal free graft urethroplasty**, anchored on the **Borchert 2022** series, harvests **anterior vaginal-wall mucosa as a free graft** rather than rotating it as a pedicled flap. It is reserved for cases where a pedicled flap is not technically feasible — for example, after prior vaginal surgery that compromised the lateral pedicle.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Indications

- Female urethral stricture in a patient with **prior vaginal surgery** preventing safe flap rotation
- Patients in whom oral and labial mucosa are exhausted, undesired, or contraindicated
- Adequate non-diseased anterior vaginal-wall mucosa for harvest

The technique is **limited by lichen sclerosus and vaginal atrophy**, which compromise donor-site integrity.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Outcomes

Reported success rates approximate **80%** in small series — generally inferior to BMG and labial mucosa, reflecting the more limited donor biology of vaginal mucosa.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
