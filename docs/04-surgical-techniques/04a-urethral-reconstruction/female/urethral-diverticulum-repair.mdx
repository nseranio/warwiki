# Urethral Diverticulum Repair

Urethral diverticulum repair excises the diverticular sac and closes the urethral communication through a multilayer transvaginal approach, typically using a Martius labial fat pad flap for interposition.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)[[3]](#ref3)</sup> The diverticulum arises most commonly from the mid-urethra and presents with post-void dribbling, dyspareunia, and recurrent infections, as systematically described in the early urologic literature.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> Meticulous closure of the urethral defect with tissue interposition minimizes the risk of fistula formation and recurrence.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
