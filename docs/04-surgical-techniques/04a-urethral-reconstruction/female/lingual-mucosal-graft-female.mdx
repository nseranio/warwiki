# Lingual Mucosal Graft (Female Urethroplasty)

The **lingual mucosal graft (LMG)** uses **ventral tongue mucosa** as a free oral-cavity graft when buccal sites have already been harvested or are otherwise unavailable. Histology and outcomes are equivalent to buccal mucosa for both [dorsal](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dorsal-onlay-urethroplasty) and ventral onlay female urethroplasty.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

For the canonical lingual-mucosa harvest article, see [Lingual Mucosa Graft](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/grafts/lingual-mucosa). For the buccal counterpart, see [Buccal Mucosa Graft](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/grafts/buccal-mucosa).

---

## Outcomes

Goel 2014 directly compared lingual and labial mucosa for female urethroplasty in a small series and found **equivalent outcomes** for both donor sites.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> Most series report success rates in the **85–93%** range, comparable to dorsal-onlay BMG.
