# Dorsal Vaginal Flap Urethroplasty (Female)

The **dorsal vaginal flap** approach places a vaginal-wall pedicled flap **dorsally (12 o'clock)** after dissecting the urethra off the pubic symphysis. It is the local-tissue counterpart to [dorsal onlay BMG](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dorsal-onlay-urethroplasty) for women in whom oral mucosa is not available, suitable, or preferred.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Concept

Dorsal placement avoids the retrusive-meatus problem of ventral approaches and capitalizes on the dorsal urethra's robust vascular bed. The vaginal-wall flap retains its subcutaneous pedicle, providing better take in scarred or previously irradiated beds than a free graft.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Outcomes

Reported short series have shown success rates comparable to dorsal-onlay BMG (~85–90%) in selected patients, although direct comparative data are limited and most modern centers prefer dorsal BMG as the default substitution graft.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
