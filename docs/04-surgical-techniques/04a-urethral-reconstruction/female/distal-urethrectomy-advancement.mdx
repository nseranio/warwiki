# Distal Urethrectomy with Advancement Meatoplasty (Female)

**Distal urethrectomy with advancement meatoplasty** is a single-stage repair for **short distal female urethral strictures** in which the strictured segment is **excised** and the proximal healthy mucosa is **advanced and sutured to the vestibular skin**. No graft or flap is required. The operation is conceptually analogous to a female "advancement meatoplasty."<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Indications

- Short distal / meatal female stricture (≤ ~1 cm) with viable proximal urethral mucosa
- Inadequate length to deflect the stream cosmetically after simple [Blandy U-flap](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-vaginal-flap-urethroplasty)
- Patient preference to avoid graft / flap morbidity

Not appropriate for proximal or panurethral disease (where dorsal or ventral onlay BMG is preferred).

---

## Technique Outline

1. Incision around the meatus with circumferential mobilization of the strictured distal segment
2. Excision of fibrotic tissue back to healthy mucosa
3. Spatulation of the proximal urethral edge
4. Advancement of healthy mucosa to the vestibular skin with absorbable interrupted sutures
5. 14 Fr Foley for 7–10 days

---

## Outcomes

Reported success rates fall in the **80–95% range** in small series for appropriately selected short distal disease.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> Direct comparison with dorsal-onlay BMG for distal disease has not been performed; the operation is best regarded as a **rapid, low-morbidity option** for the appropriate short stricture.
