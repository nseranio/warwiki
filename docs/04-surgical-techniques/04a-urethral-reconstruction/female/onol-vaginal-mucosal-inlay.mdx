# Anterior Vaginal Wall Mucosal Inlay (Önol)

The **Önol (2011) anterior vaginal wall mucosal inlay** technique opens the female urethral stricture ventrally and inlays a strip of native **anterior vaginal wall mucosa** into the urethrotomy. It uses local tissue with native blood supply and avoids both BMG harvest and ventral flap rotation.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Technique Outline

1. Suprameatal vaginal-wall incision; mobilization of the urethra off the symphysis
2. Ventral urethrotomy through the strictured segment
3. Vaginal-wall mucosa is incised, mobilized, and inlaid (rather than excised) into the opened urethra
4. Multilayer closure with vaginal-wall coverage of the inlay
5. Foley for 14–21 days

The mucosa is inset rather than transferred as a free graft, preserving its native vascular bed.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Outcomes

Önol's original series of 17 women reported **100% objective cure** (Qmax restoration, symptom resolution) at a median follow-up of 24 months in 10/17 long-followed patients, with no de novo SUI.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

The technique sits between local flap and free graft on the reconstructive ladder; head-to-head comparison with [dorsal-onlay BMG](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dorsal-onlay-urethroplasty) is awaited.
