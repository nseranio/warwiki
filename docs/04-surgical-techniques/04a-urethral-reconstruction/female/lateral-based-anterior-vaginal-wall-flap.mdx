# Lateral-Based Anterior Vaginal Wall Flap

The **lateral-based anterior vaginal wall flap**, described by **Romero-Maroto in 2018**, is an Orandi-inspired ventral flap for female urethroplasty. Unlike the U-shaped [Blandy vaginal flap](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-vaginal-flap-urethroplasty), the lateral-based design preserves the **orthotopic meatal position** and avoids the retrusive meatus and downward-deflected stream that limit the classic Blandy approach.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Concept

A laterally-based ventral vaginal-wall flap is raised on a subcutaneous pedicle and rotated medially to onlay the ventral urethrotomy. The meatus stays in its native position rather than being recessed proximally on the vaginal wall.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Outcomes

Romero-Maroto's original series reported **100% success at a mean follow-up of 80.7 months** in a small but durable cohort, with preservation of meatal position and stream direction.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> No comparative trial against dorsal-onlay BMG, Blandy, or [Önol mucosal inlay](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/onol-vaginal-mucosal-inlay) has been performed.
