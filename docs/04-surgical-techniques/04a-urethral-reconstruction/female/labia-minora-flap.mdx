# Labia Minora Pedicled Flap (Female Urethroplasty)

The **labia minora pedicled flap** uses **hairless, wet, elastic labial tissue** as a vascularized substitute when the vaginal wall is compromised by atrophy, lichen sclerosus, or prior failed surgery. The flap is raised on a labial dartos pedicle and rotated medially to onlay or tubularize a strictured female urethra.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

For the foundational labial-tissue anatomy and broader applications across reconstructive urology and urogynecology, see [Labia Majora Fasciocutaneous Flap](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps/labia-majora-fasciocutaneous) and [Labial Mucosa Graft](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/grafts/labial-mucosa).

---

## Indications

- Female urethral stricture with **compromised vaginal-wall donor tissue** (atrophy, scarring, prior surgery)
- Lichen sclerosus involving the vaginal wall but sparing the labia
- Patient preference to avoid oral harvest

---

## Outcomes

Small case series (≤ 10 patients) report success in selected complex cases.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup> Comparative data versus [dorsal-onlay BMG](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/female-dorsal-onlay-urethroplasty) and [Romero-Maroto lateral-based flap](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/lateral-based-anterior-vaginal-wall-flap) are awaited.
