# Bladder Wall Flap Urethroplasty (Patidar)

The **bladder wall flap urethroplasty**, anchored on the **Patidar 2021** 22-patient series, uses an **anterior or posterior bladder-wall flap** to reconstruct complex female urethral defects with **urethral loss or bladder-neck involvement** — scenarios beyond simple stricture in which onlay grafting alone cannot bridge the defect.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

For related bladder-flap principles in the upper tract and outlet, see [Boari Flap & Psoas Hitch](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/boari-flap-psoas-hitch) and the [bladder-flap foundations article](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps/bladder-flap).

---

## Concept

A flap of native bladder is mobilized and **tubularized or onlaid** to bridge urethral discontinuity, recreate the bladder neck, or augment the proximal urethra in patients with significant urethral loss (often after PFUI, severe obstetric trauma, or sling erosion with loss of substance). The flap is well vascularized and tolerates poor distal beds.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Outcomes

Patidar 2021 reported 22 patients undergoing bladder-flap urethroplasty for complex defects:<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Cohort | 22 |
| Indication mix | Urethral loss / BNC involvement |
| Continence rate | 82% |
| Stricture-free rate | High in short follow-up |
