# Staged BMG Urethroplasty (Female)

The **staged BMG urethroplasty** in women mirrors the male [Johanson two-stage](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/johanson-two-stage) approach for severe distal urethral disease with **complete loss or obliteration**: a buccal mucosal graft is **inlaid** at Stage 1 to create a urethral plate, and is **tubularized** at Stage 2 after maturation (typically 4–6 months later).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Indications

- Severe female urethral loss with no native plate (post-PFUI, obstetric trauma, sling erosion with substance loss)
- Failed prior single-stage repair
- Patient agreement to a two-operation pathway with a temporary perineal opening

---

## Outcomes

Reported in small heterogeneous series; success rates after the second stage are in the **75–90%** range, comparable to staged male BMG urethroplasty for analogous severity.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
