# Vaginal Wall Tubularization Urethroplasty (Female)

**Vaginal-wall tubularization** is a salvage technique for **complete urethral loss** in which a strip of anterior vaginal-wall mucosa is **tubularized over a catheter** to construct a neourethra. It is one of several reconstructive options for the female patient with no salvageable native urethra.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

For related options, see [Bladder Flap Urethroplasty (Tubularized)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/bladder-flap-tubularized), [Bladder Wall Flap Urethroplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/bladder-wall-flap-urethroplasty), and the staged-BMG path at [Staged BMG Urethroplasty (Female)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/staged-bmg-female).

---

## Outcomes

Outcome data are limited to small case series; the technique is most often used as **part of a staged complex reconstruction** rather than a stand-alone repair, and continence depends heavily on the integrity of the bladder neck.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
