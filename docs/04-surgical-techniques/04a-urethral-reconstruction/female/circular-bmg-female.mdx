# Circular (Circumferential) BMG Urethroplasty (Female)

The **circular (circumferential) BMG urethroplasty** is a **salvage technique** for severe distal female urethral strictures with **full circumferential disease** that cannot be repaired by an onlay or inlay alone. A circumferential strip of buccal mucosa is **tubularized** to reconstruct a complete distal urethral segment.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Indications

- Severe distal female stricture with full circumferential mucosal loss
- Failed prior onlay BMG urethroplasty
- Salvage in selected patients who decline urinary diversion

Data are limited to **case reports and small series**; the technique is not widely adopted because tubularized free grafts have higher contracture rates than onlay configurations.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
