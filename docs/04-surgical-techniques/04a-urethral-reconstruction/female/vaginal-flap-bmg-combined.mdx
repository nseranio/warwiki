# Vaginal Flap + BMG (Combined Female Urethroplasty)

The **vaginal flap + BMG** technique combines a **local pedicled vaginal-wall flap** with a **free buccal mucosal graft** for female reconstruction in scenarios where neither tissue alone is sufficient. The vaginal flap provides vascularized cover or augmentation on one half of the urethral circumference, and the BMG supplies additional luminal width on the opposite side.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Indications

- Long, complex female urethral strictures requiring more tissue than a single flap or graft can supply
- Patients in whom a pedicled flap is feasible only on one side of the urethra
- Salvage cases after failed onlay-only repair

Outcome data are limited to small case series; the operation is best regarded as a tailored solution for the unusual female reconstruction in which a single tissue source is inadequate.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
