# Bladder Flap Urethroplasty (Tubularized)

A **tubularized anterior or posterior bladder-wall flap** can be used to bridge complete urethral loss in scenarios where the **bladder neck is also involved** and a non-tubularized solution is not viable. The technique is closely related to — and shares biology with — the [Bladder Wall Flap Urethroplasty (Patidar)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04a-urethral-reconstruction/bladder-wall-flap-urethroplasty) onlay variant, but here the flap is fully tubularized to recreate a continent neourethra.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

For the upper-tract bladder-flap counterpart, see [Boari Flap & Psoas Hitch](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/boari-flap-psoas-hitch). For closely related concepts, see [Bladder-Flap Foundations](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps/bladder-flap).

---

## Indications

- Complete loss of distal urethra **and** involvement of bladder neck
- Failed prior reconstruction with no remaining native urethral substrate
- Salvage option before urinary diversion

Continence is the central challenge — most patients require a concomitant continence procedure (sling, AUS, bladder-neck reconstruction).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
