# Bladder Neck Reconstruction

Bladder neck reconstruction addresses contracture or obliteration of the bladder neck resulting from prior radical prostatectomy, radiation therapy, or trauma.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> Techniques range from endoscopic incision or dilation to open Y-V plasty and augmentation with buccal mucosal grafts or flaps to create a patent bladder outlet, including approaches adapted from posterior urethral disruption repair.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> Reconstruction in the irradiated field carries substantially higher complication rates and may require augmentation cystoplasty or urinary diversion in refractory cases.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
