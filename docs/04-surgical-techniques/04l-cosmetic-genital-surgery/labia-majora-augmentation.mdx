# Labia Majora Augmentation (Autologous Fat Grafting)

**Autologous fat grafting (lipofilling)** is the most commonly used and best-evidenced technique for **labia majora augmentation** — restoration of volume to atrophic, deflated, or hypoplastic labia majora using the patient's own adipose tissue. The **Jabbour 2017** *Aesthet Surg J* SR remains the largest synthesis (4 fat-grafting studies / 183 patients pooled), and modern series add the regenerative dimension of microfat / nanofat / SVF-enriched preparations.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)[[3]](#ref3)</sup> For positioning vs other female cosmetic options see [Cosmetic Genital Surgery — Female](/docs/surgical-techniques/04l-cosmetic-genital-surgery/female-cosmetic).

**Society positioning.** The **ACOG 2020 Committee Opinion No. 795** states that female genital cosmetic procedures are **not medically indicated** in patients without structural or functional abnormalities, that safety and effectiveness have not been established, and that patient-satisfaction reports should not serve as evidence of clinical effectiveness.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup> The **FIGO 2025 Statement** echoes this position. Mandatory **BDD screening** belongs at the front of every consultation. There are **no RCTs** comparing fat grafting to other augmentation methods for the labia majora; only 2 of 9 studies in the Jabbour SR were prospective.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Indications

- **Age-related volume loss / atrophy** — postmenopausal deflated, wrinkled labia majora.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
- **Genitourinary syndrome of menopause (GSM)** — vulvovaginal atrophy with dryness and dyspareunia. Microfat to the labia majora **plus** nanofat to the vaginal mucosa is the regenerative-medicine application of choice (Menkes 2021).<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
- **Correcting the labia minora-to-majora ratio** — augmenting the majora "masks" conservatively reduced minora and reduces the extent of labiaplasty needed (Hersant 2018 combined approach).<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>
- **Post-surgical / oncologic reconstruction** — after ablative surgery (e.g., for Bowen's disease) where flap reconstruction has not restored volume or relieved mucosal-exposure symptoms (Vogt 2011).<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup>
- **Gender-affirming surgery** — labial fat grafting after **penile inversion vaginoplasty** for flattened labia majora (the most common indication, 83% of cases per Patel 2021). Patients requiring fat grafting were more likely to have had introital stenosis (33% vs 6%, p = 0.007) and prolonged granulation tissue &gt; 6 weeks (83% vs 32%, p = 0.01).<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup>
- **Aesthetic dissatisfaction** — congenital hypoplasia or weight-loss-related deflation.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[8]](#ref8)</sup>

## Surgical technique

### Fat harvest

- **Donor sites**: abdomen, inner thighs, flanks, mons pubis. The **mons pubis donor** has dual benefit — simultaneous mons reduction provides graft material (Cihantimur 2021 "360" technique).<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
- Standard tumescent technique with small-bore liposuction cannulas.

### Fat processing

| Method | Description | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard centrifugation** | Harvested fat centrifuged → oil / aqueous fraction / purified fat. Most common method.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> | Volumetric augmentation |
| **Microfat** | Standard processed fat | Labia majora volume<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> |
| **Nanofat** | Mechanically emulsified and filtered → liquid rich in adipose-derived stem cells (ADSCs) and stromal vascular fraction (SVF) | Vaginal-mucosa **trophic / regenerative** effect (not volume)<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> |
| **MAFT (micro-autologous fat transplantation)** | Specialized gun delivers small fat parcels (~ 1/240 mL per injection) | Uniform distribution; improved graft survival<sup>[[9]](#ref9)</sup> |
| **SVF-enriched fat** | ADSCs + SVF concentrate (Cihantimur 360) | Higher regenerative-cell load<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> |

### Injection technique

- Blunt-tipped cannulas through small stab incisions.
- **Fan-shaped distribution** in multiple tissue planes (subcutaneous, intralabial) — maximizes graft-to-recipient contact.
- **Small aliquots per pass**; avoid large boluses (central necrosis).

### Reported volumes per session

| Series | Technique | Volume per session |
|---|---|---|
| Jabbour 2017 SR<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> | Various fat grafting | **18–120 mL** total |
| Menkes 2021<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> | Microfat + nanofat | Not separately specified |
| Lai 2023 (MAFT)<sup>[[9]](#ref9)</sup> | Micro-autologous fat transplantation | **20.8 mL vulva / mons + 21.9 mL vagina** |
| Cihantimur 2021 (360)<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> | SVF-enriched fat | Not specified per site |
| Hersant 2018<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup> | Standard fat grafting (combined with conservative labiaplasty) | Not specified |

## Outcomes

| Series | n | Population | Satisfaction / endpoint | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cihantimur 2021 (360)**<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> | 97 | Aesthetic | FGSIS 17.7 → 20.9 (6 mo, p &lt; 0.001) → 22.2 (12 mo, p = 0.013) | 12 mo |
| **Menkes 2021 (microfat / nanofat)**<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> | 50 | GSM (mean age 53) | VHI + FSD significantly improved; 80% normalized at 6 mo; **stable at 18 mo** | 18 mo |
| **Hersant 2018 (fat + conservative labiaplasty)**<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup> | 21 | Aesthetic | 81% satisfied / very satisfied; one minor wound separation (4.7%) | Not specified |
| **Lai 2023 (MAFT)**<sup>[[9]](#ref9)</sup> | 20 | Aesthetic / functional | FSFI 43.8 → 68.6 (p &lt; 0.001) | 6 mo |
| **Cihantimur 2013 (genital beautification)**<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup> | 124 | Combined procedures | 95.2% happy; 3.2% very happy | 12 mo |
| **Patel 2021 (post-PIV)**<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup> | 6 | Gender-affirming | Safe and effective; no complications | Variable |
| **Toplu 2021 (combined surgical / non-surgical)**<sup>[[10]](#ref10)</sup> | 37 | Aesthetic | 94.5% satisfied / very satisfied | 12 mo |

### Functional and histologic benefits

Beyond volume, fat grafting produces regenerative tissue effects.

- **Lai 2023 MAFT** — vulvovaginal fat grafting via the MAFT gun **significantly increased neocollagenesis, neoangiogenesis, and estrogen-receptor expression** in vaginal tissue, with decreased protein gene product 9.5 (associated with neuropathic pain). FSFI improved across all domains, with particular benefit for dyspareunia and lubrication.<sup>[[9]](#ref9)</sup>
- **Menkes 2021** — microfat / nanofat in GSM patients gave particular benefit for dryness and dyspareunia, with results stable through 18 months.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Complications

**No major or life-threatening complications** have been reported across published labia majora fat-grafting series.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> Minor complications:

- **Ecchymosis** of labia majora and clitoral hood (transient; resolved by POD 7).<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
- **Tenderness** in the pubic area (transient).<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
- **Pain at vaginal entrance points** (transient).<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
- **No infection, edema, lipoma, or granuloma formation** in the largest single series (Cihantimur 2021 n = 97).<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
- **No flap necrosis, infections, pain, or sexual dysfunction** in the Hersant 2018 combined-technique series; one small wound separation (4.7%) resolved spontaneously.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>
- **Wound dehiscence 8.1%** in the Cihantimur 2013 genital-beautification series (which included concurrent labiaplasty, not fat grafting in isolation).<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup>

## Fat-graft retention and durability

Volumetric retention in the labia majora has not been objectively quantified by MRI / ultrasound in any published series. Available signals:

- **Stable results at 18 months** in all Menkes 2021 GSM patients.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
- **Continued FGSIS improvement from 6 → 12 months** in Cihantimur 2021 — suggests ongoing tissue remodeling rather than progressive resorption.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
- General fat-grafting literature suggests **40–60% volume retention at 1 year** in other anatomic sites; the labia majora's rich vascularity may favor higher retention.<sup>[[11]](#ref11)</sup>
- **Repeat sessions** are uncommonly reported in the published series, but counseling should reflect the possibility.<sup>[[12]](#ref12)</sup>

## Comparison with alternative augmentation methods

| Method | Material | Per-session volume | Duration | Key advantages | Key limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Autologous fat grafting** | Patient's own fat | 18–120 mL | Long-lasting (stable 12–18 mo)<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup> | Autologous; regenerative (ADSCs); natural feel; no foreign-body reaction | Donor site; variable retention; possible overcorrection |
| **Hyaluronic acid (HA)** | HA filler 19–21 mg/mL | 2–6 mL | Temporary (~ 12 mo+)<sup>[[12]](#ref12)</sup> | Office procedure; no donor; reversible (hyaluronidase) | Temporary; repeat sessions; cost |
| **Calcium hydroxyapatite (CaHA)** | CaHA filler | Small volumes | Temporary<sup>[[13]](#ref13)</sup> | Biostimulatory; office | Limited data; temporary |
| **Dermal fat graft** | Autologous dermal-fat block | Surgical block | Stable long-term<sup>[[14]](#ref14)</sup> | Stable volume; no resorption | Surgical harvest; donor-site scar |

## Combined approaches

- **Fat grafting + conservative labiaplasty (Hersant 2018)** — augmenting the majora "masks" the minora, allowing more conservative labia minora reduction with fewer complications.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>
- **360 Vaginal Beautification (Cihantimur 2021)** — labia majora fat grafting + vaginal tightening using SVF-enriched fat.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
- **"Genital beautification" concept (Cihantimur 2013)** — labia minora reduction + labia majora fat augmentation + labial brightening by laser + mons-pubis liposuction ± vaginal tightening (95.2% satisfaction at 1 yr).<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup>
- **Surgical + non-surgical (Toplu 2021)** — labiaplasty + fat injection to mons / labia majora + fractional CO₂ laser (94.5% satisfaction).<sup>[[10]](#ref10)</sup>
- **Microfat + nanofat (Menkes 2021)** — microfat for labia majora volume; nanofat for vaginal regeneration; particularly effective for GSM.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Special population — gender-affirming surgery

**Patel 2021** *Aesthet Surg J* (n = 6) — labial fat grafting after **penile inversion vaginoplasty** is safe and effective for flattened labia majora, the most common indication (83% of cases). Patients requiring fat grafting were more likely to have prior **introital stenosis** (33% vs 6%, p = 0.007) and **prolonged granulation tissue &gt; 6 weeks** (83% vs 32%, p = 0.01) after initial vaginoplasty — increased scarring and contracture predict the need for subsequent fat grafting.<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup>

## Limitations

- ACOG 2020 — safety and effectiveness of labia majora augmentation **have not been well documented**; high satisfaction in non-validated reports does not establish clinical effectiveness.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>
- **No RCTs** comparing fat grafting to other augmentation methods.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
- Only **2 of 9 studies** in the Jabbour SR were prospective.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
- **No standardized approach or consensus guidelines** for labia majora augmentation.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
- **No volumetric retention measurements** by MRI / ultrasound in any published series.
- Patients should be counseled about possible repeat sessions and the unpredictability of fat-graft survival.
- BDD screening should occur before surgery per ACOG.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>

---

## Postoperative management

- **Activity restriction.** Avoid intercourse, tampon use, and strenuous exercise for **2–4 weeks**; protect the donor site per general lipofilling protocol.
- **Wound care.** Ice 48–72 h; loose-fitting clothing.
- **Compression / pressure** at the donor site; avoid pressure on the recipient site so as not to compress the graft.
- **Follow-up.** 1 week (donor + recipient sites), 6 weeks, 6 months, 12 months — with FGSIS / FSFI / VHI / FSD as relevant to indication.
- **Counseling on durability.** Counsel that fat resorption can occur; a second session may be needed for the desired volume.
