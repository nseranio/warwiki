# Hardrock Sandwich Technique (HST) — Bovine Pericardial Graft + Autologous Plasma Gel ± SLD ± Testicular Prosthesis

The **Hardrock Sandwich Technique (HST)** is a multicomponent penile-enlargement procedure that combines bovine pericardial grafts with autologous plasma gel for girth augmentation, together with suspensory-ligament division (SLD) and testicular-prosthesis placement for length and overall genital cosmesis. The only published series is a single-center retrospective study (Sertkaya 2025, n = 456 with ≥ 1-year follow-up). It reported girth +43.1% and length +20.8% on postoperative day 1 and no erectile or ejaculatory dysfunction.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

For the broader male cosmetic-genital-surgery decision framework see the [Male Cosmetic Genital Surgery atlas page](/docs/surgical-techniques/04l-cosmetic-genital-surgery/male-cosmetic). For the SLD-only deep-dive see [Suspensory Ligament Division](/docs/surgical-techniques/04l-cosmetic-genital-surgery/suspensory-ligament-division). For the only FDA-cleared cosmetic device see [Penuma & Himplant](/docs/surgical-techniques/04l-cosmetic-genital-surgery/penuma).

---

## Concept and Rationale

The authors designed the HST to avoid the resorption of fat grafts and HA fillers, the migration risk of injectable materials, and the infection or erosion risk of silicone implants. The name comes from the "sandwich" layering of biological materials around the penile shaft.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

- **Bovine pericardial grafts.** Biocompatible extracellular-matrix scaffold with satisfactory mid-term outcomes in Peyronie's-disease tunical-defect repair; longer follow-up is lacking.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup><sup>[[2]](#ref2)[[3]](#ref3)[[4]](#ref4)</sup>
- **Autologous plasma gel.** Patient-derived. It serves as a biological adhesive that holds the pericardial grafts in position and as a growth-factor matrix (PDGF, TGF-β, VEGF, EGF) proposed to promote integration and angiogenesis.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[5]](#ref5)</sup>
- The combination creates a layered construct (graft, plasma gel, graft), hence the "sandwich" configuration.

---

## Surgical Components

The HST is a single-session combined procedure addressing both girth and length simultaneously through four distinct components.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

### 1. Girth augmentation — bovine pericardial graft + autologous plasma gel

- The penile shaft is degloved to expose the subcutaneous plane between the dartos fascia and Buck's fascia.
- **Bovine pericardial grafts** are shaped and placed circumferentially around the penile shaft.
- **Autologous plasma gel** is prepared from centrifuged venous blood and processed to a gel-like consistency, then applied between and over the graft layers.
- The "sandwich" layering creates a composite biological augmentation intended to integrate with native tissue over time. A silicone implant (Penuma) remains as a permanent foreign body and HA filler resorbs.

### 2. Length augmentation — suspensory-ligament division

- Standard division of the suspensory ligament to allow anterior advancement of the intracorporeal penile shaft (see [SLD](/docs/surgical-techniques/04l-cosmetic-genital-surgery/suspensory-ligament-division) for the deep-dive on technique, anti-retraction principles, and complication profile).
- Accounts for the +20.78% length increase (+2.17 cm) reported in the Sertkaya cohort.

### 3. Testicular prosthesis placement

- Placement of testicular prostheses as part of the overall genital aesthetic enhancement.
- A unique component not typically included in other penile-augmentation techniques; the explicit rationale is comprehensive genital cosmesis (penile and scrotal in a single session).
- The published series does not specify whether all 456 patients received testicular prostheses or only a subset.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

### 4. Combined single-session approach

- All components performed in one operative session, eliminating the need for staged procedures.

## Materials — Bovine Pericardium

Bovine pericardium is used as a graft in urologic and reconstructive surgery, mainly for tunical defects in Peyronie's disease.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)[[3]](#ref3)[[4]](#ref4)[[6]](#ref6)[[7]](#ref7)</sup>

**Properties.**

- Dense collagen-based extracellular matrix with tensile strength.
- Biocompatible; serves as a scaffold for host-tissue ingrowth and remodeling.
- Available as commercially prepared, lyophilized, or glutaraldehyde-fixed products (Peri-Guard®, Hydrix®, Lyoplant®).
- Off-the-shelf availability eliminates donor-site morbidity.

**Established urologic applications.**

- **Peyronie's disease.** Repair of tunical defects after plaque incision or excision. In the Otero 2017 lyophilized-bovine-pericardium series (n = 43, 41 with follow-up, mean 14 months), complete straightening was achieved in 80.5% and 85% of patients were satisfied, with no rejection or infection. In the Choi 2021 retrospective series of H-shaped incision and bovine pericardium (n = 21), median erect curvature fell from 70° to 5° (p &lt; 0.001) at 1 year.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)[[4]](#ref4)</sup>
- **Corporoplasty with prosthesis.** Bovine pericardium (Hydrix®) used to cover tunical defects during penile-prosthesis implantation with good functional outcomes (Zucchi 2013).<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
- **Animal models.** In a rat tunical-substitution study (n = 100), cadaveric pericardial grafts allowed complete penile expansion and withstood intracorporal pressures. Fibrosis was moderate at 4 months and minimal at 6 months.<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup>

**Advantage for HST.** Unlike autologous grafts (saphenous vein, dermis, fascia lata), bovine pericardium requires no donor-site harvest, reducing operative time, morbidity, and complexity. In the rat model, Gore-Tex grafts caused moderate fibrosis at 6 months, whereas pericardium caused minimal fibrosis.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)[[7]](#ref7)</sup>

## Autologous Plasma Gel

Autologous plasma gel is related to, but distinct from, platelet-rich plasma (PRP).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[5]](#ref5)</sup>

- Prepared from the patient's own venous blood through centrifugation and processing to a gel consistency.
- Contains concentrated growth factors (PDGF, TGF-β, VEGF, EGF) that promote tissue healing, angiogenesis, and cellular proliferation.
- Functions as both a biological adhesive holding the pericardial grafts in position and a bioactive matrix promoting graft integration.
- Autologous nature minimizes immunogenic and foreign-body reactions.
- PRP-based therapies have been explored in penile rejuvenation for ED and Peyronie's disease. The first double-blinded randomized trial, in ED, reported evidence of efficacy.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>

## Efficacy Data

Sole published series: Sertkaya 2025 *Int J Impot Res*, n = 456, treated April 2022 – September 2024 at a single center.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

### Dimensional outcomes

| Timepoint | Girth change | Length change |
|---|---|---|
| Postoperative day 1 | **+43.13%** (+4.51 ± 2.17 cm, p &lt; 0.001) | **+20.78%** (+2.17 ± 2.53 cm, p &lt; 0.001) |
| 3 months | Measured; values not reported in the abstract | Measured; values not reported in the abstract |
| 12 months | Measured; values not reported in the abstract | Measured; values not reported in the abstract |

### Patient-reported outcomes at 1 year

Assessed using the APPSSI (Augmentation Phalloplasty Patient Selection and Satisfaction Inventory).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

- Satisfaction improvement: 83.4%
- Self-confidence improvement: 71.1%
- Self-esteem improvement: 75.3%

### Functional outcomes

- **No patients** experienced erectile dysfunction.
- **No patients** experienced ejaculatory dysfunction.

## Complications

Sertkaya 2025 cohort (n = 456).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

| Complication | Incidence | Management |
|---|---|---|
| Seroma | 6.12% (28/456) | Aspiration / conservative |
| Infection | 2.65% (12/456) | Antibiotic treatment |
| Wound dehiscence | 2.34% (11/456) | Wound care / revision |
| Hematoma | 1.40% (6/456) | Conservative / drainage |
| Skin necrosis (graft removal) | 0.22% (1/456) | Graft removal |
| Erectile dysfunction | 0% | — |
| Ejaculatory dysfunction | 0% | — |

The summed complication rate is approximately 12.7%, similar to the 14.6% pooled rate in the Vyas 2020 *PRS* systematic review of aesthetic augmentation phalloplasty (16 studies, 1,192 patients, poor methodologic quality). No sexual dysfunction was reported.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[8]](#ref8)</sup>

## Strengths

- The series is large for a single technique (n = 456, ≥ 1-year follow-up).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
- Girth and length are addressed in a single procedure.
- Only biological materials are used. No permanent synthetic implant remains in situ; the bovine pericardium acts as a scaffold for tissue remodeling and the plasma gel is autologous.
- No erectile or ejaculatory dysfunction was reported.
- The complication rate (approximately 12.7%) is similar to the 14.6% pooled rate in the Vyas 2020 systematic review, which found the highest complication rate with combined augmentation.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[8]](#ref8)</sup>
- Bovine pericardium has been used in Peyronie's-disease grafting with satisfactory mid-term results.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)[[3]](#ref3)[[4]](#ref4)[[6]](#ref6)</sup>

---

## Limitations and Concerns

- **Single-center, single-surgeon, retrospective study.** This design carries a high risk of bias. Results have not been independently replicated.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
- **Non-validated outcome instrument.** APPSSI has not been validated, a limitation shared with the Penuma literature.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[10]](#ref10)</sup>
- **Limited long-term follow-up.** Minimum follow-up was 1 year. The long-term fate of the bovine pericardial grafts in the subcutaneous penile space is unknown; pericardium integrates well in the tunical setting (Peyronie's disease), but its behavior in the subcutaneous space for cosmetic augmentation may differ.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
- **POD-1 girth measurement may overestimate.** The +43.1% girth gain was measured on POD 1, when surgical edema is maximal. The 3-month and 12-month values are not reported in the abstract, so graft resorption or contraction over time cannot be assessed.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
- **Testicular-prosthesis component is unexplained.** The rationale for including testicular-prosthesis placement as part of a penile-augmentation procedure is not clearly articulated. It is unclear whether all 456 patients received testicular prostheses or only a subset.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
- **No control arm, no comparator, no randomization.**<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
- **Graft-based augmentation has been associated with reduced penile length** in retrospective series (Ramazan 2026 narrative review). The HST series did not report this.<sup>[[11]](#ref11)</sup>
- **SMSNA 2024 position (expert opinion).** SMSNA recommends against graft-and-flap procedures for penile augmentation until further outcome data are available. Penile dysmorphic disorder must be ruled out before any invasive enhancement, using tools such as the Body Dysmorphic Disorder Symptom Scale or Questionnaire together with professional psychological assessment.<sup>[[9]](#ref9)</sup>
- **Reproducibility unknown.** The technique has not been described in sufficient surgical detail for independent replication; no other centers have reported outcomes.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Clinical Summary

The Hardrock Sandwich Technique is a biological approach to combined penile girth and length enhancement. It uses bovine pericardial grafts and autologous plasma gel as a "sandwich" construct, with suspensory-ligament division and testicular-prosthesis placement. The Sertkaya 2025 single-center series (n = 456) reported girth gain of 43.1% and length gain of 20.8% on POD 1, satisfaction improvement in 83.4% at 1 year, and no erectile or ejaculatory dysfunction. The evidence is a single retrospective series from one center, with a non-validated outcome instrument, POD-1 measurements that may include edema, and no long-term data on graft durability. SMSNA recommends against graft-and-flap augmentation until further outcome data are available.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[8]](#ref8)[[9]](#ref9)[[11]](#ref11)</sup>
