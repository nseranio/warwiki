# Tissue Transfer (GU Applications)

Tissue transfer is the cross-cutting toolkit that every reconstructive operation borrows from: free grafts, pedicled flaps, and free tissue transfer adapted to genitourinary defects. This atlas is a filterable index of flap and graft options by GU recipient site and indication; clicking an entry goes to the authoritative page in Foundations, where the donor anatomy, harvest, and general-surgical technique live as single-source-of-truth.

**How this section relates to Foundations.** The **named-technique articles** (gracilis, VRAM, radial forearm, buccal mucosa, STSG, etc.) live under [Foundations — Flaps in GU Reconstruction](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps-gu-reconstruction) and [Foundations — Grafts in GU Reconstruction](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/grafts-gu-reconstruction). This atlas page is a pointer-database organized by GU recipient site and indication so you can look up "what flap covers a radiated perineum" or "what graft replaces a panurethral stricture" without first deciding whether you want a flap or a graft.
