# Genital Reconstruction

Genital reconstruction in the reconstructive-urology / urogynecology atlas covers operations that restore form and function of the **external genitalia after trauma, lichen sclerosus, oncologic resection, FGM/C, congenital anomaly, or progressive obesity-driven (adult-acquired buried penis)** disease — distinct from the gender-affirming pathway, which lives at [04k Gender-Affirming Surgery](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery). The operative decision space splits into three anatomic cohorts that drive technique selection: **penile** (shaft skin, AABP, glans, cancer-related), **scrotal** (Fournier's, lymphedema, oncologic, post-orchiectomy), and **vulvar** (vulvar cancer, EMPD, VIN, FGM/C, lichen sclerosus, GSM-related fat-grafting).

**Peyronie's correction** lives at [04j Peyronie's Disease](/docs/surgical-techniques/04j-sexual-dysfunction/peyronies-disease); **gender-affirming genital surgery** lives at [04k](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery); **penile augmentation** (PDD / SPS) lives in the [Small Penis Syndrome](/docs/clinical-conditions/03g-genital-scrotal/small-penis-syndrome) clinical-conditions article.
