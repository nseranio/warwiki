# Genital Reconstruction

This atlas covers operations that restore the external genitalia after trauma, infection, inflammatory disease, oncologic resection, FGM/C or congenital differences, including adult-acquired buried penis. Explore the penile, scrotal and vulvar sections according to the defect and functional goals. Gender-affirming pathways have their own [dedicated section](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery).

Peyronie's correction lives at [04j Peyronie's Disease](/docs/surgical-techniques/04j-sexual-dysfunction/peyronies-disease); gender-affirming genital surgery lives at [04k](/docs/surgical-techniques/04k-gender-affirming-surgery); penile augmentation (PDD and SPS) lives in the [Small Penis Syndrome](/docs/clinical-conditions/03g-genital-scrotal/small-penis-syndrome) clinical-conditions article.
