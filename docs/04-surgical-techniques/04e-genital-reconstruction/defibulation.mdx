# Defibulation (Deinfibulation) for Type III FGM/C

**Defibulation** is the surgical opening of the infibulated scar in **Type III FGM/C** to expose the urethral meatus and vaginal introitus. It is the **most common FGM/C operation worldwide** and is recommended by AAP 2020 for **all girls and women with Type III FGM/C regardless of current symptoms**.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> For the disease context, see [Female Genital Mutilation / Cutting (FGM/C)](/docs/clinical-conditions/03g-genital-scrotal/female-genital-mutilation-cutting); for the broader reconstructive menu see the [Vulvar Reconstruction database](/docs/surgical-techniques/04e-genital-reconstruction/vulvar).

---

## Indications

- **All patients with Type III FGM/C** — irrespective of current symptoms (AAP 2020).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
- Symptomatic Type III: dyspareunia / apareunia, recurrent UTI, urinary obstruction, hematocolpos, dysmenorrhea.
- **Antenatal**: planned ahead of labor — markedly reduces emergency cesarean and genital-tract trauma vs intrapartum or post-partum defibulation.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
- Pre-conception, gynecologic exam access, sexual function.

---

## Timing

| Context | Preferred timing |
|---|---|
| **Non-pregnant** | Elective, any time |
| **Pregnant** | **Second trimester** (up to ~ 34 wk) — allows healing before labor<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> |
| **Intrapartum** | Possible if not done antenatally, but increases urgent-CD risk |
| **Postpartum** | Suboptimal — risk of re-infibulation by family / patient if not addressed earlier |

---

## Anesthesia

- **Regional or general anesthesia preferred.**
- **Avoid local anesthesia alone** — risk of flashback / re-traumatization given the psychological context of the original procedure.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
- Trauma-informed consent and chaperoning are standard.

---

## Operative Technique

1. Lithotomy position; thorough exam to identify the meatus and the inferior extent of the scar.
2. **Midline incision** along the fused tissue, advanced cranially in stages — **stop short of the clitoris** if buried beneath the scar; identify the residual glans before deciding on clitoral re-exposure.
3. The cut edges are everted and oversewn with fine absorbable suture, creating two labial-like edges and a patent introitus.
4. Confirm urethral and vaginal access prior to closure.
5. Send any excised tissue for histopathology if clinically indicated.

Duration is typically &lt; 30 min; day-case if non-pregnant.

---

## Outcomes — Okusanya 2026 Meta-Analysis

Cochrane-format systematic review and meta-analysis of deinfibulation vs no deinfibulation in Type III FGM/C, very-low-certainty evidence overall:<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

| Outcome | Effect (vs non-deinfibulated Type III) |
|---|---|
| Emergency cesarean delivery | **OR 0.16** |
| Genital-tract lacerations | **OR 0.48** |
| Patient acceptability | 94% would recommend to others; 100% pleased with results in one cohort<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> |

---

## Complications

Generally minimal in skilled hands:

- Hematoma, urinary retention (transient), wound dehiscence.
- **Re-infibulation**: a patient or family may request re-closure post-delivery — providers must **never perform any form of re-infibulation** (AAP 2020).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
- Psychological response to anatomic change — multidisciplinary support recommended.

---

## Positioning vs Reconstructive Procedures

Defibulation **opens the infibulation** but does not restore tissue lost in Types I / II / III. Concurrent or staged reconstruction is decided after defibulation:

| Goal | Procedure | Cross-link |
|---|---|---|
| Open Type III introital fusion | **Defibulation** (this page) | — |
| Restore the glans clitoridis from scar | Foldès reconstruction | [Foldès](/docs/surgical-techniques/04e-genital-reconstruction/foldes-clitoral-reconstruction) |
| Restore labia minora / vestibule | aOAP flap ± OD preputial flap | [aOAP](/docs/surgical-techniques/04e-genital-reconstruction/aoap-flap) |
| Alternative non-Foldès clitoral coverage | Mañero vaginal-mucosal graft | [Mañero](/docs/surgical-techniques/04e-genital-reconstruction/manero-vaginal-graft) |
| Vulvar scarring / dyspareunia | FGM/C fat grafting | [FGM/C Fat Grafting](/docs/surgical-techniques/04e-genital-reconstruction/fgm-fat-grafting) |
