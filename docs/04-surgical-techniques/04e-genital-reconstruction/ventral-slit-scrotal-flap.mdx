# Ventral Slit Scrotal Flap (VSSF — Westerman / Tausch)

The ventral slit scrotal flap is a named local-flap approach reported for adult acquired buried penis reconstruction.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> The available bibliographic record does not support the inherited operative protocol, selection framework, outpatient claim, complication rate, recurrence analysis, or comparative ranking. This page therefore preserves the technique as a specialist reconstructive reference without presenting it as a standardized pathway.

For integrated planning, see [Buried Penis Repair](/docs/surgical-techniques/04e-genital-reconstruction/buried-penis-repair). Related pages include [Escutcheonectomy](/docs/surgical-techniques/04e-genital-reconstruction/escutcheonectomy), [Penile Skin Grafting](/docs/surgical-techniques/04e-genital-reconstruction/penile-skin-grafting), and [Penile Reconstruction](/docs/surgical-techniques/04e-genital-reconstruction/penile).

## Evidence boundary

- The full Westerman/Tausch source package was not recovered in this audit.
- The available record does not establish a specific skin-viability threshold, flap design, operative sequence, staging algorithm, patient-selection rule, expected complication rate, recurrence probability, or functional outcome.
- Adult acquired buried penis has heterogeneous causes and anatomy; a named local-flap report cannot determine treatment for a different presentation.
- Selection of local tissue transfer, grafting, escutcheon/pannus treatment, or other reconstruction requires individualized specialist assessment of tissue quality, scar/inflammatory disease, body habitus, infection/lymphedema, urethral involvement, patient goals, and available expertise.
