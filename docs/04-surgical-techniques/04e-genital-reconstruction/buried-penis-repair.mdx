# Buried Penis Repair

Adult-acquired buried penis repair is a staged genital reconstruction that restores a visible, lined, catheterizable shaft by removing the overlying pannus and escutcheon, grafting the denuded shaft, and reconfiguring the scrotum. Weight optimization, lichen sclerosus control, and stricture management are built into the pathway because they drive both recurrence and failed graft take.
