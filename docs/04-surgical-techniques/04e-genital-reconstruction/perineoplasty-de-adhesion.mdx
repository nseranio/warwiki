# Perineoplasty + De-Adhesion for Vulvar Lichen Sclerosus

**Perineoplasty with de-adhesion** is the principal surgical option for **vulvar lichen sclerosus (LS) sequelae** — introital stenosis, labial / periclitoral adhesions, clitoral phimosis — when ultrapotent topical corticosteroids fail to restore satisfactory function. It is adjunctive, not curative: LS is chronic and relapsing, and topical clobetasol must be continued postoperatively.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup> For broader LS context see [Lichen Sclerosus](/docs/clinical-conditions/03g-genital-scrotal/lichen-sclerosus); for the surgical algorithm placement see the [Vulvar Reconstruction database](/docs/surgical-techniques/04e-genital-reconstruction/vulvar).

---

## Indications

Surgery is reserved for **established architectural sequelae of LS** refractory to maximum-potency topical therapy:<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[3]](#ref3)[[4]](#ref4)</sup>

- **Refractory dyspareunia or apareunia** from introital stenosis despite adequate clobetasol
- **Clitoral phimosis** with pain or sexual dysfunction
- **Labial / periclitoral adhesions** restricting introital access or burying the meatus
- **Complete introital obliteration** causing urinary retention — rare but reported<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>
- Patient-stated desire to resume sexual activity is a core selection criterion<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

Maximally treat LS topically **before** surgery; surgery does not replace medical therapy.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

---

## Procedures

Several related techniques are used individually or in combination:

| Procedure | Description | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Posterior vestibuloplasty (perineoplasty)** | Excision of scarred posterior fourchette / perineal tissue + advancement of vaginal mucosa to widen the introitus | The primary operation for introital stenosis<sup>[[3]](#ref3)[[6]](#ref6)</sup> |
| **De-adhesion / lysis of adhesions** | Division of labial and periclitoral adhesions, by scalpel or CO₂ laser | Commonly combined with perineoplasty<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup> |
| **Clitoral de-hooding** | Excision of scarred prepuce overlying the glans clitoridis | Relieves clitoral phimosis<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup> |
| **Anterior vestibuloplasty with grafting** | Free full-thickness vaginal mucosal graft to anterior vestibule | Novel; reserve for selected anterior-vestibule loss<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup> |
| **Fenton's procedure (median perineotomy)** | Incision-based introital widening | One of the most commonly used simple approaches, often paired with laser adhesiolysis<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup> |
| **Local skin-flap advancement** | For more extensive sequelae | Rangatchew long-term series uses this principally<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup> |

Operative time can be brief — Frigerio reported a **~25-minute** total-introital-obliteration repair with negligible blood loss; most cases are day-case under regional or general anesthesia.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>

## Outcomes

| Study | n | Procedure | Dyspareunia / sexual outcome | Satisfaction | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Rouzier 2002**<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> | 64 (50 evaluated) | Perineoplasty | 90% improved | 86% improved sexual quality | Variable |
| **Gurumurthy 2012**<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup> | 25 | Fenton's / laser adhesiolysis | 80% symptom improvement | — | 10 yr |
| **Burger 2016**<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup> | 23 | Posterior vestibuloplasty ± de-hooding ± grafting | Complications-focused report | — | 2008–2012 |
| **Rangatchew 2017**<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup> | 38 | Local skin flaps | 85% short-term improvement; **75% long-term benefit**; **38% severe LS relapse with apareunia** | 74% satisfied / very satisfied | Mean 8.4 yr |
| **Lauber 2021**<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> | 41 | Perineoplasty + de-adhesion | Significant complaint reduction (p = 0.02) | 90% satisfied or very satisfied; only 2 of 41 would not recommend | Median 2.3 yr |

Across series, **short-term dyspareunia improvement runs 80–90%** with high patient satisfaction. **No baseline factor** in the Lauber cohort (age, LS duration, prior steroid exposure, prior perineotomy, histologic stage) predicted surgical failure.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[3]](#ref3)[[7]](#ref7)</sup>

## Complications

Generally safe but specific issues include:<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup>

- **Wound dehiscence** / infection of advanced vaginal epithelium
- **LS reactivation with bullae** — particularly if topical steroids are stopped postoperatively; **continue clobetasol** indefinitely
- **Localized pain** (~13% in one series; causality uncertain)
- **Graft contraction / keratinization** when anterior vestibuloplasty with mucosal graft is performed

---

## Long-Term Recurrence — the Defining Caveat

Surgery does not modify LS biology. In the Rangatchew long-term cohort (mean 8.4 yr), recurrent dyspareunia was driven by **LS relapse**: 50% minor relapse (coitus preserved) and **38% severe relapse with apareunia**.<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup> Counsel patients explicitly on the relapse trajectory before surgery; relapse is high among sexually active patients even after technically successful repair.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Perioperative Principles

- **Preop**: maximize topical-steroid disease control; document LS extent; consent for relapse risk.
- **Histology**: send excised tissue — exclude VIN and invasive vulvar SCC (4–7% lifetime SCC risk in LS).<sup>[[2]](#ref2)[[5]](#ref5)</sup>
- **Postop topical steroids**: continue indefinitely; discontinuation is associated with bullous LS reactivation.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup>
- **Vaginal dilators**: consider postoperatively to maintain introital patency (analogous to lichen-planus protocols).<sup>[[9]](#ref9)</sup>
- **Indefinite follow-up** for symptom control and SCC surveillance.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Positioning in the LS Surgical Ladder

| Severity | Preferred approach |
|---|---|
| Refractory introital stenosis / focal adhesions | **Perineoplasty + de-adhesion** (this page) |
| Extensive sequelae with sexual dysfunction unresponsive to conservative care | [Skinning vulvectomy + aOAP flap (O'Dey)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04e-genital-reconstruction/aoap-flap) |
| Complementary regenerative option | [Microfat / nanofat ± PRP / SVF](/docs/surgical-techniques/04l-cosmetic-genital-surgery/microfat-nanofat-grafting) (see VLS section) |
| Clitoral burying without broader sequelae | Clitoral de-hooding (component of this page) ± [Foldès reconstruction](/docs/surgical-techniques/04e-genital-reconstruction/foldes-clitoral-reconstruction) for FGM/C-pattern scar |
