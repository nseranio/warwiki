# Bladder Neck Reconstruction

**Bladder neck reconstruction** addresses the bladder neck and vesicourethral anastomosis. It covers salvage of bladder neck contracture (BNC) after benign prostate surgery or radiotherapy with the prostate in situ, and vesicourethral anastomotic stenosis (VUAS) after radical prostatectomy with or without radiotherapy<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>, and selected bladder-neck closure for refractory stress incontinence in neurogenic lower urinary tract dysfunction, or for a devastated outlet, with dependable alternative drainage.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> Two cohorts are distinguished: BNC after endoscopic prostate surgery, and VUAS after radical prostatectomy with or without radiation. They differ in their relationship to the remaining sphincter, radiation exposure and available reconstruction. Treatment is selected by anatomy, bladder function, continence and patient goals. For reservoir-side reconstruction (augmentation cystoplasty, catheterizable channels, ileovesicostomy), see [Bladder Augmentation & Catheterizable Channels](/docs/surgical-techniques/04b-bladder-reconstruction).
