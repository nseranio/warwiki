# Urinary Diversion

Urinary diversion encompasses both incontinent and continent reconstructive options following cystectomy, bladder dysfunction, or irreparable outlet pathology. Selection depends on patient fitness, prior radiation, bowel history, manual dexterity, renal function, and patient preference regarding body image and catheterization. Incontinent diversions (ileal conduit) carry lower reoperation rates but require external appliance wear; continent diversions (orthotopic neobladder, catheterizable pouch) restore voiding mechanics or body-image normalcy at the cost of complexity and long-term complication risk.
