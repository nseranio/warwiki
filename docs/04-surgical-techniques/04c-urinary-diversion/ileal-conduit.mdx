# Ileal Conduit

The ileal conduit is the gold-standard incontinent urinary diversion, using a short (15–20 cm) isoperistaltic ileal segment to convey urine from the ureters to a right-lower-quadrant cutaneous stoma. It carries the lowest operative complexity and long-term reoperation rate among urinary diversions and is broadly applicable regardless of patient age, renal function, or prior radiation. Requires a permanent external urostomy appliance.
