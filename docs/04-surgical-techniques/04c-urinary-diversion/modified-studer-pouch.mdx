# Modified Studer Pouch

The Studer orthotopic ileal neobladder uses a U-shaped detubularized reservoir with a proximal isoperistaltic afferent limb where the ureters are anastomosed, reducing the risk of reflux and ascending pyelonephritis. The afferent limb also simplifies ureteroileal anastomosis and may reduce long-term upper tract deterioration compared to direct-anastomosis neobladder designs. One of the most frequently performed orthotopic diversions in Europe and increasingly worldwide.
