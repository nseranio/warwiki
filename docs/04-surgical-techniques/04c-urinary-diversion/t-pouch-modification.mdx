# T-Pouch Modification

A modification of the orthotopic ileal neobladder design incorporating a T-limb configuration to optimize reservoir geometry, reduce anastomotic tension, and minimize afferent-limb reflux at the ureteroileal anastomosis. A variant within the Studer/Hautmann orthotopic family with specific advantages in cases of short mesentery or asymmetric ureteral length.
