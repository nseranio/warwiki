# Colostomy Shuffle

A complex reconstructive maneuver involving the repositioning or rerouting of a colostomy in the context of combined urinary and fecal diversion, typically in pelvic exenteration or multi-organ reconstruction. Addresses the competing stomal site requirements of simultaneous urinary and colonic diversion following radical pelvic surgery.
