# Cutaneous Ureterostomy

Cutaneous ureterostomy is the simplest urinary diversion, bringing one or both ureters directly to the skin without interposed bowel. Its main advantage is avoidance of bowel resection and the shortest operative time, making it especially useful in elderly, frail, or highly comorbid patients who are poor candidates for more complex intestinal diversion.
