# Vacuum Erection Device (VED)

A vacuum erection device creates negative pressure around the penis, drawing blood into the corpora cavernosa to produce an erection, which is then maintained by placing a constriction ring at the penile base. VEDs are effective in 60–90% of patients, carry no systemic side effects, and are a key component of penile rehabilitation protocols after radical prostatectomy.
