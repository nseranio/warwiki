# Penile Traction Therapy (PTT)

**Penile traction therapy (PTT)** is a non-invasive mechanical treatment that applies controlled longitudinal force to the penis. It has been studied mainly in Peyronie's disease (curvature improvement, length preservation, and as an adjunct to intralesional therapy). Other uses are post-radical-prostatectomy penile rehabilitation, pre- and post-penile-prosthesis length optimization, and cosmetic penile lengthening (penile dysmorphophobia).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

---

## Mechanism of action

PTT is proposed to act through **mechanotransduction**, the cellular process by which mechanical stimuli are translated into biochemical responses. Proposed effects of continuous longitudinal traction include:

- **Increased matrix metalloproteinase (MMP) activity** within treated tissue, driving collagen degradation and scar remodeling.
- **Re-orientation of collagen fibrils** along the direction of applied force.
- In a rat model of Peyronie's disease, traction and vacuum devices both reduced curvature compared with controls (15.3° and 28.4° vs 38.6°). The vacuum group had more preserved smooth-muscle actin and less TGF-β1 and SMAD 2/3 staining, and the authors propose antifibrotic, antiapoptotic and smooth-muscle-preserving mechanisms.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>

## Devices

| Device | Mechanism | Daily duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RestoreX** (PathRight Medical) | Rod-based traction with counter-bending | **30–90 min** | Shortest use time; counter-bend feature for curvature; developed with Mayo Clinic; has randomized trials in PD and after radical prostatectomy.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup> |
| **Penimaster PRO** (MSP Concept) | Vacuum-adhesion + traction | 3–8 hours | Dose-dependent response in a randomized trial in stable PD; vacuum grip avoids strap compression.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup> |
| **Andropenis / Andropeyronie** (Andromedical) | Strap-based traction | 4–6 hours | Studied for cosmetic lengthening and as an adjunct to collagenase.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup> |
| **Golden Erect** (Ronas Tajhiz Teb) | Strap-based traction | 4–9 hours | Limited data.<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup> |

Randomized data in Peyronie's disease exist for RestoreX and Penimaster PRO.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)[[9]](#ref9)</sup>

## Clinical indications and evidence

### Peyronie's disease — strongest evidence base

Guideline endorsement of PTT in Peyronie's disease varies by society. The Cochrane review rated the certainty of the traction evidence as very low.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> PTT can be used as monotherapy or in combination with intralesional injections (CCH, IFN α-2b, verapamil) or surgery.<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup>

| Trial | Device / Protocol | Key outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Ziegelmann 2019 *J Urol* RCT** (n=110, Mayo; 3:1 randomization to PTT or no therapy; primary outcome safety)<sup>[[9]](#ref9)</sup> | RestoreX 30–90 min/day × 3 mo | At 3 mo vs controls: **+1.5 cm vs 0 cm length (p &lt; 0.001), −11.7° vs +1.3° curvature (p &lt; 0.01)**; IIEF-EF improved in men with ED (+4.3 vs −0.7, p = 0.01) |
| **Joseph 2020 RestoreX open-label and follow-up phases**<sup>[[10]](#ref10)</sup> | Open-label use after the randomized phase (mean use 31 min/day; n = 64 at 6 mo) | **95% of men treated for 6 mo had length gains and 61% had curvature improvements**; no controlled comparison after 3 months |
| **Moncada 2019 *BJU Int* Penimaster PRO RCT** (n=93, stable PD)<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup> | 3–8 h/day × 12 wk | Overall −31.2° curvature (41.1%) and +1.8 cm stretched length vs no change in controls. Dose-dependent: &gt; 6 h/d → **−38.4° (51.4%)**; &lt; 4 h/d → −19.7° (28.8%) |
| **Cochrane 2023 (Rosenberg)**<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> | SR | One traction trial (about 90 men); curvature MD −7.4° with **very low certainty**; no evidence on ability to have intercourse |

### Post-radical-prostatectomy penile rehabilitation

Radical prostatectomy commonly causes penile shortening, ED, and corporal fibrosis.<sup>[[11]](#ref11)</sup>

- **Toussi 2021 *J Urol* RestoreX RCT** (n = 82 randomized to control or one of two PTT protocols; 6-month data in 25 controls and 30 PTT men): stretched length +1.6 cm vs +0.3 cm in controls (p &lt; 0.01), IIEF-EF +0 vs −6.5 (p = 0.03), and less intracavernosal injection use (19% vs 50%).<sup>[[12]](#ref12)</sup>

### Pre- and post-penile-prosthesis length optimization

PTT has been used to optimize penile length before IPP placement, particularly in men with shortening from prior surgery, explantation, or PD.<sup>[[13]](#ref13)[[14]](#ref14)</sup>

- **Levine 2011 pilot** (n = 10, 2–4 h/day × 2–4 mo before IPP): no measured or perceived length loss after implant; 70% had a measured erect length gain of up to 1.5 cm over baseline stretched length.<sup>[[14]](#ref14)</sup>
- **Moskovic 2011 case report**: daily traction for 8 months in a patient with an existing prosthesis increased stretched penile length by 2.3 cm at 6 months, enabling revision with a 20% longer device (15 cm to 18 cm).<sup>[[15]](#ref15)</sup>
- Evidence in this indication is limited to a small pilot and a case report.

### Cosmetic / aesthetic penile lengthening (penile dysmorphophobia)

Most men seeking penile enhancement have normal dimensions and either have penile dysmorphophobia or are misinformed about normal size.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup>

- **AndroPenis (Nowroozi 2015)**: n = 54 (of 163 men counseled) with penile dysmorphophobia, uncontrolled, 4–6 h/day × 6 mo, mean gains at 6 mo: +1.7 cm flaccid, +1.3 cm stretched, +1.2 cm erect (all p &lt; 0.001); no effect on girth was observed.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup>
- **Golden Erect (Nikoobakht 2011)**: n = 23 (mean age 26.5 years), uncontrolled, 4–6 h/day for 2 weeks and then 9 h/day to 3 months: flaccid length 8.8 to 10.5 cm and stretched length 11.5 to 13.2 cm (p &lt; 0.05); proximal girth did not change, and glans circumference increased.<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup>
- **Oderda and Gontero 2011 review**: penile extenders were the only non-invasive lengthening method with some scientific support, and the authors judged their results not inferior to surgery.<sup>[[16]](#ref16)</sup>
- **Romero-Otero 2021 SR (BJU Int)**: non-invasive interventions, including traction therapies, were safe and mostly efficacious in 57 studies, but study quality was low and the authors could not make evidence-based recommendations for aesthetic or therapeutic indications.<sup>[[17]](#ref17)</sup>
- Structured psychosexual counseling should precede any intervention in men with cosmetic concerns; many can be reassured without treatment.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup> See [Small Penis Syndrome / PDD](/docs/clinical-conditions/03g-genital-scrotal/small-penis-syndrome) and [Cosmetic Genital Surgery](/docs/surgical-techniques/04l-cosmetic-genital-surgery).

## Combination therapy

PTT can be combined with intralesional injections or as adjunct to surgical correction of PD.<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup>

### CCH + RestoreX

The **Alom 2019 *J Sex Med*** three-arm comparison is a nonrandomized, single-site analysis of a prospective CCH registry (113 men completing therapy):<sup>[[19]](#ref19)</sup>

| Outcome | CCH alone (n=38) | CCH + other PTT (n=32) | **CCH + RestoreX (n=43)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Curvature improvement | 20.3° (31%) | 19.2° (30%) | **33.8° (49%)** |
| Length change | −0.7 cm (−4%) | −0.4 cm (−2%) | **+1.9 cm (+17%)** |
| Subjective curvature improvement | 44% | 32% | **63%** |
| Daily PTT use | — | 1.9 h | **0.9 h** |
| ≥ 20° curvature improvement | reference | NS vs CCH alone | **OR 6.9** |
| ≥ 50% curvature improvement | reference | NS vs CCH alone | **OR 3.5** |
| ≥ 20% length improvement | reference | NS vs CCH alone | **OR 10.7** |

**Results.** The authors report that CCH with RestoreX enhanced curvature outcomes by 71% and converted length loss to length gain versus CCH alone. CCH with other PTT devices showed no benefit over CCH alone, including in the subset using other devices ≥ 3 h/day. RestoreX was used less (0.9 versus 1.9 h/day), and adverse events were similar across arms.<sup>[[19]](#ref19)</sup> In a later single-center prospective database of 826 men receiving CCH, RestoreX use was associated with the largest curvature improvement of any factor assessed (5–20° depending on the test).<sup>[[20]](#ref20)</sup>

In a 2017 prospective database of 51 men completing CCH, 69% used the Andropenis device (mean 9.8 h/week) and curvature improved a mean of 20.9° overall. Curvature improvement did not differ by frequency or duration of PTT, and stretched length changed by +0.4 cm with PTT and −0.35 cm without (p = 0.21).<sup>[[21]](#ref21)</sup> Whether the difference from the RestoreX registry reflects device design, dose or patient selection is not established.

### CCH + PTT (IMPRESS-extension protocol — García-Gómez 2021)

Prospective multicenter single-arm study (n = 87) using the IMPRESS protocol plus a penile extender ≥ 4 h/day starting 24–48 h after injection: mean curvature fell from 57° to 34° (−23.3°, −41%) with a mean of 2 cycles. Without a control arm, the authors suggest that PTT may limit the number of cycles needed.<sup>[[22]](#ref22)</sup>

### CCH + IFN α-2b combinations

- **Yafi 2015 *J Urol***: in a retrospective review of 112 men receiving interferon α-2b, PTT use overall did not change curvature or stretched length. Men using PTT ≥ 3 h/day gained more stretched length (+4.4 mm versus +1.3 mm, p = 0.04) without a curvature effect.<sup>[[18]](#ref18)</sup>

### Emerging "3 Ts" multimodal protocol (Hatzichristou 2026)

The 3Ts protocol is a proposed multimodal framework: daily **Tadalafil** (proposed to improve cavernosal oxygenation and counteract hypoxia-driven fibrosis), **Tunneling** with intralesional biologics (delivers growth factors directly into or around the plaque), and daily **Traction** (mechanotransductive realignment). The paper is a protocol description, and prospective randomized studies are needed.<sup>[[23]](#ref23)</sup>

## Treatment protocols

No universally standardized protocol exists.<sup>[[11]](#ref11)[[8]](#ref8)</sup> General principles:

- **Duration:** ranges from 30 min/day (RestoreX) to 3–8+ hours/day (traditional devices). In the Penimaster PRO trial, longer daily use was associated with greater curvature improvement, and in the interferon series ≥ 3 h/day was associated with greater length gain.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)[[18]](#ref18)</sup>
- **Treatment course:** typically 3–6 months minimum; continued use beyond 3 months yields additional length gains.<sup>[[10]](#ref10)</sup>
- **Force:** progressive increase per device-specific instructions.

## Safety

PTT has been reported as safe and well tolerated across indications in the published studies.<sup>[[9]](#ref9)[[8]](#ref8)[[5]](#ref5)[[6]](#ref6)</sup>

- Adverse events are transient and mild (erythema, local discomfort, skin irritation, temporary glans numbness), typically resolving within minutes of device removal.<sup>[[10]](#ref10)</sup>
- **Serious adverse events** (penile fracture, permanent numbness, vascular compromise) were not reported in the trials cited above.
- RestoreX post-prostatectomy trial: 87% would choose to repeat therapy and 93% would recommend it.<sup>[[12]](#ref12)</sup>
- Mild AEs in about 43% of Penimaster PRO users (discomfort, glans numbness).<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>

---

## Limitations

- **Compliance** limits efficacy; daily use for extended periods is demanding, and adherence is highly variable.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[11]](#ref11)</sup>
- **No standardized protocols** across devices and indications.<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup>
- **Penile girth** did not increase in the cosmetic-lengthening series (glans circumference did in one).<sup>[[6]](#ref6)[[7]](#ref7)</sup>
- **Limited long-term data.** Most studies report at 3–9 months, and durability beyond that is uncertain.<sup>[[10]](#ref10)</sup>
- Overall quality of evidence is low, with small samples and heterogeneous designs.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)[[17]](#ref17)</sup>
- **Cost and availability** vary regionally; not typically covered by insurance.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Guideline positioning

Guideline endorsement of PTT in Peyronie's disease varies by society, and the Cochrane review rates the certainty of the traction evidence as very low.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> The International Consultation on Sexual Medicine report (2015 meeting, published 2016) concluded that PTT is "a viable therapy to modestly improve penile length" as primary therapy, before prosthesis placement in men with decreased length, or after surgery for Peyronie's disease. It may have a role in the acute phase of Peyronie's disease but has inconsistent outcomes in the long-term phase.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> For cosmetic lengthening, systematic-review evidence is of low quality and does not support graded recommendations.<sup>[[17]](#ref17)</sup>
