# Penile Traction Therapy (PTT)

**Penile traction therapy (PTT)** is a **non-invasive mechanical treatment** that applies controlled longitudinal force to the penis. Its principal evidence base is in **Peyronie's disease** (curvature improvement, length preservation, and as an adjunct to intralesional therapy), with secondary applications in **post-radical-prostatectomy penile rehabilitation**, **pre- and post-penile-prosthesis length optimization**, and **cosmetic penile lengthening (penile dysmorphophobia)**.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

---

## Mechanism of action

PTT works through **mechanotransduction** — the cellular process by which mechanical stimuli are translated into biochemical responses. Continuous longitudinal traction:

- **Increases matrix metalloproteinase (MMP) activity** within treated tissue, driving collagen degradation and scar remodeling.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
- **Decreases smooth-muscle actin** expression and induces re-orientation of collagen fibrils along the direction of applied force.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
- In animal models, decreases **TGF-β1 and SMAD 2/3** signaling, with antifibrotic, antiapoptotic, and smooth-muscle-preserving effects.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>

## Devices

| Device | Mechanism | Daily duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **RestoreX** (PathRight Medical) | Rod-based traction with counter-bending | **30–90 min** | Shortest use time; counter-bend feature for curvature; developed with Mayo Clinic; **only device with RCT-level evidence in PD and post-RP rehab**.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup> |
| **Penimaster PRO** (MSP Concept) | Vacuum-adhesion + traction | 3–8 hours | Dose-dependent response; vacuum grip avoids strap compression.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup> |
| **Andropenis / Andropeyronie** (Andromedical) | Strap-based traction | 4–6 hours | Longest track record; most studied for cosmetic lengthening.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup> |
| **Golden Erect** (Ronas Tajhiz Teb) | Strap-based traction | 4–9 hours | Limited data.<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup> |

Research currently favors **Penimaster PRO and RestoreX** for Peyronie's disease outcomes.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Clinical indications and evidence

### Peyronie's disease — strongest evidence base

PTT is recognized by the **AUA, EAU, CUA, and ISSM** as having a role in PD management, with varying recommendation strength.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> It can be used as monotherapy or in combination with intralesional injections (CCH, IFN α-2b, verapamil) or surgery.<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup>

| Trial | Device / Protocol | Key outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Ziegelmann 2019 *J Urol* RCT** (n=110, Mayo)<sup>[[9]](#ref9)</sup> | RestoreX 30–90 min/day × 3 mo | **+1.5 cm length, −11.7° curvature**, EF improvement (p &lt; 0.001) |
| **Joseph 2020 RestoreX OLE follow-up**<sup>[[10]](#ref10)</sup> | extended use | **95% length gains at 6 mo, 61% curvature improvements** |
| **Moncada 2019 *BJU Int* Penimaster PRO RCT** (n=93, stable PD)<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup> | 3–8 h/day × 12 wk | Dose-dependent: &gt; 6 h/d → **−38.4° curvature (51.4% improvement), +1.8 cm length**; &lt; 4 h/d → no significant benefit |
| **Cochrane 2023 (Rosenberg)**<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> | SR | Acknowledged potential of PTT but flagged **high uncertainty** due to limited trials and small sample sizes |

### Post-radical-prostatectomy penile rehabilitation

Radical prostatectomy commonly causes penile shortening, ED, and corporal fibrosis.<sup>[[11]](#ref11)</sup>

- **Toussi 2021 *J Urol* RestoreX RCT** (n=82): 6 mo PTT → **+1.6 cm length preservation** vs +0.3 cm controls (p &lt; 0.001), with improved EF and patient satisfaction.<sup>[[12]](#ref12)</sup>

### Pre- and post-penile-prosthesis length optimization

PTT has been used to optimize penile length before IPP placement, particularly in men with shortening from prior surgery, explantation, or PD.<sup>[[13]](#ref13)[[14]](#ref14)</sup>

- **Levine 2011 pilot** (n=10, 2–4 h/day × 2–4 mo before IPP): **no measured or perceived length loss** post-implant; 70% achieved a small erect length gain (up to 1.5 cm).<sup>[[14]](#ref14)</sup>
- **Moskovic 2011 case report**: 8 mo post-prosthesis PTT → +2.3 cm stretched penile length, enabling revision with a **20% longer device** (15 cm → 18 cm).<sup>[[15]](#ref15)</sup>
- Evidence in this indication remains very limited.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

### Cosmetic / aesthetic penile lengthening (penile dysmorphophobia)

Most men seeking penile enhancement have **normal dimensions** and either suffer from penile dysmorphophobia or are misinformed about normal size.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup>

- **AndroPenis (Nowroozi 2015)**: n=54 with penile dysmorphophobia, 4–6 h/day × 6 mo → **+1.7 cm flaccid, +1.3 cm stretched, +1.2 cm erect** (all p &lt; 0.001).<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup>
- **Golden Erect (Nikoobakht 2011)**: n=23, 4–6 h/day initially, then 9 h/day × 3 mo → flaccid 8.8 → 10.5 cm; stretched 11.5 → 13.2 cm (p &lt; 0.001).<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup>
- **Oderda-Gontero 2011 SR**: penile extenders are the **only evidence-based non-surgical technique** for penile elongation; results not inferior to surgical lengthening phalloplasty.<sup>[[16]](#ref16)</sup>
- **Romero-Otero 2021 SR (BJU Int)**: PTT confirmed as the only evidence-based non-surgical lengthening intervention; overall study quality remains low.<sup>[[17]](#ref17)</sup>
- **Structured psychosexual counseling** should precede any intervention in men with cosmetic concerns — many can be reassured without treatment.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup> See [Small Penis Syndrome / PDD](/docs/clinical-conditions/03g-genital-scrotal/small-penis-syndrome) and [Cosmetic Genital Surgery](/docs/surgical-techniques/04l-cosmetic-genital-surgery).

## Combination therapy

PTT can be combined with intralesional injections or as adjunct to surgical correction of PD.<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup>

- **Yafi 2015 *J Urol***: when combined with **interferon α-2b**, PTT ≥ 3 h/day provided a small but significant additional stretched penile length gain (**+ 4.4 mm vs + 1.3 mm**, p = 0.04).<sup>[[18]](#ref18)</sup>
- **CCH + PTT** combination: see [Peyronie's Disease Agents](/docs/foundations/pharmacology/sexual-medicine-andrology/peyronies-disease-agents) for the IMPRESS-extension and Ziegelmann 2023 incremental-cycle data.

## Treatment protocols

No universally standardized protocol exists.<sup>[[11]](#ref11)[[8]](#ref8)</sup> General principles:

- **Duration:** ranges from 30 min/day (RestoreX) to 3–8+ hours/day (traditional devices). Longer daily use correlates with greater curvature and length improvement in a dose-dependent manner.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)[[18]](#ref18)</sup>
- **Treatment course:** typically **3–6 months minimum**; continued use beyond 3 months yields additional length gains.<sup>[[10]](#ref10)</sup>
- **Force:** progressive increase per device-specific instructions.

---

## Safety

PTT is consistently reported as **safe and well-tolerated** across all indications.<sup>[[9]](#ref9)[[8]](#ref8)[[5]](#ref5)[[6]](#ref6)</sup>

- Adverse events are **transient and mild** — erythema, local discomfort, skin irritation, temporary glans numbness — typically resolving within minutes of device removal.<sup>[[10]](#ref10)</sup>
- **No serious adverse events** (penile fracture, permanent numbness, vascular compromise) reported in published trials.
- RestoreX PD trial: **87% would repeat therapy, 93% would recommend**.<sup>[[12]](#ref12)</sup>
- Mild AEs in ~ 43% of Penimaster PRO users (discomfort, glans numbness).<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>

---

## Limitations

- **Compliance** is the single greatest barrier — daily use for extended periods is demanding, and adherence is highly variable.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[11]](#ref11)</sup>
- **No standardized protocols** across devices and indications.<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup>
- **No effect on penile girth** has been consistently demonstrated.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)[[7]](#ref7)</sup>
- **Limited long-term data** — most studies report at 3–9 months; durability beyond is uncertain.<sup>[[10]](#ref10)</sup>
- Overall **quality of evidence is low**, with small samples and heterogeneous designs.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)[[17]](#ref17)</sup>
- **Cost and availability** vary regionally; not typically covered by insurance.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

---

## Guideline positioning

The **AUA, EAU, CUA, and ISSM** all acknowledge a role for PTT in Peyronie's disease management with varying levels of endorsement.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> The **2015 ICSM consensus** concluded PTT is "a viable therapy to modestly improve penile length" as primary therapy, before prosthesis placement, or after PD surgery, with possible role in acute-phase PD but inconsistent outcomes in chronic disease.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> For cosmetic lengthening, PTT is positioned as the **only evidence-based non-surgical option**, though robust recommendations are lacking due to low-quality evidence.<sup>[[16]](#ref16)</sup>
