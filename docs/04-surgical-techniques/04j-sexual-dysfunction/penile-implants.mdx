# Penile Implants

Penile implants (penile prostheses) are the definitive surgical treatment for erectile dysfunction refractory to or unsuitable for non-surgical therapies. Devices are categorized as inflatable penile prostheses (IPP — 2- or 3-piece) or malleable/semi-rigid rods implanted into the corpora cavernosa. The 3-piece IPP provides the most natural erection and flaccidity. Patient satisfaction rates of 92–98% are the highest of any ED treatment modality.
