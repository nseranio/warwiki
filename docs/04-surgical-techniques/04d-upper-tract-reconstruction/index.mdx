# Upper Tract Reconstruction

Upper tract reconstruction is the set of operations used to restore unobstructed drainage from the renal pelvis to the bladder while preserving renal function. In practice, the key variables are **location, defect length, ischemic burden, etiology, and the salvageability of the ipsilateral renal unit**. Short healthy defects tolerate direct repair; long or hostile segments require grafts, bowel, contralateral drainage, or renal relocation.

## Decision Framework

Successful ureteral reconstruction depends on a small set of universal principles regardless of technique: **adequate debridement of devitalized tissue**, **preservation of periureteral blood supply**, a **tension-free, watertight, spatulated mucosa-to-mucosa anastomosis**, **ureteral stenting**, and **retroperitoneal drainage**. Two variables drive technique selection — **stricture location** (UPJ / proximal · mid · distal) and **defect length** (short ≤ 2–3 cm · moderate 3–8 cm · long &gt; 8 cm). The expansion of robotic **buccal mucosa graft (BMG) ureteroplasty** has shifted the threshold for bowel interposition upward — strictures up to 8 cm that previously demanded ileal ureter can now often be managed with BMG onlay (94.9% pooled success vs 85.8% for ileal ureter, with significantly fewer long-term complications per You 2023 meta).

### Location × Length Matrix

| Location \\ Length | **Short (≤ 2–3 cm)** | **Moderate (3–8 cm)** | **Long (&gt; 8 cm)** |
|---|---|---|---|

| **Distal Ureter** | [Ureteral reimplantation (UNC)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/ureteral-reimplantation) | [UNC + psoas hitch](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/boari-flap-psoas-hitch) | [Boari flap ± psoas hitch](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/boari-flap-psoas-hitch) (up to 8–12 cm); pan-ureteral → [ileal ureter](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/ileal-ureter) / [Yang-Monti](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/ileal-ureter) |

### Modifying Factors

| Factor | Impact on Technique Selection |
|---|---|
| **Prior pelvic radiation** | Avoid Boari flap; favor BMG onlay, ileal ureter, or [autotransplantation](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/renal-autotransplantation) |
| **Small / contracted bladder** | Psoas hitch and Boari flap may not be feasible; consider bowel interposition |
| **Bilateral ureteral disease** | Avoid [TUU](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/trans-ureteroureterostomy); consider bilateral reconstruction or ileal ureter |
| **Impaired renal function (eGFR &lt; 40)** | Yang-Monti relatively contraindicated; minimize bowel-segment use |
| **Right-sided stricture with intact appendix** | Consider [appendiceal onlay / interposition](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/appendiceal-ileal-onlay) |
| **Failed prior reconstruction** | Escalate: BMG → bowel interposition → autotransplantation |
| **Solitary kidney** | Avoid TUU; prioritize nephron-sparing techniques; Yang-Monti safe if eGFR adequate |
| **Unstable patient / contaminated field** | Damage control: percutaneous nephrostomy + delayed reconstruction |
| **Oral mucosa unavailable / inadequate** | [Lingual mucosal graft](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/bmg-onlay-ureter) (ureter) or appendiceal onlay |

### Stepwise Escalation Ladder

For any given location and length, complexity and morbidity escalate along this hierarchy:

1. **Endoscopic management** — [endoureterotomy](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/endoureterotomy) or balloon dilation for short, non-ischemic, non-irradiated strictures
2. **Primary anastomosis** — [ureteroureterostomy](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/ureteroureterostomy), [reimplantation](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/ureteral-reimplantation), or [pyeloplasty](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/pyeloplasty)
3. **Bladder-mobilization adjuncts** — psoas hitch → [Boari flap](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/boari-flap-psoas-hitch)
4. **Adjunctive maneuvers** — [downward nephropexy](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/downward-nephropexy) (gains 3–5 cm of length; used in 19.6% of series)
5. **Tissue grafting** — [BMG onlay](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/bmg-onlay-ureter), lingual mucosa, or [appendiceal onlay](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/appendiceal-ileal-onlay)
6. **Bowel interposition** — Yang-Monti → classic [ileal ureter](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/ileal-ureter)
7. **Cross-drainage** — [transureteroureterostomy (TUU)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/trans-ureteroureterostomy)
8. **[Renal autotransplantation](/docs/surgical-techniques/04d-upper-tract-reconstruction/renal-autotransplantation)** — extensive ureteral loss when other options exhausted
9. **Nephrectomy** — last resort when ipsilateral renal function does not warrant reconstruction or all reconstructive options have failed
