# Appendiceal & Ileal Onlay / Interposition

The appendix (right-sided defects) or a detubularized ileal segment is used as an onlay flap or tubular interposition graft to bridge moderate mid-ureteral defects. The appendix retains its mesenteric blood supply and has favorable tissue characteristics for urothelial regeneration; ileum is used when the appendix is absent or the defect is bilateral.
