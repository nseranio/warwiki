# Buccal Mucosa Graft Onlay (Ureter)

Buccal mucosa graft onlay to a longitudinal ureterotomy over a ureteral stent, borrowing principles from urethral reconstruction. An emerging technique that avoids circumferential anastomosis and ureteral devascularization; early series show promise for short-segment strictures in select patients.
