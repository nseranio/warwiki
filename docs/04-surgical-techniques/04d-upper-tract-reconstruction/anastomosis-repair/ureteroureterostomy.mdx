# Ureteroureterostomy

End-to-end spatulated anastomosis after excision of a diseased ureteral segment, performed robotically or open. Best suited for short mid-ureteral defects (≤2–3 cm) after complete mobilization; requires tension-free repair over a double-J stent. Robotic approach facilitates precise intracorporeal suturing.
