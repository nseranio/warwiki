# Distal Ureterectomy with Reimplantation

Excision of the diseased distal ureteral segment followed by direct neocystostomy, with or without psoas hitch to reduce anastomotic tension. Gold standard for isolated distal ureteral injuries from iatrogenic, oncologic, or stricture etiologies; may be combined with an anti-reflux tunnel for longer defects.
