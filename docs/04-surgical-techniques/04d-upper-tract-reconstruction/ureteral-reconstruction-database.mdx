# Ureteral Reconstruction

The ureter is a vulnerable structure — long, narrow, retroperitoneal, and highly dependent on a fragile segmental blood supply. Reconstructive strategy is determined by the level and length of the injury or stricture, its etiology (iatrogenic, radiation, oncologic, inflammatory), and the functional reserve of the ipsilateral kidney. Short, well-vascularized defects tolerate direct anastomosis; longer or ischemic segments require tissue interposition or renal mobilization strategies.
