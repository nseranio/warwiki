# Trans Ureteroureterostomy (TUU)

The injured ipsilateral ureter is routed across the midline and anastomosed end-to-side to the contralateral native ureter. Avoids reconstruction in a hostile retroperitoneal field and preserves the damaged kidney's drainage. Contraindicated when the contralateral ureter is at risk (stones, prior surgery, single functioning kidney considerations).
