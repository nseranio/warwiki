# Ureterocalicostomy

Direct anastomosis of the proximal ureter to a lower pole renal calyx after partial nephrectomy of lower pole parenchyma. Reserved for failed pyeloplasty, intrarenal pelvis, or UPJ obstruction where a new ureteropelvic junction cannot be fashioned. Provides drainage bypass of the obstructed pelvis.
