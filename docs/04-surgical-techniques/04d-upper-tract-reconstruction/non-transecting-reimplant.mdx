# Non-Transecting Ureteral Reimplantation

Ureteral reimplantation preserving the adventitia and periureteral blood supply on at least one side without full transection. Reduces the devascularization risk compared to complete transection; particularly applicable in marginal ischemic or radiation-injured ureters where vascular reserve is limited.
