# Optilume Drug-Coated Balloon (Ureter)

Optilume is a paclitaxel-coated dilation balloon originally developed for urethral stricture disease, where ROBUST III demonstrated superior patency over standard dilation in bulbar urethral strictures. The same platform is now being evaluated in the ureter, extending the drug-coated balloon concept beyond the urethra: mechanical dilation to open the stricture, with simultaneous local paclitaxel delivery intended to inhibit the fibroblast proliferation that drives restenosis after endoureterotomy or balloon dilation alone.

## ENDURE-1 trial

The **ENDURE-1** study ([NCT07020520](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07020520)) is a prospective investigational trial evaluating the Optilume drug-coated balloon for the treatment of ureteral strictures. It sits on the hypothesis that paclitaxel-assisted dilation may improve durability of endoscopic treatment in a ureteral environment where plain dilation and endoureterotomy have historically produced only modest long-term patency. Until prospective data mature, Optilume in the ureter should be regarded as investigational, not standard of care.
