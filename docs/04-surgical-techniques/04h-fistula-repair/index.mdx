# Fistula Repair

**Genitourinary fistula repair** is organized by the tracts involved (bladder–vagina, ureter–vagina, urethra–vagina, vesicouterine, rectovaginal, rectourethral, urethrocutaneous, urethroperineal) and by the surgical approach that reaches them best. Four decisions recur: route (transvaginal, transabdominal, transperineal, transanal or combined), timing (immediate, early or delayed), need for an interposition flap (none, Martius, gracilis or omentum), and need for protective diversion. The female and male sections cover common anatomical presentations. The All Patients section covers shared and less common fistula sites.
