# Iliococcygeus Fascia Suspension

The vaginal apex is sutured bilaterally to the iliococcygeus fascia just inferior to the ischial spine as an alternative native tissue apical support repair. Avoids the sacrospinous neurovascular bundle and the ureteral risk of uterosacral suspension; technically simpler in some anatomies with comparable apical success rates.
