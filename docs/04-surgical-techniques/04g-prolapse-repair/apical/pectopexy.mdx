# Pectopexy

Mesh-based apical suspension bilaterally to the pectineal (Cooper's) ligament via laparoscopic or robotic approach, avoiding the sacral dissection and risk of presacral hemorrhage associated with sacrocolpopexy. A European technique with growing adoption; prospective comparative data show comparable apical success to sacrocolpopexy with potentially reduced operative risk.
