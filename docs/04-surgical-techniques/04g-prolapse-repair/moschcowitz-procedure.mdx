# Moschcowitz Procedure

Concentric purse-string sutures placed abdominally to obliterate the pouch of Douglas and prevent or repair enterocele. Performed in conjunction with sacrocolpopexy or abdominal hysterectomy; the Halban culdoplasty uses sagittal rather than concentric sutures for the same purpose.
