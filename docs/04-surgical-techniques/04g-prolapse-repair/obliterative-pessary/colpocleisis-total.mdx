# Total Colpocleisis

Complete vaginal obliteration with excision of vaginal epithelium and multilayer closure of the vaginal canal, used in post-hysterectomy patients or combined with hysterectomy. Shorter operative time and lower morbidity than full reconstructive repair; definitive treatment with very high patient satisfaction when patient selection is appropriate.
