# Anterior Midline Colporrhaphy

Central defect repair of anterior vaginal wall prolapse (cystocele) via midline plication of the pubocervical fascia beneath the bladder. Addresses central compartment defects with good short-term anatomic outcomes, though 5-year recurrence is significant without concurrent apical support.
