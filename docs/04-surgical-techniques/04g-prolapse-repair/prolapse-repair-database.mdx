# Prolapse Repair

Pelvic organ prolapse (POP) repair encompasses a wide range of native tissue, mesh-augmented, and obliterative procedures targeting the anterior, posterior, and apical compartments. Surgical planning must account for compartment-specific anatomy (DeLancey Levels I–III), patient goals including uterine preservation and sexual activity, prior surgery, and mesh history. The table below catalogs the major surgical and non-surgical techniques by compartment and approach.

> See the [Pelvic Organ Prolapse](/docs/clinical-conditions/03c-pelvic-support/pelvic-organ-prolapse) article for pathophysiology, risk stratification, and trial data (OPTIMAL, PEOPLE).
