# Perineorrhaphy

Repair and reconstruction of the perineal body, reapproximating the bulbocavernosus and superficial transverse perineal muscles to restore perineal support. Addresses distal rectocele, perineal laxity, and widened genital hiatus; frequently combined with posterior colporrhaphy as a unified posterior compartment repair.
