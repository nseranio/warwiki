# Halban Culdoplasty

Sagittal sutures placed from anterior to posterior to obliterate the cul-de-sac peritoneum and prevent enterocele, as an abdominal alternative to the Moschcowitz purse-string technique. Often performed at abdominal hysterectomy or with sacrocolpopexy when cul-de-sac depth is significant.
