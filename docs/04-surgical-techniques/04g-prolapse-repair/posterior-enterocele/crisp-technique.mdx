# CRISP Technique

A continuous running suture technique for peritoneal closure incorporating high uterosacral plication to achieve vault suspension at the time of vaginal hysterectomy or vault repair. Technical variation of native tissue apical repair emphasizing reproducibility and simplicity over formal ligament-specific dissection.
