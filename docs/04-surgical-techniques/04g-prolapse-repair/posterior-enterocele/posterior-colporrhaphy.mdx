# Posterior Midline Colporrhaphy

Central defect repair of posterior vaginal wall prolapse (rectocele) via midline plication of the rectovaginal fascia between the vaginal epithelium and the rectum. High anatomic success rates; functional outcomes variable with a risk of dyspareunia from over-aggressive midline plication.
