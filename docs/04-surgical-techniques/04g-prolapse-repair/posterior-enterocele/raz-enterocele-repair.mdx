# Raz Transvaginal Enterocele Repair

Transvaginal high ligation and excision of the enterocele sac with concurrent plication of the uterosacral-cardinal ligament complex for apical support. Targets enterocele sac excision as the primary objective while restoring apical suspension through the vaginal approach.
