# History & Physical

The bedside evaluation consists of a structured history, validated assessment instruments and a systematic urogenital examination. It underlies every later test, image and operation. In planned reconstructive care, the bedside evaluation helps define the surgical question and direct further testing.

This section links to validated questionnaires, grading scales and objective measures, the male urogenital examination, and the female pelvic examination with POP-Q.
