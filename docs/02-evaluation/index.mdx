# Evaluation & Workup

This section provides a systematic framework for evaluating patients with genitourinary and pelvic floor disorders, encompassing structured history-taking and symptom assessment, urogenital physical examination (including POP-Q staging and myofascial assessment), urodynamic testing, diagnostic endoscopy (cystoscopy and urethroscopy), a full suite of imaging modalities (RUG, VCUG, CT urogram, MRI, fistulogram), and anorectal testing (manometry, defecography, endoanal ultrasound).
