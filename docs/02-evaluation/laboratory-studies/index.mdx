# Laboratory Studies

Laboratory testing should answer a specific clinical question: infection, renal function, metabolic complications, endocrine disease, nutrition, or procedural risk. Interpret results in the context of collection method, symptoms, reconstructed anatomy, medication use, and comorbidity. The articles distinguish useful testing from low-yield screening and explain where conventional thresholds need qualification.
