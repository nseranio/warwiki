# Transferrin

An iron-binding visceral protein with a **~ 8-day half-life**, historically used as a nutritional marker alongside albumin. Largely **superseded** by prealbumin for monitoring and by formal screening tools (MUST / NRS-2002) for assessment. Confounded by both **inflammation** and **iron status** — transferrin rises in iron deficiency and falls in inflammation, opposing directions that limit its standalone interpretability.

## Key Cutoff

- **< 200 mg/dL** — abnormal (rough threshold; lab- and population-specific)
