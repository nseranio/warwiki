# C-Reactive Protein (CRP)

The preferred bedside biomarker of **inflammatory activity** and the **etiologic anchor** for interpreting low albumin / prealbumin. A patient with low albumin **and elevated CRP** has inflammation-driven hypoproteinemia; a patient with low albumin **and normal CRP** has more likely a nutritional process. CRP is part of the **GLIM etiologic criterion** for malnutrition diagnosis.

## Interpretation Frame

| Albumin | CRP | Likely picture |
|---|---|---|
| Low | High | Inflammation-driven; treat underlying disease |
| Low | Normal | Pure nutritional depletion; respond to nutrition support |
| Normal | High | Acute inflammation, nutritional reserve preserved |
| Normal | Normal | No nutritional or inflammatory concern |
