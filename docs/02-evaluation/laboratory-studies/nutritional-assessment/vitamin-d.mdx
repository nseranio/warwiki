# Vitamin D

Assessed as **serum 25-hydroxyvitamin D [25(OH)D]**. Deficiency is common in the general surgical population and **near-universal in post-bariatric patients**. Affects bone health, immune function, and wound healing.

## Key Cutoffs

- **< 30 nmol/L (12 ng/mL)** — severe deficiency
- **25–50 nmol/L (10–20 ng/mL)** — insufficiency
- Bariatric guidelines target ≥ 75 nmol/L (30 ng/mL); supplement at ≥ 3000 IU/day

## In Reconstruction

- Routine post-bariatric and post-RYGB surveillance.
- High-yield in patients on GLP-1 RAs (appetite suppression → reduced dietary intake).
- Relevant to bone health in post-cystectomy / urinary-diversion patients (chronic acidosis + diversion-related bone demineralization).
