# Copper

An essential trace element required for **oxidative phosphorylation, iron metabolism, neurotransmitter synthesis, and connective-tissue cross-linking**. Deficiency causes a syndrome that **clinically mimics vitamin B12 deficiency** — anemia + sensory ataxia + spastic gait + lower-extremity sensory loss from subacute combined degeneration. Copper deficiency is **frequently missed** because the differential reflexively lands on B12.

## Key Tests

- **Serum copper + ceruloplasmin** (low both)
- Serum zinc — **high-dose zinc supplementation is the most common reversible cause** of copper deficiency (zinc upregulates intestinal metallothionein, which binds and sequesters copper)
- 24-h urine copper (Wilson disease differential)

## In Reconstruction

- **Annual surveillance after RYGB and BPD-DS** — deficiency develops years after surgery.
- Patient on high-dose zinc supplements (for taste / smell complaints, prostatitis adjunctive, athletic supplementation) presenting with neurologic symptoms — always check copper.
- Patient with B12-like myeloneuropathy and normal B12 / MMA — check copper before invasive workup.
