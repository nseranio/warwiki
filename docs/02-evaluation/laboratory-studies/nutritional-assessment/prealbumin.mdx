# Prealbumin (Transthyretin)

A visceral protein with a **~ 2-day half-life** — short enough that it tracks acute changes in nutritional status more responsively than albumin (~ 20 days). Like albumin, prealbumin is a **negative acute-phase reactant** and falls in inflammation; ASPEN cautions against using either as a standalone nutritional marker.

## Key Cutoff

- **< 15 mg/dL** — abnormal
- Used primarily to **track response** to nutrition support after the initial assessment, not for initial screening

## In Reconstruction

- More useful than albumin for monitoring response to perioperative ONS, immunonutrition, or TPN over days-to-weeks.
- GLP-1 RA users have lower preoperative prealbumin in addition to lower albumin — a mechanistic signal for the wound-healing risk.
