# Vitamin B12 (Cobalamin)

Absorbed in the **terminal ileum** via intrinsic-factor-mediated transport. This makes B12 surveillance **universal and lifelong** in any reconstructive patient who has had **ileal-conduit, neobladder, augmentation cystoplasty, Indiana / Mainz pouch, or ileal-ureter** reconstruction, and in **post-RYGB / BPD-DS** patients. GLP-1 RA users add a dietary-intake mechanism on top.

## Key Cutoffs

- **< 150 pmol/L (203 pg/mL)** — deficient
- **150–221 pmol/L** — marginal; confirm with **serum methylmalonic acid (MMA)** and **homocysteine**
- Functional deficiency can exist with "normal" serum B12 — MMA is the more sensitive marker

## In Reconstruction

- **Ileal-conduit / neobladder / augmentation** — annual B12 measurement is part of the standard post-diversion surveillance bundle (AUA / SUFU).
- Symptoms can be delayed years after the index operation because hepatic B12 stores last 3–5 years.
- Untreated deficiency causes **macrocytic anemia + irreversible subacute combined degeneration** of the spinal cord.
