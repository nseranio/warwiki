# Iron & Ferritin

Iron deficiency is the most common micronutrient deficiency worldwide and a high-yield preoperative finding in reconstructive populations. **Ferritin** is the storage-iron marker but is also an **acute-phase reactant** — interpret in the context of inflammation (CRP).

## Key Cutoffs

- **Ferritin < 30 µg/L** — iron deficiency likely
- **Ferritin < 10 µg/L** — frank anemia risk
- In the presence of inflammation (CRP > 5 mg/L), the threshold rises (often **< 100 µg/L** considered iron-deficient)
- Adjuncts: serum iron, **total iron-binding capacity (TIBC)**, **transferrin saturation** (< 20% supports deficiency)

## In Reconstruction

- Universal screening before major elective surgery; preoperative anemia is a modifiable risk factor for transfusion, infection, and prolonged LOS.
- Post-bariatric (especially RYGB and BPD-DS) — duodenal bypass impairs iron absorption.
- GLP-1 RA users — reduced dietary intake.
- Heavy menstrual bleeding (preoperative urogynecology patient) — pre-fibroid / pre-hysterectomy iron status.
- Hematuria-driven anemia (bladder cancer, fistula, post-radiation).
