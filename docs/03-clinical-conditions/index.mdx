# Clinical Conditions

This section is organized around the clinical problems you are trying to solve, grouping conditions by functional domain rather than anatomy alone — covering storage disorders and incontinence, voiding and outlet obstruction, pelvic organ prolapse, bladder pathology, upper tract disorders, fistulas, genital and scrotal conditions, and pelvic pain syndromes, with cross-links throughout to the relevant surgical techniques and special-population overlays.
