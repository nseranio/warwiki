# Vesicocutaneous Fistula

A **vesicocutaneous fistula** is an abnormal communication between the bladder and the skin. It is most often the consequence of a **chronic suprapubic catheter tract**, **post-operative wound breakdown** with unrecognized bladder injury, **radiation-induced pelvic necrosis**, or **malignant erosion** of pelvic tumor through the abdominal wall.

See also: [The Bladder](/docs/foundations/anatomy-physiology/urinary-tract/bladder-anatomy-physiology), [Fistulas landing page](/docs/clinical-conditions/03f-fistulas).
