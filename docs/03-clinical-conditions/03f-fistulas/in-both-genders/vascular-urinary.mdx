# Vascular-Urinary Fistula

A **vascular-urinary fistula** is a communication between a major vessel and the urinary tract. The classic examples are the **uretero-arterial fistula** (most often between the common or external iliac artery and the ureter) and the **uretero-venous fistula** (between the iliac vein and the ureter, much rarer). The dominant clinical setting is a patient with **longstanding indwelling ureteral stents**, **prior pelvic radiation**, **pelvic vascular graft (aortobifemoral, aortic-bifurcation, or iliac bypass)**, and **prior extensive pelvic surgery** — usually for gynecologic or urologic malignancy. Presentation is with **massive intermittent hematuria**, often preceded by a "herald bleed," and the condition is rapidly life-threatening. Endovascular stent-graft placement has largely replaced open repair for the arterial variety.
