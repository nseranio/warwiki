# Colovesical &amp; Small Bowel Fistulas

**Enterovesical fistulas** are abnormal communications between a segment of bowel and the bladder. They are most often **colovesical** — most commonly secondary to **sigmoid diverticulitis**, followed by **colorectal malignancy**, **Crohn's disease**, and **radiation**. Small-bowel fistulas (ileovesical) are less common and are classically seen in **Crohn's disease**. The pathognomonic symptom cluster of **pneumaturia + fecaluria + recurrent polymicrobial UTI** is highly specific and often the presenting complaint.

See also: [The Bladder](/docs/foundations/anatomy-physiology/urinary-tract/bladder-anatomy-physiology), [Bowel Anatomy](/docs/foundations/anatomy-physiology/pelvis-support/bowel-anatomy), [Fistulas landing page](/docs/clinical-conditions/03f-fistulas).
