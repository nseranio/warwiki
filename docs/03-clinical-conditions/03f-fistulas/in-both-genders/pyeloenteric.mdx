# Pyeloenteric Fistula

A **pyeloenteric fistula** is an abnormal communication between the renal collecting system (usually the renal pelvis) and an adjacent segment of bowel — most commonly the **duodenum** (reno-duodenal fistula), **colon** (pyelo-colonic, particularly left-sided), or **jejunum**. It is rare, and nearly always arises from longstanding obstructive infection, chronic calculus disease, xanthogranulomatous pyelonephritis, penetrating trauma, or infiltrating malignancy.
