# Ureterocolonic Fistula

A **ureterocolonic fistula** is an abnormal communication between the ureter and the colon. The vast majority are secondary to a primary colonic process — **diverticulitis** (most common), **Crohn's disease**, **colorectal malignancy**, **radiation injury** after pelvic radiotherapy, or **iatrogenic colorectal injury** during abdominal surgery. Primary ureteral etiologies are rare.

See also: [The Ureters](/docs/foundations/anatomy-physiology/urinary-tract/ureter-anatomy-physiology), [Fistulas landing page](/docs/clinical-conditions/03f-fistulas).
