# Rectovesical Fistula

A **rectovesical fistula** is an abnormal communication between the rectum and the bladder. In contemporary practice it is most often a complication of **radical prostatectomy with rectal injury** (particularly at the apical dissection), **radiation therapy** for prostate or rectal cancer, **pelvic malignancy** with direct tumor invasion, or **pelvic trauma**. It is distinct from rectourethral fistula — the opening is into the bladder base rather than the membranous or prostatic urethra — though the two lesions are often grouped together clinically and require similar reconstructive approaches.

See also: [Rectourethral Fistula](/docs/clinical-conditions/03f-fistulas/in-males/rectourethral), [The Bladder](/docs/foundations/anatomy-physiology/urinary-tract/bladder-anatomy-physiology), [Fistulas landing page](/docs/clinical-conditions/03f-fistulas).
