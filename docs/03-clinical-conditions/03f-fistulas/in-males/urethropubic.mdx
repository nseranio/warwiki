# Urethropubic Fistula

A **urethropubic fistula** is an abnormal communication between the urethra and the **pubic symphysis** or underlying pubic bone, often with associated **pubic osteomyelitis** or **osteitis pubis**. Two clinical settings dominate: (1) **pelvic fracture urethral injury (PFUI)** with devascularized posterior urethra and contiguous bony injury, and (2) **radical prostatectomy with bladder-neck contracture and bone erosion**, particularly after radiation. The fistula is difficult to manage because the exposed bone resists closure, recurrent infection is the rule, and definitive repair often requires **partial pubectomy with flap interposition** (gracilis, rectus abdominis, or omental pedicle).

See also: [PFUI](/docs/special-populations/05a-trauma-emergencies/pfui), [Bony Pelvic Anatomy](/docs/foundations/anatomy-physiology/pelvis-support/bony-pelvic-anatomy), [Fistulas landing page](/docs/clinical-conditions/03f-fistulas).
