# Voiding & Outlet Disorders

This subsection covers obstructive and dysfunctional voiding conditions including urethral stricture, bladder neck stenosis, bladder outlet obstruction, underactive bladder, dysfunctional voiding, urethral diverticula, lichen sclerosus, hypospadias, and epispadias.
