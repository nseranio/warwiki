# Hidradenitis Suppurativa — Genitourinary Disease

*Content in development.*

Hidradenitis suppurativa (HS) is a chronic, recurrent, inflammatory disease of follicular occlusion affecting apocrine-gland-bearing skin, with a strong predilection for the **inguinal, perineal, perianal, scrotal / labial, and gluteal regions**. For the reconstructive urologist, advanced disease in these zones produces draining sinus tracts, painful nodules, scarring, scrotal / vulvar architectural distortion, and — in long-standing severe cases — secondary scrotal lymphedema and even Marjolin-type squamous cell carcinoma in chronic sinuses.

Anchors expected to include the 2024 North American HS Foundation guidelines, Hurley staging, the role of adalimumab and the IL-17 / IL-23 biologic ladder, and the wide-excision-with-secondary-intention-vs-flap-coverage reconstructive decision.
