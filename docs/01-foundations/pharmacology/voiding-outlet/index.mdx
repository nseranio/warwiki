# Voiding &amp; Outlet Pharmacotherapy

This section covers pharmacotherapy for male LUTS/BPH and drug approaches used or studied for urinary retention, stress incontinence, underactive bladder, and detrusor-sphincter dyssynergia. The classes have distinct targets: α₁ signaling in bladder-neck and prostatic smooth muscle, cholinergic receptors in the detrusor, prostatic 5α-reductase, and central or direct skeletal-muscle actions affecting the external sphincter and pelvic floor.<sup>[[7]](#ref7)[[8]](#ref8)[[9]](#ref9)</sup>

For OAB / storage-phase therapy see [Storage & OAB](/docs/foundations/pharmacology/storage-oab). For pelvic-floor hypertonicity in the context of neuropathic pelvic pain see [Neuropathic & Pelvic Pain](/docs/foundations/pharmacology/neuropathic-pelvic-pain).

  </li>
  <li>
    <a href="/docs/foundations/pharmacology/voiding-outlet/alpha-agonists" className="section-stack-title section-stack-link">α-Agonists</a>
    <span className="section-stack-desc">Midodrine, pseudoephedrine and related drugs for ejaculatory problems, with their weak evidence for stress incontinence.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <a href="/docs/foundations/pharmacology/voiding-outlet/cholinergic-agonists" className="section-stack-title section-stack-link">Cholinergic Agonists</a>
    <span className="section-stack-desc">Bethanechol for nonobstructive retention; contraindicated in bladder-neck obstruction.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> EAU 2026 strongly recommends against routine parasympathomimetics for underactive bladder.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)[[3]](#ref3)</sup></span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <a href="/docs/foundations/pharmacology/voiding-outlet/skeletal-muscle-relaxants" className="section-stack-title section-stack-link">Skeletal Muscle Relaxants</a>
    <span className="section-stack-desc">Baclofen for neurologic spasticity and sphincter dysfunction; diazepam for high-tone pelvic-floor disorders; and cyclobenzaprine for short-term musculoskeletal spasm, with off-label pelvic-floor use and safety limits.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)[[5]](#ref5)[[6]](#ref6)</sup></span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <a href="/docs/foundations/pharmacology/voiding-outlet/dantrolene" className="section-stack-title section-stack-link">Dantrolene</a>
    <span className="section-stack-desc">A muscle relaxant once used for sphincter dyssynergia after spinal cord injury, now largely historical because of liver toxicity.</span>
  </li>
</ul>
