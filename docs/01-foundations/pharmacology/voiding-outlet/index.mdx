# Voiding &amp; Outlet Pharmacotherapy

Pharmacotherapy targeting bladder outlet relaxation, voiding-phase contraction, and outlet tone is the medical companion to surgical management of BPH/LUTS, stress incontinence, underactive bladder, and detrusor-sphincter dyssynergia. Most agents act at three loci: the bladder neck and prostatic smooth muscle (α₁ receptors), the detrusor (cholinergic receptors), and the external sphincter and pelvic floor (skeletal muscle).

For OAB / storage-phase therapy see [Storage & OAB](/docs/foundations/pharmacology/storage-oab). For pelvic-floor hypertonicity in the context of neuropathic pelvic pain see [Neuropathic & Pelvic Pain](/docs/foundations/pharmacology/neuropathic-pelvic-pain).
