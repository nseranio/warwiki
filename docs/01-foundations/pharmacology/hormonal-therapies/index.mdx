# Hormonal Therapies

Hormonal pharmacotherapy for GSM, recurrent-UTI prevention, perioperative care, and gender-affirming treatment. The linked hubs distinguish approved indications and product-specific dosing from off-label use, surrogate tissue findings, and clinical outcomes. In IMPROVE (199 postmenopausal women randomized; 186 operated), perioperative vaginal conjugated-estrogen cream versus placebo did not improve 12-month composite success after native-tissue vaginal apical repair (no anatomic recurrence, bulge symptoms, or repeat prolapse treatment).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> FDA menopausal-hormone labeling changes are product-specific; check the current label for the prescribed product.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

For testosterone replacement in male hypogonadism see [Sexual Medicine & Andrology](/docs/foundations/pharmacology/sexual-medicine-andrology/testosterone-replacement). For [GSM](/docs/special-populations/05e-womens-health/gsm) and [Gender-Affirming Surgery](/docs/special-populations/05c-gender-affirming) clinical articles see the linked pages.
