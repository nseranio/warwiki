# Dermatologic &amp; Topical Urethral Pharmacotherapy

Topical and intralesional therapies include treatments for genital dermatoses (lichen sclerosus, lichen planus, plasma-cell balanitis), anogenital warts, and Hunner lesions; adjunctive treatment of established urethral strictures; and investigational regenerative urologic applications. The category includes high-potency topical corticosteroids (used for LS), topical calcineurin inhibitors (steroid-sparing), selected intralesional treatments and investigational stricture adjuncts, the Optilume paclitaxel-coated balloon to treat obstructive symptoms associated with anterior urethral strictures up to 3 cm long in adult men, and platelet-rich plasma as an investigational biologic.

For oral systemic Peyronie's pharmacotherapy see [Peyronie's Disease Agents](/docs/foundations/pharmacology/sexual-medicine-andrology/peyronies-disease-agents) in Sexual Medicine & Andrology. For [lichen sclerosus](/docs/clinical-conditions/03g-genital-scrotal/lichen-sclerosus) clinical management see the linked condition page.
