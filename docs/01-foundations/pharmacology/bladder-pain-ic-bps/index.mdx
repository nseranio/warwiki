# Bladder Pain &amp; IC/BPS Pharmacotherapy

Pharmacotherapy for **interstitial cystitis / bladder pain syndrome (IC/BPS)** is individualized. The **AUA 2022 guideline removed numbered treatment tiers**: oral drugs and bladder instillations can be combined with appropriate behavioral and physical therapies, while lesion-directed treatment matters for Hunner lesions. Evidence for many drugs remains uncertain.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

The **CUA 2025 update conditionally recommends against oral pentosan polysulfate (PPS)** and cannot identify a preferred intravesical agent or cocktail. The oral hub explains the PPS benefit–risk discussion and retinal surveillance.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

For pelvic-floor myalgia adjuncts (gabapentinoids, TCAs, vaginal diazepam) see [Neuropathic & Pelvic Pain](/docs/foundations/pharmacology/neuropathic-pelvic-pain). For chronic pelvic pain frameworks see [IC/PBS](/docs/clinical-conditions/03h-pelvic-pain/ic-pbs).
