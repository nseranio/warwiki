# Perioperative &amp; ERAS Pharmacotherapy

Systemic pharmacotherapy surrounding the perioperative window: VTE prophylaxis, anticoagulation reversal, bowel preparation, postoperative bowel / ileus management, and systemic corticosteroids. These pages are the drug-class hubs (agents, doses, FDA labels, evidence ceilings); the [Perioperative Care](/docs/foundations/perioperative-care) section provides the bedside workflow companions (Caprini in urology, bridging, stress dosing, etc.). Each pair has explicit cross-reference boxes to keep facts in one place.

Recent shifts include the andexanet alfa US market withdrawal (4F-PCC or activated PCC are primary reversal agents for serious factor Xa inhibitor-associated bleeding<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>), the move away from routine mechanical bowel preparation before urinary diversion (most cystectomy ERAS protocols omit it; Simhal 2025 NSQIP and 2023 data are observational and suggest oral antibiotics may matter more for continent diversion than for ileal conduit), and alvimopan's persistent benefit within an ERAS pathway (Hanna 2021, single-center).
