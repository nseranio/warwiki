# Perioperative &amp; ERAS Pharmacotherapy

Systemic pharmacotherapy surrounding the perioperative window: VTE prophylaxis, anticoagulation reversal, bowel preparation, postoperative bowel / ileus management, and systemic corticosteroids. These pages are the drug-class hubs (agents, doses, FDA labels, evidence ceilings); the [Perioperative Care](/docs/foundations/perioperative-care) section provides the bedside workflow companions (Caprini in urology, bridging, stress dosing, etc.). Each pair has explicit cross-reference boxes to keep facts in one place.

Recent shifts include the andexanet alfa US market withdrawal (4F-PCC or activated PCC are primary reversal options for patients taking factor Xa inhibitors when rapid reversal is needed for life-threatening or critical-organ bleeding or urgent surgery<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>), the 2026 EAU strong recommendation against preoperative bowel preparation before radical cystectomy<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> (24 of 25 cystectomy ERAS protocols reviewed in 2020 omitted oral preparation<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>; overlapping 2019–2020 NSQIP analyses associated oral antibiotics with less sepsis after continent diversion in a 2023 meeting abstract<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup> and bowel regimens overall with shorter neobladder stay but oral antibiotics with longer ileal-conduit stay in a 2025 article<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup>; a 2026 NSQIP cohort associated oral antibiotics with more postoperative ileus after cystectomy<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup>), and mixed alvimopan findings within cystectomy ERAS pathways (a 2021 single-center retrospective cohort associated alvimopan with shorter stay<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup>; a 2026 randomized meeting abstract of 132 patients found similar composite gastrointestinal recovery and median stay with alvimopan and placebo<sup>[[9]](#ref9)</sup>).
