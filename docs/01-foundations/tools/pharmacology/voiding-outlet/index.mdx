# Voiding & Outlet

Pharmacologic therapy targeting voiding-phase dysfunction and outlet obstruction — α-blockers and 5α-reductase inhibitors for BPH, α-agonists and cholinergic agonists for underactive bladder, and skeletal muscle relaxants (including dantrolene) for dyssynergic voiding and pelvic floor hypertonicity.
