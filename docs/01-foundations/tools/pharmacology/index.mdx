# Pharmacology

A drug-class reference for the reconstructive urologist and urogynecologist — organized by **functional indication** rather than by chemical class. Each drug class page covers mechanism, FDA-approved agents, dosing, clinical efficacy, adverse effects, special populations, and contemporary clinical positioning.

For intraoperative materials (ICG, methylene blue, fibrin glue, hemostatics) see the companion [Intraoperative Adjuncts](/docs/foundations/tools/intraoperative-adjuncts) section under Tools. For implanted devices see [Biomaterials](/docs/foundations/tools/biomaterials).

## Out of Scope

This pharmacology section does **not** cover:

- **Chemotherapy and immunotherapy** for urologic malignancy — out of WARWIKI scope
- **Androgen deprivation therapy** for prostate cancer — out of scope (see Sexual Medicine for TRT)
- **Medical expulsive therapy for stones** — stone disease is outside the reconstructive / functional scope

## Structure and Development Status

This database is being built **structure-first** — each drug class has a dedicated page with the WARWIKI article template (overview, mechanism, agents, indications, dosing, contraindications, perioperative considerations, evidence, pearls, related articles, references). Pages are filled out progressively as content is reviewed and ratified.

**Currently most-built:** [Storage & OAB](/docs/foundations/tools/pharmacology/storage-oab) — full articles on anticholinergics, β3-agonists, desmopressin.

**All other categories:** structure established with stub pages; content development in progress.
