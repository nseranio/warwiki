# Pharmacology

A drug-class reference for the reconstructive urologist and urogynecologist — organized by **functional indication** rather than by chemical class. Each drug class page covers mechanism, FDA-approved agents, dosing, clinical efficacy, adverse effects, special populations, and contemporary clinical positioning.

The focus is **systemic pharmacotherapy** relevant to functional and reconstructive practice. Perioperative antibiotic prophylaxis lives in [Perioperative Care](/docs/foundations/perioperative-care). Intraoperative agents (ICG, methylene blue, fibrin glue, hemostatics) live in [Intraoperative Adjuncts](/docs/foundations/tools/intraoperative-adjuncts). Biomaterials and implanted devices live in [Biomaterials](/docs/foundations/tools/biomaterials).

## Drug-Class Quick Reference

| Class | Primary indication | Core mechanism | Key agents |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Anticholinergics / antimuscarinics** | OAB, NLUTD | M3 (±M2) blockade → detrusor relaxation | Oxybutynin, tolterodine, solifenacin, darifenacin, fesoterodine, trospium |
| **β3-adrenergic agonists** | OAB, NLUTD | β3 stimulation → detrusor relaxation | Mirabegron, vibegron |
| **Desmopressin** | Nocturnal polyuria, nocturia | V2 receptor → water reabsorption | Desmopressin |
| **α1-blockers** | BPH/LUTS, MET for stones | Prostatic/bladder-neck smooth muscle relaxation | Tamsulosin, silodosin, alfuzosin, doxazosin, terazosin |
| **5α-reductase inhibitors** | BPH with prostate &gt;30 g | T → DHT inhibition → prostate shrinkage | Finasteride, dutasteride |
| **PDE5 inhibitors (ED)** | ED | cGMP-mediated cavernosal smooth muscle relaxation | Sildenafil, tadalafil, vardenafil, avanafil |
| **PDE5i for BPH** | Men with both ED + BPH/LUTS | Low-dose daily cGMP effect on prostate/bladder-neck | Tadalafil 5 mg daily |
| **Intracavernosal injection** | ED refractory to oral therapy | Direct cavernosal vasodilation | Alprostadil, bimix, trimix, quadmix |
| **Intralesional collagenase** | Peyronie's disease with curvature 30–90° | Collagenase-mediated plaque remodeling | CCH (Xiaflex) |
| **Tricyclic antidepressants** | CPPS, IC/BPS, neuropathic pelvic pain | Multifactorial — anticholinergic, serotonergic, analgesic | Amitriptyline, nortriptyline |
| **Gabapentinoids** | Neuropathic pelvic pain, CPPS | α2δ calcium channel modulation | Gabapentin, pregabalin |
| **Pentosan polysulfate** | IC/BPS | Glycosaminoglycan layer replenishment | Elmiron |
| **Intravesical therapies** | IC/BPS, refractory OAB | Local mucosal effect | DMSO, heparin, lidocaine |
| **Testosterone** | Hypogonadism | Androgen replacement | TRT (gel, IM, pellets) |

## Out of Scope

This pharmacology section does **not** cover:

- **Chemotherapy and immunotherapy** for urologic malignancy — out of WARWIKI scope (oncology focus)
- **Androgen deprivation therapy** for prostate cancer — out of scope
- **Antibiotics** for primary infection treatment — covered under [Perioperative Care](/docs/foundations/perioperative-care) and condition-specific articles
- **Medical expulsive therapy for stones** — stone disease is outside the reconstructive / functional scope
- **Hormonal therapy for gender-affirming care** — see the [Gender-Affirming Surgery](/docs/surgical-techniques) section
