# Storage &amp; Overactive Bladder Pharmacotherapy

Pharmacologic therapy for **overactive bladder (OAB)** and **neurogenic lower urinary tract dysfunction (NLUTD)** works through two complementary mechanisms at the detrusor: **blocking cholinergic contraction** (antimuscarinics) or **stimulating sympathetic relaxation** (β3-agonists). A third distinct agent — **desmopressin** — addresses nocturnal polyuria rather than storage per se.

This subcategory covers the three drug classes relevant to storage-phase pharmacotherapy. For the full clinical framework see the [AUA/SUFU OAB guideline](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38568747/) and the [OAB clinical-condition article](/docs/clinical-conditions/03a-storage-incontinence/sui-female).

## Contemporary Stepped Therapy (AUA/SUFU 2024)

1. **Behavioral therapy** (pelvic-floor muscle training, fluid management, bladder retraining) → first-line for all OAB patients
2. **Pharmacotherapy:** β3-agonist **OR** antimuscarinic (the 2024 AUA guideline slightly favors β3-agonists due to better tolerability and absence of dementia association)
3. **Combination therapy** (antimuscarinic + β3-agonist) for suboptimal response to monotherapy
4. **Third-line:** intradetrusor onabotulinumtoxinA, sacral neuromodulation, posterior tibial nerve stimulation
