# Gear

**Gear** is the equipment the surgeon wears or uses on their own body during surgery, as distinct from the instruments on the tray: optical aids, radiation protection and ergonomic supports. Perineal urethroplasty and complex reconstruction routinely run 4 to 8 hours, so physical load accumulates over a career. Selection reflects the procedure, individual fit and the operating environment.
