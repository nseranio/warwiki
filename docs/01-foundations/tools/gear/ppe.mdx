# Personal Protective Equipment

Standard OR PPE, with a few reconstructive-specific notes.

---

## Gowns

- **AAMI Level 2–3 sterile gowns** are the routine standard for reconstructive cases
- **AAMI Level 4** (fluid-impervious) for high-fluid cases — complex Fournier's debridement, TURP, bladder augmentation with heavy irrigation
- **Reinforced chest and sleeves** — critical for cases with anticipated irrigation splash (PCNL, heavy cystoscopic work)
- **Single-use vs reusable** — institutional choice; reusable requires careful laundering

---

## Double Gloves

**Standard for reconstruction** — the puncture and fluid-barrier protection is meaningful over a career:

- **Indicator system** — colored inner glove shows through when the outer glove is breached
- **Brands:** Ansell Gammex, Biogel Puncture Indication
- Some surgeons prefer **size-up inner, size-down outer** for better sensation; others the opposite. Experiment.

---

## Eye / Face Protection

- **Side-shielded glasses** for any case with irrigation or cautery splatter
- **Full face shield** for cases with expected heavy splatter (TURP, PCNL) or aerosol generation (laser procedures)
- **Radiation glasses do double duty** as splatter glasses when lead-acrylic is worn — see [Radiation Protection](/docs/foundations/tools/gear/radiation-protection)

---

## Masks

- **Standard surgical mask** — default for all reconstructive cases
- **N95 (fit-tested)** — for airborne-isolation cases, respiratory pandemic posture, or smoke-heavy laser plume when paired with ventilated mask
- **Laser-grade mask** — for holmium, thulium, CO₂, KTP laser procedures. Surgical masks do not filter laser plume nanoparticles; specific sub-0.1 μm filtering masks are required. Many laser societies and the OSHA general duty clause effectively mandate laser-grade masks plus local smoke evacuation.
