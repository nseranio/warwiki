# Technology

The hardware and energy platforms of contemporary functional and reconstructive urology — **robotics platforms** (da Vinci, Hugo, Versius, Senhance, Avatera, Hinotori, REVO-I, KangDuo, MicroHand S, Toumai, Dexter) and their reconstructive applications, single-port anatomy, and the **energy modalities** (electrosurgery, lasers, ultrasonic, vessel sealing, aquablation, morcellation) that drive modern operative technique.
