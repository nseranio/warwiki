# Porcine Small Intestinal Submucosa (SIS)

**Porcine small intestinal submucosa** (e.g., **Surgisis**, **Stratasis**) is a decellularized ECM derived from porcine small bowel. It was applied in urogynecologic prolapse and sling surgery in the 1990s–2000s.

## Composition

- Acellular extracellular matrix of porcine jejunal submucosa
- Collagen-rich with preserved glycosaminoglycans and growth factors
- Designed to serve as a resorbable remodeling scaffold

## Evidence &amp; Current Status

- No consistent advantage over native-tissue repair for prolapse
- Inconsistent tissue incorporation; unpredictable remodeling
- **Most products have been withdrawn** from urogynecologic use
- Persisting applications in general surgery (hernia repair) where context differs
