# UroLift — Prostatic Urethral Lift

**UroLift** mechanically retracts obstructing prostate tissue with permanent implants, without resecting or thermally ablating it. Each implant has a nitinol capsular tab, a stainless-steel urethral end-piece, and a connecting PET monofilament suture. The capsular tab is nitinol and the urethral end-piece is stainless steel.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

For selection and technique, see [Prostatic Urethral Lift](/docs/surgical-techniques/bph-male-luts/urolift-pul).

## Device and Labeled Selection

The checked US UroLift 2 instructions indicate treatment in men 45 years or older, including lateral- and median-lobe hyperplasia, and contraindicate use when prostate volume exceeds 100 cc. An obstructing median lobe is therefore not a labeled contraindication. The 2026 AUA guideline is narrower: clinicians should offer PUL for prostates of 30-80 cc without an obstructing median lobe (Moderate Recommendation; Evidence Level B). It also directs counseling that the permanent tines may affect future procedures and prostate imaging (Expert Opinion).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup><sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

Other listed contraindications include UTI, urethral conditions preventing delivery-system insertion, incontinence due to an incompetent sphincter, and current gross hematuria. Material allergy, the patient's anatomy and prior procedures are checked against the full instructions.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

The UroLift 2 handle is for one patient and is validated for up to eight implant-cartridge deployments; each cartridge is single use. Implant number depends on anatomy and the opening required. The instructions describe an average of four to six implants and a recommended maximum of ten per patient, which may require a second handle.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Evidence and Reconstructive Relevance

The L.I.F.T. trial randomized 206 participants and showed greater symptom improvement than sham at three months, with longer follow-up of treated patients.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> MedLift was a separate non-randomized cohort of 45 men with an obstructive median lobe, extended from L.I.F.T. under the same FDA investigational device exemption; it reported IPSS improvement of at least 13.5 points through 12 months.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup> Sexual function was preserved on average in these studies, but individual men can develop sexual symptoms, and retreatment occurs.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

Misplaced, exposed or loose components can require removal and may lead to encrustation, stones, bleeding or persistent symptoms. Implants must be accounted for in later endoscopic procedures. The implanted components are MR Conditional, with model-specific field, gradient and RF limits, and MRI artifact can affect prostate assessment. The delivery system is not taken into an MRI environment.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
