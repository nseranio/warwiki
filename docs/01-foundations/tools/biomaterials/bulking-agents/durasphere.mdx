# Durasphere — Carbon-Coated Zirconium Beads

**Durasphere** is a suspension of **carbon-coated zirconium oxide beads** in a water-based gel carrier — a bulking agent for female SUI.

## Composition

- Pyrolytic carbon-coated zirconium dioxide beads (~250–300 μm)
- Suspended in β-glucan carrier
- Radiopaque — visible on imaging

## Key Concerns

Two issues have limited its contemporary use:<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

- **Migration to regional (pelvic and inguinal) lymph nodes** — reported in multiple series; of unclear long-term significance but a clear marker of particle escape from the injection site
- **De novo urgency: 24.7%** — notably higher than competing agents

## Current Use

Largely supplanted by lower-migration, lower-urgency agents ([Bulkamid](/docs/foundations/tools/biomaterials/bulking-agents/bulkamid), [Macroplastique](/docs/foundations/tools/biomaterials/bulking-agents/macroplastique)).
