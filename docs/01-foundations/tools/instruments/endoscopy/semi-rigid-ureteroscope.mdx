# Semi-Rigid Ureteroscope

A semi-rigid ureteroscope is an upper-tract endoscope used for ureteral access. Scope characteristics, access strategy, accessories, and whether another modality is appropriate depend on the anatomy, indication, and clinical setting.

## Evidence orientation

The prospective CROES Ureteroscopy Global Study reported treatment patterns, outcomes, and complications across varied practice settings. In its location-focused analysis, semirigid ureteroscopy was commonly used for ureteral stones, while the study also documented limitations of its observational, nonuniform treatment approach.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

The inherited page included detailed scope dimensions, instructions, treatment criteria, energy settings, dilation/stenting rules, comparative outcome estimates, and complication rates. They were not re-adjudicated against complete device, guideline, and primary source packages in this review and were removed.
