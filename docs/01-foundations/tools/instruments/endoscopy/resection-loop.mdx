# Resection Loop

A resection loop is an electrosurgical electrode used with a resectoscope for transurethral tissue resection. Loop design and energy platform vary by system and clinical application; their safe use depends on the complete device instructions, procedure, and operating environment.

## Evidence orientation

Loop-based resection is part of both monopolar and bipolar resectoscope systems. The clinical evidence comparing those systems for benign prostatic obstruction is synthesized in a Cochrane review, but that evidence does not by itself establish a preferred loop, power setting, or technique for every indication.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

The inherited article contained product-specific dimensions, materials, power settings, bench-testing results, tumor-resection claims, outcome estimates, and practical rules. These details were not verified against complete primary source and device-documentation packages in this review and were removed.

## Evidence boundary

The inherited page and the cited PubMed record/abstract were read. Full device instructions, the Cochrane full text and appendices, current disease-specific guidelines, and the inherited primary source packages were not adjudicated. This is not an electrosurgical-setting guide, a TURP/TURBT protocol, or a clinical verification.
