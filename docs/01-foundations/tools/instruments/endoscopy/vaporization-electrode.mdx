# Vaporization Electrode

A vaporization electrode is a resectoscope electrode intended to ablate rather than conventionally resect tissue. Product design, energy delivery, tissue effect, and whether a specimen is obtained differ across platforms and procedures.

## Evidence orientation

This is an equipment overview, not a treatment comparison. A systematic review comparing monopolar and bipolar transurethral resection for benign prostatic obstruction provides context for the broader energy-platform question, but it does not validate a specific vaporization electrode, energy setting, or patient-selection rule.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

The inherited article included detailed device geometry, power and drag settings, pathology assertions, comparative outcomes, and a patient-selection framework. They were not confirmed against complete current source, device, and guideline packages in this review and have been removed.
