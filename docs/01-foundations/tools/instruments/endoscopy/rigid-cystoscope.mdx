# Rigid Cystoscope

A rigid cystoscope is a modular lower-urinary-tract endoscope used for diagnostic and operative cystoscopy. The telescope, sheath, bridge, and available working channels vary by system and determine the accessories that may be used.

## Evidence orientation

Rigid and flexible cystoscopy serve overlapping diagnostic purposes, while a rigid system can also support selected operative access. A systematic review and meta-analysis found substantial heterogeneity in pain comparisons between flexible and rigid office cystoscopy and did not establish a universal comfort advantage.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
