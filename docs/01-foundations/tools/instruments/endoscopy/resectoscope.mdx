# Resectoscope

A resectoscope is an operative endoscopic platform that couples a sheath, telescope, working element, irrigation, and an electrosurgical electrode. It is used in transurethral procedures such as prostate and bladder-tumor resection. Device configuration, energy system, indication, and operative steps require procedure-specific planning.

## Evidence orientation

The key clinical distinction is between monopolar and bipolar transurethral resection systems. A Cochrane review compared the two approaches for lower urinary tract symptoms attributed to benign prostatic obstruction; its findings belong in the relevant BPH and procedural decision pages, not as a universal equipment-selection rule.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

The inherited article included detailed dimensions, energy settings, selection thresholds, outcome estimates, complication rates, tumor-resection recommendations, and a procedural sequence. Those claims were not re-adjudicated against current full guideline and source packages in this instrument review and have been removed.
