# Dermatome — Overview

A dermatome is an instrument used to harvest split-thickness skin grafts. Manual, drum, and powered systems differ in configuration and require device-specific training and instructions.

## Evidence orientation

The inherited article included device comparisons, thickness classifications, donor-site and graft-selection guidance, outcome estimates, calibration claims, and harvesting instructions. These details were not re-adjudicated against complete current source, device, and procedure packages in this review and have been removed.
