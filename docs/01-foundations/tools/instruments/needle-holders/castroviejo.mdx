# Castroviejo Needle Holder

<img src="/img/instruments/castroviejo.jpg" alt="Titanium Castroviejo needle holder with spring-loaded handle and ultra-fine jaws" className="instrument-photo" />
*Castroviejo needle holder — spring-loaded pencil-grip handle with ultra-fine jaws. (Image: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Titanium_Castroveijo_Needle_holders.jpg) / Bszsurgico, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).)*

The **Castroviejo-pattern needle holder** is a spring-handle microsurgical instrument named for ophthalmic surgeon **Ramón Castroviejo**. Current catalog models vary in jaw shape and surface, length, and locking mechanism; choose the specific model for the needle rather than assuming every Castroviejo fits one suture range.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup> Fine-needle work in reconstructive urology includes microsurgical vasovasostomy and vasoepididymostomy, where 9-0 and 10-0 sutures are described.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

## Design

- **Spring/flat or round handles** — no finger rings; some models have a lock and others do not
- **Fine straight or curved jaws** — smooth, serrated, tungsten-carbide or coated variants, with model-specific needle compatibility
- **Microsurgical control** — selected for delicate needle handling under loupes or a microscope; a named pattern is not a substitute for matching jaw size and surface to the actual needle<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Key Uses

- Microsurgical vasovasostomy and vasoepididymostomy when placing appropriately fine sutures<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
- Selected fine urethral or vascular suturing when the needle and access suit the specific holder; this page does not establish a routine role in every urethroplasty
- Other testicular microsurgery **when suturing is needed**; microdissection TESE itself does not require a needle holder merely because a microscope is used

See also: [Ryder](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/needle-holders/ryder).
