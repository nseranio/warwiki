# Castroviejo Needle Holder

![Titanium Castroviejo needle holder with spring-loaded handle and ultra-fine jaws](/img/instruments/castroviejo.jpg)
*Castroviejo needle holder — spring-loaded pencil-grip handle with ultra-fine jaws. (Image: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Titanium_Castroveijo_Needle_holders.jpg) / Bszsurgico, [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).)*

Micro needle holder with **spring-loaded handles** (no ring closures) and **ultra-fine jaws** — designed by **Ramón Castroviejo** (1904–1987, New York / Madrid) for ophthalmic microsurgery. In reconstructive urology it is used for **microsurgical vasovasostomy, vasoepididymostomy, and fine urethral work** with 8-0 to 10-0 suture.

## Design

- **Spring-loaded pistol-grip handle** — held like a pencil, thumb-activated
- **Ultra-fine jaws** — grasp 8-0 to 10-0 needles
- **Fine precision** — designed for operating-microscope field

## Key Uses

- Microsurgical vasovasostomy
- Microsurgical vasoepididymostomy
- Fine urethral reconstruction
- Testicular microsurgery (microdissection TESE)
