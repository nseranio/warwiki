# Frazier Suction Tip

The **Frazier suction tip** is a narrow suction instrument used for controlled aspiration in confined or delicate operative fields. Vented and non-vented, rigid, malleable and soft-tip models exist; select the actual model and suction setting for the tissue and procedure rather than treating “Frazier” as one universal configuration.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup> It is a useful alternative to the broader [Yankauer](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/suction/yankauer) when direct visualization and fine tip positioning matter.

## Design

- **Narrow shaft and small working tip**; geometry and material vary by product. CONMED's single-use range includes standard, Baron Frazier and 10 Fr soft-tip variants.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup>
- **Finger vent on many, but not all, models**. The vent modulates aspiration at the handle; the 18 Fr CONMED Frazier listed in its catalog is non-vented.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup>
- **Model-specific sizes.** CONMED lists 3, 5, 7, 8, 10, 12 and 18 Fr variants; these are not a procedure-by-size prescription.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup>
- **Obturator/stylet** supplied with the cited single-use range for shaping and clearing obstructions; follow its instructions and keep the tip away from tissue during clearing.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup>

## Why Thumb-Controlled Suction Matters

Constant high-volume suction in a delicate reconstructive field risks **avulsing or injuring**:

- The urethral plate during urethroplasty
- The buccal mucosa graft bed on the urethrotomy edge
- The vas mucosa during vasovasostomy
- The cavernous nerves during nerve-sparing dissection
- The dorsal NVB during corporal-sparing work
- Microvascular pedicles during flap dissection

On a vented model, uncovering the finger port reduces suction at the tip. The vent is not a substitute for choosing an appropriate regulated suction source, and some [Yankauer](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/suction/yankauer) models also have vents or on/off controls.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)[[7]](#ref7)</sup>

## Reconstructive / Functional Urology Uses

- **Anterior urethroplasty** — bulbar, penile, BMG onlay / inlay; precision suction at the dorsal / ventral graft inset
- **Posterior anastomotic urethroplasty** — deep perineal dissection where Yankauer bore is incompatible with the corridor
- **Microsurgical procedures** — **vasovasostomy, vasoepididymostomy, microsurgical TESE**, varicocelectomy — a suitably small, controlled suction tip may help keep the field clear; avoid contact with the anastomosis or delicate tissue
- **Hypospadias and pediatric urology** — small operative field with a small caliber requirement
- **Phalloplasty pedicle dissection** — radial forearm / ALT / SCIP flap pedicle skeletonization
- **Vaginoplasty / GAS** — labial / clitoral / Foldès dissection
- **Penile prosthesis revision** — corporal-fossa work near the cylinder
- **Female urethroplasty + diverticulum repair** — narrow introital corridor through the [Ravini speculum](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/ravini)
- **Sacrocolpopexy and ureteral dissection** — peri-ureteral and peri-NVB dissection where bipolar safety is matched with Frazier precision
- **Peyronie's plication / grafting** — tunica precision work along the NVB

For broad-field operative suction, switch to a [Yankauer](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/suction/yankauer); for high-volume abdominal evacuation, switch to a [Poole](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/suction/poole).

## Size selection

Choose caliber by working-space width, expected fluid/debris, tip visibility and the risk of tissue capture. A small lumen gives precise access but clogs more readily; a larger one clears more fluid but demands greater distance from vulnerable structures. The 3–18 Fr range above describes one current manufacturer's available models, **not** evidence that a particular French size is optimal for a named urologic operation.<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup> An endoscopic septoplasty study used a suction tube as a dissection adjunct, but its operative-time findings do not establish a benefit in urethroplasty or microsurgery.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

## Technique Pearls

- **Lift suction, don't push** — the Frazier is for clearing the field, not retracting tissue
- **Stylet within reach** at all times — sudden clot occlusion is the most common intraoperative interruption
- **Modulate via the thumb-vent when present**, while also setting the suction source appropriately for delicate tissue and the selected tubing
- **Match caliber to corridor** — too-small clogs constantly, too-large injures
- **Saline-flush the lumen between activations** in microsurgical fields to prevent dried-blood occlusion

## Limitations

- **Low volumetric capacity** — not for high-volume operative bleeding; escalate to [Yankauer](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/suction/yankauer) or [Poole](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/suction/poole)
- **Clogs on particulate** — clot, tissue debris, dried blood require stylet clearance
- **Rigid** — not for navigating curved or deep corridors where a flexible suction would adapt
- **Single-axis suction** — the operator must move the field into the tip rather than vice versa

## Historical Context — Charles Harrison Frazier

The pattern carries the name of **Charles Harrison Frazier (1870–1936)**, a neurosurgeon at the University of Pennsylvania. Historical reviews describe the development of suction in neurosurgery, while later technical reports describe modifications and adjustable-pressure concepts.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)[[4]](#ref4)[[5]](#ref5)</sup> These reports do not establish that every present-day tip shares one inventor's original geometry or that a newer design improves urologic outcomes.

## Frazier vs Adjacent Suction Devices

| Device | Bore / tip | Best fit |
|---|---|---|
| **Frazier** | Fine tip; vent and size vary by model | Delicate reconstructive fields and narrow corridors |
| **[Yankauer](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/suction/yankauer)** | Wide-bore bulb tip | Default open-field operative suction |
| **[Poole](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/suction/poole)** | Wide-bore multi-hole rigid | High-volume abdominal evacuation |
| **[Toomey syringe](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/suction/toomey-syringe)** | Manual irrigation-aspiration via catheter | Bladder MBW, catheter-coupled irrigation |
| **[Ellik evacuator](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/suction/ellik-evacuator)** | Manual bulb + gravity trap via cystoscope sheath | Bladder chip / clot evacuation during TUR |
