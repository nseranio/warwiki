# Yankauer Suction

**Rigid oral / field suction** with a curved tip and large bore — the standard for non-critical suction in an open wound.

## Design

- **Rigid plastic or metal tube**
- **Curved distal end** with a small opening at the tip
- **Large-bore** — rapid evacuation
- No thumb vent — constant suction when connected

## Key Uses

- Open wound general suction
- Oropharyngeal suction (original intended use)
- Abdominal-cavity fluid evacuation when a fine tip is not required

## History

Named for **Sidney Yankauer** (1872–1932), American otolaryngologist who designed the suction tube for use in oral and pharyngeal surgery.
