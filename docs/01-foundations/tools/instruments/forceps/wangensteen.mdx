# Wangensteen Tissue Forceps

Wangensteen forceps are toothed spring forceps. Their dimensions, tooth pattern, and intended applications may differ by instrument system and clinical setting.

## Evidence orientation

The historical spring-forceps record is general context only; it does not establish instrument-specific tooth geometry, tissue selection, or procedure technique.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

The inherited page's detailed tooth configuration, comparisons, clinical applications, historical outcome figures, and handling instructions were not re-adjudicated against complete source, product, and procedure packages and have been removed.
