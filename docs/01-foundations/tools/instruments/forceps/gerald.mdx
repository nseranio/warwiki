# Gerald Tissue Forceps

Gerald forceps are fine spring forceps commonly used for delicate handling under magnification. Tip configuration, procedure use, and handling approach depend on the device and clinical context.

## Evidence orientation

The historical spring-forceps review provides general context for this instrument family, not a validated microsurgical technique or tissue-specific recommendation.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

The inherited article's dimensions, smooth-versus-toothed selection rules, microsurgical indications, suture pairings, and technical instructions were not re-adjudicated against complete primary and device packages in this review and have been removed.
