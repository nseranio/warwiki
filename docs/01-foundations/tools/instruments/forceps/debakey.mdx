# DeBakey Tissue Forceps

DeBakey forceps are non-toothed spring forceps with longitudinal grasping serrations, commonly used where a broad, nonpenetrating grasp is desired. Their dimensions and variants vary by manufacturer and intended use.

## Evidence orientation

The laboratory instrument–tissue study cited below found less tissue trauma with wave-pattern than toothed jaws in its experimental model, while also documenting a grip–trauma trade-off. It does not establish patient-level outcomes or a universal forceps choice.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

The inherited page's specific serration geometry, force thresholds, specialty applications, comparative claims, and handling instructions were not verified against complete source, device, and procedure packages in this review and have been removed.
