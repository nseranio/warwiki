# Adson Tissue Forceps

Adson forceps are spring forceps used for superficial tissue handling. They are available in several tip configurations; the appropriate configuration, tissue handling, and use are determined by the operative setting and local practice.

## Evidence orientation

Spring forceps transmit tension from the instrument's bend to its jaws, as described in a historical review of their structure and functions.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> The inherited page's specific dimensions, tissue-by-tissue instructions, closure recommendations, and comparative performance claims were not adjudicated against complete source and device packages and have been removed.
