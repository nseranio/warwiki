# Singley (Singley-Tuttle) Tissue Forceps

Singley forceps are long, spring forceps with fenestrated grasping tips. Their design and appropriate use vary by device and clinical context.

## Evidence orientation

A historical review describes spring-forceps structures and functions generally, but it is not validation of a specific Singley design, tissue application, or operative technique.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

The inherited article included specific dimensions, fenestration mechanics, tissue-handling rules, diversion and reconstruction applications, and technical instructions. These were not re-adjudicated against full source, product, and procedure packages and have been removed.
