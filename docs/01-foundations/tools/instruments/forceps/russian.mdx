# Russian Tissue Forceps

Distinctive **"star-burst" or "saucer" tip** — flat, circular, with multiple small teeth on the working face. Spreads grasping force broadly.

## Design

- **Flat circular tip** with multiple fine teeth around the working face
- **Heavier shaft** than Adson, lighter than Bonney
- **Distributed grip** — less focal crush than traditional toothed forceps

## Key Uses

- Handling **thick, mobile tissue** (muscle, fascia) without slipping
- Fascia retrieval during pubovaginal sling harvest
- Flap handling during perineal reconstruction
- General tissue manipulation where large purchase is needed
