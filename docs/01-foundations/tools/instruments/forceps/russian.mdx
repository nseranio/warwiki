# Russian Tissue Forceps

Russian forceps are spring forceps with broad, serrated grasping surfaces. Product designs and their intended applications vary by manufacturer and clinical setting.

## Evidence orientation

The existing instrument–tissue evidence record describes general trade-offs between grip and tissue trauma in experimental models; it does not establish a procedure-specific use or forceps-selection rule.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

The inherited page's specific tip geometry, dimensions, tissue applications, comparative performance claims, and handling instructions were not re-adjudicated against complete source, device, and procedure packages and have been removed.
