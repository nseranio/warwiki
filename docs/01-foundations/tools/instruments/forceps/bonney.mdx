# Bonney Tissue Forceps

Bonney forceps are spring forceps with toothed jaws typically used where a secure grasp is desired. Their exact form, tissue interaction, and selection relative to other forceps vary by system, procedure, and surgeon.

## Evidence orientation

An in-vitro instrument–tissue study found a trade-off between grip security and tissue trauma across jaw patterns; that experimental finding is not a procedure-specific forceps recommendation.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

The inherited page included detailed sizes, force thresholds, fascial-closure methods, application lists, comparative tables, and handling rules. These claims were not re-adjudicated against complete clinical, device, and guideline packages in this review and have been removed.
