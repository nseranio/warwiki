# Lahey Tissue Forceps

Lahey forceps are ring-handled grasping forceps with toothed jaws, historically associated with thyroid surgery. The exact design and appropriate use vary by instrument and procedure.

## Evidence orientation

The historical review of spring-forceps families provides context for the instrument lineage; it is not a validation of a specific Lahey-forceps design or operative application.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

The inherited page included tooth configuration, clinical application lists, tissue-preservation rules, thyroid technique, oncology claims, and handling instructions. These details were not re-adjudicated against complete primary, device, and guideline packages and have been removed.
