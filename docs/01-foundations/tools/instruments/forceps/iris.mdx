# Iris Tissue Forceps

Iris forceps are small, fine spring forceps originally associated with ophthalmic instrumentation. Related variants are used in diverse minor and delicate procedures.

## Evidence orientation

The cited historical review describes the general structure and functions of spring-forceps families, but does not establish an indication or technique for a particular iris-forceps variant.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

The inherited page included exact dimensions, tissue-specific application lists, distinctions from other forceps, procedural instructions, and clinical recommendations. These were not re-adjudicated against complete source, device, and procedure packages in this review and have been removed.
