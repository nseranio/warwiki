## Historical Notes

Many of these instruments carry the names of defining figures in reconstructive urology and pelvic surgery — [Richard Turner-Warwick](/docs/roots/surgeons/s-z/richard-turner-warwick), Noble Sproat Heaney, [Gerald H. Jordan](/docs/roots/surgeons/h-r/gerald-jordan), Shlomo Raz, Armand Pereyra, Jean Casimir Félix Guyon, Fessenden Nott Otis, Oscar Huntington Allis, William Wayne Babcock, Emil Theodor Kocher, Owen Wangensteen, August Breisky, Michael DeBakey, Ramón Castroviejo, and Leonard Malis. Their contributions live on in the daily work of the reconstructive tray.

See also: [Needles](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/needles), [Sutures](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/sutures), [Surgical Ergonomics](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/surgical-ergonomics), [Robotics](/docs/foundations/tools/technology/robotics).
