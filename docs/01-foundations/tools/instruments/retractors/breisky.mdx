# Breisky Retractor

**Long, narrow, right-angled vaginal retractor** — named for **August Breisky** (1832–1889, Viennese obstetrician/gynecologist).

## Design

- **Elongated narrow blade** — reaches deep vaginal recesses
- **Right-angled handle** perpendicular to the blade
- Available in multiple widths for different exposures

## Key Uses

The Breisky's **narrow elongated profile** makes it the retractor of choice for apical vaginal exposure:
- Uterosacral ligament suspension
- Sacrospinous ligament fixation
- Apical colpopexy
- Deep vaginal reconstruction
- Deep VVF repair where the fistula sits high on the anterior vaginal wall

## History

August Breisky was chair of gynecology at the University of Vienna in the late 19th century and a founding figure in European obstetric and gynecologic surgery. The retractor and the **Breisky-Navratil retractor** (a heavier variant, often called "Breisky") that share his name remain standard in vaginal surgery.
