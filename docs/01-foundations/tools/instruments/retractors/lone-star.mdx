# Lone Star Retractor System

The **Lone Star** (Lone Star Medical Products / CooperSurgical) is a **disposable elastic-stay retractor system** — a flexible plastic ring with a series of small elastic stays (hooks) anchored at any point around the ring to retract tissue edges.

## Design

- **Flexible plastic ring** (available in multiple sizes)
- **Elastic stays with small hooks** — clip to tissue edges
- **Infinitely repositionable** — stays can be moved mid-case without breaking scrub
- **Atraumatic** — elastic tension distributes force over the hook contact point, less crushing than metal blades

## Why It Became the Reconstructive Workhorse

Three advantages drove its adoption across reconstructive urology:
1. **Infinite adjustability** of retraction angles — impossible with traditional bladed retractors
2. **Atraumatic** elastic stays compared with metal retractor blades, critical for delicate tissue (urethral plate, vaginal wall, penile skin)
3. **Intraoperative repositioning** without re-setup when the operative focus shifts

## Key Uses

- Bulbar urethroplasty — perineal skin retraction
- Perineal urethrostomy
- [Vesicovaginal fistula repair](/docs/clinical-conditions/03f-fistulas/in-females/vesicovaginal)
- Vaginal prolapse repair
- Hypospadias repair — delicate penile skin handling
- Phalloplasty harvest and insetting
- Scrotoplasty and perineoplasty
- Any perineal or vaginal reconstruction
