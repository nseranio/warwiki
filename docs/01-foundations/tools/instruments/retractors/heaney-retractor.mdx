# Heaney Retractor

**Right-angled vaginal retractor** — flat blade with a handle perpendicular to the blade.

## Design

- **Flat rectangular blade** (various widths and lengths)
- **Handle at 90° to the blade** — allows traction without obscuring the surgeon's field
- **Non-self-retaining** — held by the assistant

## Key Uses

- Retracting the anterior or posterior vaginal wall during vaginal surgery
- Bladder retraction during VVF repair
- Rectum retraction during rectovaginal fistula repair
- General vaginal wall retraction in urogynecologic operations

Designed by **Noble Sproat Heaney** (1880–1955) alongside his needle driver for vaginal hysterectomy. See the [Heaney Needle Driver](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/needle-holders/heaney) page for historical context.
