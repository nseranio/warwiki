# Gelman Urethral Sound

**Contemporary reconstructive urethral sound** — graduated sizes, designed for controlled urethral instrumentation in a reconstructive setting.

The Gelman sound belongs with the modern urethral sounds used for calibration, gentle dilation, and intraoperative assessment of urethral caliber. A fuller instrument profile should distinguish its curve, tip design, and handling characteristics from the Van Buren, Guyon, and Haygrove sound families.

See also: [Van Buren Sound](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/sounds-bougies/van-buren), [Haygrove Sound](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/sounds-bougies/haygrove), [Guyon Sound](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/sounds-bougies/guyon).
