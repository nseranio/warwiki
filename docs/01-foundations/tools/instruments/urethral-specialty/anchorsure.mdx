# Anchorsure® System

The **Anchorsure® System** (Neomedic International, distributed in the UK/Europe by JUNE Medical) is a **PEEK-anchor-based sacrospinous ligament fixation (SSLF) device** — a fundamentally different mechanism from suture-capturing devices like the [Capio](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/capio-device). Rather than passing a suture through the ligament, the Anchorsure **embeds a small biocompatible PEEK anchor into the ligament substance**, to which the apical-support suture is pre-attached.

## Design

- **Straight, narrow delivery device** for atraumatic transvaginal introduction
- **PEEK (polyetheretherketone) anchor** — 100% biocompatible polymer, permanently implanted
- **Pre-attached suture** on the anchor
- **Tactile feedback** during ligament engagement — the device signals correct anchor seating

## Rationale — Anchor vs Suture Capture

Traditional suture-based SSLF requires **tight suture knots around or through the ligament**, which may:
- Compress or compromise neurovascular structures (pudendal nerve, inferior gluteal vessels)
- Cause gluteal / buttock pain (a known complication of SSLF)
- Depend on ligament tissue quality to hold the knot

The anchor-based approach **embeds fixation within the ligament substance** rather than encircling it — theoretically reducing the risk of neurovascular compression and related pain.

## Evidence

The **Anchorsure vs Capio RCT** ([NCT03565640](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03565640)) directly compared the two devices for postoperative pain. Reported outcomes align with the underlying hypothesis — anchor-based fixation may reduce the characteristic gluteal pain of SSLF.

## Regulatory / Commercial Status

- **FDA-approved** in the United States
- **CE-marked** in Europe
- Commonly distributed alongside the Uplift SCP mesh for laparoscopic prolapse repair

## Use

- **Vaginal vault prolapse after hysterectomy** (apical suspension)
- **Uterovaginal prolapse** with sacrospinous fixation (Amreich–Richter technique)
- **Post-hysterectomy vault prolapse** repair
