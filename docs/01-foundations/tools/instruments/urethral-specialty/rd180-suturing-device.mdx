# RD180 Suturing Device

The **RD180** (LSI Solutions, Victor, NY) is a **minimally invasive suturing device** that delivers a pre-loaded suture through tissue using a **squeeze-and-release trigger mechanism** with **no exposed sharps**. Originally developed for laparoscopic gynecologic, general, and colorectal surgery, it has been adopted for **endoscopic urethroplasty (TUITMR)** through the [JNW UrTrac sheath](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/jnw-urtrac-sheath).

## Design

- **Elongated shaft** with a curved distal working end
- **Pre-loaded 2-0 Monoglide** (or equivalent) suture
- **Squeeze-trigger** passes the needle through tissue
- **Release mechanism** retrieves the needle and delivers both suture tails to the surgeon
- **No exposed sharps** — the needle is housed within the device when not actively passing tissue
- **180° articulation** (hence "RD180") allows multi-angle suture placement

## Use in TUITMR Endoscopic Urethroplasty

During the procedure:

1. [**JNW UrTrac sheath**](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/jnw-urtrac-sheath) provides endoscopic access to the bladder neck / VUA
2. **RD180 is passed through the UrTrac** with a preloaded 2-0 Monoglide suture
3. Under endoscopic visualization with a flexible ureteroscope, the RD180 is positioned to pass the needle **through the stenotic scar tissue and adjacent healthy bladder mucosa**
4. Squeeze-release delivers the suture through the tissue
5. Multiple sutures are placed to **advance healthy mucosa across the stenotic segment**
6. Sutures are secured with the [**Ti-Knot fastener**](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/ti-knot-fastener)

## Advantages in Endoscopic Urethroplasty

- **Safer than free-needle endoscopic suturing** — no loose needle in the working channel
- **Reproducible suture placement** — squeeze-release eliminates technique-dependent needle handling
- **Compatible with existing laparoscopic infrastructure** — teams familiar with the device from gynecologic / colorectal applications can rapidly adopt it for TUITMR

## Other Applications

The RD180 family is used in:
- **Laparoscopic gynecologic surgery** — vaginal cuff closure, uterosacral ligament suspension
- **Laparoscopic colorectal surgery** — mesocolon closure, rectal mobilization
- **General surgery** — reinforcement and closure in minimally invasive cases
