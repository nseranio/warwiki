# RD180 Suturing Device

The **RD180** (LSI Solutions, Victor, NY) is a minimally invasive suturing device with a lever-operated, normally retracted needle. Its 5-mm version has been described for endoscopic [transurethral incision with transverse mucosal realignment (TUITMR)](/docs/surgical-techniques/04ab-bladder-neck-reconstruction/bnc-vuas/transurethral-incision-transverse-mucosal-realignment) through the [JNW UrTrac sheath](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/jnw-urtrac-sheath). This is a technical application, not evidence that the device improves urethroplasty outcomes over other suturing methods.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Design

- The 5-mm version has a 33-cm shaft; 10-mm and SP variants exist. Match the actual model, port/sheath, and current IFU.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
- Use the specified **RD QUICK LOAD** suture, such as compatible 2-0 MONOGLIDE, rather than an assumed interchangeable suture.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
- One squeeze–release passes the needle; after clearing tissue, a **second squeeze–release** remotely rearms it for a subsequent bite. The 5-mm IFU permits up to 12 bites per quick load and 12 quick loads per device.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
- The needle retracts when the lever is not squeezed, but is exposed during actuation. Avoid squeezing against dense tissue or a misaligned device; inspect function and account for any needle damage.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Use in [TUITMR](/docs/surgical-techniques/04ab-bladder-neck-reconstruction/bnc-vuas/transurethral-incision-transverse-mucosal-realignment) Endoscopic Urethroplasty

During the procedure:

1. [**JNW UrTrac sheath**](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/jnw-urtrac-sheath) provides endoscopic access to the bladder neck / VUA
2. **The model-compatible RD180 and quick load** are passed through the UrTrac in the described technique
3. Under endoscopic visualization, take bites at the **healthy mucosa–resection-bed junction**, minimizing incorporation of the scar bed rather than deliberately sewing through dense scar<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
4. Squeeze–release passes the needle; clear tissue and rearm as directed in the model's IFU before another bite<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
5. Place sutures to **realign healthy mucosa transversely** across the treated segment<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
6. Sutures are secured with the [**Ti-Knot fastener**](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/ti-knot-fastener)

## Practical Considerations

- A normally retracted needle can simplify handling in a restricted channel; passage still requires visualization, appropriate tissue capture, and attention to sharps during actuation. Comparative urethral safety or success superiority has not been established by the cited technical report.
- Use only compatible quick loads; do not reuse or resterilize the sterile single-use device. The IFU warns that exposed suture or retained foreign material in urine may form calculi.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
- LSI's **September 17, 2026 product-revision notice** says the 5-mm RD180 (SKU 021100) is being revised as **RD360 Plus** with a mode indicator and rotatable SPINI tip; it does not establish a recall or that every existing RD180 has the new mechanism. Confirm the supplied model and its own IFU before use.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

## Other Applications

The family is marketed for soft-tissue approximation in minimally invasive surgery, including gynecologic, colorectal, and general-surgical applications; case-specific indications and compatible suture differ by model.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> A separate posterior-substitution-urethroplasty technique also describes RD180 as an alternative for very deep proximal apical sutures.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>
