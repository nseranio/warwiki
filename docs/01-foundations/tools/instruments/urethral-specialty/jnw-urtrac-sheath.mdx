# JNW UrTrac Sheath

The **JNW UrTrac®** (LSI Solutions, Victor, NY) is a 26 Fr dual-lumen transurethral cannula used in transurethral incision with transverse mucosal realignment (**TUITMR**) for vesicourethral anastomotic stenosis and bladder-neck contracture. Separate ports accommodate a 3 mm scope and 5 mm instrument; the device provides access and irrigation, while companion instruments place and secure sutures.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

## Design

- **Dual obturator** for initial cannula placement, optionally over a guidewire through the obturator's guidewire channel. The IFU advises standard dilation technique and lubrication to ease insertion.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
- **Rotational seal and scope lock** accommodate a 3 mm scope and 5 mm device separately; rotating the seal closed reduces fluid loss through the 5 mm port but blocks passage of an instrument through the lower lumen, so open it before passing the instrument.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> There is no pneumoperitoneum in this transurethral procedure.
- **Irrigation and drainage connectors** and a separate instrument port permit visualization alongside the [RD180 suturing device](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/rd180-suturing-device) and [Ti-Knot fastener](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/ti-knot-fastener).<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Use in Endoscopic Urethroplasty

In the TUITMR technique:

1. Introduce the cannula with the dual obturator, with a guidewire if indicated; remove the obturator and guidewire after positioning.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
2. Seat the scope lock and rotational seal; introduce the appropriately sized scope through its dedicated port and connect irrigation or drainage.
3. After scar incision, use the separate instrument port for [RD180](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/rd180-suturing-device) to advance and appose healthy mucosa across the incised stenosis; ideally the needle enters bladder mucosa proximal to the resection bed, and the distal bite takes as little of the resection bed as possible, giving mucosa-to-mucosa apposition.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
4. Secure sutures with [Ti-Knot](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/ti-knot-fastener) fasteners as described in the technique report.

The manufacturer specifies sterile single-patient use, without reuse or resterilization. Do not force a scope or instrument through a resistant port, avoid contact between the noninsulated cannula and electrosurgical equipment, and observe the IFU's irrigation-fluid absorption and electrosurgery-medium precautions.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Clinical Context — TUITMR

**Transurethral Incision with Transverse Mucosal Realignment (TUITMR)** incises scar and apposes healthy mucosa across the stenotic segment. Abramowitz et al.'s retrospective series of 19 patients with bladder-neck contracture or vesicourethral anastomotic stenosis used a 26 Fr continuous-flow cystourethroscope sheath, then an offset scope, rather than the later JNW UrTrac platform. At median six-month follow-up, 17/19 (89%) had cystoscopic patency after one treatment and 19/19 after up to two; patency meant passage of a 17 Fr flexible cystoscope at four months.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup> No de novo incontinence was observed.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup> In a later registry series of 92 men with bladder-neck contracture after BPH surgery (median follow-up 15.9 months), freedom from reintervention after one TUITMR was 83.7% and anatomic success after up to two procedures 96.7%; no patient had de novo incontinence, and 12% had complications, mostly urinary tract infections.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup> Dash and Warner (2025) described the UrTrac step: after scar resection the sheath is passed with its obturator, a flexible digital ureteroscope goes down the visual port, and the RD180 with preloaded 2-0 suture places bites that TK Knot fasteners secure, advancing bladder mucosa across the resection bed.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Platform

The JNW UrTrac is part of an LSI Solutions ecosystem in which the UrTrac provides access, the [RD180](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/rd180-suturing-device) places sutures and the [Ti-Knot (TK)](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/ti-knot-fastener) secures them, designed to perform complete endoscopic urethroplasty without external abdominal or perineal incisions.
