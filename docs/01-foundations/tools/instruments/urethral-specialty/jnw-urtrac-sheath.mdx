# JNW UrTrac Sheath

The **JNW UrTrac®** (LSI Solutions, Victor, NY) is a **26 Fr dual-lumen transurethral cannula** used in transurethral incision with transverse mucosal realignment (**TUITMR**) for **vesicourethral anastomotic stenosis** and **bladder-neck contracture**. Separate ports accommodate a **3 mm scope** and **5 mm instrument**; the device provides access and irrigation, while companion instruments place and secure sutures.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Design

- **Dual obturator** for initial cannula placement, optionally over a guidewire; do not force passage through a tight stricture.
- **Rotational seal and scope lock** accommodate a 3 mm scope and 5 mm device separately; the seal **minimizes**, rather than eliminates, irrigation-fluid loss during exchanges. There is no pneumoperitoneum in this transurethral procedure.
- **Irrigation and drainage connectors** and a separate instrument port permit visualization alongside the [RD180 suturing device](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/rd180-suturing-device) and [Ti-Knot fastener](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/ti-knot-fastener).<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Use in Endoscopic Urethroplasty

In the TUITMR technique:

1. Introduce the cannula **with the dual obturator**, with a guidewire if indicated; remove the obturator after positioning.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
2. Seat the **scope lock and rotational seal**; introduce the appropriately sized scope through its dedicated port and connect irrigation/drainage.
3. After scar incision, use the separate instrument port for [**RD180**](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/rd180-suturing-device) to advance and appose **healthy mucosa** across the incised stenosis; avoid depicting bites through scar as the reconstructive principle.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
4. Secure sutures with [**Ti-Knot**](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/ti-knot-fastener) fasteners as described in the technique report.

The manufacturer specifies **sterile single-patient use**, without reuse or resterilization. Do not force a scope or instrument through a resistant port, avoid contact between the noninsulated cannula and electrosurgical equipment, and observe the IFU's irrigation-fluid absorption and electrosurgery-medium precautions.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Clinical Context — TUITMR

**Transurethral Incision with Transverse Mucosal Realignment (TUITMR)** incises scar and apposes healthy mucosa across the stenotic segment. An initial **19-patient technique series** reported **89% success after one treatment and 100% after repeat treatment**, with no de novo incontinence in that cohort; these are **technique-level, small-series observations**, not comparative efficacy or device-specific outcomes.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Platform

The JNW UrTrac is part of an **LSI Solutions ecosystem** — the UrTrac provides access, the [RD180](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/rd180-suturing-device) places sutures, the [Ti-Knot (TK)](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/ti-knot-fastener) secures them — designed to perform complete endoscopic urethroplasty without external abdominal or perineal incisions.
