# Saffron™ Fixation System

The **Saffron™ Fixation System** (**Coloplast Corp., Minneapolis, MN**) is a contemporary **anchor-based device (ABD)** for **sacrospinous ligament (SSL) fixation** in apical pelvic-organ-prolapse repair. Engineered to enable **precise anchor placement** in the SSL with the goal of reducing neurovascular injury risk, the system aims to facilitate a durable, fast, complication-minimizing apical-suspension procedure. Like the [Anchorsure](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/anchorsure), it delivers a permanent biocompatible anchor into the ligament rather than capturing a suture across it.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Design

- **Two-piece system**:
  1. **Saffron tool** — curved, ergonomic reusable handle; reprocessed between cases
  2. **Saffron anchors** — biocompatible, single-use, permanently implanted; sold in boxes of 12
- **Narrow distal tip** for clean entry into the dissection plane and accurate anchor placement
- **Curved body** matching the approach angle to the sacrospinous ligament
- **Intuitive ergonomics** for single-handed operation

## Evidence — Cadaveric Validation Only

The Saffron has been validated in a **single cadaveric observational study** (van Raalte 2023, *Int Urogynecol J*). **No clinical (in vivo) studies have been published.**<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

| Endpoint | Result |
|---|---|
| **Anchor placement accuracy** | **19/20 anchors (95%) correctly placed**, by independent non-implanting-surgeon assessment |
| **Neurovascular clearance** | **All anchors > 10 mm from surrounding nerves and vessels** |
| **Pull-out force (holding strength)** | **17.9 ± 5.6 N** mean |
| **Study design** | 4 human cadavers, 3 implanters; pull-out force in 3 cadavers, anatomic distance measurements in 1 |

The authors explicitly note that **"future clinical studies are needed to explore if the theoretical advantages of this device translate to improved clinical outcomes in comparison with available suturing and anchoring devices."**<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Positioning in the SSLF Landscape

The Saffron joins the [Anchorsure](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/anchorsure) as a contemporary anchor-based alternative to the dominant suture-capturing devices ([Capio](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/capio-device), [i-Stitch](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/i-stitch), [Endostitch](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/endostitch)) and the legacy suture-passers ([Miya hook](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/miya-hook), [Deschamps ligature carrier](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/deschamps-ligature-carrier)). The anchor-based concept addresses the same clinical pain point — characteristic gluteal / buttock pain after traditional SSLF — by embedding fixation within ligament substance rather than encircling or compressing it.

## SSLF Device Landscape — Level of Evidence

| Device | Type | Manufacturer | Level of Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[Capio / Capio SLIM](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/capio-device)** | Suture-capturing | Boston Scientific | Multiple clinical studies + systematic reviews |
| **[Anchorsure](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/anchorsure)** | Anchor-based | Neomedic | RCTs (Plair 2022)<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>, case-control (Evangelopoulos 2024)<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>, large retrospective series |
| **Saffron** | Anchor-based | **Coloplast** | **Cadaveric study only** (van Raalte 2023)<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> |
| **EnPlace** | Suture-passing | Neomedic | Clinical cohort studies (Neuman 2025)<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup> |
| **[Deschamps ligature carrier](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/deschamps-ligature-carrier)** | Suture-passing | Various | Extensive long-term clinical data |

The Amiri 2024 meta-analysis frames the broader pattern across SSLF devices: **overall complication rates are low across all devices**, but specific profiles diverge — **suture-capturing devices tend to have higher nerve-injury rates**, while **anchor-based devices may have slightly higher reoperation rates**.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>

## Clinical Status

**The Saffron's promising preclinical metrics — 95% accuracy, > 10 mm neurovascular clearance, 17.9 N pull-out force — have not yet been validated in human clinical use.** Until clinical data emerge, the [Capio](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/capio-device) (suture-capturing, deepest evidence base) and [Anchorsure](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/anchorsure) (anchor-based, RCT + large retrospective series) remain the SSLF devices with the strongest evidence at the bedside.

## Use

- **Apical POP repair** — vaginal vault suspension after hysterectomy
- **Uterovaginal prolapse** fixation
- **Native-tissue (mesh-free)** apical suspension

See also: [Anchorsure System](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/anchorsure), [Capio Suture Capturing Device](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/capio-device), [i-Stitch](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/i-stitch), [Endostitch](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/endostitch), [Miya Hook](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/miya-hook), [Deschamps Ligature Carrier](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/deschamps-ligature-carrier).
