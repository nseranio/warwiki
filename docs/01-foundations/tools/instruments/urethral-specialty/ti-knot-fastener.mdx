# Ti-Knot (TK) Fastener

The **Ti-Knot** (TK; LSI Solutions) is a hand-held **titanium suture fastener** used to secure compatible suture without hand-tied knots. A single-fastener **TK QUICK LOAD** is snared and loaded into the 5-mm applicator, which crimps the fastener and trims the tails. It is used through the [JNW UrTrac sheath](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/jnw-urtrac-sheath) in a published endoscopic-urethroplasty technique; the fastener is retained in the repair.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Design

- **5-mm diameter, 31-cm applicator** and separate, single-fastener TK QUICK LOAD with a hollow mushroom-shaped titanium sleeve and target/wire snare.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
- **Integrated crimp/cut mechanism**: load the compatible suture as directed, tension without overtightening, squeeze and hold to crimp/cut, release fully, and inspect the fastener and cut tails.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
- The sterile device is **single-patient use**, not a one-fastener-only handpiece: the IFU permits up to 12 firings with specified quick loads. Do not resterilize or use unapproved suture; follow the supplied model's IFU.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Use in [TUITMR](/docs/surgical-techniques/04ab-bladder-neck-reconstruction/bnc-vuas/transurethral-incision-transverse-mucosal-realignment) Endoscopic Urethroplasty

After a suture is placed at the treated **healthy mucosal edge** with the [RD180](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/rd180-suturing-device) in the described TUITMR technique:<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

1. Load one compatible TK QUICK LOAD using its snare, then route the suture tails through the applicator according to the IFU.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
2. Advance through the UrTrac under visualization, take up slack without excessive tension, and seat the fastener against the tissue.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
3. Squeeze and hold to crimp/cut, release completely, and inspect the secured fastener and tail lengths.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

## Practical Rationale and Limits

The fastener avoids hand-tied intracorporeal knots in a narrow corridor, but correct loading, controlled tension, complete firing, and visual confirmation remain essential. The cited technique does not establish comparative speed, reliability, or independence from an assistant for all cases.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Known Complications

**Exposed retained TK fasteners or suture in urine may calcify** and require endoscopic removal. Dash and Warner describe a **four-month cystoscopy in their TUITMR practice** to check whether clips have passed and remove retained clips if necessary; this is not a universal surveillance protocol. The manufacturer also warns about foreign material in the urinary tract.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Other Applications

The platform is marketed for suture fastening in minimally invasive soft-tissue surgery. Verify the particular device/suture combination and operative indication against its IFU; the urethral technique report does not prove benefit for unrelated specialties.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
