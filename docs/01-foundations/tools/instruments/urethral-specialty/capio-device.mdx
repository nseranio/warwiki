# Capio / Capio SLIM Suture Capturing Device

The **Capio™** (also marketed as **Capio SLIM**) is a **single-use suture-capturing device** manufactured by **Boston Scientific** for **transvaginal pelvic-organ-prolapse (POP) repair**. Its defining mechanic is a **single-handed throw-catch-retrieve plunger motion** that places and retrieves a suture across deep ligament tissue **without requiring the wide pararectal dissection** that traditional ligature carriers ([Deschamps](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/deschamps-ligature-carrier), [Miya hook](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/miya-hook)) demand. The dominant use is **sacrospinous ligament fixation (SSLF)** for apical prolapse; secondary uses include sacrospinous hysteropexy and vaginal paravaginal repair.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)[[3]](#ref3)[[4]](#ref4)</sup>

## Design

- **Slim-line, single-use** disposable shaft.
- **Distal hook** that catches the small curved needle on the opposite side of the target ligament after the plunger drives it through.
- **Plunger trigger** on the handle — **one actuation = pass + capture** in a single motion.
- **Pre-loaded or manually loaded suture**; compatible with **both absorbable and permanent** suture.<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>
- **Capio SLIM** = narrower-profile contemporary version for transvaginal apical POP work.

## How It Works

1. Device tip placed against the target ligament (sacrospinous, uterosacral, or arcus tendineus fascia pelvis).
2. **Plunger depression** drives the small curved needle through the ligament.
3. **Hook on the opposite side captures** the needle after pass-through.
4. Release brings the needle and suture through to the catch side **in a single motion** — no separate retrieval step.

## Reconstructive / Urogyn Uses

The dominant contemporary indications:

- **Sacrospinous colpopexy (SSLF) for vaginal vault prolapse** — the dominant use<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
- **Sacrospinous hysteropexy** — uterine-sparing apical prolapse repair<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup>
- **Vaginal paravaginal repair** for anterior compartment defects<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>
- **Uterosacral ligament suspension** (high uterosacral and Mayo-McCall variants)
- **Iliococcygeus fixation** and other deep-ligament pelvic suspensions

## Efficacy

| Endpoint | Result | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Objective success at 1–3 yr** | **86–95%** | Mowat 2018; Leone Roberti Maggiore 2013<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[3]](#ref3)</sup> |
| **Subjective success at 1–3 yr** | **92–93%** | Mowat 2018; Leone Roberti Maggiore 2013<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[3]](#ref3)</sup> |
| **Recent confirmatory series (n = 163)** | Effective, secure, low bleeding / vessel-injury / recurrence rates | Luffarelli 2025<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> |
| **Absorbable vs permanent suture** | Similar anatomic and subjective outcomes at 12 mo | Padoa 2023<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup> |

## Operative Advantages — Time and Blood Loss

Comparative data consistently favor Capio over traditional ligature-carrier techniques:

| Metric | Capio | Traditional | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sacrospinous colpopexy OR time** | **21 min** | **39 min** (p &lt; 0.001) | Leone Roberti Maggiore 2013<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> |
| **Total OR time** (broader comparative series) | **86 min** | **98 min** | Luffarelli 2025<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> |
| **Intraoperative blood loss** | Reduced vs traditional | — | Leone Roberti Maggiore 2013<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> |

## Complications

| Complication | Rate | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Postoperative buttock pain at 1 wk** | **84%** | Universal early symptom in Mowat 2018<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> |
| **Buttock pain at 6 wk** | **16%** | **7% requiring analgesia** at 6 wk<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> |
| **Gluteal / posterior-thigh pain vs anchor-based device (Anchorsure)** | **No significant difference** | Plair 2022 RCT — informs but does not resolve the suture-vs-anchor debate<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup> |
| **Nerve-injury rate (suture-capturing devices, pooled)** | **3.8% — highest among suture-capturing methods** | Amiri 2024 SR + meta-analysis<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup> |
| **Blood transfusion / hematoma** | Low | Mowat 2018; Amiri 2024<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[8]](#ref8)</sup> |

The Amiri 2024 nerve-injury signal is the most important contemporary caveat — the convenience of the throw-catch mechanic does not eliminate the **proximity-to-pudendal-bundle** risk inherent to any SSLF instrumentation, and the Capio has the highest pooled rate among the suture-capturing devices in that meta-analysis.<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup>

## Context Within POP Surgery

The Capio sits squarely within the **native-tissue (mesh-free) vaginal prolapse repair** paradigm, which remains a critical option alongside mesh-based vaginal repair and abdominal / robotic sacrocolpopexy. The Cochrane review of transvaginal repair modalities (Yeung 2024) frames the trade-offs across approaches; the Capio is one of several suture-passing instruments for SSLF, alongside the [Deschamps ligature carrier](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/deschamps-ligature-carrier), [Miya hook](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/miya-hook), [Anchorsure](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/anchorsure), [Saffron fixation system](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/saffron-fixation-system), [i-Stitch](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/i-stitch), and [Endostitch](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/endostitch).<sup>[[6]](#ref6)[[9]](#ref9)</sup>

## Capio vs Adjacent SSLF Devices

| Device | Mechanism | Key trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **Capio / Capio SLIM** (Boston Scientific) | Suture-capturing throw-catch-retrieve | Shorter OR / less EBL; pooled nerve-injury 3.8% (Amiri 2024)<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup> |
| **[Deschamps ligature carrier](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/deschamps-ligature-carrier)** | Classic curved ligature-passing needle | Reusable, traditional; requires wider pararectal dissection |
| **[Miya hook](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/miya-hook)** | Curved suture-carrying needle | Historical workhorse; largely supplanted |
| **[Anchorsure](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/anchorsure)** (Neomedic / JUNE) | PEEK anchor in ligament substance | No through-pass; Plair 2022 RCT no pain difference vs Capio<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup> |
| **[Saffron fixation system](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/saffron-fixation-system)** (Coloplast) | Anchor-based, two-piece reusable tool + single-use anchor | Newer anchor-based alternative |
| **[i-Stitch](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/i-stitch)** (A.M.I.) | Reusable, blunt ball-tipped suture, finger-guided | No sharp needle |
| **[Endostitch](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/endostitch)** (Medtronic) | Laparoscopic automated suturing device | Repurposed for transvaginal SSLF |

See also: [Miya Hook](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/miya-hook), [Deschamps Ligature Carrier](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/deschamps-ligature-carrier), [Anchorsure](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/anchorsure), [Saffron Fixation System](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/saffron-fixation-system), [i-Stitch](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/i-stitch), [Endostitch](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/endostitch), [Raz-Pereyra Trocar](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/urethral-specialty/raz-pereyra).
