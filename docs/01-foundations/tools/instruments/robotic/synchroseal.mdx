# SynchroSeal (da Vinci)

**Next-generation 8 mm EndoWrist bipolar vessel-sealing and -cutting instrument** (Intuitive Surgical, 2021) — the **successor to the Vessel Sealer Extend**, distinguished by a **finer tapered tip, faster activation cycle, and integrated simultaneous seal-and-cut** in a single foot-pedal activation. Bridges the role between the blunt-tip Vessel Sealer Extend and the fine-tip [Maryland bipolar](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/robotic/maryland-bipolar). Seals vessels **up to ~ 7 mm**; bipolar circuit eliminates the stray-energy concerns of monopolar instruments.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Design

- **8 mm shaft, full EndoWrist (7 DoF)**.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>
- **Bipolar jaws** that compress, seal, and transect tissue in a single automated cycle.
- **Fine tapered tip** (vs the blunt Vessel Sealer Extend) — enables dissection in addition to sealing/cutting.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
- **Faster activation cycle** than the predecessor — improves operative tempo.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
- **Bipolar circuit** — no return electrode required; **no stray-energy transfer** to adjacent instruments / camera / port sites.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)[[5]](#ref5)</sup>
- **Vessel-sealing capacity ≤ ~ 7 mm**.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>
- **Reposable** with limited-use life.

## Mechanism

The SynchroSeal performs **bipolar electrothermal coaptive desiccation**:<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup>

1. Jaws close with controlled compression (optimal ~ **270 mN/mm² for arteries, 200 mN/mm² for veins**).
2. **High-current low-voltage bipolar RF** denatures collagen and elastin in the vessel wall.
3. Coaptation forms the seal.
4. **Integrated mechanical cutter** transects between the sealed jaws.

Seal quality is determined by the interplay of **compressive pressure, peak temperature, and current**.

## SynchroSeal vs Vessel Sealer Extend

| Feature | **SynchroSeal** | **Vessel Sealer Extend** |
|---|---|---|
| **Tip profile** | **Fine, tapered, finely divided** | Blunt, broader |
| **Activation cycle** | **Faster** | Standard |
| **Dissection capability** | **Yes** (finer tip enables precise dissection) | Limited (blunt tip for transection only) |
| **Seal + cut** | Simultaneous, single cycle | Simultaneous, single cycle |
| **Steam generation** | Reduced | **Widespread high-temperature steam** noted in literature<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> |
| **Articulation** | EndoWrist 7 DoF | EndoWrist 7 DoF |
| **Liver-resection blood loss (propensity-matched, n = 94 each)** | **48 mL (IQR 10–143)** | 95 mL (IQR 30–200), p = 0.032<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> |

Kong 2017 specifically called out the Vessel Sealer Extend's blunt tip as a limitation requiring ancillary bipolar coagulation during robotic gastrectomy — the SynchroSeal fine-tip design was the deliberate response.<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup>

## Reconstructive-Urology and Urogyn Uses

The SynchroSeal is the **vessel-sealing instrument of choice** for any robotic urogyn / RU case with multiple pedicles to ligate-and-divide:

### Robotic sacrocolpopexy

- **Broad-ligament and sigmoid-mesentery division** when concomitant hysterectomy is performed; substitutes for clip-and-cut workflow.
- **Paravaginal-tunnel hemostasis** during deep apical dissection.

### Robotic urinary diversion / cystectomy

- **Mesenteric and ileocolic-pedicle division** during ileal-conduit / neobladder / Indiana-pouch construction — replaces clip-and-cut with seal-and-cut on the bowel mesentery.
- **Lateral pedicle division** during robotic radical cystectomy.

### Robotic partial nephrectomy and adrenal surgery

- **Asali 2026 (n = 112)** — robotic vessel sealer vs conventional bipolar grasper in partial nephrectomy: **EBL 40 vs 132.5 mL (p = 0.037)**, similar ischemia / OR / complications.<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup>
- **Hilar segmental-vessel ligation** during partial nephrectomy when bulldogs / clips would slow the warm-ischemia clock.
- **Adrenalectomy adrenal-vein and periadrenal-fat division**.

### Robotic hysterectomy and pelvic gynecologic-oncology cross-relevance

- **Bizoń 2025 robotic paraaortic lymphadenectomy for endometrial cancer** — vessel-sealer use significantly lower blood loss (p &lt; 0.05).<sup>[[9]](#ref9)</sup>
- **Hoste / Van Trappen 2015 vessel-sealer hysterectomy** for myomatous uteri — learning curve stabilizes at ~ **10 cases**.<sup>[[10]](#ref10)</sup>

### Robotic radical prostatectomy / cystectomy reconstructive components

- **Lateral prostatic pedicle division** in non-nerve-sparing cases (use cold sharp + clips for nerve-sparing — see [monopolar curved scissors](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/robotic/monopolar-curved-scissors) NVB-sparing pearl).
- **Mesenteric division** when bowel reconstruction is required.

### Hepatic parenchymal transection (cross-specialty)

- **Pilz da Cunha 2024 two-center series (n = 155)** of robotic liver resection with SynchroSeal: **conversion 0.6%**, median EBL **40 mL**, median LOS **3 d**, overall morbidity 19.4%, severe morbidity 11.0%.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
- **SAMBA technique (Birgin 2026)** — Sealer and Moisture-Based Approach: SynchroSeal + targeted saline irrigation; **n = 72**, median OR 174 min, EBL 200 mL, **no post-hepatectomy hemorrhage, no 90-d mortality**. The saline minimizes carbonization on the jaws and enhances visualization of the transection plane.<sup>[[11]](#ref11)</sup>

The SAMBA "wet sealing" concept transfers to **deep-pelvic urogyn / RU** plane work where carbonization-obscured visualization is a problem.

### RAMIE (robotic esophagectomy)

- **Hirahara 2022** — SynchroSeal positioned as alternative to Vessel Sealer Extend with finer tip enabling more precise mediastinal dissection and LN harvesting, with caveat that the fine tip demands careful handling.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## SynchroSeal vs Other da Vinci Energy Instruments

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Energy** | Bipolar RF | Bipolar RF | Bipolar RF | Monopolar RF | Ultrasonic 55 kHz |
| **Seal + cut integrated** | **Yes** | Yes | No | Yes (mechanical cut only) | Yes |
| **Vessel-seal capacity** | **≤ 7 mm** | ≤ 7 mm | Small only | Minimal | ≤ 5 mm |
| **Tip profile** | **Fine tapered** | Blunt broad | Very thin | Curved blades | Blade |
| **Dissection precision** | Moderate-high | Low | **Highest** | High | Moderate |
| **Hemostatic strength** | **Strong** | Strong | Moderate | Weak | Moderate |
| **Thermal spread (1 s, 60 W)** | 2.8–3.9 mm | 2.8–3.9 mm | 2.2 mm | 3.5 mm | 2.9 mm |
| **Stray-energy risk** | **No** | No | No | **Yes** | No |
| **Steam generation** | Reduced | **Widespread** | Minimal | None | None |
| **Best for** | **Vessel sealing + plane transection + selective dissection** | Vessel sealing + transection | Precise coagulation + dissection | Primary dissection | Rapid transection |

The clinical headline: SynchroSeal replaces the Vessel Sealer Extend and can **substitute for both the monopolar scissors and a separate vessel sealer** in many robotic RU workflows, at the cost of higher disposable instrument expense.

## Safety Profile

- **No stray energy / no return pad** — bipolar advantage.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>
- **Steam thermal injury** — reduced vs Vessel Sealer Extend but not eliminated; maintain awareness of plume direction in confined corridors (pelvis, mediastinum).<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
- **Carbonization on the jaws** — SAMBA-style targeted saline irrigation minimizes; clean the jaws between activations if performance degrades.<sup>[[11]](#ref11)</sup>
- **Lateral thermal spread** is in the bipolar range (2.8–3.9 mm at standard settings) — much lower than monopolar at 2 s (&gt; 20 mm).<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>
- **Vessel-size ceiling at ~ 7 mm** — clip / suture-ligate above this; do not rely on the seal alone for renal vein, IVC, iliac trunks.

## Practical Pearls

- **Wet sealing (SAMBA)** — targeted saline irrigation reduces carbonization and improves visualization, particularly during parenchymal / dense-tissue transection.<sup>[[11]](#ref11)</sup>
- **For meticulous dissection near nerves / small structures** the **[Maryland bipolar](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/robotic/maryland-bipolar) remains preferred** — the SynchroSeal's tip is finer than the Vessel Sealer Extend but not as fine as the Maryland.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
- **For nerve-sparing NVB / ureteral wall** use **cold sharp [monopolar curved scissors](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/robotic/monopolar-curved-scissors)** — energy-dependent seal-and-cut is not the right tool.
- **Instrument-pair convention** — SynchroSeal in the dominant hand (replacing both the monopolar scissors and a dedicated vessel sealer), grasper (ProGrasp / Cadiere / Tip-Up) in the contralateral arm, retractor on the fourth arm.
- **Learning curve short** — ~ 10 cases to stabilize technique (Hoste 2015 hysterectomy series).<sup>[[10]](#ref10)</sup>

## Limitations

- **Higher disposable cost** vs monopolar scissors + bipolar grasper alone — case-mix justification rests on time saved and EBL reduction.
- **Not as fine as the Maryland** for delicate dissection.
- **Energy-dependent** — no cold-cutting alternative within the instrument.
- **Vessel size ceiling ~ 7 mm**.
- **Steam plume awareness** required in confined spaces.
