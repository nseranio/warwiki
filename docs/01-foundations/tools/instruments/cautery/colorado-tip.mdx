# Colorado Tip

An **ultra-fine needle-tip monopolar electrode** — essentially a sharpened wire that concentrates current into a millimeter-scale effect.

## Design

- **Tungsten or fine-steel wire tip**, sharpened to a point
- **Concentrates electrosurgical current** into a very small area
- **Minimal lateral thermal spread** compared with blade-tip monopolar

## Key Uses in Reconstructive Urology

- Penile skin and preputial dissection (hypospadias, phalloplasty)
- Urethral plate dissection and mobilization
- Vaginal wall flap development
- Dartos dissection during UCF repair
- Any field where a blade-tip monopolar would damage adjacent critical tissue

## Cautions

- Fragile tip — bends and breaks easily
- Higher current density means tissue char accumulates quickly; clean frequently
