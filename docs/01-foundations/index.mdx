# Foundations

This section establishes the scientific and surgical bedrock of functional urology and genitourinary reconstruction, covering the anatomy and physiology of the lower urinary tract and pelvic floor, the biology of wound healing and tissue repair, the properties and applications of grafts and flaps, biomaterials including mesh and biologic substitutes, relevant pharmacology, the effects of radiation on tissue, essential instruments and technology, and the principles of perioperative care that frame every reconstructive intervention.
