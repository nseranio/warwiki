# Foundations

WARWIKI is a specialist reference for functional urology and genitourinary reconstruction — written for urology residents, fellows, and practicing reconstructive surgeons. It is organized around the two reconstructive subspecialties — **Urogynecology & Reconstructive Pelvic Surgery (URPS)** and **Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgery (GURS)** — and is built to serve as a rapid, referenced clinical companion rather than a textbook replacement.

The **Foundations** section collects the core scientific and surgical principles that underlie every later topic on the site — regional anatomy and physiology, reconstructive principles, perioperative care, and the surgical tools and biomaterials in daily use. Subsequent sections apply these foundations to specific conditions, operations, and populations.
