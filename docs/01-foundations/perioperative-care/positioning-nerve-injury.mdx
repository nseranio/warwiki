# Positioning & Nerve Injury

Patient positioning for GU and pelvic reconstructive surgery — lithotomy, steep Trendelenburg, lateral decubitus, and prone — carries specific risks of peripheral nerve injury, compartment syndrome, and pressure-related tissue damage. Lithotomy position is particularly relevant for perineal urethral, vaginal, and combined abdominoperineal procedures, and is associated with common peroneal, femoral, sciatic, and obturator nerve injuries when incorrectly applied or maintained for prolonged durations.
