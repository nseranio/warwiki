# Postoperative Nausea & Vomiting (PONV)

Postoperative nausea and vomiting affects 20–30% of unselected surgical patients and up to 80% of high-risk individuals. It is a leading cause of unanticipated hospital admission after ambulatory surgery, patient dissatisfaction, and delayed oral intake — directly undermining ERAS goals. Risk stratification using validated tools (Apfel score) guides prophylaxis intensity, and multimodal antiemetic combinations are more effective than single-agent approaches.
