# Analgesia

Effective perioperative analgesia in GU reconstructive surgery relies on multimodal, opioid-sparing strategies that minimize side effects while maintaining adequate pain control. Combining systemic agents (acetaminophen, NSAIDs, gabapentinoids), regional techniques (nerve blocks, neuraxial anesthesia), and local wound infiltration reduces opioid requirements, shortens ileus duration, improves early mobilization, and accelerates discharge. Pain management is a central pillar of ERAS protocols.
