# Antithrombotic Therapy

Perioperative antithrombotic management involves two distinct challenges: (1) VTE prophylaxis in surgical patients at risk for deep venous thrombosis and pulmonary embolism, and (2) management of patients on chronic anticoagulation or antiplatelet therapy requiring bridging or temporary discontinuation. GU reconstructive procedures carry variable VTE risk depending on duration, extent of dissection, and patient factors.
