# Risk Calculators

Validated surgical risk calculators integrate patient demographics, comorbidities, functional status, and procedure type to estimate the probability of postoperative complications, mortality, and resource utilization. These tools support preoperative informed consent, guide optimization timing, stratify patients for monitoring intensity, and inform shared decision-making about operative vs non-operative management.
