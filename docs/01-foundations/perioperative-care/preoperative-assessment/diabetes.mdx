# Diabetes — Perioperative Management

Diabetes mellitus is prevalent in the GU reconstructive population and significantly impacts surgical outcomes — hyperglycemia impairs wound healing, increases infection risk, and is associated with worse outcomes after prosthetic implantation. Perioperative glucose management targets euglycemia (typically 140–180 mg/dL intraoperatively) while avoiding hypoglycemia, which carries independent harm. Preoperative HbA1c optimization is particularly critical before elective prosthetic surgery (AUS, penile implant).
