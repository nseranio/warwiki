# Cardiovascular Risk

Preoperative cardiac evaluation for non-cardiac surgery follows the ACC/AHA stepwise framework — assessing urgency of surgery, presence of active cardiac conditions, functional capacity, and procedure-specific risk to determine whether further workup or optimization is warranted before proceeding. The Revised Cardiac Risk Index (RCRI) quantifies major adverse cardiac event (MACE) probability and guides perioperative beta-blocker, statin, and aspirin management.
