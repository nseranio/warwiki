# Steroids — Perioperative Management

Patients on chronic glucocorticoid therapy are at risk for perioperative adrenal insufficiency due to HPA axis suppression. Stress dosing protocols provide supplemental corticosteroids during the surgical period proportional to the magnitude of physiologic stress. Additionally, dexamethasone is commonly used as an antiemetic and anti-inflammatory adjunct in ERAS protocols, with a favorable effect on PONV and opioid requirements.
