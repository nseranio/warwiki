# Frailty

Frailty is a state of decreased physiologic reserve and increased vulnerability to stressors, distinct from chronological age and comorbidity burden. In surgical patients, frailty predicts postoperative complications, prolonged hospitalization, discharge to skilled nursing facilities, and mortality. Assessment with validated tools (Fried Frailty Phenotype, Clinical Frailty Scale, FRAIL questionnaire) identifies patients who may benefit from prehabilitation programs before elective GU reconstructive surgery.
