# Electrolyte Abnormalities

Perioperative electrolyte disturbances arise from fluid shifts, bowel preparation, urinary diversion physiology, nasogastric losses, and renal impairment. In GU reconstructive surgery, ileal and colonic urinary diversions introduce metabolic complications specific to bowel-urine contact — hyperchloremic metabolic acidosis (ileal conduit, colon reservoir), hypokalemia, and vitamin B12 deficiency with long-term ileal segments. Prompt identification and correction of electrolyte abnormalities is essential before and after major reconstruction.
