# Constipation

Postoperative constipation and ileus are common after major pelvic and abdominal GU surgery, driven by opioid use, bowel manipulation, immobility, and perioperative fluid shifts. Opioid-induced constipation (OIC) deserves specific attention given its distinct pathophysiology from other causes — peripheral μ-receptor antagonists (methylnaltrexone, naloxegol) target OIC without reversing central analgesia. Return of bowel function is a key ERAS discharge criterion and a driver of length-of-stay.
