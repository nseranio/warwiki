# Enhanced Recovery After Surgery (ERAS)

Enhanced Recovery After Surgery (ERAS) protocols are multimodal, evidence-based perioperative care pathways designed to reduce surgical stress, maintain physiologic function, and accelerate return to baseline. Originally developed for colorectal surgery, ERAS principles have been adapted for urological and pelvic reconstructive procedures including cystectomy, prostatectomy, and major reconstructive cases. Core elements span preoperative optimization, intraoperative management, and structured postoperative care.
