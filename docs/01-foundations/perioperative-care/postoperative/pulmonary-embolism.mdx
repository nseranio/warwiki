# Pulmonary Embolism

Pulmonary embolism (PE) is a potentially life-threatening complication of major urological and pelvic reconstructive surgery. Prompt recognition, risk stratification (massive vs submassive vs low-risk), and appropriate anticoagulation are critical. The perioperative context introduces complexity — bleeding risk from recent surgery must be weighed against the thrombotic risk of delayed or withheld anticoagulation.
