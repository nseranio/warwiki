# Parenteral Nutrition (TPN / PPN)

Total parenteral nutrition (TPN) and peripheral parenteral nutrition (PPN) provide intravenous macronutrients when the enteral route is unavailable or insufficient. In GU reconstructive surgery, indications include prolonged ileus after bowel incorporation procedures, enterocutaneous fistula, severe malnutrition preceding major elective reconstruction, and inability to meet caloric needs enterally within 5–7 postoperative days. Enteral nutrition is preferred when feasible — parenteral nutrition is reserved for cases where enteral access is impossible or inadequate.
