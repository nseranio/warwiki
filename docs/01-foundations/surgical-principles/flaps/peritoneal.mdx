# Peritoneal Flap

A thin, highly vascular flap of parietal or visceral peritoneum, harvested robotically or laparoscopically and used as an onlay or wrap in ureteral reconstruction. Based on underlying mesenteric and peritoneal perforators.

See the overview article for the reference summary: [Flaps in GU Reconstruction](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps-gu-reconstruction).
