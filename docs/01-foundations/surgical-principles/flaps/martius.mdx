# Martius Flap

A pedicled adipofascial flap of the labial fat pad (bulbocavernosus fat pad) based on the **posterior labial artery** (branch of the internal pudendal artery) or the **anterior labial artery** (branch of the external pudendal artery). Described by **Heinrich Martius** in 1928 as an interposition layer for urogenital fistula repair.

See the overview article for the reference summary: [Flaps in GU Reconstruction](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps-gu-reconstruction).

## GU Applications

- Urethrovaginal fistula repair — interposition layer (standard of care)
- Vesicovaginal fistula repair — interposition layer in complex or recurrent fistulas
- Female urethral reconstruction — vascularized backing for onlay or tube repair
- Radiation-associated fistula — non-irradiated vascularized tissue into a hypovascular field
