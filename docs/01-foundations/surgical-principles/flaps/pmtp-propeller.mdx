# Posteromedial Thigh Perforator (PMTP) Propeller Flap

The PMTP is a posteromedial-thigh perforator-flap term. Published reports describe a pedicled island flap supplied by a profunda-femoris or, in some cases, medial-circumflex-femoral perforator and transferred with variable movement patterns, including propeller rotation. This is a terminology and evidence-context overview—not operative, flap-selection, or defect-management guidance.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Reported evidence context

Scaglioni and colleagues reported 15 pedicled posteromedial-thigh perforator flaps for diverse regional defects, including three perineal/genital cases. This was a case series with varied transfer mechanisms, not a comparative standard.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

Kwon and colleagues reported eight PMTP propeller flaps for extensive perineal defects, with 180-degree rotation in that series. Wishart and colleagues reported 21 vertical posteromedial-thigh flaps in 12 patients with perianal and/or genital defects, using both propeller and V-Y movements. These small retrospective reports cannot establish universal survival, complication, donor-site, radiation-field, or selection claims.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

## Evidence boundaries

The accessible source package for this recovery consists of PubMed abstracts/records for three small clinical case series. It does not support retaining inherited anatomy maps, operative steps, dimensions, perforator-dissection guidance, outcome pooling, comparison tables, reconstructive algorithms, radiation-field claims, technical recommendations, or postoperative protocols.
