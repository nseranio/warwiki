# Radial Forearm Free Flap (RFFF)

A workhorse free fasciocutaneous flap for complex urethral and penile reconstruction, based on the radial artery with venous drainage via paired venae comitantes and/or the cephalic vein. Can be made sensate by including the lateral antebrachial cutaneous nerve (LACN).

See the overview article for the reference summary: [Flaps in GU Reconstruction](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps-gu-reconstruction).
