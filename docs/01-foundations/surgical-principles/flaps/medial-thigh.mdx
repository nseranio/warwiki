# Medial Thigh Flap Family

“Medial thigh flap” is an umbrella term for several local fasciocutaneous, perforator, and musculocutaneous flap concepts. Individual designs differ in tissue composition, vascular basis, and evidence base; the term alone does not specify an operative plan. This page is a terminology and literature-context overview—not flap-selection, operative, or defect-management guidance.

## Reported variants

Wang and colleagues described a medial-thigh fasciocutaneous flap in a cadaveric investigation and 13-patient clinical series for vaginal, perineal, vulvectomy, groin, and ischial defects.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> Persichetti and colleagues reported a medial-thigh septo-fascio-cutaneous island flap in 26 vulvoperineal reconstructions after vulvar-cancer resection.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

Another retrospective report described 27 pedicled perforator flaps in 16 patients after vulvar cancer surgery, encompassing several named flap categories—including profunda, MCFAP, external-pudendal, medial-thigh freestyle, and internal-pudendal perforator flaps. Results from that mixed, small cohort do not establish a preferred variant or an outcome standard.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

Dedicated pages separate the most frequently referenced related concepts: [Gracilis flap](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps/gracilis), [MCFAP flap](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps/mcfap), [PMTP propeller flap](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps/pmtp-propeller), and [Singapore / pudendal-thigh flap](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps/singapore-pudendal-thigh).

## Evidence boundaries

The accessible source package for this recovery consists of PubMed abstracts/records for two older single-design series and one mixed retrospective perforator-flap cohort. It does not support retaining inherited vascular maps, operative steps, dimensions, selection algorithms, comparative claims, outcome pooling, donor-site rules, functional claims, treatment recommendations, or postoperative protocols.
