# Omental Flap

The greater omentum mobilized on its gastroepiploic vascular arcade (right or left **gastroepiploic artery**) and transferred to the pelvis or perineum. Provides abundant lymphatic and vascular tissue with a porous structure that drains dead-space fluid.

See the overview article for the reference summary: [Flaps in GU Reconstruction](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps-gu-reconstruction).

## GU Applications

- Buccal mucosa graft ureteroplasty backing (promotes inosculation, prevents fluid accumulation)
- Urethrovaginal / vesicovaginal fistula interposition barrier
- Pelvic dead-space obliteration after exenteration or radical cystoprostatectomy
- Radiation-wound revascularization
