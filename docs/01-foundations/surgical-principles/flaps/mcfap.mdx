# Medial Circumflex Femoral Artery Perforator (MCFAP) Flap

The MCFAP, also called a gracilis perforator flap, is a medial-thigh fasciocutaneous flap described in relation to perforators of the gracilis pedicle. It is distinct from musculocutaneous gracilis-family flaps because its literature describes preservation of the gracilis muscle. This is a terminology and evidence-context overview—not operative, flap-selection, or defect-management guidance.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Reported evidence context

Hallock reported four local groin reconstructions and described a conjoint option in which muscle and cutaneous components could be separately mobilized; that small series does not establish a universal indication or technique.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> An anatomic study with clinical application reported gracilis perforator findings in 43 cadaveric dissections and 14 clinical cases, with muscle preservation described for the perforator-flap approach.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

Coskunfirat and colleagues reported seven post-Fournier scrotal reconstructions. Their publication title’s “superiority” is an author conclusion from a small non-comparative series, not comparative evidence of superiority over other reconstruction options.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

## Evidence boundaries

The accessible source package for this recovery consists of PubMed abstracts/records for two small clinical/anatomic reports and a seven-patient scrotal-reconstruction series. It does not support retaining inherited vascular measurements, harvest steps, dimensions, free-flap, breast-, extremity-, head-and-neck, or perineal outcome claims, comparison tables, superiority rankings, technical recommendations, or postoperative protocols.
