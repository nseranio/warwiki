# Deep Inferior Epigastric Perforator (DIEP) Flap

The **deep inferior epigastric perforator (DIEP) flap** uses lower-abdominal skin and fat supplied through perforators connected to the deep inferior epigastric vessels. It was developed principally for autologous breast reconstruction and may also be reported in selected pelvic, perineal, and vulvar reconstruction settings. This is an anatomic orientation, not a flap-selection guide or operative protocol.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Anatomy and terminology

The deep inferior epigastric artery arises from the external iliac system and provides perforators that traverse the abdominal wall toward skin and subcutaneous tissue. A DIEP flap is distinguished from TRAM or VRAM configurations by its intent to preserve rectus muscle while dissecting around selected perforators. Muscle-sparing does not mean that donor-site risk is absent; perforator anatomy and the intramuscular dissection vary between patients.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

Preoperative vascular mapping is commonly discussed in DIEP planning literature, but the method, interpretation, and any decision based on it belong to a specialist reconstructive evaluation rather than this page.<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

## Pelvic and perineal reconstruction context

The published pelvic-oncology literature includes pedicled DIEP reconstruction after tumor resection. One multicenter retrospective series reported both donor-site and recipient-site morbidity, including flap loss and other recipient-site complications. Such reports are useful context but do not establish comparative superiority, a universal indication, or an individual patient recommendation.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

The literature on secondary vulvar reconstruction also describes use of DIEP among several flap options in a retrospective single-center series. Flap choice depends on the specific defect, prior treatment, available tissue, oncologic plan, and multidisciplinary expertise; this overview does not reproduce an algorithm or technical details.<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>
