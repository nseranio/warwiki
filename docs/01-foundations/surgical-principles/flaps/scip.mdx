# Superficial Circumflex Iliac Artery Perforator (SCIP) Flap

An emerging free perforator flap based on the **superficial circumflex iliac artery (SCIA)**, a branch of the femoral artery. Thin and pliable, with very low donor-site morbidity and a scar concealed in the natural groin crease.

See the overview article for the reference summary: [Flaps in GU Reconstruction](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps-gu-reconstruction).
