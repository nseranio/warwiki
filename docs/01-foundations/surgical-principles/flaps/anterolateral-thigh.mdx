# Anterolateral Thigh (ALT) Flap

A large, versatile free or pedicled flap based on the **descending branch of the lateral circumflex femoral artery (LCFA)**, with perforators passing through or around the vastus lateralis muscle to supply the lateral thigh skin. Can be harvested as perforator-only fasciocutaneous, myocutaneous, or chimeric.

See the overview article for the reference summary: [Flaps in GU Reconstruction](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps-gu-reconstruction).
