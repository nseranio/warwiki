# Vertical Rectus Abdominis Myocutaneous (VRAM) Flap

A pedicled myocutaneous flap based on the **inferior epigastric artery** (branch of the external iliac artery), raised on a vertical skin paddle overlying the rectus abdominis muscle. Provides a large volume of well-vascularized tissue for pelvic dead-space obliteration.

See the overview article for the reference summary: [Flaps in GU Reconstruction](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps-gu-reconstruction).

- Pelvic floor and perineal reconstruction after radical cystectomy, pelvic exenteration, APR
- Vaginal reconstruction (neovagina after total pelvic exenteration)
- Perineal wound closure after Fournier's gangrene involving lower abdominal wall
- Bladder exstrophy reconstruction
