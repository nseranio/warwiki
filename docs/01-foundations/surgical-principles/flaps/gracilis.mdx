# Gracilis Flap

A Type II Mathes-Nahai muscle flap based on the **medial circumflex femoral artery** (branch of profunda femoris), with the dominant pedicle entering ~8–10 cm from the pubic tubercle. Can be harvested as muscle-only, myocutaneous, pedicled, or free.

See the overview article for the reference summary: [Flaps in GU Reconstruction](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps-gu-reconstruction), and the anatomy article for gracilis muscle anatomy: [The Leg & Thigh](/docs/foundations/anatomy-physiology/lower-extremity/leg-thigh).

## GU Applications

- Perineal reconstruction after APR, pelvic exenteration, Fournier's gangrene
- Vaginal reconstruction (neovagina) — bilateral pedicled myocutaneous
- Scrotal reconstruction
- Rectourethral fistula repair with Martius-inadequate or recurrent fistula
- Graciloplasty neosphincter (sphincter reinnervation with obturator-to-pudendal nerve coaptation)
- Long-segment urethroplasty coverage / salvage perineal wound closure
