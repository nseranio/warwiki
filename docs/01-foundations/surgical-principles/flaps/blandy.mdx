# Blandy Flap

**Blandy flap** is an eponym that can refer to different urethral-reconstruction uses of local tissue. This page is a limited terminology and anatomy overview, not a patient-selection guide or an operative protocol.

## Female urethral reconstruction context

In female urethral-stricture literature, a Blandy flap generally describes use of an anterior vaginal-wall flap in urethral reconstruction. Published reports describe it in the setting of distal female urethral stricture, while other vaginal flap orientations and free grafts are also described. The available evidence base is predominantly small observational series and case reports; it does not establish a single universally preferred reconstruction.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)[[3]](#ref3)</sup>

Assessment, diagnostic confirmation, tissue quality, reconstruction choice, perioperative care, and follow-up need to remain individualized in the relevant condition and procedure pages. This overview intentionally does not provide incision design, flap dimensions, catheter plans, outcome percentages, or comparative treatment recommendations.

## Male perineal-urethrostomy context

The Blandy name is also used for a male perineal urethrostomy technique involving local perineal or scrotal skin. This is a distinct reconstructive context from a female vaginal-wall flap. The dedicated perineal-urethrostomy article carries the separately reviewed procedural and evidence detail; this page does not duplicate it.
