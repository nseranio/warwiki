# Lingual Mucosa Graft

Mucosal graft harvested from the ventral or lateral surface of the tongue. Histologically similar to buccal mucosa and used as a secondary or supplementary donor site when buccal is unavailable or when extra length is needed.

See the overview article for the reference summary: [Grafts in GU Reconstruction](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/grafts-gu-reconstruction), and the anatomy article: [The Oral Cavity](/docs/foundations/anatomy-physiology/oral-cavity).
