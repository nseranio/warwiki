# Bladder Mucosa Graft

Transitional (urothelial) epithelium harvested from the bladder wall. Used historically in hypospadias and anterior urethroplasty (Memmelaar 1947 and later Hendren descriptions), largely **abandoned** in favor of buccal mucosa because of inferior handling, cystotomy requirement, and equivalent-to-worse outcomes.

See the overview article for the reference summary: [Grafts in GU Reconstruction](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/grafts-gu-reconstruction).
