# Split-Thickness Skin Graft (STSG)

Epidermis + partial dermis harvested with a dermatome, commonly from thigh, buttock, or scalp. Takes readily on well-vascularized beds; undergoes significant primary and secondary contraction, making it suitable for coverage where contraction is acceptable or desired.

See the overview article for the reference summary: [Grafts in GU Reconstruction](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/grafts-gu-reconstruction).

## Immobilization & Take

*Stub — to be built out.*

- Bolster / tie-over dressings
- Negative-pressure wound therapy as graft bolster
- 48–72 h immobilization

## GU Applications

- **Post-Fournier's scrotal resurfacing** — meshed STSG the standard
- Penile shaft after oncologic resection or extensive skin loss
- Perineal wound coverage
- **McIndoe neovagina** — STSG over vaginal mold in MRKH
