# Penile / Preputial Skin Graft

Non-glabrous preputial or penile shaft skin used as a free graft (without dartos pedicle). Distinct from the [pedicled penile/preputial skin flap](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/flaps/penile-preputial), which retains its dartos vascular supply. As a graft (no pedicle), used primarily in pediatric hypospadias repair.

See the overview article for the reference summary: [Grafts in GU Reconstruction](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/grafts-gu-reconstruction).

- Short-segment anterior urethroplasty in uncircumcised patients
- Penile shaft resurfacing after degloving (non-hair-bearing only)
