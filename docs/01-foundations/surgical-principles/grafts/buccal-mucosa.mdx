# Buccal Mucosa Graft (BMG)

The most widely used graft in urethral reconstruction and the emerging standard for ureteral augmentation. Harvested from the inner cheek, with bilateral harvest possible for longer grafts (combined lengths up to 12–14 cm).

See the overview article for the reference summary: [Grafts in GU Reconstruction](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/grafts-gu-reconstruction), and the anatomy article for oral cavity harvest anatomy: [The Oral Cavity](/docs/foundations/anatomy-physiology/oral-cavity/oral-cavity).
