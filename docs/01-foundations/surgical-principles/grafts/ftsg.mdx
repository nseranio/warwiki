# Full-Thickness Skin Graft (FTSG)

Epidermis + full dermis harvested as a single layer from a donor site that can be primarily closed (groin, postauricular, preputial). More metabolically demanding than STSG with a lower take rate, but minimal contraction — the reason it is preferred on the penile shaft and for hypospadias.

See the overview article for the reference summary: [Grafts in GU Reconstruction](/docs/foundations/surgical-principles/grafts-gu-reconstruction).
