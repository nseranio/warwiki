# Parker-Kerr Stitch

The Parker-Kerr stitch is a historical name for an inverting hand-sewn closure of a bowel end or stump. It is sometimes discussed alongside other hand-sewn bowel-closure patterns in surgical teaching.

This page does not provide a step sequence, suture selection, clamp recommendation, leak test, or indication for urinary diversion or bowel reconstruction.
