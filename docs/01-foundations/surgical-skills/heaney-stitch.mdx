# Heaney Stitch

The term *Heaney stitch* is used for a transfixing suture-ligature in pelvic surgery, particularly in historical descriptions of vaginal hysterectomy. It is also associated with instruments bearing the Heaney name. Terminology and exact maneuver descriptions vary across surgical teaching sources.

## Clinical boundary

This page is a historical orientation, not an operative guide. It does not prescribe clamp selection, bite placement, suture choice, or use in a particular hysterectomy or prolapse procedure. Those decisions require the relevant contemporary procedure guidance, local protocol, supervision, and intraoperative judgment.
