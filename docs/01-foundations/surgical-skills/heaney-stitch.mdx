# Heaney Stitch

The **Heaney stitch** is a transfixing pedicle suture-ligature used in hysterectomy, particularly for a clamped uterosacral pedicle. The named stitch is distinct from a second, separate ligature that a surgeon may choose for a vascular or large pedicle. It is associated with Noble Sproat Heaney and the [instruments bearing his name](/docs/foundations/tools/instruments/needle-holders/heaney).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

## Technique

- Identify and protect nearby structures, especially the ureter, while placing a Heaney or suitable pedicle clamp close to the uterus; divide the pedicle on the specimen side.
- In the described uterosacral technique, pass an absorbable suture through the upper pedicle near the clamp tip, withdraw the needle and pass it again through the midportion of that pedicle. Tie the suture as the clamp is released and retain the tagged pedicle for subsequent cuff-support steps where applicable.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
- Other pedicles may instead receive a simple ligature, a fixation stitch or a separate second tie according to their vascularity and the operative plan; these are not an obligatory second wrap of every Heaney stitch.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>

The two transfixing passes at the clamp tip and pedicle midpoint distinguish this described Heaney-type stitch from a simple free tie; the exact trajectory varies by pedicle and operative approach.

## Why It Works

The described mechanism combines:

1. **Transfixion.** The suture passes through the pedicle tissue near the clamp tip and through its midpoint.
2. **Clamp-assisted control.** The pedicle is held during division and placement of the suture.
3. **Fixation where intended.** In an uterosacral pedicle, the retained suture can assist later identification and cuff suspension.

Choice of ligature and whether an additional tie is needed depend on the specific pedicle and exposure.

## Key Uses

- **Vaginal hysterectomy.** A described stitch for the uterosacral or other selected nonvascular pedicle; vascular pedicles may require a different ligature strategy.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
- **Open abdominal hysterectomy.** Transfixing sutures may secure selected clamped pedicles; laparoscopic clips or energy devices are different methods, not Heaney stitches.
- **Associated cuff support.** A retained uterosacral suture can contribute to cuff fixation in the described vaginal hysterectomy sequence; formal prolapse suspension is a separate procedure.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
