# Quilting Stitch

Quilting describes fixation sutures placed between a graft or flap and its recipient bed. The intended mechanical goals are close contact and limitation of dead space or shear. It is used in varied settings, so evidence from one graft type or operation should not be treated as a universal technical rule.

## Evidence orientation

A 2020 review of full-thickness skin-graft fixation found that the available literature was dominated by descriptive and observational reports. It did not find a significant benefit for tie-over dressings versus quilting/mattress suturing or other alternatives in the outcomes assessed, and emphasized the limited evidence base.<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>

For urethral reconstruction, graft placement and fixation are part of procedure-specific techniques and should be read in their own evidence context. A systematic review of free-graft bulbar urethroplasty found no single graft-placement technique clearly superior and described overall low-quality evidence for many comparisons.<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup>
