# Free Tie

The **free tie** is the simplest ligature — a length of suture passed around an exposed, clamped, or isolated pedicle and tied flat. The workhorse for accessible pedicles where the clamped tissue can be fully circumscribed by the suture.

## Technique

- Pass the free suture around the pedicle, behind any clamp
- Tie a flat square knot (or surgeon's knot for slippery material) around the pedicle
- Cut the tails short

## Key Uses

- Visible, accessible pedicles during open dissection
- Mesenteric branches during bowel harvest for urinary diversion
- Dartos and periurethral vessels during penile dissection
- Small pedicles during perineal exposure

## Limitations

- **Slipping:** large or firm pedicles may slip out from under a simple free tie — use a [suture ligature](/docs/foundations/surgical-skills/ligatures/suture-ligature) or [transfixion stitch](/docs/foundations/surgical-skills/ligatures/transfixion-stitch)
- **Depth:** a free tie requires both hands to reach the pedicle — not feasible in deep pelvic or laparoscopic fields without a knot pusher

See also: [Suture Ligature](/docs/foundations/surgical-skills/ligatures/suture-ligature), [Transfixion Stitch](/docs/foundations/surgical-skills/ligatures/transfixion-stitch).
