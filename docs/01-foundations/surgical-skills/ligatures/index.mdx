# Ligatures

A **ligature** is any technique that controls a vessel or pedicle through compression. The reconstructive surgeon chooses among five options based on pedicle size, tissue quality, field depth, and whether cautery-related tissue damage is acceptable in context.

## Techniques

<ul className="section-stack">
  <li>
    <a href="/docs/foundations/surgical-skills/ligatures/free-tie" className="section-stack-title section-stack-link">Free Tie</a>
    <span className="section-stack-desc">Simple ligature around a vessel or pedicle with a free suture — fast, ergonomic for accessible pedicles.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <a href="/docs/foundations/surgical-skills/ligatures/suture-ligature" className="section-stack-title section-stack-link">Suture Ligature</a>
    <span className="section-stack-desc">Ligature delivered on a passer, used when the pedicle is clamped but a free tie cannot reach — deep pelvic and hepatic pedicles.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <a href="/docs/foundations/surgical-skills/ligatures/transfixion-stitch" className="section-stack-title section-stack-link">Transfixion Stitch (Stick Tie)</a>
    <span className="section-stack-desc">Suture passed through the pedicle itself before tying, preventing slip — the workhorse for large, firm, or short pedicles.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <a href="/docs/foundations/surgical-skills/ligatures/clip-ligation" className="section-stack-title section-stack-link">Clip Ligation</a>
    <span className="section-stack-desc">Titanium or polymer clips (Hem-o-lok, Ligaclip) applied to vessels — fast, reliable, radiopaque.</span>
  </li>
  <li>
    <a href="/docs/foundations/surgical-skills/ligatures/energy-sealing" className="section-stack-title section-stack-link">Energy Sealing</a>
    <span className="section-stack-desc">Bipolar or ultrasonic tissue sealers (LigaSure, Harmonic, Enseal) — seal vessels up to 7 mm without suture or clip.</span>
  </li>
</ul>
