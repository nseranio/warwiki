# Energy Sealing — Bipolar and Ultrasonic Tissue Sealers

**Energy-based tissue sealers** deliver bipolar radiofrequency or ultrasonic energy to coapt and divide vessels without suture or clip. Seal up to **7 mm** vessels reliably with appropriately sized devices.

## Devices

- **LigaSure** (Medtronic) — bipolar RF with tissue-response algorithm; standard for large-vessel sealing
- **Harmonic** (Ethicon) — ultrasonic; vibrating blade cuts and coagulates simultaneously
- **Thunderbeat** (Olympus) — combined bipolar + ultrasonic energy in one device
- **EnSeal** (Ethicon) — bipolar RF

## Key Uses in Reconstructive Urology

- Mesenteric vessel division during bowel harvest (ileal conduit, neobladder)
- Periuretric and periurethral vessel control during prostatectomy
- Broad-ligament takedown during hysterectomy-related urogynecologic procedures
- Vascular pedicles during laparoscopic / robotic partial nephrectomy
- Dartos and periuretric tissue during penile surgery (with low-energy settings)

## Considerations

- **Thermal spread** — energy devices cause lateral tissue damage of 1–3 mm beyond the sealed zone; avoid near critical structures
- **Ureter** — direct sealing of the ureter delays stricture but does not ensure division safety; if near the ureter, stent or skeletonize before energy use
- **Nerve sparing** — avoid energy devices near the neurovascular bundle during nerve-sparing prostatectomy; use cold scissors plus clips
- **Vessel size limits** — respect the device's rated vessel diameter; larger vessels need suture or clip
