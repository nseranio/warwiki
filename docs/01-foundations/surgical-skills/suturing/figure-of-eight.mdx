# Figure-of-Eight

The **figure-of-eight** stitch places two passes through tissue in an **X pattern** — the resulting knot compresses tissue in two intersecting planes and holds securely on a pedicle or bleeding vessel.

## Technique

- **First pass:** through one edge of the pedicle, out the opposite edge (or through a vessel if ligating as hemostatic)
- **Second pass:** advance or reverse, pass perpendicular to the first, forming an X
- Tie — both passes compress in the same knot

## Key Uses

- **Hemostasis at a pedicle or vessel** when a simple suture ligature cannot grasp
- **Transfixion ligature** modification — the figure-of-eight is a transfixion stitch variant with a second pass
- **Reinforcement closure** at high-stress wound corners
- **Troublesome bleeders** that resist clip or simple-tie hemostasis
