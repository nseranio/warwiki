# Horizontal Mattress

The **horizontal mattress** stitch places two passes through tissue **perpendicular to the wound axis** at the same depth, creating a broad area of edge approximation with a single tied knot on one side.

## Technique

- **First pass:** enter one edge, traverse wound, exit opposite edge
- **Second pass:** re-enter on the exit side a short distance along the wound, traverse back to the entry side at the same depth
- Tie — the wound edges are drawn together over a broad footprint

## Key Uses

- **High-tension skin closure** (e.g., scalp, perineum)
- **Flap corners** — the half-buried horizontal mattress is the classic corner stitch
- **Bolstered closures** — with gauze or silastic bolsters to distribute tension away from the skin
- **Fascial closure** where broad purchase is needed
- **Pexy stitches** — holding soft tissue to underlying fascia

## Half-Buried Horizontal Mattress (Flap Corners)

A modification where the flap apex is caught with a buried throw; the recipient tissue is caught with normal entry-exit throws. Avoids piercing the delicate flap tip twice on the skin surface.
