# Purse-String

The **purse-string** suture is a **circumferential gathering stitch** that, when tightened, closes a circular opening or inverts a tubular structure — as the drawstring of a coin purse.

## Technique

- Place continuous running stitches in a circle around the opening or structure
- Bites should be superficial enough to stay in the seromuscular (for bowel) or dartos/periurethral (for tubular structures) layer — not through the lumen
- Tighten the two tails to close the circle
- Knot at the exit point

## Key Uses in Reconstructive Urology

- **Appendiceal stump closure** (Mitrofanoff)
- **Appendiceal inversion** for continent channel
- **Bowel segment end closure** during urinary diversion
- **Stoma creation** at the skin level (for ileal conduit, Mitrofanoff)
- **Catheter stab cystotomy closure** during complex bladder surgery
- **Ureteral cuff inversion** in some techniques
