# Subcuticular

The **subcuticular** stitch runs a suture **within the dermal layer** without piercing the skin surface. Produces a near-invisible scar line — the cosmetic standard for closing skin incisions that will heal by primary intention.

## Technique

- Enter the dermis at one end of the wound
- Run the suture in **small bites entirely within the dermis** — never emerging to the skin surface
- Alternate passes parallel to the wound axis, advancing in zigzag fashion just beneath the epidermis
- Exit at the far end of the wound; tie or terminate with a buried knot, **Aberdeen knot**, or external bolster

## Key Uses in Reconstructive Urology

- Skin closure of **Pfannenstiel**, midline, flank, and perineal incisions
- Inguinal and scrotal skin closure where cosmesis matters
- Hypospadias and GAS closures — dartos layer approximation with subcutaneous extension
- **Dermal** closure beneath a running surface subcuticular or surface skin adhesive

## Suture Choice

- Typically **4-0 or 5-0 Monocryl** or similar slowly-absorbing monofilament
- **Non-absorbable** (polypropylene) with knots pulled at 7–10 days in select high-tension areas
