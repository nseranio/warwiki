# Question Bank

**Rounds** is the free question bank shared by WARWIKI and its oncology sister site, WHITWIKI. It holds more than 800 board-style questions across reconstructive urology, URPS, general urology and urologic oncology. Each question is a clinical vignette with five choices; the answer screen explains the correct choice and why each other choice is wrong, adds a clinical pearl and, for trials, a card with the numbers to quote, and links to its source.

## How Rounds Works

- **Blocks:** choose All, GURS/URPS, General or Oncology, then any mix of topics, a yield level and a question type (pathophysiology, evaluation, management or trials). Tutor mode shows the answer after each question; timed mode allows 90 seconds a question and shows answers at the end.
- **Yield:** *Must know* covers guideline statements, landmark trial results and standard decisions; *Good to know* adds subspecialty depth; *Everything* adds detail such as small-series numbers.
- **Recall first:** an optional mode that hides the choices until you have answered from memory.
- **Review:** a missed question returns the next day; a correct one returns after 3 days, then at widening intervals up to 120 days.
- **Trial cards:** the Trials tab collects the key numbers of every trial in the bank.
- **Progress and sync:** progress saves in your browser. Sync links WARWIKI, WHITWIKI and your other devices with a private code, without an account.

## How Questions Are Written

Each question is written from a WARWIKI or WHITWIKI page and the trials it cites, or from current AUA, EAU and NCCN guidelines and the AUA Core Curriculum. A second, independent check compares every answer, number and claim with the same source; questions that fail are corrected or removed, and each question shows the passage it rests on. Questions are written and checked with AI models and are not individually reviewed by a clinician. Errors and suggestions go to [warwikihq@gmail.com](mailto:warwikihq@gmail.com).

## Anki Decks

WARWIKI also publishes four free **Anki decks** on AnkiWeb. Three cover reconstructive, functional and female pelvic urology: one full deck and two fellowship tracks (GURS and URPS) drawn from it. The fourth is an oral-boards deck covering all of urology. Every reconstructive card links back to the WARWIKI section it came from, usually the exact heading.

Each card falls into one of five lanes: landmark trials, treatments, conditions, anatomy and pharmacology. **Core** cards hold the must-know material and appear first in every subdeck; **Extension** cards add trial detail and nuance. Guidelines are condensed to about ten cards each, and each landmark trial to at most five (design, main finding, harms, conclusion, and a limitation or follow-up).

The track decks are subsets of the full deck with their own note identities, so a track and the full deck can be installed side by side. Pick one to start; studying both duplicates the shared cards.

The board deck spans oncology, stones and endourology, pediatrics, infertility and transplant in addition to reconstruction, so it reaches beyond WARWIKI's own scope. It uses two note types. **Cloze** cards test one fact, number or nuance. **Case** cards give an oral-board stem; answer aloud with workup, management, complications and pearls, then flip. Tags mark priority: `yield::high` for facts tested repeatedly on past self-assessment examinations, `yield::med` for guideline content tested less often, `oral-classic` for the case cards, and `verify` where guidelines disagree (the card follows the AUA or current guideline and the alternative view is in the Extra field).

### Getting Started With Anki

1. Install [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) (free on desktop, Android and the web; AnkiMobile is paid on iOS).
2. Open a deck link above and choose Download, then open the file in Anki.
3. Click a topic subdeck to study one area, or the parent deck to study everything. About 20 new cards a day is a sustainable pace.
4. Tap the WARWIKI link on an answer to read the full page.

To update, download and re-import the deck. Retained cards keep their review history when updated; retired cards are not deleted automatically. An update from the older flat full deck also leaves existing cards in the parent deck until they are moved into the topic subdecks in Browse. Errors and suggestions go to [warwikihq@gmail.com](mailto:warwikihq@gmail.com).
