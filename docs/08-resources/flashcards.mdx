# Question Bank

**Rounds** is the free question bank shared by WARWIKI and its oncology sister site, WHITWIKI. It holds more than 2,000 board-style questions across reconstructive urology, URPS, general urology and urologic oncology, some with clinical images. Each question is a clinical vignette with five choices; the answer screen explains the correct choice and why each other choice is wrong, highlights the key finding, adds a clinical pearl and, for trials, a card with the numbers to quote.

## How Rounds Works

- **Today:** a daily set of 20 questions, mostly ones due for review, with the rest new high-yield questions from your three weakest topics.
- **Blocks:** choose All, GURS/URPS, General or Oncology, then any mix of topics, a level (High-yield, Supplemental or In-depth) and a question type (pathophysiology, evaluation, management or trials). Tutor mode shows the answer after each question; timed mode allows 90 seconds a question and shows answers at the end.
- **Recall first:** an optional mode that hides the choices until you have answered from memory.
- **Confidence and review:** submit each answer as Guessed, Fairly sure or Knew it. A wrong or guessed answer returns the next day; the surer you were of a correct answer, the longer it waits, up to 120 days.
- **Your questions and search:** find questions you missed, missed when sure, guessed right or flagged, and search every stem, explanation, trial and pearl.
- **Progress and sync:** progress saves in your browser. Sync links WARWIKI, WHITWIKI and your other devices with a private code, without an account.

## How Questions Are Written

Each question is written from a WARWIKI or WHITWIKI page and the trials it cites, or from current AUA and NCCN guidelines and standard urology texts, and is keyed to US practice. A second, independent check compares every answer, number and claim with the same source; questions that fail are corrected or removed. Questions are written and checked with AI models and are not individually reviewed by a clinician. Errors and suggestions go to [warwikihq@gmail.com](mailto:warwikihq@gmail.com).
