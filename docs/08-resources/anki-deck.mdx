# WARWIKI Anki Cards v1.0

A curated Anki spaced-repetition deck distilled from the site for residents, fellows, and surgeons preparing for board exams or fellowship-level rounds. **2,038 cards** covering the core curriculum across **foundations**, **evaluation**, **clinical conditions**, and **special populations**.

## Scope

The deck covers the parts of the site that test as core curriculum:

| Section | Cards | Coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Foundations** | 621 | Anatomy & physiology (150+), surgical principles (~90), surgical skills (30), perioperative care (~80), pharmacology (~200), tools (~65) |
| **Evaluation** | 264 | History & physical / PROMs, imaging (RUG, VCUG, MRI, US, MAG3, PDUS, CT, radiation safety), laboratory studies, ancillary tests (urodynamics, cystoscopy, anorectal) |
| **Clinical Conditions** | 724 | Storage / incontinence, voiding / outlet, pelvic support, NLUTD (SCI, MS, Parkinson, spina bifida, dysreflexia), upper tract, fistulas, genital / scrotal, pelvic pain (CPP, BPS/IC, PeVD, CP/CPPS, pudendal neuralgia), defecatory disorders |
| **Special Populations** | 429 | Trauma & emergencies (~165), gender-affirming surgery, cancer survivorship, women's health (GSM, endometriosis, rUTI, cancer screening, osteoporosis, STI), lifelong care (geriatric, hypospadias / epispadias, transitional, PUV, BEEC) |

**Deliberately excluded** from v1.0: treatment-atlas operative-step content (covered on the site at `/docs/surgical-techniques/`) and the library (Journal Club, History & Lineage, Resources). These are reference rather than core-recall material; if you want active recall for a specific atlas page, use the page itself rather than this deck.

## How to import

1. **Download** the [CSV file](/anki/warwiki-anki-v1.csv).
2. Open Anki on desktop (the importer is more reliable than the mobile clients).
3. **File → Import…** and select `warwiki-anki-v1.csv`.
4. The file's header instructs Anki to use **tab** as the field separator, **column 3** as tags, and the deck name **WARWIKI Anki Cards v1.0**. Confirm these settings in the import dialog if Anki prompts.
5. Anki creates the deck and applies hierarchical tags (`WARWIKI::Foundations::Anatomy::UrinaryTract`, etc.) so you can filter by topic or build custom sub-decks.

## How to study

- **All cards** — let Anki's scheduler triage by spaced-repetition interval. At steady state (~3 reviews per card per year, ~15 s per card), the full deck averages ~25 min/day.
- **By section** — filter `tag:WARWIKI::Clinical*` or `tag:WARWIKI::Foundations*` to focus.
- **By topic** — filter to subtopics, e.g. `tag:WARWIKI::Clinical::NLUTD*` for the neurogenic-bladder sub-deck, `tag:WARWIKI::Evaluation::Urodynamics*` for UDS-interpretation drills.
- **Rotation prep** — filter `tag:WARWIKI::SpecialPops::Trauma*` for ED-call review; `tag:WARWIKI::Evaluation::Imaging*` for fluoroscopy week.
- **Board / fellowship review** — work the full deck across 2–3 months of daily study; expect retention >90% if reviews are kept up.

## Card design

Every card follows the same shape:

- **Front** ≤ 200 chars — one specific question or prompt, no rambling lead-ins.
- **Back** ≤ 350 chars — one specific factual answer with a clinical hook (numbers, named criteria, thresholds, decision rules).
- **Tag** — single hierarchical tag, `WARWIKI::<Section>::<Subsection>::<Topic>`.

Card content is anchored in the corresponding WARWIKI articles. The site is the long-form reference; the deck is the active-recall layer. When a card surprises you, open the matching article on the site rather than memorizing the card in isolation.

## What this is, and isn't

**Is:** a structured core-curriculum deck of clinical thresholds, named criteria, classification systems, guideline anchors, named tests, and decision rules across the WARWIKI scope. Surgeon-facing voice. Specific, decision-relevant content.

**Isn't:** an exhaustive board-review course, a substitute for primary literature, or a treatment-atlas substitute. Operative-step detail belongs on the corresponding atlas page where it can be illustrated and updated as technique evolves. Several subsections were under-supported by the site at deck-build time (notably upper-tract pathology beyond UPJO + ureteral stricture); coverage will deepen as the site does.

## Contributing

Spotted an error or want to propose cards? Email **warwikihq@gmail.com** or open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/nseranio/warwiki/issues). Card additions should:

- Map to existing site content (or trigger a new page if the topic isn't yet covered).
- Use the existing tag taxonomy (`WARWIKI::<Section>::<Subsection>::<Topic>`).
- Be tab-separated three-column TSV: `Front<TAB>Back<TAB>Tag`.
- Respect the length caps (200 / 350 chars).
- Prefer one decision-relevant fact per card over piling related facts together.
