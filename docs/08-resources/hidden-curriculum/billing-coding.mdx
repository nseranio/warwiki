# Billing, Coding & Reimbursement

Reference tables for CPT procedure codes, ICD-10 diagnosis codes, and billing modifiers relevant to functional urology, GU reconstruction, and female pelvic medicine. Code descriptions are paraphrased and wRVU values are for educational reference. wRVUs are Medicare Physician Fee Schedule work RVUs from the CMS CY2026 relative value file (RVU26C, July 2026 release). The CY2026 conversion factor is $33.4009 ($33.5675 for clinicians who are qualifying APM participants). Values change each January, and "Carrier priced" codes have no national wRVU. Verify against the AMA CPT database and the CMS Physician Fee Schedule before billing.

> **Note on terminology.** Several ICD-10 code descriptions below (e.g., F64.0 *Transsexualism*, Q56.0–Q56.4 *Hermaphroditism / Pseudohermaphroditism*, Z87.890 *Personal history of sex reassignment*) use clinical language that is now outdated and, in some cases, considered stigmatizing. The text is reproduced verbatim from the WHO/CMS code set because billing accuracy requires matching the official descriptor. It does not reflect preferred contemporary clinical language. In clinical documentation and patient-facing communication, use current terminology (e.g., *differences of sex development (DSD)*, *gender dysphoria* or *gender incongruence*, *gender-affirming surgery*).

## Coding and Coverage Notes (2025–2026)

Dated practice notes from the AUGS Coding Committee commentaries and CMS documents. Re-check each January and against the local payer.

- **E/M with procedures.** Append modifier 25 to the E/M code, never to the procedure. A same-day procedure and E/M also block the add-ons +99459 and G2211, so separating the visits is sometimes cleaner (Thompson 2025).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup> Inside a global period, use 99024 for related follow-up. Unrelated care during a global period takes modifier 24 (E/M) or 79 (procedure).<sup>[[1]](#ref1)</sup>
- **E/M level selection.** Choose by medical decision-making or total time on the date of the visit. Prolonged-service code 99417 is added for each full 15 minutes beyond the highest code's minimum time (Davidson 2025).<sup>[[2]](#ref2)</sup> Medicare does not accept 99417 (status I on the CY2026 file) and uses G2212, which has its own time thresholds.
- **+99459 (pelvic examination).** Practice-expense-only add-on to an office E/M, once per patient per day, nonfacility settings only. Document the indication and chaperone presence (Price and Mamik 2025).<sup>[[3]](#ref3)</sup> Its RVUs are in the table above.
- **G2211.** Add-on for longitudinal care of a serious or complex condition, such as recurrent prolapse followed with a pessary. It cannot be reported when a same-day procedure carries modifier 25 (Mamik and Price 2025).<sup>[[4]](#ref4)</sup>
- **Modifiers 51, 59 and 22.** Prolapse repair plus midurethral sling reports 57240 with 57288-51. Cystoscopy (52000) is bundled with a sling and is not unbundled. Reserve modifier 22 for work substantially greater than typical, with the reason, added time and specific challenges in the operative note (Molden 2026).<sup>[[5]](#ref5)</sup>
- **Sacral neuromodulation.** Code the electrode by approach: 64561 percutaneous (10-day global, imaging included) or 64581 open (90-day global; add 76000-26 for fluoroscopy interpretation). A peripheral nerve evaluation is 64561, with 95971 or 95972 when programmed. A replacement reports only the new lead and/or generator code (Muñoz and Davidson 2025).<sup>[[6]](#ref6)</sup>
- **Urodynamics.** Bill the highest-valued complex code first (51726–51729) and add 51741-51 or 51784/51785-51 for uroflowmetry or EMG. 51797 needs no modifier. Post-void residual is bundled, and a simple cystometrogram is not combined with complex codes (Raza-Khan and Baker 2025).<sup>[[7]](#ref7)</sup>
- **Implantable tibial nerve stimulation.** Category III codes carry no national RVUs, so payers may ask for a comparable Category I code. Obtain prior authorization and document diagnosis and prior treatments (Shah and Panza 2026).<sup>[[8]](#ref8)</sup>
- **Onabotulinumtoxin A coverage.** LCD L35170, effective February 22, 2026, requires an objective OAB severity scale at baseline and at each follow-up, at least 12 weeks of standard treatment including behavioral and drug therapy, and 100 U for idiopathic OAB. Injections may not be repeated more often than every 12 weeks. These are local policies that other contractors may not have adopted (AUGS advisory; Hall 2026).<sup>[[9]](#ref9)[[10]](#ref10)</sup>
- **Budget neutrality.** When CMS projects a payment change above $20 million, it offsets it through the conversion factor. Overestimated G2211 use cut the 2024 factor by 2.18% when actual use was about 11% of visits rather than the projected 38% (Hall 2026).<sup>[[11]](#ref11)</sup>
