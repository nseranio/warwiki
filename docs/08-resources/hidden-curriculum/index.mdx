# The Hidden Curriculum

Clinical training teaches you to take care of patients. It doesn't teach you how the system in which you practice actually works — **how money flows, how decisions are made, how operations are structured, how organizations scale**. The consequence is a specialist who can perform a complex posterior urethroplasty but cannot read an RVU report, negotiate a contract, or distinguish bundled payment from fee-for-service.

This section closes that gap. It is the operational literacy layer that surgical training leaves unfilled — the knowledge every practicing surgeon eventually needs and most acquire by accident. Framed for the reconstructive urologist, it covers **healthcare finance, payment models, hospital administration, billing and coding, practice management, and the pathway to physician leadership**.
