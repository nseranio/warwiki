# Journal Club — Landmark Trials

**Journal Club** is a searchable collection of studies in functional and reconstructive urology and urogynecology. It includes randomized trials, observational studies, and evidence syntheses. Select a study to see its population, comparison or study design, primary outcome, result, and caveat. Filter by domain or search by acronym, topic, or finding.
