# Reconstructive & Functional Urology Trial Finder

Search US trials in incontinence, prolapse and urogynecology, urethral stricture, bladder and pelvic pain, BPH, upper tract reconstruction and trauma, sexual health and gender-affirming surgery by disease state, ZIP and institution. Results come from a daily ClinicalTrials.gov snapshot, and opening a study rechecks its live registry record. Study status and site status come from the registry; cohort openings, slots and eligibility are confirmed by the study team. Cancer treatment trials are on WHITWIKI's [GU Oncology Trial Finder](https://whitwiki.org/docs/resources/clinical-trial-finder).
