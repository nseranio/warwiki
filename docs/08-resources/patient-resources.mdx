## Smoking Cessation

Smoking significantly impairs wound healing and increases surgical complication rates. These resources can be shared directly with patients planning elective reconstruction.

<div className="web-grid">

<a className="web-card web-card--urps" href="https://smokefree.gov" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">
  <div className="web-card-name">Smokefree.gov</div>
  <div className="web-card-desc">National Cancer Institute's free smoking cessation platform. Personalized quit plans, text support (SmokefreeTXT), apps, and live chat with trained quit specialists.</div>
  <div className="web-card-url">smokefree.gov</div>
</a>

<a className="web-card web-card--urps" href="https://www.exprogram.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">
  <div className="web-card-name">EX Program</div>
  <div className="web-card-desc">Evidence-based digital quit-smoking program from Truth Initiative. Combines behavioral coaching, community support, and NRT guidance. Free to access with high quit rates in clinical studies.</div>
  <div className="web-card-url">exprogram.com</div>
</a>

<a className="web-card web-card--urps" href="https://www.cdc.gov/tobacco/campaign/tips/quit-smoking/index.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">
  <div className="web-card-name">CDC — Tips to Quit Smoking</div>
  <div className="web-card-desc">CDC's Tips From Former Smokers quit-smoking resource. Printable guides, quit-line numbers (1-800-QUIT-NOW), and links to state quitline services. Excellent for patient handouts.</div>
  <div className="web-card-url">cdc.gov · Tips to Quit Smoking</div>
</a>

</div>
