# WARWIKI Patient Handouts

Plain-language handouts to give patients about a condition, test, medicine or procedure, free to download and print. Each is a two-page PDF in everyday language: what it is, how to prepare, what happens and what to expect afterward. They are general information; adapt the advice to your own practice.
