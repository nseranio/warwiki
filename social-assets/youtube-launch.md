## Step 1 — Create the channel

1. Sign in to YouTube with the account you want to own the channel
2. Create a **new channel** named `WARWIKI` (not a personal channel — a branded one so you can transfer ownership later if needed)
3. YouTube Studio → **Settings → Channel → Basic info**
   - **Country:** United States (or wherever you're based)
   - **Keywords:** paste the keyword block from Step 5
4. YouTube Studio → **Customization → Basic info**
   - **Channel handle:** `@warwikihq`
   - Verify this matches Instagram and Twitter

## Step 4 — Channel settings

**Settings → Channel:**

- **Advanced → Audience:** select **"No, set this channel as not made for kids"** (specialist content for adult professionals)
- **Advanced → Default video language:** English
- **Feature eligibility:** enable all standard and intermediate features; advanced features unlock after you verify the account

**Settings → Upload defaults** (saves future-you time):

- **Category:** Education (most WARWIKI content) — can override per video
- **License:** Standard YouTube License (default)
- **Language:** English
- **Title prefix (optional):** none — keeps titles clean
- **Tags (default applied to every video):** `urology, reconstructive urology, urogynecology, GURS, URPS, functional urology, medical education`

## Step 6 — Playlist structure (create empty now)

Pre-create these so every video auto-organizes on upload. Each playlist also becomes a **Channel section** on your homepage.

1. **WARWIKI — Site Tour** — feature walkthroughs on web + mobile
2. **Operative Atlas** — technique deep-dives (urethroplasty, prolapse repair, sling, AUS, Peyronie's, etc.)
3. **Surgeons & Lineage** — named surgeon biographies + dynasty explainers
4. **Journal Club** — commentary on recent papers in the database
5. **Hidden Curriculum** — healthcare finance, billing & coding, practice management
6. **Quick References** — 1–2-minute shorts explaining a single concept, stitch, or instrument

For each playlist:
- Title exactly as above
- Short description (1–2 sentences) pointing to the site section it tracks
- Privacy: **Public**
- Auto-add settings: leave default; you'll add videos manually

## Step 10 — Thumbnails (optional: I can generate a template)

YouTube thumbnails are 1280×720. Strong ones share three properties:

1. **Face or object, not just text** — a single surgical instrument, a pelvic-anatomy still, or an article screenshot with an annotation
2. **Title overlay in Inter 800 white** (matches the site)
3. **WARWIKI badge in corner** — use the 40px W

## After launch — cadence I'd recommend

- **Week 1:** trailer + 1 upload (Video 2 above)
- **Weeks 2–4:** one upload per week
- **After 4 videos:** pick the best-performing pillar and double down; retire the weakest

Long-form educational channels build slowly — that's fine; the audience you want finds you via search, not trending. Each video has a 2–3-year tail on YouTube search if the title + description + closed captions are well-keyworded.
