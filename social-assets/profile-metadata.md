```
WARWIKI is a specialist reference for functional urology and genitourinary reconstruction — written for urology residents, fellows, and practicing reconstructive surgeons.

Organized around the two reconstructive subspecialties — Genitourinary Reconstructive Surgery (GURS) and Urogynecology & Reconstructive Pelvic Surgery (URPS) — WARWIKI codifies the operative thinking, evidence base, and surgical lineage of modern reconstruction in one searchable library.

What's here:
• Anatomy and physiology framed for the reconstructive surgeon
• Clinical conditions, evaluation, and a full treatment atlas
• Surgical skills — signature GURS and URPS techniques
• Pharmacology, instruments, biomaterials, robotics — searchable databases
• Surgeon profiles and fellowship lineage trees (GURS and URPS)
• Historical context for every major technique
• Audio articles and hover-to-view citations

Edited by Nicolas Seranio, MD. Advised by Humberto Villarreal, MD.

Visit: warwiki.org
```

## Positioning notes (consistent across platforms)

**What WARWIKI is:**
- A specialist, open-access reference wiki for functional urology, genitourinary reconstruction, and urogynecology
- Written for residents, fellows, and practicing surgeons — not patients

**What WARWIKI is not (for clarity if ever asked):**
- Not a consumer-health site
- Not affiliated with any single institution
- Not a replacement for textbooks, guidelines, or clinical judgment — a clinical companion

**Voice across all platforms:**
- Formal, confident, specialist
- No emoji
- No exclamation points
- Academic-journal tone — the reader is a colleague, not a follower

**The tagline is non-negotiable:** *Reconstruction, codified.* Use it as the consistent sign-off on every platform, banner, and cover.
